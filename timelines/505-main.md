## July 1922 fire detail

The 20 July 1922 *Banner-Courier* says the pool hall adjoining the former Alex Schiermann tailoring premises had billiard tables damaged by water and part of its roof burned. The 21 July *Oregon City Enterprise* says the building immediately north of the fire was **saved by fireproof construction**. In the integrated frontage reconstruction, the origin building is **503 Main** (Miller shoe store + vacant former-Schiermann portion) and the adjoining surviving pool/billiard building is **505 Main**.

This supersedes older timeline wording that left “505 as pool hall/ignition room” as an equal unresolved alternative. **505 is not the ignition building and was not razed in the July 1922 fire.** Legal ownership of 505 at the instant of the fire remains a separate question unless/until the Harding/Wolf/Gates deed geometry proves it.

## Ownership controls

Business operation, business ownership, leasehold, and real-estate ownership must not be merged. In particular:

- Wolf & Miller's 1918 operation at 505 does not by itself prove real-estate ownership.
- The July 13, 1922 Harding sale says S. Wolf took Harding's “remainder,” but the exact legal relationship of that wording to the separately surviving 505 pool-hall building still needs deed geometry.
- Richard/Sheila Wiitanen's later building ownership is independently supported by family evidence plus official/assessment snapshots.
- **Current real-estate owners are Rodney Young and Mitchell Young.**
- Alin Guria/Thirsty Duck is the current operating-business/lessee side, not the real-estate owner.
