# 507 Main Street — Unified Timeline

This timeline tracks the numbered address and related building/business history. It does not imply physical-building continuity across reconstruction events. Business ownership, operation, tenancy, building ownership and land ownership remain separate questions.

## 1912–1914 — G. H. Young / 507 Main

George H. Young's second-hand/curio business is documented at **507 Main** after the earlier 505 Main phase. The archive treats the movement from 505 to 507 as strongly supported by the dated business evidence, without assuming the same physical building.

## 1915 — W. W. Bradley furniture

Dated 1915 records place **W. W. Bradley's furniture business at 507 Main** (`BUS-026`). No uninterrupted succession from Young is assumed.

## Spring 1921 — Dr. Harvey Freeze at 507½

Dr. Harvey Freeze advertised at **507½ Main** through at least early May 1921 before moving to 505/505½ Main. The half-address demonstrates internal/sub-premises numbering in this frontage and should not be treated as a separate fee parcel without additional evidence.

## 22 September 1921 — Red Front Clothing Store at 507 Main

The project steward located and visually read an advertisement in the **Aurora Observer, 22 September 1921, p.3** that explicitly places the **Red Front Clothing Store at 507 Main Street, Oregon City**.

Evidence state: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT — STEWARD VISUALLY VERIFIED** for the printed address. The archive still seeks its own preserved page/crop or independent scan review for ordinary repository visual-verification credit.

This exact-address advertisement is the strongest current anchor for Red Front's location.

## 13 July 1922 — Harding property sale

A visually verified **Banner-Courier, 13 July 1922, p.1** report states that George A. Harding's Main Street property group included the Fifth Street Restaurant, Miller's shoe store, the adjoining barber shop and **Red Front Clothing Store**. The property was sold to **S. Wolf and C. Gates, both of Portland**; Gates took the Fifth Street Restaurant location and Wolf took **the remainder** (`E-096` / `S-111`).

The complete page is preserved at `newspapers/banner-courier/1922-07/1922-07-13-seq-01.pdf`.

The article does not print Red Front's number, but its inclusion in the same named business/property group less than a year after the exact 507 advertisement materially supports continuity.

## 18–21 July 1922 — fire / Red Front damage

Contemporary fire reporting continues to identify the **Red Front Clothing Store** as a distinct fire-loss business. The **21 July Oregon City Enterprise p.1** identifies **L. Wolf** as owner of Red Front and says the business was damaged by the fire. The same report credits fireproof construction with saving the building immediately north of the fire-origin structure.

Current evidence state for **Red Front at 507 at the time of the fire**: **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE**. The direct 22 September 1921 exact-address ad, the 13 July 1922 Harding property-group article and the July fire accounts all fit 507, but no recovered July 1922 source yet prints `Red Front, 507 Main`.

Do not equate business ownership with real-estate ownership. The July sources name **S. Wolf** in the property sale and **L. Wolf** as Red Front owner; that initial conflict remains unresolved.

## 1922 fire-frontage reconstruction context

The current project reconstruction is:

- **501 Main** — fire-destroyed corner property / Fifth Street Restaurant reconstruction context; exact ownership/building mapping remains source-sensitive.
- **503 Main** — **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE** for Gilbert Miller's shoe-store / vacant former Alex Schiermann tailor portions and the fire-origin building.
- **505 Main** — adjoining pool/billiard premises; water damage and partial roof burning, but not razed; contemporary reporting says fireproof construction saved the building immediately north of the fire.
- **507 Main** — Red Front Clothing Store directly documented there in September 1921 and strongly inferred to remain there for the July 1922 fire.

This sequence is a synthesis; each numbered assignment retains its own evidence state.

## 1967–1968 — later Harding Building replacement context

The 1 September 1967 Main Street fire affected the later 507–511 commercial group. ODOT historic-resource work dates the **present Harding Building at 507–511 Main to 1968**. This later building must not be conflated with the 1921–1922 Red Front premises.
