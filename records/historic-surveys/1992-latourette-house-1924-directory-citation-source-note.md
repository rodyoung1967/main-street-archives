# 1992 Latourette House NRHP Nomination — 1924 Oregon City Directory Source Lead

Source: National Park Service, National Register of Historic Places nomination, DeWitt Clinton Latourette House, certified 1992.  
Canonical PDF: https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/152322a5-6e9f-44b2-8871-c4fef169dad0/

## Relevant Source Information

On PDF page 6, the bibliography includes:

- **Polk, R.L. and Company, Oregon City Directory, 1924.**
- Sanborn fire maps of Oregon City, 1926.
- Oregon City Historic Resource Inventory, 1982.

The same page identifies the **Oregon City Planning Department** as the specified repository under “Primary location of additional data.”

## Archive Interpretation

This official nomination is strong evidence that a **1924 Oregon City Directory** existed and was used as a historical research source. It gives us a concrete institutional lead for locating the actual directory.

It does **not** establish what business occupied 501, 503, or 505 Main in 1924 because the directory entries themselves are not reproduced in the nomination.

## Follow-up

Request or inspect the 1924 directory from Oregon City Planning / Development Services or any successor archive. Priority pages:

1. Main Street address index covering **501, 503, 505, 507 and adjacent addresses**.
2. Business-classified listings for undertakers/funeral directors, restaurants/cafes, billiards/pool/cigars, clothing/shoes, and general stores.
3. Alphabetical entries for **R. L. Holman**, **O. A. Pace / Holman & Pace**, and known 503/505 businesses.
