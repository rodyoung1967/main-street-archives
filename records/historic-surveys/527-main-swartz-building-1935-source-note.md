# 527 Main Street / Swartz Building — 1935 source note

## Purpose

This note preserves the verified source details used to date the **current building at 527 Main Street, Oregon City** (the Swartz Building) to **1935** and to distinguish it from the earlier 527 Main storefront documented in 1912.

## S-062 — City of Oregon City, Downtown Final Report, 2000

Canonical PDF:
https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/4025/Downtown-Final-Report---2000-PDF

Official source: City of Oregon City historic-resources survey/report.

Verified 20 August 2026.

Relevant findings:

- The report's historic-resource summary identifies **527 Main St.** as the **Swartz Building**.
- Construction date is **1935**.
- The report classifies the building as historic but not eligible in its then-current state.
- On p. 14, the report discusses changes since the 1983 survey and specifically notes an alteration of the entrance of the **Swartz Building (527 Main)**. It says earlier inappropriate materials were removed, but still-inappropriate materials were used in the alteration.

Interpretation:

- The municipal survey is strong official secondary evidence that the present surveyed Swartz Building dates to 1935.
- The 1935 date means the building currently associated with 527 Main is not the same structure occupied by Bailey & Price in January 1912.
- The entrance-alteration note shows that the building has experienced later facade/entrance changes, so current appearance should not be assumed to match the 1935 facade exactly.

## S-063 — ODOT Cultural Resources Planning Document, 12 May 2021

Canonical PDF:
https://www.oregon.gov/odot/Projects/Project%20Documents/Cultural%20Resources%20Baseline%20Report_REDACTED%20VERSION.pdf

Document title: *Cultural Resources Planning Document for the Oregon City-West Linn Pedestrian & Bicycle Bridge Concept Plan (Redacted Version)*.

Prepared through the University of Oregon Museum of Natural & Cultural History for ODOT; dated 12 May 2021.

Verified 20 August 2026.

Relevant finding on p. 16, Table 2:

| Address | Description | Build date | NR status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 527 Main St | Swartz Building | **1935** | Not Eligible |
| 507-511 Main St | Harding Building | **1968** | Not Eligible |
| 517-525 Main St | Klossen Building | 1931 | Not Eligible |

Interpretation:

- The ODOT table corroborates the City survey's **1935** date for the Swartz Building at 527 Main.
- The same official table dates the present 507-511 Harding Building to **1968**, providing a documentary replacement-building date after the 1 September 1967 fire.
- Because the 2021 report may rely on earlier municipal/state inventory work, it should be treated as strong corroboration rather than assumed to be a wholly independent original dating investigation.

## Relationship to the 1912 storefront

`S-061` documents **Bailey & Price, Billiard and Pool Parlors, at 527 Main Street** on 5 January 1912. Since the present Swartz Building is dated to 1935 by `S-062` and `S-063`, the 1912 Bailey & Price business necessarily occupied an **earlier structure at the same numbered address** (assuming no later address renumbering evidence changes the interpretation).

This gives the archive a useful building transition:

- **5 January 1912:** Bailey & Price documented at 527 Main in the predecessor storefront/building.
- **1935:** present Swartz Building constructed according to official historic-resource surveys.
- **1 September 1967 fire:** the present 527 building already existed. The 1935 dating means it was not a wholesale post-1967 replacement, although this evidence alone does not establish whether it suffered lesser fire, smoke, water, or repair damage.
- **By 2000 survey:** entrance/facade alterations were documented.
