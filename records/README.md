# Administrative and Property Records

This folder is the canonical home for administrative, legal, ownership, property, tax, assessor, permit, lease, and similar documentary records used by The Main Street Archive.

## Suggested Subfolders

Create these as source volume requires:

```text
records/
  deeds/
  tax/
  assessor/
  permits/
  leases/
  historical-society/
```

Existing standalone records may remain directly under `records/` until moving them materially improves organization.

## Handling Rules

- Preserve the original source file whenever possible.
- Keep transcriptions or summaries distinct from the original document.
- Add or update the relevant `S-###` source entry and `E-###` evidence entry when a record supports a historical claim.
- Distinguish real-estate ownership from business ownership, business operation, trade names, and liquor-license ownership.
- Record parcel/APN, recording number, permit number, document date, parties, and provenance when available.
- Preserve uncertainty when a record is incomplete or ambiguous.

## Legacy Paths

The top-level `deeds/` and `tax-records/` folders are older holding areas. Do not add new material there. Their contents and references should be audited before removal, and no deletion should occur without project-steward approval.
