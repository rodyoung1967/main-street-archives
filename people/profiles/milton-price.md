# Milton Price

Person ID: `P-402`.

The visually verified 19 January 1912 *Oregon City Courier* page 7 says the annual jury was drawn from the Clackamas County assessment roll and, under **Oregon City, Oregon, No. 1**, prints **“Milton Price, pool hall.”** (`E-282` / `S-310`).

Treat **pool hall** as the source's occupation/descriptor only. The page does not identify a trade name, street address, proprietor/employee status, or connection to H. N./H. H. Smith at 503 Main, Clem Dollar, A. Leland at 505½, or later 505 pool-hall operators.
