# Dr. Bailey

Person ID: `P-002`. Related address: `B-003`.

An earlier archive statement placed a Dr. Bailey dental practice upstairs in the Harding Building in 1909, but no supporting source has been recovered. The Museum of the Oregon Territory reference spreadsheet for external object `MOT:P-391` identifies a Dr. Bailey among four men in the institutionally dated 1910 G. H. Young storefront photograph (`IMG-0725`). That identification does not establish an office location or 1909 tenancy.

The Harding Building occupancy remains an unresolved lead under `OQ-030`; do not cite it as established.
