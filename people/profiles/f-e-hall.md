# F. E. Hall (`P-100`)

Role: former Palace Saloon partner of Ralph O. Smith.

A visually verified 25 June 1909 *Oregon City Courier* item says Ralph O. Smith purchased the interest of his former partner, **F. E. Hall**, in the Palace Saloon (`S-177` / `E-157`). The source does not give Hall's full name, partnership start date, percentage interest, consideration, liquor-license role, real-estate ownership or building ownership.
