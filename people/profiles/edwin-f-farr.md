# Edwin F. Farr

Person ID: `P-028`. Related business/address: `BUS-013`, `B-002`.

The City historic-resource inventory identifies Edwin F. Farr and Alice Farr as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main** and says they acquired their residence in 1934 (`E-033`, `S-036`).

The 1934 residence date is not treated as the pool hall's opening date. The exact business span, property interest at 505, and transition to Harr's Recreation Center remain unresolved.

## Identity control

A 19 July 1917 *Oregon City Courier* draft-number page has an **OREGON CITY NO. 1** entry for **Edwin Franklin Farr**. The primary newspaper text is a strong candidate expansion of the later `Edwin F. Farr`, but the page image could not be visually certified in the current environment and no recovered source yet connects Edwin Franklin Farr to 505 Main, Farr's Pool Hall, or Alice Farr. Therefore `P-028` is **not silently expanded or merged** on that evidence alone.

A related identity checkpoint also preserves an unresolved Alice Farr conflict: the 2002 City survey says Edwin F. and Alice Farr remained at 401 Third until their respective deaths in the late 1960s, while a secondary cemetery aggregator reports an **Alice Clarinda Sprague Farr (1893–1934)** buried at Logan Pleasant View Cemetery. It is not established that this is the same Alice Farr. Primary marriage, deed, census, and death records are required before resolving the identities.
