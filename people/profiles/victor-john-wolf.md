# Victor John Wolf — Oregon City identity control

## Archive ID

- Person ID: `P-399`
- Related evidence: `E-113`, `E-281`
- Related sources: `S-126`, `S-308`, `S-309`

## What is established

The visually verified 1920 Portland directory lists **Victor (Alice) Wolf**, a Goodyear Rubber Co. clerk, with residence **Oregon City, Oregon** (`S-126`).

The visually verified 27 June 1922 *Morning Oregonian* reports that **Victor Wolf**, Germany, Oregon City Route 6, received final citizenship papers at Oregon City on 26 June (`S-308`). The Oregon Historical Records Index separately catalogs **Wolf, Victor John** and **Wolf, Victor** under the same 1922 Clackamas County naturalization-certificate case 1693777 (`S-309`). This supports a strong cross-source identification of the contemporary Oregon City candidate as **Victor John Wolf**, subject to inspection of the underlying papers.

## Separation rule

Do not merge `P-399` with `P-035` S. Wolf, the July 1922 Harding-property buyer and reported fire-origin building owner. The initials differ, and the close-dated newspaper descriptions say **Oregon City Route 6** for Victor versus **of Portland** for S. Wolf. No deed, signature, address, business, family or property source bridges them.

The index's Alice entries and the directory's parenthetical Alice are useful comparison points, but the index pages alone do not define the relationship or explain the Alice Bertha/Alice Marie forms.
