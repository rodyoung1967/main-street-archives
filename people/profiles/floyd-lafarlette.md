# Floyd LaFarlette

Person ID: `P-022`. Related business/address: `BUS-004`, `B-001`.

Family history identifies Floyd as Raye LaFarlette's first husband and associates Floyd and Raye with **The Wheel at 503 Main**. Raye LaFarlette later used the surname Grisham; she is one person, not two operators.

## Pre-Wheel business record

Floyd's business history is now independently documented outside The Wheel context.

- A visually reviewed **18 October 1951 Coquille Valley Sentinel** advertisement directly identifies **Charlie Elkins and Floyd LaFarlette as the new owners of The Myrtle Lodge**, a steaks/seafood/chicken establishment advertising Saturday-night dancing from 10 p.m. to 1 a.m.
- A visually reviewed **11 June 1953 Coquille Valley Sentinel** article names **Mr. and Mrs. Floyd LaFarlette** among the hosts of a banquet and dance at The Myrtle Lodge.
- Indexed/OCR *Myrtle Point Herald* text from **16 August 1962** identifies Floyd with ownership of the **Myrtle Hotel**, its **coffee shop**, and **Lehua** in Myrtle Point.
- Indexed/OCR text from the **21 February 1963** article “Myrtle Hotel Begins Major Renovation This Week” explicitly places **Mr. and Mrs. Floyd LaFarlette** in the owner/renovation context and associates the article with **Myrtle Hotel, Myrtle Coffee Shop, and Club Lehua**.
- Indexed text from **6 May 1965** calls Floyd an **operator** in connection with Myrtle Hotel building plans.

The 1962–65 Myrtle Point items remain **OCR/TEXT ONLY** because the NewspaperArchive page images were not accessible in the present research environment. Their exact legal wording must be visually verified before assigning a definitive ownership interest to Raye or treating Club Lehua as a separately owned entity.

The Myrtle Lodge and Myrtle Hotel / Club Lehua must remain separate unless contemporary evidence explicitly bridges them: The Myrtle Lodge was on the Highway 101 corridor between Coquille and Coos Bay, while the later hotel/nightlife cluster is explicitly in Myrtle Point.
