# R. L. Holman

Person ID: `P-050`. Related business: `BUS-035`.

The Holman undertaking establishment was on Main near Fifth when Holman & Randall formed in May 1915. On 6 April 1916, the *Courier* reported that R. L. Holman bought T. P. Randall's interest (`E-116`). Holman-only advertising appears by August, although joint-name copy continued for a time (`E-119`).

The records do not establish a numbered 501/503/505 premises, real-estate ownership, or identity with a Portland Holman estate. Sources: `S-130`, `S-133`.
