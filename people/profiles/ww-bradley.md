# W. W. Bradley

Person ID: `P-041`. Related business/address: `BUS-026`, `B-003`.

Dated 1915 and selected 1916 directory advertisements place W. W. Bradley's furniture business at **507 Main** (`E-107`, `E-109`, `E-116`, `E-119`).

The sources do not expand the initials, establish property ownership, or prove a transfer from G. H. Young's earlier 507 business. Sources: `S-120`, `S-121`, `S-130`, `S-133`.

## CCHS MOT:P-502 conflict with the 1916 507 Main advertisements

CCHS photograph MOT:P-502 (`S-209` / `E-189` / `IMG-0738`) is cataloged as **G. H. Young Store — 507 Main Street — 1916** and visibly bears G. H. Young collection lettering. That institutional dating/address conflicts with selected visually verified 1916 newspaper business-directory advertisements placing Bradley at **507 Main**. Neither source family is discarded. A dated Young/Bradley move, sale, opening, license, or directory bridge is still required.
