# Justin S. Lageson

**Person ID:** `P-403`

## Documented role

A visually verified 1 April 1910 *Oregon City Enterprise* advertisement directly identifies **Justin S. Lageson, Prop.** of **Oregon City Shoe Store** on Main Street. A 28 January 1910 *Oregon City Courier* advertisement also places his name directly beneath the Oregon City Shoe Store name and identifies the location as opposite the Post Office.

## Limits

The 26 April 1912 advertisement directly places Oregon City Shoe Store at **508 Main**, but it does not print Lageson's name. Continuous proprietorship from 1910 to April 1912 is therefore unresolved.

Do not merge Lageson/Oregon City Shoe Store with C. Schoenheinz's separately documented Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop without direct transfer/identity evidence.

Related: `BUS-109`, `S-389`, `E-355`.
