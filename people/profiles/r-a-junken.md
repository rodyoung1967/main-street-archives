# R. A. Junken

Person ID: `P-092`. Related business/site: `BUS-070` Fifth Street Restaurant replacement.

Visually reviewed 17–18 August 1922 reports identify **R. A. Junken** as contractor for C. E. Gates's two-story hollow-tile building at Fifth/Main, replacing the fire-destroyed restaurant building (`E-097`). The sources do not give a permit number, design authorship, exact dimensions, numbered storefronts, or completion date.
