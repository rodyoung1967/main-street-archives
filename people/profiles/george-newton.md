# George Newton

- Person ID: `P-106`
- Direct business relationship: proprietor, `BUS-088` Quality restaurant, August 1922.
- Evidence: `E-180`; sources `S-200`, `S-201`, `S-202`.

Original newspaper scans say firefighters saved his restaurant during the recent Fifth Street fire. The Banner-Courier locates it **across the way from Oregon City Laundry**. He entertained the fire department on calendar-derived **15 August 1922**, reported 17–18 August.

The reports do not print a street number. **427 Main is probable, not directly proved**: the June Quality Restaurant advertisement at that address names no proprietor (`BUS-087`, `E-179`). `BUS-089` Quality Cafe roster wording also remains unmerged. No known source here places Newton at 503 or proves property ownership.

Ariel Newton is named in the banquet's marriage/gift account, but no kinship with George is stated. The Mrs. George Newton/Genevieve Steen personal notice remains an OCR-only identity lead, not a family assignment.
