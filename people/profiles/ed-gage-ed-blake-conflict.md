# Ed Gage / Ed Blake — Source Conflict

Person/identity record: `P-088`. Related business: `BUS-070` Fifth Street Restaurant.

Contemporary July 1922 fire accounts variously name **Ed Gage of Portland**, **Ed. Blake**, and **J. Blake** in owner/operator descriptions of the Fifth Street Restaurant. This record preserves the conflict; it does not assert that Gage and Blake were the same person, choose a corrected spelling, or establish the property's legal owner.

Resolve through the original local report, business licenses, directory entries, deed/assessment records, or another same-context source.
