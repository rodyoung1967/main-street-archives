# Alice Farr

Person ID: `P-029`. Related business/address: `BUS-013`, `B-002`.

The City historic-resource inventory identifies Alice Farr and Edwin F. Farr as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main** and says they acquired their residence in 1934 (`E-033`, `S-036`).

The source does not establish that 1934 was the pool hall opening date, the division of operating roles, real-estate ownership at 505, or a Farr-to-Harr transfer date.
