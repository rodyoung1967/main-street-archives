# E. M. Blakeslee

Person ID: `P-078`. Related business: `BUS-068` Oregon City Laundry.

The visually verified 17 February 1916 *Courier* report identifies **E. M. Blakeslee, of the Oregon City Laundry**, and says he traded the rear-Courier property where he had intended to build for a Fifth Street location (`E-125` / `S-139`).

This proves his role in the reported site transaction/plan change, not the exact legal interest, parcel, or relationship to O. L. and M. L. Blakeslee.
