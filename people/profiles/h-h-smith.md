# H. H. Smith

Person ID: `P-023`. Related business/address: `BUS-019`, `B-001`.

A March 1912 notice records H. H. Smith applying for a pool-hall license at his place of business, **503 Main Street** (`E-023`, `S-024`). Another source variant has produced an H.N./H.H. initials conflict that remains preserved.

The application establishes the business place and requested license; it does not prove that the license was granted, property ownership, or a continuous pool-hall succession at 503.
