# Minnie L. Foster

Person ID `P-410`. The weekly Enterprise on August 13, 1915 names Mrs. M. L. Foster as owner of the southeast Fifth/Main Portland House property and describes her objection to the nearby public-market sheds (`E-371`). The July 21, 1916 Enterprise gives her full name, identifies her as the Portland House owner and reports the Beck lease (`E-318`). These reports give a strong identity match at the same property.

The [Portland House site](../../buildings/426-main-portland-house-site.md) is mapped in 1911 at historic 426 Main. Foster's property role does not independently establish who operated the lodging business. Her acquisition and subsequent conveyance dates are unresolved.
