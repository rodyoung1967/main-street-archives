## Identity Correction (2026-08-16)

Earlier archive notes recorded `P-006` as **Ray Grisham**, possibly a male 505 / The Wheel owner/operator, and kept that name distinct from Ray Hansen (`P-005`) because the two might have been a memory conflict.

That reading is **superseded, not deleted**:

- **Raye Grisham** is a woman, Rodney Young's grandmother.
- First marriage: **LaFarlette** (with Floyd LaFarlette, `P-022`).
- After divorce she remarried and became **Raye Grisham**.
- She **bought The Wheel at 503 Main Street** (`BUS-004`).
- She is **not** the same person as **Ray Hansen** (`P-005`), who sold the **505 Tavern business** at 505 Main to Dick and Sheila Wiitanen.

Keep `P-005` and `P-006` as separate people. Do not merge Hansen and Grisham.

## Working Biography

Family history places Raye in the continuing Wheel operation at 503 and describes a later transition involving Richard / Dick and Sheila Wiitanen. Sheila was Floyd and Raye's daughter.

Victor (`P-010`) independently managed the restaurant portion during Raye Grisham's years and the early Dick/Sheila period (`E-007`). Exact operating dates, ownership interests, liquor-license succession, and Victor's full identity still need documentary confirmation.
