# Percy Cross

Person ID: `P-399`  
Related business: Harding Drug Store (`BUS-007`)  
Related source/evidence: `S-299` / `E-273`

## Direct 1912 evidence

A visually verified 19 January 1912 *Oregon City Courier* local-news item reports that **Percy Cross had accepted a position in Harding Drug Store during the absence of A. B. Wilmot**.

The notice directly establishes Cross's association with the business by that date. It does not state his formal job title, professional credentials, employment duration, ownership interest, or a street number for the store on that page.

Do not infer that Cross was a pharmacist or druggist unless another source states that role.
