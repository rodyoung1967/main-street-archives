# Carl G. Hodes

Person ID: `P-066`. Related business: `BUS-056`.

The current evidence places Carl G. Hodes at **327 Main** in a September 1908 liquor-license application (`E-123`), at an unnumbered **Sixth/Main** saloon in 1909 (`E-076`), and later records his vacation of the Sixth/Main saloon in 1910 (`E-079`).

No source yet proves a 327-to-Sixth/Main move, a purchase from J. Stewart & Son, occupation of the Gambrinus property, or the reason for departure. Sources: `S-137`, `S-090`, `S-093`.
