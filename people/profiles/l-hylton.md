# L. Hylton

Person ID: `P-024`. Related business/address: `BUS-020`, `B-001`.

February and April 1914 newspaper evidence places L. Hylton at **503 Main**, dealing in cigars, tobacco, smokers' supplies, and soft drinks (`E-024`, `S-025`).

The sources establish the dated business occupancy, not given names, property ownership, uninterrupted operation, or succession from H. H. Smith or to a later 503 restaurant.
