# Walter L. Little

Person ID: `P-090`. Related business lead: `BUS-077` Leland & Little.

The 8 June 1922 merchant roster names **Walter Little** separately from O. H. Leland and Timms Pool Hall. Later evidence establishes Walter Little's middle initial as L.; 1921–1922 records also contain W. L. Little / Little, W. L.

The identity equation is a strong inference, but the February cigar-dealer entry does not explicitly expand the name. No recovered source yet states that Walter and O. H. Leland were the partners in Leland & Little or gives the billiards business an address (`E-126`).
