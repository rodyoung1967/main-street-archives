# Henry Brightbill

Person ID: `P-034`. Related business: [`BUS-078`, Henry Brightbill's store](../../businesses/henry-brightbill-store.md).

The *Oregonian* of 25 August 1908 directly places merchant Henry Brightbill's store at **503 Main Street** (`E-042` / `S-049`). This is exact address/occupancy evidence, not proof that the surviving later building was standing.

By September 1910, H. P. Brightbill is listed at 509 Main. The archive does not merge Henry Brightbill and H. P. Brightbill or infer a move solely from surname and address chronology; an independent identity bridge is still required.
