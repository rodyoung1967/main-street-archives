## What is established

The visually verified 13 July 1922 *Banner-Courier* reports that **S. Wolf**, described as being of Portland, bought the remainder of a George A. Harding Main Street property group after the Fifth Street Restaurant location was allocated to C. Gates.

The preserved and visually verified 20 July fire report independently says **S. Wolf of Portland owned the building where the 18 July fire began** in the vacant former Alex Schieremann tailor room (`E-103` / `S-117`).

The tight chronology and common property cluster create a strong contemporary property-chain inference. The recorded deed, legal description, effective date, exact storefront, and Wolf's full identity have not yet been recovered.
