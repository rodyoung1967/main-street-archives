# S. Fesznecker / S. P. Pesznecker

ID: `P-398`

## 12 September 1919 source wording

The visually verified *Oregon City Enterprise*, 12 Sep. 1919, p. 2 prints the name as **S. Fesznecker** and reports that he moved his tin and repair shop from Broadway to the building just south of the post office.

The source spelling is preserved. The earlier archive interpretation that this described an **Oregon City Fifth/Main** move is superseded.

## Estacada locality correction

A next-week **19 Sep 1919 Oregon City Enterprise** follow-up appears within the clearly labeled **Estacada** correspondence section and states that a new building was being erected just north of **S. Pesznecker's residence** for his machine/plumbing shop and auto-livery business.

Independent contemporary records repeatedly place **S. P. / Stephen Pesznecker** in Estacada and in the same mechanical trades, including Cascade Garage in 1917 and an S. P. Pesznecker machine-shop advertisement in 1920.

Current classification:

- `S. Fesznecker` = exact 12 Sep 1919 source spelling/transcription;
- `S. Fesznecker` = **S. P. Pesznecker of Estacada**: **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE IDENTITY INFERENCE**;
- full-name expansion to **Stephen Pesznecker**: strongly supported by independent Estacada records, but the 12 Sep source itself prints only `S.`.

Do **not** use `P-398` as an Oregon City Main Street tenant, Oregon City Post Office neighbor, or 501/503/505 spatial anchor.
