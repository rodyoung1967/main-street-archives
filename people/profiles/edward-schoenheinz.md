# Edward Schoenheinz

**Person ID:** P-406

## Project role

Schoenheinz-family identity and shoe-business control.

## Documented evidence

The 26 January 1912 *Oregon City Courier* marriage notice for **Edward Schoenheinz** reports that he had lived in Oregon City for many years and had been **connected with his father in the shoe business**. This independently establishes that the Schoenheinz shoe operation involved the family and is important context for identifying the 1911 C. Schoenheinz proprietor.

The July 1912 obituary for Mrs. Charles Schoenheinz names Edward among her sons.

A separately documented adult Edward was at Washougal in 1916. Do not confuse him with the **son Edward** named as the child of younger Charles and his wife in the May 1917 Camas-move report.

## Limits

Edward's 1912 report does not identify the shop address, though separate 1911 advertising places **C. Schoenheinz, proprietor**, Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop at 716 Main. It does not place Edward or his father at 503 Main.

## 19 September 2026 full-name lead

The Genealogical Forum of Oregon WWI draft-registration index lists **Edward Hugo Schoenheinz**, birth year **1885**, in the Wasco grouping. A separate genealogical compilation pairs Edward Hugo Schoenheinz with Saloma, matching the first name of the bride in the visually recovered January 1912 newspaper marriage report. Treat **Edward Hugo** as a strong research lead pending inspection of the underlying draft card or another primary identity bridge.
