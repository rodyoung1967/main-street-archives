# T. P. Randall

Person ID: `P-051`. Related business: `BUS-035`.

T. P. Randall was named in the Holman & Randall undertaking partnership. On 6 April 1916, the *Courier* reported that Randall sold his business interest to R. L. Holman (`E-116`).

Later joint-name advertising does not independently prove that Randall retained ownership after the reported sale. The legal transaction day, exact numbered premises, and any real-estate interest remain unresolved. Sources: `S-130`, `S-133`.
