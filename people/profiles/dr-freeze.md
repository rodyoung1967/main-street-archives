# Dr. Freeze

Person ID: `P-027`. Related business/address: `BUS-021`, `B-002`.

Newspaper advertisements place Dr. Freeze, styled an eye specialist, at **505 / 505½ Main** in 1921–1922 and later in rooms 207–208 of the Masonic Building (`E-028`, `S-035`).

The evidence supports the dated professional location and later move, not ownership of 505, residence there, or an exact upstairs-room footprint.
