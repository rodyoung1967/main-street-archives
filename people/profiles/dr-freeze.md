# Dr. Freeze

Person ID: `P-027`. Related business/addresses: `BUS-021`, `B-003` (507½ context), `B-002` (505/505½).

A visually verified 18 March 1921 article names **Dr. Harvey Freeze** and reports that he moved his practice from Sixth and Washington in Portland to Oregon City, locating **over Hardings Drug Store**. Ads on 25 March and 1 April visibly place him at **507½ Main**; by 8 April the existing verified ad places him at **505½ Main** (`E-137`, `S-153`; `E-028`, `S-035`). He remained associated with 505/505½ through late June 1922 before moving to rooms 207–208 of the Masonic Building.

The evidence supports the dated professional locations and address change, not property ownership, residence, exact physical move day, or a proved upstairs-room/modern-building footprint.
