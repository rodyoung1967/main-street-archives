# Washburn

Person ID: `P-084`. Related business: `BUS-069` May & Washburn garage.

Washburn is documented through the May & Washburn partnership by December 1920 and in the 1921–1922 garage chronology. The July 1922 fire destroyed the partnership's frame garage and two automobiles (`E-103`).

The visually verified 16 February 1922 joint horseshoeing advertisement names May & Washburn with S. F. Scripture but gives the partnership no address (`E-347`). It does not establish a merger, shared premises or ownership.

Given name, legal partnership interest, exact premises, and relationship to any earlier Fifth Street business remain unresolved.
