# Ray Hansen

Person ID: `P-005`. Related building/business: `B-002`, `BUS-005`.

Family history places Ray Hansen in the earlier 505 Tavern business chain before Dick and Sheila Wiitanen's Brass Rail period. He is a different person from **Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham** (`P-006`), who belongs to The Wheel at 503.

Exact operating dates, business-sale terms, liquor-license records, and the transition from Hansen to the Wiitanen Brass Rail operation remain open. The archive does not use the family chronology as proof that Hansen owned the 505 real estate.
