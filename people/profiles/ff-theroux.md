# F. F. Theroux

Person ID: `P-044`. Related business/address: `BUS-029`, `B-011`.

Dated advertisements place F. F. Theroux's piano and sewing-machine business at **519 Main Street** in 1915–1917 (`E-107`, `E-109`, `E-116`, `E-119`, `E-120`).

The sources do not expand the initials, establish property ownership, or prove that earlier Fifth/Main wording represents a move. Sources: `S-120`, `S-121`, `S-130`, `S-133`, `S-134`.
