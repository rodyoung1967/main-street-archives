# A. E. Wilmot

Person ID: `P-401`.

The visually verified 19 January 1912 *Oregon City Courier* page 8 says **Percy Cross accepted a position in the Harding drugstore during the absence of A. E. Wilmot** (`E-282` / `S-310`). This establishes Wilmot only as the person whose absence is mentioned in the store-staffing notice. The page does not state a job title, ownership role, street number, or expanded given names.
