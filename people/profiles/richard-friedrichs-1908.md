# Richard Friedrichs — 1908 CCHS hardware-store identification

Person ID: `P-392`. Related business: `BUS-090` Friedrich's Hardware.

CCHS `P-6974` identifies **Richard Friedrichs on right** in the 1908 photograph of Friedrich's Hardware and Little's Confectionery at the combined **510–512 7th Street** address group (`E-199`). This is an institutional catalog identification; the project is not independently identifying him from facial features.

Later repository material contains references to **C. W. Friedrich and Son**. Richard's relationship to that later firm is unresolved and must not be inferred from surname/business type alone.
