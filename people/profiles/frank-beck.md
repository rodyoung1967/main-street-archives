# Frank Beck

Person ID `P-412`. The July 21, 1916 Enterprise names Frank and Mark Beck as Portland House co-lessees dating June 1 (`E-318`). January 11, 1917 Courier p1 reports that Frank bought the 34-by-105-foot lot at Fifth and Main from the prior Minnie Foster ownership for about $8,500. The newspaper names Dillman & Howland as intermediary. January 8 is inferred from the reported Monday date (`E-378`). The deed and exact interests remain open. This is the [historic 426 Main site](../../buildings/426-main-portland-house-site.md).
