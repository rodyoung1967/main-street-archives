# Herman Schrader

Person ID: `P-096`. Related businesses: `BUS-063`, `BUS-080`.

January 1912 advertising places Schrader's Bakery near the Post Office, and a 20 December 1912 *Courier* report says **Herman Schrader sold two Oregon City bakeries, including one on Main Street, to Wemmo & Haas** (`E-093`). A 1908 advertisement separately establishes the Schrader's Bakery name on Main Street (`E-123`).

The evidence does not establish an exact Main number, real-estate ownership, uninterrupted 1908–1912 occupancy, or the terms of sale. Sources: `S-137`, `S-102`, `S-106`.

## 1902 Main-Street Bakery background

`S-161` / `E-145` preserves a 1902 profile stating that Schrader had worked nearly eighteen years in the **Main-Street Bakery** before buying his then-current bakery about a year earlier. Exact premises and continuity to the later Main Street bakery remain unresolved.
