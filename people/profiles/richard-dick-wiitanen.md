# Richard / Dick Wiitanen

Person ID: `P-007`.

## 503 Main / The Wheel

Family history says Dick and Sheila purchased The Wheel business from Raye, later purchased the 503 building, and later sold the business and building together. The exact counterparties and dates remain documentary targets. An August 1985 *Oregonian* report independently names **Richard M. Wiitanen** as owner of The Wheel Cafe business at 503 (`E-049`). Official OLCC indexed license text later associates **Maurice Enterprises Inc** with **The Wheel Bar & Grill, 503 Main**, license 104145 expiring 31 March 2009 (`E-225`; OCR/TEXT ONLY pending page-image verification). This narrows the later business-successor bracket but does not date Richard/Sheila's sale or the 503 deed transfer.

## 505 Main

Dick and Sheila operated Brass Rail before they owned the 505 building. They later purchased the 505 real estate from Mrs. Nesmeth / Nemeth, remained landlords through changing business operators, and appear as transferors in the 1988 Dug Inn record (`E-030`). After a later return of business control, they renamed the tavern **505 Tavern** before selling it to Mitch in March 1991.

A 2017 permit continued to list Richard Martin Wiitanen as owner/co-trustee of the 505 real estate. He died in November 2024; family history says 505 passed through his estate to Rodney and Mitchell Young.

Property ownership, business ownership, operation, and liquor-license roles remain separate throughout this chronology.
