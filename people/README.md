# People Directory

The canonical complete list of named people, families, partnerships, and organization-style entities is [people-index.md](people-index.md), synchronized with `database/people.yml`.

`people/profiles/` contains standalone pages for people whose chronology, identity conflict, or project role needs more explanation than an index row. A person can be fully registered without a standalone profile; one-off applicants and names with only a single bounded fact normally remain in the index until more evidence is recovered.

Central profile groups include:

- 503/505 family and operator history;
- early Main Street merchants and exact-address occupants;
- the 1916–1922 Fifth Street laundry, blacksmith, garage, restaurant, shoe-repair, confectionery, and billiards reconstruction;
- unresolved Wolf/Gates/Schieremann/fire identities.

Never merge people solely by surname, initials, business name, or address chronology. Preserve source spellings and conflicts until a direct identity bridge is found.
