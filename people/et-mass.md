# E. T. Mass

Person **P-045**; business **BUS-030**; evidence **E-109**; source **S-121**.

The October 28 and November 4, 1915 *Courier* reports identify ex-Sheriff **E. T. Mass** with the opening of **Mass's Hotel and Restaurant** in the former Brunswick quarters. His wife is mentioned without a name. No expansion of his initials, deed ownership or unrelated Mass/Maas identity is assumed.
