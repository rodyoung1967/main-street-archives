# 426 Main Street — historic Portland House site

- Building ID: `B-021`. Related business `BUS-118`, person `P-410`, evidence `E-371`, `E-318`, `E-279`.
- **November 1911** — [Sanborn Sheet 16](../maps/sanborn-map-register.md) (`SM-010` / `S-298`) maps the Portland House at the southeast Fifth/Main corner. It places 426 along Main and 204 along the Fifth side of the mapped premises, in original Block 27. The map identifies boarding use.
- **August 13, 1915** — the weekly Enterprise reports that Mrs. M. L. Foster owned the corner property and calls it the Portland House. The cross-source site match is strong. The article gives no civic number or title instrument.
- **July 1916** — the Enterprise reports $800 in improvements and separately describes the Portland House as recently rebuilt. The scope, exact date and permit remain open.
- **December 19, 1919** — the Enterprise reports a small fire at the frame Portland House at Fifth/Main. It names Mr. and Mrs. Beck as owners. The ownership wording does not resolve property title.

This is the 400-block, uphill side of the intersection, outside the odd-numbered 500-block frontage. Physical continuity between 1911, 1915, the reported 1916 rebuild and 1919 remains unresolved. See the [visual audit](../evidence/source-captures/1915-enterprise-pages-226-280.md).
