# 427 Main Street — Historic Storefront Address

- Building ID: `B-016` (address record; no claim of a single continuous physical building).
- Direct dated business: `BUS-087` Quality Restaurant, 8 June 1922 (`E-179`, `S-141`).
- Map context: `E-182`, `S-021`, `SM-008`.

The June advertisement prints **427 Main St.** and **phone 517**, with no proprietor. The May 1925 map places 427 south of Fifth opposite 501, with the laundry north of Fifth/west of Main, and marks the 427 corner clothing/gentlemen's furnishings.

Newton's restaurant (`BUS-088`, `P-106`) is reported across the way from the laundry in August 1922. Its identification with this address is **PROBABLE / WORKING HYPOTHESIS**, not a direct address/person association. June-to-August continuity, any move, name change, sale, parcel identity, building ownership and physical survival remain unresolved. The 1925 mapped use does not itself date a restaurant's closure or relocation.
