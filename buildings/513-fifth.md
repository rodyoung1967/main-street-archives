# 513 Fifth Street — Historic Residence

## Archive ID

- Building ID: `B-006`
- Related evidence: `E-106`
- Related source: `S-118`

## What Is Established

On 19 March 1912, the *Morning Enterprise* reported that Mr. and Mrs. H. M. Templeton intended to return to their home at **513 Fifth Street**, then occupied by M. D. Phillips and family (`E-106` / `S-118`).

## Limits

This is a residential-address record only. The source does not identify the building's modern parcel, owner, construction history, side of Fifth Street, or relationship to the Main/Water commercial segment and July 1922 fire cluster.
