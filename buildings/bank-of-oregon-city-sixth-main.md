# Bank of Oregon City — Sixth and Main

Building ID: `B-020` (address/building question; continuity qualified). Business ID: BUS-115.

December 29 Enterprise p3 reports contracts awarded Wednesday for $25,000 remodeling of Bank of Oregon City at Sixth/Main, expected ninety days: ground-floor bank enlarged 13 feet north, Main entrance moved from corner toward middle, upper offices gutted by September 2 fire rebuilt with three extra suites (14 rooms total), modern uniform exterior. Bank said to own frontage from Sixth to alley and occupy location since 1881. This is a contract/planned remodel, not completed work, not First National Bank at 512, and not Gates/Junken replacement.
