## What Is Established

Oregon City Laundry changed its planned replacement-plant site to Fifth Street in February 1916, and the new building on Fifth just off Main was nearing completion by 2 April (`E-125`). A visually reviewed 24 October 1918 business guide directly lists **Oregon City Laundry, M. L. Blakeslee proprietor, at 109 Fifth Street** (`E-124`). A 1921 advertisement places the plant on Fifth near Main, and the July 1922 fire destroyed the Fifth/Main-area laundry plant (`E-103`).

The chain strongly supports the 1916 Fifth Street replacement as the plant later numbered 109 Fifth, but exact footprint continuity remains an inference. By May 1925, a rebuilt Oregon City Laundry complex was again physically present in the Fifth/Main/Water block and lay behind multiple Main Street storefront positions on the Sanborn map.

## Limits

This address record does not equate the pre-fire 1916–1922 building with the post-fire 1925 footprint, establish legal ownership or lot boundaries, or prove that every laundry photograph depicts the same structure. The July 1922 fire destroyed the pre-fire plant; a later laundry at or near the address therefore requires a rebuild, replacement, or different footprint.
