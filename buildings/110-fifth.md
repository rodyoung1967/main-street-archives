# 110 Fifth Street — Historic Stable Address

## Archive ID

- Building ID: `B-010`
- Related evidence: `E-117`
- Related source: `S-131`

## What Is Established

The visually reviewed 1914–1915 Oregon City water-rent ledger directly records **110 Fifth**, original **Block 3**, with the classification **Stable**.

## Limits

Layered tenant handwriting appears consistent with Scripture & May, but the reading is only probable. Do not identify the tenant as fact, equate 110 Fifth with the unnumbered Fashion Stable, or treat the later 108 Fifth address as proved renumbering or a move without an independent bridge.
