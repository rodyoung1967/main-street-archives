## What Is Established

Selected visually reviewed advertisements place **Scripture & May at 108 Fifth Street** from September through December 1916 and again on 4 January and 1 February 1917. The visually reviewed 24 October 1918 business guide again lists Scripture & May at 108 Fifth and describes the business as carriage and auto works.

S. F. Scripture is independently advertised at **108 Fifth** on 8 June 1922, shortly before the July fire.

## Limits

The address history does not prove that the same physical building survived throughout the period, identify its parcel or construction date, or establish that the 1914–1915 ledger's **110 Fifth / Stable** row was the same premises. The possible connection from Scripture & May to May & Washburn remains unresolved; shared surname/business chronology is not a transfer record.
