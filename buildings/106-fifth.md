# 106 Fifth Street

Building ID: `B-018`.

## Direct evidence

A visually verified **25 May 1917 Oregon City Enterprise** article states that Oregon National Guard **“enlistments are being received at 106 Fifth street”** (`E-253` / `S-280`). This establishes a dated exact-address recruiting/enlistment use.

## Limits

Owner, ordinary tenant, exact room, parcel, footprint, construction date, physical fabric, continuity and duration are unresolved. Do not merge 106 Fifth with 107/108/109/110 Fifth, Fashion Stable, or any Main Street target solely from this evidence.

## Audit history

The 29 August entity audit did not create 106 Fifth because no direct exact-address use had then been recovered. `E-253` supplies that missing direct observation; the old audit wording is retained as historical process documentation.
