# 527 Main Street / Swartz Building

## Archive ID

- Building ID: `B-004`
- Address: **527 Main Street, Oregon City, Oregon**
- Historic name: **Swartz Building**
- Current-building construction date: **1935**
- Primary supporting sources for the current-building date: `S-062`, `S-063`
- Detailed source note: `../records/historic-surveys/527-main-swartz-building-1935-source-note.md`

## Strongly documented facts

Two official historic-resource sources identify the present building at **527 Main Street** as the **Swartz Building** and give a construction date of **1935**:

- City of Oregon City, *Downtown Final Report* (2000), `S-062`.
- ODOT / University of Oregon Museum of Natural & Cultural History, *Cultural Resources Planning Document* (2021), p. 16 Table 2, `S-063`.

The agreement between the two official sources gives **high confidence** that 1935 is the accepted inventory date for the present building. The 2021 source may derive partly from earlier inventory work, so the two dates should be treated as strong corroboration rather than necessarily two wholly independent construction-date investigations.

## Earlier 527 Main storefront

The current 1935 Swartz Building is **not** the structure that stood at this address in 1912.

On **5 January 1912**, the *Oregon City Courier* advertised:

- **Bailey & Price**
- **Billiard and Pool Parlors**
- **527 Main Street**
- **Opposite Grand Theater**

See `S-061` and `newspapers/oregon-city-courier/1912-01-05-page-6-transcription.md`.

Because the present structure is dated 1935, Bailey & Price necessarily occupied an **earlier building/storefront at 527 Main** unless later evidence demonstrates a relevant address renumbering. This creates a documented predecessor-to-current-building transition at the address.

## Earlier Price Bros. occupancy and 1915 incident

The visually rechecked **5 August 1915 Courier, page 3**, lists **Price Bros. clothing store at 527 Main Street**, phone 107 (`S-120` / `E-107`; `BUS-028`). Page 1's “PRICE HAS FIRE” names **Al Price** (`P-043`) as proprietor and reports smoke and an electric iron burning rear-store boards on **Friday night of the preceding week**. Switching off the current averted a potentially serious fire. The incident date is **30 July 1915**, derived from that wording and the publication date; an earlier batch note incorrectly said Thursday and is explicitly corrected in the [integration audit](../evidence/source-captures/1915-sheets-073-100-integration-audit.md).

The incident's connection to **527** is a strong same-issue business-name inference: the incident report itself prints no number. It does not establish closure, demolition, rebuilding, dollar loss, or a real-estate owner. The 1915 store predates the present 1935 Swartz Building. Do not merge Price Bros. with **Bailey & Price (1912)** or identify their Price partners without further evidence; the change between the two dated business listings is unresolved.

## 1935 construction and the 1967 fire

The 1935 construction date is important to interpretation of the major Main Street fire of **1 September 1967** (`E-045`).

The present Swartz Building at 527 Main was already about 32 years old when that fire occurred. Therefore **527 Main was not wholly replaced by a new post-fire building**, unlike the present 507-511 Harding Building, which `S-063` dates to **1968**.

This evidence does **not** prove that 527 Main escaped all fire, smoke, water, or repair damage. It establishes only that the current surveyed building is dated to 1935 and therefore survived as the underlying building resource.

## Later alterations

The City of Oregon City's 2000 downtown report (`S-062`) specifically mentions an alteration of the entrance of the **Swartz Building (527 Main)** in its discussion of changes since the 1983 survey. The report says earlier inappropriate material was removed but that the alteration still used materials considered inappropriate to the historic resource.

Accordingly, the building's modern storefront appearance should not automatically be treated as its original 1935 appearance.

## Open questions

- Who commissioned, designed, financed, and constructed the Swartz Building in 1935?
- Exactly when was the earlier 527 Main building demolished or replaced?
- Who was "Swartz," and what ownership/business connection gave the building its historic name?
- What businesses occupied 527 between Bailey & Price in 1912 and construction of the Swartz Building in 1935?
- Price Bros. is now documented at 527 in August 1915. When did its occupancy start/end, and did this involve a move, renumbering, subdivision, or succession from Bailey & Price? No relationship between their Price partners is established. What repairs, if any, followed the July 1915 incident?
- What, if any, damage did 527 Main sustain in the 1 September 1967 fire?
- What was the date and scope of the entrance alteration noted by the 2000 City report?
- Can assessor cards, permits, deeds, or a 1935 newspaper construction notice independently confirm the exact construction year?
