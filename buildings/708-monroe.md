## Scope

This is an **address/premises research-control record**, created because a February 1946 Oregon City municipal claim explicitly identifies **708 Monroe** as the City's recreation-department rental. It is outside the 501/503/505 Main frontage and must not be used to merge the C. P. Farr municipal-rent thread with Farr's Pool Hall or Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main.

## Evidence

- `S-273` / `E-247` — 6 March 1946 Commission claims text for February: **C. P. Farr — Rent at 708 Monroe - Rec. Dept. — $150.00**. **OCR/TEXT ONLY** pending scan certification.
- `S-276` / `E-249` — 2017 City permit control places **919 7th** and **709 Monroe** on the same APN, strongly favoring 708 as a separate premises from the Farr Store parcel.
- `S-275` — official City Farr Store history is context for Clarence Farr only; it does not identify 708.
