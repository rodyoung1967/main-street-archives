## What Is Established

The Clackamas County Historical Society catalog record for object **2008.008.004.169** identifies the photographed storefront as **516 Main Street, Oregon City**, in **December 1919**, during a snowfall described as **22 inches**. The photograph visibly shows a storefront fascia reading **B. J. Staats Hardw…**, supporting identification as B. J. Staats Hardware.

The image also visibly shows deep snow piled along the street and storefront signs reading **Closing Out Sale** and **Big Sale**. Those signs document a sale message at the photographed moment; they do not, without another record, prove the business's closure date, sale completion, ownership transfer, or reason for the sale.

## Limits

- The street number and month/year come from the institutional catalog metadata, not a visible address number in the photograph.
- The exact exposure day is not supplied.
- The catalog record does not establish building ownership, construction date, or whether the photographed fabric survives.
- The full hardware wording is partly cropped/obscured, although **B. J. Staats Hardw…** is visually legible.
- Image rights were not supplied with the user-provided watermarked access copy; no public-domain conclusion is made.

## November 1919 newspaper-address conflict
<!-- 1919 November-December annual online closeout — 2026-09-08 -->

The institutional **516 Main** catalog address for the December Staats photograph now has a direct contemporary conflict: the visually verified **28 November 1919 Enterprise p8** advertisement (`S-304` / `E-278`) prints **B. J. Staats Hardware Co., 520–22 Main Street** with selling-out wording. Do not silently replace 516 with 520–22 or vice versa. The photograph itself does not visibly print a street number, so reconciliation requires another contemporary address/property control.
