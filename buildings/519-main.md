# 519 Main Street — Historic Storefront Address

## Archive ID

## What Is Established

Dated directories and advertisements place F. F. Theroux's piano/sewing-machine business at **519 Main** in 1915–1917. The address later changed occupants: by 20 July 1922 the Pantorium was at 519, and Oregon City Laundry took a temporary post-fire office with it there.

## Limits

The records do not establish uninterrupted occupancy, exact move dates, property ownership, construction history, or that the same physical storefront survived across the entire chronology. The laundry arrangement was a temporary office, not proof of a production plant at 519.
