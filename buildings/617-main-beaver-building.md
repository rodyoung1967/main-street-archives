# 617 Main Street — Beaver Building Address

## Archive ID

- Building ID: `B-013`
- Related businesses: `BUS-031`, `BUS-037`, `BUS-040`
- Related evidence: `E-109`, `E-116`, `E-119`, `E-120`, `E-227`
- Related sources: `S-121`, `S-130`, `S-133`, `S-134`, `S-253`

## What Is Established

The Electric Store / Portland Railway Light & Power Co. is directly advertised at **617 Main, Beaver Building**, on 25 November 1915 and on selected dates in 1916. Wm. A. Schilling also advertised an optometry practice at 617 before announcing a move to 612. By **15 March 1917**, Jones Drug Company directly advertised at **617 Main**; the exact-number advertisement recurs on **22 March**, with another exact 617 advertisement on 1 November. The 29 March Jones Drug advertisement says only Main Street and is not used to infer the number.

## Limits

The shared number does not prove a single-room succession, ownership, lease terms, or exact move dates. The Electric Store moved to 619 Main in the Andresen Building by July 1916; a retained 617 footer in one notice is preserved as an internal source conflict rather than used to erase the reported move.
