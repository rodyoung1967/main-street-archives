# 526 Main Street — Historic Storefront Address

## Archive ID

- Building ID: `B-012`
- Related business: `BUS-032`
- Related evidence: `E-109`
- Related source: `S-121`

## What Is Established

A visually reviewed 25 November 1915 advertisement directly places **Eddy's Dependable Store at 526 Main**.

## Limits

The advertisement does not name a proprietor, establish a move or title interest, date the building, or connect the store to the separate Watson B. Eddy & Son record without an independent identity/succession bridge.
