# 107 Fifth Street — Historic Blacksmith Address

## Archive ID

- Building ID: `B-007`
- Related evidence: `E-117`
- Related source: `S-131`

## What Is Established

The visually reviewed 1914–1915 Oregon City water-rent ledger directly records **107 Fifth**, original **Block 4**, with the classification **Blacksmith**.

## Limits

The tenant handwriting is not clear enough for a responsible name transcription. The record is a dated municipal address/use anchor; it does not prove a modern parcel, construction date, later continuity, or identity with Scripture & May, S. F. Scripture, or another named blacksmith.
