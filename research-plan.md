# Research Plan

This plan turns the archive's open questions into practical research tasks. Every new finding should be entered into the Evidence Register, Source Register, relevant building/business/person pages, and structured database files when appropriate.

## Priorities

1. Complete annual online reviews against `registers/year-status.md`, beginning with the active 1915 pass.
2. Resolve 503 and 505 construction/footprint dates and separate address continuity from surviving-building continuity.
3. Reconstruct property ownership separately from business ownership/operation at 503 and 505.
4. Recover high-value restricted/manual sources: the 1905–1906 and 1916–1917 directories, 1906 numbering plat, OHS title abstract, and logged newspaper gaps.
5. Complete The Wheel / Brass Rail / Dug Inn / Sports Keg / 505 Tavern / Thirsty Duck chronology and licensing.
6. Obtain primary records for the 1968 Harding replacement and resolve the unverified Dr. Bailey claim.

## City Directories and Telephone Books

Purpose: establish year-by-year occupants, business names, addresses, and people.

Tasks:

- Search Polk directories for 503 Main, 505 Main, and 507 Main.
- Search Oregon City telephone books for telephone 6106.
- Track entries for Kwality Cafe, New Kwality Cafe, Larry's Kwality Cafe, The Wheel, 505 Tavern, Harding Drug Store, Tony's Restaurant, and any bakery tenant.
- Track people: J. Jager, Larry, George A. Harding, Dr. Bailey, Ray Hansen, Raye Grisham, Floyd LaFarlette, Dick, Sheila, Willie, Victor, Mitch Young, Mike Berman, and Scott Forvilly.

- Determine Tony's Restaurant's role and the exact storefront occupied by Dixon's Bakery at the time of the fire.

Related lead: `RL-004`

## Evidence Handling Rules

- Do not delete conflicting evidence.
- Do not renumber existing IDs.
- Record confidence for every evidence item.
- Preserve source URLs and local file paths.
- Distinguish primary documents, artifacts, direct testimony, family oral history, secondary sources, and community recollections.
- Update related pages whenever a new finding is added.
