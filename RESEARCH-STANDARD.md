# Research Standard

## Newspaper review requirement

For every year researched in the 503/505 Main Street archive, the required standard is a **page-by-page visual review of every available page of every relevant newspaper issue**.

A year is **not complete** until that visual review has been performed for every available page in every relevant newspaper title for that year.

### What counts

A page counts as reviewed only when the **actual scanned newspaper page image** (including PDF/JP2/image representation of the original page) has been visually inspected.

### What does not count

The following are discovery aids only and do **not** satisfy the review requirement by themselves:

- OCR text
- keyword search
- archive index/search results
- snippets
- metadata
- text extraction
- search-engine results

OCR may be used to locate likely material or to help decipher difficult print, but it must never substitute for viewing the original scanned page.

### Coverage gaps

If an issue or page exists but the scan cannot be retrieved or viewed, record it explicitly as a **retrieval gap**. A retrieval gap is not negative evidence and prevents that year from being certified complete unless no visual copy is available after reasonable recovery attempts.

### Audit states

- **VISUALLY VERIFIED** — actual scanned page inspected.
- **OCR/TEXT ONLY** — OCR or extracted text reviewed, but scan still requires visual inspection.
- **RETRIEVAL GAP** — known page/issue could not be visually retrieved.
- **PENDING** — not yet reviewed.

### Certification rule

Do not describe a year as complete or exhaustively reviewed unless the visual page-by-page standard above has been satisfied and any unavoidable gaps are explicitly documented.
