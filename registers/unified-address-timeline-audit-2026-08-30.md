Status: completed repository reconciliation of the three address-centered timelines. This is not an annual `ONLINE COMPLETE` certification and adds no negative occupancy inference.

## Scope and method

The audit compared [`501 Main`](../timelines/501-main.md), [`503 Main`](../timelines/503-main.md), and [`505 Main`](../timelines/505-main.md) against the building pages, canonical business and people records, evidence/source registers, address and business timelines, Sanborn/aerial findings, property research, and the master timeline.

The unified files include registered address-specific occupants and uses, material physical changes, business-name/operator transitions, and shared events needed to explain the three premises. Generic annual page-review checkpoints and records that only document bounded search coverage remain in the master timeline and research registers. An undated artifact or oral-history event is not assigned a guessed year merely to make the sequence look continuous.
