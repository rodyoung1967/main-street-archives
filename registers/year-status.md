## Shared manual-work callouts

These callouts do not replace year-specific closeout records. When a year reaches online completion, copy the applicable item into that year’s numbered closeout list and record the result there.

1. **1905–1906 Oregon and Washington business directory.** Access the FamilySearch-restricted volume or another holding; inspect Oregon City address/business listings for 501, 503, 505, 507, 509, 511, and relevant named operators. It could not be inspected online without sign-in (`S-073`, `RL-023`).
2. **1906 Oregon City numbering ordinance and filed numbering plat.** Request the ordinance, recorder’s plat, and any implementation records from Oregon City/Clackamas County holdings; determine how the modern Main Street numbers map to pre-1906 descriptions (`E-061`, `RL-025`).
3. **Unretrievable newspaper pages and issues.** Use institutional microfilm, alternate archive derivatives, or holding-library assistance for every precisely logged online gap before manual completion is claimed. Record each issue/date/page result in the applicable annual checklist.
4. **1916–1917 Sohns & Woodbeck directory, Oregon City section.** Inspect the actual Oregon City pages—not the partial Oak Grove/Jennings Lodge scan—through FamilySearch catalog 1844954, Oregon Historical Society, or another holding. Extract the full 500 block of Main and relevant name/business indexes.
5. **Oregon City Courier, 13 December 1917, true page 21.** Recover the correct page from microfilm or an alternate derivative. The online sequence’s page 21 is visibly a 27 December page; 23 genuine pages have been reviewed.

## Handling rules

- Absence of a hit is never treated as evidence that an address was vacant, unused, demolished, or nonexistent.
- `OCR/TEXT ONLY` and search-index coverage do not count as visual review.
- Moving chronologically to a later year does not change an earlier year’s certification level.
- Update this register whenever an annual status changes, and link the full year closeout that supports the change.
