## Purpose

This request supports the unresolved **505 Main Street** business/operator chronology:

**Harr's Recreation Center → Well's Recreation → unnamed beer/pool business offered for sale in November 1955 → later 505 Tavern**.

The original 20 November 1955 *Eugene Register-Guard* classified has been visually inspected and prints the contact telephone number **9179** for the long-established beer-and-pool business offered for sale at **505 Main Street, Oregon City**. The advertisement does not name the business or seller.

Public-online recovery has established that Oregon City had its own Pacific Telephone directory issue, while a surviving September 1955 Portland Pacific Telephone directory explicitly lists several Portland-area exchanges but not Oregon City. No publicly inspectable 1953–1956 Oregon City telephone-directory page has been recovered. The local Wilmer Gardner Research Library is therefore the best first manual holdings/lookup route.

A telephone listing can establish a dated published subscriber-number/address association. It does **not** by itself prove business ownership, building ownership, tenancy, liquor-license ownership, or a sale date.
