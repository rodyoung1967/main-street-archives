## Subject

**Historical license-record survival inquiry — 505 Main Street, Oregon City / Farr and Harr, 1934–1948**

## Ready-to-send body

Hello,

I am researching the business history of 505 Main Street in Oregon City and am trying to determine whether any legacy OLCC licensing records survive for the pool/recreation business at that premises during 1934–1948.

The current Oregon State Archives OLCC retention schedule names record series including **License Files, License Tradename Cards, Licensee History Cards, Applications Received Lists and License Count Lists**. An older OLCC administrative-rule compilation also refers to **microfiche copies of the Commissioner's official files**, county/renewal-district licensee lists, monthly supplements of license actions, and license-nonrenewal lists.

Would you please determine whether OLCC still holds, has imaged/microfilmed, or transferred any legacy records in those series (or predecessor series under different names) for the following search keys:

- Carl R. Harr / Carl Harr
- Harr's Recreation Center
- Edwin F. Farr / Edwin Franklin Farr / E. F. Farr
- Farr's Pool Hall
- 505 Main Street, Oregon City
- 505½ Main Street, Oregon City
- Oregon City / Clackamas County
- date range 1934–1948

I am especially interested in **Licensee History Cards, License Tradename Cards, License Files, historical license-action/nonrenewal lists, and any microfiche or retired card-index records** that could identify applicant/licensee, trade name, licensed premises, license class, renewal/transfer/change date, or predecessor/successor licensee.

If OLCC no longer holds records from this period, could you please provide any available **disposition, transfer, accession, microfilm/microfiche, or successor-custodian information** showing whether the older license files/cards were destroyed, transferred to the Oregon State Archives or another repository, or preserved in another legacy medium?

I am not asking for modern licensing data or a broad statewide search; the request is limited to the names/premises above and 1934–1948.

Thank you,

Rodney Young

## Evidence / handling note

A response that OLCC does not currently hold these records is a **survival/custody finding**, not evidence that Farr or Harr lacked a license. Preserve any stated destruction authority, transfer date, accession number, microform reference, predecessor-series name or successor custodian exactly.
