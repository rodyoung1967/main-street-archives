# MF-057 — 505 Main 1953–1956 operator-transition record requests

Prepared: **7 September 2026**  
Status: **READY TO SEND — NOT SENT / NO FEES AUTHORIZED**  
Scope: **505 Main business/operator transition only**  
Do not combine with the separate 503 Main / Wheel stream.

Purpose: recover primary or near-primary records capable of identifying the operator behind **Well's Recreation**, the seller/buyer around the **20 November 1955** 505 Main beer/pool business advertisement, or a post-sale 1955–1956 liquor-license successor.

The two requests below are independent record-series routes. A result from one does not substitute for the other:

- City Commission proceedings can establish a municipal liquor/beer applicant, licensee, premises, action date and action type when printed.
- An assumed-business-name record can establish the registered assumed name, owner/registrant and filing information when printed.

Neither should be converted into building ownership, tenancy, sale completion, or another relationship the record does not state.

## Request A — Oregon City Public Library Reference Department

**Recipient:** Oregon City Public Library Reference Department — use the Library's official **Email Reference** route or Reference Desk contact.  
**Phone control:** 503-657-8269, ext. 1017 is listed on the Library's Microfilm Scanning System page for questions about specific publication dates/material.  
**Subject:** Historical Oregon City Commission proceedings index lookup — 505 Main, late 1955–1956

### Draft

Hello,

I am researching the business history of **505 Main Street in Oregon City** and am trying to identify the operator or liquor-license successor around a documented business sale offering in late 1955.

The Oregon City Public Library's Microfilm Scanning System page lists an **“Oregon City Council and Commission Proceeding — Index: August 6, 1850 to June 26, 1978.”** The City's older electronic Commission archive is currently difficult to search comprehensively during the records-system transition, so I am hoping the Library's proceedings index can identify the exact meeting date/page for the relevant licensing action.

Would you please check the Council/Commission proceedings index for the period **November 1, 1955 through December 31, 1956** for entries involving any of the following:

- **505 Main** or **505 Main Street**;
- **Well's Recreation** / Wells Recreation;
- Well / Wells / Welle / Welsh variants, only as separate search variants;
- **Carl R. Harr** / Carl Harr;
- **Harr's Recreation Center**;
- pool hall / poolroom / billiards / recreation / tavern;
- liquor license / beer license;
- application / renewal / transfer / substitution / change of ownership / approval.

The immediate historical question is whether a new liquor/beer applicant or licensee appears at 505 Main after a **November 20, 1955** newspaper advertisement offered the long-established beer-and-pool business at that address for sale.

If the index identifies a likely meeting or page, I would appreciate either:

1. a scan/image of the relevant original Commission page(s), including the full meeting header/date and the complete liquor-license action; or
2. the exact date, minute-book/page/reel locator so I can request or inspect the original page separately.

If a liquor-license list or batch is involved, please include the **complete applicant list/action**, not only the 505 entry, because the surrounding applicants help establish whether the record is a renewal batch, a supplemental action, or a one-business application.

For context only, Oregon City Commission material from **July 19, 1956** shows that the City could approve a single named business's liquor license during a special meeting. That business was unrelated to 505 Main; I mention it only because a 505 successor action may likewise be indexed as a small standalone matter rather than a year-end renewal list.

Please let me know before doing any work that would involve a fee. **No fee is authorized at this stage.**

Thank you,

Rodney Young

### Integration instructions when material arrives

- Preserve the original scan/page.
- Visually inspect it; OCR/index text is navigation only.
- Record exact meeting date, printed page, applicant/licensee, trade name, premises, license class if printed, and action wording.
- Preserve the complete applicant universe if the entry is part of a batch.
- Do not infer sale completion, business ownership, tenancy or real-estate ownership unless the source states it.
- A no-hit must record the exact index/date range checked and remain a retrieval/coverage result, not evidence that no 505 action occurred.

## Request B — Clackamas County Clerk / Recording

**Recipient:** `recording@clackamas.us`  
**Office:** Clackamas County Clerk — Recording Division, 1710 Red Soils Court, Suite 110, Oregon City, Oregon.  
**Subject:** Historical assumed-business-name register custody question — 1936–1958 / Well's Recreation, Oregon City

### Draft

Hello,

I am researching a historical Oregon City business and am trying to locate the **Clackamas County assumed-business-name register/index for approximately 1936–1958**, especially 1953–1956.

The Oregon State Archives' online **Clackamas County Inventory** describes an **Assumed Business Names Registers** series covering **1928–1964** and says the records can include the assumed business name, business description, filing date, business owner and related filing information.

However, the holdings listed online appear to jump from:

- assumed-business-name certificates at the Oregon State Archives for **1928–1935**, to
- **Assumed Business Names [Record], volumes 10–13, 1959–1964**, four reels of microfilm, at the Clackamas County Clerk's Recording public research area.

Could you please tell me whether the intervening **1936–1958 volumes/indexes (apparently the earlier volumes before volume 10), or a predecessor/successor series covering those years, still survive** and where they are held?

If that interval is available for lookup, I am specifically seeking any filing or index entry for:

- **Well's Recreation**;
- Wells Recreation;
- Well Recreation;
- Well / Wells / Welle / Welsh variants, kept separate unless the original record connects them;
- **Harr's Recreation Center**;
- **Carl R. Harr**; and
- **505 Main Street, Oregon City**, if the register/index is searchable by address.

The historical context is that an official Oregon historic-resource survey says the **1953 city directory listed “Well's Recreation” at 505 Main**, but the proprietor is not identified in the material currently available to me. A November 1955 newspaper advertisement later offered an unnamed beer/pool business at 505 Main for sale.

If a relevant assumed-name filing is found, I would appreciate a copy/image of the **complete filing or register page**, including any owner/registrant, filing date, business description, address, cancellation, amendment, withdrawal, successor, or changed-owner notation actually shown by the record.

If the 1936–1958 assumed-name volumes are no longer in the Recording Division, could you please identify whether they were transferred to **Clackamas County Archives/Records Management, the Oregon State Archives, another custodian, another microfilm series, or another record title**? I am not assuming that they were placed in the County's miscellaneous-record series; I am trying first to establish the correct custody and series continuity.

Please advise me of any search/copy fee **before** performing paid work. **No fee is authorized at this stage.**

Thank you,

Rodney Young

### Integration instructions when material arrives

- Preserve the original register/index/filing image and title/front-matter or volume control needed to identify the series.
- Transcribe the business spelling exactly; do not normalize Wells/Well/Welle/Welsh into **Well's Recreation** unless the source does so.
- Record registrant/owner wording, filing date, business description, address, amendments/cancellations and page/volume/reel identifiers.
- Treat an assumed-business-name filing as registration evidence only. It does not automatically prove liquor-license ownership, daily operation, tenancy, building ownership, or completion of a business sale.
- If the custodian reports a no-hit or non-survival, preserve the exact years/volumes/indexes checked and any disposition/transfer information. Do not convert it into negative evidence about the business.
