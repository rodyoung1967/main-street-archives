Purpose: resolve the project-steward family-memory hypothesis that **Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham worked at the Wheel Cafe in Morton, Washington; its owners later owned The Wheel at 503 Main Street, Oregon City; and Raye later bought the Oregon City business from them.** On 6 September Rodney Young's brother **Mitch Young independently recalled** that Morton was **before Coquille**, that Sheila “wasn't of school age,” and, as far as he knew, that the family **came to Morton from Chicago**. Rodney independently remembers the same basic sequence. The requests intentionally seek names and dates rather than assuming either the chronology or the ownership bridge is correct.

Sheila Wiitanen's published obituary reports that she was born **13 June 1944**. The visually rechecked 11 March 1954 *Coquille Valley Sentinel* profile says Sheila was 9 and gives Raye's own contemporary account as returning to Chicago after a 1944 Coos County vacation, selling the Chicago rooming-house business, and moving to Coquille without mentioning Morton. Floyd is independently documented in a southern Coos County business by **7 August 1947**. Accordingly, if the family recollection is substantially correct, the highest-value working Morton search bracket is **13 June 1944 through 7 August 1947**, especially **1945–7 August 1947** because *The Morton Journal* begins in 1945. This remains an **UNRESOLVED FAMILY / CONTEMPORARY-SOURCE CONFLICT**, not a documented Morton residence.

A separate property-control pass also found a public-record-derived but **unverified 1950 build-date lead for the surviving 145 Main building**. Therefore, do not assume that a pre-1950 Wheel Cafe, if found, had to occupy the present building or even the later 145 Main address.
