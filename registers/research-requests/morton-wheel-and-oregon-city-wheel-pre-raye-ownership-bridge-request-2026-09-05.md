## Request A — Washington State Library: Morton Wheel proprietor / LaFarlette lookup

### Recipient

Washington State Library Reference / Ask a Librarian  
Reference email: **askalibrarian@sos.wa.gov**  
Current route: https://www.sos.wa.gov/library/ask-librarian

### Subject

**Specific Morton, Washington lookup — Wheel Cafe proprietor and LaFarlette listings, 1950s–1960s**

### Ready-to-send body

Hello,

I am researching the ownership history of **The Wheel Cafe in Morton, Washington**, later known as **Wheel Cafe & Jubilee Room**, at **145 Main Street**. I am also testing a family recollection involving **Raye / Rae LaFarlette and Floyd LaFarlette**.

Your telephone-book holdings page lists Morton volumes for **1962 and 1964–1976**. Would you please make a specific lookup in the earliest available Morton books for:

- **Wheel Cafe** / **The Wheel Cafe** / **Wheel Cafe & Jubilee Room**;
- **145 Main Street, Morton**;
- any proprietor, owner, manager, or associated personal name printed with that business;
- **Floyd LaFarlette**;
- **Raye LaFarlette / Rae LaFarlette**;
- **Raye Grisham / Rae Grisham**, if present in later volumes.

I am especially interested in the **1962 and 1964–1967** books, including the Wheel's telephone number and any business-heading or proprietor information.

I am also trying to identify the Wheel Cafe's proprietor in the **1950s**. Your public holdings list does not show a Morton telephone book for that decade. Was the Morton exchange included in a broader Centralia, Lewis County, or regional telephone book under another catalog title during approximately **1950–1959**? If so, could the Wheel Cafe / 145 Main listing be checked there as well?

The Library of Congress catalog identifies *The Morton Journal* as a weekly Morton newspaper from **1945–1973** (LCCN `sn88085057`, OCLC `17315663`). If Washington State Library holds the relevant microfilm, I would also appreciate any guidance on the most practical access route for a targeted **1950–1965 Wheel Cafe ownership/new-management search**.

The goal is simply to identify the Morton Wheel's historical proprietor(s) and determine whether the LaFarlette names appear in Morton. A no-hit should be reported as such; I am not assuming the family recollection is correct.

Thank you,

Rodney Young

### Handling note

A directory or newspaper no-hit is a coverage result, not proof that Raye did not work there or that a particular proprietor did not exist. Preserve exact volume/year, listing wording, phone number, address, and any proprietor notation supplied by staff.

## Request B — OLCC: 503 Main / Wheel Cafe licensee chain before Raye

### Recipient

Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission — Public Records  
Email: **OLCC.PublicRecords@olcc.oregon.gov**  
Current route: https://www.oregon.gov/olcc/pages/public-records-request.aspx

### Subject

**Historical licensee/trade-name lookup — Wheel Cafe, 503 Main Street, Oregon City, 1958–1967**

### Ready-to-send body

Hello,

I am researching the ownership/operator history of **The Wheel / Wheel Cafe / Wheel Cafe & Lounge at 503 Main Street, Oregon City** and am trying to identify the business owner or OLCC licensee immediately before **Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham** acquired the business.

Contemporary newspaper advertising now places **Wheel Cafe & Lounge at 503 Main Street by 22 August 1958**, telephone **9106**. Oregon City Commission minutes later place **the Wheel at 503 Main Street on 7 June 1967** in connection with a liquor application, but the public minute text available to me does not identify the individual applicant.

Would you please determine whether OLCC still holds, has imaged/microfilmed, or transferred any legacy licensing records for:

- **The Wheel**
- **Wheel Cafe**
- **Wheel Cafe & Lounge**
- **503 Main Street, Oregon City, Oregon**
- date range **1958–1967**, extending later if necessary to capture the transfer into Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham's ownership

I am especially interested in any surviving **Licensee History Card, License Tradename Card, License File, application, renewal, transfer/change-of-ownership record, county/renewal-district licensee list, microfiche record, or predecessor series** that identifies:

1. the individual/corporate licensee or applicant;
2. the licensed trade name and address;
3. change-of-ownership or transfer dates;
4. predecessor and successor licensees;
5. the first record naming **Raye LaFarlette, Raye Grisham, Rae LaFarlette, or Rae Grisham**, if any.

I am testing, not assuming, a family recollection that the people who owned a **Wheel Cafe in Morton, Washington** may later have owned the Oregon City Wheel before selling it to Raye. For that reason, the actual pre-Raye licensee name is the key fact sought.

If records from this period are no longer held by OLCC, could you please provide any disposition, transfer, accession, microfilm/microfiche, or successor-custodian information for the relevant historical license series?

Thank you,

Rodney Young

### Handling note

Keep liquor-license identity distinct from business ownership, real-estate ownership, tenancy, and trade-name use unless a source explicitly bridges them. A custody/no-record response is a retrieval/survival finding, not proof that the Wheel lacked a license.
