**Recipient:** `recording@clackamas.us`  
**Phone control:** 503-655-8551  
**Office:** Clackamas County Clerk — Recording Division, 1710 Red Soils Court, Suite 110, Oregon City, OR 97045  
**Subject:** Historical assumed-business-name lookup — 505 Tavern / Ray Hansen, Oregon City, 1959–1964

### Draft

Hello,

I am researching the historical business/operator chain at **505 Main Street, Oregon City**, and would like to identify any assumed-business-name filing that may connect **505 Tavern** or its predecessor to **Ray or Raymond Hansen** around the early 1960s.

The Oregon State Archives' Clackamas County Inventory states that the County Clerk Recording Office holds:

- **Assumed Business Names [Record], volumes 10–13, 1959–1964**;
- **four reels of microfilm**.

The same inventory says these records can include the assumed business name, business description, filing date and business owner/registrant.

Would you please check the 1959–1964 assumed-business-name record/index for:

- **505 Tavern**;
- **505 Main** / **505 Main Street**, if address searching is available;
- **Ray Hansen**;
- **Raymond Hansen**;
- surname **Hansen**;
- `Hanson` only as a separate spelling-search variant; and
- any predecessor business name shown at 505 Main in the same relevant record/index sequence.

Historical context: a November 20, 1955 newspaper advertisement offered an unnamed established beer/pool business at 505 Main for sale, and Oregon City Commission minutes directly document the trade name **505 Tavern** by November 11, 1965. Family history places Ray Hansen in the tavern business during approximately this transition period, but I am seeking contemporary documentary confirmation rather than assuming the connection.

If a relevant filing is found, I would appreciate an image/copy of the **complete filing or register page**, including the exact business spelling, registrant/owner wording, filing date, address/business description, volume/page/reel number and any amendment, cancellation, withdrawal, successor or changed-owner notation actually present.

Please advise me before any paid search or copy work. **No fee is authorized at this stage.**

Thank you,

Rodney Young

## Request D — Oregon State Archives: permanent OLCC Commission Meeting Records, 1959–1965

**Recipient:** `Reference.Archives@sos.oregon.gov`  
**Phone control:** 503-373-0701, option 1  
**Subject:** Historical OLCC Commission records lookup — 505 Tavern / 505 Main, Oregon City, 1959–1965

### Draft

Hello,

I am researching the historical operator/licensee chain for the tavern business at **505 Main Street, Oregon City**, and am trying to identify the individual licensee or change-of-ownership action associated with the transition to the trade name **505 Tavern**.

The current Oregon State Archives OLCC records-retention schedule identifies **Commission Meeting Records, 1934–ongoing** — minutes, agendas and exhibits — as a permanent record series transferred to the State Archives after 20 years.

Could you please advise whether the permanent OLCC Commission Meeting Records for approximately **1959 through 1965** are searchable by licensee, trade name, premises/address or meeting date, and whether staff can locate any Commission action involving:

- **505 Tavern**;
- **505 Main** / **505 Main Street, Oregon City**;
- **Ray Hansen**;
- **Raymond Hansen**;
- surname **Hansen**;
- and, as predecessor controls only, any earlier trade name/licensee shown for the same premises.

The documentary bracket is currently:

- November 20, 1955 — an unnamed established beer/pool business at 505 Main was advertised for sale; and
- November 11, 1965 — Oregon City Commission minutes directly list **505 Tavern** among liquor-license renewals.

Family history places Ray Hansen in the tavern-business chain during roughly the early 1960s, but I am specifically seeking the contemporary OLCC applicant/licensee identity and action type rather than assuming that family recollection is documentary proof.

If a relevant Commission record is found, I would appreciate the complete meeting/action material needed to establish:

- meeting date;
- applicant/licensee name;
- trade name;
- premises/address;
- license class if stated;
- whether the action was a new application, renewal, transfer/change of ownership, substitution or another action; and
- the associated agenda/exhibit pages when they identify parties or transaction type more fully than the minutes.

I am also interested in whether any **legacy licensee-history cards, tradename cards, applications, license files or microfilm** from this 1959–1965 premises survive despite the finite retention periods shown for several current operational licensing series. I am asking this as a custody/survival question only and do not assume that those records survive.

Please tell me before performing any work that would incur a fee. **No fee is authorized at this stage.**

Thank you,

Rodney Young

### Integration rules

- Preserve original Commission scan(s), full meeting header/date and exhibits.
- Visually inspect the original images before promoting OCR/index text to certified evidence.
- Record applicant/licensee and action type exactly; do not convert a renewal into a sale or a license transfer into real-estate ownership.
- If the historical license-card/file series does not survive, record the custodian's exact survival/disposition statement as a records gap, not as evidence that Hansen was not involved.
