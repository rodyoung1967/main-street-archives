## Purpose

Use two confirmed GFO Oregon City directory holdings as independent bookends around the unresolved acquisition/ownership history of **The Wheel / Wheel Cafe at 503 Main Street, Oregon City**:

- **TF0763 — Oregon City Directory 1964 — R. L. Polk & Co.**
- **TF0764 — Oregon City Directory 1977**

The 1964 volume is especially valuable as a pre-/early-transition control before the unresolved pre-7-June-1967 Wheel liquor action. The 1977 volume is a strong later control before the unexplained 6-June-1979 Wheel liquor-license application and the later documented Richard M. Wiitanen ownership by August 1985.

A directory association is a published occupancy/operator/contact bracket only. It does **not** by itself prove a business sale, legal ownership, liquor-license status, tenancy, or ownership of the 503 Main real estate.

**Subject:** Research request — 1964 and 1977 Oregon City directories / The Wheel, 503 Main

Hello,

I am researching the business history of **The Wheel / Wheel Cafe at 503 Main Street in Oregon City** and would like to request a focused lookup in two Oregon City directories shown in the GFO holdings/sale inventory:

- **TF0763 — Oregon City Directory 1964**
- **TF0764 — Oregon City Directory 1977**

This is one historical research question: I am trying to identify the published business/operator/contact information for The Wheel at 503 Main on the two sides of an unresolved ownership transition.

For **each volume**, would you please check and, if possible, provide clear scans or photographs of the relevant pages for:

1. the street/address listing for **503 Main Street**;
2. alphabetical/business listings for **The Wheel**, **Wheel Cafe**, **Wheel Cafe & Lounge**, or similar wording;
3. any restaurant, cafe, tavern, cocktail lounge, bar, or related classified listing for the business;
4. alphabetical personal listings for:
   - **Raye LaFarlette / Rae LaFarlette / Raye Grisham**;
   - **Floyd E. LaFarlette / Floyd LaFarlette / La Farlette**;
   - **Richard M. Wiitanen / Richard Wiitanen / Dick Wiitanen**;
   - **Sheila Wiitanen**;
5. any numerical telephone-directory entry corresponding to the business or 503 Main, if the volume contains one;
6. the directory title/publication page sufficient to identify the exact edition.

For the **1964** volume, I am especially trying to identify the person/business associated with The Wheel immediately before the suspected LaFarlette ownership period.

For the **1977** volume, I am especially trying to determine whether the directory associates The Wheel with Raye LaFarlette/Grisham, Richard or Sheila Wiitanen, or another party before a June 1979 liquor-license action.

Please let me know the expected research/copying charge **before beginning any work that would incur a fee**. If this lookup would exceed the minimum research charge, a page-number/availability estimate first would be very helpful.
