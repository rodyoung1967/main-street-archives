# 503 Main 2000 historic survey-form / source-notes request — draft only

Date prepared: 2026-09-15  
Manual follow-up: **MF-068**  
Status: **DRAFT ONLY — NOT SENT**

## Purpose

Retrieve the individual **503 Main Street / Kwality Cafe** form created during the **2000 Oregon City Historic Downtown Re-Survey** and any surviving source notes, worksheets, photocopies, bibliography annotations, or research-file material that support the statement that the property appeared **“in 1926 newspaper ads as the Kwality Restaurant.”**

The official final report states that the project generated **59 Oregon Inventory of Historic Properties Historic Resource Survey Forms**, that copies were available through the **City of Oregon City**, and that the originals were housed at **SHPO in Salem**. The report's selected bibliography also lists **Oregon City Enterprise (daily and weekly newspapers)**, making the source file potentially decisive for identifying the exact 1926 newspaper issue.

## First route — Oregon City Planning

**Subject:** Historic survey form/source-notes request — 503 Main Street / Kwality Cafe (2000 re-survey)

Hello,

I am researching the history of **503 Main Street, Oregon City**, historically associated with **Kwality Cafe / Kwality Restaurant**.

The City's September 2000 *Oregon City Historic Downtown Re-Survey Final Report* states that the project created individual **Oregon Inventory of Historic Properties Historic Resource Survey Forms** and that copies were available through the City. The later City historic-resource narrative for 503 Main states that the building appears **“in 1926 newspaper ads as the Kwality Restaurant.”**

Could you please provide a scan or digital copy of the **individual 503 Main Street / Kwality Cafe survey form created in the 2000 re-survey**, along with any surviving **research notes, source worksheets, newspaper photocopies, bibliography annotations, or attachments** for that property?

My highest-priority question is whether the research file identifies the **newspaper title, exact 1926 date, page, or advertisement** supporting the Kwality Restaurant statement.

I am not asking the City to conduct new historical research; I am trying to locate the existing form and underlying source notes from the 2000 survey.

Thank you,

Rodney Young

## Evidence limits

- A City/SHPO survey form is still a **secondary historic-resource source** unless it reproduces or precisely cites the primary newspaper advertisement.
- If the form gives only “1926 newspaper ads” without issue-level detail, continue under **MF-031** with the Oregon City Public Library microfilm.
- Do not treat the 2000 bibliography's project-wide reference to *Oregon City Enterprise* daily/weekly as proof that the 503 ad came from a specific Enterprise issue.
- Do not send either request without project-steward authorization.
