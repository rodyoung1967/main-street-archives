**Subject:** Historical Oregon City pool-hall license — Barry & McAnulty, November 1922 — premises address

Hello City Recorder Team,

I am researching the history of Oregon City's pool halls and restaurants in 1922, including the buildings at 503 and 505 Main Street. A contemporary *Banner-Courier* newspaper notice published **November 30, 1922**, page 1, under the heading **“Granted Pool Hall License,”** reports that **Barry and McAnulty** were granted a pool-hall license for a room they had previously operated as a restaurant. The newspaper does not provide the room's address.

Could you help locate the underlying **November 1922 pool-hall application, license, approval, Council/Commission minute entry, license ledger, bond, receipt, or other related municipal record** for **Barry & McAnulty / McAnulty & Barry**, ideally covering **October 1–December 31, 1922**? I would particularly appreciate the **exact premises street number or room description**, licensee's full name(s), business name, date of approval, fee/receipt, and any property owner or transfer notation.

An Oregon historic-property inventory later associates the McAnulty family and Barry & McAnulty with commercial activity at the **Petzold Building, 714 Main Street**, but this does **not** establish their 1922 address. I am seeking to determine whether the November 1922 license concerned **714 Main, 505 Main, another address, or a different room**; please do not presume any of these.

If no individual application survives, the **typewritten post-1911 City Council minutes** for the meetings immediately preceding November 30 and any corresponding license/receipt indexes would be very useful. If the records are not held by your office, could you identify their present custodian or surviving microfilm/index series?

The newspaper page can be consulted here:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-11-30/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

I prefer legible digital copies of the complete relevant entries/pages, including surrounding headings, and would appreciate a cost estimate **before** any chargeable research or copying.

Thank you for your assistance,

Rodney Young
