# MF-053 — 1979 Wheel applicant / Raye → Wiitanen transition record requests

Prepared: **7 September 2026**  
Status: **READY TO SEND — NO REQUEST SENT**  
Scope: **The Wheel (`BUS-004`), 503 Main Street, Oregon City only**

## Research question

Identify the applicant/licensee and application type behind the Oregon City Commission's **6 June 1979** approval of a liquor-license application for **The Wheel**, and use independent contemporary records to bracket when the Wheel business passed from **Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham** to **Richard (Dick) and Sheila Wiitanen**.

The controlling evidence boundary is strict: a liquor-license action is not automatically a business sale, business ownership, tenancy, or real-estate transfer. The Wheel was only at **503 Main Street** and must not be merged with the separate 505 Main tavern chain.

## Why manual retrieval is now justified

Reasonable public-online recovery has been attempted through Oregon City's WebDrawer/Meeting Document Archive, Oregon Secretary of State / State Archives indexing, Historic Oregon Newspapers, regional digitized papers, general web indexing, and Oregon's business-registry routes.

What is known:

- Oregon City Commission bound minutes, WebDrawer record **4746159**, bound page **195**, directly record approval of **the liquor license application for The Wheel** at the regular meeting of **6 June 1979**.
- The minute does **not** name the applicant/licensee or state whether the action was a renewal, ownership/licensee change, new outlet, or another category.
- **1979 ORS 471.290(1)** expressly used an application for either a license **or renewal**, so the generic word `application` cannot be converted into a transfer claim.
- Oregon City's current archive explicitly catalogs **1970–1979 City Commission Agenda Packets and Minutes**, but the specific 6 June 1979 packet has not been retrievable through the public route.
- Oregon State Archives' current OLCC retention schedule identifies **Commission Meeting Records, 1934–ongoing** — minutes, agendas, and exhibits — as **permanent** and transferred to the Archives after 20 years.
- Oregon City Public Library confirms microfilm of the **Oregon City Enterprise Courier, 1 March 1950–8 April 1992**. The 6 June 1979 Commission minutes state that the meeting agenda had been delivered to the *Enterprise-Courier* and published on 6 June.
- The 1979 and 1980 Oregon City/Gladstone/West Linn directories survive; University of Idaho Special Collections independently lists an **Oregon City, Oregon — 1980** directory.

No applicant identity or application category has been recovered online. This is a **retrieval gap, not negative evidence**.
