## Purpose

This request supports the unresolved ownership/operator chronology of **The Wheel / Wheel Cafe at 503 Main Street, Oregon City**. It is separate from the 505 Main / Ray Hansen directory work even though the same GFO holdings were first identified during that research.

GFO's inventory confirms:

- **TF0763 — Oregon City Directory 1964 — R. L. Polk & Co.**
- **TF0764 — Oregon City Directory 1977**

These are unusually useful bookends for the Wheel investigation:

- **1964** is before the project's leading c.1967 Raye/Floyd LaFarlette acquisition hypothesis and may identify the pre-Raye business listing, proprietor, telephone number, or address association;
- **1977** is after the suspected Raye acquisition but before the unexplained **6 June 1979** Wheel liquor-license application and may identify Raye/LaFarlette/Grisham or another operator immediately before the later transition question.

A directory entry is evidence of a published business/residential/address/telephone association only. It does **not** by itself prove legal business ownership, a sale, liquor-license status, tenancy, or 503 real-estate ownership.
