# 505 Main — 1983 historic-inventory owner-field request

Date prepared: 5 September 2026

Status: **DRAFT READY / NOT SENT**

Purpose: recover the **original 1983-era Oregon City historic-resource inventory form and associated research file for 505 Main Street** as an independent dated ownership snapshot during the unresolved pre-Wiitanen interval.

## Why this source matters

The repository already establishes that downtown Oregon City resources were surveyed in the **1982–1983 / 1983-era cultural-resource inventory** and later re-surveyed in 2000. Oregon City's current preservation guidance says inventory forms are cumulative rather than replacements for older forms.

A separate original Oregon City Oregon Inventory of Historic Properties form from the same survey tradition explicitly contains an **`OWNER:`** field and cites title/tax research among its sources. That means the original 505 form may directly state the property owner at the time of the survey.

The 505 form itself has not been recovered online. The result must therefore remain a **RETRIEVAL GAP / MANUAL CALLOUT** until the original form or faithful scan is inspected.

## Evidence boundary

An owner named on a 1983 survey form would be strong contemporaneous administrative/historic-survey evidence for a dated ownership snapshot, but it would not by itself prove the legal conveyance into or out of that ownership. The Wiitanen vesting deed remains the preferred legal proof of the acquisition transaction (`MF-034`).
