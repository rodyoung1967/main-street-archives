Subject: **The Wheel / D & S Incorporated historical business-record lookup — Oregon City**

## Purpose

This request supports the 503 Main Street / The Wheel ownership chronology and specifically tests whether Oregon Secretary of State business-registration records can identify:

1. the historical entity **D & S Incorporated** associated in later tertiary material with Richard M. and Sheila Wiitanen;
2. any assumed-business-name filings for **The Wheel / Wheel Cafe / Wheel Cafe & Lounge** at **503 Main Street, Oregon City**;
3. registration or amendment dates that may bracket either the **pre-Raye → Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham** transition or the later **Raye → Richard/Dick and Sheila Wiitanen** business transition.

These records must be treated as business-registration evidence only. A filing date, entity formation date, officer listing, DBA registration, amendment, or dissolution does **not** by itself prove a sale of The Wheel business, a liquor-license transfer, tenancy, or ownership of the 503 Main real estate.

## Official access controls verified 7 September 2026

Oregon Secretary of State Corporation Division states that:

- most business-registration documents filed since 2009 are available online;
- **business registrations before 2009 are stored on microfiche** and may be ordered with the **Request for Copy** form;
- the **Request for Search of Old Stock Entity** is used to search archives for an old entity not found in the Business Registry database;
- the old-stock search form currently states a **$10 prepayment** requirement;
- a plain **Document File** or **Assumed Business Name File** copy request is currently listed at **$5** on the Request for Copy form;
- forms/payments are not accepted by email, but the Corporation Division lists `CorporationDivision.SOS@sos.oregon.gov` for questions/help.

Official routes:

- Ordering copies/certificates: `https://sos.oregon.gov/business/register/Pages/ordering-copies-and-certificates.aspx`
- Ordering-copy help / pre-2009 microfiche statement: `https://sos.oregon.gov/business/register/Pages/ordering-copies-help.aspx`
- Corporation Division help/contact: `https://sos.oregon.gov/business/Pages/corporation-division-help.aspx`
- Request for Search of Old Stock Entity: `https://sos.oregon.gov/business/Documents/copies-forms/search-old-stock-entity.pdf`
- Request for Copy: `https://sos.oregon.gov/business/Documents/copies-forms/301-request-for-copy.pdf`

No fee is authorized by this research note. If staff cannot identify the registry/file route without a paid search, stop and obtain project-steward approval before submitting payment.
