Date prepared: **6 September 2026**

Status: **READY TO SEND — NOT SENT**

Manual callout: **MF-050**

Purpose: test project-steward family recollection that Raye and Floyd LaFarlette owned or operated something called the **Town Club** in the mid-1950s, when their daughter Sheila was about ten. The current documentary candidate is a **Town Club House in South Powers**, which is directly named as a polling place in 1946 and 1948, but no source yet connects that place to the LaFarlettes or proves it was a commercial hospitality business.

The best initial date window is **1 January 1953 through 31 December 1957**, with particular attention to **March 1954 through December 1955**. Sheila was born 13 June 1944; the 11 March 1954 *Coquille Valley Sentinel* profile says she was nine and describes Raye as working at the Coquille Hotel coffee shop while Floyd worked at Coos Bay Lumber Company. If the family recollection is substantially correct, a Town Club acquisition or licensing event after March 1954 is especially important to test.

I would be grateful for a lookup in the relevant index/journal and, if applicable, related County Court orders or filings for **1 January 1953 through 31 December 1957**, with particular attention to **March 1954 through December 1955**.

Could you please check for any entries under the following names or terms?

- Town Club
- Town Club House
- South Powers
- Powers
- Floyd LaFarlette / Floyd La Farlette
- Raye LaFarlette / Rae LaFarlette / Ray LaFarlette
- LaFarlette / La Farlette

I am especially interested in records concerning:

- liquor-license applications or renewals;
- local governing-body approval of Oregon Liquor Control Commission applications;
- new licenses;
- change of ownership or change of licensee;
- transfer or sale of a licensed premises;
- change of trade name;
- restaurant, club, tavern or package-store licensing;
- any entry that gives an address or premises description for a Town Club.

If an index entry is found, could you please provide the **date, journal/order volume and page or filing reference, exact applicant/licensee name, trade name, premises/address if stated, license type, and action taken**, and a scan or copy of the underlying record if available?

If these records are held in a different County office or if another index is the better route, I would appreciate being directed to it.

Thank you for your help.

Rodney Young

## Request 2 — Oregon State Archives / historical OLCC records

Recipient: **Reference.Archives@sos.oregon.gov**

Subject: **1953–1957 OLCC historical license search — Town Club / LaFarlette / Coos County**

Hello,

I am researching a Coos County family-business history and am trying to identify a mid-1950s establishment remembered as the **Town Club**, possibly in or near Powers, Oregon, and determine whether **Floyd and/or Raye LaFarlette** were applicants, licensees, owners or operators.

Could the Oregon State Archives please identify and, if possible, search the surviving **Oregon Liquor Control Commission records for 1953–1957** that would show license applications, renewals, transfers, changes of ownership/licensee, trade-name changes, Commission actions or license indexes for Coos County?

Please check the following names/terms, including spelling variants:

- Town Club
- Town Club House
- South Powers
- Powers
- Floyd LaFarlette / Floyd La Farlette
- Raye LaFarlette / Rae LaFarlette / Ray LaFarlette
- LaFarlette / La Farlette

The highest-value period is **March 1954 through December 1955**, although I would like the search bounded broadly to **1 January 1953 through 31 December 1957** so that an earlier application or later renewal is not missed.

I am particularly interested in records that identify:

- applicant or licensee;
- trade name;
- premises location/address;
- license class/type;
- new application versus renewal;
- ownership or licensee transfer;
- Commission approval, denial or other action;
- local governing-body approval;
- predecessor or successor licensee/trade name.

Two contemporary controls may help locate the correct record series:

1. On **9 December 1954**, the *Coquille Valley Sentinel* reported that Coos County's **166 retail licensees** had been sent 1955 renewal forms and that renewal applications should bear approval of the local governing body.
2. On **7 December 1955**, an Oregon newspaper reported that the Commission had just acted on renewal applications after an investigation involving Coos County enforcement complaints and had rejected **28 renewal applications statewide**. If Commission minutes or renewal-action lists survive for the meeting immediately preceding 7 December 1955, I would particularly like that material checked for Town Club / LaFarlette / Powers references.

For context only, Coos County election notices in 1946 and 1948 identify a **“Town Club House” in South Powers**. This is a candidate location, not an established identity bridge.

If the relevant OLCC records are not held by the Archives, could you please identify the holding agency/series or accession and the best way to request a search or copies?

Thank you for your assistance.

Rodney Young
