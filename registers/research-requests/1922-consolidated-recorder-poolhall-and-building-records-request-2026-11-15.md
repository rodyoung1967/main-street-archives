# 1922 consolidated Oregon City Recorder request — 505 poolrooms and building transition

**Status: READY FOR REVIEW / NOT SENT.** Prepared 15 November 2026. This is the **preferred single City Recorder request**, replacing separate dispatch of the September Leland & Little and November Barry & McAnulty draft messages. Keep the earlier drafts as provenance; do not send all three as duplicate inquiries.

**To:** Oregon City Office of the City Recorder — recorderteam@orcity.org

**Subject:** Historical records request — 1920–1923 poolroom licenses and 1921–1923 construction at 501–505 Main Street

Hello City Recorder Team,

I am researching the history of the buildings and businesses at **501, 503, 505, and 505½ Main Street, Oregon City**, especially the transition around the **July 18, 1922 fire**. I would appreciate assistance locating surviving historical records or identifying the appropriate custodian/series. My questions are divided into two high-priority categories and one secondary cross-check.

**1. Pool/billiard-room licensing and exact premises, 1920–1923 (highest priority).** I am trying to identify who held the poolroom/billiards license for **505 or 505½ Main** immediately before and after the July 1922 fire. Relevant contemporary names and leads include **A. Leland / A. H. Leland; O. H. Leland (identity unconfirmed); Leland & Little; Walter Little; Timms Pool Hall**, and any other licensee explicitly associated with these addresses. A June 1920 report identifies **A. Leland** operating a pool hall at **505½ Main**, while 1922 advertising names **Leland & Little** as a billiards business without proving their street address. Please check available applications, licenses, registers/indexes, bonds, transfer approvals, City Recorder receipt/cash books, and Council minutes, including **February 1, 1922** proceedings about Sunday poolroom opening. Please do not assume that similarly named operators are identical or that the 1922 businesses occupied 505.

**2. Building construction/demolition/rebuilding, 1918–1925 (highest priority; concentrate on 1921–1923).** Could you identify any surviving building permit applications, permit ledgers, inspection records, construction approvals, demolition notices, fire-damage/rebuilding files, occupancy records, street-improvement correspondence, or related Council/Recorder entries for **501, 503 and 505 Main Street**, at **Fifth and Main**? The July 1922 fire reporting indicates that the **pool-hall building adjoining the fire survived**, with roof and water damage; its exact numbered-address identification as 505 is a strong cross-source inference. Please search **separately** for records of (a) repair of that surviving pool-hall building, (b) any subsequent demolition or replacement of the older 505 structure, and (c) construction of the Gates corner building. These may be different projects, with different owners, permit dates, and footprints. An **August 18, 1922 Oregon City Enterprise p.5** article describes a new two-story hollow-tile building on **C. E. Gates's property** at the corner of Fifth and Main, built by **R. A. Junken**, with an expected completion around October 1. We are trying to determine the **exact parcel/street frontage, start date, actual completion/first occupancy date, and whether any separate record concerns the later replacement of the older 505 building**. We are *not* assuming the Gates construction report proves 505 was rebuilt. Names for searching include **C. E. Gates / C. Gates; S. Wolf; George A. Harding; R. A. Junken; Alex Schieremann/Schiermann**. If the Recorder does not hold historical building permits or assessments, please identify the custodian or successor series.

**3. Barry & McAnulty November 1922 license (secondary, brief cross-check).** The **November 30, 1922 Banner-Courier p.1** reports that **Barry and McAnulty** were **granted** a pool-hall license in a room previously used as their restaurant. The notice gives no address. Later historical material associates the family/partnership with **714 Main**, but this does not establish their 1922 premises. If a November 1922 license entry or Council minute is readily identifiable, please include its **exact street address/room description** so we can determine whether it was 714, 505, or elsewhere.

For each relevant item, the most useful details are the **record series/title, date, volume/page or document/license/permit number, exact premises address and legal description if present, applicant/owner/operator, approval and occupancy dates, and any related record references**. Legible scans of the relevant full pages and their headings are preferred. If the records are not online or have been transferred, please identify the **holding institution, index, microfilm, or retention/disposition information**. I understand the City has been moving historical records into an electronic document system, so guidance on a searchable public archive is also welcome.

I would appreciate a **fee estimate before any chargeable research or copying**. Please do not incur charges without my approval.

Primary newspaper references:
- July 1922 fire context: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-07-20/ed-1/seq-1.pdf
- August 18 Gates construction report: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1922-08-18/ed-1/seq-5.pdf
- November 30 Barry & McAnulty license: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-11-30/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

Thank you for your assistance,

Rodney Young
