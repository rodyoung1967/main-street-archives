# MF-013 — focused 1922 Leland & Little / 505 Main poolroom records request

Status: **DRAFT READY / NOT SENT**

Date drafted: 13 September 2026

Purpose: focused follow-up to the broader MF-013 request, incorporating the project's newly developed Leland & Little chronology. This draft is intended to make the exact-address question easy for Oregon City Recorder staff to understand without asserting that Leland & Little occupied 505 Main as a proven fact.

**To:** Oregon City Office of the City Recorder — recorderteam@orcity.org  
**Subject:** Historical records request — 1922 Oregon City poolroom license / Leland & Little / 505 Main

Hello City Recorder Team,

I am researching the history of the pool and billiard businesses in the 500 block of Main Street in Oregon City, particularly the premises at **505 and 505½ Main Street** during 1922.

I am trying to identify the exact location and licensed operator of a pool/billiard room that was operating in this area around the time of the **July 18, 1922 Fifth and Main Street fire**.

Contemporary newspaper research has established several useful clues, but it has not supplied the exact address:

- **Leland & Little** was operating as an Oregon City cigar dealer by February 1922;
- a June 1922 Oregon City business list separately names **O. H. Leland, Walter Little, and Timms Pool Hall**;
- the July 1922 fire reports describe an **unnamed pool hall adjoining the former Alex Schieremann tailoring premises**, with its billiard tables damaged by water and part of its roof burned;
- beginning in August 1922 and continuing into the fall, local business listings identify **Leland & Little as a billiards business**; and
- earlier municipal reporting from June 1920 places **A. Leland operating a pool hall at 505½ Main Street**.

I would therefore appreciate help locating any surviving **1922 Oregon City poolroom applications, licenses, license registers/books, bonds, petitions, transfer consents, City Recorder receipt/cash entries, Council minutes, or related files** that could identify the particular premises and operator.

Please search, if practical, for:

- **Leland & Little / Leland and Little**;
- **O. H. Leland / A. Leland**;
- **Walter Little / W. L. Little**;
- **Timms / Timms Pool Hall**;
- **505 Main Street**;
- **505½ Main Street**; and
- other pool/billiard-room applicants in the 500 block of Main Street during 1922.

For any relevant record, the fields most useful to this research are the applicant/licensee name, trade or business name, **exact premises/address or room description**, property owner/landlord if stated, application date, license number and term, fee or receipt number, bond/surety information, approval or denial date, transfer/change-of-operator notation, and any Council minute or ordinance reference.

I would especially like to determine whether **Leland & Little, either partner individually, or Timms Pool Hall can be documented at 505 or 505½ Main Street before or after the July 1922 fire**. I am not assuming that any of them occupied that address; that is the question I am trying to resolve from the original municipal records.

If the original applications or license files no longer survive, a scan or transcription of any **license-register/index entry and the corresponding Council minute page** would still be extremely helpful. If the records were transferred, microfilmed, or are held by another repository, I would appreciate any series title, reel/volume number, accession, or successor-custodian information that would help locate them.

Electronic scans of the complete relevant pages are preferred if available. If staff research or copying would involve a fee, please let me know the estimated amount before incurring charges.

Thank you very much for your help,

Rodney Young

## Research handling note

This request is intentionally phrased as a test rather than a conclusion. Current project evidence supports a strong hypothesis connecting 1920 A. Leland / 505½, 1922 Leland & Little billiards activity, and the later 505 Main billiards/cigars use, but no recovered contemporary source yet directly states `Leland & Little = 505 Main` or `505½ Main`. A response must therefore be visually reviewed and integrated under the normal evidence standard before any exact-address claim is promoted.

This draft supplements the broader MF-013 text in `registers/research-requests/active-manual-outreach-drafts-2026-09-05.md`; it does not mark MF-013 sent or complete.
