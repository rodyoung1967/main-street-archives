## Current research focus

## Key recent findings already committed

- F. D. Cox was granted an Oregon City pool/billiard license on **2 Oct 1912**, but the council entry does not print his premises.
- A 1910 newspaper source places **Asay & Cox, The Pool Hall, at 708 Main Street**. Identity of the Cox partner with Frank D. Cox remains unproven.
- By **23 Dec 1910**, G. P. Miller was applying for a liquor license at **708 Main**, showing the Asay & Cox phase there was short-lived.
- Frank Cox is securely in Oregon City by Aug 1912 and is independently confirmed by Oregon City historic-resource research as having operated a **downtown billiards room and cigar store**.
- H. H. Smith applied in Mar 1912 for a pool-hall license at **503 Main**. No Smith→Cox transfer has been found.
- L. Hylton is directly documented at **503 Main** in Feb and Apr 1914.
- Gross & Ruconich were granted a pool-hall license in Mar 1914 while Hylton was still at 503; therefore Gross's hall was apparently concurrent and should not be treated as Hylton's successor at 503.
- Gross & Hungate were granted a six-month pool-hall license on 2 Sep 1914; exact location unresolved.
- Cox's pool hall is directly described as on **Main Street** in Jun 1915 and was still operating in Mar 1916; exact number unresolved.
- Raasch & Lamb were a separate 1916 pool/billiards operation near/in the Andresen Building.
- Wade/Oldstein pool/billiards parlor is directly documented at **504 Main** in Dec 1916.
- 1916–1917 Sohns & Woodbeck / Clackamas County & Oregon City directory is a high-priority source. FamilySearch catalog ID: **1844954**. Oregon Historical Society also holds the volume.
- 2002 Oregon City historic-resource methodology explicitly used city directories, including a 1916 Oregon City directory.
