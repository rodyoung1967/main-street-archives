# E-105 — Oregon City Numbering Plat/Plan Still on File, 1913

## Status

**VISUALLY VERIFIED primary municipal-record lead.**

## Claim

The 3 October 1913 *Morning Enterprise* prints a City Recorder notice stating that a **plat and plan showing the proper street number for all property in Oregon City** was on file in the recorder's office. It says proper numbers for houses or buildings could be obtained there and quotes Section 5 of **Ordinance No. 357**, requiring owners or custodians to place the assigned number on buildings.

## Research value and limit

This corroborates the 1906 numbering-plat sequence in `E-061` and gives an exact 1913 retrieval target: Ordinance No. 357 and the recorder-held plat/plan. The page does not reproduce the plan and therefore does not establish any exact original Block 4 lot or building corresponding to 501, 503, or 505 Main.
