# E-061 — Fair Store Opposite Post Office and Preparing to Move, 1903

Type: Primary newspaper advertisements  
Dates: 23 January, 24 April, and 15 May 1903  
Sources: *Oregon City Courier* and *Oregon City Enterprise*

Canonical sources:
- 23 January 1903 Courier, p. 3: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1903-01-23/ed-1/seq-3/
- 24 April 1903 Courier, p. 9: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1903-04-24/ed-1/seq-9/ocr/
- 15 May 1903 Enterprise, p. 8: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1903-05-15/ed-1/seq-8/ocr/

## Claims supported

- On 23 January 1903, **The Fair Store** (William Robison/Robinson spelling varies in OCR) advertised **"Opposite Postoffice."**
- On 24 April 1903, the Fair Store advertised a stock-reduction sale to avoid moving its large stock, tied to the approaching May 1 move.
- On 15 May 1903, the Enterprise still advertised **"The Fair Store Must Move,"** corroborating that the relocation was real and apparently still underway.

## Interpretation

The Fair Store is a strong member of the early post-office business cluster. The recovered 1903 advertisements do **not** state the new destination or a numbered Main Street address.

Later 1905 evidence places the Fair Store in the **Willamette Building**, raising a useful hypothesis that the 1903 move may have been into that building. This remains unproven until a move announcement or directory explicitly identifies the destination.

Confidence: **Very High** for the January 1903 opposite-post-office location and the April-May relocation; **hypothesis only** for the destination.
