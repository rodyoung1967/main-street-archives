# E-057 — Schrader / Main-Street Bakery Background, 1902

Type: Primary newspaper profile  
Date: 3 January 1902  
Source: *Oregon City Courier-Herald*, New Year edition, p. 24  
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063697/1902-01-03/ed-1/seq-24.pdf

## Claim supported

A profile of Herman Schrader says that before buying his then-current bakery about a year earlier, he had worked for nearly eighteen years in the **Main-Street Bakery**.

## Interpretation

This strengthens the existing Schrader/Main Street bakery chronology and shows a Main-Street Bakery tradition extending well before the 1912 advertisements already under study.

It does **not** establish the exact later 5xx address, nor does it prove that the older Main-Street Bakery occupied the same storefront as Schrader's later bakery described as near the post office.

Confidence: **Very High** for the newspaper statement; **unresolved** for exact address continuity.
