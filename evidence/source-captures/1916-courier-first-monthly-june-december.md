## Verified findings and limits

1. **Andresen rearrangement announced, June 1 pp3/5.** Jones Drug advertises a move two doors south into the present PRL&P room. The news item describes seven businesses affected: Burmeister & Andresen would expand into the Jones room; Jones into the PRL&P/Schilling room; PRL&P into Raasch & Lamb's room next to the entrance; Raasch & Lamb into a basement room after council permission. Greaves & McNeil would occupy Barclay Building offices; E. P. Elliott & Sons was seeking a new home. The jeweler's approximate $5,000 expenditure and floor-plan changes are prospective. This does not prove all moves completed on June 1 or assign a pool hall to 503/505.
2. **Schilling's move announcement, June 1 p8.** The ad says he must vacate at the first of June, after five months, and gives a new location opposite at **612 Main**. Its future tense is preserved. Earlier verified ads give **617 Main**; a completion day and legal sale record are still missing.
3. **Electric Store at 619 by July 6 p7.** The explicit move announcement says the store and light/power offices have moved **one door north**, now in the **Andresen Building, 619 Main**. The same ad retains a **Beaver Building / 617 Main** footer. That internal conflict is preserved as apparently stale footer copy, not proof of two operating offices. Subsequent selected ads give Andresen/619: August 3 p7, September 7 p7, October 5 p4, November 2 p8 and December 7 p6. This advances the saved October 19 anchor without asserting the exact move day.
4. **Holman-alone wording by August 3 pp2/7.** June 1 and July 6 still carry joint Holman & Randall ads at Fifth/Main; August 3 and later selected issues name Holman alone. This advances the previously cited August 31 ad date. The already verified April 6 sale-of-interest report (E-116) remains controlling; advertising persistence is not continued partnership ownership. No ad supplies 501, 503 or 505.
5. **Scripture & May at 108 Fifth by September 7 p4.** The exact number is visually clear and repeats October 5 p6, November 2 p7 and December 7 p3. This advances the saved October 5 anchor. It does not backdate 108 to the 1914–15 ledger, resolve probable Scripture & May handwriting at 110 Fifth, map 108 against 109, or prove succession to May & Washburn.
6. **Pruden, not the earlier Cruden reading.** October 5 p5 clearly typesets **Pruden / Optometrist / 612 Main / Successor to Wm. A. Schilling**. November 2 p7 and December 7 p5 repeat the signature-style name, address and successor line. The earlier December 14 OCR-based Cruden interpretation is superseded as the identity basis; **December 14 itself is still unreviewed**. Pruden's given name and the exact transfer date/terms remain open. September 28 remains a separate OCR-only successor lead.
7. **Hawley proposed unit postponed, August 3 p8.** Enlarged original text specifies approximately **$1,000,000**, **east side of Main between Third Street and the alley between Fourth and Fifth**, with a requested vacation of Fourth Street. The second unit was postponed until the existing addition was completed, not abandoned. Do not turn this proposal into completed construction, demolition, a legal parcel boundary or a 503/505 change. The enlarged reading supersedes a discarded preliminary overview reading of west side/$180,000; that preliminary reading was not accessioned as a finding.
8. **Dated neighboring addresses.** June 1 p6 and July 6 p6 visually confirm Bradley **507**, Brightbill **509**, Harding **511** (not OCR 611), Theroux **519**, Price **527**, and Schooley/Wilkinson **612**. Panels confirm Champion Smith & Telford **502** and Streibig **528** on June 1 p5, July 6 p3, August 3 p8, September 7 p8 and October 5 p8. Wilkinson **612** repeats November 2 p6 and December 7 p6; Williams office **612** repeats through December 7 p8. These are dated ads, not proof of uninterrupted operation or a one-business-per-address arrangement.
9. **Other bounded leads.** November 2 p7 prints Brady Mercantile **1110–1112 Main**; November 2 p8 places The Modern at **418 Seventh**, and p10 gives Alta Lamm **709 Seventh**, not Main. December 7 p1 announces plans/site selection for a Moose lodge/store building on Main between Eleventh and Twelfth, not completed construction. December 7 p5 says Theroux returned to reopen his Main Street music store, but gives no number; do not assume the earlier 519 location automatically continues.

**No new exact 503/505 occupant or target-frontage demolition/rebuild is established by this batch.** This is a bounded coverage result, never evidence of vacancy, non-use or absence. The July 6 p7 Block 4 sheriff-sale item is in **West Side Addition**, not automatically original Oregon City Block 4. Unnumbered pool/laundry ads and other-town fires/moves remain separate from the target.
