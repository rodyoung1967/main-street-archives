# 1930 Municipal Activities Report — Fifth/Main coffee-house and 525 Main pool-hall leads

Date: 3 September 2026

Status: **PUBLIC SEARCH-INDEX / OCR TEXT ONLY — ORIGINAL REPORT PAGE NOT VISUALLY VERIFIED.**

Purpose: preserve two newly surfaced exact-location/use leads from Oregon City's 1930 Municipal Activities Report without using them as visually verified occupant evidence. These leads are relevant to the post-1925 reconstruction because they demonstrate nearby food-service and pool-hall uses that must not be conflated with the 503 restaurant or 505 billiards/cigars premises.

## Indexed fire-table wording

The search-index extraction includes apparent fire-department rows for:

- **July 20 — 5th & Main, Coffee House**
- **July 24 — 525 Main, Pool Hall**

The indexed extraction also exposes cause/damage material from surrounding table rows, but column order is not reliable enough to assign a particular cause or loss to either entry without viewing the original page.

## Research consequence

Subject to visual confirmation, these rows would establish two useful neighboring-use anchors in 1930:

1. a **coffee-house use at Fifth & Main**, with no street number or proprietor supplied in the recovered text; and
2. a **pool hall at 525 Main**, demonstrating that a numbered Main Street pool-hall location other than 505 existed nearby by 1930.

The second point is methodologically important even before promotion: it reinforces the project's existing rule that generic `pool hall` / `billiards` references cannot be assigned to 505 solely from business type or proximity.

Neither lead establishes:

- that the Fifth/Main coffee house was at 501 or 503;
- that the 525 Main pool hall had any relationship to Farr's, Timms, Leland & Little, McAnulty & Barry, or the unnamed July 1922 adjoining hall;
- a move from 505 to 525 or vice versa;
- any property ownership or building-continuity event.
