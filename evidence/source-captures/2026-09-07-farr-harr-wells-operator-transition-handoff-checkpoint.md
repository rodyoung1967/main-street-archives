## Assumed-business-name route now at manual threshold

The official Oregon State Archives Clackamas County Inventory describes an **Assumed Business Names Registers** series covering **1928–1964**, with fields that may include assumed business name, business description, filing date, and **business owner**.

The public holdings presentation is discontinuous around the decisive years:

- certificates **1928–1935**; then
- record volumes **10–13, 1959–1964**, four microfilm reels.

Therefore **1936–1958**, including 1953–1956, is a **CUSTODY / ACCESS GAP** in the public inventory presentation. It is not evidence that no filing existed.

A surviving filing for Well's Recreation could identify an owner/registrant and filing date, but an assumed-name filing is registration evidence only; it does not automatically establish liquor-license ownership, daily operation, tenancy, building ownership, or completion of the November 1955 sale.
