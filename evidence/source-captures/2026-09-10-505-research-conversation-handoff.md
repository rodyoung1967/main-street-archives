## Current hard 505 documentary chain

The following are the important direct or carefully bounded anchors for the active mid-century question:

### 1945–1955 predecessor sequence

- **1945** — the visually reviewed SHPO survey says a 1945 city directory listed **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main**. The underlying directory page remains unrecovered.
- **11 Feb. 1948** — municipal material names Harr and McDougald as pool-hall operators, but that sentence does not itself print Harr's first name/business/address.
- **Late 1948** — primary municipal text directly gives **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**.
- **1950 Sanborn** — 505 is mapped as billiards / cigars / beer; use only, not operator identity.
- **1953** — the visually reviewed SHPO survey says a 1953 city directory listed **“Well's Recreation” at 505 Main**. The actual directory page and proprietor remain unresolved.
- **20 Nov. 1955** — visually verified *Eugene Register-Guard* ad offers the long-established beer/pool business at **505 Main Street** for sale and prints contact **9179**. It does **not** identify seller, trade name, proprietor, licensee, or buyer.

### 1965 onward

- **11 Nov. 1965** — Oregon City Commission minutes directly list **505 Tavern** in a liquor-license renewal batch. The readable minute text does **not** name the individual licensee.
- **1 Sep. 1967** — block fire reporting names 505 Tavern separately from The Wheel/503 and says 505 was spared with smoke/water damage.
- **Mar. 1972** — 505 Tavern liquor renewal.
- **Feb. 1976** — 505 Tavern classified, phone **655-4321**.
- **6 Apr. 1988** — primary municipal transfer record identifies **Dug Inn (formerly Brass Rail), 505 Main**, transfer from Wiitanen to the Cotterells.
- **Before Mar. 1991** — participant/family evidence says Dick and Sheila Wiitanen regained control and had renamed the business **505 Tavern** before selling it to Mitch.
- **Mar. 1991–Mar. 2004** — Mitch / Mitchell C. Young directly confirmed he owned/operated already-named **505 Tavern**.
- **May 2000** — individual SHPO survey names Richard and Sheila Wiitanen in the owner field; this is an official ownership snapshot, not a vesting deed.
- **2009** — Mike Berman states he bought 505 Tavern.
- **2017** — Scott Forvilly purchases/reopens as Thirsty Duck; permit evidence distinguishes business from Wiitanen real-estate ownership.
- **Current** — 505 real estate is held through the current family/505 LLC structure; Alin Guria is the lessee/operating-business owner, not the real-estate owner.

## Ray Hansen / 505 Tavern continuation — what this conversation established

### Evidence boundary

Family history places **Ray Hansen** in the earlier 505 Tavern business chain, approximately in the early 1960s and before the later Wiitanen / Brass Rail period.

However, **no contemporary documentary source recovered in this conversation names Ray Hansen at 505 Main or identifies him as the 505 Tavern licensee/proprietor**.

Do not promote the family chronology to a documentary Hansen ownership/license date until an original contemporary record supplies an independent link.

### 1960 Oregon City directory — confirmed high-value source

A 1960 Oregon City directory is now a confirmed source target.

A current genealogy/directory index identifies:
- `Oregon City, Oregon, City Directory, 1960` in the U.S. City Directories collection.

More importantly, an official Oregon City historic-review source independently cites:
- **Polk's Oregon City Directory, 1960 — R. L. Polk & Co.**

This elevates the 1960 volume from a commercial-index hint to a source with official local research provenance.

The actual 505 Main target page was **not** recovered through the public/restricted routes available in the research environment.

Classification: **DOCUMENTED SOURCE / RESTRICTED-DIGITAL OR MANUAL RETRIEVAL GAP**.

When obtained, inspect:
- title/front matter;
- 505 Main street guide;
- 505 Tavern and any predecessor trade name;
- Ray Hansen / Raymond Hansen / R. Hansen;
- Hanson only as a separate spelling-search variant;
- tavern/bar/pool/billiards/recreation classified sections;
- numerical phone section if present;
- the 501–511 odd Main block for address control.

Do not treat a directory association as automatic proof of sale, liquor-license ownership, tenancy, or real-estate ownership.

### Local 1960 directory holding lead

The public catalog did not expose historical issue-level holdings, so it is **not yet proof that MCL has the 1960 issue**.

Use this as a local holdings-confirmation route before paying for a commercial/GFO lookup.

### 1959–1964 assumed-business-name records

The Oregon State Archives Clackamas County inventory explicitly identifies:
- **Assumed Business Names [Record], vols. 10–13, 1959–1964**
- **4 reels of microfilm**
- held at the Clackamas County Clerk Recording Office.

The inventory says the series can include:
- assumed business name;
- business description;
- filing date;
- business owner/registrant.

This is exceptionally well aligned with the remembered c.1963 Hansen transition.

Priority keys:
- 505 Tavern;
- 505 Main / 505 Main Street if searchable by address;
- Ray Hansen;
- Raymond Hansen;
- Hansen;
- Hanson only as a separate variant;
- any predecessor trade name recovered from the 1960 directory or another source.

A filing proves only what it prints. Registration is not automatically liquor licensing, daily operation, tenancy, property ownership, or purchase terms.

### OLCC permanent Commission-record route

The Oregon State Archives retention material identifies **OLCC Commission Meeting Records, 1934–ongoing** as a permanent series including minutes, agendas, and exhibits.

This is the preferred state route for the 1959–1965 505 licensee question because underlying exhibits may identify:
- applicant/licensee;
- trade name;
- premises;
- license class;
- new/renewal/transfer/substitution/change action type.

Older operational license cards/files may have finite retention. Any failure to recover them is a **records-survival/retrieval gap**, not evidence that Hansen was absent.

### Enterprise-Courier 1960–1965

The local *Enterprise-Courier* 1960–1965 period is a **manual/microfilm route** rather than a fully accessible public-online source in the current workflow.

Search for:
- 505 Tavern;
- 505 Main;
- Ray/Raymond Hansen;
- sale/change-of-ownership reporting;
- liquor application/renewal/transfer notices;
- business opening/renaming;
- ads printing proprietor or phone.

Do not launch a blind multi-year page-order request if the directory/assumed-name/OLCC records first produce a name/date that can narrow the search.

Treat this only as **approximate secondary historical context** consistent with an early-1960s tavern culture.

Do **not** convert it into a 1960/1961 opening date or a Ray Hansen identification.
