## Oregon City Laundry / 1916 site-change resolution

Canonical files:

- `evidence/source-captures/1916-02-17-laundry-fifth-street-property-trade.md`
- `evidence/source-captures/oregon-city-laundry-location-chronology.md`

The formerly apparent 1916 location conflict is **resolved** by visually verified primary evidence:

- 3 Feb 1916: laundry planned a replacement plant at the earlier rear-Courier/city-hall-area site;
- **17 Feb 1916: E. M. Blakeslee traded that intended property for a Fifth Street location**;
- 2 Apr 1916: Sunday Oregonian says the new plant on Fifth just off Main was nearing completion;
- by 1918 Oregon City Laundry is directly documented at **109 Fifth**.

Thus the February and April reports describe a **change of site**, not contradictory descriptions of a single fixed site.

Post-fire production resumed quickly in 1922, but the exact late-1922 replacement production location remains unresolved.
