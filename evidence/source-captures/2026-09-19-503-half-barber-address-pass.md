# 503½ Main barber — address/proprietor pass — 2026-09-19

## Question

Identify the proprietor/occupant of the **503½ Main — Barber Shop** row in Oregon City's 1914–1915 Water Rent Record Book 3 and determine whether that shop can be carried forward to the unnamed barber adjoining Miller in the Harding property in July 1922.

## Existing direct municipal anchor

The already-preserved visual review of Water Rent Record Book 3 directly establishes:
- **503 Main — Store**
- **503½ Main — Barber Shop**
- G. A. Harding, Agt. repeats through the target frontage.
- The tenant handwriting at 503 and 503½ remains too faint/layered for a responsible transcription.

Source/capture:
`evidence/source-captures/1914-1915-water-rent-record-book-3-visual-review.md`

The 1916–1917 Book 4 review again separately records **503 Main** and **503½ Main**, with faint tenant writing, and no separately written 505 Main in the expected sequence.

## New primary newspaper control: Myers & Wentworth are not a good 503½ candidate in April 1914

The *Oregon City Courier*, **16 April 1914**, p.4, published a numbered Oregon City business/address list. It directly prints:

> **Myers & Wentworth, 722 Main**

Public scan:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-04-16/ed-1/seq-4/

This is **DIRECT PRIMARY exact-address evidence** for Myers & Wentworth at 722 Main in April 1914.

A separate *Courier* item on **5 February 1914**, p.5, reports that Myers & Wentworth, then occupying the Jaggaar building on Main Street, had leased the south first-floor room of the new Gambrinus Brewing Company building for a modern barber shop expected ready about February 10. The April exact-address list fixes that operation at 722 Main shortly afterward.

Public scan:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-02-05/ed-1/seq-5/

### Consequence

**Myers & Wentworth should be excluded as the default identification of the 503½ barber in the 1914–1915 water ledger**, at least for the April 1914 address snapshot. Their directly printed address is 722 Main, on a different part/side of Main Street.

This is useful because Myers & Wentworth are one of the more visible named Oregon City barber operations in the contemporary newspaper corpus and could otherwise be falsely attracted to the unidentified ledger row.

## 1921 control

The *Oregon City Enterprise*, **23 December 1921**, p.4, reports a disturbance at the **Bennett barber shop**, located in the **Electric Hotel building on Main Street**.

https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1921-12-23/ed-1/seq-4.pdf

This supplies another named late-1921 Oregon City barber shop but does not presently bridge to 503½ or the Harding property.

## Result

The proprietor of **503½ Main** remains unresolved, but the candidate field is cleaner:

- Myers & Wentworth — directly at **722 Main** in Apr 1914; poor 503½ candidate.
- Green's — **7th & Center** in Apr 1915.
- New/Cut Rate shop — **217/267 7th near depot** in 1915; later Geo. Schultz manager.
- Bennett barber shop — **Electric Hotel building, Main Street** in Dec 1921.
- Ed Johnson / O.K. Barber Shop — separate 705 Main historical-marker hypothesis, still awaiting contemporary exact-address bridge.

None should be assigned to **503½ Main** without a direct source.

## Highest-value next move

The unresolved tenant name is physically present in the water ledgers. The best next evidence is therefore not another generic newspaper-name search but a **higher-resolution handwriting comparison of the 503½ tenant line in Book 3 and Book 4**, followed by searches for any plausible reading. If that cannot be resolved, a 1915–1917 or 1921–1922 city/business directory with address indexing is the next strongest route.
