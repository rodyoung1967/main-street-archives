## 4. Raye surname search-key caution for the 1976–1979 interval

The online pass did **not** recover a defensible marriage date for Raye and Cliff Grisham.

An existing 1975 Oregon Supreme Court case caption names **Floyd E. and Raye E. LaFarlette**. That caption is useful only as a dated name-form control; it is **not** proof of marital status or co-residence in 1975.

Floyd LaFarlette is independently known to have died in **1976**. A newspaper-index/search lead points to a Floyd LaFarlette obituary in the **Jonesboro Sun, 10 February 1976, page 20**, but the original page was **not visually inspected in this pass** and the obituary text was not recovered.

Classification of the obituary target: **INDEX / RETRIEVAL LEAD ONLY — ORIGINAL NOT INSPECTED**.

Research consequence: for records in the unresolved **1976–1979** interval, search **both LaFarlette and Grisham** name forms. Do not assume when Raye began using Grisham until a contemporary marriage, directory, newspaper, licensing, or other dated source establishes it.
