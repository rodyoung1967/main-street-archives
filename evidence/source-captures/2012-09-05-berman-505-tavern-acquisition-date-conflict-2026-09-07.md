## Source

**Oregon City Commission Meeting Minutes, 5 September 2012**  
City record: `4238300`  
URL: https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4238300/File/document

The relevant discussion concerns Michael Berman's residency qualification for City Commission candidacy.

## Directly conflicting statements in the same minutes

The minutes first summarize Berman as saying that **"In 2009 he bought the 505 Tavern"** (printed meeting-minutes page 4).

On the following printed page, the minutes say Berman clarified that he was receiving mail at the 505 Tavern, **"which was purchased in June 2010."**

These are two incompatible acquisition dates in the same primary municipal record.

## Evidence handling

**Do not choose 2009 or June 2010 as settled fact from this source alone.**

What the source directly supports:

- Michael Berman personally associated himself with ownership of 505 Tavern by the time of the 5 September 2012 meeting.
- The minutes preserve one statement placing acquisition in **2009**.
- The same minutes preserve a later clarification placing purchase in **June 2010**.

What remains unresolved:

- Whether one date refers to an agreement/possession/business-control event and the other to a formal closing, license, entity, or other transaction event.
- Whether one date is simply a minutes-preparation error.
- The exact legal purchaser/entity and formal closing/license-transfer date.

Evidence state: **UNRESOLVED CONFLICT** pending an independent transaction or licensing record.
