# 1932 Oregon City telephone directory — McLean nomination source conflict

Date reviewed: 4 September 2026

Status: **SOURCE-CONFLICT / ACCESS-SET DISCREPANCY — 1932 DIRECTORY SURVIVAL INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED; NO FARR/505 ENTRY INSPECTED.**

Purpose: prevent a parenthetical statement in the 2024–2025 McLean National Register nomination from being misread as proof that no 1932 Oregon City directory existed. Wilsonville Public Library independently catalogs an August 1932 Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. Oregon City/Clackamas County directory in its Heritage Collection.

## McLean nomination wording

The final Oregon National Register nomination for the **Dr. Edward and Anne McLean House** discusses Dr. McLean's Oregon City office history. Footnote **114** says:

> The address listed for McLean in the 1927-1933 Oregon City directories (and for Dr. Steele in 1933; **no directory in 1932**) is 720 Main Street.

Official Oregon SHPO nomination:

`https://www.oregon.gov/oprd/OH/Documents/SG100011430.pdf`

Earlier in the same nomination, footnote **57** identifies a Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. directory sequence used for McLean residence research:

- **1927:** “Clackamas County”;
- **1928, 1929, 1931, 1933:** “Oregon City and Clackamas County”;
- **1941:** “Oregon City, West Linn, and Gladstone,” with the 1941 McLean listing on p. 112.

The nomination does not explain whether the phrase “no directory in 1932” means no 1932 edition existed, no 1932 edition was available to the researcher, or that a particular directory series/source set skipped 1932.

## Independent 1932 holding contradicts a literal “no directory existed” reading

Wilsonville Public Library's **Northwest Heritage Index** explicitly identifies a physical Heritage Collection source:

> **OR CLAC D-OR.C 1932T — Telephone Directory, Oregon City, Clackamas County, Oregon, August 1932. (Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co., 1932.)**

Official Wilsonville Library index:

`https://www.wilsonvillelibrary.org/sites/default/files/fileattachments/library/page/26561/nw_heritage_index_2023-04-25.pdf`

The index says the directory was indexed only to identify communities covered in addition to Oregon City. It gives page ranges for several surrounding communities, demonstrating that the catalog code refers to an actual multi-community directory volume rather than a generic bibliography entry.

Therefore the nomination's parenthetical **cannot safely be promoted as “no 1932 Oregon City directory existed.”** A 1932 PT&T Oregon City/Clackamas County telephone directory demonstrably survives in a current local-library collection.

## Correct interpretation

Current evidence supports only this narrower statement:

- the McLean nomination researcher did not cite/use a 1932 directory in the specific 1927–1933 sequence summarized in footnote 114, or regarded that source sequence as lacking 1932;
- Wilsonville Public Library independently holds an **August 1932 PT&T Oregon City/Clackamas County telephone directory**;
- whether the nomination's 1927–1933 “Oregon City directories” are exactly the same PT&T series listed in footnote 57, a partially overlapping source set, or a different city-directory series is **not resolved by the nomination text alone**.

Do not create a third directory series merely from the shorthand wording “Oregon City directories,” and do not erase the confirmed 1932 PT&T holding because of the parenthetical note.

## Research consequence for 505 Main

The conflict **increases**, rather than decreases, the value of visually inspecting Wilsonville's August 1932 volume. It is a direct contemporary source only months after Oregon City's March 1932 pool-hall licensing action and can test:

- Edwin F. / Edwin Franklin Farr;
- Alice Farr;
- Farr's Pool Hall;
- 505 Main / 505½ Main;
- all Oregon City pool/billiard operators and classifications;
- neighboring 501–511 Main entries.

If the 1932 directory prints a Farr/505 association, it would provide a contemporary published-name/address bridge independent of the later nomination's source-set limitations. It still would not by itself prove municipal licensing or legal ownership.

## Evidence boundary

No 1932 Farr, Harr, 505 or pool-hall listing has been inspected or promoted. This capture resolves only how to treat a **source-availability conflict**.
