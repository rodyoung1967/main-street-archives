# 1930–1939 Clackamas County telephone directories — West Linn 1988 survey provenance route

Date reviewed: 4 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL HISTORIC-SURVEY SOURCE-PROVENANCE LEAD + CURRENT INSTITUTIONAL FOLLOW-UP ROUTE. PRESENT HOLDING LOCATION UNRESOLVED; DIRECTORY CONTENTS NOT INSPECTED; NO FARR/505 CLAIM PROMOTED.**

Purpose: strengthen the holding-location search for the **1930, 1931, 1935 and 1939 Clackamas County telephone directories** already identified in the 1932 Oregon City telephone-directory route. This finding does not establish where the volumes are held today. Its value is that it identifies a specific historic-resource research project that actually used this multi-year source set and a current City office that may retain the consultant research files, photocopies, notes or provenance information.

## 1988 West Linn historic-resource project

An official City of West Linn historic-resource document identifies the project as:

> **HISTORIC RESOURCE UPDATE — CITY OF WEST LINN, OREGON — by Koler/Morrison Preservation and Planning Consultants — Prepared for The City of West Linn — 1988**

Official City source:

`https://westlinnoregon.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/planning/page/6488/1988_national_register_nomination_for_the_lewthwaitte-moffat_house.pdf`

A separate official West Linn record describes the same project as conducted **March through June 1988** by **Koler/Morrison Historic Preservation and Planning Consultants** under contract with the City. Its methodology identifies **Julie Koler, Jane Morrison and Dana Koler** as the consulting team and says the work included basic historic research on inventoried properties.

Official methodology route:

`https://westlinnoregon.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/planning/project/9173/supplemental_record_part_d.pdf`

## Telephone-directory source set used by the survey

Resource forms within the 1988 West Linn historic-resource material cite:

> **Clackamas Co. Telephone Directory, 1930, 1931, 1935, 1939.**

This is materially stronger than a generic modern catalog title: it shows that preservation researchers working on the 1988 City project had access to this exact four-year Clackamas County telephone-directory sequence as working historical sources.

The current web environment can recover the City PDF/search-extracted survey text but cannot reliably render the approximately 80 MB source document page-by-page. The exact individual resource-form page carrying the citation therefore still needs scan-level certification before the citation is treated as visually reverified in this pass. The repository's earlier telephone-directory capture already preserves this source sequence as a bibliographic lead.

### Evidence boundary

The 1988 citation does **not** establish:

- that the City of West Linn owns the directories today;
- that Koler/Morrison owned them;
- whether the researchers used originals, library copies, microfilm or photocopies;
- whether all four volumes contain Oregon City listings in the same format;
- any Edwin Farr, Alice Farr, Carl Harr, 505 Main or pool-hall listing.

Current state: **DOCUMENTED USE IN 1988 / PRESENT SOURCE LOCATION UNRESOLVED.**

## Exact follow-up request

Ask West Linn Planning whether the City retains any of the following from the **1988 Koler/Morrison Historic Resource Update**:

1. consultant research files;
2. source-note files or bibliographies with repository/call-number information;
3. photocopies or scans of the cited **Clackamas Co. Telephone Directory** editions for **1930, 1931, 1935 and 1939**;
4. the original directory volumes themselves;
5. records identifying the library, archive or private collection from which the consultants obtained them.

If any directory pages survive, request the **title page/publication information plus the complete relevant Oregon City coverage**, using a fixed target set across all years:

- Edwin F. Farr / Edwin Franklin Farr / E. F. Farr;
- Alice Farr;
- Farr's Pool Hall;
- Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center;
- Harris E. McDougald / Pastime Pool Hall;
- 505 Main / 505½ Main;
- neighboring 501–511 Main entries;
- pool hall / pool room / billiards / recreation / cigars / tobacco / soft drinks / tavern classifications;
- all Oregon City establishments/operators appearing under the same pool/billiard classification.

Record exact printed wording, directory year/effective date, page, business/subscriber name, address and telephone number. Keep residential and business entries separate.

## Research consequence

If this route locates the 1930/1931/1935/1939 volumes or photocopies, it creates a four-point published-name/address bracket around the separately confirmed **August 1932 Oregon City telephone directory** held in the Wilsonville Public Library Heritage Collection.

That sequence could materially narrow when Farr becomes publicly associated with a pool-hall/address listing, but it still would not by itself prove municipal licensing, legal ownership or uninterrupted occupancy. Any resulting directory claim must be based on the actual page image and synchronized with the existing 1932 access capture and Farr/505 chronology.
