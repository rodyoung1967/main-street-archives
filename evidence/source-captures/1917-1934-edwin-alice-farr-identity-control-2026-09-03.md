# 1917–1934 Edwin / Alice Farr identity-control leads

Date checked: 3 September 2026

Status: **MIXED PRIMARY OCR/TEXT-ONLY IDENTITY LEADS + SECONDARY CONFLICT — DO NOT MERGE OR PROMOTE WITHOUT VISUAL / VITAL-RECORD CONFIRMATION.**

Purpose: preserve identity evidence relevant to the later official statement that **Edwin F. and Alice Farr** were proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main Street**, while preventing a name-only merger or an incorrect chronology.

## 1917 Edwin Franklin Farr — Oregon City draft-list lead

A 19 July 1917 *Oregon City Courier* draft-number page contains a section headed **OREGON CITY NO. 1** and lists:

- `1643, Edwin Franklin Farr`

The accessible PDF text layer clearly exposes both the **OREGON CITY NO. 1** heading and **Edwin Franklin Farr** entry. A page-image screenshot request failed with a cache-miss in the current research environment, so this remains **PRIMARY NEWSPAPER OCR/TEXT ONLY** rather than visually certified evidence.

### Interpretation boundary

This is a useful candidate expansion of the later City's **Edwin F. Farr**, but it is **not yet a proved identity bridge** to `P-028` or Farr's Pool Hall. The name, middle initial and Oregon City context fit, but no recovered 1917 source connects Edwin Franklin Farr to 505 Main, a pool hall, or Alice Farr.

A separate February 1918 draft-classification OCR result prints **Edwin Franklin Farr, Molalla, Ore.** This may concern the same man, but OCR/layout and wartime address context require visual review before using it as a location chronology.

## Alice Farr — local pre-1934 name anchor

A 28 February / 1 March 1918 Oregon City newspaper obituary for a member of the Sprague family names surviving daughters **Mrs. Bertha Cordill and Mrs. Alice Farr**, both associated with Molalla. This confirms that an **Alice Farr** with Sprague-family context existed locally by 1918, but the article does not identify Alice's husband.

Historic Oregon Newspapers routes:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-02-28/ed-1/seq-7/
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1918-03-01/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

A 28 December 1922 *Banner-Courier* page also contains the name **Alice Farr** in a long monetary list. That occurrence does not identify the person further and is not used as a business or address bridge.

## Important conflict with the 2002 City historic-resource narrative

Oregon City's 2002 historic inventory for **401 Third Street** states that **Edwin F. and Alice Farr**, proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main Street**, acquired the residence in **1934** and says the couple continued to live there until their respective deaths in the late 1960s.

Official City page:
https://www.orcity.org/2014/401-3rd-Street

Official inventory PDF:
https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/7826/401_3rd

A secondary cemetery aggregator, PeopleLegacy, separately reports:

- **Edwin F. Farr — 1890–1970**, birthplace Oregon City, burial Logan Pleasant View Cemetery;
- **Alice Clarinda Sprague Farr — 1893–1934**, burial Logan Pleasant View Cemetery.

Secondary routes:
https://peoplelegacy.com/burial-records/edwin/farr/
https://peoplelegacy.com/burial-records/alice/farr/

If the secondary Alice Clarinda Sprague Farr is the same Alice Farr named in the City survey, the reported **1934 death year conflicts directly** with the City's statement that Alice continued living at 401 Third until the late 1960s. However, the current evidence does **not** establish that these are the same Alice Farr. Possible explanations include different people, remarriage, a secondary memorial error, or an error/overgeneralization in the 2002 City narrative.

The conflict must therefore remain unresolved.

## What this does NOT establish

This checkpoint does **not** establish that:

- `P-028` Edwin F. Farr is definitively Edwin Franklin Farr;
- the 1918 Mrs. Alice Farr is the later 505 Main pool-hall proprietor;
- Alice Clarinda Sprague Farr is the Alice Farr in the City 401 Third survey;
- Edwin or Alice Farr operated the unnamed 1925 billiards/cigars occupancy at 505 Main;
- Farr's Pool Hall was operating in 1917, 1918, 1922, or 1934;
- the 1934 residence acquisition date is the pool hall's opening date; or
- the secondary cemetery dates override the official City narrative.

## Highest-value identity checks

Before merging or extending the Farr chronology, retrieve contemporary records that explicitly connect the identities:

1. marriage record / announcement naming **Edwin F. or Edwin Franklin Farr** and Alice, including Alice's maiden name;
2. 1930 and 1940 census entries for Edwin and Alice Farr in Oregon City / Clackamas County;
3. the **1934 deed** or deed index for 401 Third naming the grantee(s);
4. primary death certificate / obituary for the relevant Alice Farr;
5. primary death certificate / obituary for Edwin F. Farr;
6. 1928–1935 Clackamas County assumed-business-name certificates for Farr's Pool Hall / Edwin Farr;
7. city directory or municipal license records explicitly naming Edwin/Alice Farr with **505 Main**.

Until one of those bridges is recovered, preserve **Edwin F. Farr**, **Edwin Franklin Farr**, the 1918 **Mrs. Alice Farr**, and **Alice Clarinda Sprague Farr** as related research candidates rather than silently collapsing them into one biography.
