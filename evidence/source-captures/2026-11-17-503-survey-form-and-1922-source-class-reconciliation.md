# 503 Main survey form and 1922 source reconciliation

The three original scan pages of Michelle L. Dennis's May 2000 form for the Kwality Cafe property at 503 Main Street were recovered and visually inspected on November 17, 2026. The PDF was retrieved again on November 25 and matched the previous SHA-256 exactly. **S-406 / E-369**.

- Page 1 tentatively dates the surviving building to circa 1919 and notes that little historical information was found. It reports a restaurant on the 1925 Sanborn map, Kwality Restaurant in 1926 newspapers, Kwality Cafe in 1947 and the Flor restaurant/cocktail bar in 1957.
- Page 1 attributes circa 1922–1972 Wolf family ownership to the 1983 survey. Page 3 lists general sources without an exact 1926 issue or page. Both remain leads to the underlying records.
- Page 2 contains a survey-context photograph of The Wheel frontage and adjacent 505 and a locator map. It supplies no 1922 photographic evidence.
- Page 3 names Richard and Sheila Wiitanen in the owner field for 503 Main. This records the May 2000 survey snapshot. Acquisition, conveyance and private building completion dates remain unresolved.

The page 3 bibliography lists Enterprise daily and weekly newspapers, 1917/1941/1948 directories, 1892/1911/1925 Sanborn maps and 1983 individual files. It does not identify the issue behind the 1926 statement. The survey image of the neighboring frontage remains embedded in the source PDF.

It returned resource 30537, Kwality Café, c.1919 and a 2009 reconnaissance survey date. Those are database metadata. The downloaded form itself is dated May 2000. Raw query JSON and summary HTML are retained beside the PDF.
