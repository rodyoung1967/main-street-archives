## Material findings and limits

- **Building use, 1914–1915 ledger period.** At 503 the City ledger prints Store and at 503½ it records Barber Shop. The row in the expected 505 position bears a repeated ledger vacancy symbol, with the street number missing. That is a qualified vacancy hypothesis only. The numbered 107 Fifth blacksmith and 110 Fifth stable are distinct records. [Book 3 visual audit](1914-1915-water-rent-record-book-3-visual-review.md).
- **Named controls.** Holman & Randall advertise at or near Fifth/Main without a number. Cox's raid-period pool hall is directly located between Seventh and Eighth on Main. The new checks place **W. S. Eddy** on Fifth between Main and Water by January 8 and at Fashion Stable on Main between Fourth and Fifth by October 8, with repeats through December 31. Portland House is on the southeast corner at the 1911 mapped 426 Main site. [Cox batch](1915-enterprise-pages-161-200.md) · [Portland House](../../buildings/426-main-portland-house-site.md) · [Fashion Stable](../../businesses/fashion-stable.md).
- **Numbered neighbors.** The Courier directories carry 507 Bradley, 509 H. P. Brightbill, 511 Harding, 519 Theroux and 527 Price Bros. through December 30. The November 19 Enterprise independently confirms **H. P. Brightbill at 509**. The December 17 Enterprise independently confirms **Price Bros. at Sixth/Main**, alongside the Courier's precise 527 number. The December 31 Enterprise corroborates Mass' Hotel without its number. [Year-end visual audit](1915-enterprise-pages-353-428.md).
- **Property and construction.** The Main Street paving audit records contract, street-surface work, labor case, assessment and acceptance milestones. No visually verified 1915 record in the online audit establishes demolition or reconstruction of 503 or 505, exact title changes, or a named tenant at either address. The annual chronology keeps street work, building fabric, ownership, tenancy and licensing distinct.
- **OCR-only or uncertain leads.** Earlier provisional OCR references are superseded only where a later visual source verifies or corrects them. The January 8 ad initials were corrected on the original scan under `E-377`. Book 3's layered 110 Fifth handwriting, the exact Fifth Street work limits and the municipal licensing and title-record leads remain open. No OCR-only target occupant or construction claim was promoted. See the [evidence register](../evidence-register.md), [research leads](../research-leads.md) and [open questions](../open-questions.md).
