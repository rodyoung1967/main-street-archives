Status: **active / partial page-level review**

This file records recovered December 1915 *Oregon City Courier* pages relevant to the 503/505 Main Street project. It does **not** substitute for the project-wide requirement that every available page of every relevant Oregon City newspaper be reviewed. Unretrieved pages remain explicit gaps.

## 16 December 1915

### Page 8 reviewed — direct exact-address continuity
Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1915-12-16/ed-1/seq-8/

The classified business directory directly lists:

- **W. W. Bradley — 507 Main — Tel. 139** (new and second-hand furniture/furnishings)
- **H. P. Brightbill — 509 Main — Phone 74** (staple, fancy and green groceries)

This is direct/high-confidence late-1915 exact-address evidence for 507 and 509.

The successfully recovered OCR segment does not include a 503 or 505 listing.

Remaining pages require page-level review.

### Page 6 reviewed — direct exact-address continuity, with OCR caution

The classified directory directly lists:

- **H. P. Brightbill — 509 Main — Phone 74**
- W. W. Bradley's line OCRs as **`607-Main`**, but the same directory repeatedly prints Bradley at **507 Main** throughout July–December 1915, including 16 December. Therefore `607-Main` is treated as an OCR error unless the page image proves otherwise; it is **not** evidence of an address move.

No direct 503 or 505 listing appears in the recovered OCR segment.

Pages 1–5 and 7–8 remain pending page-level review.

## December interpretation

December strengthens the already well-supported late-1915 sequence:

- 507 Main — W. W. Bradley
- 509 Main — H. P. Brightbill
- 511 Main — George A. Harding

The evidence also shows Brightbill's grocery actively trading immediately before Christmas. The month still does **not** resolve 503 or 505.

Because much of December remains unreviewed page-by-page, the absence of a recovered 503/505 listing is **not** a month-wide negative conclusion.
