# Wheel Cafe & Lounge at 503 Main — 28 March 1958 exact-address control and February guide controls

Date of research pass: **7 September 2026**

Status: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT — VISUALLY VERIFIED PRIMARY NEWSPAPER SCAN; EARLIEST RECOVERED EXACT-ADDRESS WHEEL ADVERTISEMENT IN THIS PASS**

## Purpose

Continue the backward newspaper review without duplicating the parallel Flor/Remus directory, EDR, Historic Oregon City, and proprietor-license search streams. The target is the public advertising-state boundary for **Wheel Cafe & Lounge at 503 Main**, not an assumed opening, ownership, license, rename, or sale date.

## 28 March 1958 — direct Wheel advertisement

The actual scan of *The Clackamas County News*, **28 March 1958, p. 3**, was visually inspected. In the recurring **WHERE TO FIND AND BUY IT IN OREGON CITY** business guide, the advertisement directly reads:

- **WHEEL CAFE**
- **& LOUNGE**
- **STEAKS**
- **503 Main St.**
- **Ph: 9106**

Primary page route:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088135/1958-03-28/ed-1/seq-3.pdf

Classification: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT — VISUALLY VERIFIED PRIMARY NEWSPAPER ADVERTISEMENT** for the Wheel Cafe & Lounge trade name, exact 503 Main address, and telephone 9106 on 28 March 1958.

The advertisement does **not** name a proprietor, owner, licensee, seller, buyer, predecessor, or state `formerly Kwality` / `new management`. It therefore does not establish who controlled the business or the mechanism of the Kwality/Flor → Wheel transition.

This supersedes the visually verified **16 May 1958** (`S-283` / `E-256`) and **22 August 1958** (`S-282` / `E-255`) pages only as the archive's **earliest currently recovered exact-address Wheel advertisement**. Those later controls remain valid corroboration.

## 2 May 1958 corroborating earlier-than-May-16 page

The actual scan of *The Clackamas County News*, **2 May 1958, p. 3**, was also visually inspected. Its Oregon City guide carries the same Wheel Cafe & Lounge / 503 Main / phone 9106 advertisement. This was found before the March 28 page during the same backward pass and confirms continuity between late March and May; March 28 is the earlier endpoint.

## Comparable pre-March guide controls

The actual page scans for **31 January, 7 February, 14 February, and 21 February 1958** were visually inspected. Each contains the recurring **WHERE TO FIND AND BUY IT IN OREGON CITY** business guide. In those visually inspected guide panels, **no Wheel Cafe advertisement is present**.

These controls are useful only for the **advertising state of this recurring guide**. They are **not negative evidence that the Wheel business did not yet exist**, and they do not establish an opening date.

No Kwality advertisement was recovered in the inspected comparable guide panels. That absence likewise does not prove Kwality had ceased operating.

## Critical digitized-coverage gaps

The title's online browse sequence is not weekly-complete in this period:

- after **21 February 1958**, the next linked surviving issue in the reviewed sequence is **28 March 1958**;
- after **10 January 1958**, the previous linked issue jumps to **25 October 1957**;
- some other spring issues use Gresham rather than Oregon City guide panels.

Therefore the currently recoverable newspaper evidence can say that the recurring Oregon City guide **does not carry the Wheel ad in the inspected Jan. 31–Feb. 21 controls and does carry it on Mar. 28**, but it cannot date the first insertion precisely or prove the underlying business transition occurred in that interval.

Classification for the missing intervening issues: **RETRIEVAL / COVERAGE GAP**, not negative evidence.

## Revised documentary endpoint

The current same-address transition endpoints are:

- **1957** — City official-secondary synthesis places Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor with the restaurant/cocktail-bar operation at 503 Main (`E-032` / `S-026`); and
- **by 28 March 1958** — visually verified primary advertisement directly identifies **Wheel Cafe & Lounge, 503 Main, phone 9106**.

Classification when paired: **DOCUMENTED ENDPOINTS / UNRESOLVED TRANSITION**.

Do **not** infer from this bracket that Albert Flor owned the Wheel, renamed Kwality Cafe, sold the business, sold to Raye LaFarlette/Grisham, or ceased operating on any specific date.
