# 27 October highway-route reports and 30 November pool-hall license — OCR recheck

**Date:** 15 November 2026. **Status:** OCR/TEXT ONLY in this pass; original scanned pages not newly visually inspected. **1922 IN PROGRESS.**

## Oregon City Enterprise — 27 October 1922, p. 1
**Heading:** 'GUARANTEE IS ASKED UPON SOUTH END ROAD'. OCR: The State Highway Commission's projected route was **Railroad Avenue and Seventh Street**, while Oregon City sought a cheaper **Main Street and Fifth Street** route to the bluff. The article says the city lacked funds for Railroad Avenue, that Fifth Street would cost a fraction as much, and that the state required a satisfactory guarantee that Railroad Avenue would ultimately be improved. It reports no final commission action at the time of this article. If a bluff route failed, Main Street and the lower basin route toward Canemah remained an alternative.

Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1922-10-27/ed-1/seq-1.pdf ; repo OCR `newspapers/oregon-city-enterprise/1922-10/ocr/1922-10-27-seq-01.txt`.

## Oregon City Enterprise — 27 October 1922, p. 8
**Heading:** 'FIFTH STREET LINE BIDS ADVERTISED BY COMMISSION'. OCR: R. A. Booth announced bids for **21 November 1922** for the South End Road via Fifth Street, contingent on a future permanent improvement involving Seventh Street. Also discusses vacation of lower Main/Canemah roadway and PRL&P conditions for Canemah streetcar service, lighting and pedestrian walk. This is **public-road infrastructure** and **not** evidence that 503/505 building work was completed or caused by highway work.

## Banner-Courier — 30 November 1922, p. 1
**Heading:** 'Granted Pool Hall License'. OCR wording: **'Barry and McAnulty have been granted a license to operate a pool hall, in the room formerly occupied by them as a restaurant.'** This is stronger than the previous description of a mere application; the report claims **license granted**. No numbered address is supplied. Treat exact premises as **UNRESOLVED**, and do not attach to 505 Main or Leland & Little without independent address evidence.

Same page OCR also reports a South End Road cost estimate of **$79,404** for the segment toward the southern city limits, plus **$13,150** for Fifth-to-Seventh via Railroad Avenue, total **$92,554**. These are reported estimates, not proof of construction.
