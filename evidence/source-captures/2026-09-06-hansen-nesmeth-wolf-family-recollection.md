# Ray Hansen c.1963 and Wiitanen 503/505 seller-name conflict — 6 September 2026

Status: **FAMILY / ORAL-HISTORY LEADS — UNRESOLVED CONFLICT; DOCUMENTARY CONTROLS ADDED**

Purpose: preserve Rodney Young's latest family recollection about the early-1960s 505 Main business and the later Wiitanen real-estate acquisitions without silently overwriting the earlier family account. The parcel seller names remain open until assessor/deed records resolve them.

## Latest project-steward recollection

On 6 September 2026 Rodney Young reported that he currently believes:

1. **Ray Hansen bought the business at 505 Main in roughly 1963**;
2. **Dick and Sheila Wiitanen may have bought the 503 Main building from a woman remembered as Mrs. Nesmeth / Nemeth**; and
3. **Dick and Sheila may have bought the 505 Main building from someone possibly named Wolf**.

Classification: **FAMILY / ORAL HISTORY — UNVERIFIED LEADS**. The date `roughly 1963`, the transaction type, and both real-estate seller assignments require independent documentation.

## Conflict with earlier family account

Earlier family testimony already preserved in the archive (`E-022` / `E-038`, `P-026`) placed **Mrs. Nesmeth / Nemeth as the seller of the 505 Main real estate** to Dick and Sheila after they had begun operating Brass Rail there. The latest recollection instead tentatively places Nesmeth/Nemeth at **503** and a **Wolf** at 505.

These versions are now an **UNRESOLVED FAMILY-TESTIMONY CONFLICT**. Neither version should be described as deed-proven, and neither should be deleted merely because the other is newer.

## Documentary controls

### 505 business by November 1965 — direct municipal

Oregon City Commission minutes for **11 November 1965** directly list **505 Tavern** among liquor-license renewals. This establishes the trade/business at 505 by that date but does **not** identify the individual licensee in the readable minute text and does not prove a 1963 purchase by Ray Hansen.

Source: https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4753762/File/document (`E-029` context).

Current bracket: family memory places a Hansen business purchase at about 1963; direct City evidence places **505 Tavern** at the premises by November 1965. The exact Hansen acquisition/license date remains unresolved.

### 1964 Polk directory — high-value unresolved source

The Genealogical Forum of Oregon current inventory confirms **TF0763 — Oregon City Directory 1964 — R. L. Polk & Co.** This is the most targeted surviving directory source yet identified for the approximate Hansen date. It should be checked for:

- `505 Main` street-directory occupant/business;
- `505 Tavern` or any predecessor name;
- **Ray / Raymond Hansen** personal and business listings;
- telephone number and cross-directory proprietor/manager associations;
- `503 Main` / Wheel as a same-year control.

Inventory source: https://gfo.org/file_download/inline/fc3b79ad-cc0d-45eb-babb-5e294ec31089

The book has **not** been inspected and no purchase/lookup has been authorized in this research pass.

### Wolf family is presently tied more strongly to 503 than 505

A City-derived description of **503 Main / Kwality Cafe**, reproducing research from the **1983 survey**, states that **the property was owned by the Wolf family from about 1922 to 1972**. This is an official-secondary historical synthesis, not a deed, but it is a material control on the latest memory that places a Wolf as the later 505 seller.

Current public City attachment reproducing the wording:
https://mccmeetingspublic.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/oregoncity-meet-73673237b9124664a790d047658a9813/ITEM-Attachment-001-49626a7ef29942e0be519c46b2f656fc.pdf

Archive context: `S-026` / `E-032`.

### Wolf at 505 in 1918 — business occupancy only

The **10 October 1918 Oregon City Courier** directly advertises **Wolf & Miller, 505 Main**. This establishes a Wolf-associated business at 505 in 1918, but it does **not** establish later fee ownership of the 505 parcel and cannot by itself bridge to a Wiitanen real-estate purchase decades later.

Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-10-10/ed-1/seq-4/

### 1922 Wolf property context remains spatially unresolved

The project also has 1922 Harding/Wolf/Gates conveyance evidence in the Fifth/Main block. The exact modern-parcel mapping and later title continuity must remain separate from the 1918 Wolf & Miller occupancy and from the 1970s–1980s Wiitanen acquisition question.

## Interpretation

The evidence currently does **not** support selecting one remembered seller assignment as correct.

- `Wolf -> 503` has meaningful official-secondary support through the 1983-survey ownership statement.
- `Wolf -> 505` has an early 1918 business-association clue but no later title bridge yet.
- `Nesmeth/Nemeth -> 505` is the earlier family account.
- `Nesmeth/Nemeth -> 503` is Rodney's latest recollection.

The correct answer should come from the **parcel-first ownership books / tax-lot cards / recorded deeds**, not from forcing the surname memory onto an address.
