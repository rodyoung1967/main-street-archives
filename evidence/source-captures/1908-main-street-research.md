## S-084 — Main Street overhead-wire and pole removal, 1908

Type: Primary newspaper / utility and streetscape evidence  
Related evidence: `E-070`

Verified pages:

- 10 January 1908, *Morning Oregonian*, page 7: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83025138/1908-01-10/ed-1/seq-7/
- 14 February 1908, *Oregon City Enterprise*, page 1: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1908-02-14/ed-1/seq-1/
- 31 July 1908, *Oregon City Enterprise*, page 8: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1908-07-31/ed-1/seq-8/

Observed claims:

- all telegraph, telephone, and electric-light poles on Main **between Eleventh Street and the Basin** were ordered removed;
- companies were to put wires underground or bring them into Main from side streets;
- Pacific Telephone & Telegraph and Portland General Electric were arranging removal by February;
- by July Pacific Telephone again stated that all wires would be moved from Main.

Significance: the target Fifth–Sixth block lies inside the stated corridor. This is direct physical-change evidence following the 1907 track/paving/grade reconstruction.

Limitation: no target-building demolition or replacement is stated.

## S-087 — Pacific Telephone Oregon City expansion, 1908

Type: Primary newspaper / utility-business evidence  
Related evidence: `E-073`  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1908-07-31/ed-1/seq-8/

Observed claims: Pacific Telephone & Telegraph's Oregon City office received a **$10,000 appropriation** for expanding facilities; Manager George J. Hall was notified; additional cable facilities were planned; and all wires would be moved from Main Street.

Limitation: the article does not print the office's exact 1908 street number. Later 1910 advertisements give **510 Main**, but that later number is not projected backward.
