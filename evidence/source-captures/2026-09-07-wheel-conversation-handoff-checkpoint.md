## 6 June 1979 — dated Wheel liquor application, but NOT transfer proof

Official Oregon City Commission searchable text, WebDrawer record `4746159`, bound page 195, records approval of **the liquor license application for The Wheel**.

The direct 1979 Oregon statutory check materially changed the interpretation:

- **ORS 471.290(1)** treated both an original license and a **renewal** as an `application`;
- **ORS 471.355** provided annual license expirations at quarter ends, including June 30.

Therefore:

- the phrase `liquor license application` does **not** imply a transfer;
- June timing does **not** distinguish renewal from new/change activity;
- the 6 Jun. 1979 action must not be called `transfer-like`, `probable change of ownership`, or a Wheel sale date from wording/timing alone.

Safe classification:

- **Direct:** City approved a Wheel liquor-license application on 6 Jun. 1979.
- **Unresolved:** applicant/licensee, application category, renewal vs. change, seller/buyer, sale date.
