# Albert D. Flor / Kwality Cafe / Wheel Cafe proprietor search status — 7 September 2026

## Research question

Who operated, owned, licensed, renamed, sold, or acquired the restaurant/cocktail-bar business at **503 Main Street** between the City's **1957 Mr./Mrs. Albert D. Flor** control and the visually verified **22 August 1958 Wheel Cafe & Lounge** advertisement?

## Established control points

- `E-032` / `S-026`: official-secondary City historic-resource synthesis places **Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor** with the restaurant/cocktail-bar operation at **503 Main in 1957**.
- `E-255` / `S-282`: visually verified primary advertisement, **22 August 1958**, directly reads **WHEEL CAFE & LOUNGE — 503 Main St. — Ph: 9106**.
- Existing related controls remain: **5 December 1951 Albert D. Flor + Margaret Remus liquor-license approval**, **September 1953 `Al & Marge Kwality Cafe`** city finance wording, and the separate 1953 City historic-resource statement that **Fred Remus was a cook at Kwality Cafe**.

This is a high-value target because one volume may be checked three ways: alphabetical person entries for **Flor / Remus**, business-name entries for **Wheel / Kwality**, and reverse-address entries for **503 Main**. Access to the page images/text has not yet been obtained in this pass.

3. **Regional newspaper alternate route identified.** Sandy-area newspapers covering the 1953–1963 period are digitized on Newspapers.com and may contain Oregon City business notices, liquor items, or cross-county advertising not exposed through the home-paper route. No proprietor-identifying Wheel/Flor result has yet been recovered from this alternate route.
4. **Environmental-report directory abstract route identified.** A recent Oregon City/ODOT environmental report states that consultants reviewed city directories including **1953, 1960, 1964, 1969, and later years** and includes an EDR City Directory Abstract appendix. Whether the abstract covers **503 Main** has not yet been established.
5. **Annual liquor-renewal batch route remains active.** Oregon City Commission year-end liquor-license approvals/renewals may identify a 1957→1958 change in licensee even if no newspaper sale notice survives. The decisive search is for **Albert D. Flor, Margaret/Marge Remus, Wheel Cafe, Kwality Cafe, 503 Main, or another licensee at the same premises** in late-1957 and late-1958 municipal records.

## Current search result

No new source recovered in this continuation yet names the **August 1958 Wheel proprietor/licensee**, states that Flor renamed Kwality to The Wheel, records a Flor sale, or identifies the person from whom Raye later bought The Wheel.

Classification: **BOUNDED SEARCH / RETRIEVAL GAP — NOT NEGATIVE EVIDENCE.** The absence of a proprietor hit in the currently indexed/open-web material does not establish that Flor was or was not the Wheel operator. The key primary sources are still incompletely accessible.

## Disconfirming-evidence test

The current working theory must be rejected or revised if any 1957–1959 primary source identifies a different person as proprietor/licensee of 503 Main, shows Flor at another premises while a different operator holds 503, or documents a transfer/assumed-business-name event inconsistent with Flor continuity.
