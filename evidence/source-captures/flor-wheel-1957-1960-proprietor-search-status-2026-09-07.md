# Albert D. Flor / Kwality Cafe / Wheel Cafe proprietor search status — 7 September 2026

## Research question

Who operated, owned, licensed, renamed, sold, or acquired the restaurant/cocktail-bar business at **503 Main Street** between the City's **1957 Mr./Mrs. Albert D. Flor** control and the visually verified **28 March 1958 Wheel Cafe & Lounge** advertisement?

## Established control points

- `E-032` / `S-026`: official-secondary City historic-resource synthesis places **Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor** with the restaurant/cocktail-bar operation at **503 Main in 1957**.
- `E-259` / `S-285`: visually verified primary advertisement, **28 March 1958**, directly reads **WHEEL CAFE & LOUNGE — 503 Main St. — Ph: 9106**. This supersedes the earlier May/August 1958 controls only as the earliest currently recovered exact-address Wheel advertisement; it does not identify the proprietor or opening date.
- Existing related controls remain: **5 December 1951 Albert D. Flor + Margaret Remus liquor-license approval**, **September 1953 `Al & Marge Kwality Cafe`** city finance wording, and the separate 1953 City historic-resource statement that **Fred Remus was a cook at Kwality Cafe**.

No proprietor-identifying Wheel/Flor result has yet been recovered from this alternate route.

5. **Annual and one-off liquor-action route remains active.** Oregon City Commission year-end renewals and isolated in-year liquor approvals may identify a 1957→1958 change in applicant/licensee even if no newspaper sale notice survives. The decisive search is for **Albert D. Flor, Margaret/Marge Remus, Wheel Cafe, Kwality Cafe, 503 Main, or another applicant at the same premises**.
