## Target / construction screening result

Fresh July–August exact-address and building-language searches did not recover a safe new bridge between the 10 May 1912 citywide Main Street demolition/rebuilding statement (`E-090` / `RL-038`) and **501, 503, 505, 507, 509, or 511 Main**.

Useful controls encountered:

- 9 July page 2 repeats the paper's established sales locations: **E. B. Anderson — Main near Sixth** and **M. E. Dunn — next door to P.O.** These remain relative-location controls only.
- The same 9 July page carries a classified offering a **40 x 60 two-story heavy-frame building at Fourth and Water streets** for sale by Hawley Pulp & Paper Co. This is a real building-disposition item but is outside the target block and must not be used as evidence for the May Main Street teardown.
- 9 August page 3 places the Portland Railway Light & Power local office **on Main Street in the Beaver Building** and separately gives businesses at Seventh/Main and Thirteenth/Main. None maps a target odd-side storefront or identifies the May demolition buildings.
- 23 August page 2 places the Oregon City Commercial Club publicity department on **Main between Eighth and Ninth**, not the target block, and continues a classified for dressmaking in **Room 5, Willamette Building**. The latter strengthens continued use of the Willamette Building name in 1912 but still does not supply an exact numbered address.

No new `E-###` or `S-###` is created from these controls. They do not change current claim-level 1912 address conclusions.
