## Correction — the Rosenberry / Ice Works / Meten passage is June 7 business

An earlier continuation treated a searchable-text passage discontinuing condemnation proceedings involving names/entities including **John Rosenberry**, **Oregon City Ice Works**, **Walker and Green (Estes)**, **Leona Young**, and **Nels Meten** as if it belonged to the unnamed Commission section preceding the printed 7 June heading. That was an over-reading of the indexed text sequence.

A tighter official-text recheck shows that the condemnation-discontinuance passage flows into proposed **Ordinance No. 1624**. Ordinance No. 1624 expressly states that it was **read first time and ordered published at a regular meeting of the City Commission held on the 7th day of June, 1967** and would return for final passage on 21 June 1967.

Therefore the Rosenberry / Oregon City Ice Works / Estes / Meten discontinuance passage belongs to the **7 June 1967 meeting section**, not to the unnamed meeting that ends immediately before the printed June 7 heading.
