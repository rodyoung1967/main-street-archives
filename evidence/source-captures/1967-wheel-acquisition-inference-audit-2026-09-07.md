# The Wheel — 1967 acquisition-date inference audit

Research date: **7 September 2026**  
Business: **The Wheel (`BUS-004`) — 503 Main Street only**  
Status: **ARCHIVAL LOGIC CORRECTION — NO NEW OWNER OR TRANSFER DATE ESTABLISHED**

## Why this audit was needed

A canonical `registers/business-timeline.md` row still combined `E-025` (the unnamed 1967 municipal liquor action) with `E-022` (family evidence that Raye later bought The Wheel) under the period label **By Jun. 1967**. Read together, that row could imply that Raye's purchase was established by June 1967. The evidence does not support that inference.

## Separate propositions

### `E-025` — municipal licensing action

Official Oregon City Commission searchable text establishes a liquor-application action for **the Wheel at 503 Main** in the meeting section immediately before the printed **7 June 1967** heading. The exact preceding-meeting date remains unresolved. The surviving text does not name the applicant/licensee or application category.

This establishes a dated **Wheel licensing action and exact premises**, not Raye/Floyd ownership or a purchase.

### `E-022` — family ownership chronology

Project-steward/family evidence establishes that **Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham bought The Wheel business at 503 Main**. It does not supply a documentary purchase date or identify the seller.

This establishes the family ownership sequence, not a June 1967 date.

## May 1965 southwest-Oregon control is also not a legal boundary

Indexed/OCR *Myrtle Point Herald* material describes Floyd LaFarlette as a Myrtle Hotel **operator** on **6 May 1965**. That source remains OCR/TEXT ONLY pending visual page review. Even if its wording is confirmed, operation of a Myrtle Point business does not by itself exclude simultaneous investment, licensing, travel, or business activity elsewhere. Therefore **6 May 1965 is not a proved terminus post quem for acquiring The Wheel**.

Likewise, the unnamed pre-7-June-1967 Wheel liquor action is not a proved terminus ante quem for a LaFarlette acquisition because no LaFarlette applicant is identified.

## Correct evidence state

The safe current statement is:

> Floyd LaFarlette is indexed as a Myrtle Hotel operator in May 1965. A later 1967 municipal record documents an unnamed liquor-application action for The Wheel at 503 Main. Family history establishes that Raye bought The Wheel, but no recovered contemporary source yet connects a LaFarlette name directly to The Wheel or dates that purchase.

Thus **c.1967 remains a research hypothesis / priority interval, not a closed acquisition bracket**.
