# 1921 — Oregon City Laundry at Fifth Street near Main

Status: **verified primary-source address bridge**

Purpose: strengthen the pre-July-1922 location chain for Oregon City Laundry and connect the 1918 exact address evidence to the 1922 fire-era Fifth Street cluster.

## Source

**The Morning Oregonian, 13 Aug 1921, p. 16** carried an advertisement/classified notice from Oregon City Laundry offering two vertical steam engines for sale. The notice identifies the seller/location as:

> Oregon City Laundry, Oregon City, Or., **5th st., near Main**.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83025138/1921-08-13/ed-1/seq-16.pdf

## Significance

This independently confirms that Oregon City Laundry was operating on **Fifth Street near Main** in August 1921.

It bridges two previously established anchors:

- **24 Oct 1918** — Oregon City Courier business guide: **Oregon City Laundry, 109 Fifth St.**
- **Jul 1922** — the Fifth Street fire destroyed the Oregon City Laundry plant together with the May & Washburn frame garage and neighboring Fifth Street businesses.

The 1921 source therefore makes it substantially more likely that the laundry destroyed in July 1922 was the same Fifth-near-Main operation documented in 1918 and 1921, although exact footprint continuity still should not be asserted without a map/photo bridge.

## Photo implication

A project photograph showing Oregon City Laundry in the Fifth/Main area is fully consistent with a **pre-fire 1918–Jul 1922** setting. If the same photograph can also be matched to the specific May & Washburn frame garage documented by Apr 1921 and destroyed in Jul 1922, the stronger working photo window remains **Apr 1921–Jul 1922**.

## Confidence

- Oregon City Laundry at Fifth near Main in Aug 1921: **Very High**
- Same business/site as 109 Fifth in 1918: **High, but not yet exact-footprint proven**
- Same exact structure destroyed in Jul 1922: **Probable / requires visual or parcel confirmation**
