# 1925–1926 503 / 505 Main business-identification checkpoint

Status: **research checkpoint; 1925 trade names/operators remain unresolved**

Review date: **1 September 2026**

Purpose: preserve the completed portion of the focused pass to identify the restaurant mapped at **503 Main** and the billiards/cigars business mapped at **505 Main** on the May 1925 Sanborn. This note also records incidental Fifth Street evidence without converting intersection-level references into numbered Main Street addresses.

## Firm source boundary

The visually reviewed **1925 Sanborn Sheet 6** (`E-020` / `S-021` / `SM-008`) proves the following uses and nothing more:

- **503 Main — restaurant**
- **505 Main — billiards/cigars**

Sanborn does **not** print either trade name or proprietor. A compatible earlier or later business cannot be assigned to the 1925 premises solely from its type of trade.

## 503 Main result

Oregon City's official historic-resource synthesis (`S-026`) says that:

- the 1925 Sanborn depicts a restaurant at 503; and
- **1926 newspaper advertisements** identify the business as **Kwality Restaurant**.

The underlying 1926 advertisement, page, date, and proprietor wording were **not recovered** during this pass. The local newspaper calendars checked online do not expose a usable 1925 or 1926 run for the *Banner-Courier* or *Oregon City Enterprise*. Therefore:

- **Kwality Restaurant at 503 in 1926** remains an official secondary-source synthesis pending recovery of its cited advertisement;
- **Kwality is a strong next-year candidate for the unnamed 1925 restaurant**, but is **not yet proved to be the May 1925 map occupant**;
- the undated New Kwality Cafe and Larry's Kwality Cafe artifacts remain later/undated exact-address anchors and do not fill the 1925 gap.

The **Quality Restaurant at 427 Main** advertised on 8 June 1922 is a different exact-address record and must not be merged into the 503 Kwality sequence without transfer evidence.

## 505 Main result

The official Farr residence history (`S-036` / `E-033`) proves that Edwin F. and Alice Farr were proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main**, but it does not supply the pool hall's opening, closing, or acquisition date. No address-specific source recovered in this pass places Farr's at 505 in May 1925. Therefore:

- **Farr's is confirmed at 505 at an unresolved date**;
- it is a plausible candidate for the unnamed 1925 billiards/cigars premises, but the archive must **not identify the 1925 map occupant as Farr's yet**;
- A. Leland's visually verified 1920 permission to operate a pool hall at **505½ Main** (`E-135` / `S-150`) is a separate earlier anchor, not proof of continuity to 1925;
- the 1922 Timms / Leland & Little billiards and cigar evidence is functionally compatible but lacks an exact 505 address bridge;
- Harr's Recreation Center is independently documented later and remains a separate business from Farr's.

## 1923–1924 regional newspaper bridge search

A follow-up online pass inventoried **1,487 issues / 12,096 OCR pages** across seven exposed regional titles for 1923–1924. OCR was used only to identify candidate scans. Exact-name-like and strongest Oregon City-context candidates were then visually checked.

The scan review found only unrelated Farr personal/news references, an ordinary “quality” OCR error, `Jager` errors produced by **Manager Harris** and **Hager**, and Fifth Street references in Salem or Estacada. ## Incidental Fifth Street handling

The research instruction is to preserve other Fifth Street addresses encountered during this work. Apply the following rule:

- record an exact Fifth Street number when the source prints one;
- preserve **Fifth & Main** or **5th and Main** as intersection-level context when that is all the source says;
- do not translate an intersection reference into 501, 503, 505, or another numbered Main Street storefront without independent address-mapping evidence.

The only new Fifth-related item from this municipal-report pass is the **11 November 1926 false alarm at 5th and Main**. It identifies no building or business.
