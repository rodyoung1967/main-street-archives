- **1936:** CCHS photo P-1128 plus City identification strongly supports Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main. Edwin F. Farr and Alice Farr are relevant; Clarence P. Farr at 708 Monroe is a separate person/path.
- **1945:** May 2000 Oregon SHPO resource form, RESOURCE_ID 30539, is visually verified and explicitly states a 1945 city directory lists **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main**. This is official-secondary synthesis; the underlying 1945 directory page remains unrecovered.
- **11 Feb 1948:** Oregon City Commission primary text names Harr and McDougald as pool-hall operators but does not give first name/business/address in that sentence.
- **late 1948:** separate municipal liquor-license list directly identifies **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**. Surviving placement is after 23 Nov 1948 and before 3 Jan 1949; do not invent an exact December meeting date without the missing header page.
- **9 Nov 1949:** five-license beer-renewal batch does not include Harr/505. Coverage only; not negative evidence.
- **1950:** Sanborn maps 505 as billiards/cigars/beer; use only, proprietor unnamed.
- **5 Dec 1951:** bounded 19-applicant liquor batch already researched; Harr absent, but supplementary/one-off actions prevent a departure inference.
- **1952:** supplementary exact-address liquor actions already researched and saved.
- **1953:** visually inspected SHPO form says the 1953 city directory lists **"Well's Recreation"** at 505 Main. Preserve the SHPO spelling. Do not infer proprietor surname, opening/closing date, or ownership until the underlying directory is inspected.
- **2 Dec 1953:** large 1954 liquor-renewal batch has no 505 in that batch. Coverage only.
- **20 Nov 1955:** original Eugene Register-Guard Image 30 JP2 was recovered and visually inspected.
