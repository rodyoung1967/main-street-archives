# 1926 Kwality municipal evidence and newspaper-ad follow-up — 15 September 2026

## Scope

This capture records the current 1926 evidence for the restaurant at **503 Main Street** and keeps direct/secondary/text-only evidence states separate while the underlying 1926 newspaper advertisements are still being sought.

## 1. City historic-resource synthesis — 1926 newspaper advertisements

A City of Oregon City Planning historic-resource description, reproduced in a later Oregon City public-meeting attachment, states that 503 Main appears on the **1925 Sanborn map as a restaurant** and **“in 1926 newspaper ads as the Kwality Restaurant.”** The same synthesis says a 1947 business directory used **Kwality Cafe**.

## 1A. 2000 re-survey provenance narrows the newspaper family

A follow-up review of the official **2000 Oregon City Historic Downtown Re-Survey Final Report** materially improves the source trail behind the City's later 503 narrative.

The report says the 2000 project generated **59 individual Oregon Inventory of Historic Properties Historic Resource Survey Forms**, with copies available through the City and originals housed at **SHPO in Salem**. Its methodology says archival research used newspapers and was conducted from sources at the **Oregon City Public Library, Clackamas County Historical Society and Museum, University of Oregon, and Oregon State University**.

Most importantly, the report's selected bibliography explicitly lists:

- **Oregon City Enterprise (daily and weekly newspapers).**

The current Oregon City Public Library microfilm page independently confirms that both Enterprise-family runs span all of **1926**:
- Oregon City Enterprise Weekly, 1871–1942;
- Oregon City Enterprise, 1911–1950.

This is a **provenance advance, not recovery of the underlying ad**. The bibliography is project-wide and does not prove whether the 503 advertisement was in the weekly Enterprise or daily Morning/Enterprise run, nor does it give an issue date or page. The best next step is now to retrieve the **individual 503 Main / Kwality Cafe 2000 survey form and any source notes** (`MF-068`) before beginning a blind 1926 microfilm pass (`MF-031`).

## 2. December 1926 finance report continued at 5 January 1927 meeting

The Oregon City Commission record for **5 January 1927** contains bills described as **“a continuation of the December Finance Report.”** In that list appears:

- **Kwality Cafe**
- purpose: **Meals**
- amount: **$5.00**

## 3. Consequence for the supposed 1947 “name change”

The City historic synthesis says that in the 1947 business directory “the name had changed to Kwality Cafe.” The December-1926 finance-report text, if visually confirmed, shows that **Kwality Cafe** was already in municipal use by the 1926/27 boundary. Therefore the 1947 wording should not be interpreted as proving that the Restaurant → Cafe name change occurred in 1947. Both **Kwality Restaurant** and **Kwality Cafe** may have been used earlier, or the later synthesis may simply have described differences between the source labels it consulted.

## 4. J. Jager / “New Kwality Cafe” lead

A surviving advertising artifact reproduced by the American Eateries blog reads:

- **New Kwality Cafe**
- **503 Main St. — Oregon City, Ore.**
- **J. Jager, Prop.**
- “We Never Close.”

This corresponds to the repository's existing `A-001` / `E-002` artifact evidence. The reproduction is undated and must **not** be assigned to 1926 without independent dating evidence.

Important control from the current repository timeline: an **18 October 1940** visually verified primary advertisement places **JAGERS at 503 Main**. That later exact-address Jager evidence makes it especially unsafe to assume the undated “New Kwality Cafe — J. Jager, Prop.” artifact belongs to the 1926 opening period. The Jager artifact should be dated independently.
