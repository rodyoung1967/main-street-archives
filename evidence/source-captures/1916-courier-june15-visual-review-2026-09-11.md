## Important findings

### Electric Store completion anchor

Page 7 is stronger than the previously used July 6 sample. Its explicit completed-move wording establishes that by **15 June 1916** the Electric Store and Light/Power offices had moved one door north into the **Andresen Building at 619 Main Street**. The retained **Beaver Building / 617 Main** footer is internally inconsistent with the completed-move statement and is preserved as stale ad copy rather than used to infer two operating locations.

This does **not** connect the Electric Store to 503/505.

### Seventh–Eighth Main property sale

Page 4 reports Richard Petzold acquiring Gustav Freiwald's Main Street property between Seventh and Eighth. The property is described as **66 by 210 feet**, running from Main to the railroad tracks, with a frame building and several houses that might be replaced by a modern brick building. The printed geography places this outside the Fifth/Main target frontage, so no 503/505 relation is created.

### Neighboring numbering controls

- **502 Main — Champion Smith & Telford**
