## Historical status at closeout

The exact **Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center** transition date remains unresolved.

Controlling evidence:

- **1936:** Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main is supported by CCHS photo P-1128 plus City identification.
- **1945:** Oregon City/SHPO historic-resource synthesis reports **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main**. This remains an official-secondary directory synthesis; the exact underlying directory title/effective year/page is still unrecovered.
- **Aug–Sep 1946:** Pacific Directory Service was conducting county-wide data gathering. A later 1947–48 directory listing may reflect late-1946 canvass conditions rather than an observation first made in 1948.
- **11 Feb 1948:** Oregon City Commission text directly identifies Harr and McDougald as Oregon City pool-hall operators; the sentence itself does not give Harr's address.
- **late 1948:** municipal liquor text directly identifies **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**.
- **1953:** SHPO synthesis states the 1953 directory lists **"Well's Recreation"** at 505 Main; underlying directory page remains unrecovered.
- **20 Nov 1955:** visually verified classified advertisement offers the established beer/pool business at **505 Main**; phone **9179**; seller/buyer/trade name not stated.
- **11 Nov 1965:** municipal liquor-renewal material directly documents **505 Tavern**.

Nothing in this conversation proves a different Farr → Harr transition date.
