## 505 interpretation

Neither **505 Main**, **Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center**, nor **Well / Well's / Wells Recreation** appears in this recovered nineteen-applicant batch.

That omission is **not proof** that:

- Harr had left 505 by 5 December 1951;
- 505 lacked a liquor or beer license;
- Well's Recreation was already operating there;
- Harr transferred directly to a Well/Wells proprietor; or
- the 505 business was closed.

Oregon City Commission records in this era demonstrably handled liquor licensing in split and one-off actions. In particular:

- the 9 November 1949 record preserves only five beer-license renewals;
- a **22 December 1952** supplemental action approved only three applicants; and
- the **7 January 1953** regular meeting separately approved renewals for John Harold Krempin / Center Street Grocery and Linn Avenue Grocery.

Those controls show why even a relatively broad December batch cannot be treated as an exhaustive annual roster.
