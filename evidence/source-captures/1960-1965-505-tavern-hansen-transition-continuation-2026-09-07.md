## 2011 secondary historical context — “five decades”

A 28 Feb. 2011 *Oregon Business* item about the Blue Heron mill closure states that Oregon City pulp-mill workers had been coming to the **505 Tavern** after work “for five decades.” It also quotes then-owner/bartender Michael Berman describing the bar's mill-worker identity.

Source:
https://oregonbusiness.com/2405-paper-mill-closure-affects-local-bar/

This is a useful later secondary recollection because, read approximately, it is consistent with an established 505 Tavern culture reaching back toward the early 1960s. It is **not** an exact opening date, trade-name adoption date, or Ray Hansen identification. Do not convert “five decades” into a documented 1961 start date.

Classification: **SECONDARY APPROXIMATE CONTEXT / LEAD ONLY**.

## Clackamas assumed-business-name records — exact Hansen-era coverage

The Oregon State Archives' official Clackamas County Inventory states that assumed-business-name registers can include the assumed name, business description, filing date and **business owner**. It explicitly lists at the Clackamas County Clerk Recording Office:

- **Assumed Business Names [Record], vols. 10–13, 1959–1964**;
- **4 reels of microfilm**.

Official inventory:
https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/county-records-guide/pages/clackamas-inventory.aspx

This is unusually well aligned with the remembered c.1963 Hansen transition.

Priority search keys:

- `505 Tavern`;
- `505 Main` if address-indexable;
- `Ray Hansen`;
- `Raymond Hansen`;
- `Hansen`;
- any predecessor trade name recovered from the 1960 directory or another contemporary source.

An assumed-name filing can directly identify a registrant/business owner as stated by that filing, but it is not automatically evidence of liquor-license ownership, daily operation, tenancy, real-estate ownership, or the date/terms of a business purchase.

## OLCC permanent Commission-record route is the strongest state archive class

The Oregon State Archives' indexed January 2024 OLCC retention schedule describes:

- **Commission Meeting Records, 1934–ongoing**;
- minutes, agendas and exhibits retained **permanently**;
- transfer to the State Archives after 20 years.

Current State Archives contact information identifies the Reference Unit at:

- `Reference.Archives@sos.oregon.gov`
- 503-373-0701, option 1.

References:
https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/Pages/services-fees.aspx
https://sos.oregon.gov/Pages/contact-us.aspx

The retention-schedule PDF itself did not render from its current direct URL during this continuation; the official Oregon search/index result supplied the quoted series description. Treat direct-PDF access as a current retrieval gap unless the file is later recovered and visually inspected.

The Commission meeting series is therefore the preferred archival state route for any 505 license application/transfer/change considered around **1959–1965**.

Older OLCC license files, history cards, tradename cards and processing records should be asked about only as a **survival/custody question**. Current retention schedules for several of those operational series are finite; that does not prove all historical 1960s cards/files were destroyed or that no legacy microfilm/transfer survives.
