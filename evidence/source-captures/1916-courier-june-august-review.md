# 1916 Oregon City Courier — June–August continuation

Status: **in progress / continuation of `1916-courier-visual-audit.md`**

Under `RESEARCH-STANDARD.md`, OCR/search results are discovery aids only. Printed page/PDF renderings control where inspected.

## June 1916

Recurring business-directory evidence continues to show the neighboring address ladder around the target properties:

- **W. W. Bradley — 507 Main**
- **H. P. Brightbill — 509 Main**
- **George A. Harding — 511 Main**

Examples recovered from June 8 and June 15 support Bradley at 507 and Harding at 511. OCR continues to corrupt digits on some June pages; June 1 and June 29 OCR render Harding as `611 Main`, which is treated as an OCR defect rather than a move because clean recurring listings establish 511.

Holman & Randall continue advertising as leading undertakers at **Fifth and Main St.** on June 1, June 22, and June 29.

No reliable exact-address June listing for **503 Main** or **505 Main** has yet been recovered. This is search status only, not evidence of vacancy or non-use.

## July 1916

The neighboring address pattern remains stable:

- **H. P. Brightbill — 509 Main** is explicitly present on July 20 and July 27 directory pages.
- A July 13 OCR rendering turns W. W. Bradley's known **507 Main** into `607-Main`; this is preserved as another OCR-digit warning and not treated as an address change.

Holman & Randall remain at **Fifth and Main St.** in July, including July 13.

No reliable exact-address July listing for **503 Main** or **505 Main** has yet been recovered.

## August 1916 — undertaker wording transition

By **31 August 1916**, the recurring undertaker advertisement reads:

> **R. L. Holman, Leading Undertaker, Fifth and Main St.**

rather than the earlier **R. L. Holman and T. P. Randall** partnership wording.

This proves the advertising/partnership wording had changed by August 31. It does not by itself establish the legal date or nature of the partnership dissolution, nor does the Fifth/Main description independently map the undertaking business to 503 or 505.

This wording change should be bracketed more tightly by checking the August 3, 10, 17, and 24 issues page-by-page.

No reliable exact-address August listing for **503 Main** or **505 Main** has yet been recovered.

## Significance to the target block

Across the first eight months of 1916, the newspaper evidence repeatedly stabilizes the neighboring odd-numbered sequence at 507/509/511 and a Fifth/Main undertaking business, yet still does not produce a defensible exact 503 or 505 occupant. Combined with the separate 1916–1917 water-rent ledger evidence, this strengthens the need to distinguish:

- absence from a recurring newspaper business directory;
- absence of a separate municipal water account;
- actual vacancy or non-use.

Those are not equivalent conclusions.
