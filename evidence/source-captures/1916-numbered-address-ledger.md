## 1916 address-grid observations

1. **502, 504, 512 and 528 are now direct 1916 even-numbered anchors.** This materially improves reconstruction of the opposite side of Main Street around the unresolved odd-numbered 503/505 frontage.
2. **504 Main had a pool and billiard parlor by Dec. 1916.** C. C. Wade's notice proves the address and identifies Sam Oldstein as the seller of an interest. This is separate from the Raasch & Lamb pool-hall relocation to the Andresen Building unless evidence later links the businesses.
3. **512 Main is strongly established for Williams Bros. Transfer & Storage across 1916.** This also corrects the earlier ledger ambiguity around a June reference to 612 Main: Williams Bros.' repeated house advertising clearly prints **512 Main**, so any 612 transcription for Williams Bros. should be treated cautiously and checked against the scan.
4. **612 Main still appears to have multiple other business occupants/transitions in 1916** (optical/florist evidence), but Williams Bros. should no longer be used as evidence that 612 itself was necessarily multi-occupancy.
5. **506, 508, 510, 514 and 524 have useful earlier/later anchors but not yet direct 1916 exact-address proof.** Do not carry an occupant forward merely because the address is stable in another year.
6. The numbered-address ledger is useful for testing OCR errors: an isolated `602 Main` reading for Champion Smith & Telford on 27 Apr 1916 conflicts with many clean `502 Main` entries and is treated as an OCR error unless a scan proves otherwise.
7. Street-address evidence does **not** automatically map one numbered storefront to one original platted legal lot. The Block 26 lot-to-address problem remains separate.
