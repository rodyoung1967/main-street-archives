## 1916 address-grid observations

1. **502 Main is now directly carried through 26 Oct 1916.** The Oct. 26 Courier business panel still prints Champion Smith & Telford at **502 Main Street**, extending that firm's exact-address continuity well beyond the summer.
2. **No direct 503 or 505 exact-address result has yet been recovered in the August–October targeted OCR passes.** This is not negative evidence; the scans are not visually complete and OCR may omit display ads or damaged type.
3. **506 Main has a longer documented address history but still lacks direct 1916 proof.** The address appears with Yokohama Clothing Co. in 1905, as a temporary real-estate office in 1911, and with Terk Bros. by Oct. 1918. The 1918 profile's statement that Terk Bros. had owned the furniture store `for years` makes them a substantially stronger 1916 candidate than before, but the project should not convert that retrospective wording into a 1916 fact without a contemporary source or the 1916–17 directory.
4. **612 Main is conclusively not a one-business address in 1916.** Williams Bros. Transfer & Storage and W. F. Schooley & Co. are both independently and repeatedly advertised at **612 Main** during overlapping months. Schilling also moved there in June, a successor optometrist appears there by late September, and Wilkinson also uses 612. The city directory and original scans are needed to determine the internal/shared-address arrangement.
5. **Schilling's 612 tenure was short.** He moved from 617 to 612 in June, but by 28 September another optometrist was already advertising at 612 as his successor.
6. **619 Main anchors the Andresen Building in 1916.** This materially weakens any attempt to equate the Raasch & Lamb Andresen-Building pool hall with the separate 504 Main Oldstein/Wade pool hall.
7. **504 Main has a documented amusement/commercial sequence:** glass-blower exhibition (1906), Electric Theatre (1912–1913), pool/billiard parlor (Dec. 1916).
8. **A. A. Mundin/Munden has a 514-versus-614 conflict in October 1918.** Because the 1918 feature also says he had been in business nearly two years, resolving this conflict could yield a useful late-1916 address anchor.
9. **508, 510 and 524 have useful earlier/later anchors but not yet direct 1916 exact-address proof.**
10. Street-address evidence does **not** automatically map one numbered storefront to one original platted legal lot.
