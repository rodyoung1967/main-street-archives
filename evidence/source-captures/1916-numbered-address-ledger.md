## 1916 address-grid observations

1. **502 and 528 are now strong 1916 even-numbered anchors.** Both appear repeatedly over many months, giving useful control points on the opposite side of Main Street from 503/505/507/509/511.
2. **612 Main is a multi-occupancy / transition address in 1916.** Williams Bros. Transfer & Storage, opticians, and later Wilkinson florist appear at that number in contemporary advertising; this may reflect suites, shared premises, sequential occupancy, or OCR/page-context issues and should be resolved before assuming one storefront = one business.
3. **506, 508 and 510 have useful earlier/later anchors but not yet direct 1916 exact-address proof.** Do not carry an occupant forward merely because the address is stable in an earlier year.
4. The numbered-address ledger is useful for testing OCR errors: an isolated `602 Main` reading for Champion Smith & Telford on 27 Apr 1916 conflicts with many clean `502 Main` entries and is treated as an OCR error unless a scan proves otherwise.
5. Street-address evidence does **not** automatically map one numbered storefront to one original platted legal lot. The Block 26 lot-to-address problem remains separate.
