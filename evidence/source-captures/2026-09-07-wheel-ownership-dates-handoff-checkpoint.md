# Controlling 503 / Wheel chronology

## 1951–1957 — Flor / Remus / Kwality predecessor chain

Current repository synthesis supports a strong but not fully direct continuity hypothesis around Albert D. Flor / Margaret (Marge) Remus / Kwality Cafe:

- **5 Dec. 1951:** official City searchable text names **Albert D. Flor and Margaret Remus** among approved liquor applicants; premises/trade name are not stated and visual rendering remains a gap.
- **Sep. 1953:** official City text names **`Al & Marge Kwality Cafe`**; surnames/address are not printed in that line.
- The City's 902 Fifth historic inventory says **Fred Remus was a cook at Kwality Cafe in 1953**.
- The 503 historic-resource synthesis places **Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor** managing a restaurant/cocktail-bar operation at 503 in **1957**.

Classification remains **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE CONTINUITY HYPOTHESIS**, not direct proof that every one of these lines is the same legal ownership/license relationship.

## 28 March 1958 — earliest currently recovered exact-address Wheel ad

`S-285` / `E-259` is a **VISUALLY VERIFIED** *Clackamas County News* advertisement reading:

- **WHEEL CAFE & LOUNGE**
- **503 Main St.**
- **Ph: 9106**

This is the earliest currently recovered exact-address Wheel advertisement. It does **not** name the proprietor/licensee and is not an opening or sale date.

Current documented transition bracket:

**1957 Flor same-address restaurant/cocktail-bar control → by 28 Mar. 1958 Wheel Cafe & Lounge at 503.**

The mechanism remains unresolved: rename, sale, continuation under a new trade name, or another arrangement are all still possible.

## 1960 / mid-1960s — Wheel continuity

- A **30 Nov. 1960 Oregonian** item (`E-044`) names the Wheel Cafe at Fifth and Main.
- CCHS mid-1960s imagery (`P-4565`, `S-220` / `E-200` / `IMG-0749`) supports Wheel signage/motifs at 503.
- A second CCHS bluff view (`P-1311`, `S-232` / `E-206` / `IMG-0753`) supports The Wheel at 503 while the separate 505 Tavern is visible next door; exact exposure year is unresolved.

- it belongs to the **preceding meeting**;
- exact preceding-meeting date remains unresolved;
