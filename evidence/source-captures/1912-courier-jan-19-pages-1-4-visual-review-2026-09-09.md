## Useful non-target Oregon City context observed

### Willamette River high-water event — page 1

The page-1 article **“UP AND GOING SOME.”** reports high Willamette River conditions that had forced many departments at the large mills to close temporarily, putting about **1,200 workers** off for several days. It states that the rising river began coming in at the **end of Main Street**, that workmen built a plank barrier, that Hawley pulp mill A was surrounded, that the street-car tracks between Oregon City and Canemah were submerged, and that Abernethy Creek was out of its banks.

Classification: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT, VISUALLY VERIFIED — NON-TARGET CITY / INFRASTRUCTURE CONTEXT**.

This does not by itself place floodwater at 501–511 Main and is not propagated to a target-building event.

### Portland–Oregon City river-channel proposal — page 1

The page-1 article **“TO DREDGE THE CHANNEL.”** reports a Secretary of War recommendation for a **$24,000** appropriation to dredge a six-foot Willamette River channel from Portland to Oregon City, based on Major Mclndoe’s survey. The article describes a proposed channel width of 200 feet to Clackamas Rapids and 100 feet above that point, removal of County’s Rock, and estimated annual maintenance of **$3,500**. It also reports Congressman Hawley’s work for river improvements above Oregon City.

Classification: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT, VISUALLY VERIFIED — NON-TARGET TRANSPORTATION / INFRASTRUCTURE CONTEXT**.

This is retained as Oregon City context only; it does not establish a 503/505 property fact.
