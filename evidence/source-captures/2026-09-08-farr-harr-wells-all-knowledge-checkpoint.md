## Canonical documentary sequence

### 1936 — Farr's Pool Hall

CCHS photograph `P-1128`, combined with the City's independent Farr/505 identification, strongly supports **Farr's Pool Hall operating at 505 Main** in the 1936 photographed state. Edwin F. Farr and Alice Farr are the relevant Farr identities. Clarence P. Farr / 708 Monroe remains a resolved false path and must not be merged with Edwin F. Farr.

### 1945 — Harr's Recreation Center

The visually verified May 2000 Oregon SHPO resource 30539 survey explicitly says:

**“The 1945 city directory lists Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main.”**

This verifies the survey wording only. The underlying directory title/page is still unrecovered, so the 1945 point remains **official-secondary directory synthesis**, not a visually inspected original directory entry.

### 11 February 1948 — Harr pool-hall operator

Oregon City Commission indexed/searchable primary text directly names **Harr** and **McDougald** as pool-hall operators. The sentence itself does not provide Harr's first name, business name, or address.

### Late 1948 — direct Harr / 505 anchor

Separate municipal liquor-license text directly identifies:

**Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street.**

The defensible date remains **after 23 November 1948 and before 3 January 1949** because the opening meeting-header page is missing. Do not assign an exact December 1948 date.

### 9 November 1949 — bounded five-license renewal batch

The recovered municipal batch lists five beer-license renewals and does not include 505 / Harr / Well-Wells. This is **bounded coverage only** and is not evidence that Harr left, that 505 lacked a license, or that Well's had arrived.

### 1950 — mapped use, operator unnamed

The 1950 Sanborn maps 505 as **billiards / cigars / beer**. This establishes the use class, not the proprietor or licensee.

### 6 April 1950 — one-off pool-hall license procedure control

A standalone municipal liquor-license approval names **Walter F. Nebert — Pastime Poolhall**. This is not a 505 event. Its value is procedural: Oregon City could record a pool-hall licensee/operator substitution in a single-business action outside a large annual batch.

### 5 December 1951 — bounded nineteen-applicant batch

The recovered indexed municipal liquor-license batch contains nineteen applicants and does not include 505 / Harr / Well-Wells. This remains **bounded coverage only**, not negative evidence.

### 10 October 1952 — standalone exact-address liquor application control

Oregon City Commission indexed primary text records a single liquor-license application for **Seid's Restaurant, 517 Main Street**. This is not 505 evidence, but it confirms that small exact-address in-year approvals occurred.

### 1953 — Well's Recreation

The visually verified SHPO 30539 survey says the **1953 city directory lists “Well's Recreation” at 505 Main**. Preserve that exact survey spelling. The underlying directory page, proprietor/operator, phone number, exact opening date, and relationship to Harr remain unresolved.

### 20 November 1955 — unnamed beer/pool business sale advertisement

The original `Eugene Register-Guard` JP2 is preserved and visually verified. It advertises a long-established beer/pool business at **505 Main Street**, with roughly 50 kegs/month, bottled beer/wine, three pool tables, owner-controlled amusement machines, and contact **Phone 9179**.

The ad does **not** identify the seller, proprietor, trade name, liquor licensee, buyer, or telephone subscriber. It does not prove that the business was still named Well's Recreation or that a sale actually closed.

### 1965 — 505 Tavern
