- **10 May Main Street demolition/rebuilding:** the *Oregon City Courier* directly states that buildings on Main Street were then being torn down and new buildings erected in their places (`E-090` / `S-105`). This remains citywide Main Street structural-change evidence only; it is **not** mapped to 501/503/505/507 or the Fifth–Sixth frontage without an independent bridge (`RL-038`).

- **Harding/Willamette geometry:** later September 1912 evidence places the Willamette Building over Harding Drug Store (`E-138` / `S-154`), confirming that building-name/address relationships are more complex than a simple one-building/one-number equation.
- **19 January Courier:** pages 1–8 are visually verified; page 8 directly states Percy Cross accepted a position in Harding drugstore during A. E. Wilmot's absence (`E-282` / `S-310`). No street number is imported into that item.
- **26 April cross-street control:** the Oregon City Shoe Store is visually verified at **508 Main St. — Opposite Postoffice**. This strongly supports the earlier Post Office being on the opposite odd/west side of Main, but does **not** prove the exact Post Office bay or number. The later 1918 Post Office relocation remains a separate chronology.
- **505 Main:** no direct exact-number 1912 occupant is currently established in the annual synthesis. The Chicago Store's 1911 exact 505 address is not mechanically carried forward merely because its 5 January 1912 advertisement says `Main St. just below Postoffice`.

Exact-address occupancy remains separate from surviving-building identity. None of the 1912 address evidence proves that the later surviving 503/505 building fabric was already standing.
