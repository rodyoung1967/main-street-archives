# 1928–1935 Clackamas County assumed-business-name route — Farr's Pool Hall / 505 Main

Date: 3 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL ARCHIVAL HOLDINGS VERIFIED — TARGET CERTIFICATE NOT YET RETRIEVED.**

Purpose: preserve a high-value archival route for determining when **Farr's Pool Hall** became an identifiable business at **505 Main Street**, while avoiding any inference that Edwin F. Farr operated the unnamed 1925 billiards/cigars occupancy before a contemporary record proves it.

## Official holdings verified

The Oregon Secretary of State's **Clackamas County Inventory** in the Oregon Historical County Records Guide identifies an **Assumed Business Names Registers** series covering **1928–1964**.

Official guide:
https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/county-records-guide/Pages/clackamas-inventory.aspx

The guide says the series documents registration of business names with the county clerk or recorder and may contain:

- assumed business name;
- description of business;
- filing date;
- business owner;
- notary public;
- witness;
- clerk or recorder; and
- deputy name.

It also says the series may include an alphabetical index by business name.

Most importantly for this project, the guide identifies the following original holding at the **Oregon State Archives**:

> **Assumed Business Names [Certificates], 1928-1935 (.25 cu.ft.).**

That exact date span overlaps the unresolved interval between the visually verified **1925 Sanborn 505 Main billiards/cigars use** and Oregon City's later official identification of **Edwin F. and Alice Farr as proprietors of Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main**.

## Why this is a high-priority bridge

The current 505 chronology has three important anchors:

1. **1925:** 505 Main is directly mapped as **billiards / cigars**, but the operator is unnamed.
2. **Later official historic-resource evidence:** Edwin F. and Alice Farr are identified as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main**. The same source says they acquired their 401 Third Street residence in **1934**, but that residence date does not prove the pool hall was already operating in 1934.
3. **1945 / late 1948:** Harr's Recreation Center becomes the later documented recreation business at 505.

If a surviving 1928–1935 assumed-business-name certificate exists for **Farr's Pool Hall**, **Edwin F. Farr**, or a predecessor trade name, it could supply a contemporary filing date and owner identity inside the exact gap that remains unresolved.

The archive guide does **not** promise that every certificate contains a numbered street address. Therefore an assumed-name record could materially narrow the business chronology without by itself proving the 505 premises. Any address must be transcribed only if present on the original certificate or a related contemporary record.

## Online-index limitation

The Oregon State Archives' **Oregon Historical Records Index** describes the record types currently indexed for Clackamas County. Its Clackamas County list includes items such as marriages, naturalizations, deaths, divorces and other record groups, but **does not list assumed business names** as an indexed Clackamas record type.

Index information:
https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/pages/about-ohri.aspx

Therefore the current public name index is **not an established online search path** to these 1928–1935 certificates. This is a retrieval limitation only; it is not evidence that a Farr certificate does not exist.

## Exact retrieval route

The Oregon State Archives states that it provides remote reference services and accepts orders for copies of records in its custody.

Reference/contact information:
https://sos.oregon.gov/Pages/contact-us.aspx
https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/records/pages/order.aspx

Reference Unit:
- phone: **503-373-0701, option 1**
- email: **Reference.Archives@sos.oregon.gov**

A focused request should ask staff to search the Clackamas County **Assumed Business Names [Certificates], 1928–1935 (.25 cu.ft.)** for:

- **Farr's Pool Hall** / **Farrs Pool Hall**;
- **Edwin F. Farr**;
- **Edwin Franklin Farr**;
- **Alice Farr**;
- any assumed business name filed by Edwin F. Farr describing a **pool hall, billiards, cigars, recreation, or similar business** in Oregon City; and
- if an alphabetical index survives with the certificates, the complete **Farr** entries and their certificate references.

If a match is found, request a scan of the **entire certificate, front and back**, plus any index entry or associated filing/withdrawal document, preserving the exact business name, filing date, owner name, business description, and any address.

## What this does NOT establish

This archival-holdings checkpoint does **not** establish:

- that Farr's Pool Hall filed an assumed-business-name certificate;
- that Edwin or Alice Farr filed any certificate in 1928–1935;
- that Farr was the unnamed 1925 billiards/cigars operator at 505;
- that the Farr business began in 1928, 1934, or any other specific year;
- that an assumed-name certificate, if found, will include 505 Main;
- ownership of the 505 building or land; or
- the Farr-to-Harr transfer date.

## Identity controls

Keep the following identities/businesses separate unless direct evidence connects them:

- **Edwin F. / Edwin Franklin Farr** and Alice Farr;
- **Farr Brothers**;
- **C. P. Farr**;
- Timms Pool Hall;
- Leland & Little;
- Ed Wheeler's pool room;
- McAnulty & Barry; and
- Harr's Recreation Center.

Name similarity alone is not a merger.
