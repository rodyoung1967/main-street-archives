## Result

No 1921 regional-newspaper result surfaced in this pass that securely identifies a 501/503/505/505½ Main occupant, ownership event, rebuild/demolition event, or target business beyond evidence already known from the Oregon City Enterprise.

Search results repeatedly collapsed back to:
- 1921 Enterprise Clem Dollar social/local items, which do not establish a target address;
- the already verified 1921 Enterprise Dr. Freeze 505½ Main advertisements;
- 1922 Banner-Courier / Morning Register fire and post-fire material, which is useful chronological control but is outside 1921.

The 19 July 1922 Eugene Morning Register regional report confirms the following day's cross-city reporting potential: it reported the Oregon City Main Street fire and named the Fifth Street Restaurant and Miller shoe shop. This supports continuing regional-newspaper searches, but it must not be back-projected into 1921.

## Control finding from 1922 Banner-Courier

A 19 Jan. 1922 Banner-Courier Dr. Freeze advertisement is OCR-corrupted as “605J4 Main St.” while the ad is part of the same 505½-address advertising sequence. This reinforces the known OCR failure mode around the half-number and the need for visual verification.

By 19 Oct. 1922 Banner-Courier, Freeze advertised at **207-8 Masonic Bldg.**, providing a later-location control. No inference is made here about the exact move date.
