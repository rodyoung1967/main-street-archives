### 28 January 1921, page 8 — historic-photo display lead

A locally relevant article, **“B. & A. Picture Display Viewed With Interest,”** reports that George Hankins arranged a Burmeister & Andresen display window containing a loaned collection of photographs of Oregon City “in former years.” The article says the photographs drew crowds interested in changes to the city and recognizable people.

This does **not** state that the collection included the Fifth/Main target frontage, and the newspaper page does not reproduce the collection. It is nevertheless a useful **UNVERIFIED LEAD** for provenance research: a surviving Burmeister & Andresen / George Hankins-associated photograph collection, if identifiable in CCHS/OHS/local holdings, could potentially contain pre-1922 Main Street views.
