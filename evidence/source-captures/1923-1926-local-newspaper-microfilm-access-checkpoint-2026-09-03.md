## Official Oregon City Public Library holding confirmation

The Oregon City Public Library's current **Microfilm Scanning System** page directly lists the surviving local holdings:

- **Oregon City Enterprise Weekly — 10 November 1871 through 25 December 1942**
- **Oregon City Banner Courier — 17 July 1919 through 28 February 1950**

Official source:
https://www.orcity.org/490/Microfilm-Scanning-System

The same page states that the library has a ScanPro 3000 microfilm system, that users may print or save images to USB, and directs questions about specific publication dates to the library reference department. This is a confirmed custody/access route, not a claim that every issue/page survives without gaps.

## Why the online local run stops at 1922

The Oregon City Public Library's **Digitizing Historic Oregon City Newspapers** page states that its Early Oregon Voices project digitized Oregon City newspapers through **1922**. It specifically lists:

- **Oregon City Enterprise — 3 April 1891 through 29 December 1922** in the digitized project coverage;
- **The Banner-Courier — 5 January 1922 through 28 December 1922** in the digitized project coverage.

Official source:
https://www.orcity.org/475/Digitizing-Historic-Oregon-City-Newspape

Therefore the absence of 1923–1926 local issues in the checked Historic Oregon Newspapers calendars is now explained by the documented digitization boundary while the later local newspapers survive on library microfilm. This is an **access-status conclusion**, not evidence that any target business did or did not operate.

Oregon Digital Newspaper Program's title histories independently confirm that the underlying publications continued well beyond 1922:

- Banner-Courier title history: 1919–1950 — https://odnp.uoregon.edu/oregon-city-oregon-city-courier/
- Oregon City Enterprise title history: 1891–1950 — https://odnp.uoregon.edu/oregon-city-oregon-city-enterprise/

### Morning Enterprise title/run clarification — 3 September continuation

A separate follow-up tested whether the **Morning Enterprise** might expose an overlooked online 1925–1926 run under its distinct LCCN `sn00063701`. Oregon Digital Newspaper Program's title history confirms that the daily Morning Enterprise itself was published from **1911 through 1933**. However, Historic Oregon Newspapers' current **All Titles** inventory lists only **913 digitized issues for this LCCN, with online coverage from 8 January 1911 through 23 December 1913**.

Sources:
- Title record: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063701/
- All Titles inventory: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/newspapers/

Therefore the Morning Enterprise does **not** provide a hidden digitized 1923–1926 local run. Its long publication span must not be confused with the much shorter online holdings span. The manual microfilm conclusion below remains unchanged.
