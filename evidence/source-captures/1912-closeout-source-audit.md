# 1912 Main Street source closeout audit

Date audited: 21 August 2026

Purpose: preserve the primary-source checks used to close the 1912 year pass, including corrections and unresolved conflicts that should not be flattened into unsupported conclusions.

Important OCR warning: machine text can misread the address digits; the visual page is controlling for the numbered-address transcription.

## 2. Morning Enterprise — 24 March 1912 — page 2

Canonical page:
`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063701/1912-03-24/ed-1/seq-2/`

Searchable OCR:
`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063701/1912-03-24/ed-1/seq-2/ocr/`

The page contains a **Notice of Application for Pool Hall License** stating that the applicant intended to apply to operate a pool hall at **503 Main Street** for three months. The OCR renders the signature as a mangled form of **H. H. Smith**.

### Citation correction

The existing `S-024` register entry cites **31 March 1912, page 4**. The directly verified searchable primary copy is **24 March 1912, page 2**. Until the 31 March reference is independently reverified, use the 24 March page as the verified citation for this notice.

### Identity conflict

The 5 January advertisement prints **H. N. Smith** at 503 Main, while this March notice is recorded as **H. H. Smith**. Do not silently normalize the initials or state that these are certainly the same person. Possible explanations include a newspaper/typesetting error, OCR error, initial variation, or different operators; additional evidence is required.

## 3. M. E. Dunn — Post Office adjacency

The 5 January *Courier* places **M. E. Dunn** near the Post Office. *Morning Enterprise* advertisements during 1912 repeatedly describe the confectionery as **next door to the P.O.**

This materially strengthens adjacency but still does **not** provide a numbered address. Therefore **515 Main remains only a hypothesis** contingent on separately proving the Post Office at 513 and accounting for Harding at 511.

## 4. Oregon City Courier — 20 December 1912 — page 8 — Schrader sale

Canonical page:
`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-12-20/ed-1/seq-8/`

Searchable OCR:
`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-12-20/ed-1/seq-8/ocr/`

The report states that **Herman Schrader**, after about seven years in the local baking business, sold his two bakeries: one at **Seventh and John Adams streets** and the other on **Main Street**. The purchasers were **Wemmo & Haas**. Miss Marjory Schrader was to remain with the new firm until January 1.

This establishes the late-1912 business transition but does **not** supply the numbered address of the Main Street bakery.

## 5. 1912 closeout rule

The 1912 archival pass can be treated as complete with the following questions deliberately left open:

1. Exact numbered address of the Oregon City Post Office / Postoffice Building.
2. Exact numbered address of M. E. Dunn.
3. Exact numbered address of Schrader's Main Street bakery / Wemmo & Haas.
4. Whether H. N. Smith and H. H. Smith are the same person.
5. Precise geographic meaning of the Chicago Store phrase "just below Postoffice."

These are research leads, not reasons to manufacture an address. The next chronological year can proceed while these remain open.
