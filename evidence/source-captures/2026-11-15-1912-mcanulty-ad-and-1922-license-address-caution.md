# 1912 McAnulty advertisement and 1922 license — address-source caution

**Research date:** 15 November 2026. **1922 status:** IN PROGRESS. **1912 newspaper state:** OCR/TEXT ONLY — visual page image not retrieved.

## New historical search lead
The **5 January 1912 Oregon City Courier p.6** contains a multi-column montage of individual merchant advertisements. Searchable PDF text includes **'J. W. McANULTY'**, **'Fine Cigars and Tobacco'**, **'Corner Main and 7th'**, and **'Main Street near 7th'** in a nearby run of OCR text. **Crucial layout caveat:** OCR interleaves adjacent independent advertising boxes. **Do not assign either street location to McAnulty without visually inspecting the full page.** A screenshot attempt failed in this research pass. Original: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-01-05/ed-1/seq-6.pdf

The advertisement establishes a **1912 McAnulty surname/business-name search lead**, not identity with the later McAnulty & Barry partnership and not a proved 1912 street address.

## Later corroborating, not retroactive, 714 Main association
Oregon Historic Sites Database Petzold Building, **714 Main**, reports a **McAnulty family restaurant/confectionery 1924–1930** and **Barry and McAnulty pool-hall ownership in Petzold family records 1936–1938**. Source: https://heritagedata.prd.state.or.us/historic/index.cfm?do=v.dsp_siteSummary&resultDisplay=29986

A separate Oregon City historic-planning document describes the McAnulty restaurant as 1924–1930 and a pool hall in the early 1930s. Source: https://mccmeetingspublic.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/oregoncity-meet-d021bf79df8749c08a26e185885e3dc2/ITEM-Attachment-002-6b3987c14854445e853df3a53d57b779.pdf

These records are **secondary historic inventory** and do not date the 1922 pool-hall room or prove its address. Preserve the 1922 November 30 newspaper license **grant** separately from the later 714 occupancy.
