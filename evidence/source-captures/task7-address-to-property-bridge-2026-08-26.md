The matching name, Portland residence wording, dates, Main/Fifth cluster, and Harding-sale context make Wolf's newly reported remainder the strongest current property chain for the fire-origin building.

The source still gives no numbered address, deed description or exact boundary.

Classification: **STRONG INFERENCE** for continuity from Harding's reported remainder to Wolf's fire-origin building; **UNRESOLVED** for exact 501/503/505 number.
