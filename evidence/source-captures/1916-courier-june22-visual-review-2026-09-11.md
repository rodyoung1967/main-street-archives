## Raasch & Lamb permit dispute

Page 1 materially strengthens the municipal chronology already hinted by the May 18 permission and June 1 rearrangement coverage. The printed article states that the council had recently voted a temporary permit to **Raasch and Lamb, pool hall operators, for the removal of their business to the basement of the Andresen Building**. It says the council had promised an ordinance covering the move after expiration of the three-month permit, but then refused to recognize that pledge when an ordinance was presented for the pool-hall owners by W. A. Dimick. Dimick accused the council of changing faith and forcing a legitimate business from the city. The article also states that the existing ordinance required pool or billiard halls to be on the ground floor and visible from the street.

This establishes a permit and policy dispute. It does **not** state that Raasch & Lamb actually completed the basement move, and no street number is printed for the pool-hall business.
