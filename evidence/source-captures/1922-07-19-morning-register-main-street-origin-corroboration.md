# 19 July 1922 Morning Register — independent Main Street fire-origin corroboration

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED from scanned newspaper page**

Purpose: preserve an independent contemporary wire report that directly locates the July 1922 fire's origin in an unoccupied **Main Street** building and independently confirms the core Fifth Street destruction cluster.

## Source

**Morning Register (Eugene), 19 July 1922, p. 1**

Direct scanned page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088115/1922-07-19/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

The actual scanned page was visually inspected. The relevant short item appears in the lower-right portion of page 1 under the heading **“Oregon City Has $30,000 Fire.”**

## Directly supported facts

The report states that:

- the fire was discovered early on 18 July in an **unoccupied building on Main Street** in Oregon City's older business section;
- the Oregon City Laundry plant was destroyed;
- the May & Washburn/May & Washburne garage with two automobiles was destroyed;
- the Fifth Street restaurant was destroyed;
- the Miller shoe shop was destroyed;
- the H. Ketchner home/confectionery store was destroyed.

The wire wording varies slightly from the fuller 20 July Banner-Courier account in spelling and loss estimate, but the spatial sequence is independently consistent.

## Why this matters

The 20 July Banner-Courier already identifies the ignition room as the vacant former Alex Schieremann tailor shop and says its building was owned by **S. Wolf of Portland**. This independent 19 July report adds a separate contemporary statement that the **unoccupied ignition building itself was on Main Street**.

That strengthens the project conclusion that the fire started in a Main Street storefront/premises rather than merely somewhere within the broader Fifth/Main cluster.

It still does **not** identify the exact numbered storefront as 501, 503, or 505 Main.

## Related Banner-Courier detail

The fuller 20 July Banner-Courier report also identifies the destroyed **Fifth Street restaurant as owned by Ed. Blake**. That proprietor detail is useful for continued reconstruction of the pre-fire Fifth Street cluster, but no exact street number for Blake's restaurant has yet been recovered.
