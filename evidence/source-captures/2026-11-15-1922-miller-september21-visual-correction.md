# 21 September 1922 Banner-Courier p. 5 — Gilbert Miller / 209 Fifth ad-boundary correction

**Date inspected:** 15 November 2026  
**Source:** *The Banner-Courier*, Oregon City, 21 September 1922, p. 5, sequence 5.  
**Canonical scan:** https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-09-21/ed-1/seq-5.pdf  
**Repository PDF:** `newspapers/banner-courier/1922-09/1922-09-21-seq-05.pdf`  
**Evidence state:** **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT**, **VISUALLY VERIFIED** original newspaper page.

## Visual layout finding
The upper-right page area has two separately boxed advertisements. The upper box, headed **I Have Sold Out**, contains the statement that Gilbert Miller has sold out hardware, brought in workingmen's clothing/shoes/gloves, and set up his shoe machinery to resume the same shoe repairing as before the fire. It is signed **GILBERT MILLER**, followed by **Formerly Goldman's Second-Hand Store**. **There is no street address in this box.**

The separate, lower, independently bordered box is headed **PORTLAND OVERTIRE CO.** and **209 Fifth Street**, **Oregon City**. The 209 Fifth address belongs to the Overtire company, not Gilbert Miller. The page OCR reading order had concatenated text from adjacent boxes, producing the erroneous attribution in earlier project interpretation.

## Corrected interpretation
- **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT:** Gilbert Miller was advertising the resumption of his pre-fire shoe-repair work by 21 September 1922.
- **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT:** Portland Overtire Co. advertised at **209 Fifth Street** on the same page.
- **SUPERSEDED:** The assertion that Gilbert Miller operated at 209 Fifth Street on 21 September 1922. No evidence for a Miller relocation to that address remains in this advertisement.
- **Separate earlier visual evidence:** Miller's Shoe Store at **419 Main** on 24 August and 26 October 1922. September 7 page-2 OCR also reports 419 Main and a Gilbert Miller signature; its fresh enlarged visual recheck remains pending.
- This does **not** establish a precise pre-fire 503 street number or a new construction date.
