## What is established

### 1. Map / physical-change control

- The target downtown frontage is on **1911 Sanborn Sheet 16**, not Sheet 22.
- Direct comparison of the 1911 target sheet and 1925 Sheet 6 shows a materially changed 501/503/505 storefront/footprint arrangement by 1925.
- Fifth Street is printed as **60 feet wide on both target Sanborn sheets**. The maps do not support a 60→50 foot street-width explanation for the footprint change.
- The maps establish a **physical-change bracket**, not the date of each demolition/rebuild.
- City historic-survey provenance has since been clarified: 503's **c.1919** is an approximate official survey estimate; 505's old **c.1908** survey value is superseded on the City's own page by a **1911–1925** Sanborn-based construction bracket, with **c.1920 tentative**. Neither is primary construction proof.

### 2. Wolf & Miller / Gilbert Miller chronology

- A visually verified 1916 Springfield newspaper directly names **Gilbert Miller of the Wolf & Miller Harness shop** (`S-125` / `E-113`; `P-046`).
- Existing verified 1918 Springfield material shows Gilbert Miller leaving Springfield for Oregon City, consistent with the later Oregon City chronology. Preserve the contemporary **C. E. Miller / Gilbert Miller** naming conflict where it appears; do not erase it by inference.
- Wolf & Miller is directly documented at **505 Main, Oregon City in October 1918** (`E-027`).
- The 11 July 1919 *Oregon City Enterprise* page was later visually verified and promoted to `S-160` / `E-144`: the Miller family was living at **Ninth and Division** and had come to Oregon City from **Springfield about a year earlier**.
- `E-144` is household migration/residence evidence only. It does **not** itself name Wolf & Miller, place Gilbert at 505, establish ownership, or date building work.
- Do not merge the unidentified Wolf partner in Wolf & Miller with **S. Wolf of Portland** (`P-035`) or the later reported 503 Wolf-family owners without a direct contemporary/deed/family bridge.

### 3. 1919 cleaner/dyer location control

The unnamed cleaner/dyer classified first visually verified on 1 August 1919 was then traced through the weekly run. `S-151` / `S-152` strengthen `E-136`.

The same advertisement appears in **eight consecutive weekly Enterprise issues, 18 July–5 September 1919**. Visual comparison of the original scans resolves the OCR conflict: **every inspected scan prints `500`, not `600`**.

Stable wording includes:

- `500 Fifth and Main streets`
- `two doors south of new post office`
- `Phone 260`

Important interpretation rule:

- `500` is deliberate printed newspaper text, not an OCR hallucination.
- The unusual phrase has **not** been normalized to either modern `500 Main` or `500 Fifth`.
- The advertiser has **not** been identified as Pantorium. Pantorium was documented at 500 Main in 1918 but used a different telephone number (**185-J** versus this ad's **260**).
- The repeated ad is a spatial/occupancy clue only until Phone 260 and the address syntax are independently bridged.

### 4. 1920 pool-hall chronology

`S-150` / `E-135` visually verify three relevant *Enterprise* pages:

- **13 February 1920:** **Clem Dollar** is proprietor of a pool hall on Main **between Fifth and Sixth**. No numbered address is printed.
- **4 June 1920:** the City Council report says **A. Leland's application to operate a pool hall at 505½ Main Street and erect an electric street sign was granted**. The report describes the Wednesday meeting corresponding to **2 June 1920**.
- **3 December 1920:** a pool hall on Main between Fifth and Sixth is again referenced at block level.

Do not merge these three pool-hall references into one continuous operation without additional evidence. Do not merge **A. Leland** with **O. H. Leland**, Timms, or later Leland records without an independent identity bridge.

The June approval establishes an exact usable/business premises at **505½ Main by June 1920**. It does **not** prove the surviving 505 building was constructed, rebuilt, or enlarged in 1920.

### 6. Broader occupancy chain retained

**Wolf & Miller at 505 (Oct. 1918) → repeated cleaner/dyer/new-Post-Office spatial clue (Jul.–Sep. 1919) → Clem Dollar block-level pool hall (Feb. 1920) → A. Leland approved at 505½ (Jun. 1920) → Dr. Freeze at 505/505½ in 1921–22 → 1925 Sanborn shows 505 billiards/cigars.**
