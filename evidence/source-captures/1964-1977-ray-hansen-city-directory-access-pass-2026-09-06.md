## Interpretation

The prior wording that no directory record had been found for Hansen should be read narrowly as **no target directory page has yet been inspected**. It must not be read as a claim that the directories contain no Ray Hansen reference.

Given the steward's recollection of roughly ten years of Hansen business ownership, the strongest lookup sequence is now:

1. **1963 telephone directory** — pre/c. acquisition control;
2. **1964 city directory** — early/post-acquisition control;
3. **1965 telephone directory** — same-year control against the first direct municipal 505 Tavern anchor;
4. **1968–69 / 1969–70 telephone directory** — midpoint control;
5. **1971 city + telephone directory** — mature-period control;
6. **1972 city + telephone directory** — same-year control against the direct 505 Tavern liquor renewal;
7. **1973 city + telephone directory** — likely transition-bracketing control if the remembered decade is approximately correct;
8. **1974–1975** — determine whether the published proprietor/address/telephone association changes while the 505 Tavern name continues.

Search each city directory in all relevant sections rather than relying only on the alphabetical personal listing. A proprietor may be visible under the business name, street address, telephone number, or occupation/employer cross-reference even if the personal entry does not state ownership.
