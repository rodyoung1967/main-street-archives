## Interpretation

The prior wording that no directory record had been found for Hansen should be read narrowly as **no target directory page had yet been inspected or recovered in the public-web pass**. It must not be read as a claim that the directories contain no Ray Hansen reference.

Given the steward's recollection of roughly ten years of Hansen business ownership, the highest-value sequence is now:

1. **1964** — early/post-acquisition control;
2. **1969** — midpoint control;
3. **1971** — mature-period control;
4. **1972** — same-year control against the direct municipal 505 Tavern renewal;
5. **1973** — likely transition-bracketing control if the remembered decade is approximately correct;
6. **1974–1976** — determine whether the published proprietor/address association changes while the 505 Tavern name continues.

Search each year in all relevant sections rather than relying only on the alphabetical personal listing. A proprietor may be visible under the business name, street address, telephone number, or occupation/employer cross-reference even if the personal entry does not state ownership.
