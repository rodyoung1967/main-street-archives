## 3. Little's Confectionary — CCHS MOT:P-549 metadata supplied by project steward

The project steward supplied the following Clackamas County Historical Society catalog metadata. The photograph itself was **not supplied in this conversation**, so this is preserved as **STEWARD-SUPPLIED INSTITUTIONAL CATALOG METADATA / IMAGE RETRIEVAL LEAD**, not as a visually verified photograph.

- **Name/Title:** `Little's Confectionary, 1908`
- **Entry/Object ID:** `MOT:P-549` (external museum identifier; do not confuse with project `P-###` person IDs)
- **Description:** `Little's Confectionary. Interior. 512 7th Street, Oregon City. Richard Friedrich and Walter Little in photos.`
- **Date made:** `1908`
- **Place:** Oregon City, Clackamas County, Oregon, United States
- **Medium:** Photographic Paper
- **Other numbers:** `B.0155.000250`; `B-0155-000250`

Historical consequence, bounded to the metadata:

- The catalog metadata moves the currently known institutional-photo anchor for **Little's Confectionary at 512 7th Street** back to **1908**, one year earlier than the existing City historic-inventory statement that Walter Little was operating a confectionery/ice-cream business there by 1909.
- The metadata explicitly names **Walter Little** and **Richard Friedrich** as appearing in the photographs.
- The metadata does **not by itself** state Walter Little's legal ownership of the business, and it does not state Richard Friedrich's role (partner, employee, relative, customer, or otherwise). Do not manufacture those relationships.
- The metadata should be considered alongside the existing Walter Little / W. L. Little identity control and the separate 1922 `Leland & Little` cigar/billiards evidence. It strengthens the chronology of Walter Little's association with confectionery activity but does not itself prove a continuous confectionery → billiards business succession or place `Leland & Little` at 505 Main.

## 4. Current Walter Little / Leland & Little chronology to retain

- Property-history synthesis already in the archive: Walter Little acquired the 512 7th property in **1898**.
- **1908:** CCHS `MOT:P-549` metadata now supplies an earlier cataloged interior-photo anchor for **Little's Confectionary, 512 7th Street**, naming Walter Little and Richard Friedrich in the photographs.
- **By 1909:** Oregon City's historic inventory says Little operated a confectionery/ice-cream business at 512 7th.
- **25 February 1922:** `Leland & Little` appears among Oregon City Portina cigar dealers; `Little, W. L.` is separately listed.
- **8 June 1922:** Walter Little, O. H. Leland and Timms Pool Hall appear as separate merchant-roster entries.
- **17 August 1922:** `LELAND AND LITTLE, Billiards` is visually verified.
- No recovered exact-address source yet places `Leland & Little` at 505 Main. The functional match with the 1925 Sanborn's **505 Main = billiards/cigars** remains a research lead, not an address proof.
