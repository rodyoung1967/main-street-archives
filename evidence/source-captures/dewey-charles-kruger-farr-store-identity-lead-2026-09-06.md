# Dewey Charles Kruger / Farr Store purchaser identity lead

Date reviewed: **6 September 2026**

Status: **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE IDENTITY LEAD / PROPERTY-PURCHASE BRIDGE NOT YET DIRECTLY RECOVERED.**

Purpose: test the identity and spelling behind Oregon City's 2002 historic-resource statement that **“Dewey Krueger”** bought the Farr Store property at **919 7th Street** from Clarence Farr in the mid-1940s. This pass does not recover the conveyance deed and therefore does not promote the purchaser identity to deed-level proof.

## Official City property-history statement

Oregon City's current historic-resource page and the underlying 2002 HPNW survey form for **919 7th Street / Farr Store** state that the Farr Brothers store remained there until the **mid-1940s**, when **Dewey Krueger** bought the property from Clarence Farr. The same synthesis says Krueger held the store until 1951 and sold it to **Lee Faust**.

Official sources:

- `https://www.orcity.org/2142/919-7th-Street`
- `https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/7877/919_7th`

Classification: **OFFICIAL SECONDARY PROPERTY-HISTORY SYNTHESIS**. The form does not provide the deed number or full purchaser name.

## 1918 direct full-name anchor: Dewey Charles Kruger

The *Oregon City Enterprise*, **11 October 1918**, directly lists **Dewey Charles Kruger, Parkplace** among men reporting for vocational/military training.

Source:

`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1918-10-11/ed-1/seq-1/`

This is a direct contemporary full-name anchor for a local **Dewey Charles Kruger**.

## 1918 Oregon City employment anchor

The *Oregon City Courier*, **19 December 1918**, reports that **Dewey Kruger** had resumed his former position with **Staats Hardware** after release from training in Portland.

Source:

`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-12-19/ed-1/seq-8/`

This independently places Dewey Kruger in Oregon City's commercial community shortly after the full-name training reference.

## 1933 family / Oregon City anchor

A University of Oregon alumni publication dated **11 January 1933** reports the birth of a son, **Dewey Charles Jr.**, to **Mr. and Mrs. Dewey Kruger (Mildred Hull, ex-'28) of Oregon City**.

Source:

`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/2004260239/1933-01-11/ed-1/seq-3.pdf`

This supplies an independent Oregon City adult/family anchor and connects the Dewey Kruger name to wife **Mildred Hull** and son **Dewey Charles Jr.**

## Cemetery control

A Mountain View Cemetery transcription for Oregon City lists:

- **KRUGER, Dewey C.** — death **20 October 1952**, age **55**, Unit J / Block 1 / Crypt 2 / Niche B;
- **KRUGER, Mildred** — death **17 October 1956**, age **49**, same Unit J / Block 1 / Crypt 2, Niche C.

Source:

`https://www.interment.net/data/us/or/clackamas/mt-view-cemetery/records-k-l.htm`

This is a secondary cemetery transcription, not a death certificate or obituary. Its same-crypt placement is consistent with the 1933 Dewey/Mildred household but should not by itself be used to prove marital identity.

## Spelling assessment: Kruger vs. Krueger

The strongest contemporary sources found in this pass consistently use **Kruger**:

- 1918: **Dewey Charles Kruger**;
- 1918: **Dewey Kruger** at Staats Hardware;
- 1933: **Dewey Kruger** with Mildred Hull and son Dewey Charles Jr.;
- cemetery transcription: **Dewey C. Kruger** and **Mildred Kruger**.

The 2002 City/HPNW historic-resource form instead uses **Dewey Krueger** for the mid-1940s Farr Store purchaser.

The current evidence therefore supports a **STRONG WORKING HYPOTHESIS** that the City form's “Dewey Krueger” is the local **Dewey Charles Kruger** documented in the contemporary record, with `Krueger` representing a spelling variant or later transcription. However, the project has not yet recovered a deed, assessment roll, directory entry tied to 919 7th, or other same-property source that prints both the purchaser and the parcel/address. Do not silently normalize the City form until that bridge is found.

## Important same-surname disambiguation

Oregon City municipal records from the early 1940s also contain **W. G. H. Krueger** in a mayoral role while **C. P. Farr** appears as commissioner. That is a separate named person and must not be merged with Dewey Charles Kruger merely because both surnames occur in the same city and period.

## Property research still required

The highest-value next record is the actual conveyance or parcel-history entry for the **mid-1940s Clarence Farr → Dewey Krueger/Kruger** transaction at the Farr Store property:

- current/historic property: **919 7th Street**;
- current City tax-map control: **22E31AD**, tax lot **5000**, Block **129**, Lots **3x / 4x**;
- later City permit control: APN **2-2E-31AD-05000**;
- modern Monroe-side address on the same APN: **709 Monroe**.

The deed or assessor ownership book should be checked under both **Kruger** and **Krueger**, and should establish:

1. exact grantor and grantee names;
2. recording / transaction date;
3. legal description;
4. whether Clarence Farr held title individually or through another ownership form;
5. whether the later 1951 transfer to Lee Faust follows the same parcel chain.

## Evidence boundary

This pass establishes a strong biographical identity candidate behind the historic-survey purchaser name. It does **not** prove that Dewey Charles Kruger was the legal grantee of 919 7th, does not resolve the exact mid-1940s purchase date, and does not prove that every Kruger/Krueger reference belongs to the same person.
