## Important methodological result

The Freeze series demonstrates that OCR corruption of half-number addresses is a major failure mode. The second pass must therefore inspect page images and search by:
- occupant surname without address;
- service/business terms;
- neighboring landmarks such as Post Office;
- multiple OCR approximations of half-number addresses.
