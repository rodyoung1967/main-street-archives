# 18 April 1925 — Main between Fifth and Sixth fire

Status: **primary municipal text recovered; direct page-image visual verification pending**

Purpose: preserve a high-value 1925 fire entry that may affect the 501/503/505 predecessor/surviving-building chronology while maintaining the archive's strict visual-review standard.

## Source

City of Oregon City, **1925 Municipal Activities Report**:
https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/6214/1925-Municipal-Activities-Report

Web/indexed extraction of the report's Fire Department table shows an entry for:

- **18 Apr 1925, 10:15 a.m.**
- **Main bet. 5th and 6th**
- apparent damage: **$2,500**
- the table extraction appears to associate a **spark from chimney** with this call, but column alignment must be checked visually before treating the cause as settled.

The same table includes nearby entries and hose/damage/cause columns. Because the city PDF is large and the current renderer did not provide a stable page image during this review, the exact column alignment has **not** yet been visually cleared.

## Current assessment

This is a strong primary-source textual lead and directly resolves the earlier question of whether the 1925 Municipal Activities Report contains a substantial fire on Main between Fifth and Sixth: **yes, the indexed municipal text does contain such an entry**.

However, do **not** yet state that the fire specifically damaged or destroyed:

- 501 Main;
- 503 Main;
- 505 Main;
- the Oregon City Laundry;
- any particular pool hall, restaurant, or storefront.

The municipal entry gives the block-level location only.

## Why this matters

The fire occurred only weeks before the May 1925 Sanborn map condition and within the same Fifth-to-Sixth Main Street frontage containing 501/503/505. If the exact building can be identified, it may help explain:

- post-1922 rebuilding in the block;
- differences between predecessor structures and surviving 503/505 building fabric;
- the c.1919 (503), c.1920 (505), and later 1940 (505) historic-resource date estimates;
- any changes visible between pre-1925 photographs and the 1925 Sanborn.

## Newspaper availability note

The Historic Oregon Newspapers digitization for the Banner-Courier currently does not provide a straightforward 1925 local issue for follow-up through the same online corpus used for 1922. Oregon City Public Library microfilm holdings cover the Banner-Courier through 1950 and may be the best route for a local newspaper account.
