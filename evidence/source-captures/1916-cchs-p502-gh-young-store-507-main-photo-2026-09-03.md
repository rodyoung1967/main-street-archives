## Visual review — VISUALLY VERIFIED

The actual JPEG was inspected, not merely OCRed. The storefront/interior display is densely filled with baskets, woven goods, rugs/textiles, a guitar, curios and other objects. Hand-painted lettering across the lower window reads **“G. H. Youngs Collection. Oregon City Ore.”** The image also visibly carries the **Clackamas County Historical Society** watermark.

The image itself does **not** visibly print **507 Main** or **1916**. Those two locators come from the institutional catalog metadata. Do not convert them into image-text claims.

## Relationship to the existing Young chronology

The archive already has direct exact-address evidence for G. H. Young at **507 Main in January 1912** (`E-092`) and again in a 1914 city-business listing. Earlier CCHS records `MOT:P-370` and `MOT:P-384`, saved as catalog leads in the MOT:P-391/Young review, also describe Young/Young's Curio Store at **507 Main in 1916**. MOT:P-502 is therefore an additional independent cataloged photograph supporting the **institutional-photo side** of that 1916 association.

It does **not** prove the photographed room survived unchanged from 1912, establish title/ownership, or prove that the photograph was exposed on a specific day in 1916.

## UNRESOLVED CONFLICT — W. W. Bradley at 507 in 1916

Selected visually verified 1916 newspaper business-directory advertisements place **W. W. Bradley's furniture business at 507 Main** (`E-116` / `S-130`, with additional 1916 recurrence in `E-119`). CCHS MOT:P-502 instead catalogs this G. H. Young store photograph as **507 Main, 1916**.

Both source families are preserved. The archive does **not** choose one chronology by assumption. Plausible explanations to test include an imprecise catalog date, a photograph made earlier but cataloged to 1916, publication lag/carry-forward in the business directory, a mid-year transition, or some unproved subdivision/overlap. None is established by MOT:P-502 alone.
