## Property-side controls — Wolf

The working property question is now explicitly:

> **Did a Wolf individual/family own the 505 Main real estate, and was a Wolf the owner/seller before the Wiitanens acquired the building?**

This must remain separate from the Hansen business search.

Existing controls:

1. Primary 1922 newspaper evidence documents **S. Wolf** acquiring the remainder of George Harding's Main/Fifth property group while C. Gates received the Fifth Street Restaurant location; a second report names S. Wolf as owner of the fire-origin building. Exact modern storefront mapping remains unresolved (`E-096`, `E-103`, `E-108`).
2. The City/1983-survey-derived history separately says the **Wolf family owned 503 Main from about 1922 to 1972** (`E-032`). That is meaningful evidence for a Wolf property chain in the block but is not a deed and does not by itself place that chain at 505.
3. Wolf & Miller is documented as a **business at 505 Main in 1918**. That is business occupancy only and cannot be converted into fee ownership.
