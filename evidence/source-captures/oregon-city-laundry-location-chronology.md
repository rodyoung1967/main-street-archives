# Oregon City Laundry — location chronology and Fifth Street photo implications

Status: **active reconstruction**

Purpose: separate the several documented Oregon City Laundry locations and use them to interpret the project photographs around Fifth/Main/Water. Do not assume all laundry photographs show the same building.

## 1910–1912 — Third/Main location

Primary newspaper business listings place Oregon City Laundry near Third and Main before the later Fifth Street plant:

- **23 Sep 1910, Oregon City Courier** — Oregon City Laundry, O. L. Blakeslee, **corner Third and Main Streets**.
- **5 Jan 1912, Oregon City Courier** — Oregon City Laundry, Blakeslee & Son, **Main Street near 3rd**.

Sources:
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1910-09-23/ed-1/seq-3/ocr/
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-01-05/ed-1/seq-6/

## 1916 — replacement-plant location conflict

Two contemporary sources agree that Oregon City Laundry needed a new plant because Hawley Pulp & Paper acquired the former laundry property, but they disagree on the replacement location.

### 3 Feb 1916 Oregon City Courier

The Courier says a new two-story brick/mill-construction laundry building would be erected on the **block bounded by Seventh, Main, Eighth and Railroad**, fronting on the alley beside city hall, with the rear adjoining the Courier property.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-02-03/ed-1/seq-6/

### 2 Apr 1916 Sunday Oregonian

The Sunday Oregonian says the new Oregon City Laundry building was **“on Fifth street just off Main”** and was nearing completion.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83045782/1916-04-02/ed-1/seq-14.pdf

### Current assessment

This is a real source conflict and must remain unresolved until another primary source bridges it. Possibilities include:

1. the February plan changed before construction;
2. one newspaper misstated the location;
3. the reports refer to different phases/properties;
4. there were temporary or multiple laundry facilities.

Do **not** state as settled fact that the 1916 replacement plant was on Fifth based on the Oregonian alone.

## 1918 — exact Fifth Street number

The 24 Oct 1918 Oregon City Courier business guide lists:

- **Oregon City Laundry — M. L. Blakeslee, Prop., 109 Fifth St.**
- **Scripture & May — 108 Fifth St., carriage and auto works**
- **F. L. Davis — 5th & Water, blacksmithing / horseshoeing**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-10-24/ed-1/seq-6/ocr/

This is the best exact-address anchor currently recovered for the pre-1922 laundry plant and independently proves that Oregon City Laundry was at **109 Fifth by 1918**, regardless of the unresolved 1916 newspaper conflict.

## 1922 fire — pre-fire Fifth Street cluster destroyed

The 20 Jul 1922 Banner-Courier reports a major fire in the older Oregon City business district. The fire destroyed:

- the **Oregon City Laundry**, the largest loss;
- the **frame garage of May & Washburn** with two automobiles;
- a Fifth Street restaurant;
- the Miller shoe-repair shop;
- the home/confectionery store of H. Ketchner;
- other adjoining structures.

The article states that the laundry had recently installed modern machinery and was operated by **Knoepel & Schofield**. Immediately after the fire, the laundry arranged to continue business through a **temporary office with the Pantorium at 519 Main Street**.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-07-20/ed-1/seq-1/

A 19 Jul 1922 Morning Register wire report similarly says the Oregon City Laundry plant, **May's Warehouse garage**, Fifth Street restaurant, Miller shoe shop and other buildings were destroyed.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088115/1922-07-19/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

### May & Washburn lower-bound evidence

May & Washburn appears in Clackamas County road-payment records by **29 Apr 1921**. That proves the partnership/business existed by then, although the county-payment entry does not establish the Fifth Street address.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1921-04-29/ed-1/seq-7/

**Photo implication:** if a project laundry photograph can be visually matched to the laundry plus May & Washburn garage cluster, its strongest current working window is **Apr 1921 to Jul 1922**. This remains a hypothesis until the garage signage/building is visually matched.

## Late 1922 — business resumes quickly, replacement plant still unresolved

Although the July fire destroyed the plant, Oregon City Laundry was again appearing in county expense records by **September 1922** and **November 1922**, showing that the business resumed service quickly.

Sources:
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-09-28/ed-1/seq-5/ocr/
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-11-23/ed-1/seq-7/ocr/

These entries prove resumed business activity but do **not** identify the replacement plant address. The temporary 519 Main office may have continued while production was performed elsewhere.

## 1925 Sanborn / corridor reconstruction

A City/ODOT technical memorandum summarizes the 1925 Sanborn as showing **Oregon City Laundry on the east side of 5th Street between Water Street and Main Street**.

Source:
https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/12817/Tech-Memo-6-Most-Promising-Alternatives

Important issue: this 1925 summary appears chronologically difficult to reconcile with the July 1922 destruction of the 109 Fifth plant. Possibilities include:

1. the laundry rebuilt at/near the same Fifth Street location before 1925;
2. the corridor memo is summarizing a later-revised Sanborn condition;
3. the pre-fire and post-fire laundry occupied different structures on the same side of Fifth;
4. the exact mapping of the 1922 destroyed plant versus the 1925 mapped laundry remains unresolved.

Do not assume continuity without checking the actual 1925 Sanborn sheet and its revision history.

## Later McLoughlin Boulevard location — resolved correction

Three sources now align on the later laundry location:

- **3 Feb 1953** — laundry manager R. W. Knoefel Jr. requests a loading zone at **510 McLoughlin Blvd**.
- **15 Nov 1967** — city resolution identifies Oregon City Laundry at **510 McLoughlin Boulevard (Highway 99E)**.
- Oregon City's **2000 Downtown Final Report** lists **510–514 McLoughlin Blvd — Oregon City Laundry — 1939**.

Sources:
- https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4722849/File/document
- https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4753768/File/document
- https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/4025/Downtown-Final-Report---2000-PDF

### Correction to prior note

A previous table-parsing read incorrectly associated **610 McLoughlin Blvd** with Oregon City Laundry. The 2000 survey actually associates **610 McLoughlin** with the **Elks BPOE #1189** and places Oregon City Laundry at **510–514 McLoughlin**. The 510/610 conflict is therefore withdrawn.

**Current confidence:** High that the later surviving/recorded Oregon City Laundry property was at **510–514 McLoughlin Blvd**, with a survey construction date of **1939**.

## Two-laundry-photo issue

The Time Capsule Maps source package contains two laundry photographs:

- `04-oregon-city-laundry-co-front.jpg`
- `05-oregon-city-laundry-main-street.jpg`

The source manifest grouped them together but did not prove they show the same building.

User visual identification provides an important camera-direction anchor for the **first photo** (`04`): it faces **west toward West Linn**, based on recognition of a building across the river.

Current rule:

- treat the two photos as **separate identification problems**;
- do not assume either image is automatically the 109 Fifth plant;
- use camera direction, curb/street geometry, river background, and Sanborn footprints to identify street side;
- preserve the possibility that the photos show different generations/locations of Oregon City Laundry;
- also preserve the user's observation that another photograph appears to show a laundry on the opposite side of Fifth until the images are matched to mapped footprints.
