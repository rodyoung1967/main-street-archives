## Fifth & Main — Holman alone continues

Late-year page-level retrieval supports **R. L. Holman, Leading Undertaker, Fifth and Main St.** after the August advertising wording change. Examples include:

- **26 Oct 1916, p7** — Holman alone at Fifth and Main.
- **16 Nov 1916, p7** — Holman alone at Fifth and Main.

This extends the Holman-alone advertising continuity into late 1916.

However, a newly recovered **6 Apr 1916 Courier page-5 OCR lead** states that **T. P. Randall sold his interest in the Holman-Randall undertaking establishment to R. L. Holman**. Until that April page is visually verified, retain it as a high-value lead. If confirmed, it means the old Holman & Randall advertisements that continued into the summer were stale copy and do not prove Randall remained an owner/partner.

April 6 lead:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-04-06/ed-1/seq-5/

## 503 / 505 status in late 1916

Targeted searches of September–December 1916 have still not produced a defensible exact-address occupant for **503 Main** or **505 Main**. This remains a search-coverage statement only. It is **not evidence of vacancy, demolition, or non-use**.

The same caution applies when this newspaper silence is compared with the 1916–1917 water-rent ledger, where 503 and 503½ are separately recorded and 505 is absent from the expected sequence. Newspaper-directory absence and water-account absence are separate facts and must not be conflated into a vacancy conclusion.

## New cross-year lead recovered during the 1916 review

A targeted search for Holman also recovered a **28 Aug 1914 Oregon City Enterprise** page whose text states that **R. L. Holman had moved to more commodious parlors at 5th and Main Sts.** This appears to be the contemporaneous 1914 move notice that the repository has been seeking.

Source page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1914-08-28/ed-1/seq-3/

Because the project standard requires visual confirmation, treat this as a **very high-value candidate source pending page-image verification**, not yet as a closed evidence item.
