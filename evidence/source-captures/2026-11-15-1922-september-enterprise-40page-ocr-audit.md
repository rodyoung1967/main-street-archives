## Coverage
Read/search-screened all **40 OCR files**, eight pages each, for the five Friday issues of *Oregon City Enterprise*: **1, 8, 15, 22 and 29 September 1922**. Searches targeted Gates, Wolf, Junken, Timms, Leland, McAnulty, Barry, pool hall, billiards, shoe store, Fifth Street Restaurant, rebuilding, new building, 501/503/505 Main, and Fifth/Main. Context was checked for relevance, not just raw keyword counts. OCR omissions remain possible; this does not certify an exhaustive visual-content audit.

This is a *geographic intersection anchor*, **not** a 501/503/505 building, tenant, construction, or tavern identification. Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1922-09-29/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

- **22 September, sequence 6:** 'POOL HALL SOLD' refers explicitly to **Sandy** and Frank Christianson/Shroll, **not** the 505 Main pool hall. Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1922-09-22/ed-1/seq-6.pdf
- **29 September, sequence 6:** references to new-building completion and a new school relate to other institutions/locations; not established as Fifth/Main.
- Other name matches include unrelated Gates/Wolf/Barry/Junken people or generic words. No credible exact-address 503/505 construction completion notice emerged from this **bounded OCR screen**.
