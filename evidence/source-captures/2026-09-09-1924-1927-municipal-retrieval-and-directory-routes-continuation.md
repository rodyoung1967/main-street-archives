## 3. 1923–1924 and 1925–1926 Polk statewide gazetteers — concrete surviving-volume route

A public historical auction record independently documents two physical volumes:

**Holabird Western Americana Collections, lot 3414, `Oregon, Washington Directories; 1923-1924, 1925-1926 [145003]`**

https://holabirdamericana.liveauctiongroup.com/Oregon-Washington-Directories-1923-1924-1925-1926-145003_i44229859

The lot description identifies both as **Polk's Oregon and Washington State Gazetteer and Business Directory**, published by **R.L. Polk & Co.**, for **1923–1924** and **1925–1926**. It describes alphabetical resident listings with occupations and addresses, classified business directories, and advertisements that may include street addresses and telephone numbers.

A second Holabird listing for a separate 1923–1924 copy provides more structural detail:

**Lot 1245, `Polk Oregon and Washington Directory [145010]`**

https://holabirdamericana.liveauctiongroup.com/Polk-Oregon-and-Washington-Directory-145010_i44776233

It states that pp. **13–188** are a Classified Buyers' Guide for Oregon and Washington and that the remainder is paginated by state, with alphabetical lists of residents, occupations, and addresses. This makes the volume a potentially high-value source for Oregon City businesses even though it has not yet been shown to be a house-by-house Oregon City street directory.

## 4. June 1927 Pacific Telephone & Telegraph directory — new Oregon City source lead

A second public historical auction lot documents a **June 1927** telephone directory with explicit Oregon City coverage:

**Holabird Western Americana Collections, lot 3412, `Oregon Telephone Directories; 1911, 1927 [144853]`**

https://holabirdamericana.liveauctiongroup.com/Oregon-Telephone-Directories-1911-1927-144853_i44229857

The description says the two directories were issued by the **Pacific Telephone and Telegraph Company** in **July 1911** and **June 1927** and include **Salem, Portland, Oregon City, and Vancouver**, plus **Marion, Multnomah, Clackamas, Polk, and Clarke Counties**. It says both contain alphabetical business/resident listings with telephone numbers and business advertisements.

This is a particularly useful **1927 source-access lead** because it could independently test business names and subscribers near the end of the 1924–1927 interval. The auction listing does not itself identify 503, 505, Farr, Kwality, or any target business.

A Washington State Library catalog record for the same PT&T geographic directory family confirms that an **August 1916** issue was titled around **Portland, Oregon City, Salem**, the named Oregon counties, Vancouver and Clarke County, with Pacific Telephone & Telegraph as publisher. That earlier holding supports the continuity of this directory family but does not prove a current 1927 institutional holding.

No public library-catalog holding for the June 1927 issue was confirmed in this pass. The auction copy was sold in 2022, so that specific copy is not represented as currently obtainable from the auctioneer.
