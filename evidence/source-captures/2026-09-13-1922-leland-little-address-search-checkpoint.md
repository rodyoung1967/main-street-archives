## Previously visually verified anchors

The existing billiards chronology remains valid:

1. **25 February 1922 — Morning Oregonian, p. 15:** visually verified Portina cigar advertisement lists **Leland & Little** among Oregon City dealers and separately lists **Little, W. L.** No address is printed.
2. **8 June 1922 — Banner-Courier, p. 10:** visually verified merchant roster separately names **Walter Little**, **Timms Pool Hall**, and **O. H. Leland**. No address is printed for any of those three. The same scanned page also directly advertises **Dr. Freeze, 505½ Main St.**, confirming active use of that half-address immediately before the July fire but not tying Leland & Little to it.
3. **17 August 1922 — Banner-Courier, p. 2:** visually verified business list prints **LELAND AND LITTLE, Billiards**. No address is printed.

These facts establish that Leland & Little existed before the July fire and was publicly identified as a billiards business shortly afterward. They do not identify the July fire-adjacent pool hall.

## Interpretation

The new repeated September–October listings materially strengthen the evidence that **Leland & Little continued to be publicly advertised as an active Oregon City billiards concern for months after the July 1922 fire**.

The chronology is now:

- **25 Feb 1922:** Leland & Little exists as an Oregon City cigar dealer — visually verified;
- **18 Jul 1922:** unnamed pool hall adjoining the Schieremann fire-origin room suffers water/roof damage — separately verified fire evidence;
- **17 Aug 1922:** Leland & Little identified as **Billiards** — visually verified;
- **14 Sep–26 Oct 1922:** recurring **Leland and Little, Billiards** business-list copy — OCR/TEXT ONLY in this checkpoint.

This continuity is compatible with Leland & Little having operated the July fire-adjacent hall and with rapid continuation/reopening after the fire. It is also compatible with the business having operated at another Oregon City location. **The newspaper evidence still does not choose between those possibilities.**

The repeated September–October appearances appear to be recurring/standing business-list copy. They should therefore be treated as continuity of public advertising, not six fully independent occupancy confirmations.

## Relationship to 505 Main

The functional match remains unusually strong:

- Leland & Little is a cigar dealer by February 1922;
- Leland & Little is a billiards concern from at least August through October 1922;
- the 1925 Sanborn later maps **505 Main as billiards/cigars**;
- the project's best current frontage reconstruction places the July fire-adjacent pool hall in the **505 physical position** as a strong cross-source/photo-geometry inference.

Nevertheless, no contemporary recovered source yet prints the required exact bridge `Leland & Little = 505 Main` or `505½ Main`. Do not promote that equation to direct fact.

## June 8 control and 505½ caution

The 8 June scanned page is especially useful as a control because it simultaneously shows:

- **Dr. Freeze at 505½ Main** by exact printed address; and
- separate merchant-roster entries for **Walter Little**, **Timms Pool Hall**, and **O. H. Leland** without addresses.

Therefore the newspaper itself demonstrates that an exact 505½ address could be printed when an advertiser supplied it, but the merchant roster does not provide enough location data to assign any of the billiards/pool-hall names to 505/505½.
