# Red Front location caution — 1906–1922

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED historical address anchors; 1922 identity/location unresolved**

Purpose: prevent the same-day July 1922 Eugene fire report's reference to a damaged `Red Front clothing store` from being automatically placed at Fifth & Main based on older Red Front advertising. The Red Front name moved and/or was reused in Oregon City.

## 4 May 1906 — Red Front at Main & Fifth

The actual scanned page of the **Oregon City Courier, 4 May 1906, p. 3** was visually inspected.

A large Red Front advertisement at the bottom right gives:

- **THE RED FRONT**
- groceries, dry goods, millinery, shoes
- **Main & 5th St.**
- Oregon City, Ore.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1906-05-04/ed-1/seq-3.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This proves a Red Front general-merchandise business at the Fifth/Main intersection in May 1906.

## February 1907 — Red Front at Ninth & Main

The **8 Feb 1907 Oregon City Enterprise** reports that the Red Front had been closed temporarily on attachment and explicitly describes it as a **grocery and general store at Ninth and Main streets**, owned and managed by **E. C. Hamilton**.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1907-02-08/ed-1/seq-3/

The text is a strong contemporary address statement. Direct page-image review remains desirable for full project-standard certification of this specific February item.

## 19 Jul 1907 — Ninth/Main premises called the old Red Front Building

The actual scanned page of the **Oregon City Courier, 19 Jul 1907, p. 8** was visually inspected.

A local item states that the **old Red Front Building on the corner of Main and Ninth streets** was being remodeled and would be occupied by grocer **J. E. Seeley**. The property owners are identified as the parish of St. Paul's Episcopal Church.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1907-07-19/ed-1/seq-8.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

Thus by July 1907 the Ninth/Main premises was already remembered as the former Red Front location.

## 1918 — old Red Front building on upper Main

A **21 Mar 1918 Oregon City Courier** report says the new Clackamas County Banner was rumored to be preparing to occupy the **old Red Front store building, on Main street**. A follow-up **28 Mar 1918** item calls it the remodeled Red Front store on **upper Main street**.

Sources:
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-03-21/ed-1/seq-1/
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-03-28/ed-1/seq-4/

Given the explicit 1907 Ninth/Main identification, these 1918 `old Red Front` / `upper Main` references are much more naturally connected to the Ninth/Main former store than to the 1906 Fifth/Main location. This is a **strong inference**, not a quoted 1918 street-number statement.

## July 1922 fire-report issue

A visually reviewed same-day **18 Jul 1922 Eugene Daily Guard** account of the Fifth/Main fire says a **Red Front clothing store** was damaged rather than destroyed.

Related source capture:
`evidence/source-captures/1922-07-18-eugene-daily-guard-fire-account.md`

Because the Red Front name had already shifted/reappeared at different Oregon City locations by 1906–1907, the 1922 phrase cannot be used by itself to place that damaged business at the 1906 Main & Fifth premises.

It is also possible that the Eugene wire report used an old building/trade name, misidentified a business, or referred to a different Red Front concern. A contemporary 1922 Oregon City address/business source is required.

## Research consequence

Do **not** currently use the 1922 `Red Front clothing store` as a fixed Fifth/Main boundary marker.

The 1906 Main & Fifth Red Front address is historically important for the target corner, but continuity into July 1922 is **not established** and the 1907 Ninth/Main move/history demonstrates that such continuity cannot be assumed.

## Confidence

- Red Front at Main & Fifth in May 1906: **Very High / visually verified**
- Red Front identified at Ninth & Main in Feb 1907: **High / contemporary text; page-image check still desirable**
- old Red Front Building at Main & Ninth in Jul 1907: **Very High / visually verified**
- 1918 `old Red Front` on upper Main = former Ninth/Main premises: **Strong inference**
- 1922 damaged Red Front = 1906 Fifth/Main store/site: **Unproven; do not assume**
