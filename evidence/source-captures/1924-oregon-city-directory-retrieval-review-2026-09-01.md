## Result and evidentiary boundary

The original DeWitt Clinton Latourette House National Register nomination bibliography cites **Polk, R.L. and Company, Oregon City Directory, 1924**. This was visually verified on **PDF page 7**, not page 6 as earlier archive notes stated. The same page names Oregon City Planning Department as the repository for supporting data and dates Julie Koler's preparation of the form to **15 August 1991** (Koler/Morrison Planning Consultants).

This proves what the nomination cites and supplies a supporting-file lead. It does **not** independently confirm the directory's exact edition/title-page identity, a present library holding, or any occupant. A shortened or mistaken bibliographic date/title is a possibility to test against an actual volume, not a finding.

The full original 19-page nomination is now preserved at `records/historic-surveys/1992-latourette-house-nrhp-nomination.pdf`. Canonical source: https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/152322a5-6e9f-44b2-8871-c4fef169dad0/

**No actual 1924 directory pages were recovered or inspected.** No new Main/Fifth business, person, street number, construction event, or timeline claim resulted. Farr's at 505 remains undated, and Kwality's reported 1926 presence at 503 remains unbridged to the May 1925 Sanborn. Catalog silence/access failure is not evidence of vacancy, nonpublication, or demolition.

Source: [GFO shelflist linked by the catalog gateway](https://gfo.org/file_download/inline/db79e42b-d91f-4d72-8a31-973c337e3805). Page headers date the list **30 June 2025**. The downloaded full source has **978 PDF pages**; it is a historical snapshot of holdings, not a live shelf-availability check.

Preserved three-page source excerpt: `records/library-catalogs/gfo-shelflist-2025-06-30-directory-excerpts.pdf`. Excerpt pages 1–3 correspond to original PDF pages **567, 568, 622**, respectively, with page content retained; the 978-page source is not represented as fully mirrored. The derived excerpt was optimized with Ghostscript to remove bulky unused source resources; searchable target lines and a final page render were checked. It is not a byte-original copy of the complete catalog.

- PDF p. 567 lists a **1921–1922 Oregon and Washington gazetteer**, including call numbers `979.50 .CtyDir 1921-1922 Bus` and `979 .Bus Dir 1921-1922`. These are earlier statewide-directory leads, not the sought 1924 city volume.
- PDF p. 568 lists the **1953 Oregon City and Vicinity Directory**, call number `979.55 Clac .CtyDir 1953`.
- PDF p. 622 lists Polk Oregon City directories for **1962, 1964, 1965, 1967–1970, 1975–1977, 1979–1981, 1984, 1986 and 1987**. No 1924 edition appears in that checked block. These later holdings can support the separate long-gap occupant research, not the 1925 map identification by themselves.

## Numbered manual callouts / exact retrieval targets

No inquiry was sent, no paid lookup ordered and no sign-in attempted. Contacts below were checked on official institutional pages on the review date; confirm terms before ordering copies or visiting.

1. **City Planning — locate the source behind the citation.** Contact **ocplanning@orcity.org**, **503-722-3789**, via the [official Planning page](https://www.orcity.org/634/Planning). Identify the DeWitt Clinton Latourette House nomination, prepared by Julie Koler / Koler-Morrison on 15 August 1991 and certified in 1992; attach or reference NPS PDF page 7. Ask whether the supporting file contains directory extracts, title-page/copyright copies, source notes, a call number, or a lender/repository name. Ask whether the cited 1924 item is a standalone city directory or a section of a broader Polk publication. Do not presume Planning owns the book.
2. **Confirm an actual holding.** Ask OHS (**libreference@ohs.org**, **503-306-5240**) and CCFHS (**ccfhsociety@gmail.com**, **503-655-5574**) for the exact cited edition or a referral. Supply the bibliography and ask for the full title, covered years, call number and scan/lookup availability. GFO's [contact route](https://gfo.org/) / **503-963-1932** is a secondary confirmation route; its checked 2025 shelflist does not supply the target volume. FamilySearch catalog **95422** remains a title-series lookup gap, not a confirmed edition.
3. **If the volume is located, establish identity before interpreting entries.** Obtain cover/title/copyright pages, edition date, geographic coverage, contents, abbreviations and pagination. Record whether a street index exists; do not invent page numbers or assume every statewide gazetteer has house-by-house entries.
4. **Read the complete target address ladder.** If present, inspect Main Street **501–511**, including **503, 505, 505½ and 507½**, nearby bays, and both sides of Fifth Street. Preserve exact printed syntax, upstairs/half-number distinctions, page images and negative findings only within actually checked pages. Do not normalize Fifth & Main to 501 or half-numbers to ground-floor storefronts.
5. **Cross-check names and trades.** Inspect Farr (including Edwin F. and Alice), Kwality/Quality, Jager, Leland, Little, Timms, Holman and Pace; then billiards/pool, cigars/tobacco, restaurants/cafes, soft drinks and funeral-director categories. Distinguish a person's residence from business address and a classified listing from a building-ownership record. Capture all incidental numbered Fifth Street businesses encountered.
6. **If the citation cannot be resolved, continue independent sources.** Municipal business/pool-hall/restaurant licenses and commission minutes for 1924–1927, the City's underlying 503 historic-resource file and its cited 1926 Kwality advertisement, and local newspaper microfilm remain the highest-value independent routes. Adjacent-year directories can narrow intervals but cannot automatically supply May 1925 occupants or physical rebuild dates.
