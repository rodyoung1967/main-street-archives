Two additional exact-address anchors on November 25:

- **p 2:** Portland Railway Light & Power Co., **Beaver Building, 617 Main Street** (BUS-031). Earlier batch advertisements say Electric Store / Beaver Building / Main without a number. The November ad supplies the numbered anchor; it does not date a move or equate every Beaver Building tenant with 617.
- **p 6:** **Eddy's Dependable Store, 526 Main** (BUS-032), advertising clothing and household apparel. No proprietor is named. Do not merge it with the separately recorded 1910 Watson B. Eddy & Son at Main/Sixth without an identity/succession source; do not confuse even-numbered 526 with 527.

### Former Brunswick becomes Mass's Hotel and Restaurant

- **October 28 p 5, “Will Happen This Week”:** ex-Sheriff **E. T. Mass** announces opening Saturday at eleven. The article names **Mass's Hotel and Restaurant** and explicitly identifies its quarters as those formerly occupied by the Brunswick, now renovated/rebuilt and completely altered and modernized. This is an announcement, not alone proof of opening.
- **November 4 p 5, “New Place Popular”:** reports more than 200 people at the opening **last Saturday**, with the former Brunswick quarters refurnished/decorated and permanent lodgers already installed. This confirms the announced opening occurred; **October 30, 1915** is calendar-derived from the two reports.
- November 18 p 1 mentions Governor Moses Alexander dining at Mass's; p 5 reports a loganberry-growers' meeting there. These corroborate subsequent activity, not uninterrupted occupancy.

**BUS-030 / P-045; T-019.** The same-premises business succession is explicit. Main-near-Seventh location is inherited only from the separately reviewed September 23 report (S-120); the October/November reports give no street number. No deed, demolition/new-building claim, final cost or exact historic parcel is established. RL-043's opening/name questions are resolved; its number/property-interest questions remain open.

### Fifth Street is included, not just 503/505 Main

- **Holman and Randall:** Fifth and Main advertisements occur October 28 pp 2/7, November 4 p 7, November 11 p 7/seq 8, November 18 pp 2/7 and November 25 p 7. These corroborate E-054's intersection location; no numbered Fifth or Main storefront is supplied.
- **Scripture & May:** scientific horseshoeing / emery-wheel service, Pacific 297-J, appears in all five directory pages identified above. **These notices print no street address.** They extend dated business-name evidence only; they do not backdate the independently documented 1916 **108 Fifth** address or establish a May & Washburn succession. The later 108/109 Fifth and 513 Fifth records remain separate.
- **November 18 pp 1/8:** Main Street track/paving defects and a proposed city report about Fifth Street improvement funding.
- **November 25 p 1, “Source of City Deficit Is Told”:** reports Fifth Street improved by day labor at city expense, with crushed rock charged to the general street-repair fund over three months. The cited **$37,895.68 is the broader street-maintenance account, NOT the isolated cost of Fifth Street work**. Exact segment, dates and authorizing records are not supplied. This is road work, not proof of alteration/demolition at any storefront.

No new numbered Fifth Street occupant was found in this batch. **No defensible new exact 503/505 occupant or building-status claim was identified.** These are bounded review results, not evidence of vacancy, destruction, or unchanged survival after the 1913 fire.
