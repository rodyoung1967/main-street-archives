# Oregon City Courier — 9 November 1916 visual review

Date reviewed: 11 September 2026
Workstream: ANNUAL-1916
Title: *Oregon City Courier*
Issue date: 9 November 1916
Canonical issue: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-11-09/ed-1/
Coverage: 8/8 pages visually inspected from the original University of Oregon page scans/PDFs.

## Method

Each of the eight original page PDFs was opened and visually inspected. OCR/search text was used only as discovery support and is not treated as visual evidence. Business operation, business ownership, property ownership, tenancy, licensing, trade names, addresses, and physical buildings remain separate evidence classes. Absence of a target-address mention is not treated as evidence of vacancy or non-use.

## Page-by-page review

### Page 1

Election, city-budget, county-budget, constitutional-revision, prohibition, and general local reporting. No exact 501/503/505/507 Main Street premises fact was identified.

### Page 2

County/suburb correspondence and general advertising. No exact target-address fact was identified.

### Page 3

State/general material and advertising. R. L. Holman appears as Leading Undertaker at Fifth and Main, an established intersection control only. Burmeister & Andresen appears at Suspension Bridge Corner. Neither establishes a numbered 503/505 assignment.

### Page 4

Editorial/general material and advertising. R. L. Holman again appears at Fifth and Main. No exact 503/505 premises fact was identified.

### Page 5

County and city locals and advertising. Roy A. Prudden advertises as optometrist at **612 Main Street** and explicitly as successor to W. A. Schilling, continuing the previously documented Schilling-to-Prudden business transition at that address. Jones Drug Co. is advertised without a numbered Main Street address in the ad. No exact 503/505 premises fact was identified.

### Page 6

General material and advertising. Williams Bros. Transfer & Storage advertises office **612 Main Street**. Larsen & Company advertises at **Corner 10th and Main Sts.** The Electric Store / Portland Railway Light & Power Company advertises **Andresen Bldg., 619 Main St.** These are established controls outside the target pair. No exact 503/505 premises fact was identified.

### Page 7

Legal notices, classified advertising, and professional notices. R. L. Holman appears as Leading Undertaker at **Fifth and Main St.** No exact 503/505 premises fact was identified.

### Page 8

Election continuation, general features, and an illustrated Oregon City business directory. The directory includes Oregon City Garage at Main and 4th, Jones Drug Co. as an unnumbered modern drug store, and **Raasch & Lamb** as **Pool and Billiards / Everything for Smokers** without a street number. The absence of a number means Raasch & Lamb must remain unassigned to 503/504/505 absent a separate explicit address bridge. No exact 503/505 premises fact was identified.

## Result

The 9 November 1916 *Courier* is **8/8 visually verified**. No page establishes an exact 503 or 505 Main occupant, owner, tenant, licensee, trade-name change, sale, transfer, construction, demolition, closure, or vacancy event.

Useful non-target controls are retained separately:

- Roy A. Prudden — 612 Main Street; successor to W. A. Schilling.
- Williams Bros. Transfer & Storage — 612 Main Street.
- Electric Store / Portland Railway Light & Power Company — Andresen Building, 619 Main Street.
- R. L. Holman — Fifth and Main.
- Raasch & Lamb — pool/billiards and smokers' goods, **unnumbered**.

No source silence is treated as evidence of vacancy or non-use at 503 or 505 Main.
