## 4 February 1921

**8/8 page images visually scanned.**

- p.1, **“Mammoth Poles Fall in Street at Busy Corner”**: two light/power poles fell across the business intersection; one at **Seventh and Main**, the other across the street near **Price Bros. store**. Business-district context, not a secure 501/503/505 address.
- p.1, **“Harry Draper New Member of Huntley Firm”**: Huntley-Draper Drug Company business change; no secure target address established.
- pp.2–8: no secure 501/503/505/505½ target-address evidence promoted.

## 11 February 1921

**8/8 page images visually scanned.**

- p.4, **“Oregon City Shoe Firm in New Location”**: business-relocation item present, but no secure 501/503/505 address established from the scan.
- pp.1–8: no secure target-address evidence promoted.

## 18 February 1921

**8/8 page images visually scanned.**

- p.5, **“Main Street Store Robbed Sunday Night”**: Joe Kerrick's cigar and notion store is explicitly at **Fourth and Main**. Non-target location control.
- p.8, **“Robbers Get $130 From Main St. Pool Hall”**: the pool hall is explicitly **“on Main street, between Fourth and Fifth”** and had **“recently opened up for business.”** Entry was through a rear window and $130 was taken. This is a same-era pool-hall control in the **400 block**, not 505 Main, and must not be conflated with the 505 pool-hall chain.
- same p.8 item: attempted entry at **F. H. Cross Harness Shop, 7th Street, hill section**; non-target.

## 25 February 1921

**8/8 page images visually scanned at high resolution.**

Useful address/location controls:

- p.3: full display advertisement for **A. C. Howland, Real Estate and Insurance, 620 Main Street**, “Between Price Bros. and Eddy's Dept. Stores.” This confirms a non-target Main Street address and neighboring-business context.
- p.4: **Miller-Parker Co.**, automobile business, explicitly **Ninth and Main**. Non-target Main Street control.
- pp.1–8: no secure 501/503/505/505½ target-address evidence found in the visual scan.

## February conclusion

The Oregon City Enterprise February run is now **32/32 visually scanned**. No secure 501/503/505/505½ hit was found in the four issues. That is bounded coverage only and **must not be treated as evidence that the target businesses/buildings were absent**.

The February 18 pool-hall article is the strongest new February item because it positively locates a newly opened pool hall between Fourth and Fifth and therefore protects the project from accidentally assigning that pool hall to 505.
