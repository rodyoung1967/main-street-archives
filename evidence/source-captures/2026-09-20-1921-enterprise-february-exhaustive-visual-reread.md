### 18 February 1921

**8/8 page images visually scanned.**

Two Main Street crime items are useful location controls:

- p.5, **“Main Street Store Robbed Sunday Night”**: visually verified as the cigar and notion store of **Joe Kerrick, Fourth and Main streets**. The article says entry was gained through the rear door and about $1.50 in pennies, a revolver, ammunition and roughly $5 in tobacco were taken. This is explicitly Fourth/Main and therefore is **not** evidence for 501/503/505.
- p.8, **“Robbers Get $130 From Main St. Pool Hall”**: visually verified. The article states that **“The pool hall on Main street, between Fourth and Fifth, recently opened up for business”** was robbed Friday night of $130. Entry was through a rear window; the money was part of the day's receipts and had been hidden under the counter. This is an important same-era pool-hall control, but its location **between Fourth and Fifth** places it in the 400 block, not at 505 Main. It must not be conflated with the later/other 505 pool-hall chain.
- The same p.8 item also reports another attempted entry at the **F. H. Cross Harness Shop, 7th Street, hill section**; separate non-target business.
