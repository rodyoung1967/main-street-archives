## December 1917 — issue-size correction

The **13 Dec 1917 Oregon City Courier is exactly 24 images/pages**.

The archive previously recorded this issue as "at least 23 images." The second issue-index page explicitly lists pages **21, 22, 23, and 24**, while the front page itself identifies the issue as a 24-page Christmas shopping number. These provide independent confirmation of the complete issue size.

Other December anchors:
