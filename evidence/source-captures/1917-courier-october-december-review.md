## December 1917 — archive-sequence conflict

The 13 Dec front page identifies the Christmas shopping number as a **24-page** issue, and the archive's issue record exposes sequence positions 1–24. That does not mean the currently supplied sequence is complete and internally correct.

On 24 Aug 2026 all 24 archive-supplied page PDFs were downloaded, rendered, and visually inspected:

- sequences **1–20** and **22–24** have printed headers dated **Thursday, December 13, 1917**;
- sequence **21** has the printed header **Thursday, December 27, 1917**.

The archive has therefore associated a 27 Dec page with the 13 Dec issue record. The true 13 Dec page 21 has **not** been visually recovered. Record the result as **23 genuine 13 Dec pages visually reviewed plus one retrieval conflict**, not as 24 pages cleared.

Other December anchors:
