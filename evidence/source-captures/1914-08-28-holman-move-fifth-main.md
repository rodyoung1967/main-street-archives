# 28 August 1914 — R. L. Holman move to Fifth & Main

Status: **primary newspaper page recovered and page-level rendering reviewed**

Source: *Oregon City Enterprise*, 28 August 1914, page 3 / image 3.

Permanent page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1914-08-28/ed-1/seq-3/

## Finding

The page contains an advertisement for **R. L. Holman, Leading Funeral Director** stating that he:

> **has moved to more commodious parlors 5th and Main Sts.**

The advertisement also describes the establishment as carrying undertaking supplies and having a private reception room, private family room, sanitary laying-out room, and private chapel.

## Significance

This directly resolves the archive's earlier open question about whether a contemporaneous source could establish Holman's move to **Fifth & Main in 1914**. The direct Fifth-and-Main chronology should now begin no later than **28 August 1914**, rather than 1915.

The notice establishes the **intersection location**, but it does **not** itself print the numbered address 501, 503, or 505 Main. Therefore it must not be used to assign the undertaking establishment to a specific numbered storefront without independent mapping evidence.

## Confidence

- Holman had moved his funeral-directing/undertaking establishment to **5th & Main by 28 Aug 1914**: **very high / primary page evidence**.
- Exact numbered address of those parlors: **unresolved**.
