# 1904 Oregon City Courier — Main Street Review

Reviewed: 20 August 2026  
Scope: 1904 *Oregon City Courier* issues, focused on the Main Street frontage between 5th and 6th and evidence that could map to later 501–527 Main addresses. Supplemental Oregon City newspaper material is identified separately when it supplies a useful adjacency clue.

## Review result

No defensible 1904 *Courier* hit was recovered that explicitly gives a later-numbered **501, 503, 505, 507, 509, 511, 513, 515, 517, 519, 521, 523, 525, or 527 Main** address.

No new 1904 *Courier* advertisement was recovered that explicitly says **Main Street between 5th and 6th** or otherwise securely places a named business within the study block.

This is **not evidence that the storefronts were vacant or unnamed**. OCR is imperfect, some page rendering is intermittent, and Oregon City was still using building names, neighboring businesses, intersections, and landmarks heavily during this period. The 1906 house-numbering ordinance context (`E-063`) reinforces caution about expecting later 5xx numbers in 1904.

Use: establishes a Main Street business/office but does not place it within the 5th-to-6th study block.

## 12 August 1904 — Oregon City Courier, page 8

Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1904-08-12/ed-1/seq-8/
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1904-08-12/ed-1/seq-8.pdf

Evidence: a merchant-voting promotion lists a useful cross-section of Oregon City businesses, including **Howell & Jones, J. M. Price, Thompson's Bargain Store, Miles & McGlashan, Lamb & Sawyer, W. L. Block, S. Oldstein, Brunswick Restaurant, and R. Petzold**. Petzold separately advertises the **Cash Meat Market — Main Street**.

Handling: later direct evidence places Petzold's Cash Market at **696 Main** in 1910, outside the 5th-to-6th study block. Therefore the 1904 Petzold ad should **not** be used as a candidate occupant of 501–527 Main.

## 26 August 1904 — Oregon City Courier, page 3

Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1904-08-26/ed-1/seq-3/
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1904-08-26/ed-1/seq-3.pdf

Evidence and context:

- A **New Book Store** announces an opening on **1 September** in **Lamb & Sawyer's Building, next to Oregon City Bank**.
- Dr. O. A. Stuart is described as having an office in the **Willamette Building**, confirming that the named building was active as commercial/office premises in 1904.
- **Sugarman & Son** announce that they will move their second-hand business to Portland about September 1; the notice does not give their Oregon City street location.
- The same issue advertises **Willamette Grocery, Stevens Building, Sixth and Main Sts.** This is retained only as a geographic anchor; the current research priority is the frontage between 5th and 6th rather than the Sixth Street corner itself.

Use: the Lamb & Sawyer/Oregon City Bank adjacency and Willamette Building reference are useful mapping anchors, but neither establishes a later 501–527 address.

## 2 December 1904 — Oregon City Courier, page 10

Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1904-12-02/ed-1/seq-10.pdf

Evidence: Petzold's Cash Market again appears as **Main Street**. As above, later evidence maps this business to 696 Main, so it is excluded from the target-block candidate set.

## Supplemental cross-paper context — 15 January 1904 Oregon City Enterprise, page 3

Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1904-01-15/ed-1/seq-3/
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1904-01-15/ed-1/seq-3.pdf

This is **not a Courier issue**, but it supplies useful relational evidence:

- **The Farmers' Store**, M. Leibowitch manager — **Main Street, opposite Barlow's Grocery**.
- **The Electric Grocery**, D. M. Klemsen proprietor — **Main Street next to Albright's Market**.

These are valid Main Street adjacency clues, but neither has yet been mapped to the 5th-to-6th block and neither should receive a later numbered address without additional evidence.

## 1904 handling conclusion

For the target block, 1904 is currently a **mapping-context year rather than an occupant-identification year**. The strongest usable information is a set of building/adjacency anchors—Willamette Building, Lamb & Sawyer's Building next to Oregon City Bank, and supplemental Main Street business adjacencies—not a specific 501–527 tenant assignment.
