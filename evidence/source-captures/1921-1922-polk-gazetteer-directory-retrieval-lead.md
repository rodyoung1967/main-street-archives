## Why this source is unusually high value

The Oregon City section could potentially resolve several active questions in one contemporary source:

1. **Alex Schieremann / spelling variants** — tailor/cleaner listing and exact business address;
2. **Timms Pool Hall** — exact premises;
3. **Leland & Little** — exact premises and possibly business classification;
4. **O. H. Leland / Walter L. Little** — individual listings and occupations;
5. **Pantorium** — whether it was at 500 Main, 519 Main, or another address when the directory was compiled;
6. **S. Wolf** — Oregon City business/residence listing and possible full name;
7. **Gilbert Miller / Miller's Shoe Store** — pre-fire business address;
8. **May & Washburn, S. F. Scripture, H. Ketchner, Fifth Street Restaurant** — numbered Fifth Street placements.
