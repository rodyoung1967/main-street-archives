## Shoe-store separation note

A focused same-session follow-up located independent contemporary controls showing that the generic **Oregon City Shoe Store** should not be casually identified with Schoenheinz's **Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop**:

- 28 January 1910 *Oregon City Courier* p.6 visually shows a large **Oregon City Shoe Store** advertisement with **Justin S. Lageson** printed immediately beneath the store name and the location **Opposite Postoffice**.
- 26 April 1912 *Oregon City Courier* p.8 visually shows **Oregon City Shoe Store — 508 Main St. — Opposite Postoffice**.
- Separately, 1911 primary newspaper advertising directly identifies **C. Schoenheinz, proprietor**, of the **Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop**, **716 Main between Sixth and Seventh**.

These records establish two differently named, differently located contemporary shoe-business strands. They materially caution against using the April 5 Oregon City Shoe Store ads as evidence for C. Schoenheinz or 503 Main. Exact ownership/continuity of Oregon City Shoe Store across 1910–1912 remains to be documented independently.
