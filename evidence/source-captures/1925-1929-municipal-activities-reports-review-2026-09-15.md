## Visual-review scope

### 1925 report — 22/22 PDF pages

The steward copy is byte-identical to the already preserved report and therefore does not create a new source identity. The existing complete visual audit under `S-157` was rechecked against the supplied copy for the project-relevant passages.

Most important retained item:

- **30 April 1925, 6:15 a.m. — “Main bet. 5th and 6th” — “Spark from chimney” — approximately $15 damage — 300 ft. hose.**
- No numbered premises, street side, occupant, proprietor, or business is printed.
- This remains block-level context only and must not be assigned to 501, 503, or 505.

Also retained from the prior complete review: Fifth Street paving from High to Jackson (`E-140`) and aggregate license/police-recordkeeping context (`E-141`).

### 1928 report — 29/29 PDF pages

All 29 PDF pages were visually reviewed, including covers, map/aerial spread, departmental narratives, fire-call table, engineering work, health/library/cemetery sections, and auditor schedules.

#### Direct exact-address finding — 1 March 1928

The Fire Department call table on PDF page 10 (printed pp. 16–17 spread) directly prints:

- **Mar. 1 — 503 Main Res. — Overheated Stove — None**

This is the strongest target-specific finding in the 1928 report.

Evidence limits:

- Preserve the source abbreviation **“Res.”** exactly; do not silently expand it to “residence” as a proved classification.
- The entry establishes that the Fire Department recorded a call at **503 Main** on 1 March 1928.
- It does **not** identify Kwality, a proprietor, tenant, property owner, or business operator.
- It does **not** prove that the entire 503 storefront/building was residential or that the source's address label describes the primary commercial use.
- It does provide direct, dated exact-address evidence that a premises identifiable as **503 Main** existed and was in municipal fire-call records by that date.

Registered as `E-348`.

Nearby but non-numbered/intersection context in the same table includes:

- **2 January — “5th & 6th on Main” — Gas Stove — None.**
- **11 September — “5th & Main Garage” — Cigarette — None.**

Neither is assigned to 501/503/505.

The report's aerial/map spread is visually useful but does not print an exposure date. It is retained as contextual imagery only and is not promoted into a target-building dating claim.

### 1929 report — 33/33 PDF pages

All 33 PDF pages were visually reviewed, including covers, aerial/map spread, financial material, fire-call table, engineering/street work, photographs, health/library/cemetery material, and auditor schedules.

#### Fifth/Main fire-call context — 10 June 1929

The Fire Department table on PDF page 11 (printed pp. 18–19 spread) directly prints:

- **June 10 — 5th & Main — Wiring — $25.00 estimated damage.**

No numbered premises, business, proprietor, or street side is printed. This is intersection context only and is not assigned to 501, 503, or 505.

The same table includes **“Farr Bros., Slaughter House”** on 3 January with a defective flue and $100 estimated damage. The source does not place that slaughter house at 505 Main and it must not be conflated with the later Farr's Pool Hall / Edwin and Alice Farr chain at 505.

#### Main Street ornamental lights — Fifth to Ninth

The Engineering and Street Department section on PDF page 13 (printed p. 23) directly states:

- **“MAIN STREET LIGHTS”**
- installation of ornamental street lights on **Main Street from 5th to 9th Street**;
- **28 lights** installed;
- total cost **$5,600.00**.

This directly documents a 1929 public-realm improvement beginning at the Fifth/Main target intersection and extending north through the downtown corridor. It does not identify or alter private-building occupancy, title, tenancy, or construction.

The same spread contains a photograph captioned **“Railroad Avenue from Fifth to Sixth Street, after improvement.”** It is nearby urban context rather than a photograph of the 501/503/505 Main frontage.

Registered as `E-349`.

As in the 1926 and 1928 volumes, the opening aerial/map imagery is not assigned a report-year exposure date without independent photo provenance.

## Cross-source / timeline consequences

1. The 1925 and 1926 steward uploads add **no new binary or new source identity** because they exactly match `S-157` and `S-159`.
2. The 1928 report supplies a new direct exact-address event at **503 Main on 1 March 1928** (`E-348`).
3. The 1928 exact-address call does **not** improve the earliest rebuilt-building endpoint beyond the **May 1925 Sanborn**, which already proves a distinct 503 restaurant. It does, however, independently confirm continued municipal recognition of 503 as an address in 1928.
4. The CCHS P‑267 photograph remains cataloged only **1924–1928** (`S-312` / `E-284`), not exactly 1924. These annual-report aerials do not narrow that range.
5. The 1929 report adds Fifth/Main fire-call context plus the 28-light Main Street improvement (`E-349`).
6. None of these reports identifies the proprietor behind the City's later statement that 1926 newspaper advertisements named the **Kwality Restaurant**.
7. Review of these four reports is a source-class completion only. It does not make 1925, 1926, 1928, or 1929 ONLINE COMPLETE under `RESEARCH-STANDARD.md`.
