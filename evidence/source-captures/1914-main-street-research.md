# 1914 Main Street Research

Status: **in progress**

## Initial confirmed findings

### L. Hylton — 503 Main

**Oregon City Courier, 12 February 1914, p. 15**

Canonical source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-02-12/ed-1/seq-15/

A business profile explicitly states that **L. Hylton** was located at **503 Main Street** and sold cigars, tobacco, smokers' supplies, and soft drinks.

This is direct primary evidence and gives us a firm 1914 occupant/use for 503 Main. It also shows a clear change from the H. H./H. N. Smith pool-room evidence documented in 1912.

### G. H. Young — 507 Main

**Oregon City Courier, 16 April 1914, p. 4**

Canonical source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-04-16/ed-1/seq-4/

A city-business listing explicitly gives **G. H. Young — 507 Main**.

This converts the 1913 high-confidence continuity inference into a direct 1914 exact-address anchor and confirms that Young remained at 507 at least into April 1914.

### H. P. Brightbill — active Main Street grocery

The same April 1914 business-listing page names **H. P. Brightbill** among Oregon City merchants. His exact street number is not preserved in the available OCR snippet, but the surrounding archive chronology places Brightbill at 509 Main before and after 1914. Continue searching for a 1914 exact-address print source rather than relying solely on continuity.
