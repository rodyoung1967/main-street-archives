## Prohibition chronology relevant to Oregon City

For project interpretation, distinguish Oregon statewide prohibition from federal Prohibition:

- Oregon voters adopted statewide prohibition before the federal period; statewide prohibition took effect in **1916**.
- Federal Prohibition began in **January 1920**.
- Oregon repealed its own prohibition regime in **1932**.
- Federal Prohibition ended in **December 1933**.

For Main Street research, **1916–1933** is the practical prohibition-era window, with the important distinction that 1916–1919 is Oregon-state prohibition before national prohibition.
