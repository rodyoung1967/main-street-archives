# Fred J. Remus Jr. disambiguation pass — 9 September 2026

Research stream: **503 Main / Kwality Cafe — Fred Remus identity control**  
Status: **UNVERIFIED IDENTITY LEAD / CONFLICTING 1951 LOCATION CONTROLS — DO NOT MERGE WITH OREGON CITY FRED REMUS**

## Purpose

Test whether the **Fred Remus** whom Oregon City's later historic-resource survey identifies as a **1953 tenant at 902 Fifth Street and cook at Kwality Cafe** can be connected to, or distinguished from, a contemporaneous Pacific Northwest science-fiction fan named **Fred J. Remus Jr.**

This pass is an identity-disambiguation exercise only. Shared name and regional proximity are not sufficient to merge the men.

## Established Oregon City control

The City of Oregon City's historic-resource record for **902 Fifth Street** states that in **1953** tenant **Fred Remus** was a **cook at the Kwality Cafe**.

Official City source:

- https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/7857/902_5th

Classification: **OFFICIAL SECONDARY / HISTORIC-RESOURCE SYNTHESIS** for the 1953 tenant/occupation statement. The current online City source does not give Fred Remus a middle initial, age, spouse, birthplace, or prior address.

## Candidate Fred J. Remus Jr. — fannish / military controls

### By April 1949 — exact-name fandom control

A later reproduction of the International Science Fiction Correspondence Club charter lists **Fred J. Remus Jr.** among the charter members and dates the charter/notarization to **7 April 1949**.

Source surfaced in FANAC's October 1953 *Explorer* scan:

- https://www.fanac.org/fanzines/Explorer/explorer_higgs_1953-10.pdf

This establishes an exact-name fandom control, not an Oregon/Washington residence or occupational link to Kwality Cafe.

### October 1949 / January 1950 — `Tales From Uncle Remus`

FANAC bibliographic indexing records **Fred Remus** as editor/creator of the fanzine **_Tales From Uncle Remus_**, with issues represented for approximately **October 1949** and **January 1950**.

Bibliographic source:

- https://www.fanac.org/Fannish_Reference_Works/Bibliographies/Pavlat_Evans.pdf

A separate February 1951 fan-directory source explicitly links **Sgt. Fred Remus Jr.** to `Tales From Uncle Remus`, strongly supporting continuity between the full-name military/fan listing and the fanzine editor. It does not connect him to Oregon City.

### June 1950 — marriage-status lead

The June 1950 issue of *The Explorer* contains a fandom-news item reporting that **“Sarge Fred Remus”** was taking a wife around that time.

Source:

- https://fanac.org/fanzines/Explorer/Explorer195006.pdf

The PDF text layer was inspected and the passage is on the second scanned page. The web screenshot renderer failed on the PDF during this continuation, so classify the passage **OCR/TEXT ONLY / SCAN VISUAL CERTIFICATION OPEN**. The item does not name the wife or give an address.

This is direct contemporary directory-text evidence that the candidate Fred J. Remus was being listed as an Air Force sergeant at Moses Lake in early 1951. It is not evidence that he lived in Morton, Washington, or Oregon City.

### April 1951 — Chanute Field, Illinois conflict/control

The April 1951 issue of *The National Fantasy Fan* contains an NFFF membership roster listing:

- **Sgt. Fred J. Remus Jr.**;
- a service number rendered by the text layer as **AF 19273700**;
- a military postal address at **Chanute Field, Illinois**.

Source:

- https://www.fanac.org/fanzines/TNFF/TNFF-1002.pdf

The same issue states that the membership roster was compiled by NFFF secretary-treasurer Bill Berger and instructs members to report address changes. This supports treating the roster as an intended contact-address source, while still allowing publication lag or stale member data.

The PDF text layer was inspected; the screenshot renderer again failed with a cache miss. Classification: **OCR/TEXT ONLY / SCAN VISUAL CERTIFICATION OPEN**.

The service-number transcription differs by one digit from the February source (`19278700` versus `19273700`). Because both sources give the same uncommon full name, military rank, and near-contemporary context, they are a strong candidate for the same serviceman, but the number discrepancy must remain unresolved until the original scans or an independent military record are checked.

### Interpretation of the two 1951 locations

The February and April publications create **CONFLICTING / MULTIPLE CONTEMPORARY CONTACT-LOCATION CONTROLS**:

- February publication: Moses Lake, Washington;
- April publication: Chanute Field, Illinois.

Do **not** automatically narrate this as a February→April transfer. Either publication may contain older contact information, and the NFFF roster itself warns about address changes. The safe conclusion is only that the candidate Fred J. Remus Jr. was associated in contemporary fan directories with both an Air Force weather-station address at Moses Lake and a Chanute Field military postal address during the 1951 publication interval.

This makes the candidate's precise residence/posting chronology unresolved.

## Later fandom continuity controls

Public FANAC scans/indexes show a **Fred Remus** still active in SAPS/fandom in the mid-1950s:

- September 1954 *Nandu* discusses a Fred Remus `Saga for SAPS` project;
- December 1954 **_The Love of SAPS_** is indexed to Fred Remus in SAPS mailing 30;
- March 1955 *Outsiders* publishes a piece by Fred Remus;
- June 1955 **_Furry_** is indexed to Fred Remus in SAPS mailing 32;
- Winter 1956 *Sinisterra* identifies **Fred Remus Jr.** in a convention-photograph caption.

These sources make continued fandom activity plausible for the same Fred J. Remus Jr., but none of the currently recovered text provides the missing **1952–1954 residence** needed to bridge him to or separate him definitively from Oregon City's 1953 cook.

The convention-photo caption is text-layer evidence only in this pass; no successful scan screenshot was obtained.

## Current identity assessment

### What is supported

A contemporary fan/military person named **Fred J. Remus Jr.** existed and was active in fandom by 1949–1951; he was referred to as a sergeant, was associated with `Tales From Uncle Remus`, and appears in early-1951 directory material with Moses Lake and Chanute Field military addresses.

### What is NOT supported

No recovered source currently proves that this Fred J. Remus Jr. was:

- the **Fred Remus at 902 Fifth Street, Oregon City, in 1953**;
- the **Kwality Cafe cook**;
- related to **Margaret Remus**;
- connected to **Albert D. Flor**;
- connected to **Morton, Washington** or the Morton Wheel Cafe;
- resident in Oregon in 1953.

The correct classification is therefore:

**UNVERIFIED IDENTITY LEAD / DISAMBIGUATION CONTROL — DO NOT MERGE.**

The multiple 1951 military-contact locations make a simplistic Washington→Oregon continuity narrative less secure, but they do not exclude a later Oregon move by 1953.
