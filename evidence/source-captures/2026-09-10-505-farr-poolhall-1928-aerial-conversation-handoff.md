### 505 pool-hall / recreation chronology

Preserve the current chain and evidence boundaries:

- **1925:** visually verified Sanborn maps 505 Main as **billiards/cigars**; operator unnamed.
- Oregon City's official historic-resource evidence for 401 Third identifies **Edwin F. Farr and Alice Farr** as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main Street**.
- The same City source says the Farrs acquired their 401 Third residence in **1934**. That residence date is **not** the pool hall opening date.
- CCHS photograph `P-1128`, independently cataloged as **1936**, combines with the separate City identification to support a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE** that Farr's Pool Hall was operating at 505 in the photographed 1936 state. It is not an opening-date proof.

- **1950 Sanborn:** continues billiards/cigars/restaurant/beer-related use at 505.

The exact start of Farr's Pool Hall and the exact **Farr → Harr** transition remain unresolved.
