# Oregon City Shoe Store / Justin S. Lageson / 508 Main — separation from Schoenheinz shoe business

Date reviewed: 19 September 2026

## Research purpose

The 29 March and 5 April 1912 *Oregon City Courier* issues contain advertisements for an **Oregon City Shoe Store** without printing an address or proprietor. Because the active 503 Main water-ledger investigation now has a probable `C. Schoenheinz` tenant transcription and separate evidence of a Schoenheinz shoe business, this pass tests whether the generic Oregon City Shoe Store name belongs to the Schoenheinz strand.

Result: **do not merge them.** Contemporary primary advertisements establish Oregon City Shoe Store as a separately named shoe business associated with **Justin S. Lageson** and, by 26 April 1912, exact **508 Main Street**.

## 28 January 1910 — Justin S. Lageson / Oregon City Shoe Store

Primary source: *Oregon City Courier*, 28 January 1910, p.6 / sequence 6.  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1910-01-28/ed-1/seq-6/

The original scan was visually inspected. A large bordered advertisement directly reads:
- **Oregon City Shoe Store**
- **JUSTIN S. LAGESON**
- **Opposite Postoffice**

The advertisement is headed as the store's first semi-annual clearance sale of shoes.

A separate visually/textually recoverable 1 April 1910 *Oregon City Enterprise* advertisement is even more explicit, printing:
- **Oregon City Shoe Store**
- **JUSTIN S. LAGESON, Prop.**
- **MAIN STREET**

Primary page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1910-04-01/ed-1/seq-5/

This directly establishes Justin S. Lageson as proprietor of Oregon City Shoe Store in 1910.

## 26 April 1912 — exact 508 Main address

Primary source: *Oregon City Courier*, 26 April 1912, p.8 / sequence 8.  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-04-26/ed-1/seq-8/

The original scan was visually inspected. The large shoe advertisement directly reads:
- **Oregon City Shoe Store**
- **508 Main St.**
- **Opposite Postoffice**

The page does not print Lageson's name inside this 1912 advertisement, so continuous proprietorship from 1910 through April 1912 is not assumed from these two endpoints alone.

## April 1912 annual-audit controls

- 29 March 1912 p.5: Oregon City Shoe Store advertisement; no address/proprietor.
- 5 April 1912: Oregon City Shoe Store appears in the S&H Green Trading Stamp merchant roster and in a standalone shoe advertisement; no address/proprietor on those appearances.
- 26 April 1912 p.8: exact **508 Main St. / Opposite Postoffice**.

These establish the named Oregon City Shoe Store as an active 1912 business and provide an exact-address endpoint by 26 April.

## Separation from Schoenheinz

Independent primary evidence already in the project directly advertises **C. Schoenheinz, proprietor**, of the **Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop**, at **716 Main between Sixth and Seventh** in 1911.

Therefore:
- **Oregon City Shoe Store** = distinct named business, Justin S. Lageson directly proprietor in 1910, exact 508 Main by 26 Apr. 1912.
- **Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop** = C. Schoenheinz proprietor, 716 Main in 1911.

Do not use the generic March/April 1912 Oregon City Shoe Store advertisements to support a Schoenheinz-at-503 hypothesis. A later sale, rename, relocation, or ownership change would require its own direct bridge.

## Classification

- Oregon City Shoe Store + Justin S. Lageson proprietor in 1910: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT**.
- Oregon City Shoe Store at 508 Main on 26 Apr. 1912: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT**.
- Lageson continuous proprietor through 26 Apr. 1912: **UNRESOLVED** from these endpoints alone.
- Identity with C. Schoenheinz / Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop: **REJECTED AS AN UNSUPPORTED MERGE**; contemporary trade names and documented locations are separate.
