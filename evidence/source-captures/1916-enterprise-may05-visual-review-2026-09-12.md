## Federal-building / post-office control — page 4

Page 4 contains **“CHANCES FOR FEDERAL BUILDING HELD SLIM.”** Direct visual inspection shows a Washington-datelined report about proposed federal public-building appropriations. The article says Oregon City was among Oregon communities in Representative Hawley's bills and gives Treasury estimates for **building and site combined**. For **Oregon City and Corvallis** that combined estimate is **$70,000**. It separately says site estimates were **$10,000** in each listed city except **Oregon City and Ashland, where ground was estimated at $15,000**.

Subtracting the stated Oregon City site estimate from the stated combined estimate yields a **$55,000 building component**. That $55,000 figure is arithmetic from the article's two printed figures, not a separately printed sentence in this page. It is consistent with the previously documented 24 March 1916 federal-building proposal, while this page supplies the useful additional control that the Treasury's Oregon City **site estimate was $15,000**.

The article does **not** identify an Oregon City site, street address, parcel, owner, acquisition, construction start, completed post-office move, or connection to 501/503/505/505½/507 Main. It therefore must not be used as evidence that the proposed federal building/post office occupied or acquired the target frontage.
