# 1916 Main Street research — initial collection

Status: **active / incomplete**

This file begins the 1916 pass. Under `RESEARCH-STANDARD.md`, OCR/search results are discovery evidence only; the year cannot be certified until every available page of every relevant Oregon City newspaper has been visually reviewed from the actual scan.

## Fifth & Main continuity

### R. L. Holman and T. P. Randall
30 Mar 1916, p2 includes:

- **R. L. Holman and T. P. Randall, Leading Undertakers, Fifth and Main St.**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-03-30/ed-1/seq-2.pdf

This continues the Fifth-and-Main undertaker presence documented in 1915. It does **not** by itself establish a numbered address such as 501 Main.

## Other useful downtown anchors

The 1916 classified directories also repeatedly identify:

- F. F. Theroux — pianos and sewing machines — **519 Main**
- Price Bros. — clothing — **527 Main**
- James Wilkinson town shop — **612 Main**
- Fashion Livery — **4th and Main**
- Electric Hotel — described as between **4th and 5th on Main**

These are useful for reconstructing the commercial sequence and checking address numbering consistency.

## 503 and 505 Main

No direct 1916 exact-address hit for **503 Main** or **505 Main** has yet been recovered in the initial targeted searches.

This is **not negative evidence**. The full 1916 Courier and Oregon City Enterprise visual page-by-page review has not been performed, and targeted OCR/search can miss ads, display type, damaged print, or badly recognized address lines.
