## O.K. Barber Shop / Ed Johnson

### Historical-marker lead

Historical Marker Database records an Oregon City **OK Barber Shop** marker. The marker says the shop was built by **Ed Johnson** as a full-service barber shop with four chairs and bathing facilities. HMDB places the marker at/near modern **705 Main Street** / Main near 7th.

A photographic/transcription route records the marker heading as **“OK Barber Shop — Circa 1907.”**

These are **secondary historical-marker/building-location evidence**, not sufficient by themselves to assign a 1922 address.

HMDB:
https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=8746

### Primary Ed Johnson barber evidence

*Oregon City Courier*, 17 Feb 1916, p.9, carries an Ed Johnson proprietor barber advertisement emphasizing sanitary service, skilled barbers and clean baths:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-02-17/ed-1/seq-9/

The same Ed Johnson barber advertising is recoverable 23 Nov 1916, p.8:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-11-23/ed-1/seq-8/

This is **direct primary evidence** that Ed Johnson operated an Oregon City barber business in 1916, but those advertisements do not themselves print the O.K. trade name or address.

### Earlier Johnson barber continuity

Fresh searches recovered:
- *Oregon City Enterprise*, 4 Oct 1901, p.8 — Troy Laundry notice directs orders to **Johnson's barber shop**.
- *Oregon City Enterprise*, 1 Nov 1901, p.8 — same.
- *Clackamas County Record*, 21 May 1903, p.1 — cleaning/dyeing ad uses **Johnson's Barber Shop** as Oregon City order location.
- *Clackamas County Record*, 23 Jul 1903, p.1 — same.

These are direct for a Johnson-named barber shop in Oregon City, but the recovered text does not print a street address or Ed Johnson's given name. Treat linkage to the later Ed Johnson/O.K. shop as a **strong continuity hypothesis**, not direct identity proof.

### June 1922 O.K. control

The already-known *Banner-Courier*, 8 Jun 1922, p.10, names **O. K. Barber Shop** among Oregon City businesses, separately from Gilbert Miller:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-06-08/ed-1/seq-10/

It does not print proprietor/address.

### Current O.K. inference

The combined sequence is:
- Johnson's barber shop documented in Oregon City by 1901–03.
- Ed Johnson directly operates an Oregon City barber business with baths in 1916.
- Historical marker identifies O.K. Barber Shop as built by Ed Johnson, modern 705 Main, circa 1907.
- O.K. Barber Shop is directly listed in Oregon City in June 1922.

This creates a **strong location/identity hypothesis** that the June 1922 O.K. Barber Shop was the long-running Johnson shop near Seventh/Main and was separate from the Harding-property barber near Fifth/Main.

Do **not** promote that to documented exclusion until a contemporary source explicitly joins O.K./Ed Johnson to 705 Main or Seventh/Main in the relevant period.
