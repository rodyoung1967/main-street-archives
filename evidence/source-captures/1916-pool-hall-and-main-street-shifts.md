# 1916 pool-hall and Main Street business-shift leads

Status: **active lead file / not visually certified**

Under `RESEARCH-STANDARD.md`, the newspaper material below is OCR/text/search-derived unless and until the original page scan is visually inspected. These findings are useful for narrowing the 503/505 search but do not complete the 1916 visual review.

## Raasch & Lamb were operating a pool/billiards business elsewhere on Main Street

The *Oregon City Courier*, 1 June 1916, p.5, reports a substantial rearrangement of businesses in the **Andresen Building on Main Street**. The article says:

- Burmeister & Andresen would expand into the Jones Drug Store room.
- Jones Drug Store would move into the Portland Railway, Light & Power/Dr. Schilling room.
- **Raasch & Lamb would vacate the pool hall next to the entrance of the Andresen Building and move into a basement room** after city permission.
- Greaves & McNeil and E. P. Elliott & Sons were displaced from that basement space.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-06-01/ed-1/seq-5/

The *Courier*, 22 June 1916, p.1, independently confirms the municipal issue: the council had recently granted Raasch & Lamb a temporary permit to move their pool hall to the **basement of the Andresen Building**, despite an ordinance requiring pool/billiard halls to be on the ground floor and visible from the street.

### Why this matters for 503/505

503 Main had previously housed cigar/tobacco/soft-drink activity, and 505 had a history of clothing and later billiards-related use in other periods. A 1916 pool/billiards operator could therefore look superficially plausible as a candidate for the unresolved addresses.

The June 1916 relocation evidence instead places **Raasch & Lamb in the Andresen Building**, not at a presently demonstrated 503 or 505 Main address. Unless future numbering evidence ties part of the Andresen Building to 503/505—which currently appears unlikely—Raasch & Lamb should **not** be used as the default 1916 occupant for either target address.

This is useful exclusion/narrowing evidence, not proof of who did occupy 503 or 505.

## Current narrowing conclusion

The 1916 target block remains unresolved at 503 and 505, but this pass removes one attractive false lead:

- **Raasch & Lamb = confirmed 1916 pool/billiards and smokers business, but located in the Andresen Building.**
- **Champion Smith & Telford = confirmed at 502 Main**, useful as an opposite-side numbering anchor.
- 503 and 505 still require direct exact-address evidence, preferably from the 1916-17 Sohns & Woodbeck directory, city license records, lease/sale notices, or visually reviewed newspaper pages.
