# 505 Main — 1953–1956 operator-transition online closeout and manual retrieval routes

Date reviewed: **7 September 2026**

Status: **TARGETED PUBLIC-ONLINE CLOSEOUT / RETRIEVAL-GAP REFINEMENT; NO NEW 505 OPERATOR, SELLER, BUYER, LICENSEE, TRADE NAME, TENANT, TELEPHONE SUBSCRIBER, OR BUILDING OWNER IDENTIFIED.**

Purpose: continue the unresolved **Harr's Recreation Center → Well's Recreation → unnamed 20 November 1955 beer/pool business sale → later 505 Tavern** chronology at **505 Main Street, Oregon City**, and determine whether the remaining late-1955/1956 City liquor record or a 1953–1956 assumed-business-name filing can be recovered through public online access before assigning a manual action.

## Evidence boundary carried into this pass

The current defensible sequence remains:

- **late 1948:** primary municipal text directly identifies **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**;
- **1950:** Sanborn maps **billiards / cigars / beer** at 505, without operator identity;
- **1953:** the visually reviewed May 2000 Oregon SHPO survey says the 1953 city directory lists **“Well's Recreation”** at 505, but the underlying Salisbury directory page and proprietor remain unrecovered;
- **20 November 1955:** the original *Eugene Register-Guard* page visually verifies an unnamed long-established beer/pool business for sale at **505 Main**, with three pool tables and contact telephone **9179**; the ad names neither seller nor business;
- **19 July 1956:** an Oregon City special meeting approved a single-business liquor license for **Carlise Grocery, 102 Molalla Avenue**; this is a procedure control only and has no 505 connection;
- **11 November 1965:** City Commission liquor material directly names **505 Tavern**.

Nothing in this pass bridges those points into a continuous proprietor narrative.

## 4. Clackamas County assumed-business-name series — exact 1953–1956 custody gap

The Oregon State Archives **Clackamas County Inventory** describes an **Assumed Business Names Registers** series covering **1928–1964**. The official series description states that these records may include the:

- assumed business name;
- description of business;
- filing date; and
- **business owner**,

with related filing/index information.

Official inventory:

`https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/county-records-guide/Pages/clackamas-inventory.aspx`

The inventoried holdings exposed online are discontinuous around the target period:

- Oregon State Archives: assumed-business-name certificates, **1928–1935**;
- Clackamas County Clerk / Recording public research area: assumed-business-name record volumes **10–13, 1959–1964**, four reels of microfilm.

The crucial **1936–1958** interval — including 1953–1956 — is therefore a **CUSTODY / ACCESS GAP in the public inventory presentation**. Do not interpret the missing listed volumes as proof that no assumed-name filing was made.

A surviving 1953–1956 register/index entry for **Well's Recreation** could be decisive because the series description says business-owner and filing-date fields may be present. A filing would establish only the registered assumed-name relationship shown by that record. It would not by itself prove liquor-license ownership, actual daily operation on a particular date, building ownership, tenancy, or completion of the November 1955 business sale.

### Search keys if the missing interval is located

Preserve separate searches for:

- `Well's Recreation`;
- `Wells Recreation`;
- `Well Recreation`;
- `Well`, `Wells`, `Welle`, `Welsh` as discovery variants only;
- `Harr's Recreation Center` / Carl R. Harr;

- any cancellation, amendment, successor, withdrawal, or changed-owner notation linked by the record itself.

Do not normalize a spelling variant into canonical **Well's Recreation** without source support.
