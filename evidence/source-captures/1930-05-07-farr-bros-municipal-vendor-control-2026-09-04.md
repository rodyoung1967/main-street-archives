## Evidence meaning

This record directly supports only the following limited proposition at the present verification level:

- a municipal claims record for April 1930, presented 7 May 1930, contains a payee written **Farr Bros.** associated in the OCR/table extraction with **Clover** for **$2.50**.

It does **not** identify:

- Edwin F. Farr or Edwin Franklin Farr;
- Alice Farr;
- Farr's Pool Hall;
- 505 Main or 505½ Main;
- a pool/billiard license;
- a pool-hall premises;
- a business-license application, renewal, transfer, receipt, or denial.

No identity bridge is made between this `Farr Bros.` municipal vendor occurrence and the separately documented Farr Brothers Store, Louis/Clarence Farr, Edwin/Alice Farr, or any other Farr entity/person. It is nevertheless useful disconfirming/search-control evidence: **bare Farr/Farr Bros. hits in 1930 municipal material must not be assumed to concern the 505 Main pool hall.**
