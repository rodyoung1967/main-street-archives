## 1. Library of Congress current guide — Business Group holdings

Current Library of Congress research guide:

`https://guides.loc.gov/united-states-city-telephone-directories/city-directories/business`

The LOC page states that the U.S. business directories in this microfilm collection were published between **1902 and 1935** and are shelved in the Library's **Microform and Electronic Resources Center**, in drawers labeled **Business Group**.

Under **Oregon**, the guide lists:

> `Oregon / Washington Gazetteer - reel 135-146 - Business Group | 1903-20, 1923-26, 1931-32`

The same listing appears under **Washington**.

Classification: **CURRENT OFFICIAL INSTITUTIONAL HOLDINGS / ACCESS-GUIDE FACT**.

Historical consequence: LOC is now a demonstrated institutional microfilm route for the target **1923–1926 Oregon/Washington Gazetteer** interval. This materially improves the earlier directory-recovery picture.

### Reconciliation with the earlier LOC per-state metadata no-hit

The prior telephone-directory provenance pass searched LOC's separate Oregon per-state directory metadata dataset for `Oregon City`, `Clackamas`, `1926`, and `1927` and returned no target row. That result remains valid **for that dataset only**.

It must not be read as `LOC has no target directory`. The Business Directories guide explicitly says these particular business directories are listed on their own page and are **not included on the separate state pages**. The two results are therefore different catalog/collection scopes, not contradictory evidence.

This is an important correction to source-routing interpretation, not a historical occupancy correction.

## 2. Gale/Cengage `City Directories of the United States — Author Index`

Public Cengage-hosted PDF:

`https://assets.cengage.com/gale/psm/3065000A.pdf`

The actual PDF page image was visually inspected. PDF page index **565** (printed page **566**) visibly lists the Oregon/Washington Gazetteer sequence:

- `1919/20-1923/24` — `Missing years: 1921, 1922.` — **Reel: 27**;
- `1923/24-1925/26` — **Reel: 28**;
- `1925/26-1931/32` — `Missing years: 1927, 1928, 1929, 1930.` — **Reel: 29**;
- `1931-1932` — **Reel: 30**.

Classification: **VISUALLY VERIFIED MICROFILM AUTHOR-INDEX METADATA**.

The target **1925/26** biennium is therefore explicitly represented in the Gale/Cengage sequence at the boundary between the index entries for **reel 28** and **reel 29**. Until the reel contents/title frames are inspected, do not assume whether the complete 1925/26 volume is duplicated on both reels, split across them, or represented through another filming convention. The index proves the year-range/reel associations, not page-level reel content.

## 3. Do not merge the reel-number systems

The LOC guide uses **Business Group reels 135–146** for the broader Oregon/Washington Gazetteer collection.

The Gale/Cengage Author Index uses a local sequence in which the target range appears as **reels 28 and 29**.

These are **not safely interchangeable reel numbers**. No source in this pass provides a one-to-one mapping between LOC's Business Group numbering and the Gale/Cengage Author Index numbering.

Therefore:

- cite `LOC Business Group reels 135–146` when describing the LOC holding;
- cite `Gale/Cengage Author Index reel 28 / reel 29 year-range entries` when describing that index;
- do not tell a future researcher to request `LOC reel 28` or equate `Gale reel 135` without an explicit collection-level mapping.

## 4. Why this matters for 503/505

The project has already established that the 1925–1926 Polk Oregon/Washington Gazetteer is a historically relevant source class:

- the official 1990 Oregon City Park Place context statement visually cites the **1925–1926 R. L. Polk Oregon and Washington Gazetteer and Business Directory** among Koler/Morrison research sources;
- Spokane Public Library and University of Idaho Special Collections independently document surviving physical copies;
- CiNii provides a bibliographic control for the 1925–26 volume;
- auction descriptions indicate these volumes contain resident/business listings, occupations/addresses, classified business material and advertisements.

The LOC/Gale discovery adds a major independent **microfilm preservation/access route**. It does **not** yet establish whether the Oregon City section contains house-by-house street listings, which particular businesses appear, or whether the target May 1925 Sanborn occupants can be identified from it.

## 5. Target inspection protocol if a readable reel/derivative is recovered

First establish source identity from the microfilm title/copyright/front-matter frames:

1. exact title;
2. R. L. Polk publisher/imprint;
3. stated biennium/publication year;
4. geographic coverage;
5. contents/abbreviation structure;
6. pagination and whether the directory has an Oregon City subsection, classified buyers' guide, or street-address index.

Then inspect, preserving exact page/frame locators:

- Oregon City entries for **503 Main, 505 Main, 505½, 501, 507 and nearby Main/Fifth controls**, if house/street indexing exists;
- alphabetical names: **Farr, Jager/Jager variants, Leland, Little, Timms, Kwality/Quality, Holman, Pace**, and known neighboring controls;
- trade categories: **restaurants/cafes, billiards/pool, cigars/tobacco, soft drinks**, and relevant business-sale/advertising material.

A residence/address association must not be converted into business proprietorship, and a classified business listing must not be converted into property ownership.
