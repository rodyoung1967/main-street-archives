# Red Front Clothing Store — 507 Main — Aurora Observer lead

Date recorded: 2026-09-14

## New lead supplied by project steward

The project steward reports finding a Red Front Clothing Store advertisement in the **Aurora Observer, 22 September 1921, p. 3**, which prints the address **507 Main St.** for the Red Front Clothing Store in Oregon City.

This is potentially decisive for the previously unresolved numbered location of Red Front. It also materially improves the July 1922 Fifth/Main fire reconstruction because Red Front is independently named in the July 1922 fire reporting.

## Spatial implications

The reported 507 address is highly compatible with the emerging frontage reconstruction:

- 501 — burned in July 1922.
- 503 — strong cross-source reconstruction as the Miller shoe-store / vacant former-Schiermann-tailor fire-origin building.
- 505 — adjoining pool/billiard premises; damaged by water/roof fire but not destroyed, with contemporary reporting crediting fireproof construction for stopping/saving the building north of the fire.
- **507 — reported Red Front Clothing Store exact address in September 1921.**

This may explain why Red Front suffered stock/fire damage while 505 survived: Red Front need not have occupied the fire-origin building or the pool-hall storefront. The precise July 1922 fire path and whether Red Front remained at 507 still require direct evidence.

## Ownership / identity controls

The July 21, 1922 Oregon City Enterprise visually inspected by the steward identifies **L. Wolf** as owner of the Red Front clothing store and also says Wolf had recently purchased the shoe-store building in which the fire started. Keep these as separate propositions:

1. L. Wolf owned the Red Front business in July 1922.
2. L. Wolf had recently purchased the shoe-store/fire-origin building.
3. A September 1921 ad reportedly places Red Front at 507 Main.

Do not infer that Wolf necessarily owned the 507 real estate merely because he owned the Red Front business.

The existing working hypothesis that L. Wolf may be the Wolf of the earlier Wolf & Miller concern remains PROBABLE / UNPROVEN pending a direct identity source.
