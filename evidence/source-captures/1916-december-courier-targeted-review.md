## 14 December — 504 Main
Page 20 states that **C. C. Wade purchased Sam Oldstein's interest in the pool and billiard parlor at 504 Main Street**.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-14/ed-1/seq-20/

This establishes a pool/billiard parlor at 504 Main by 14 Dec, Oldstein's prior interest, and Wade's purchase of that interest. It does not establish the formal business name, opening date, building owner, or any connection to 503/505 or Raasch & Lamb.

## 21 December — repeated notice
The same notice repeats, but OCR renders the address as **604 Main Street**. Because the 14 Dec notice clearly gives 504 and the wording is otherwise the same continuing notice, 604 is treated as a probable OCR error pending scan inspection.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-21/ed-1/seq-8/

## Raasch & Lamb are a separate pool-hall line of evidence
The Courier's recurring `Picture Tells the Story` business grid advertises **POOL AND BILLIARDS / Everything for Smokers / RAASCH & LAMB** repeatedly during 1916, including 30 Mar, 1 Jun, 3 Aug and again at year-end on **28 Dec**.

Representative sources:
- 30 Mar: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-03-30/ed-1/seq-6/
- 1 Jun: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-06-01/ed-1/seq-5.pdf
- 3 Aug: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-08-03/ed-1/seq-8.pdf
- 28 Dec: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-28/ed-1/seq-3/

This continuous named advertising overlaps the **14 Dec Wade/Oldstein transaction at 504 Main**. That overlap materially strengthens the conclusion that **Raasch & Lamb and the Oldstein/Wade 504 Main pool parlor were separate establishments**, unless future original-scan or directory evidence proves an unusual ownership/management relationship. The project should not merge them by default.

The `Picture Tells the Story` Raasch & Lamb ads recovered here do **not** print a numbered address, so this evidence still does not assign Raasch & Lamb to 503, 505, or 504. Separate evidence locating the Andresen Building at 619 Main is therefore important to the ongoing Raasch & Lamb address investigation.

## 14 December — Cruden at 612 Main
December advertising identifies **Cruden, optometrist, at 612 Main**, fitting the fall `Successor to Wm. A. Schilling` campaign.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-14/ed-1/seq-8.pdf

Working sequence:
- Jan–May: Schilling at 617 Main
- June: Schilling moves to 612 Main
- by 28 Sep: successor advertising at 612
- Nov: successor campaign continues
- by 14 Dec: advertiser represented as Cruden

## 21 December — additional streetscape context
Page 3 includes Oregon City Garage at **Main and 4th Streets** and the recurring Raasch & Lamb pool-and-billiards advertisement in the same business-grid composition. This is useful context but does not by itself identify the pool hall's numbered address; the 504 Main Wade/Oldstein notice remains separate direct address evidence.

## 28 December — additional year-end context
Page 3 again carries the `Picture Tells the Story` business grid, including **Raasch & Lamb — Pool and Billiards — Everything for Smokers**. This proves the Raasch & Lamb advertising campaign continued after the 14 Dec Wade purchase at 504 Main and is the strongest current evidence that the two pool-hall lines should remain separate.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-28/ed-1/seq-3/

Page 4 discusses W. P. Hawley's acquisition/use of Main Street property for industrial expansion. This is broader Main Street context and is not evidence for a 503/505 occupant.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-28/ed-1/seq-4/
