## 14 December — 504 Main
Page 20 states that **C. C. Wade purchased Sam Oldstein's interest in the pool and billiard parlor at 504 Main Street**.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-14/ed-1/seq-20/

This establishes a pool/billiard parlor at 504 Main by 14 Dec, Oldstein's prior interest, and Wade's purchase of that interest. It does not establish the formal business name, opening date, building owner, or any connection to 503/505 or Raasch & Lamb.

## 21 December — repeated notice
The same notice repeats, but OCR renders the address as **604 Main Street**. Because the 14 Dec notice clearly gives 504 and the wording is otherwise the same continuing notice, 604 is treated as a probable OCR error pending scan inspection.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-21/ed-1/seq-8/

## 14 December — Cruden at 612 Main
December advertising identifies **Cruden, optometrist, at 612 Main**, fitting the fall `Successor to Wm. A. Schilling` campaign.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-14/ed-1/seq-8.pdf

Working sequence:
- Jan–May: Schilling at 617 Main
- June: Schilling moves to 612 Main
- by 28 Sep: successor advertising at 612
- Nov: successor campaign continues
- by 14 Dec: advertiser represented as Cruden

## 21 December — additional streetscape context
Page 3 includes Oregon City Garage at **Main and 4th Streets** and a pool-and-billiards advertisement in the same page composition. This is useful context but does not by itself identify the pool hall's numbered address; the 504 Main Wade/Oldstein notice remains the direct address evidence.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-21/ed-1/seq-3/

## 28 December — additional year-end context
Page 4 discusses W. P. Hawley's acquisition/use of Main Street property for industrial expansion. This is broader Main Street context and is not evidence for a 503/505 occupant.
Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-28/ed-1/seq-4/
