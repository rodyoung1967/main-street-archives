## 29 October 1945 City Commission record

Official Oregon City WebDrawer record:

`https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4730420/File/document`

The four-page municipal record contains the **29 October 1945 special meeting** on page 4 of the PDF. Its readable text states that **Ordinance No. 1325** was read for the first time and ordered published.

The ordinance is titled as regulating the operation of **eating and drinking establishments in Oregon City** by adopting the State of Oregon sanitary code for eating and drinking establishments, Chapter 432, Oregon Laws 1945.

Its emergency clause states that Oregon City did not have an adequate license regulating the operation of eating and drinking establishments and therefore declared the measure immediately necessary for public peace, health and safety.

A screenshot attempt for the page failed in the current research environment, so the 29 October wording remains **PRIMARY MUNICIPAL OCR/TEXT ONLY** rather than visually verified under project policy.

## Later official-code cross-check

Oregon City's official 1963 Municipal Code carries the eating/drinking-establishment chapter forward and cites **Ord. 1325; 11-7-45** for adoption of the sanitary code.

Official code:

`https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/11782/1963-Oregon-City-Municipal-Code`

The same later chapter requires a City license before operating a food establishment and states that the Recorder may not issue such a license until the application has been investigated and approved in writing by the Chief of Police or Commission. The 1963 codification is useful as a records-route clue, but this capture does **not** assume that every later procedural detail was already present in exactly that form in November 1945.

## Research significance

This creates a useful administrative breakpoint in the same general period as the unresolved Farr-to-Harr transition:

- the City itself stated in late October 1945 that its eating/drinking-establishment licensing framework was inadequate;
- Ordinance 1325 is officially cross-referenced to **7 November 1945**;
- therefore late-1945 and 1946 Recorder/Police/Commission records concerning food/drink licenses, inspections, approvals and renewals deserve targeted review alongside OLCC liquor-license records.

This is a **records-route finding**, not occupancy evidence. Nothing in the recovered Ordinance 1325 text names Edwin F. Farr, Alice Farr, Farr's Pool Hall, Carl R. Harr, Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main or 505½ Main.

The ordinance also must not be treated as proof that a pool hall necessarily required a food-establishment license. That depends on the actual services/use of the premises and the contemporaneous licensing provisions.
