## Why this follow-up matters

The project has not recovered an accessible online scan of the Oregon City portion of the 1947–1948 Clackamas County directory. The directory remains one of the strongest independent sources for identifying who occupied or operated 505 Main in the immediate pre-late-1948 period.

The Genealogical Forum of Oregon (GFO) currently advertises a research/lookup service in which volunteers will search or copy identified GFO resources for up to one hour. The posted fee is **$15 per half hour ($15 minimum)** and includes up to **15 emailed digital images** or five printed/mailed images. GFO requests **one inquiry per request**.

## What to preserve when a response arrives

Do not reduce the reply to only Farr/Harr. Preserve and visually review:

1. title page / publisher / publication year or edition statement;
2. full Oregon City alphabetical pages containing Farr, Harr, and McDougald;
3. any street/address directory pages covering 501–511 Main, if present;
4. classified pages for pool halls, billiards, recreation, taverns, beer, cigars, restaurants, and similar categories;
5. exact page numbers and scan filenames;
6. all businesses shown at 501, 503, 505, 505½, 507, 509, and 511 Main;
7. any evidence that distinguishes residence entries from business/proprietor entries.

Keep Edwin F. Farr, Alice Farr, Carl R. Harr, Harris E. McDougald, C. P. Farr, Marvin Farr, and other similarly named persons separate unless a source explicitly supports identity linkage.
