## 919 7th / Farr Store official historic-resource control

Oregon City's current historic-resource page and 2002 survey form identify **919 7th Street** as the **Farr Store**, at the corner of **7th and Monroe**. The form gives:

- Tax Lot **5000**;
- Map **22E31AD**;
- Block **129**;
- Lots **3x, 4x**;
- historic name **Farr Store**;
- construction date **c.1910**;
- a corner entry at Monroe and 7th;
- a later storefront cut near the rear on Monroe Street.

The same official historical synthesis says the store remained there until the **mid-1940s**, when **Dewey Krueger bought the property from Clarence Farr**.

Official City sources:

- `https://www.orcity.org/2142/919-7th-Street`
- `https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/7877/919_7th`

This is official secondary/property-survey evidence. It does not by itself identify 708 Monroe.

## Direct 2017 address-to-parcel bridge: 919 7th and 709 Monroe are the same APN

A City of Oregon City permit log dated **19 September 2017** provides a useful modern parcel/address control. On the same permit-log page:

- permits **BM-17-0295** and **BM-17-0296** list **919 7th St** with owner **Mouser Elmer G Trustee** and APN **2-2E-31AD-05000**;
- permit **BM-17-0297** lists **709 Monroe St** with the same owner **Mouser Elmer G Trustee** and the same APN **2-2E-31AD-05000**.

Official City permit log:

`https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/638/September-11-2017-to-September-15-2017-PDF`

This is **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT** evidence that, by 2017, **709 Monroe was an addressed portion of the same tax parcel as 919 7th**. It independently fits the historic-resource form's description of a Monroe-side storefront near the rear of the Farr Store building.

The 2017 permit evidence does **not** prove that the 709 Monroe sub-address existed in the 1940s, nor does it by itself identify the historical parcel of 708 Monroe.

## Strong spatial conclusion

The new 2017 parcel control materially narrows the question. The historic Farr Store property at **919 7th** is demonstrably associated in modern City records with the Monroe-side address **709 Monroe**, on APN **2-2E-31AD-05000**.

The 1946 recreation-department rent instead names **708 Monroe**.

Because the historical City rent gives **708**, while the Farr Store parcel's documented Monroe-side address is **709**, the evidence now strongly favors **separate premises on opposite address-number sides of Monroe**, not a simple alternate-address reading of the Farr Store parcel. This is a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE SPATIAL INFERENCE**, not yet a deed-level historical parcel proof for 708 Monroe.

A current geocoded control is consistent with that separation: current 708 Monroe is about **44 meters** from the GPS point recorded on the 2002 919 7th historic-resource form. Modern coordinates are orientation evidence only and are not used as proof of the 1946 parcel boundary.

This pass further closes a false path. The municipal recreation-building rent paid to C. P. Farr / Clarence P. Farr should **not** be used as evidence for:
