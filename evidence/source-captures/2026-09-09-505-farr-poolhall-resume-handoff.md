## 505 pool-hall / recreation chronology

Preserve these anchors and their evidence limits:

- **1925:** visually verified Sanborn maps 505 Main as **billiards / cigars**; operator unnamed.
- Oregon City's historic-resource inventory for 401 Third identifies **Edwin F. and Alice Farr** as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main**.
- That same source says the Farrs acquired their residence in **1934**. The residence-acquisition date is not the pool hall opening date.
- Newer canonical work records CCHS photograph `P-1128` (`S-236` / `E-210` / `IMG-0757` / `T-087`) as an independently cataloged **1936** Fifth/Main photograph. Project-steward storefront identification plus enlarged review yields probable `FARR'S` signage and likely pool-related wording. Combined with the independent City source naming Farr's Pool Hall at 505, the repository classifies this as a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE** that Farr's Pool Hall was operating at 505 in the photographed 1936 state. It is not an opening-date proof.
- City synthesis places **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 in 1945**, but the underlying directory/source page remains unresolved.
- **11 February 1948** municipal text names Harr and McDougald as pool-hall operators but does not print Harr's first name/business/address on that page.
- A separate late-1948 municipal liquor-license list directly prints **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**; exact meeting date remains unresolved because the recovered excerpt lacks its opening page.
- 1950 Sanborn continues the billiards/cigars/restaurant/beer-related use pattern at 505.

The exact beginning of Farr's Pool Hall and exact **Farr → Harr** transition remain unresolved.
