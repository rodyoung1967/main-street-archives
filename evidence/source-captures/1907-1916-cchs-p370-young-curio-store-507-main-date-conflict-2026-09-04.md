## Visual review — VISUALLY VERIFIED

The supplied photograph was visually inspected. The storefront sign clearly reads **NEW & SECONDHAND YOUNG'S CURIO STORE**. Two broad display windows flank a recessed central entrance, with merchandise in the windows and along the sidewalk. Several people stand at the storefront. A CCHS watermark crosses the image.

A faint marking above the doorway and faint handwriting near the lower-right edge are not clear enough to use as independent address or date evidence. Therefore **507 Main** and the date values are treated as catalog metadata rather than confidently legible image text.

## UNRESOLVED DATE CONFLICT — 1907 versus 1916

CCHS MOT:P-370 has structured date **1916**, but its own description says it duplicates **MOT:P-6253** and that the same picture there has date **1907**. Until MOT:P-6253 and underlying accession/postcard/negative/cataloging notes are inspected, the exposure date is **UNRESOLVED CONFLICT: 1907 vs. 1916**.

MOT:P-370 must therefore not be used as unqualified independent proof that Young occupied 507 Main in 1916. If the image is actually from 1907, it does not conflict with W. W. Bradley's selected 1916 newspaper listings at 507. If 1916 is correct, it adds to the existing Young/Bradley occupancy conflict. Current evidence does not choose between those possibilities.

## Relationship to the documented Young chronology

- **505 Main, August 1907:** Young's Second Hand Store exact-address advertisement (`E-168` / `S-189`).
- **505 Main, 1910:** CCHS MOT:P-391 photograph visibly shows Young's storefront and 505 (`E-134` / `S-149` / `IMG-0725`).
- **March 1911:** newspaper report says Young moved one door north from the Harding Building to the Willamette Building.
- **507 Main, January 1912 and 1914:** exact-address records (`E-092` and later 1914 listing).
- **507 Main, CCHS cataloged 1916:** separate MOT:P-502 photograph (`E-189` / `S-209` / `IMG-0738`), while selected 1916 newspaper ads place W. W. Bradley at 507.

MOT:P-370 strengthens the visual record for Young's storefront but does not presently settle its exposure year.
