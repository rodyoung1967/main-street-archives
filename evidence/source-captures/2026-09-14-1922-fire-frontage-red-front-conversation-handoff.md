- **Wolf & Miller** is directly documented at **505 Main in 1918**, selling men's clothing, shoes and providing shoe repair.
- October 1918 wording says one partner would go to Idaho and the other would restock/continue the business.
- User recalled a partnership dissolution/division; current evidence supports a partner split/continuation, but no recovered source yet literally says “Wolf took clothing and Miller took shoes.”

- Therefore it is reasonable and useful to investigate **L. Wolf as probably the Wolf of Wolf & Miller**, and the later Miller-shoe / Wolf-clothing split as a possible continuation of the former partnership.
