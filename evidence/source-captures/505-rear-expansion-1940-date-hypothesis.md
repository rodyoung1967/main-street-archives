# 505 Main — rear expansion / 1940 inventory-date hypothesis

Status: **WORKING HYPOTHESIS — UNRESOLVED**

Date: 24 August 2026

Purpose: preserve a testable interpretation linking (1) the **1940** primary construction date on official historic inventories for **505 Main**, (2) family/oral and field observations that **505 may extend farther rearward than 503**, and (3) the long-documented conflict between **1940** and earlier **c.1920 / 1925** evidence for the same address.

This is **interpretive synthesis**, not primary proof. Absence of a confirming permit, assessor card, or dated construction notice keeps the hypothesis open.

Related evidence: `E-101`

---

## Interpretation rules preserved

- **1940** on `S-116` / `E-100` and `S-063` proves only what those inventories **report**; it does not by itself prove original erection of the entire 505 premises.
- **1925 Sanborn** billiards/cigars at 505 and **1936 aerial** roof separation at 501/503/505 (`E-021`) prove a **505-address structure existed before 1940**.
- Separate **front/historic-core chronology** from **rear addition / major alteration** questions unless a record explicitly ties them.
- Mitch Young's rear-addition recollection (`E-010`) is oral history until confirmed by permits, assessor sketches, maps, tax records, or physical analysis.

## The hypothesis (plain language)

The **1940** inventory date may reflect a **rearward expansion, major remodel, or assessor re-dating event** at 505 Main — not the first construction of the entire street-front building — while an earlier **c.1920** city synthesis (`S-033` / `E-032`) may better describe the **Main Street front / historic core**.

Under this model:

| Element | Working date | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Address use / predecessor premises at 505 | by **1911** (Chicago Store), **1925** (Sanborn billiards/cigars) | Very High |
| Surviving front / historic-core estimate (city 2000 inventory) | **c.1920** | Medium-high as inventory estimate only |
| Later inventory "primary construction date" | **1940** | Very High that inventories say 1940; Unresolved for physical meaning |
| Rear portion added or materially enlarged | **possibly ~1940** | **Unresolved hypothesis only** |

## Evidence supporting plausibility (not proof)

### Oral / field observations

- **`E-010`** — Mitch Young recalled that the **rear portion of 505 may have been added later** (undated).
- **`E-011`** — working observation that **505 may extend farther back than 503** while 503 may be wider.

### Inventory-date conflict already in archive

- **`E-032` / `S-033`** — City 2000 Downtown Final Report lists **505 Main — c.1920** (visually verified appendix page).
- **`E-100` / `S-116`** — 2009 Oregon Historic Site Form lists **primary construction date 1940**.
- **`S-063`** — ODOT 2021 Table 2 lists **505 Main / 505 Tavern — 1940**.

The archive already treats these as **conflicting survey estimates**, not settled construction history.

### Pre-1940 structure / occupancy at 505

| Anchor | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| `E-020` / 1925 Sanborn | Separate **505 billiards/cigars** occupancy |

| `E-027` | Wolf & Miller at **505 Main**, 1918 |
| 1949 recreation anchor | Harr's Recreation Center at 505 (`E-032` chain) |

Therefore **1940 cannot mean first-ever existence** at the 505 address.

### Block context circa 1940

- **`E-021`** — **501 Main removed between 1936 and 1944** during visible McLoughlin / US99E corridor change. Timing overlaps the inventory **1940** line but does **not** prove 505 rear work was part of the same project.
- **`IMG-0008` (1940)** exists in the aerial series specifically for comparison against 1936 and 1944; **formal rear-footprint transcription for 505 is still pending** (`RL-005`).

### Modern parcel geometry (retrieval clue only)

- Current public-record summaries place **505 Main** on **parts of Lots 3 and 4, Block 4**, while **503** occupies **part of Lot 4** (`evidence/source-captures/current-parcel-legal-descriptions-503-505-507.md`). This is consistent with complex depth/parcel geometry but does **not** prove a 1940 rear addition.

## Evidence that limits or contradicts treating the hypothesis as answered

1. **No 1939–1941 permit** for 505 Main has been recovered.
2. **`S-116`** lists **primary construction date 1940**, not "addition date" or "remodel date"; architect and builder fields are blank.
3. **Mitch Young's recollection is undated** — the rear work could be much later than 1940.
4. **Sanborn 1925 vs. 1950** comparison confirms continued separate 503/505 occupancies but has **not yet transcribed a confirmed rear-depth change** at 505 between those sheets (`E-020`).
5. Alternative explanations for **1940** remain live: full rebuild of surviving fabric, facade/material upgrade (form notes **concrete** siding), assessor convention, or survey inheritance from an earlier undocumented event.
