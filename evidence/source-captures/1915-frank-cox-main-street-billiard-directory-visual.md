## Visually verified Oregon City listing

In the right-side merchant directory, under **Oregon City**, the page lists:

**BILLIARD PARLOR**  
**Frank D. Cox**  
**Main St**

The listing does not print a numbered Main Street address.

## Why this matters

This is independent commercial-directory-style evidence that **Frank D. Cox was the named proprietor/operator associated with a Main Street billiard parlor in Oregon City by 7 February 1915**. It predates the June 1915 police/court coverage that also identifies Cox's Main Street pool hall.

It strengthens the continuity between:

- F. D. Cox's October 1912 municipal pool/billiard license;
- the unidentified Cox who acquired Eugene Ward's interest in Cox & Ward in February 1912 (identity still unproven);
- Frank D. Cox's clearly named Main Street billiard business in February 1915;
- Cox's Main Street pool hall documented in June 1915 and still operating in March 1916.

However, the 1915 listing still **does not identify the street number**. It does not place Cox at 503 or 505.

## Address caution

The same directory column contains OCR-prone addresses for other Oregon City merchants. Use the visually reviewed Cox name/business/location claim, but do not infer a numbered address from neighboring entries or OCR layout.
