- **p. 8 / PDF 6:** Recorder receipts separate licenses collected **$7,480.20**, permits/dog licenses **$533.25**, and franchises **$275.00**. No payers, license numbers, categories within the first line or premises are listed.
- **p. 29 / PDF 16:** combined franchise/license operating revenue is **$8,356.99**, compared with **$6,576.91** in 1925; printed increase **$1,780.08**. The three Recorder lines above sum to **$8,288.45**, leaving a **$68.54 arithmetic difference** from this operating statement. Its cause is unresolved: do not force reconciliation, label it fraud/error, or infer a missing tenant. These schedules use differently described accounting categories.

**Research inference:** the health-payment line gives a contemporary reason to investigate a city–county arrangement and related county claims/vouchers, alongside the existing City Recorder route. It does not retroactively establish such an arrangement for 1925. Actual agreements, invoices, inspection returns and custody history remain unverified.

Printed p. 7 reports **60 meetings total** and **59 ordinances**; these are useful inventory controls for a future 1926 minutes/ordinance pass. They do not count as reviewed minutes.
