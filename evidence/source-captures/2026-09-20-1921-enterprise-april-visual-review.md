# 1921 Enterprise visual review — April

## Scope and method

Continuation of ANNUAL-1921. **1921 remains IN PROGRESS.**

All five weekly Oregon City Enterprise issues in April 1921 were inventoried and all **40 page images were visually inspected page by page**:
- 1 April — 8/8
- 8 April — 8/8
- 15 April — 8/8
- 22 April — 8/8
- 29 April — 8/8

Review explicitly included the entire **500 block of Main Street**, not only 503/505. Search attention included printed addresses, named businesses/persons, Fifth/Main and 500-block descriptions, adjacent-business spatial controls, demolition/teardown, rebuilding/construction, remodeling, fire/damage, property sale/ownership transfer, landlord changes, leases, tenant/business changes, and openings/closings/moves.

## Results

All 40 pages were present and visually inspectable.

No April issue produced a new sufficiently specific fact tying a person, business, ownership/tenancy change, demolition, rebuild, remodeling, or property transfer to 501, 503, or 505 Main.

The visual pass also did not establish a new address-specific fact elsewhere in the 500 block strong enough to propagate into the target-building chronology. General Main Street and Oregon City commercial references were treated as geographic/context material rather than target evidence unless they could be anchored to the block.

## Earlier Jan-March 500-block material

The strongest 500-block-adjacent item recovered so far in the weekly 1921 Enterprise pass remains the 28 January historical-photograph display at Burmeister & Andresen: George Hankins arranged a window display of old Oregon City photographs from multiple local custodians, including a Miller-family photograph loaned by Miss Rose Miller. This is an archival/photo provenance lead, **not proof that any image depicted the 500 block**.

The 7 January issue also carried a Main Street merchants' protective/night-police time-clock item explicitly describing merchants between Sixth and Eighth. Because that geography is north of the target 500 block, it is a spatial control rather than evidence about 501/503/505.

## Evidence limitation

No-hit findings are coverage facts only. They do not establish that no physical, ownership, tenancy, or business change occurred. The Morning Enterprise daily run remains essential, particularly for events too brief to appear in the weekly paper.
