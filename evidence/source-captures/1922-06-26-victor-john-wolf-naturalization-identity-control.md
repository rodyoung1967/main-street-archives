# Victor John Wolf naturalization identity control — June 1922

Date checked: 9 September 2026  
Purpose: test whether the separately listed 1920 Oregon City resident **Victor (Alice) Wolf** can be identified more precisely and whether that person can be merged with **S. Wolf of Portland**, the July 1922 Harding-property buyer and reported fire-origin building owner.

## Result

The original *Morning Oregonian* page and the Oregon Historical Records Index establish a strong cross-source identification of the local directory candidate as **Victor John Wolf**, a German-born Oregon City Route 6 resident naturalized in Clackamas County in June 1922. This does **not** identify him as `P-035` S. Wolf. The printed initial differs, and the contemporary residence descriptions conflict: Victor is reported on Oregon City Route 6, while the July property reports call S. Wolf a Portland resident.

Classification: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT** for each source's printed statements; **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE** for the newspaper's Victor Wolf and the indexed Victor John Wolf being the same person; **NO IDENTITY BRIDGE** to S. Wolf.
