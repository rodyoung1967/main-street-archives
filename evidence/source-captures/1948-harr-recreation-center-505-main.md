# Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main, late 1948

Status: **primary municipal exact-address evidence; exact late-1948 license meeting date still to be pinned down**

A City of Oregon City Commission-minutes volume directly identifies:

**Carl R. Harr, Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main Street**

The entry appears in a list of applicants whose **liquor licenses were approved by the City Commission**. The same list includes other named Oregon City businesses and exact addresses, including Pastime Pool Hall at 723 Main Street.

Primary municipal source:
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4722834/File/document

The relevant minutes text reads, in part, that the Commission approved the following applicants for liquor licenses, followed by a list including `Carl R. Harr, Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main Street`.

## 11 February 1948 — Harr already appears as a pool-hall operator

A separate City Commission record dated **11 February 1948** states:

> `Messrs. Harr and McDougald appeared before the Commission requesting permission to allow card playing in the pool halls.`

After discussion, the Commission took no action at that time.

Primary municipal source:
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4723051/File/document

This is important because the later-1948 liquor-license list independently identifies **Harris E. McDougald** as proprietor of **Pastime Pool Hall, 723 Main Street**, while it identifies **Carl R. Harr** as proprietor of **Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main Street**. Taken together, the records strongly support that the `Harr` appearing with McDougald in February 1948 was **Carl R. Harr** and that he was already operating a pool-hall/recreation business by then.

However, the **11 February entry itself does not print Harr's business address**. Do not back-assign `505 Main` to the February meeting solely from the later liquor-license list. The defensible formulation is:

- **11 Feb 1948:** Harr is directly identified as one of the local pool-hall operators appearing before the Commission; address not printed in that entry.
- **late 1948:** Carl R. Harr is directly identified as proprietor of **Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main Street**.

This narrows the opening/continuity window for Harr's operation but does not prove that he was already at 505 in February.

## Date handling for the exact-address liquor approval

The scanned record segment containing `Carl R. Harr, Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main Street` is immediately before the minutes for the **3 January 1949 special meeting** and contains letters dated 16 and 23 November 1948. The exact header/date of the meeting containing the Harr license approval has not yet been recovered from the exposed text lines. Therefore this archive records the exact-address finding conservatively as **late 1948** pending recovery of the actual meeting header.

Do not silently convert the date to December 1948 or January 1949 without the actual meeting header.

## Why this matters

This adds a new primary municipal bridge in the 505 Main chronology between the earlier pool-hall era and the later 505 Tavern era:

- 1934-era city historic-resource research identifies Edwin F. and Alice Farr as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main**;
- **11 Feb 1948:** Harr is directly one of the Oregon City pool-hall operators petitioning the Commission over card playing; exact address omitted;
- **late 1948:** Carl R. Harr — **Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main** — liquor-license approval;
- later evidence establishes **505 Tavern** by 1965 and subsequent tavern history.

The Harr records show that 505 retained a recreation / pool-hall-adjacent business use into the late 1940s. They do **not** by themselves establish that Harr acquired Farr's business, that there was uninterrupted Farr→Harr business continuity, or that Harr's Recreation Center should be called a tavern.
