## Official City archive route

Oregon City's official **Meeting Document Archive** states that the City participates in the Oregon Records Management Solution program and provides direct links into the legacy WebDrawer archive. It also instructs researchers who cannot locate a document to contact the Office of the City Recorder.

Official City page:

`https://www.orcity.org/1844/Meeting-Document-Archive`

The City's **1940 to 1949 City Commission Agenda Packets and Minutes** link resolves to the following legacy WebDrawer query:

`https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record?q=webdrawercode%3A%22%2A023%2A%22+And+recTitle%3A%22%2A194%2A+city+commission%2A%22+And+recContainer%3A3AAgenda+Packets+and+Minutes&sortBy=recTypedTitle-`

This exact decade-query URL is useful because it exposes the collection filter even when ordinary full-text web search does not surface the individual 1944–1948 minute packets.

### Current access limitation

The decade result page returned a cache/retrieval failure in the current research environment. Direct search-engine queries continued to recover isolated 1948 packets but did not expose the missing December 1944–January 1948 renewal packets.

## Current City records migration

Oregon City's official **Public Records Self-Service Portal** now states that the City is transitioning to a new electronic records-management system and that there may be delays accessing records during the transition.

Official page:

`https://www.orcity.org/596/Public-Records-Self-Service-Portal`

The page identifies **Laserfiche** as the current archive system and links to the Oregon City E-Vault Public Portal. It also instructs users who cannot locate a document to contact the City Recorder or submit a public-records request.

Current E-Vault entry route:

`https://portal.laserfiche.com/Portal/Welcome.aspx?repo=r-5a2dbf36`

The current research environment could reach the E-Vault loading page but could not interact with the JavaScript repository browser sufficiently to retrieve the historical minute packets.

### Research consequence

The missing 1944–1948 packets now sit across an active legacy-to-Laserfiche migration boundary. The correct next step is not to treat WebDrawer search silence as absence; it is to request the exact minute ranges and page locators from the City Recorder.
