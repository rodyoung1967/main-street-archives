## Why acquisition is higher value than a narrow lookup

If the volume is still available, obtaining the actual directory would allow the project to inspect the full evidence context rather than only selected surname pages:

- title page, publisher and edition wording;
- front matter explaining compilation/canvass timing;
- Farr, Harr and McDougald alphabetical entries;
- 501–511 Main address/street context;
- classified pool/billiards/recreation/tavern/cigar sections;
- any reverse/street index;
- neighboring businesses needed to validate address interpretation.

That is especially important because separate contemporary evidence shows Pacific Directory Service was canvassing Clackamas County in **August–September 1946**. The eventual 1947–48 listing cannot automatically be dated to 1948 without the volume's own front matter and entry context.

## Evidence boundary

The GFO sales row and federal registration cross-reference are **acquisition/access/provenance evidence only**. They supply no Farr, Harr, 505 Main, or transition fact from inside the directory.

Until the volume itself is obtained and visually inspected, the Farr → Harr chronology remains unchanged:

- official-secondary City synthesis reports Harr's Recreation Center at 505 in 1945, exact underlying directory citation unresolved;
- direct municipal evidence places Harr among pool-hall operators on 11 February 1948;
- late-1948 municipal text directly identifies Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street;
- exact transition date unresolved.
