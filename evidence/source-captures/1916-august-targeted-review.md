## Side lead worth preserving — very early 503 attribution

A 2023 Oregon statewide National Register multiple-property document, citing modern historical scholarship, states that Jacob Vanderpool's Oregon Saloon and Boarding House was at **503 Main Street, Oregon City** in 1850. The same document notes conflicting scholarship over the business location, so this should be treated as a later historical attribution rather than contemporary 1850 numbered-address proof.

Source:
https://www.oregon.gov/oprd/OH/Documents/OR_Statewide_BlackHistoricResourcesInOregonMPD.pdf

This is relevant to the long-term 503 site history but does not affect the unresolved 1916 tenant question.
