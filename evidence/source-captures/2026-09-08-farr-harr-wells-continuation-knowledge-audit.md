## 2. Apparent 1947 “Oregon City Directory, Vol. XIV” hit is a false route for this project

A later web search surfaced a National Register bibliography line that, in isolated search-result text, could be misread as evidence for an **Oregon City municipality** directory published by T. W. Salisbury in 1947.

The original National Park Service PDF was opened and **page 54 was visually inspected**. The actual bibliography entry is:

> `Salisbury's Eugene-Springfield Lane County, Oregon City Directory. Vol. XIV.`

with publication information:

> `Eugene, Oregon; T.W. Salisbury and Co., 1947.`

Source:

- National Register of Historic Places, Woodmen of the World Hall, Eugene, Oregon, NPS PDF: `https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/96000618.pdf`
- visually inspected PDF page **54**, bibliography item **50**.

Context on the same page and throughout the nomination makes clear that this is a **Eugene / Springfield / Lane County directory**, with “Oregon City Directory” functioning as state/type wording rather than identifying the municipality of Oregon City in Clackamas County.

Classification: **VISUALLY VERIFIED BIBLIOGRAPHIC FALSE ROUTE / NOT OREGON CITY (CLACKAMAS COUNTY) DIRECTORY EVIDENCE.**

Research consequence: **do not use this 1947 Vol. XIV citation as evidence of a Salisbury Oregon City municipality directory, as a Farr/Harr transition source, or as a 505 Main retrieval route.**

This correction prevents a misleading search snippet from being carried into the active 505 chronology.
