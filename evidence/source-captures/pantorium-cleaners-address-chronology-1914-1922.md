# Oregon City Pantorium address chronology — 1914 to July 1922

Status: **1914 and post-fire July 1922 address anchors verified; pre-fire 1922 move date unresolved**

Purpose: test whether the Oregon City Pantorium could be the cleaning-and-dyeing establishment reported by a same-day Eugene source to have occupied the July 1922 fire-origin room until only a few days before the fire.

## 12 February 1914 — Pantorium at 416 Main Street

The actual scanned page of the **Oregon City Courier, 12 Feb 1914, p. 16** was visually inspected.

A profile headed **“Oregon City Pantorium”** states that the business was owned by **F. Champion** and that its quarters were located at:

- **416 Main street**

The article describes the establishment as cleaning, dyeing, pressing, and repairing clothing and specifically says it made a specialty of **French and dry cleaning**.

The same profile says the business had operated for about seven years and had been established by Champion and Follansbee/Falansbee; Champion had taken over the deceased partner's interest the preceding December. The surname spelling varies in the newspaper text/OCR and should not be normalized without additional evidence.

## 25 January 1922 — active dry-cleaning and dyeing concern in Oregon City

A visually reviewed **Morning Oregonian, 25 Jan 1922** help-wanted advertisement identifies **Pantorium Cleaners, Oregon City** and seeks an experienced **dry-cleaning and dyeing solicitor**.

This proves that the Pantorium was actively engaged in the same general cleaning/dyeing trade less than six months before the Fifth/Main fire, but the advertisement does **not** give a street address.

## 8 June 1922 — Pantorium still active

The visually reviewed **Banner-Courier, 8 Jun 1922, p. 10** Buyers' Carnival roster includes **Pantorium** as an Oregon City merchant/business.

The roster gives no address.

## 20 July 1922 — Pantorium at 519 Main after the fire

The visually reviewed **Banner-Courier, 20 Jul 1922, p. 1** says Oregon City Laundry arranged to continue business after the July 18 fire by taking a temporary office **with the Pantorium at 519 Main Street**.

Canonical page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-07-20/ed-1/seq-1/

This directly places the Pantorium at **519 Main by 20 July 1922**, two days after the fire.

## Why this matters

A same-day **18 Jul 1922 Eugene Daily Guard** fire account says the unoccupied Main Street room where the fire began had reportedly been occupied until only a few days earlier by a **cleaning-and-dyeing establishment**.

Pantorium is therefore a legitimate candidate to test because:

- it was an established Oregon City cleaning/dyeing business;
- it was active in January and June 1922;
- it was at 519 Main immediately after the fire.

However, the recovered chronology currently has a crucial gap:

- **1914:** 416 Main;
- **Jan/Jun 1922:** active, address not stated in recovered sources;
- **20 Jul 1922:** 519 Main.

No pre-fire source has yet been recovered proving when Pantorium moved from 416 Main to 519 Main.

Therefore **Pantorium cannot yet either be identified as, or excluded as, the cleaner/dyer that had just vacated the ignition room.**

If a source proves Pantorium was already established at 519 Main before 18 July, that would strongly weigh against it being the recent occupant of the separate fire-origin room. Conversely, a documented move to 519 immediately after the fire would materially strengthen the candidate hypothesis. Neither scenario is presently proven.

## Later continuity

Later newspaper advertising places Pantorium Cleaners at **519 Main** in 1930 and again in March 1946. A later historic-resource inventory identifies the Pantorium Cleaners building at **624 McLoughlin Boulevard**, dated c.1945. These later records show a longer business/location history but do not establish the July 1922 move date.

## What is NOT established

Do not currently claim that:

- Pantorium occupied the July 1922 ignition room;
- Pantorium was at 519 Main before the fire;
- Pantorium moved to 519 because of the fire;
- the cleaning/dyeing establishment mentioned in the Eugene report was Pantorium;
- the 1914 and 1922 physical premises were the same building under renumbering.

## Confidence

- Oregon City Pantorium at 416 Main on 12 Feb 1914: **Very High / visually verified**
- Pantorium performed cleaning/dyeing/dry cleaning in 1914: **Very High / visually verified**
- Pantorium active as a dry-cleaning/dyeing concern in Jan 1922: **Very High / visually verified advertisement**
- Pantorium active in June 1922: **Very High / visually verified merchant roster**
- Pantorium at 519 Main by 20 Jul 1922: **Very High / visually verified local fire report**
- Date of move to 519 Main: **Unresolved**
- Pantorium = recent ignition-room cleaner/dyer: **Hypothesis only**
