## Scope

- Title: *The Banner-Courier* (LCCN `sn00063699`).
- Calendar probe: **1919-07-09 through 1920-12-31**.

- Method: probe each calendar date for sequence 1, then enumerate page OCR only for discovered issues.
- Purpose: test whether the successor title could close the 1919–1920 newspaper gap after the *Oregon City Courier* ended in July 1919.

## Availability finding

The zero-result probe is now explained by independent institutional holdings information rather than treated as search silence:

- Historic Oregon Newspapers identifies *The Banner-Courier* as an Oregon City title published **1919–1950**, with its description based on the **17 July 1919** issue, but the site's available digitized run begins with **5 January 1922**.
- Oregon Digital Newspaper Program documentation identifies the digitized/re-filmed *Banner-Courier* run as **January–December 1922**.
- Oregon City Public Library's current microfilm inventory explicitly lists **Oregon City Banner Courier: July 17, 1919 to February 28, 1950**.
- The same library inventory lists an **Oregon City Council and Commission Proceeding Index: August 6, 1850 to June 26, 1978**, making the 1920 council record a related manual-source route.

Institutional URLs:

## Research consequence

The **17 July 1919–31 December 1920 Banner-Courier interval cannot presently be certified through the online Historic Oregon Newspapers run**. It survives on Oregon City Public Library microfilm and is therefore a **MANUAL CALLOUT**, not negative evidence and not an OCR-review omission.

Highest-priority manual searches in that microfilm interval:

1. **Wolf & Miller / 505 Main** — sale, departure, successor, lease, closure, remodeling, or relocation after October 1918.
2. **Clem Dollar** — pool hall location, acquisition, transfer, or license.
3. **A. Leland / 505½ Main** — pool-hall opening, prior premises, sign permit, lease, transfer, or building work before/after June 1920.
4. **501 / 503 / 505 Main** — new building, construction, demolition, rebuilding, alteration, excavation, permit, lease, storeroom, vacancy, or relocation language.
5. **Fifth–Sixth Main / Harding / Post Office cluster** — property sales, storefront changes, and relative-location advertisements that can map the pre-1922 bays.

## Limits

- This audit establishes source availability and a retrieval gap; it is **not evidence about building occupancy or construction**.
- Zero online issues do not imply no newspaper coverage existed in 1919–1920.
- The original bounded script and machine-readable zero-result inventory remain in the repository for reproducibility: `scripts/discover_1919_1920_banner_courier.py` and `evidence/source-captures/1919-1920-banner-courier-ocr-inventory.json`.
