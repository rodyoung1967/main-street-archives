## 1. Current OLCC records schedule names the licensing series

Oregon State Archives' current OLCC records-retention schedule is indexed at:

`https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/Documents/recordsmgmt/sched/schedule-liquor-control.pdf`

The official indexed text identifies the License Processing section and names records documenting license processing and license/compliance history, including:

- **Applications Received Lists**;
- **License Count Lists**;
- **License Files**;
- **License Tradename Cards**;
- **Licensee History Cards**;
- License Process Reports;
- Regulatory Action Lists; and
- the later RBS database.

The indexed schedule is effective **January 2024**, schedule number **2001-0010**. It assigns finite destruction periods to the license-file/card series. The search text currently renders an amended retention for License Files and Licensee History Cards as **"20 10 years after inactive, destroy"**; because the current PDF URL returned 404 when opened in this research environment, do **not** normalize that flattened amendment text to a single number without the actual current schedule image. The indexed text clearly gives **License Tradename Cards — retain 3 years, destroy** and confirms that the overall current retention model is not permanent.

### Evidence boundary

The current schedule does **not** prove that 1930s–1940s license records were destroyed. It does not establish what retention rules governed those records when created, whether older material was microfilmed before destruction, whether legacy cards/files were transferred to another custodian, or whether archival accessions survive outside the current active-series retention system.

Accordingly, use the schedule to identify the exact series names and to frame a **survival / legacy-media / transfer-location request**, not as negative evidence.

## 2. Historical OLCC rules prove that Commission official files existed on microfiche

Oregon Secretary of State records preserve the **Oregon Administrative Rules Compilation, 2000 Edition**, Chapter 845, Oregon Liquor Control Commission:

`https://records.sos.state.or.us/ORSOSWebDrawer/Record/8135962/File/document`

Rule **845-004-0020, Fees for Certain Services**, printed page **15-548**, describes several licensing-record products then available from the Commission. The official PDF text includes:

- a complete list of all licensees;
- lists of on-premises, dispenser/restaurant, package-store and other licensees;
- **Clackamas County licensees** as a county-specific list;
- **licensees by renewal district**;
- **monthly supplements of license actions**;
- **license nonrenewals by renewal district**;
- individualized lists of licensed premises by type and location; and
- a fee for finding/producing for inspection and providing a **microfiche copy, if available, of the Commissioner's official files**, with paper copies of microfiche documents also available.

This is direct evidence that by the 2000 rule compilation OLCC maintained or served at least some official-file material in **microfiche form**, and that licensing changes/nonrenewals were maintained in list products beyond individual active files.

The rule-history block for 845-004-0020 traces the rule family to **LCC 11-1980** and later amendments. That history does not establish when the microfiche subsection itself was first adopted and does not prove that any 1940s fiche survives.

## 3. Why this materially improves the Farr → Harr retrieval plan

Prior work had identified OLCC Commission Meeting Records and the annual renewal cycle, but the project did not have exact names for the operational license-history/card series or evidence that OLCC historically served official files from microfiche.

The new route should therefore ask not merely for "old liquor licenses," but whether any of the following survive for the target period/person/premises:

1. **Licensee History Cards**;
2. **License Tradename Cards**;
3. **License Files**;
4. **Applications Received Lists** and annual **License Count Lists**;
5. historical **monthly license-action supplements**;
6. **license-nonrenewal lists by renewal district**;
7. county-specific historical licensee lists for **Clackamas County**;
8. microfiche of the **Commissioner's official files** or successor/predecessor microform series;
9. retired card indexes, legacy microfilm/microfiche masters, conversion-image sets, or database predecessor records; and
10. transfer/accession/disposition records identifying another custodian if the original series no longer remains at OLCC.

## 5. Current OLCC public-records route

Current official OLCC page:

`https://www.oregon.gov/olcc/pages/public-records-request.aspx`

OLCC instructs requesters to provide a date range, specific persons/licenses/entities/keywords and a sufficiently detailed record description. It states that the public-records request form is preferred, while a sufficiently detailed request may also be emailed to:

`OLCC.PublicRecords@olcc.oregon.gov`

The current page says OLCC will acknowledge a request and assign a PRR number within five business days. Fee and timing language is current administrative information only, not historical evidence.
