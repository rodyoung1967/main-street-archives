# 501 Main — 18 August 1922 Oregon City Enterprise construction finding

Date preserved: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

Preserve the conclusions and correction trail from the conversation concerning the post-fire building at 501 Main Street / corner of Fifth and Main, Oregon City.

## Verified primary-source finding

The project steward checked the source scan already present in the project/research materials: **Oregon City Enterprise, Friday, August 18, 1922, page 5**.

The page masthead/date is **OREGON CITY ENTERPRISE — FRIDAY, AUGUST 18, 1922 — Page Five**.

The relevant article is headed **“New Building Being Erected at Corner of Fifth and Main.”**

The article reports that:

- a new building was being erected at the corner of Fifth and Main;
- it was a **two-story hollow-tile building**;
- it was being erected on **C. E. Gates's property**;
- it replaced the building destroyed in the July 1922 fire that had housed the **Fifth Street Restaurant**;
- **R. A. Junken of Willamette** was identified as the contractor;
- the first floor was to contain **two storerooms**;
- one storeroom had already been leased for a **barber shop**; and
- most importantly for the rebuilding chronology, the newspaper stated: **“The building will be ready for occupancy about October 1st.”**

### Evidence state

**DOCUMENTED / DIRECT; VISUALLY VERIFIED by the project steward from the actual newspaper page scan.**

The completion date is a projected readiness/occupancy date reported on 18 August 1922, **not proof that construction was actually completed on October 1**. Use wording such as “expected to be ready for occupancy about October 1, 1922” unless a later source establishes actual completion/occupancy.

## Chronological significance

This source establishes that by **18 August 1922** construction of the replacement building associated with the Fifth Street Restaurant fire loss was underway, and the contemporary projected readiness date was **about October 1, 1922**. It therefore provides a useful construction bracket for the post-July-fire Fifth/Main frontage reconstruction.

Keep this finding distinct from evidence concerning the physical histories of 503 and 505 Main. Do not infer that the Gates building report independently establishes the construction/reconstruction dates of neighboring 503 or 505.
