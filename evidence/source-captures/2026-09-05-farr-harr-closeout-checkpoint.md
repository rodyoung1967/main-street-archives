## Historical status at closeout

The exact Farr → Harr transition date remains **UNRESOLVED**.

Current evidence remains:

- CCHS `P-1128` is independently dated **1936**; steward mapping of the 505 storefront plus probable **FARR'S** / likely pool-related signage, combined with the City's independent Farr/505 business identification, provides a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE** that Farr's Pool Hall was operating at 505 in the photographed state. This is an operating-state anchor, not an opening date.
- Oregon City's later historic-resource synthesis reports **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main in 1945**, but the exact underlying directory/source title, effective year, page, and source-note lineage have not yet been recovered.
- The **11 February 1948** Oregon City Commission text directly identifies **Harr** as a pool-hall operator, but the line itself does not supply the street address.
- A later **1948 municipal liquor-license list** directly identifies **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**.
- The unrecovered **1947–48 Clackamas County Directory** remains the best candidate for bridging the transition because its production campaign was already underway in **August–September 1946**. Any eventual listing must therefore be interpreted in light of the 1946 canvass, not automatically as a 1948 occupancy observation.

No recovered source yet states whether **Farr or Harr occupied 505 Main during the 1946 canvass**.

## Strongest current chronological bracket

The best current sequence is:

1. **1936 — Farr operating-state anchor:** probable Farr signage at 505 in CCHS `P-1128`, supported by the independent City identification of Farr's Pool Hall at 505. Classification: **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE**.
2. **1945 — Harr secondary chronology anchor:** Oregon City historic-resource directory synthesis reports Harr's Recreation Center at 505. Classification: **OFFICIAL SECONDARY / UNDERLYING DIRECTORY PAGE UNRECOVERED**.
3. **August–September 1946 — directory observation window:** Pacific Directory Service was conducting its county-wide canvass for the directory later cited as 1947–48 / 1948. This gives the unrecovered directory unusual value for the transition question.
4. **11 February 1948 — Harr direct municipal operator evidence:** Harr is directly named as an Oregon City pool-hall operator; the line itself does not print 505 Main.
5. **Late 1948 — Harr direct exact-address evidence:** Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center / 505 Main is directly printed in the municipal liquor-license applicant list.

The transition should therefore **not** be narrated as proven to have occurred in 1945. The City gives 1945 as an official-secondary chronology point, but the direct last-Farr / first-Harr transition evidence remains unrecovered.
