# 1907 Main Street research source capture

Research date: 21 August 2026  
Scope: 501, 503, 505, and 507 Main Street, Oregon City, with Fifth–Sixth intersection anchors and construction/demolition/public-works evidence used to reconstruct the block.

## S-079 — Pacific States Telephone & Telegraph new central office, 1907

Type: Primary newspaper / construction evidence  
Related evidence: `E-065`  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83025138/1907-04-01/ed-1/seq-4/

Observed claim: the company had nearly completed a modern new Oregon City central-office plant on the **east side of Main Street between Fifth and Sixth Streets**. Work had begun more than a year earlier and the company reported approximately $30,000 in local plant improvements.

Limitation: no numbered address or lot is printed. The east-side location must not be converted into a later odd-numbered 501/503/505/507 address without the 1906 numbering plat or equivalent mapping evidence.

## S-080 — Main Street railway-track, paving, and grade reconstruction, 1907

Type: Primary newspaper / public works and railway evidence  
Related evidence: `E-066`

Verified pages:

- 1 April 1907, *Morning Oregonian*, page 14: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83025138/1907-04-01/ed-1/seq-14/
- 9 May 1907, *Morning Oregonian*, page 10: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83025138/1907-05-09/ed-1/seq-10/
- 10 May 1907, *Oregon City Enterprise*, page 5: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1907-05-10/ed-1/seq-5/
- 16 August 1907, *Oregon City Courier*, page 5: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1907-08-16/ed-1/seq-5/

Observed claims:

- Oregon Water Power & Railway planned to reconstruct its Main Street line from **Eleventh Street to the Basin**, effectively creating a new roadbed through the paved business district.
- Heavy new city rail was delivered in May. Source descriptions differ on nominal rail weight (90 pounds versus 86 pounds per yard), so the discrepancy is preserved.
- Main Street paving was disturbed and relaid as the work moved south.
- The 16 August report states that Main Street was being **raised to meet the new track, approximately six inches higher than the former track**.

Significance: Fifth–Sixth lies inside the stated Eleventh-to-Basin corridor, making this direct physical-change evidence for the target block.

Limitation: no source states that 501/503/505/507 predecessor buildings were demolished, moved, or rebuilt because of the work.

## Exact-address search result

Targeted searches for contemporary 1907 references to **501 Main**, **503 Main**, **505 Main**, and **507 Main** in Oregon City did not recover a primary source explicitly naming an occupant at those later-numbered addresses.

This is a research-status statement only. It is **not evidence of vacancy, absence of buildings, or failure of the 1906 numbering system**. Primary numbered-address evidence for the target frontage is known from nearby years, and the 1906 numbering plat remains the preferred source for establishing the original mapping.

## Construction / demolition / rebuilding watch — 1907

Recovered:

- completion of a substantial Pacific States telephone central office on the east side of Main between Fifth and Sixth;
- wholesale Main Street streetcar-track/roadbed reconstruction through the target corridor;
- removal and relaying of brick paving;
- an approximately **six-inch increase in track/street elevation**;
- contemporary business anchors at Fifth & Main and Sixth & Main.

Not recovered:

- a direct report that the predecessor buildings corresponding to later **501, 503, 505, or 507 Main** were demolished, moved, replaced, or newly constructed during 1907;
- a fire in 1907 that can be securely mapped to one of those target storefronts.

The negative result is logged as research status, not proof that no building work occurred.

## Cross-year Block 26 leads found during the 1907 sweep

Later evidence should be used to continue `RL-026`, not projected backward as a 1907 claim:

- By 1910, newspaper reporting describes a **new two-story Gambrinus Brewing Company business block at Main and Sixth** under construction, creating a likely structural-change window after the old three-story frame William Tell property was documented there in 1906. Direct proof of demolition/replacement mechanics remains to be recovered.
- A 1913 assessment/tax notice lists Block 26 ownership by lot, including **lot 5 — Mary E. Barlow / Juliette E. David; lot 6 — First National Bank; lot 7 — Frank Jaggar; lot 8 — Gambrinus Brewing Company**. This may materially improve lot-to-address reconstruction when combined with the 1906 numbering plat and Sanborn footprints.

These are follow-up clues and should receive dedicated evidence/source registration when the reconstruction reaches those years or when the underlying pages are formally integrated.

## Year status — 1907

Documented:

- major Main Street track, paving, and grade reconstruction affecting the Fifth–Sixth block;
- roughly six-inch roadway/track elevation change;
- new telephone central-office construction/completion within the Fifth–Sixth block on the east side of Main;
- Mount Hood Cafe as a Fifth/Main anchor;
- John Gohra's saloon as a Main/Sixth anchor;
- Tolpolar opposite the Post Office.

Still unresolved:

- exact 1907 occupant names for later-numbered 501, 503, 505, and 507 Main;
- exact 1907 street numbers for the Mount Hood Cafe, Gohra saloon, Tolpolar store, and telephone office;
- whether any target odd-side predecessor building changed structurally in 1907;
- precise effect of the six-inch street-grade increase on storefront thresholds/facades;
- original 1906 numbering plat and full Block 26 lot-to-address mapping.

## Handling rule

Do not infer an exact modern address from an intersection description, opposite/near wording, later business address, or the east/west side of Main. Keep street/public-works changes separate from building-replacement claims unless a source explicitly links them.
