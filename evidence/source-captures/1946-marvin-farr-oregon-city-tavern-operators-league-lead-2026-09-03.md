# 1946 Marvin Farr — Oregon City tavern-operators league lead

Date checked: 3 September 2026

Status: **CONTEMPORARY TRADE-PRESS TEXT VERIFIED; PAGE IMAGE NOT VISUALLY CERTIFIED IN CURRENT ENVIRONMENT. IDENTITY / 505 MAIN CONNECTION UNRESOLVED.**

Purpose: preserve a new postwar Oregon City Farr-name lead that may help explain the Farr family's recreation/tavern context, while preventing an unsupported merge with Edwin F. Farr, Alice Farr, Farr's Pool Hall, or 505 Main.

## 19 October 1946 — The Billboard

The 19 October 1946 issue of *The Billboard*, Coin Machines section, page 89, carries an item headed **“Oregon Local Option Threat for Taverns.”** It is datelined **OREGON CITY, Ore., Oct. 12**.

The article reports that the threat of a county local-option vote had caused **Clackamas County tavern operators** to form a league. It states that the league elected:

- **Marvin Farr, Oregon City — president**;
- Tommy Barry, Oregon City — secretary;
- E. O. McLaughlin, Milwaukie — treasurer;
- Frank Rice, Canby; Charles Bennett, Molalla; and Walt Felbrich, Oregon City — directors.

It further says the league would canvass licensees and that tavern owners were urging customers to register for the November vote.

Search-index rendering directly identifies the issue as **October 19, 1946 — The Billboard — Coin Machines — 89** and reproduces the Oregon City item. A separate web-access copy exposes the same article. The full PDF was too large for the current page-open route, so this source is not recorded as visually certified in this session.

## What the article establishes

The article directly places a **Marvin Farr in Oregon City** in October 1946 and identifies him as president of a newly formed league composed of tavern operators. This is strong occupational/business-context evidence that Marvin Farr was part of the local licensed tavern trade in 1946.

The article does **not** print:

- Marvin Farr's tavern/business name;
- a street address;
- 505 Main;
- Harr's Recreation Center;
- Farr's Pool Hall;
- Edwin F. Farr or Alice Farr; or
- any family relationship among the named Farr individuals.

Therefore it cannot be used to assign Marvin Farr to 505 Main or to make him the operator/successor of Farr's Pool Hall.

## Possible family lead — not a merge

A separate genealogy-index search result for **Alice Clarinda Sprague** reports a 1913 marriage and a son named **Marvin**, born in 1914. That result does not expose the husband's name in the accessible snippet and is not a primary record. Because the project separately has a 1918 local-newspaper reference to **Mrs. Alice Farr of Molalla**, this creates a potentially useful lead that the 1946 Marvin Farr could belong to the Edwin/Alice Farr family.

That relationship is **UNVERIFIED**. It must not be used as a person merge or 505 occupancy claim until a marriage, birth, census, obituary, deed, directory, or other primary record explicitly bridges the individuals.

## Why this matters for the 505 chronology

The project's present 505 sequence has:

- unnamed billiards/cigars use in the 1925 Sanborn;
- official-secondary evidence naming **Edwin F. and Alice Farr** as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main**;
- official-secondary placement of **Harr's Recreation Center** at 505 by 1945;
- primary municipal text evidence for Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center at 505 in late 1948.

The 1946 Marvin Farr item does not alter that sequence. Its value is as a new research lead into whether another Farr family member remained active in Oregon City's tavern/recreation trade during the exact period when the Farr→Harr transition is still unresolved.
