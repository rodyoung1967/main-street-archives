# Frank D. Cox — family identity anchors and cautions

Status: **identity-reconciliation note; do not use later name matches to assign the 1915 pool-hall address**

This note separates several later Frank Cox records so that the Main Street pool-hall research does not accidentally merge unrelated men.

## Strong Oregon City family anchor — Ollie Jane Hugger Cox

A 10 November 1983 *Sandy Post* obituary for **Ollie Wilson**, born Ollie Jane Hugger, states that:

- she was born 15 September 1887;
- she married **Frank D. Cox** in Oregon City on **3 July 1907**;
- they lived in Oregon City;
- Frank died in **1957**;
- Ollie later married Fred Wilson in 1964;
- a survivor was her **stepson Frank D. Cox Jr. of Woodburn**.

Primary newspaper source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/2024240281/1983-11-10/ed-1/seq-3/

This is a strong biographical anchor for an Oregon City **Frank D. Cox** family. It is consistent with the 2002 Oregon City historic-resource survey for 514 Jefferson, which says Frank and Ollie Cox bought that house in 1915 and that Cox operated a downtown billiards room and cigar store.

Official city source:
https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/7953/514_Jefferson

## Independent family-line corroboration

A 24 July 1928 *Capital Journal* item states that **Mr. and Mrs. Frank Cox of Oregon City** visited Frank's parents, **Mr. and Mrs. Sol Cox**, in Independence.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn90066132/1928-07-24/ed-1/seq-3/

A 4 October 1937 *Capital Journal* item identifies **Mrs. Frank Cox of Oregon City** as the daughter-in-law of Sol Cox Sr. and says she was present with her husband and their son **Frank Jr.**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn90066132/1937-10-04/ed-1/seq-2/

These later items make the Sol Cox → Frank Cox of Oregon City family relationship strong. They still do not, by themselves, prove that every earlier `F. D. Cox` or `Frank Cox` business reference is the same man, although the chronology is compatible.

## Separate Mount View Cemetery name match — do not merge

A third-party transcription of Mount View Cemetery lists a **Frank Cox** who died **30 June 1952**, age 76. This conflicts with Ollie's 1983 obituary, which says her husband Frank D. Cox died in **1957**.

Source:
https://www.interment.net/data/us/or/clackamas/mt-view-cemetery/records-c.htm

Until a primary death record, obituary, grave record, spouse/family association, or other direct identity source resolves the 1952 burial, **do not merge the 1952 Frank Cox into Frank D. Cox, husband of Ollie**. The cemetery transcription may describe another Frank Cox.

## 1916 Lyle `Mrs. Frank Cox` remains unresolved

The 24 August 1916 *Oregon City Courier* says Mrs. J. H. Graham went from Oregon City to **Lyle, Washington**, to visit her sister, **Mrs. Frank Cox**.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-08-24/ed-1/seq-5/

The later Oregon City Frank/Ollie family evidence does **not** prove that this Lyle Mrs. Frank Cox was Ollie. Keep the Lyle identity unresolved unless a Graham/Hugger sibling record establishes the relationship.

## Business-history implication

The safest current handling is:

- **F. D. Cox** is directly licensed by Oregon City for a pool/billiard business in October 1912.
- **Frank Cox / F. D. Cox** is directly tied to the Cox Main Street pool hall in 1915–March 1916.
- Oregon City's 2002 survey independently says **Frank Cox** operated a downtown billiards room and cigar store and that **Frank and Ollie Cox** owned 514 Jefferson from 1915 to 1931.
- Later family records strongly establish a **Frank D. Cox + Ollie + Sol Cox family line in Oregon City**, but later family evidence should support identity cautiously and must not substitute for a direct premises/address record.

The exact 1915–1916 Cox pool-hall street number remains unresolved.
