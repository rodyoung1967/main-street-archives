## Historical-use boundary

CCHS directly places the scene on Main Street looking south after the 1890 flood waters had receded, between the alley and Sixth Street. This gives valuable fixed-location streetscape context. The photo/catalog record by itself does **not** assign an exact 501/503/505 address to a particular facade and does not prove physical-building continuity to later structures.

The CCHS description refers to a Sanborn map “below” for visible-structure locations, and the public entry includes an associated Sanborn-map image. That map attachment is **not interpreted or separately committed in this integration**; any structure-by-structure mapping should be visually reviewed and recorded as a separate evidence step.
