## Research consequence

The **1960 Polk Oregon City Directory** is now supported by both:

1. a current genealogy/catalog index pointing to surviving image/catalog copies; and
2. an official Oregon City historic-review report explicitly citing the 1960 R. L. Polk volume.

This makes the volume a high-confidence source target, not a speculative directory year.

For the Hansen / 505 Tavern question, inspect the original volume for:

- 505 Main street listing;
- 505 Tavern or predecessor trade name;
- Ray / Raymond Hansen and separate Hanson spelling variant;
- tavern/bar/pool/billiards/recreation classified listings;
- numerical telephone section if present;
- full 501–511 odd Main block;
- title/front matter.

A directory association proves only the relationship actually printed; it does not automatically prove business sale, liquor-license ownership, tenancy, or real-estate ownership.
