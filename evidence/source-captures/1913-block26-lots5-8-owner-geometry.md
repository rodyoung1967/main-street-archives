# 1913 Block 26 Lots 5–8 — owner and geometry reconstruction

Status: **primary newspaper text verified at archive text layer; official city lot-dimension context; storefront mapping remains proportional/inferential**

Purpose: consolidate the original Block 26 legal-lot sequence along Main Street between Fifth and Sixth and provide a safer framework for tracing the July 1922 S. Wolf fire-origin property.

## 23 October 1913 Morning Enterprise assessment notice

The University of Oregon Historic Oregon Newspapers page for **Morning Enterprise, 23 Oct 1913, image 8** contains a municipal assessment notice whose archive text layer states that the assessment boundary reaches Fifth Street and then runs:

- east along Fifth Street to the line between **Lots 4 and 5, Block 26**;
- **north along the line between Lots 1, 2, 3, 4 and Lots 5, 6, 7, 8 of Block 26 to Sixth Street**.

Canonical archive page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063701/1913-10-23/ed-1/seq-8/ocr/

The same notice lists the assessed owners of the four Block 26 lots in that row:

- **Lot 5 — Mary E. Barlow and Juliette E. David — $10.71**
- **Lot 6 — First National Bank — $10.71**
- **Lot 7 — Frank Jaggar — $21.42**
- **Lot 8 — Gambrinus Brewing Company — $32.13**

Verification state for the exact wording above: **ARCHIVE TEXT/OCR LAYER CONFIRMED; page-image visual certification still desirable.** The names and lot numbers are unusually clear in the archive transcription, but the research standard still distinguishes the text layer from direct page-image inspection.

## Independent Lot 8 anchor

Separate 1906 primary newspaper / realty-transfer evidence already registered as `E-064` ties the **William Tell Hotel at Sixth & Main** to **Lot 8, Block 26**. The reported property measured approximately **61 by 100 feet** and was sold to Gambrinus Brewing Company.

This independently fixes **Lot 8 at the Sixth/Main end** of the Main-facing Block 26 row.

Related evidence:
`evidence/E-064-1906-william-tell-hotel-block26.md`

## Official Oregon City lot-dimension context

Oregon City's McLoughlin Conservation District documentation states that blocks from the original Oregon City plat are generally approximately **200 by 200 feet** and divided into **50 by 100-foot lots** (with stated exceptions elsewhere in the district).

Official city source:
https://www.orcity.org/1240/McLoughlin-Conservation-District

This is general plat-pattern context, not a survey of Block 26 itself.

## Strong geometry inference

Combining:

1. the 1913 boundary line separating Lots 1–4 from Lots 5–8 and running Fifth-to-Sixth;
2. the independent Lot 8 = Sixth/Main anchor; and
3. the general original-plat pattern of four approximately 50-foot lots along a roughly 200-foot block side,

supports the following **strong working sequence along the Main Street side from Fifth north toward Sixth**:

**Lot 5 → Lot 6 → Lot 7 → Lot 8**.

This sequence is substantially stronger than a generic guess from modern addresses and should be used when searching 1913–1922 deeds, assessment rolls and realty-transfer notices.

## Relationship to 501 / 503 / 505 — NOT YET FIXED

The official May 1925 Sanborn Sheet 6 has been visually reviewed and shows separate numbered storefronts **501, 503 and 505 Main** at the Fifth end of the block, with 503 used as a restaurant and 505 as billiards/cigars.

Official sheet:
https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/4866/1925-6-PDF

Current public parcel records report both the 503 and 505 parcels at approximately **0.05 acre (~2,178 sq ft)**. If each retains roughly 100 feet of depth, that is compatible with narrow roughly 20–22-foot frontage parcels. This makes it geometrically plausible that **501 + 503 together occupy approximately the first 50-foot original lot and that 505 begins at or near the Lot 5/Lot 6 boundary**.

That is currently a **PROPORTIONAL HYPOTHESIS ONLY**. It is not a deed/survey/Sanborn-lot-line proof and must not be used to state that 503 = Lot 5 or 505 = Lot 6.

Current parcel context:
- 503 Main: APN 00572650 / tax lot 2-2E-31AB-08200 / approx. 0.05 acre
- 505 Main: APN 00572669 / tax lot 2-2E-31AB-08300 / approx. 0.05 acre

The current legal descriptions belong to a later assessor/legal system and must not be silently equated with original Block 26 lot numbers.

## Why this matters to the 1922 fire

The 18 July 1922 fire began in a vacant Main Street storeroom formerly occupied by Alex Schieremann, owned by **S. Wolf of Portland**, with a pool hall adjoining it.

If later evidence establishes the ignition room in the first 50 feet north of Fifth, the 1913 predecessor chain to prioritize is **Lot 5 (Barlow/David)**. If the relevant room lies immediately north of that boundary, **Lot 6 (First National Bank)** becomes the parallel chain.

This geometry is compatible with—but does not prove—the current strongest reconstruction:

- **503 predecessor = ignition room**
- **505 = adjoining pool hall**

**501 remains a live alternative.**
