# 2014 Lake Oswego survey — direct-use provenance route for the 1947–1948 Clackamas County Directory

Date reviewed: 5 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL SURVEY PDF TEXT / DIRECT DIRECTORY-USE PROVENANCE — TARGET OREGON CITY PAGES NOT INSPECTED; NO FARR/HARR/505 OCCUPANCY CLAIM PROMOTED.**

Purpose: preserve a newly verified institutional route to the exact **Pacific Directory Service, 1947–1948 Directory of Clackamas County** needed for the unresolved Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center transition at 505 Main. This route is stronger than a bibliography-only citation because a 2014 City of Lake Oswego historic-resource survey actually uses information from the directory to resolve a specific residence chronology.

## Direct-use evidence — 469 Seventh Street survey

City of Lake Oswego, **Oregon Inventory of Historic Properties Historic Resource Survey Form, 469 Seventh Street**, recorded August 2014:

`https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/sites/default/files/fileattachments/boc_hrab/webpage/18341/104_469_seventh_street_ils_0.pdf`

On survey-form page 3 of 8, the statement of significance discusses Charles D. and Kay Needham. It contrasts an older inventory's 1948 purchase date with the **1947–1948 Clackamas County Directory**, which places the Needhams at **“First and C”** and uses that entry to suggest that they purchased or rented the property a year earlier.

This is important because it demonstrates that the 2014 research team was not merely copying the directory title into a bibliography. The form uses an actual directory entry as evidence in its historical analysis.

The form's research-source checklist also marks **City Directories** and identifies **Lake Oswego Public Library** as the local library used for the project. The bibliography identifies:

**Pacific Directory Service. 1947–1948. _Directory of Clackamas County_. Portland, Oregon.**
