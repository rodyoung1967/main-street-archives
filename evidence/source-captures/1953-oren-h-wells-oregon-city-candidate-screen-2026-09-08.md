# Oren H. Wells — 1953 Oregon City candidate screen for Well's Recreation / 505 Main

Date reviewed: **8 September 2026**

Status: **CONTEMPORARY NEWSPAPER OCR/TEXT-ONLY LOCALITY LEAD + SECONDARY CEMETERY CORROBORATION; NO 505 / WELL'S RECREATION / POOL / LIQUOR / 9179 BRIDGE; UNVERIFIED LEAD ONLY.**

## Purpose

Test a newly surfaced exact-name candidate, **Oren H. Wells**, against the unresolved proprietor/operator identity behind the Oregon SHPO survey's statement that a **1953 city directory listed “Well's Recreation” at 505 Main Street**.

This is a candidate-screen record, not a person-identification record. The surname resemblance must not be converted into a business relationship without contemporary evidence connecting the person to the premises, trade name, occupation, liquor license, or telephone number.

## 1. Contemporary 1953 Oregon City locality anchor

Search indexing for the **Capital Journal (Salem), 14 September 1953, page 7 / image 7** exposes an article about the golden wedding anniversary of John H. Wells and his wife.

Historic Oregon Newspapers OCR route:

`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn90066132/1953-09-14/ed-1/seq-7/ocr/`

The indexed/OCR text includes two useful exact-name locality statements:

- **Mrs. Oren H. Wells of Oregon City** is named as assisting; and
- the caller list includes **Mr. and Mrs. Oren H. Wells**.

This is a direct contemporary **1953 Oregon City locality lead** for an adult Oren H. Wells household in the exact year of the unrecovered Salisbury directory.

### Visual-verification boundary

The project attempted to recover the original page object after locating the OCR/indexed text, but the Historic Oregon Newspapers image endpoint could not be retrieved in the current research environment. The page has therefore **not been visually certified in this pass**.

Classification: **CONTEMPORARY NEWSPAPER OCR/TEXT ONLY / SCAN CERTIFICATION OPEN**.

Do not promote the wording to visually verified primary evidence until the original page image is inspected.

## 2. Later Oregon City cemetery corroboration

A public Mountain View Cemetery transcription lists:

- **WELLS, Oren H.**
- death: **15 September 1961**
- age: **52**
- Mountain View Cemetery, Oregon City.

Source:

`https://www.interment.net/data/us/or/clackamas/mt-view-cemetery/records-w-z.htm`

A separate burial-index aggregator also identifies an Oren H. Wells buried at Mountain View Cemetery in Oregon City.

This later burial record is useful only as independent evidence that an Oren H. Wells of approximately the expected adult age had an Oregon City-area death/burial association by 1961. It does **not** establish his 1953 occupation, home address, telephone number, business ownership, liquor-license status, or any connection to 505 Main.

## 4. Comparison with Dal Wells candidate

The repository already preserves **Dal Wells** as a surname/geography candidate because a 1950 census index places him at 1306 Harrison Street, Oregon City. Oren H. That does **not** make Oren the more likely proprietor in any historical sense. Neither candidate has a recovered business/address/license/telephone bridge, and neither should be promoted by surname proximity alone.
