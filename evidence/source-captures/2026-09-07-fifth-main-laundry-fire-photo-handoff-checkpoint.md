## Oregon City Laundry chronology — superseding sequence

### Earlier location
- 1910: Third & Main.
- 1912: Main near Third.

### 1916 site-change sequence — resolved
- **3 Feb 1916:** initial new-laundry plan behind the Courier office / Seventh–Eighth block.
- **17 Feb 1916:** visually verified Courier item says E. M. Blakeslee traded that intended site for a **Fifth Street location** and would rebuild there.
- **2 Apr 1916:** Sunday Oregonian says the new laundry **on Fifth Street just off Main** was nearing completion.

Thus the old February/April “location conflict” is superseded: the sources document a **site change**.

### Fifth Street anchors
- 1918: **109 Fifth St.**
- Aug 1921: laundry's own ad says **5th St., near Main**.
- Jul 1922: Fifth Street plant destroyed by fire.

### May & Washburn / garage
- May & Washburn is documented by **December 1920**, earlier than the old April 1921 lower bound.
- Scripture & May is documented at **108 Fifth** in 1918; whether May & Washburn inherited that exact premises remains unresolved.
- May & Washburn continued after the July 1922 fire, so the business name alone is not an upper-bound dating clue.
- If a photo is matched to the **specific pre-fire frame garage** destroyed with the laundry, it must predate the fire. Current business-name window: **Dec 1920 → Jul 1922**, pending facade/address confirmation.

## July 1922 Harding / Wolf / Gates / fire sequence

### 13 Jul 1922 property sale
Visually verified Banner-Courier report says George A. Harding's Main Street property group, including stores occupied by the Fifth Street Restaurant, Miller's shoe store, an adjoining barber shop and Red Front Clothing Store, was sold to **S. Wolf** and **C. Gates**, both of Portland.

The report's wording assigns the **Fifth Street Restaurant location to C. Gates** and the **remainder to S. Wolf**.

Registered evidence: `E-096`.

### 18 Jul / 20 Jul 1922 fire
Visually verified local report says:
- fire began in a vacant Main Street storeroom formerly occupied by **Alex Schieremann as a tailor shop**;
- the building where the fire began was owned by **S. Wolf of Portland**;
- a pool hall adjoined the former tailor premises;
- affected cluster included Oregon City Laundry, May & Washburn garage, Fifth Street Restaurant, Gilbert Miller shoe-repair shop and Ketchner confectionery/home;
- a same-issue item says Ketchner's confectionery was **next to the laundry**.

The sale five days earlier plus the fire-owner wording creates a **strong inference** that the ignition building belonged to the newly reported Harding-to-Wolf remainder, but this remains short of deed-level proof.

Registered evidence: `E-103`.

### August 1922 Gates rebuild
Visually inspected August reports show **C. E. Gates** rebuilding the Fifth/Main restaurant property destroyed in the fire with a new two-story hollow-tile building containing ground-floor stores and upper apartments.

Registered evidence: `E-097`.

### Exact storefront remains unresolved
Do **not** claim the ignition room was definitively 501, 503 or 505 Main.

Current model testing leaves multiple arrangements live. The familiar **503-predecessor ignition / 505 adjoining pool-hall** model remains a hypothesis; **501 remains a live alternative**. Later 1925 uses cannot simply be backdated to July 1922.

Cadastral correction: the odd-numbered **501/503/505/507 frontage is original Block 4, not Block 26**. Older notes placing that odd-side frontage in Block 26 are superseded by `E-094` and the block-side correction research.

## S. Wolf / Wolf & Miller / Gilbert Miller identity rules

### Established
- Wolf & Miller visually verified at **505 Main** in Oct 1918 and dissolving partnership.
- Springfield sources strongly identify **Gilbert Miller** as the partner moving to Oregon City in Aug 1918.
- A separate 20 Aug 1918 Eugene Daily Guard report genuinely prints **C. E. Miller**; preserve this as a contemporary-source discrepancy, not merely OCR error.
- Gilbert Miller later appears in the July 1922 fire cluster and at **209 Fifth** after the fire.
- `S. Wolf` appears separately in an **8 Jun 1922 Oregon City merchant/business roster**.
- 13 Jul Harding sale and 20 Jul fire provide a direct contemporary property sequence involving **S. Wolf of Portland**.

### Do not merge without a bridge
- S. Wolf = Wolf partner in Wolf & Miller;
- S. Wolf = Samuel Wolf;
- S. Wolf = Simon Wolf;
- Victor Wolf = S. Wolf;
- S. Wolf = later Wolf-family owner of 503;
- Clara Rose Wolf's family = S. Wolf / later 503 Wolf family.

The chronology currently weakens a simple `S. Wolf = Wolf & Miller partner` equation because Gilbert Miller is the most natural candidate for the partner who stayed in Oregon City after the Oct 1918 dissolution, implying the Wolf partner may have been the one leaving for Idaho. This is inference, not explicit wording.
