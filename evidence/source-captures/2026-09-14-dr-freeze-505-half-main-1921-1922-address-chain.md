## Independent online corroboration from the same address sequence

Historic Oregon Newspapers search results independently expose later advertisements that continue to place **Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist** at **505½ Main Street**:

- **26 Aug 1921, Oregon City Enterprise, p.3 / image 3** — ad reads `Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist` and `505½ Main St. Oregon City`, opposite the post office. Stable page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1921-08-26/ed-1/seq-3/
- **9 Dec 1921, Oregon City Enterprise, p.6 / image 6** — ad again lists `Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist`, `505½ Main St., Oregon City`, opposite post office. Stable PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1921-12-09/ed-1/seq-6.pdf
- **30 Dec 1921, Oregon City Enterprise, p.3 / image 3** — ad lists `Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist`, `505½ Main St. Oregon City`, opposite post office. Stable page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1921-12-30/ed-1/seq-3/
- **5 Jan 1922, Banner-Courier, p.3 / image 3** — ad lists Dr. Freeze at `505½ Main St. Oregon City`, opposite post office. Stable page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-01-05/ed-1/seq-3/
- **19 Jan 1922, Banner-Courier, p.2 / image 2** — ad OCR renders the half-address imperfectly, but the recurring advertisement is the same 505½ Main office sequence. Stable page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-01-19/ed-1/seq-2/
- **20 Jan 1922, Oregon City Enterprise, p.3 / image 3** — ad lists Dr. Freeze at `505½ Main St. Oregon City`, opposite post office. Stable page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1922-01-20/ed-1/seq-3/
- **8 Jun 1922, Banner-Courier, p.10 / image 10** — visually reviewed in the 1922 workstream; ad lists `DR. FREEZE, EYE SPECIALIST`, `505½ Main St`, opposite post office. Stable page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-06-08/ed-1/seq-10/

Existing project work also records a late-June 1922 notice that Dr. Freeze was leaving the 505½ Main office after 1 July for the Masonic Building. By **19 Oct 1922**, his advertising is at **207–8 Masonic Building**, Oregon City.

## Revised address chronology

The current documented sequence should therefore be expressed conservatively as:

- **27 May 1921 — directly documented at 505½ Main Street** (project-steward visual review; photograph included in item).
- **Aug–Dec 1921 — repeated contemporary advertising at 505½ Main Street**.
- **Jan–Jun 1922 — repeated contemporary advertising at 505½ Main Street**.
- **After 1 July 1922 — departure from 505½ Main is reported in project newspaper work**.
- **By 19 Oct 1922 — Dr. Freeze advertises at 207–8 Masonic Building**.

This is substantially stronger than treating June 1922 as the first known Dr. Freeze / 505½ anchor.

## Structural significance for 505 / 505½

The May 1921 evidence materially strengthens the conclusion that **505½ was a durable, functioning address designation before the July 1922 fire**, rather than a short-lived numbering artifact introduced immediately before the fire.

It still does **not** establish what 505½ represented physically. Under the project's photographic/frontage controls, do not model 505½ automatically as a fourth Main-facing storefront or as an upstairs suite. It may represent a secondary/interior/rear subdivision within the 505 footprint, but that remains a working hypothesis pending direct floor-plan, permit, deed, assessment, or other spatial evidence.
