# Dr. Freeze — Oregon City address chain, 1921–1922

Date recorded: 14 September 2026

## Status

**DOCUMENTED / DIRECT for the published professional addresses below.** Original newspaper scans are visually verified where stated in the canonical evidence/source records and in project-steward review.

This note supersedes the earlier shorthand that treated **13 May 1921** as the earliest 505½ occurrence. The preserved 13 May page actually prints **505 Main St.** without the half-number. The repo's earlier canonical evidence (`E-137`, `S-153`, `E-028`, `S-035`) establishes Dr. Freeze at **505½ Main by 8 April 1921**.

## Pre-505 office: 507½ Main

- **18 Mar 1921 — Oregon City Enterprise:** article names **Dr. Harvey Freeze**, says he moved his practice from Portland to Oregon City, and describes his office as **over Hardings Drug Store**; no numbered Oregon City address is printed.
- **25 Mar 1921 — Oregon City Enterprise:** visually verified advertisement prints **507½ Main St.**
- **1 Apr 1921 — Oregon City Enterprise:** visually verified advertisement again prints **507½ Main St.**
- **8 Apr 1921 — Oregon City Enterprise:** visually verified advertisement prints **505½ Main Street**.

Therefore the advertised exact-address change from **507½ → 505½** occurred **after 1 April and by 8 April 1921**. The exact physical move day is not established.

### 505½ Main
- Earliest exact half-numbered address currently documented: **8 Apr 1921**.

### 505 Main without the half-number

### Masonic Building
- The **30 Jun 1922** advertisement prospectively states that **after July 1** Freeze will be at **207 Masonic Bldg.**
- Later 1922 advertising places him at the Masonic Building; by October he is documented there in the existing project chronology.

## Structural significance

The 8 Apr 1921 → 30 Jun 1922 run establishes sustained use of the **505/505½ address designation** for Dr. Freeze before the July 1922 Fifth/Main fire. The half-number alone does **not** establish a floor. Under the project's frontage/photo controls, do not model 505½ automatically as a fourth Main-facing storefront or as an upstairs suite.
