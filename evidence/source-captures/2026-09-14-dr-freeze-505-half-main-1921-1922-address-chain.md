# Dr. Freeze — 505½ Main Street address chain, 1921–1922

Date recorded: 14 September 2026

## Status

**DOCUMENTED / DIRECT for the published 505½ Main address in multiple contemporary advertisements.**

The project steward has visually inspected original *Oregon City Enterprise* issue images and directly verified Dr. Freeze at **505½ Main Street** on the following dates:

- **27 May 1921** — item includes a photograph of Dr. Freeze and explicitly lists **505½ Main Street**.
- **7 Oct 1921, p.3** — Dr. Freeze is listed at **505½ Main Street**.
- **10 Mar 1922** — Dr. Freeze is listed at **505½ Main Street**.
- **30 Jun 1922** — Dr. Freeze is listed at **505½ Main Street**.

These four dates are therefore treated as **PROJECT-STEWARD VISUALLY VERIFIED** original-newspaper evidence, not OCR/text-only leads. Repository copies of the corresponding page images remain source-file ingest tasks where the binary has not yet been preserved locally.

Existing project work also records a late-June 1922 notice that Dr. Freeze was leaving the 505½ Main office after 1 July for the Masonic Building. By **19 Oct 1922**, his advertising is at **207–8 Masonic Building**, Oregon City.

## Revised address chronology

The current documented sequence should therefore be expressed conservatively as:

- **27 May 1921 — visually verified at 505½ Main Street; item includes Dr. Freeze photograph.**
- **26 Aug 1921 — contemporary ad at 505½ Main Street.**
- **7 Oct 1921, p.3 — project-steward visually verified at 505½ Main Street.**
- **9 Dec and 30 Dec 1921 — contemporary ads at 505½ Main Street.**
- **5, 19 and 20 Jan 1922 — continued 505½ Main advertising sequence.**
- **10 Mar 1922 — project-steward visually verified at 505½ Main Street.**
- **8 Jun 1922 — visually reviewed 505½ Main advertisement.**
- **30 Jun 1922 — project-steward visually verified at 505½ Main Street.**
- **After 1 July 1922 — departure from 505½ Main is reported in project newspaper work.**
- **By 19 Oct 1922 — Dr. Freeze advertises at 207–8 Masonic Building.**

This is substantially stronger than treating June 1922 as the first known Dr. Freeze / 505½ anchor. The evidence now documents a sustained 505½ Main office presence from at least **27 May 1921 through 30 Jun 1922**.

## Structural significance for 505 / 505½

The repeated 1921–1922 evidence materially strengthens the conclusion that **505½ was a durable, functioning address designation before the July 1922 fire**, rather than a short-lived numbering artifact introduced immediately before the fire.

The **30 Jun 1922** visual verification is especially significant because it places Dr. Freeze at 505½ Main immediately before his reported move and less than three weeks before the July fire.

It still does **not** establish what 505½ represented physically. Under the project's photographic/frontage controls, do not model 505½ automatically as a fourth Main-facing storefront or as an upstairs suite. It may represent a secondary/interior/rear subdivision within the 505 footprint, but that remains a working hypothesis pending direct floor-plan, permit, deed, assessment, or other spatial evidence.
