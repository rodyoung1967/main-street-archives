# 525 Main in 1915 — Grand Theatre; Cox pool hall candidate eliminated

Status: **strong secondary synthesis backed by contemporary newspaper and Sanborn sources**

The Oregon Theater Project's Oregon City Grand Theatre history identifies the theater's enduring location as **525 Main Street** beginning in **1909** and continuing through its known operating period to **1929**.

Source:
https://oregontheaterproject.uoregon.edu/theaters/grand-theatre-0

The project specifically cites multiple contemporary Oregon City newspaper items from **1914–1916**, including 1915 reports and advertisements, and reproduces/identifies Sanborn map evidence for the 525 Main theater premises. Its narrative states that in 1915 the theater facade was remodeled to resemble a `big town theater`, further confirming active theater use of the premises during the exact year being studied.

Relevant 1915 primary citations listed by the Oregon Theater Project include:

- *Oregon City Courier*, 1 Apr 1915, `Censorship Planned`;
- *Oregon City Courier*, 17 Jun 1915, `Chickens Must Behave`;
- *Oregon City Courier*, 23 Sep 1915;
- *Oregon City Courier*, 2 Dec 1915, `Back to Ten Cents`;
- additional 1915 page images reproduced on the project page.

## Implication for Frank Cox location research

The 1915 Cox-pool-hall raid narrative says officers left the Moose lodge and moved **down Main Street** before reaching Cox's hall. Because the Moose rooms had been associated with Sixth/Main over Price Bros., storefronts south of Sixth are being tested as candidates for Cox's unidentified Main Street pool hall.

**525 Main is now excluded as a Cox candidate for 1915.** The Grand Theatre was occupying 525 Main during that period.

This does not determine Cox's exact location. It simply removes one otherwise geographically plausible storefront from the candidate set.

Current odd-side address controls in the Fifth–Sixth block include:

- 503 — unresolved after L. Hylton's 1914 business;

- 507 — W. W. Bradley documented in 1915–16;
- 509 — H. P. Brightbill documented in 1915–16;

- 513 / 515 / 517 — still under investigation;

- 521 / 523 — still under investigation;
- **525 — Grand Theatre, therefore excluded for Cox**;

Do not infer Cox occupied any remaining unresolved number merely because other storefronts have been eliminated.

## Research-standard note

This elimination is based on a university research project's detailed theater history and its cited contemporary newspaper/Sanborn evidence. It is strong enough to remove 525 from the working candidate set, but the project should still capture and visually inspect the relevant original 1915 newspaper pages and Sanborn sheet if 525 becomes important to a formal final address reconstruction.
