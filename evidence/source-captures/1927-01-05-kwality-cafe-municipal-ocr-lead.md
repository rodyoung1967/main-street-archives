## Recovered text-layer wording

The indexed text identifies the meeting as **Oregon City, Oregon, January 5, 1927** and says the listed bills were a **continuation of the December Finance Report**. In the vendor/expense list it includes:

- `Kwality Cafe`
- associated expense description: `Meals`

The text layer therefore directly supports that a business styled **Kwality Cafe** was known to Oregon City municipal finance records at the turn of **December 1926 / January 1927**.
