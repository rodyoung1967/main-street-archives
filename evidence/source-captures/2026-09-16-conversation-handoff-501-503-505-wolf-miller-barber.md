## Wolf & Miller / Gilbert Miller research

### Direct 1918 Oregon City evidence

The **10 October 1918 Oregon City Courier** directly advertises/reports **Wolf & Miller, 505 Main**. The business sold men's clothing/furnishings, shoes and operated a shoe-repair department.

The wording is particularly important: the stock was being reduced/closed out; **after the sale one partner was going to Idaho and the other would restock and continue the business**. The article does not identify which partner left and which remained.

### New Springfield→Oregon City bridge

A **22 August 1918 Springfield News** report says **Gilbert Miller left Springfield for Oregon City to join his family**, which had moved there earlier. It says Miller had remained in Springfield to **close out his business**.

This fits a tight sequence:

- June–July 1918: Springfield Wolf & Miller advertising/closeout context.
- 22 August 1918: Gilbert Miller leaves Springfield for Oregon City after closing out his business.
- 10 October 1918: **Wolf & Miller is directly at 505 Main, Oregon City**, with a partnership/stock transition underway.

Current evidence classification:

**Gilbert Miller was the Miller of the 1918 Oregon City Wolf & Miller at 505 Main — STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE.**

Do not upgrade to DOCUMENTED/DIRECT until a source explicitly joins Gilbert Miller's full name to the Oregon City Wolf & Miller partnership.

### Wolf identity remains unresolved

Do **not** assume the Wolf partner was the later S. Wolf or L. Wolf.

The July 1922 sources currently conflict:

- **13 July 1922 Banner-Courier:** Harding property sold to **S. Wolf and C. Gates**, both Portland; Gates gets Fifth Street Restaurant location; Wolf gets remainder.
- **20 July 1922 Banner-Courier:** identifies **S. Wolf of Portland** as owner of the building where the fire started.
- **21 July 1922 Oregon City Enterprise:** identifies **L. Wolf** as owner of Red Front Clothing Store.

Preserve the S./L. initial conflict. Do not silently normalize it.

A compiled genealogy found during this conversation identifies Gilbert Miller's first wife as **Clara Rose Wolf**, daughter of Henry and Elizabeth (Conrad) Wolf. Treat this only as a **secondary genealogical lead**. It does not prove that Clara's Wolf family is the Wolf family later reported at 503, nor that her relative was the Wolf & Miller partner, S. Wolf, or L. Wolf. Primary deed/directory/probate/business evidence is required.

Therefore Freeze had vacated 505½ before the July 18 fire. Do not equate Freeze's former room with Schiermann's origin portion.
