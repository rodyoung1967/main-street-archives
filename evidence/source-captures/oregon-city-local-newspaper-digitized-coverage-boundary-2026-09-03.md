# Oregon City local-newspaper digitized-coverage boundary

Date checked: 3 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL DIGITAL-REPOSITORY HOLDINGS VERIFIED — ACCESS/RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT.**

Purpose: distinguish a newspaper title's historical publication span from the subset of issues actually digitized in the University of Oregon's Historic Oregon Newspapers database. This prevents false assumptions that post-1922 Oregon City local newspapers can be exhaustively searched online merely because the titles continued publishing.

## Official source

Historic Oregon Newspapers — **All Titles**:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/newspapers/

The title table separately reports the historical publication span, number of digitized issues, and first/last digitized dates.

## Oregon City titles relevant to this project

### Morning enterprise

The catalog describes the title as:

- place: **Oregon City, Clackamas**;
- publication span: **Oregon City, Or., 1911–1933**;
- digitized issues: **913**;
- first digitized date: **1911-01-08**;
- last digitized date: **1913-12-23**.

Therefore, the fact that *Morning enterprise* continued to 1933 does **not** mean its 1920s or 1930s issues are available in the Historic Oregon Newspapers online corpus. The presently listed digitized run ends in 1913.

### Oregon City enterprise

For the principal 1891-era title entry, the catalog reports:

- place: **Oregon City, Clackamas**;
- publication span: **Oregon City, Or., 1891–194?**;
- digitized issues: **1,624**;
- first digitized date: **1891-04-03**;
- last digitized date: **1922-12-29**.

Thus the title's survival/publication beyond 1922 does not create online full-text coverage after **29 December 1922** in this database.

### The banner-courier

The catalog reports:

- place: **Oregon City, Clackamas**;
- publication span: **Oregon City, Or., 1919–1950**;
- digitized issues: **52**;
- first digitized date: **1922-01-05**;
- last digitized date: **1922-12-28**.

The online collection therefore supplies the project's valuable 1922 *Banner-Courier* run but not a continuous digitized 1923–1950 local run.

## Research consequence

This corrects a potentially misleading search assumption: **publication span is not digitized-coverage span**.

For the unresolved 1925–1945 505 Main chronology — especially the first contemporary appearance of Farr's Pool Hall and the Farr→Harr transition — failure to find a hit in Historic Oregon Newspapers cannot be treated as negative evidence from Oregon City's local newspapers because the key local titles are not digitally represented online for most or all of that interval.

Broad searches of other digitized Oregon newspapers can still surface syndicated items, regional notices, advertisements or business-for-sale listings involving Oregon City, but they are not a substitute for the missing local run.

## Manual/repository implications

The project should continue to treat post-1922 local newspaper work as a **manual/microfilm or other repository-access route** unless a specific later local issue has been independently digitized elsewhere.

High-value manual date windows now include:

- **September 1930** — Ordinance 1219 / pool-hall licensing changes;
- **March 1932** — Ordinance 1234 / pool-hall license cap;
- **1933–1935** — beer-license era and the period surrounding the Farrs' 1934 residence acquisition;
- **early 1940s** — Farr's Pool Hall to Harr's Recreation Center transition.

## Evidence boundary

This checkpoint establishes only the **online holdings/access boundary** shown by the official title catalog. It does not establish that later physical issues do not survive, that a particular article was never published, or that Farr/Harr/505 Main is absent from the undigitized local newspapers.

Any future statement of “no local newspaper result” for the post-1922 period must preserve that distinction.
