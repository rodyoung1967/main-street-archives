## 1. 1988 Koler/Morrison West Linn inventory — explicit 1920 / 1926 / 1927 directory citation

Official City of West Linn source:

`https://westlinnoregon.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/planning/page/6488/1988_national_register_nomination_for_the_lewthwaitte-moffat_house.pdf`

Current public indexing exposes a City of West Linn Historic Resource Inventory sheet for **22840 Willamette Drive**, recorded by **Koler/Morrison Consultants**, dated **4/88**. The extracted sheet bibliography reads:

> `Clackamas County Telephone Directory, 1920, 1926, 1927.`

and separately cites TICOR Title Co. records in Oregon City.

The same indexed sheet says the property changed to William Moehnke in 1927 and describes him as owner of Oregon City Garage while retaining an Oregon City residence. That property narrative is not relevant to 503/505 occupancy and is not promoted here.

### Verification boundary

The West Linn PDF is a very large file and could not be rendered as page images in the current research environment. The directory citation is therefore **OCR/TEXT ONLY / VISUAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.

What it establishes at the current evidence level:

- Koler/Morrison's 1988 West Linn research explicitly cited telephone-directory issues labeled **1920, 1926, and 1927** under the source wording `Clackamas County Telephone Directory`;
- this is a concrete source-provenance lead for local directory material covering the exact 1926–1927 interval under study;
- it does **not** identify publisher, month, exact title page, holding institution, call number, completeness, or current survival of those specific issues;
- it does **not** identify 503 Main, 505 Main, Kwality, Farr, Jager, or another target business.

Failed rendering is a retrieval gap, not evidence that the cited directory pages no longer survive.

## 2. Repeated Koler/Morrison local-directory citations reduce the chance of a one-off bibliographic typo

Additional public indexing of the same West Linn historic-inventory corpus exposes other Koler/Morrison sheets with bibliography lines including:

- `Clackamas Co. Telephone Directory, 1929, 1930.`
- another indexed property sheet citing local Clackamas directory years **1930, 1931, 1935, and 1939**.

These remain **OCR/TEXT ONLY** because the underlying large PDF pages were not visually rendered in this pass.

Interpretation: the repeated year-specific citations make it substantially more likely that `Clackamas County Telephone Directory, 1920, 1926, 1927` reflects a systematic local telephone-directory source class used by the consultants rather than an isolated mistaken date. This is **source-provenance inference**, not proof of the exact physical identity or current repository of the 1926/1927 volumes.

## 3. Wilsonville Public Library — visually verified 1932 source-family control

Official Wilsonville Public Library source:

`https://www.wilsonvillelibrary.org/sites/default/files/fileattachments/library/page/26561/nw_heritage_index_2023-04-25.pdf`

The actual PDF page was visually inspected. Under the heading **Oregon – Clackamas County**, the source key prints:

> `OR CLAC D-OR.C 1932T = Telephone Directory, Oregon City, Clackamas County, Oregon, August 1932.`
>
> `(Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co., 1932.)`
>
> `Indexed only to identify other communities covered in addition to Oregon City.`

Classification: **VISUALLY VERIFIED INSTITUTIONAL SOURCE-FAMILY / HOLDINGS METADATA**.

This confirms that a local Oregon City/Clackamas telephone-directory issue in this source family existed by **August 1932**, was published by **Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co.**, and covered communities beyond Oregon City.

The Wilsonville key does **not** list 1926 or 1927 in the checked source-key page. Do not represent Wilsonville as holding the target 1926/1927 issue unless a direct catalog/holding record is later recovered.

The Northwest Heritage index also uses the `OR CLAC D-OR.C. 1932T` source for community-specific entries, including Milwaukie and Oak Grove page ranges. This further confirms that the 1932 directory was a regional/local directory rather than an Oregon City-only alphabetic pamphlet.

## 4. Oregon City / Koler-Morrison — visually verified 1916 local-directory citation

Official Oregon City historic-resource source:

`https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/7451/13600-Redland-Rd`

The actual source-list page and recorder/date page were visually inspected. The inventory was recorded by **Koler/Morrison** in **August 1990**, and its bibliography explicitly includes:

> `Oregon City Telephone Directory, 1916.`

Classification: **VISUALLY VERIFIED HISTORIC-RESOURCE SOURCE-PROVENANCE EVIDENCE**.

This is important because it demonstrates that the same Koler/Morrison preservation-research program explicitly used local **Oregon City telephone directories** as historical sources. Together with the 1988 West Linn `Clackamas County Telephone Directory, 1920, 1926, 1927` citation, it strengthens the conclusion that the 1926/1927 references belong to a real local-directory research source class.

It does not prove that the 1916 and 1926/1927 books had identical titles, publishers, geographic coverage, pagination, or holding institution.

## 5. Washington State Library — 1916 PT&T regional-title control

Washington State Library catalog metadata identifies an **August 1916** Pacific Telephone & Telegraph directory with title/coverage wording encompassing:

- Portland;
- Oregon City;
- Salem;
- Multnomah, Clackamas, Marion, Polk, Tillamook, Washington and Yamhill Counties in Oregon;
- Vancouver and Clarke County in Washington.

Catalog metadata gives call number `RARE V 917.95 PORTLAN 1916` and OCLC `923386267`.

Classification: **INSTITUTIONAL CATALOG / HOLDINGS FACT**, not visual inspection of the directory pages.

This independently confirms a PT&T regional directory family that included Oregon City and Clackamas County before the target period. It does not establish that Washington State Library holds the 1926 or 1927 issues.

## 6. Relationship to the already-preserved June 1927 auction copy

The earlier continuation already preserved a Holabird Western Americana auction description for a **June 1927 Pacific Telephone & Telegraph directory** covering Salem, Portland, Oregon City, Vancouver and the surrounding Oregon/Washington counties, including Clackamas County.

The new Koler/Morrison `Clackamas County Telephone Directory, 1927` citation is **compatible with** that June 1927 PT&T source family and materially strengthens the case that a local 1927 directory can answer Oregon City business/subscriber questions.

However, no title page or bibliographic bridge has yet proved that Koler/Morrison's cited `1927` directory is the **same June 1927 issue** represented by the auction copy. Treat same-issue identity as **PROBABLE / WORKING HYPOTHESIS**, not documented fact.
