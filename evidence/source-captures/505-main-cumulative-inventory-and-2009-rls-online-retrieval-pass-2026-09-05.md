## 1. Oregon City says historic inventory forms are cumulative

Current City Historic Resources page:

`https://www.orcity.org/1230/Historic-Resources`

Oregon City's current preservation guidance explains that SHPO inventory frameworks change over time and that a property can therefore have several inventory forms with different levels of detail. The City explicitly says the forms are **cumulative**: a newer form does not replace an older form; the forms should be read together to understand the building's evolution.

Research consequence for **505 Main**: request and inspect **every surviving 505 Main inventory-form version and attachment**, especially older survey material that may have supplied the unexplained 1941 / 1945 / 1953 business chronology later summarized on the City's legacy 505 page.

The current City page identifies Christina Robertson-Gardiner as Preservation Planner and states that inventory forms can be updated or annotated when errors or additional information are documented.

## 5. 2021 ODOT report visually proves the 505 row came from the SHPO historic-sites database context

Official ODOT / University of Oregon Museum of Natural & Cultural History report:

`https://www.oregon.gov/odot/Projects/Project%20Documents/Cultural%20Resources%20Baseline%20Report_REDACTED%20VERSION.pdf`

Title: **Cultural Resources Planning Document for the Oregon City-West Linn Pedestrian & Bicycle Bridge Concept Plan (Redacted Version)**, dated **12 May 2021**. This source is already registered as `S-063` for other Main Street building chronology work.

The relevant pages were visually inspected on 5 September 2026.

Printed page **15** states under **Other Historic Built Resources** that:

> The historic sites database at the State Historic Preservation Office was also consulted for built structures (Tables 1 and 2; Figures 25 and 26).

Printed page **16**, Table 2, visibly aligns:

| Address | Description | Build date | NR status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **503 Main St** | Kwality Café | c.1919 | Not Eligible |
| **505 Main St** | **505 Tavern** | **1940** | **Not Eligible** |
| **507-511 Main St** | Harding Building | 1968 | Not Eligible |
| 517-525 Main St | Klossen Building | 1931 | Not Eligible |
| 527 Main St | Swartz Building | 1935 | Not Eligible |

This does **not** make 1940 proven first construction of the 505 building. That date remains in conflict with the City's c.1920 synthesis, the 1925 Sanborn occupancy, aerial/building-continuity evidence, and the unresolved possibility that 1940 records a major remodel/expansion rather than whole-building erection.

The new provenance value is narrower and important: by 2021, a professional ODOT cultural-resources review was drawing the **505 Main / 505 Tavern / 1940 / Not Eligible** row from the **SHPO historic-sites database context**. Therefore a 505-specific SHPO record was not merely hypothetical; the record trail existed and was being consulted.

Because `S-116 / E-100` already preserves the 2009 505 form, this ODOT evidence is best treated as **visual corroboration and provenance**, not a new independent construction-date source.
