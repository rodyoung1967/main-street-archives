# 505 Main cumulative historic-inventory + 2009 RLS online retrieval pass

Date reviewed: 5 September 2026

Status: **CURRENT OFFICIAL ONLINE RETRIEVAL ROUTE + PRECISE CUSTODY GAP. NO NEW FARR/HARR OCCUPANCY DATE PROMOTED.**

Purpose: continue the narrow online provenance search behind Oregon City's official-secondary statement that **Harr's Recreation Center** was at **505 Main** in **1945**, without repeating completed broad Farr/Harr newspaper, assumed-business-name, aerial, or generic directory searches.

This pass strengthens the exact source package that now needs to be recovered. It does **not** establish a new Farr → Harr transition date.

## 1. Oregon City says historic inventory forms are cumulative

Current City Historic Resources page:

`https://www.orcity.org/1230/Historic-Resources`

Oregon City's current preservation guidance explains that SHPO inventory frameworks change over time and that a property can therefore have several inventory forms with different levels of detail. The City explicitly says the forms are **cumulative**: a newer form does not replace an older form; the forms should be read together to understand the building's evolution.

Research consequence for **505 Main**: the target is no longer just the 2000 Michelle L. Dennis form already identified in the Downtown Re-Survey custody statement. Request and inspect **every surviving 505 Main inventory-form version and attachment**, including older survey material that may have supplied the unexplained 1941 / 1945 / 1953 business chronology later summarized on the City's legacy 505 page.

Current City page gives the preservation contact as Christina Robertson-Gardiner, Preservation Planner, and states that forms can be updated or annotated when errors or additional information are documented.

## 2. Current Downtown Historic Resources page does not expose individual 505 forms

Current City page:

`https://www.orcity.org/1237/Downtown-Historic-Resources`

The current Downtown Historic Resources page exposes four broad related documents:

- Downtown Final Report — 2000;
- Oregon City Final Report;
- Oregon City Historic Photos;
- Oregon City Stats.

It does **not** presently expose an individual 505 Main property page or 505 survey-form attachment in the ordinary page navigation.

This is an **online presentation/retrieval gap**, not evidence that the City or SHPO lacks the 505 form. The 2000 Final Report independently states that copies of the individual inventory forms were available through the City and that originals were housed at SHPO.

## 3. 2009 Oregon Main Street RLS creates a second exact 505 record target

Official City report:

`https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/4026/Oregon-City-Final-Report-PDF`

The 2009 Oregon Main Street Reconnaissance Level Survey states that the survey boundaries included **all properties along Main Street from 5th through 18th Streets** and that **133 buildings** were inventoried. It directs readers seeking property-specific detail to the **Historic Building Report/Counts list included with the database information**.

The six-page final report itself does not expose a searchable 505 Main entry in the current text layer. Therefore the property-level target is the **underlying 2009 RLS database record / Historic Building Report data for 505 Main**, not merely the six-page report.

A screenshot request for the relevant report page returned a cache-miss error in this environment on 5 September 2026. This 2009-report statement is therefore preserved from the official PDF text layer and is **NOT newly scan-certified in this pass**.

## 4. 2011 Historic Review Program packet is a useful control, but not the missing 505 file

Oregon City Granicus packet:

`https://oregon-city.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?clip_id=655&meta_id=31497&view_id=6`

This 213-page City Historic Review Program / Historic Survey and Update packet states that:

- the **Historic Downtown was re-surveyed in 2000**;
- the **Main Street area was surveyed in 2009**;
- the 2011 update generally did not re-survey those areas, except for a specific Petzold Building update at 714 Main.

The packet includes a later citywide SHPO architectural-survey-data printout and aggregate Historic Building Report/Counts material. Targeted text searches did **not** recover a direct `505 Main`, `503 Main`, `501 Main` or `509 Main` property line from the packet. The isolated OCR numbers `505` on its final map/data pages cannot be treated as 505 Main evidence because street/address columns are flattened and detached.

Screenshot attempts for the relevant late packet pages (PDF pages 210–212) returned cache-miss errors. No visual interpretation of those flattened final-page number columns is promoted.

Research consequence: do **not** treat the 2011 packet as a substitute for the underlying 2009 505 record. It is a control confirming the distinct survey cycles and the existence of database-style survey output.

## 7. SHPO database route remains live but ordinary indexing did not expose 505

Oregon Historic Sites Database:

`https://heritagedata.prd.state.or.us/historic/`

SHPO's live search page says **City + Street Name** is the usual search and warns that building numbers/street names can change and corner or multi-tenant buildings can have more than one address.

Current web indexing readily exposes other Oregon City Main Street records, including 707–709 Main and 714 Main, with their survey groupings, scanned-document links and research fields. Repeated exact web-index searches for **505 Main / 503 Main / 501 Main / 507 Main / 509 Main** did not expose equivalent SHPO summary pages in this pass.

That result is a **search/indexing gap**, not an absence finding. The live database form itself should still be searched interactively by City = Oregon City and Street = Main, including address-range / alternate-address checks.

## 8. Why the 1945 Harr provenance is still unresolved

The current evidence remains:

- the City legacy 505 resource synthesis reports **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main in 1945**;
- the visually inspected 2000 Downtown Final Report's **selected** bibliography names the 1941 Oregon City and 1948 Clackamas County Pacific Directory Service volumes, but no specifically identified 1945 directory title;
- direct municipal evidence independently places Harr among Oregon City's pool-hall operators by **11 February 1948**;
- the late-1948 liquor-license list directly prints **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**.

This pass found no additional online source that directly reproduces the underlying 1945 Farr/Harr entry. No transition bracket is changed.
