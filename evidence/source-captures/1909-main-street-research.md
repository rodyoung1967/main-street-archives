## S-088 — Water Street Fifth–Sixth improvement, 1909

Type: Primary newspaper municipal notice + primary Oregon City Council record  
Related evidence: `E-074`

Canonical newspaper page:
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1909-03-12/ed-1/seq-7/

Official council record:
- https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/6375928/File/document

Observed claims:

- March notice calls for improvement of Water Street from Fifth to Sixth with grading, crushed rock/gravel, sidewalks, concrete curbs, crosswalks, corner blocks and drains.
- 8 September Council orders a fire hydrant on Water Street either at Sixth or at the Water Street end of the alley running through the block between Fifth and Sixth.
- 27 September the City Engineer reports contractor Harry Jones's Fifth–Sixth Water Street work satisfactory and according to contract; Council accepts the work and authorizes payment.
- 6 October Council records that the Water Street assessment notice had been published in the *Courier* on 27 August and 3 September.

Significance: direct public-works evidence on the river-side edge of the Fifth–Sixth block. It does not establish demolition/replacement of any Main Street target building.

## S-091 — Harding Building c.1909 chronology correction

Type: Primary newspaper retrospective + later direct location evidence  
Related evidence: `E-077`

Canonical pages:
- 26 September 1918, *Oregon City Courier*, p. 6: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-09-26/ed-1/seq-6/
- 10 March 1911, *Oregon City Courier*, p. 8: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-03-10/ed-1/seq-8/

Observed claims:

- 1918 retrospective says the Christian Science church was holding services in an office room in the **Harding building nine years earlier**, supporting existence around 1909.
- 1911 article says George Young formerly occupied the **Harding building on Main street between Fifth and Sixth streets** before moving one door north into the Willamette building.

Archive consequence: these sources support a Harding Building around 1909 and a Fifth–Sixth/Main context by 1911, but **do not support the prior exact statement that 507 Main was the Harding Building by 1909**.

## Additional same-block municipal context

The 1909 Council record also contains useful mapping context that is not assigned a separate evidence ID in this pass:

- 6 October: complaint about blockage of the **alley next to the Post Office between Main Street and Water Street**;
- 1 December: a new arc light designated on the east side of Water Street **opposite the Post Office alley**;
- 1 December: all new/remounted Main Street arc lights were to be placed on 60-foot poles.

These observations reinforce the Main-to-Water alley relationship but do not establish the Post Office's exact number.

## Citywide building-movement context — not target-block evidence

The 10 September 1909 *Courier* reports that the historic McLoughlin house had been moved from its former site to McLoughlin Park and restored. This demonstrates substantial building-moving/restoration activity in Oregon City in 1909, but it is **not evidence that a Fifth–Sixth/Main target building was moved** and is not assigned a target-block evidence ID.
