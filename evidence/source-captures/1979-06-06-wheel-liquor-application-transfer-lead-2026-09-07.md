# The Wheel — 6 June 1979 Oregon City liquor application control

Research date: **7 September 2026**  
Business: **The Wheel (`BUS-004`) — 503 Main Street only**

## Purpose

Preserve a newly recovered municipal control near the unresolved family-reported **Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham → Dick and Sheila Wiitanen** purchase of The Wheel business. This source is potentially valuable for bracketing that transaction, but it does **not** identify the applicant or state the application type.

## Primary municipal source

Oregon City Commission bound minutes, WebDrawer record **4746159**, bound page **195**.

Public PDF:

`https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4746159/File/document`

The bound volume prints the heading:

> Oregon City, Oregon, June 6, 1979 — REGULAR MEETING

Later in the same meeting, immediately after Ordinance No. 1929, the official searchable text records a motion by Commissioner Woolsey, seconded by Commissioner Andersen, **to approve the liquor license application for The Wheel**. The recorded vote was Cartales, Andersen, Woolsey, Allick and McKnight — ayes; none — nays.

### Date control

The date is direct, not inferred. The meeting heading on bound page 190 identifies **6 June 1979**, and Ordinance No. 1929 immediately preceding the Wheel motion independently states that its first reading occurred at the regular Commission meeting held on **6 June 1979**.

## What the source establishes

Classification: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT — OFFICIAL SEARCHABLE MUNICIPAL TEXT**.

The record directly establishes that on **6 June 1979** the Oregon City Commission approved a liquor-license application for **The Wheel**.

## What the source does not establish

The indexed/minute text does **not** state:

- the applicant or licensee name;
- whether the applicant was Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham;
- whether the applicant was Richard "Dick" Wiitanen, Sheila Wiitanen, or both;
- whether the application was a renewal, change of ownership, change of licensee, new outlet, or another application type;
- a sale date, purchase price, seller, purchaser, or business-sale terms;
- ownership of the 503 Main real estate.

Therefore this record must **not** be converted by itself into a claim that Dick and Sheila bought The Wheel on 6 June 1979.

## Relationship to controlling family chronology

The repository's controlling family chronology states that **Raye owned The Wheel business; Dick and Sheila later purchased The Wheel business from Raye; and only later did Dick and Sheila separately purchase the 503 Main building**. Richard M. Wiitanen is independently documented as owner of The Wheel business in August 1985 (`E-049`).

The 6 June 1979 liquor action therefore falls inside a high-value interval for the unresolved Raye → Wiitanen business transition. It is a **HIGH-VALUE TRANSFER / LICENSE LEAD**, not transfer proof.

## 1979 comparison control

A separate Oregon City Commission record, WebDrawer record **4723353**, shows that the **4 April 1979** regular meeting also approved liquor-license applications for **Edgewater Inn** and **Cedar Inn-Cedar Cellar** without stating in the minutes whether those applications were renewals, new outlets, ownership changes, or another type.

This comparison prevents over-reading the June Wheel wording: a stand-alone 1979 Commission approval does not itself reveal the transaction category.
