## E-121 — Exact 501 anchor

The 4 October 1907 Surber notice reads:

> Notice for Liquor License. Notice is hereby given that the undersigned will apply to the City Council of Oregon City, Oregon, for a renewal of his license as a retail liquor dealer, at the corner of Main and Fifth streets, his present place of business, No. 501, in Oregon City, Oregon, at the next meeting of the City Council in said city. A. J. SURBER.

The notice is direct evidence of **the address asserted by the applicant for his existing premises**. It is not the Council's grant, a deed, an opening-date record, a trade-name notice or a structural survey. The descriptive archive business name “A. J. Surber liquor premises” is not a claimed historic trade name. The 1908 Surber application gives the same intersection but **does not print 501**; continuation at 501 is plausible, not a second exact-number attestation.

This supersedes the earlier archive search-status statement that no exact 1907 target occupant had been recovered and the summary that Brightbill's August 1908 listing was the earliest registered target-number anchor. Brightbill's **503** finding remains valid. Surber does not prove that the later 501 building or the surviving 503/505 structures existed in their later form in 1907.

## E-122 — Fifth/Main does not by itself mean 501

The 13 December 1907 notice reads:

> NOTICE OF APPLICATION FOR A liquor license—Notice is hereby given that we will apply to the city council at the next regular meeting for a license to sell liquor at our place of business, corner of Main and Fifth streets, for a period of six months. JUSTIN & HITCHMAN.

On 18 September 1908, separate notices name **Justin & Hitchman** at Main/Fifth for **three months** and **A. J. Surber** at Main/Fifth for **six months**. They appear on the same page. This defeats a simple chronology inferred only from the filenames in which Surber disappeared and Justin & Hitchman took over. It does not prove two distinct rooms or corners either: common intersection wording is insufficient to establish the physical relationship or license arrangements. These applications are not proof that licenses were granted or operations continued throughout the requested terms.

## Deeper connections: useful, still unproved

1. **M. Justin and rear pool tables, 4 May 1910.** The existing repository capture `1910-fifth-main-pool-billiard-council-premises-example.md` reports a City Council Volume7 petition for a soft-drink concern and pool/billiard tables **back of his saloon at Fifth/Main**, read and tabled. Compare this with Justin & Hitchman, but no source here proves the Justin identity, exact numbered premises, granted permission or any 1922 survival. The municipal PDF was not newly inspected in this upload review; retain its prior review status rather than recertifying it.
2. **E. R. Case and an adjoining restaurant, 1 June 1910.** Existing E-080 places Case's saloon at Fifth/Main and describes an adjoining restaurant. It is an independent geometry/occupant lead, not a proved successor to either 1907–1908 applicant or the 1922 Fifth Street Restaurant.
3. **Strebig / Streibig.** Existing E-081 names **Pioneer Market / H. W. Streibig** at Fifth/Main in September 1910. The 1907 ad spells **Strebig** and gives Albright's old stand. Test the spelling/identity and business history with licenses/directories or transfer notices; do not silently expand the 1907 surname to H. W. Streibig or assume an unchanged corner footprint.
4. **327 Main and Hodes.** Stewart & Son (October 1907) and Carl G. Hodes (September 1908) are independent exact-address observations. E-076 later places Hodes at Sixth/Main in October 1909, with departure context in E-079 during 1910. A transfer at 327 and a move to Sixth/Main remain questions, not established events.
5. **Earlier 501 chain.** Surber 1907, Joe Wilson/Palace 1912 and Hunsaker & Taylor 1913 are separately named exact-address notices. Retrieve intervening license books and applications to identify dates/relationships. The present findings prove neither a common trade name nor business or property continuity.
