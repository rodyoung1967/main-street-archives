# 1916 online source-class closeout

Status: **ONLINE COMPLETE**

Closeout date: 13 September 2026

This record closes the defined online research pass for 1916 under `RESEARCH-STANDARD.md`. It does **not** claim manual work is complete, and it does not treat inaccessible sources or negative searches as evidence that a business, occupant, building, or event did not exist.

## Newspaper coverage

Relevant online Oregon City titles for 1916 were inventoried and reviewed page-by-page from the actual scans:

- *Oregon City Courier* (`sn00063698`): **52 issues / 458 pages / 458 visually verified**.
- *Oregon City Enterprise* (`sn00063700`): **52 issues / 426 archive slots / 425 genuine weekly pages visually verified**. The separate *Morning Enterprise* (`sn00063701`) has no 1916 issues in the University of Oregon digitized run; its surviving 1916 material is manual microfilm work (`MF-064`).

## Other online source classes reviewed

### Directories

The critical Sohns & Woodbeck, *Clackamas County & Oregon City Directory*, vol. 1 (1916–1917), is bibliographically confirmed. Public-online recovery routes were rechecked: FamilySearch catalog `1844954`, the Oak Lodge History Detectives excerpt, open-web mirrors, and Oregon Historical Society holding references. The Oak Lodge PDF is explicitly only Oak Grove/Jennings Lodge and does not contain the Oregon City section. The Oregon City pages remain restricted/physical-access work and are carried as `MF-062`.

The 1915–1916 R. L. Polk Oregon/Washington statewide gazetteer was also rechecked. Four FamilySearch image ARKs and a Spokane Public Library physical holding are known, but no unrestricted online route exposing the Oregon City pages was recovered. This is carried as `MF-063`.

### Maps

No 1916 Sanborn edition for Oregon City was identified. The relevant available sequence brackets 1916 with 1911 and 1925. The map class is therefore reviewed; there is no missing 1916 Sanborn image to retrieve. The absence of a 1916 edition is not premises evidence.

### Municipal records

Oregon City's current Meeting Document Archive explicitly exposes a decade query labeled **1910 to 1919 City Commission Agenda Packets and Minutes**. The public link resolves to an Oregon Records Management Solution / WebDrawer query for titles matching the 1910s Commission series. The City also states that the archive is under construction and that additional records continue to be added.

The decade link itself was recovered, but the offsite WebDrawer record query would not render through the research environment (`Cache miss`), and a direct network request from the container could not resolve the offsite host. The public historical-documents page separately exposes selected 1913 and 1914 Commission minutes but no general 1916 minute set.

Contemporary newspapers confirm that potentially relevant 1916 municipal actions exist—for example the 22 June *Courier* report of the Raasch & Lamb pool-hall temporary permit / proposed ordinance—but a newspaper report is not a substitute for the underlying Commission record. The remaining direct-minute / ordinance inspection is therefore carried as `MF-066`, with exact targets and portal route documented.

### Property / utility records

The official 1916–1917 Water Rent Record Book 4 was previously reviewed from the City scan via user-supplied screenshots. The target Main Street run visibly records **511, 509, 507, 503, 503½, 501 Main**, separately recognizes 503 and 503½, associates G. A. Harding, Agt. with target-block entries, and does not show a separate 505 Main account in the expected sequence. This is positive ledger evidence only and does not prove vacancy or non-use at 505.

The 1916 Clackamas County tax list is confirmed as a physical/manual source rather than an online image set and is carried as `MF-065`.

### Historic-resource records

Oregon City's downtown historic-resource pages and the official 2000 Downtown Final Report were reviewed online. The report's Appendix B was visually inspected in the source PDF: page 30 lists **503 Main — Kwality Cafe — c.1919** and **505 Main — Unknown — c.1920** under resources with irretrievable loss of integrity. Those later construction-date estimates do not identify a 1916 occupant and are not projected backward into 1916.

The City's historic-resource portal and Oregon historic-resource search routes produced no additional source-explicit 1916 503/505 occupant assignment in this closeout pass.

### Photographs

The City Historic Documents page exposes an online `Photographs.pdf` (72 pages). The PDF metadata/index was recovered, but page-image rendering repeatedly failed in the research environment with a cache-miss condition, and the direct file could not be downloaded through the available retrieval path. Search-engine indexing did not surface a securely dated 1916 Fifth/Main image. Because the known online source itself could not be visually inspected, the photograph source is not treated as a negative result; a user-assisted/direct-browser visual check is carried as `MF-067`.

## Target-category reconciliation

Verified facts were propagated to the appropriate source captures and registers; unresolved identity and occupancy questions remain unresolved rather than inferred.
