## Research significance

The issue is a negative target result but adds a useful control for the unresolved Raasch question. A. F. Raasch is explicitly named in a gambling-raid account, while the same issue independently carries the unnumbered **Raasch & Lamb — Pool and Billiards** directory entry. The shared surname is not enough to merge the person with the business or to assign the business to 503/505. Preserve the distinction until a direct identity/address source is recovered.

The issue also continues the stable late-1916 address controls at 612 Main, 619 Main, and Fifth/Main.
