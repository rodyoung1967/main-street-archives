# 2002 OLCC license/compliance microfiche-files refinement

Date reviewed: 5 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL OAR PDF TEXT — LICENSE/COMPLIANCE MICROFICHE CONFIRMED BY 2002; ORIGINAL 1940s FILE SURVIVAL UNRESOLVED.**

Purpose: refine the newly documented OLCC legacy-record route for the Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center transition at 505 Main Street by confirming that OLCC's microfiche system specifically covered **license and compliance records for licensed businesses**, not merely unspecified Commissioner files.

## Source

Oregon Administrative Rules Compilation, **2002 Edition**, Chapter 845, Oregon Liquor Control Commission:

`https://records.sos.state.or.us/ORSOSWebDrawer/Record/8136539/File/document`

The PDF identifies itself at the relevant rule as **2002 Edition**, with compilation date notation **11-15-01**.

Rule **845-004-0020, Fees for Certain Services**, PDF page index 8 / printed page 15-453, states that the Commission offered lists of license or compliance actions, including:

- weekly applications-received lists;
- monthly compliance-action ratification lists;
- monthly staff and Commission action lists covering licensing and permit actions;
- complete licensee lists;
- licensees by license type;
- licensees by county or city; and
- licensees by license type and county or city.

More importantly for archival survival, section (4) describes **license and compliance records for licensed businesses** and says the Commission could make **microfiche copies of records** available under stated circumstances. The fee schedule expressly provides for **file review of paper copies or microfiche files**.

This is stronger than a generic reference to agency microfiche because the text directly places microfiche within the Commission's license/compliance-file access system.

## Rule-history boundary

The rule-history block lists amendments beginning with **LCC 11-1980**, effective 1 April 1980, and later amendments in 1980, 1986, 1990, 1991, 2000 and 2001.

Do **not** infer that the microfiche wording itself existed in the original 1980 version merely because the current rule's lineage begins then. The present finding establishes the wording by the **2002 compilation**, not the date when the Commission first converted licensing records to microfiche.

## Research consequence

When OLCC or Oregon State Archives staff are contacted, specifically ask whether any retired **license/compliance microfiche files**, fiche masters, card indexes, conversion-image sets or predecessor file systems survive for:

- Edwin F. Farr / Edwin Franklin Farr / E. F. Farr;
- Farr's Pool Hall;
- Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center;
- 505 Main Street / 505½ Main Street, Oregon City;
- 1934–1948.

Also ask whether historical weekly applications-received lists or monthly licensing/permit-action lists survive in paper, fiche, microfilm or transferred archival series.

A staff statement that modern active files no longer reach this period must not be treated as evidence of non-licensure; ask for disposition/transfer/microform information.
