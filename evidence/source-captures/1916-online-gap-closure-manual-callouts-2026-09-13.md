## 1. Oregon City Enterprise — 12 May 1916 genuine weekly page 8

The University of Oregon archive delivers eight sequences for the 12 May 1916 issue. Sequences 1–7 visually belong to the weekly *Oregon City Enterprise*. Delivered sequence 8 is visibly headed *Morning Enterprise*, so it cannot certify the genuine weekly page 8.

Prior recovery work tested bounded alternate sequence/title/edition routes and did not recover a genuine weekly replacement. The rest of the 1916 online newspaper universe is now visually reviewed: Courier 458/458 and Enterprise 425/426, with this one page the sole remaining newspaper retrieval gap.

**Disposition:** convert the unresolved page to **MF-061 — YEAR-CLOSEOUT / MANUAL CALLOUT**. Seek the genuine weekly *Oregon City Enterprise*, 12 May 1916, page 8 through University of Oregon source-film/derivative review or another institutional microfilm holding. ## 2. Sohns & Woodbeck, Clackamas County & Oregon City Directory, 1916–1917

The volume is bibliographically secure and repeatedly cited by official historic-resource work. Public-online checks confirm:

- FamilySearch catalog **1844954** lists the 1916 Oregon City directory, but the current public route does not expose usable Oregon City page images in this research environment.
- Oregon Historical Society is explicitly cited by National Register documentation as holding the 1916–1917 Sohns & Woodbeck directory.
- Oak Lodge History Detectives' public 1916–1917 PDF is explicitly limited to the **Oak Grove and Jennings Lodge** portions and is therefore not an Oregon City substitute.
- Current open-web searching recovered citations and secondary uses of the directory but no complete publicly viewable Oregon City section or mirror.

**Disposition:** reasonable public-online recovery is exhausted. Convert to **MF-062 — YEAR-CLOSEOUT / MANUAL CALLOUT**. Retrieve the Oregon City pages from FamilySearch Center/affiliate access or Oregon Historical Society. Visually inspect the address/business material for **501, 503, 503½, 505, 507, 509, and 511 Main**, plus relevant names/businesses including Hylton, Bradley, Brightbill, Harding, cigar/pool/billiard/soft-drink and restaurant entries. If a reverse/street directory exists, transcribe the 500 block of Main. Do not promote an occupant from later quotations without viewing the directory page itself.
