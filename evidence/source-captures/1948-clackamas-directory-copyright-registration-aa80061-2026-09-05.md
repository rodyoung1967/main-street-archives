## 1. Federal Catalog of Copyright Entries hit

The U.S. Copyright Office's **Catalog of Copyright Entries, Third Series, Part 1B: Pamphlets, Serials and Contributions to Periodicals, January–December 1948, Volume 2** is publicly digitized. The Internet Archive text layer exposes the following entry in the alphabetical `CLACK...` sequence:

> **CLACKAMAS COUNTY [OR.] DIRECTORY, 1947-1948. 28 p. © Pacific Directory Service, Portland, Or.; QMarlh8; AA80061.**

Discovery text URL:

`https://archive.org/stream/catalogofcopyr321libr/catalogofcopyr321libr_djvu.txt`

The exact entry is a **federal registration-index control**. It independently establishes from the Copyright Office catalog text:

- title: **Clackamas County [Or.] Directory, 1947-1948**;
- extent: **28 pages**;
- claimant / credited copyright party: **Pacific Directory Service, Portland, Oregon**;
- registration number: **AA80061**.

The OCR token between the claimant and registration number appears as **`QMarlh8`**. It is visibly corrupt OCR and must **not** be silently expanded into a March 1948 date or any specific day until the catalog page or corresponding registration card is visually inspected.

## 2. What this improves over the existing bibliography trail

The repository already had strong cross-source evidence that the volume called **1947–48** and the Oregon City bibliography's **1948 Directory of Clackamas County** are the same Pacific Directory Service county directory. This federal record adds a source-independent identifier that those local bibliographies did not provide:

**Copyright registration AA80061.**

That identifier materially improves retrieval because it can be used to distinguish this directory from later R. L. Polk Oregon City directories and from other Clackamas-area telephone/business directories with similar titles.

The federal title also directly supports the **1947-1948** date form used by CCHS and Lake Oswego historic-resource research. It does not by itself establish when the directory's individual listings were canvassed; the separate August–September 1946 Pacific Directory Service compilation notices remain the controlling timing caution.

## 3. Current official Copyright Office retrieval route

The U.S. Copyright Office's current **Historical Public Records Program** states that pre-1978 records are preserved through the Copyright Card Catalog, Catalog of Copyright Entries, record books and microfilm. Its current **Virtual Card Catalog** includes a dedicated **1946–1954** registration index.

Official routes reviewed 5 September 2026:

- Historical Public Records Program: `https://www.copyright.gov/historic-records/`
- Virtual Card Catalog: `https://copyright.gov/vcc/`
- Card Browser: `https://vcc.copyright.gov/browse`
- Card guide: `https://www.copyright.gov/vcc/card-guide.html`
- Copyright online-record resources: `https://copyright.gov/records/ccrr.html`

The Copyright Office explains that catalog cards typically preserve core registration facts such as title, claimant, registration number and effective date. For the 1946–1954 index, the card images are digitally browsable but are not among the later indexes that can be reliably filtered by OCR card text.
