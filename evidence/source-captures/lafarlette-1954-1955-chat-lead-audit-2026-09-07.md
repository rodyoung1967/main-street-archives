## 29 April 1954 — Floyd LaFarlette address lead

Source: *Coquille Valley Sentinel*, 29 April 1954, p. 2, “Here Are The New Addresses of Coquille’s Residents — Save This List.”  
Archive URL: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088222/1954-04-29/ed-1/seq-2.pdf

Archive text identifies:

- **Floyd LaFarlette — 361 E. 7th**

This is a useful residential chronology control shortly after the visually preserved 11 March 1954 Raye profile (`S-271` / `E-245`). It does **not** prove business ownership, liquor-license ownership, or Town Club involvement.

## 29 December 1955 — Floyd and wife chaperone teen dance

Source: *Coquille Valley Sentinel*, 29 December 1955, image/page 6.  
Archive URL: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088222/1955-12-29/ed-1/seq-6.pdf

Archive text says that a group of teenagers came to dance and that the chaperones included **Mr. and Mrs. Floyd LaFarlette** (along with Mrs. Howard Marsh).

This supports continued Coquille-area social presence of Floyd and his wife in late 1955. It does **not** identify the venue as the Town Club and does not establish any ownership, operation, or liquor-license relationship.
