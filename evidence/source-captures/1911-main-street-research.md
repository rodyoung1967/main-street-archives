# 1911 Main Street Research — Consolidated Source Capture

Year status: **IN PROGRESS**
Research scope: Oregon City Main Street, especially the Fifth–Sixth block and later-numbered 501 / 503 / 505 / 507 Main.  
Method: targeted address/business/construction searches plus issue/page-level review of accessible/indexed *Oregon City Courier* material, with cross-checks in the *Morning Enterprise* and municipal/public-works reporting.  
Standing construction watch: `RL-024`.

## High-confidence 1911 findings

### Chicago Store relocates from 405 to 505 Main

Primary *Courier* advertisements establish:

- 27 January 1911, p. 6 — **The Chicago Store, Wolf & Nat, Proprietors, 405 Main Street**. The ad also says Gents' Furnishers and Tailors.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-01-27/ed-1/seq-6/
- 3 February 1911, p. 6 — same store/proprietors at **405 Main Street**, with **Electric Hotel Building** printed in the ad.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-02-03/ed-1/seq-6/
- 31 March 1911, p. 6 — The Chicago Store now advertises at **505 Main St.**; new/second-hand clothing, second-hand purchasing, cleaning and repairing.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-03-31/ed-1/seq-6/
- 7 April 1911, p. 6 — 505 Main repeated.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-04-07/ed-1/seq-6/
- 14 April 1911, p. 7 — 505 Main repeated.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-04-14/ed-1/seq-7/
- 28 April 1911, p. 7 — 505 Main repeated.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-04-28/ed-1/seq-7/

Research conclusion: Chicago Store moved/readdressed from 405 to 505 **after 3 February and by 31 March 1911** (`E-084`, `S-097`). Wolf & Nat are directly named as proprietors at 405; the available 505 advertisement text does not directly name proprietors, so proprietorship continuity is not assumed.

### Harding Building descriptions and Willamette adjacency

- 24 February 1911, p. 5 — O. G. Elliott and Mort Parks opened a general repair shop in the **Harding building, on 6th street between Main street and Railroad avenue**.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-02-24/ed-1/seq-5/
- 10 March 1911, p. 8 — George Young moved his second-hand store **one door north into the Willamette building**; the article says he formerly occupied the **Harding building on Main street between Fifth and Sixth streets**.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-03-10/ed-1/seq-8/

Research conclusion: contemporary 1911 wording associates the Harding Building with both Sixth Street and Main Street contexts and proves that George Young's new Willamette Building premises were one door north of his former Harding premises. Exact number/lot/footprint is unresolved (`E-085`, `S-098`, `RL-029`, `RL-035`). Do not force Harding = 507.

Later clue retained only for follow-up: a 5 January 1912 *Courier* page places **G. H. Young at 507 Main**. That may help test whether the Willamette Building was 507 and the prior Harding premises a neighboring storefront, but it is not backdated to 1911 without continuity evidence.

### New Gambrinus block occupied by March 1911

- 3 March 1911, p. 5 — J. Tobin, proprietor of the Electric Hotel, rented the **second story of the new Gambrinus block** as a hotel annex. The block is described as having steam heat, electric lights, and some rooms with hot/cold water connections.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-03-03/ed-1/seq-5/

Research conclusion: the new Gambrinus block under construction in summer 1910 (`E-078`) was occupied/in service by 3 March 1911 (`E-086`, `S-099`). The exact William Tell demolition/replacement mechanics remain unresolved (`RL-031`).

### Sixth Street grade/improvement activity

- 3 March 1911, p. 1 — Council reporting records disagreement over the **improvement of Sixth Street** and a proposed grade change intended to eliminate a break in the middle of a block; city engineer/street committee favored the change and the engineer's report was accepted.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-03-03/ed-1/seq-1/
- 24 March 1911, p. 8 — additional Sixth Street grade discussion; a petition concerning the block between Madison and Jefferson was acted upon.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-03-24/ed-1/seq-8/
- 21 July 1911, p. 8 — Council considered property-owner assessments for the improvement of Sixth Street.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-07-21/ed-1/seq-8/
- 6 October 1911, p. 1 — assessment ordinance for improvement of **Sixth Street from High to Jackson**.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-10-06/ed-1/seq-1/

Research conclusion: Sixth Street underwent a documented 1911 grade/improvement process crossing Main at the north end of the target block (`E-087`, `S-100`). No target-building demolition is established.

### Pacific Telephone continues at 510 Main

- 6 January 1911, p. 2 — Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. advertisement gives **510 Main St., Oregon City**.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-01-06/ed-1/seq-2/

Research conclusion: exact 510 Main occupancy documented in late 1910 continues into January 1911 (`E-088`, `S-101`). This strengthens, but does not prove, physical continuity with the 1907 central-office plant.

### George Young later in 1911

An April 1911 *Morning Enterprise* page advertises George Young's new furniture/curio store but does not print a numbered address in the recovered text. This is consistent with the March move but does not independently resolve the Willamette Building number.

## Courier page-review coverage limitation

The annual *Courier* issue/page-review protocol was applied alongside targeted search. Indexed/retrievable pages surfaced through the current Historic Oregon Newspapers research interface were reviewed for target addresses, ads, moves, construction, fire, street work, and relative-location clues. The page-level pass materially helped in 1911 by exposing the Chicago Store relocation sequence, the Harding/Willamette location wording, and the Sixth Street grade work.

Some 1911 issue/page images are not reliably exposed or retrievable through this research interface. Those pages are **not** counted as visually reviewed. Completing a physical image-by-image checklist through Historic Oregon Newspapers/Oregon Digital, library microfilm, or another full-page viewer is retained as `RL-034`.
