# 1921 Oregon City Enterprise second-pass correction — Dr. Freeze at 505½ Main

Date: 20 September 2026  
Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED — DIRECT 500-BLOCK HIT**

## Why this capture exists

A targeted search performed while beginning the 1921 regional-newspaper expansion exposed a material defect in the first-pass Enterprise annual-audit conclusion. The prior statement that the 52-issue / 416-page visual review yielded no item securely attributable to the 500 block is **incorrect**.

The second-pass search found recurring Dr. Freeze advertisements explicitly giving **505½ Main St., Oregon City**. The original page scans were opened and visually inspected; this is not an OCR-only promotion.

## Visually verified examples

### 26 August 1921 — Oregon City Enterprise, p. 3

Bottom-right display advertisement reads:

> Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist  
> 505½ Main St. Oregon City  
> Opposite Post Office  
> Lenses ground while you wait.

Original page: Historic Oregon Newspapers, Oregon City Enterprise, 26 Aug. 1921, p. 3 / image 3.

Classification: **direct address/business-operation evidence**. It securely places Dr. Freeze's eye-specialist office at 505½ Main on this publication date.

### 11 November 1921 — Oregon City Enterprise, p. 5

Bottom-right display advertisement visually reads **Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist — 505½ Main St., Oregon City — Opposite Postoffice** (with service copy beneath).

Original page: Historic Oregon Newspapers, Oregon City Enterprise, 11 Nov. 1921, p. 5 / image 5.

Classification: **direct address/business-operation evidence**.

### 30 December 1921 — Oregon City Enterprise, p. 3

Bottom-right display advertisement visually reads:

> Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist  
> 505½ Main St. Oregon City  
> Opposite Postoffice  
> Lenses ground while you wait.

Original page: Historic Oregon Newspapers, Oregon City Enterprise, 30 Dec. 1921, p. 3 / image 3.

Classification: **direct address/business-operation evidence**.

## OCR warning

The page OCR variously renders the address as forms such as “50514”, “5054” or “5052”. Visual inspection of the original advertisements resolves the printed address as **505½ Main St.** This is exactly why OCR is discovery-only under project policy.

## Consequence for the annual audit

The earlier “zero securely attributable 500-block hits” characterization must not be retained as an annual finding. The first-pass page review was complete in page count but **not reliable enough in target extraction**, because small display advertisements containing a direct target address were missed.

This materially strengthens the steward's decision to return 1921 to **IN PROGRESS**. A targeted second pass of all 416 Enterprise pages is now required, in addition to recovery/review of Banner-Courier and Morning Enterprise.

The second pass should explicitly inspect small advertisements and hunt address/name variants, including 501/503/505/505½, Fifth/Main, Freeze, Leland, Dollar, Miller, Wolf, Pollock, Steiner, and related target businesses/occupants.

## Historical limits

These advertisements establish Dr. Freeze's **business/office presence at 505½ Main** on the verified publication dates. They do not by themselves establish property ownership, tenancy terms, building construction date, or uninterrupted occupancy between dates.
