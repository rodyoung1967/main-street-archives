## W. S. Eddy and Fashion Stable

The professional notice on p7 of [October 8](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-08/ed-1/seq-7.pdf), [October 15](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-15/ed-1/seq-7.pdf), [October 22](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-22/ed-1/seq-7.pdf) and [October 29](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-29/ed-1/seq-7.pdf) identifies **W. S. Eddy, V.S., M.D.V.** at Fashion Stable, **“between Fourth and Fifth on Main Street.”** The notice is not present in the October 1 p7 directory. **October 8 is the earliest verified date for that location in the archive.** The April 23 and June 11 evidence (`E-112`, `E-114`) had placed Eddy at Fashion Stable on Fifth between Main and Water. The October wording advances the previously documented April 1917 location by about 18 months in the business profile and precedes the independently verified September 1916 ads (`E-324` / `S-351`) by nearly 11 months.

The names and credentials strongly bridge the 1915 notices to the 1917 sequence. The observations establish the advertised relative location on each date. The actual change date, whether there was a move or a different office/stable arrangement, street number, title and tenant status remain unresolved. Fourth to Fifth is south of the project frontage. The October notice supplies no basis to assign 110 Fifth, 427 Main or any 500-block storefront, or to conflate W. S. Eddy with W. B. Eddy or the unresolved January A. J. Eddy transcription.

## Public works and neighboring controls

- [October 8 p3](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-08/ed-1/seq-3.pdf) reports that the council ordered Main Street assessment corrections and the remaining balances for improvements on Main, Seventh and Tenth paid. The report also describes a conditional arrangement for a flusher for the new Main Street pavement. This independently corroborates the October 7 Courier on the corrections and payments.
- [October 15 p1](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-15/ed-1/seq-1.pdf) and [5](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-15/ed-1/seq-5.pdf) locate a disputed triangle at the head of Main by the basin. The paper reports that Hawley Pulp & Paper used it under a lease from the railway, that the railway filed suit, and that the city answered claiming street rights. These reports record competing claims and distinguish the location from the 500-block parcels.
- [October 29 p5](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-10-29/ed-1/seq-5.pdf) says Standard Paving, identified as the former Worswick concern, intended to leave its plant on the riverbank over the winter and expected possible spring contracts. It names Main, Third, Seventh, Tenth and John Adams as streets the firm improved during the summer. Formal acceptance is separately reported by the November 4 Courier (`E-109`).
- The October 1 and 15 Block 4 transfers explicitly name Mountain View and Pleasant Place additions, respectively. Neither is original Oregon City Block 4. The Grand Theatre notices corroborate weekly film activity and supply no new street number.
