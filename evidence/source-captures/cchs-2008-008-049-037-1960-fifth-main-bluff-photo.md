## Visual review

The photograph is a high elevated/bluff view centered on **Fifth and Main** with the Willamette River and West Linn beyond. The Fifth–Sixth commercial frontage is clearly visible in its **1960 pre-September-1967-fire state**.

The frame is useful as a structural/street-context control because it preserves:

- the Main/Fifth intersection and roadway geometry;
- the low-rise commercial frontage in the 503/505 area;
- the larger pre-fire commercial block immediately north;
- rooflines, party-wall relationships, rear/river-side massing, sidewalks, utility poles, and adjoining street context;
- an exact institutional date of **1960**, providing a tighter visual control than the broader 1955–1965 range attached to `IMG-0753`.

### Interpretation limit

The supplied 2048-pixel scan is adequate for building massing and block context, but several storefront signs are too soft for reliable transcription. **Do not create a new Wheel/505 Tavern trade-name claim from this photograph alone.** Use existing independent business/address evidence for those names and locations. The photograph may be compared with `IMG-0753` / `S-232` / `E-206`, which already supplies a separate same-era bluff-view sign control.
