Date reviewed: **7 September 2026**

Status: **PRIMARY MUNICIPAL INDEXED TEXT / PROCEDURAL COVERAGE CONTROL; NO 505 OCCUPANCY OR OPERATOR CLAIM; PAGE IMAGE RENDERING FAILED IN THIS PASS.**

Purpose: preserve direct Oregon City Commission evidence that an exact-address liquor-license application could be approved in a small in-year action outside the large annual/December renewal batches. This matters methodologically to the unresolved **Harr's Recreation Center → Well's Recreation** transition at **505 Main Street** because omission from a single annual renewal batch cannot be treated as proof of non-operation, non-licensing, or operator departure.

## Standalone exact-address liquor-license approval

Near the end of the 10 October meeting, the primary municipal text states:

**“Upon motion the following application for a liquor license was approved:”**

followed by:

**Seid's Restaurant, 517 Main Street, Oregon City.**

The action appears on printed municipal page **240** in the recovered text sequence.

## Why this matters for the 505 search

This record does **not** identify an occupant, owner, licensee, or trade name at 505 Main, and it creates no connection between Seid's Restaurant and Harr's or Well's Recreation.

It does, however, directly establish the licensing procedure relevant to the active 505 problem: Oregon City could approve a single exact-address liquor-license application during an ordinary in-year Commission meeting rather than only in a large year-end renewal batch.

That strengthens the project's existing evidence rule for the 505 chronology:

- the absence of **505 / Harr / Well's** from the recovered **2 December 1953** 1954-renewal batch is only a bounded no-hit for that batch;
- supplementary approvals, new applications, transfers, or substitutions elsewhere in the year remain live possibilities;
- the highest-value municipal search remains small-batch and in-year liquor actions between the late-1948 Carl R. Harr anchor and the 1953 directory's official-secondary **Well's Recreation** point.
