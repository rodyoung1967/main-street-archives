## 505 Main interpretation

Neither **505 Main**, **Well's Recreation / Wells Recreation**, nor **Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center** appears in this particular indexed renewal batch.

That omission is **not negative evidence** that the 505 business lacked a liquor license, had closed, or was no longer operating. The project already has direct municipal evidence that Oregon City liquor applications and renewals could be handled in separate, small in-year batches rather than one exhaustive annual list. Therefore this record establishes only that **505 is not named in the recovered 2 December 1953 batch**.

This meeting is nevertheless valuable because it is a substantial comparison universe immediately adjacent to the 1953 Salisbury directory year. Several businesses that also appear in the late-1948 municipal liquor list remain represented, and some 1953 entries include changed operator names in parentheses, confirming that this source class can expose operator succession when the relevant application is recovered.
