# 1921–1922 R. L. Polk Oregon City directory — user-assisted visual review

Status: **VISUALLY REVIEWED FROM USER-SUPPLIED FAMILYSEARCH IMAGES**

Date reviewed: 14 September 2026

Source: R. L. Polk & Co., *Oregon and Washington State Gazetteer and Business Directory, 1921–1922*, FamilySearch image viewer. Images were opened and photographed by Rodney Young because the research environment could not render the FamilySearch image set directly.

## Scope reviewed

User-supplied images visually established the following portions of the directory:

1. Oregon City general/gazetteer listing.
2. Classified **Restaurants** section for Oregon City.
3. Classified **Billiard Rooms** section for Oregon City.
4. Classified **Cigars and Tobacco—Retail** section for Oregon City, including the continuation check; user reports no additional Oregon City cigar entries on the next page.

## Direct visual findings

### Oregon City general listing

The Oregon City section was visually located and reviewed in user images. The visible alphabetical run includes entries through the relevant L/T ranges. No direct general-listing entry for `Leland & Little`, `Walter Little`, or `Timms Pool Hall` was identified in the supplied images. This absence is **not evidence of non-operation**, because the same general directory also omits businesses independently documented in 1922 newspapers, including Fifth Street Restaurant.

### Restaurants — Oregon City

The classified Restaurants section visually lists these Oregon City entries:

- **Cattau, Chris**
- **Newton & Wyrick**
- **Pollock, W. W.**
- **Crescent Cafe**

`Fifth Street Restaurant` is not listed under that trade name in the visually reviewed Oregon City restaurant block.

Interpretive control: the omission of Fifth Street Restaurant, despite its independent July 1922 documentation, shows that the 1921–22 Polk volume must not be treated as a complete snapshot of all Oregon City businesses operating throughout calendar 1922. The directory may reflect an earlier canvass date and/or selective coverage.

### Billiard Rooms — Oregon City

The classified **BILLIARD ROOMS** section visually lists the following Oregon City entries:

- **Dollar Clem**
- **Huerth & Sondergaard**
- **Leland A H**
- **Moulding Bros**
- **Trusdall & Walker**

This is a significant new independent source for **A. H. Leland as an Oregon City billiard-room operator**.

Cross-source significance:

- June 1920 contemporary reporting already places **A. Leland** operating a pool hall at **505½ Main Street**.
- Polk 1921–22 independently classifies **A. H. Leland** under Oregon City **Billiard Rooms**.
- This materially strengthens the hypothesis that the 1920 A. Leland pool-hall operator and the Polk A. H. Leland billiard-room operator are the same person, while not by itself proving identity or exact premises.
- Preserve distinction between **A. H. Leland** and the separately reported **O. H. Leland** in June 1922; do not merge first initials without same-context identity evidence.

The same Polk category also independently corroborates **Clem Dollar** as an Oregon City billiard-room operator, consistent with the February 1920 newspaper report identifying Clem Dollar as proprietor of a pool hall on Main between Fifth and Sixth Streets.

### Cigars and Tobacco—Retail — Oregon City

The visually reviewed Oregon City cigar/tobacco entries are:

- **Schinkel Fred**
- **Otterson Sam**

No `Leland`, `Little`, or `Leland & Little` entry is visible in the Oregon City cigar/tobacco block, and Rodney Young reports that the next page contains no additional Oregon City continuation entries.

Again, this is a bounded classified-section no-hit only. It must not override the independently verified February 1922 newspaper evidence listing **Leland & Little** among Oregon City cigar dealers.

## Revised chronology / interpretation

The combined evidence now supports this sequence without promoting unproven succession:

- **Feb. 1920:** Clem Dollar — pool-hall proprietor on Main between Fifth and Sixth (newspaper).
- **Jun. 1920:** A. Leland — pool hall at **505½ Main** (newspaper/municipal-reporting lead; underlying municipal file still MF-041).
- **1921–22 Polk:** **A. H. Leland** — Oregon City, classified under **Billiard Rooms**.
- **Feb. 1922:** Leland & Little — Oregon City cigar dealer (newspaper list).
- **Jun. 1922:** O. H. Leland, Walter Little, and Timms Pool Hall appear separately in an Oregon City business list.
- **Aug. 1922 and later 1922:** Leland & Little — **Billiards**.

The strongest current hypothesis remains:

`A. Leland / 505½ pool hall (1920) → ? → Leland & Little, cigars/billiards (1922) → ? → 505 Main billiards/cigars (1925)`

This remains a **research hypothesis, not documented succession**.

## Research implications

The Polk route is no longer an unresolved access-only gap for the key classified categories. User-assisted FamilySearch access recovered the most relevant Oregon City sections and produced a positive Leland hit.

The source does **not** provide an exact address for A. H. Leland in the classified billiard-room listing, so it does not independently prove `A. H. Leland = 505½ Main`. The exact-address bridge still depends most strongly on:

- **MF-041** — June 2, 1920 Council minutes and related A. Leland / 505½ pool-room file;
- **MF-013** — complete 1922 poolroom applications, notices, bonds, license records and Council actions;
- any surviving address-bearing directory/telephone/license source not yet recovered.

## Evidence handling

- Treat the user-supplied images as visual review evidence.
- Do not infer absence from the general or classified Polk listings as proof a business was not operating.
- Do not merge A. H. Leland with O. H. Leland without direct identity evidence.
- Do not claim the Polk volume supplies a 505/505½ address for A. H. Leland; it supplies only city + business classification in the reviewed entry.
