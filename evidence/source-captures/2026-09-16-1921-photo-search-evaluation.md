# 1921 / immediate pre-fire Oregon City photograph search — evaluated leads

Date: 16 September 2026  
Status: **ONLINE PHOTO LEADS EVALUATED; no new direct 501/503/505 pre-fire photograph established**

Purpose: preserve the current search for photographs of Main Street, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, or bluff views close to 1921, especially anything capable of showing the 501/503/505 frontage before the July 1922 fire.

## CCFHS PC050 — dated 21 April 1921 postcard

Clackamas County Family History Society's public postcard gallery identifies:

**PC050 — “Section of Oregon City from the Bridge, Mt. Hood in distance. Dated April 21, 1921. 1888 suspension bridge.”**

Gallery:
https://www.clackamascountyfamilyhistory.org/photo-gallery

Direct public image:
https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/690a3ed512f2ba14fd7549b6/9f18a9c7-848d-4f5d-addf-2fef1edbec87/Scan_20260102.png

### Visual evaluation

The postcard was directly viewed online. It looks from/near the bridge toward the Oregon City hillside and clearly preserves the old suspension-bridge-era setting. It is useful as an **exactly dated postcard/use control** in the immediate pre-fire period.

However:
- the later 501/503/505 frontage is **not shown clearly enough for a defensible individual-building comparison** in the available derivative;
- the gallery wording says “Dated April 21, 1921,” but does not on the public text establish whether that is the photographic exposure date, postcard inscription date, mailing/use date, or another catalog dating convention;
- therefore **do not silently promote 21 April 1921 to an exact exposure date** without the reverse/catalog record or other provenance.

Evidence state: **DOCUMENTED external catalog/postcard date wording + VISUALLY REVIEWED image; target-frontage NO-HIT / insufficient view**.

## Interim pedestrian bridge photograph — 1921 / c.1922 date conflict

West Linn Historical Society publishes a photograph captioned:

**“Interim pedestrian bridge during construction of Oregon City/West Linn Bridge, 1921.”**

Source:
https://www.westlinnhistory.org/history/bridges

The same page states that construction of the present bridge began **29 July 1921** and was completed in **December 1922**.

Old Oregon Photos publishes what appears to be the same or a closely related temporary pedestrian-bridge exposure under the title:

**“Temporary Pedestrian Bridge, Oregon City - c. 1922”**

Source:
https://www.oldoregonphotos.com/temporary-pedestrian-bridge-oregon-city-c-1922.html

### Visual / dating evaluation

The publicly accessible Old Oregon derivative was visually reviewed. The photograph is valuable for the temporary-bridge construction sequence and Oregon City background, but it does **not** presently supply a secure readable 501/503/505 frontage.

The date conflict must be preserved:
- West Linn Historical Society: **1921**
- Old Oregon Photos: **c.1922**

Until original-negative metadata or another dated publication resolves the exposure, treat the image as **bridge-construction-era 1921–1922**, not an exact 1921 target-frontage photograph.

## OHS 376G0143 / 376G0144 — old suspension bridge negatives

Oregon Historical Society catalogs two Oregon Journal negatives:

- **376G0143 — “1888 Oregon City suspension bridge?” — 1902–1922**
- **376G0144 — “1888 Oregon City suspension bridge?” — 1902–1922**

OHS description: each is taken from one end of the wooden suspension bridge, probably the 1888 Oregon City/West Linn bridge; the negative sleeve says **“Bridges - Clackamas County.”**

Public OHS browse/record:
https://digitalcollections.ohs.org/bridges-oregon-oregon-city
https://digitalcollections.ohs.org/1888-oregon-city-suspension-bridge

These are worth preserving as potential pre-1922 visual controls, but their institutional range **1902–1922 is too broad to call them 1921**, and the currently exposed online derivatives/descriptions do not establish a usable target-frontage view.

## OHS 379G0008 related sequence

OHS `379G0008`, **“Unidentified man on bluff overlooking Oregon City, Oregon,”** is institutionally dated **1910–1924** and points to related negatives `379G0002`–`0012` and `379G0017`.

The currently indexed related items include McLoughlin House / Willamette Falls portrait scenes and the bluff portrait. This remains a possible background-cityscape route, but the broad range does **not** establish 1921 and no reviewed catalog description yet proves a readable 501/503/505 frontage.

## Search conclusion

At this checkpoint, the search has produced **real 1921-era visual material**, but **no newly found photograph can yet be promoted to a direct 1921 image of the 501/503/505 frontage**.

Best controls remain:
- exact/near-exact **postcard use dating** for PC050, with exposure-date caution;
- **1921–1922 bridge-construction** imagery, with conflicting 1921 vs c.1922 labeling on the temporary-bridge exposure;
- broad OHS pre-1922 negatives that need image-level inspection;
- the already preserved **1918 CCHS bluff photograph** as the strongest dated pre-fire structural control currently in the repository.
