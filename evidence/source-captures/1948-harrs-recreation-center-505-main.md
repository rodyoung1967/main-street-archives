# Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main — 1948 primary municipal evidence

Status: **primary municipal text evidence; PDF page images not visually certified in this session**

## 11 February 1948 — Harr identified as a pool-hall operator

An Oregon City City Commission special-meeting record dated **11 February 1948** states:

> Messrs. Harr and McDougald appeared before the Commission requesting permission to allow card playing in the pool halls.

Primary municipal source:
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4723051/File/document

The entry does not print Harr's first name, business name, or address. However, the later 1948 liquor-license record identifies **Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center / 505 Main Street**, while that same later list identifies **Harris E. McDougald / Pastime Pool Hall / 723 Main Street**. Taken together, the municipal records strongly support Harr and McDougald as the two pool-hall operators referenced in the February meeting.

The February record therefore establishes direct municipal evidence of a Harr pool-hall operation by **11 February 1948**, although the 505 address is supplied only by the later 1948 license list and should not be silently backdated as an exact-address statement from the February page itself.

## Late 1948 — Carl R. Harr directly at 505 Main

A later Oregon City City Commission record includes the Commission's approval of liquor-license applicants. The list directly identifies:

**Carl R. Harr, Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main Street**

Primary municipal source:
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4722834/File/document

The same list includes other address controls such as Harris E. McDougald / Pastime Pool Hall at 723 Main Street and Wallace & Mary R. Seid / Seid's Restaurant at 517 Main Street.

## Date handling for the later record

The retrieved later PDF is a four-page excerpt. The Harr license list appears immediately after correspondence dated **16 November 1948** and **23 November 1948**, and immediately before minutes headed **3 January 1949**. The opening date of the meeting containing the Harr list is not visible in the retrieved excerpt. Therefore this archive records that event conservatively as **late 1948** rather than assigning an exact December meeting date without the missing opening page.

## Why this matters

The repository already carried an official-secondary city-inventory statement placing **"Harr's Recreation Center" at 505 Main in 1945** (`E-032`). The 1948 municipal records materially strengthen that chronology by:

- directly establishing a **Harr pool-hall operation by 11 February 1948**;
- directly confirming later in 1948 the business name **Harr's Recreation Center**;
- directly confirming the exact address **505 Main Street**;
- identifying the operator/license applicant as **Carl R. Harr**; and
- extending documentary continuity of the recreation/pool-hall use into late 1948, immediately before the 1950 Sanborn depiction of billiards, cigars, and beer at 505.

This does **not** establish the exact Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center transfer date. That transition remains open.
