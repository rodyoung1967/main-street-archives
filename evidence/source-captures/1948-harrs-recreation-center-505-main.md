# Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main — late 1948 primary municipal evidence

Status: **primary municipal text evidence; PDF page image not visually certified in this session**

An Oregon City City Commission record from late 1948 includes the Commission's approval of liquor-license applicants. The list directly identifies:

**Carl R. Harr, Harr's Recreation Center, 505 Main Street**

Primary municipal source:
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4722834/File/document

The same list includes other address controls such as Harris E. McDougald / Pastime Pool Hall at 723 Main Street and Wallace & Mary R. Seid / Seid's Restaurant at 517 Main Street.

## Date handling

The retrieved PDF is a four-page excerpt. The Harr license list appears immediately after correspondence dated **16 November 1948** and **23 November 1948**, and immediately before minutes headed **3 January 1949**. The opening date of the meeting containing the Harr list is not visible in the retrieved excerpt. Therefore this archive records the event conservatively as **late 1948** rather than assigning an exact December meeting date without the missing opening page.

## Why this matters

The repository already carried an official-secondary city-inventory statement placing **"Harr's Recreation Center" at 505 Main in 1945** (`E-032`). This municipal record materially strengthens that chronology by:

- directly confirming the business name **Harr's Recreation Center**;
- directly confirming the exact address **505 Main Street**;
- identifying the operator/license applicant as **Carl R. Harr**; and
- extending direct documentary continuity of the recreation/pool-hall use into late 1948, immediately before the 1950 Sanborn depiction of billiards, cigars, and beer at 505.

This does **not** establish the exact Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center transfer date. That transition remains open.
