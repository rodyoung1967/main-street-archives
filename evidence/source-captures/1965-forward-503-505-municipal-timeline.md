# 503 / 505 Main — municipal and permit timeline from 1965 forward

Status: **working chronology; commission-minute PDFs not visually re-certified in this session**

Purpose: organize City of Oregon City commission minutes, permit logs, and related municipal records for **503 Main** and **505 Main** from **1965 forward**, while retaining adjacent-address findings when they help block context.

Primary focus addresses: **503 Main** (`B-001`) and **505 Main** (`B-002`).

Interpretation rules:

- Separate **business operation** from **real-estate ownership** unless a record explicitly proves both.
- **The Wheel** belongs only to **503 Main**. **505 Tavern** and successor tavern names belong to **505 Main**.
- Preserve `"The Wheel Tavern"` only as 2017 secondary-source wording; it was never an official name.

## Chronological gaps — online municipal follow-up targets

2. **1973–1987** — Brass Rail operating interval, Hansen→Wiitanen transition, and pre-Dug Inn records.
3. **1989–2008** — Sports Keg, Mitch Young March 1991 acquisition, and pre-Berman operator chain.
4. **2013–2016** — Thirsty Duck opening permits and Forvilly-era records.
5. **503 deed/tax/assessor chain** — Wolf-family ownership to 1972 is city-inventory secondary only; no post-1972 property-transfer municipal record is registered yet.
