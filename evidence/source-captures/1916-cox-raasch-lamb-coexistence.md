# Cox and Raasch & Lamb — separate concurrent pool halls in early 1916

Status: **strong primary newspaper evidence; exact Cox address still unresolved**

This note resolves an important part of the 1916 Oregon City pool-hall chronology: **Frank D. Cox's pool hall and Raasch & Lamb were separate concurrent businesses**, not a simple Cox→Raasch & Lamb succession.

## 13 January 1916 — Raasch & Lamb already active

The *Oregon City Courier* business-grid advertisement on 13 January 1916 includes:

**POOL AND BILLIARDS / Everything for Smokers / RAASCH & LAMB**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-01-13/ed-1/seq-11/

The same advertising grid is repeated later in the issue / recovered page material. The Raasch & Lamb ad itself does not print a numbered street address in the recovered text.

## 30 March 1916 — Cox pool hall independently still operating

The *Courier* reports that a man cashed a check at the **Cox poolhall**. Constable Jack Frost then called **Frank Cox from the pool hall** to identify the man.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-03-30/ed-1/seq-1/

This independently proves that Cox's hall was active more than two months after Raasch & Lamb were already advertising their own named pool/billiards business.

## May–June 1916 — Raasch & Lamb continuity

The 25 May 1916 Booster Edition again advertises:

**POOL AND BILLIARDS / Everything for Smokers / RAASCH & LAMB**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-05-25/ed-1/seq-8/

On 1 June the *Courier* reported that Raasch & Lamb would move their existing pool hall from beside the Andresen Building entrance into the building's basement, and on 22 June the council-related coverage says a temporary permit was granted for that move.

Sources:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-06-01/ed-1/seq-5/
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-06-22/ed-1/seq-1/

## Conclusion

The chronology now establishes:

- **13 Jan 1916:** Raasch & Lamb already operating a named pool/billiards business.
- **30 Mar 1916:** Frank Cox's pool hall independently operating.
- **25 May–22 Jun 1916:** Raasch & Lamb still independently operating and relocating their hall.

Therefore **Cox and Raasch & Lamb were concurrent Oregon City pool-hall operations in early 1916**. Do not describe Raasch & Lamb as simply succeeding Cox, and do not use their June relocation to infer that Cox's hall became the Andresen Building hall.

This does not resolve Cox's exact Main Street number. His hall remains a candidate for an unresolved lower-Main storefront, potentially including 503 or 505, but no direct address evidence has yet been recovered.
