https://www.loc.gov/item/sn88085057/

A separate genealogy newspaper inventory also lists **Morton Journal (1945–1973)** under **Offline Newspapers for Morton**:

https://ldsgenealogy.com/WA/Morton.htm

Repeated public-web searches in this pass used combinations of `Morton Journal`, `1945`, `1946`, `1947`, `LaFarlette`, `La Farlette`, `Raye`, `Rae`, `Floyd`, `Wheel Cafe`, `The Wheel`, proprietor/owner/manager, sale/purchase/new ownership, and later 1948–1956 continuity terms. No readable public digitized run for the target **1945 through 7 August 1947** interval surfaced.

Classification: **OFFICIAL CATALOG CONTROL** for the newspaper title/date span; **RETRIEVAL GAP / MANUAL SOURCE REQUIRED** for the target pages. This is not evidence that the LaFarlettes or Wheel Cafe were absent from Morton.

Searchable text from **22 April 1948**, p. 17, explicitly gives **“Ray LaFarlette, Powers”** as toastmistress at a Royal Neighbors convention.

Classification: **OCR/TEXT ONLY / STRONG IDENTITY CONTEXT**. These records place Raye in the Powers social/community environment soon after the working Morton window. They do **not** prove she was not in Morton during 1944–7 August 1947.

## 5. 28 July 1949 — direct-text Raye hotel-employment anchor and a Washington network lead

Searchable text from the **28 July 1949 Coquille Valley Sentinel**, p. 10, says a women's softball group consisted of **“the girls who work at the Coquille Hotel”** and lists **Rae LaFarlette** among the participants.

The same page reports that **Carl Anderson**, operator of the Anderson-LaFarlette truck line serving Powers, said he had **operated buses in Washington for 21 years**.

Route: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088222/1949-07-28/ed-1/seq-10/

As with the 23 June page, the PDF text was recoverable but the screenshot/page-image rerender returned a cache-miss error.

Classification for Raye: **OCR/TEXT ONLY / DIRECT-TEXT EMPLOYMENT ANCHOR** for Coquille Hotel employment by July 1949, pending visual certification. This is more precise than merely inferring an approximate start from the 1954 phrase “several years,” but it lies after the target Morton interval.

Classification for Anderson: **PERSON-NETWORK LEAD ONLY**. The article says Washington, not Morton, and supplies no restaurant, Wheel, residence or LaFarlette pre-1947 relationship. Anderson must **not** be treated as a Morton Wheel proprietor or as the remembered ownership bridge unless an independent contemporary source explicitly connects him to Morton/The Wheel.
