# 1922 — Miller's Shoe Store / Gilbert Miller continuity lead

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED primary advertisements + STRONG IDENTITY/CONTINUITY INFERENCE; exact pre-fire street number and business-name identity not yet directly proved**

Purpose: preserve the unusually tight trade and fire-continuity evidence connecting the pre-fire `Millers Shoe Store` advertisements to Gilbert Miller, while keeping the missing explicit proprietor statement and exact pre-fire storefront unresolved.

## 23 February 1922 — Millers Shoe Store opposite Post Office

The actual scanned page of the **Banner-Courier, 23 Feb 1922, p. 3** has been visually inspected.

The advertisement reads substantially:

- **Millers Shoe Store**
- Men's and Boys' Shoes, Gloves, Laces
- Shoe Polish, Oils and Greases
- **EXPERT SHOE REPAIRING**
- **Main Street — Opposite Post Office**

Canonical page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-02-23/ed-1/seq-3.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

The ad does **not** print a proprietor's first name, surname with apostrophe, or exact numbered street address. Therefore it does not by itself prove that Gilbert Miller operated the business or identify 501, 503, or 505 Main.

## 4 May 1922 — same pre-fire advertisement repeated

The actual scanned page of the **Banner-Courier, 4 May 1922, p. 5** has been visually inspected.

It again displays the same `Millers Shoe Store` trade pattern:

- Men's and Boys' Shoes, Gloves, Laces
- Shoe Polish, Oils and Greases
- **EXPERT SHOE REPAIRING**
- **Main Street — Opposite Post Office**

Canonical page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-05-04/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This demonstrates continuity of the named store and trade through at least early May 1922, about two and a half months before the July fire.

## 20 July 1922 — fire report names Gilbert Miller

The visually reviewed local fire report in the **Banner-Courier, 20 Jul 1922** identifies **Gilbert Miller's shoe-repair shop** among the destroyed businesses and reports that his **stock of shoes** was destroyed.

Related capture:
`evidence/source-captures/1922-fire-s-wolf-main-street-property-lead.md`

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED** in the project fire evidence.

This is especially important because it combines the same two functions seen in the pre-fire `Millers Shoe Store` ads: **shoe retail stock + shoe repairing**.

## 21 September 1922 — Gilbert Miller explicitly describes resuming pre-fire shoe repair

The actual scanned page of the **Banner-Courier, 21 Sep 1922, p. 5** has been visually inspected.

A signed advertisement by **GILBERT MILLER** states that he has sold out the hardware and has a new stock of workingmen's clothing, **shoes** and **gloves**, and says:

- his **shoe machinery is set up**;
- he is ready for the **same high-class shoe repairing as before the fire**;
- location: **209 Fifth Street**;
- wording also says **Formerly Goldman's Second-Hand Store**.

Canonical page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-09-21/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This directly proves that Gilbert Miller had conducted shoe repairing before the July fire and resumed it afterward.

## Current identity assessment

The combined evidence makes it a **strong inference** that the pre-fire **Millers Shoe Store** was Gilbert Miller's business:

1. the store name is `Millers`;
2. it sold shoes and gloves while advertising expert shoe repairing;
3. the July fire report names **Gilbert Miller's shoe-repair shop** and specifically mentions his **stock of shoes**;
4. the September signed Gilbert Miller ad says his shoe machinery was restored for the same shoe repairing he had done **before the fire** and again sells shoes/gloves.

However, the project has **not yet recovered a pre-fire source explicitly saying `Gilbert Miller, proprietor` of `Miller's/Millers Shoe Store`**. Under the archive standard, the identity therefore remains a **strong inference rather than proven fact**.

## Fire-map implication

The pre-fire Miller's Shoe Store was advertised on **Main Street opposite the Post Office**. Separate evidence places the fire-period Post Office on the east/even side of Main between Fifth and Sixth, within the later **504–514 Main** range, and Dr. Freeze at **505½ Main** also advertised `Opposite Postoffice`.

If the Gilbert-Miller/store identity is eventually directly proved, these advertisements would place Gilbert's pre-fire shop on the **odd-numbered west side of Main in the fire-area frontage**, but they still would **not by themselves identify 501, 503, or 505**.

Do not use this evidence to assign Miller's shop to a specific modern storefront until either:

- the store receives an exact street number in a primary source; or
- the exact Post Office bay and a sufficiently precise spatial relationship independently establish the storefront.
