# Fifth & Main photographic dating research

Status: **active / hypothesis building**

Purpose: date undated or loosely dated Fifth/Main street-level photographs using businesses, street improvements, parade events, and independently dated newspaper references. These are dating aids, not substitutes for visual source verification.

## Critical camera-geometry correction

The undated parade photograph is annotated **“Taken at 5th & Main St.”**, but the large **GARAGE** sign discussed in earlier notes is on **Fifth Street**, not on the Main Street frontage. It therefore must **not** be used as evidence that Elliott Garage occupied the Main/Fifth corner or the premises later mapped as 501 Main.

The correct working geometry is:

- the camera is at/near Fifth & Main;
- the photograph includes a view along Fifth Street toward the Water Street side of the block;
- the GARAGE / auto-work building is on Fifth Street;
- the Oregon City Laundry could therefore appear behind the Main Street frontage, roughly behind the 501/503 area depending on exact lot depths and building footprints.

This correction materially weakens the earlier 1913 Elliott-Garage argument.

## Fifth Street curb / streetscape lower bound

Repo evidence `E-059` establishes Oregon City's 1905 improvement of Fifth Street across Main, including grading, crushed rock, sidewalks, **concrete curbs**, corner blocks, and drains.

Source record:
`evidence/E-059-1905-fifth-main-street-improvement.md`

If the visible curbs in the photograph are the completed work described by this project, **circa 1906 or later** remains a reasonable working lower bound.

## Price Bros. upper bound

Price Bros. is repeatedly documented at **Sixth & Main** from 1910 through 1917 and announced a move to Seventh & Main after April 1, 1918.

Key references:

- 23 Sep 1910, Oregon City Courier — corner Main and Sixth Streets.
- 5 Jan 1912, Oregon City Courier — Sixth and Main Streets.
- 3 Jun 1913, Morning Enterprise — 6th and Main Sts.
- 20 Sep 1917, Oregon City Courier — still Sixth and Main Streets.
- 13 Dec 1917, Oregon City Courier — announces new home after April 1 at Seventh and Main.

If the visible Price Bros. sign is correctly identified, the image should be **pre-April 1918**.

## Fifth Street side-block anchors — high value

The side-street businesses are now more useful than the former Elliott Garage theory.

### Oregon City Laundry — 109 Fifth Street

The 24 Oct 1918 Oregon City Courier business guide lists:

- **OREGON CITY LAUNDRY, M. L. Blakeslee, Prop., 109 Fifth St.**

The same page places:

- **F. L. Davis — 5th & Water**, blacksmithing / horseshoeing;
- **Scripture & May — 108 5th**, carriage and auto works.

This creates a highly coherent Fifth Street business cluster between Main and Water.

A later City/ODOT corridor study says the **1925 Sanborn** shows Oregon City Laundry on the **east side of 5th Street between Water and Main** and that the laundry was later relocated as Fifth/Water was realigned for the highway.

### Block-geometry implication

The 108/109 Fifth numbering and the 1925 laundry location strongly support treating the Fifth Street garage/auto-work and laundry buildings as **side/rear-of-block structures**, not Main Street storefronts. Depending on the exact property-line geometry, the laundry could sit behind the corner frontage later mapped as **501 Main**, behind **503 Main**, or overlap the rear depth of both.

This question should be resolved by direct 1925 Sanborn footprint comparison rather than by address inference alone.

## Possible 501 Main visibility

The 1925 Sanborn explicitly labels **501 Main** as the corner store space at/near Fifth & Main, with 503 and 505 immediately north along Main. Because the photograph is taken at Fifth & Main, some or all of the corner frontage later mapped as 501 Main may be visible.

However, the pre-1925 corner-to-address mapping remains unresolved. Preserve the distinction:

- **high-confidence:** the Fifth/Main corner frontage may be visible;
- **hypothesis:** that visible frontage is the same premises later numbered 501 Main.

## Parade-event hypotheses

Two previously identified parade candidates remain possible:

### 8 Jun 1912 Rose Show

The 7 Jun 1912 Oregon City Courier describes a children's rose parade and decorated automobile parade on Main Street, with concerts at several intersections including Sixth/Main.

### 7 Jun 1913 Rose Show

The 6 Jun 1913 Oregon City Courier and Morning Enterprise describe a large Main Street parade with automobiles, floats, pedestrians, hose carts, and cowboy/horse entries.

### Current status of those event hypotheses

The earlier argument favoring 1913 because of the GARAGE sign is **withdrawn**. The sign is on Fifth Street and cannot be tied to Elliott Garage at Fifth/Main from the photograph geometry.

The 1912 and 1913 Rose Show dates therefore remain **possible event matches only**, dependent on visual comparison of parade participants, decorations, and route evidence.

If the Fifth Street GARAGE building proves to be Scripture & May at 108 Fifth, then both 1912 and 1913 would be too early and the photograph would need to be redated to **1916 or later**.

## Current evidence-based bracket

- Lower bound from Fifth Street concrete-curb work: **circa 1906 or later**.
- Upper bound from Price Bros. at Sixth/Main: **before April 1918**, if that storefront identification is correct.
- Strong new side-street hypothesis: **1916–early 1918** if the GARAGE/auto-work premises are Scripture & May at 108 Fifth.

This **1916–early 1918** zone is now at least as important as the older 1912/1913 Rose Show hypothesis and should be tested first through building/sign comparison.
