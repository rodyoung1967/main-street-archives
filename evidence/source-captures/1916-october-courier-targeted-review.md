## 19 October — Andresen Building address-grid control

Page 4 contains a Portland Railway Light & Power Company advertisement giving:

- **Andresen Bldg., 619 Main St.**
- Pacific Main 115 / Home A-229

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-10-19/ed-1/seq-4/

### Why this matters

This is a clean contemporary 1916 number for the **Andresen Building**. Earlier in June, Raasch & Lamb were reported moving from a ground-floor pool hall next to the Andresen Building entrance into its basement. The October **619 Main** anchor materially sharpens the geographic interpretation of that June story and makes it even less plausible to identify the Raasch & Lamb operation with the separately documented **504 Main** Wade/Oldstein pool hall without additional evidence.

This does **not** by itself prove the exact internal suite/address used by Raasch & Lamb.

## New transition evidence at 612 Main — Schilling already has a successor by late September / October

A recurring optometrist advertisement creates a useful intra-year business transition at **612 Main**.

### 28 September 1916

Courier page 5 carries an optometrist advertisement at **612 Main** with the line:

- **Successor to Wm. A. Schilling**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-09-28/ed-1/seq-5/ocr/

The OCR does not recover the successor's name cleanly from the display signature, but it does establish that Schilling was no longer the advertised optometrist at 612 by 28 September.

### 12, 19 and 26 October 1916

The same successor wording repeats in October:

- 12 Oct p5: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-10-12/ed-1/seq-5/ocr/
- 19 Oct p5: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-10-19/ed-1/seq-5/
- 26 Oct p5: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-10-26/ed-1/seq-5/

Each advertisement gives **OPTOMETRIST — 612 MAIN ST. — OREGON CITY — Successor to Wm. A. Schilling**.

### Later name resolution

By 14 Dec 1916 the optometrist at **612 Main** is explicitly identified in the recovered advertisement representation as **Cruden**. The December page therefore strongly suggests that the unnamed display-signature successor appearing from late September through November is Cruden, but the earlier pages still require direct scan inspection before assigning the name to each ad with visual-level certainty.

December source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-12-14/ed-1/seq-8.pdf

### Interpretation

This sharpens the Schilling timeline:

- **Jan–May 1916:** Schilling at 617 Main.
- **June 1916:** Schilling moves to 612 Main.
- **By 28 Sep 1916:** an optometrist at 612 advertises as **successor to Wm. A. Schilling**.
- **By Dec 1916:** that 612 optometrist is explicitly represented as **Cruden**.

So Schilling's tenure at 612 lasted only part of 1916, and the transition occurred no later than late September. This also helps explain some of the multiple 612 references: not all represent simultaneous optometrist occupancy; at least Schilling → successor is a genuine sequential change.
