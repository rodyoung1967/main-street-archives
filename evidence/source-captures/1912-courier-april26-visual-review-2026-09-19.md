## Sequence 4 header contamination / date anomaly

Sequence 4 visibly carries a top strip/header reading **“MORNING ENTERPRISE, FRIDAY, APRIL 19, 1912”**, while the page body is an *Oregon City Courier* editorial page.

This is not treated as an April 19 Morning Enterprise page. The body text explicitly discusses the already-completed Friday primaries and their results (“Friday’s primaries showed…”), which necessarily postdates the 19 April primary day. The page body therefore belongs to the 26 April Courier issue, while the foreign April-19 Morning Enterprise strip is preserved as a scan/header contamination anomaly.

Independent comparison with the actual 19 April *Oregon City Courier* sequence 4 shows a different page/body, with its own correctly dated Courier header. The April-26 sequence-4 body is therefore not a duplicate of the prior Courier page.

Classification:
- April 26 sequence 4 underlying Courier body: **VISUALLY VERIFIED / genuine intended-date page content**.
- top “Morning Enterprise, April 19” strip: **SOURCE-IMAGE / HEADER CONTAMINATION ANOMALY**, not issue-date evidence.
