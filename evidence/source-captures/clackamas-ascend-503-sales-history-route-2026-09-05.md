## What an Ascend parcel page can provide

Indexed official Ascend parcel pages for other Clackamas County properties were inspected to establish the system's fields and capabilities. Their **Sales History** tables include:

- Sale Date;
- Entry Date;
- Recording Date;
- **Recording Number**;
- **Sale Amount**;
- Excise Number;
- Deed Type;
- Transfer Type;
- **Grantor (Seller)**;
- **Grantee (Buyer)**; and
- Other Parcels.

The same parcel pages can also expose **Events** entries such as “Recording Processed,” property-transfer filing numbers, deed/instrument types, recording numbers, and taxpayer changes.

This is exactly the evidence needed to test the reported **2 December 2002 / $200,000** 503 transaction (`E-228`) without assuming the parties.
