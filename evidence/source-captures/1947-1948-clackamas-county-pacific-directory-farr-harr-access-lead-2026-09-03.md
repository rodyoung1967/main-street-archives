# 1947–1948 Clackamas County Pacific Directory — Farr/Harr 505 Main access lead

Date: 3 September 2026

Status: **SOURCE/ACCESS LEAD ONLY — OREGON CITY DIRECTORY ENTRIES NOT YET INSPECTED.**

Purpose: preserve a newly verified contemporary directory route that may sharply narrow the unresolved **Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center** transition at 505 Main. Catalog/bibliography evidence proves the directory's identity and survival/access routes, but no Farr/Harr/505 entry is promoted until the actual Oregon City pages are inspected.

## Directory identity

Multiple historic-resource bibliographies identify the source as:

**H. C. Prudhomme, 1947–1948 Directory for Clackamas County. Pacific Directory Service, Portland.**

A National Register bibliography explicitly gives this author/title/publisher form. Other Oregon historic-resource records cite the **Clackamas County Directory, 1947-48** for named residents and occupations.

Reference examples:
- NPS nomination search result identifying H. C. Prudhomme / Pacific Directory Service: https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/6060943b-4d9a-4e66-8852-5f1635eb7948

## Direct Oregon City scope confirmation

The Clackamas County Historical Society published a transcription headed **“FACTS You Should Know About Oregon City (Pacific Directory, 1947-48)”** and attributes the Oregon City material to the Chamber of Commerce. This confirms that the Pacific Directory contains Oregon City material and is not merely a rural-county source.

CCHS page:
https://clackamascountyhistoricalsociety.wordpress.com/2012/02/27/facts-you-should-know-about-oregon-city/

The CCHS transcription does not reproduce the alphabetical/business/street listings needed for 505 Main and therefore is not occupant evidence by itself.

## Why this source is high value for 505 Main

The repository currently has the following bracket:

- May 1925 Sanborn: **505 Main = billiards/cigars**, operator unnamed;
- official historic-resource evidence: Edwin F. and Alice Farr were proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main**, exact span unresolved;
- City historic-resource synthesis: **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 in 1945**, underlying contemporary page still needed;
- 11 February 1948 City Commission text: **Harr and McDougald** appear as pool-hall operators, no premises printed;
- late 1948 municipal license text: **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street** directly printed.

A 1947-48 directory entry could independently establish whether the business at 505 was already Harr's, remained Farr's, or had another operator in the immediate pre-license period. It could also identify Harris E. McDougald's separate Pastime Pool Hall and prevent cross-business conflation.

## Exact inspection targets

When the Oregon City pages are accessible, visually inspect and preserve:

1. **505 Main Street** in any street/address guide, including 505½ and neighboring 501–511 Main;
2. **Edwin F. Farr / Alice Farr / Farr's Pool Hall** in alphabetical and classified sections;
3. **Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center**;
4. **Harris E. McDougald / Pastime Pool Hall**;
5. classified headings for **pool rooms, billiards, recreation, cigars/tobacco, beer/taverns, soft drinks**;
6. any proprietor/business cross-reference or advertisement naming 505 Main;
7. **503 Main** restaurant/cafe occupants encountered in the same street ladder, because the directory may also improve the Kwality chronology.

Residences, business premises and property ownership must remain separate unless the directory explicitly connects them.

## Evidentiary boundary

No 1947-48 505 occupant, Farr→Harr transition date, 503 occupant, or business-owner relationship is promoted from the catalog/bibliography evidence. No source/evidence ID is created until actual directory content is inspected under project policy.

This source should be prioritized ahead of repeating broad newspaper keyword searches for the Farr→Harr transition because it is contemporary, address-oriented, and lies immediately before the February/late-1948 municipal anchors.
