# 1941 Oregon City directory — online catalog route correction

Date reviewed: 4 September 2026

Status: **ACCESS-ROUTE CORRECTION / CATALOG DISAMBIGUATION ONLY. DIRECTORY PAGE NOT INSPECTED; NO FARR/HARR/505 CLAIM PROMOTED.**

Purpose: prevent a misleading FamilySearch catalog route from being mistaken for the **1941 Pacific Directory Service** Oregon City directory while preserving the genuinely useful online access leads for the unresolved Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center transition at 505 Main.

Related main capture: `evidence/source-captures/1941-oregon-city-directory-farr-harr-access-lead-2026-09-03.md`.

## Confirmed 1941 bibliographic identity

The City of Oregon City's 2000 Downtown Historic Resource Survey bibliography cites:

**Pacific Directory Service. _Directory of Oregon City_. Portland: Pacific Directory Service, 1941.**

A recent official West Linn National Register nomination independently cites the same source as:

**Pacific Directory Service 1941 Directory: Oregon City-West Linn-Gladstone.**

The West Linn nomination uses the directory substantively—for example, it states that the 1941 directory lists two Columbia Food Stores in Oregon City and one in West Linn—showing that the preservation researcher consulted directory content rather than merely copying a bibliography.

Official West Linn nomination:

`https://westlinnoregon.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/communications/page/54306/final_nomination_application_city_hall_and_area_history.pdf`

This remains secondary use of the directory; it does not expose the Farr/Harr/505 pages.

## FamilySearch catalog 95422 is NOT the 1941 volume

The LDSGenealogy Oregon City directory page lists both:

- a generic FamilySearch Library link titled **“Oregon City (Clackamas County, Oregon) city directory : including Gladstone, West Linn and Willamette, also a buyers' guide and a complete classified business directory”**; and
- a distinct **“Oregon City, Oregon, City Directory, 1941 (in U.S. City Directories collection)”** Ancestry image link.

Discovery page:

`https://ldsgenealogy.com/OR/Oregon-City.htm`

Following the generic FamilySearch Library title resolves to FamilySearch catalog **95422**:

`https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/95422`

The live FamilySearch catalog identifies that record as:

- author: **R. L. Polk & Co.**;
- publication date: **1960-**;
- publisher: **R. L. Polk & Co.**;
- place: Monterey Park, California;
- annual publication;
- listed contained volumes beginning with **1960**, then 1964, 1965, 1967, 1969, 1973, 1977, 1980, 1986 and 1987.

Therefore **FamilySearch catalog 95422 must not be cited as a holding or digital route for the 1941 Pacific Directory Service volume**. Its geographic title resembles the earlier directory's coverage closely enough to create a false lead, but its publisher and date series are different.

## Useful 1941 online route that remains

LDSGenealogy separately lists:

**Oregon City, Oregon, City Directory, 1941 (in U.S. City Directories collection) — Ancestry — Image: online.**

The linked Ancestry collection is:

`https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2469/`

The current research environment can reach the Ancestry `U.S., City Directories, 1822–1995` collection shell but not the underlying 1941 Oregon City page images without the necessary signed-in image access. No Edwin Farr, Alice Farr, Carl Harr, 505 Main or classified pool-hall page was recovered through the public layer.

This remains a **REAL ACCESS LEAD / AUTHENTICATION BARRIER**, not a negative directory result.

## Forebears secondary searchable-book route

Forebears separately indexes an **“Oregon City, Oregon City Directory (1941–1960)”** resource under its Oregon City genealogy page and describes the resource group as searchable books relating to residents and businesses.

Route:

`https://forebears.io/united-states/oregon/clackamas-county/oregon-city`

The page returned a 403 in the current research environment. No underlying directory page or name result was exposed. Preserve this only as a second discovery route.

## Exact next inspection target

2. Alice Farr;
3. Farr's Pool Hall / Farr under pool, billiards, recreation, cigars/tobacco or related classifications;
4. Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center;

Do not merge the R. L. Polk 1960+ series into the Pacific Directory Service 1941 source history.

## Evidence boundary

This correction changes **source routing only**. It does not establish who occupied 505 in 1941 and does not narrow the Farr→Harr transition. The actual 1941 directory pages remain uninspected in this research environment.
