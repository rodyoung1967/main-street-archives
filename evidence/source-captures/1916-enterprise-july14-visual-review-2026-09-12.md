### Page 4 — same-block construction control, exact number not printed

A city-improvements summary lists **Mrs. Mary E. Barlow and J. W. David, Main Street, between Fifth and Sixth streets**, with **improvements to a store building; cost $500**. The scan does **not** print a street number or otherwise identify which Main Street premises between Fifth and Sixth received the work. This is therefore valuable same-block physical-building context, but it must not be assigned to 501/503/505/505½/507 without an independent address bridge.

The same list also gives **Mrs. M. L. Foster, Fifth and Main streets**, with improvements costing $800. Again, no target street number is printed.

### Page 3 — explicit non-target business control

## Evidentiary limits

- The store-building improvement cannot be assigned to any one of 501/503/505/505½/507 from this issue alone.
- No-hit language above is bounded to this visually inspected issue and is not evidence of vacancy, non-use, or absence.
