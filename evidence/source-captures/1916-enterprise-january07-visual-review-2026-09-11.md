## Page-by-page review

- **p1:** Front-page local, county, prohibition and industrial news. No source-explicit 503 Main or 505 Main premises assignment.
- **p2:** County/community material and advertising. W. J. Wilson & Company is explicitly at **12th and Main Streets**, outside the target frontage. No 503/505 premises fact.
- **p3:** Milwaukie and Oswego department material. No target-address fact.
- **p4:** Editorial/local page. A prohibition-era editorial says that business locations formerly occupied by saloons were being taken by other lines of business and that some former saloon operators continued as soft-drink establishments. This is **general citywide/business-district commentary only**: it supplies no numbered address and is not evidence that 503 or 505 changed use, became occupied, or housed any particular business.
- **p5:** Local briefs and general news. No exact 503/505 fact.
- **p6:** Large visually clear advertisement for **Eilers Music House — Oregon City Branch, 519 Main Street**. This is a useful numbered Main Street control outside the target frontage. No exact 503/505 fact.
- **p7:** International/state/county material, real-estate transfers and Mayor E. C. Hackett's annual-address coverage. A brief item says the new First National Bank building was built of eastern marble, but no numbered address is supplied. This may be useful contextual evidence for later relative-location research such as the Tolpolar “next door 1st Nat'l Bank” wording, but it does **not** itself establish a Tolpolar street number. No 503/505 premises fact.
- **p8:** City-council coverage, state news, legal notices and professional directory. The First National Bank is named in the professional directory without a numbered street address. No exact 503/505 fact.

## Finding

The complete 7 January 1916 issue does **not** print an exact 503 Main or 505 Main occupant, proprietor, property owner, tenant, licensee, trade-name change, sale, transfer, construction, demolition, closure, or vacancy statement.

The most useful new numbered control is **Eilers Music House, Oregon City Branch — 519 Main Street**. The issue also provides First National Bank context without a street number and a general post-prohibition reuse statement that must not be projected onto either target building.

Absence of a target-address hit in this issue is a coverage result only and is not evidence of vacancy, non-use, demolition, or absence of a business.
