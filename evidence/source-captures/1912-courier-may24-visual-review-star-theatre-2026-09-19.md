## Star Theatre follow-forward — Main/Eighth project

Three sources form a strong same-project chain:

1. **2 February 1912 Courier p.1 — primary, previously recovered.** The article headed “HERE’S THE STARTER” says Mt. Hood Brewing Co. took a long lease of the Hood property at the **corner of Main and Eighth**, going north 66 feet, and planned a two-story business block containing a saloon, store and **up-to-date moving picture theatre**. It directly says **George Simmons had already leased the picture theatre for a long term**.

2. **24 May 1912 Courier p.5 — primary, visually verified in this pass.** The unnamed new motion-picture theatre **on Main Street** was nearly ready to open.

3. **31 May 1912 Morning Enterprise p.3 clipping — primary clipping visually reviewed through the University of Oregon Oregon Theater Project.** Headline: **STAR THEATRE WILL OPEN TOMORROW**. It directly says the Star Theatre, under management of **George Simmons**, would open Saturday evening; the building was **on Main Street near Eighth**, constructed especially for a moving-picture theatre, and seated 300.

The matching manager/lessee, location, construction timing and theatre type make the May 24 unnamed theatre a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE CONTINUITY MATCH** to the same Main/Eighth Star Theatre project.

### Address-number caution

The 1912 primary evidence fixes the project geographically at/near **Main and Eighth** but does not supply a numbered Star Theatre address.

Later sources disagree on the numbered address:
- City historic-resource table: **716 Main**, c.1912 Star Theatre.
- Oregon Theater Project / 1925 Sanborn-derived metadata: **720 Main**.

Do not choose between 716 and 720 for the 1912 opening state without reconciling numbering/building continuity. For this project, the controlling 1912 location is **Main Street at/near Eighth**, explicitly outside the 501–511 target frontage.

## Relationship to the 10 May “tear down / build up” editorial

The Star/Mt. Hood block gives a documented 1912 Main Street redevelopment project underway during the same season as the 10 May editorial saying Oregon City was tearing down Main Street buildings and replacing them.

This makes the Star block a concrete **non-target example of the citywide 1912 rebuilding context**, but the 10 May editorial does not name the project. Do not claim the editorial specifically referred to the Star block, and do not let this documented Eighth/Main project resolve the separate question of whether any 501/503/505 predecessor building was also replaced in 1912.
