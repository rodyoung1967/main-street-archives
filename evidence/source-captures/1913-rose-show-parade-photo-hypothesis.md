# 1913 Rose Show parade — Fifth & Main photo hypothesis

Status: **working hypothesis; garage-sign inference corrected**

## Candidate photograph

Undated historical photograph supplied to the project, handwritten/annotated **“Taken at 5th & Main St.”** Visible features previously noted during project review include:

- Price Bros. Clothiers signage farther north along Main;
- a large **GARAGE** sign visible **along Fifth Street**, not on the Main Street frontage;
- mounted riders / horse entries;
- developed curbs and sidewalks;
- parade activity at the Fifth/Main intersection.

The photograph may also include some or all of the corner premises later mapped as **501 Main Street**.

## Corrected street geometry

The camera position/annotation is at **Fifth & Main**, but the large GARAGE sign is in the **Fifth Street view toward Water Street**, not on Main Street. Therefore:

- the GARAGE sign must **not** be used as evidence that Elliott Garage occupied the later 501 Main corner premises;
- the earlier inference linking the sign to Elliott Garage at Fifth & Main is withdrawn;
- Fifth Street businesses/buildings visible in that direction may sit **behind the Main Street frontage**, including behind the later 501/503 premises depending on exact lot geometry;
- the Oregon City Laundry's later known east-side-of-Fifth location between Water and Main is geographically compatible with a building behind the Main Street storefront row.

This corrected geometry makes the photo especially useful for reconstructing the depth of the Fifth/Main block, not just the Main Street storefront line.

## Independent dating anchors

### Fifth Street concrete-curb work — 1905

`E-059` documents Oregon City's 1905 improvement of Fifth Street across Main, including grading, crushed rock, sidewalks, **concrete curbs**, corner blocks and drains. If the photograph's visible curb treatment is this completed work, the image is reasonably interpreted as **post-1905**, with circa 1906 or later as a working lower bound.

### Price Bros. at Sixth & Main

Price Bros. is repeatedly documented at **Sixth & Main** from 1910 through 1917 and announced a move to Seventh & Main after April 1, 1918. If the visible Price Bros. sign is correctly identified, the photograph is **pre-April 1918**.

### Fifth/Main corner business sequence

Recovered newspaper evidence gives the following corner sequence:

- Dec. 1910 — Mt. Hood Cafe, 5th & Main.
- Jan. 1912 — Clem's Chop House, 5th & Main.
- Jan.–Mar. 1913 — Elliott Garage explicitly at Fifth & Main Streets.
- Aug. 1914 onward — R. L. Holman funeral-directing establishment at the corner of Fifth & Main.

These remain useful documentary anchors for the intersection, but **the garage sign in this photograph cannot currently be tied to Elliott Garage** because the sign is on Fifth Street rather than the Main frontage.

## June 7, 1913 Rose Show parade

Contemporary coverage describes an unusually large Oregon City Rose Show parade on **7 June 1913**:

- parade formed near Fourteenth/Fifteenth and Main and traveled along Main;
- entries included automobiles, business floats, lodges, pedestrians and children's groups;
- prize classes included **“small cowboys on horses, other animals, or on foot”**;
- Oregon City and Redland bands and the G.A.R. fife-and-drum corps participated;
- advance coverage said motion-picture men would record the rose exhibits and parades;
- post-event coverage says the parade traveled up Main Street and returned, and describes numerous decorated vehicles and novelty entries.

## Revised assessment

**June 7, 1913 remains a plausible candidate, but it is no longer strengthened by the GARAGE sign.**

Current positive fit:

1. Price Bros. was at Sixth/Main.
2. The parade specifically included mounted/cowboy entries, matching the visible horse riders.
3. The parade traveled Main Street and was large enough to fill the Fifth/Main view.
4. The post-1905 curb treatment is chronologically compatible.

The former point tying the visible garage sign to Elliott Garage is withdrawn.

## New block-layout lead

The Fifth Street view may show rear/side structures **behind 501 or 503 Main**. The later Oregon City Laundry location on the east side of Fifth between Water and Main is a strong comparison target for reconstructing this geometry. The next visual comparison should map:

- Main Street frontage line (501/503/505);
- Fifth Street curb line and camera direction;
- laundry footprint between Main and Water;
- any garage/auto-service footprint on Fifth;
- lot depths from the 1925 Sanborn.

## Important caution

The parade date is **not proven**. The decisive next step is direct high-resolution visual comparison against:

- Fifth Street business/building photographs;
- the 1913 parade's known entry types;
- the Fifth/Main corner footprint and later 501 Main mapping;
- the Oregon City Laundry footprint;
- Historical Society catalog records for an exact matching print/negative.

Until that match is made, retain **1913 Rose Show parade** as a plausible working hypothesis rather than assigning the image an exact date.
