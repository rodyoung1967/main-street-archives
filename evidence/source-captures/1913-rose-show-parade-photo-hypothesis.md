# 1913 Rose Show parade — Fifth & Main photo hypothesis

Status: **strong working hypothesis; not yet visually proven**

## Candidate photograph

Undated historical photograph supplied to the project, handwritten/annotated **“Taken at 5th & Main St.”** Visible features previously noted during project review include:

- Price Bros. Clothiers signage farther north along Main;
- a large **GARAGE** sign near the Fifth/Main corner area;
- mounted riders / horse entries;
- developed curbs and sidewalks;
- parade traffic along Main Street.

The photograph may also include some or all of the corner premises later mapped as **501 Main Street**.

## Independent dating anchors

### Fifth Street concrete-curb work — 1905

`E-059` documents Oregon City's 1905 improvement of Fifth Street across Main, including grading, crushed rock, sidewalks, **concrete curbs**, corner blocks and drains. If the photograph's visible curb treatment is this completed work, the image is reasonably interpreted as **post-1905**, with circa 1906 or later as a working lower bound.

### Price Bros. at Sixth & Main

Price Bros. is repeatedly documented at **Sixth & Main** from 1910 through 1917 and announced a move to Seventh & Main after April 1, 1918. If the visible Price Bros. sign is correctly identified, the photograph is **pre-April 1918**.

### Fifth/Main business sequence

Recovered newspaper evidence gives the following corner sequence:

- Dec. 1910 — Mt. Hood Cafe, 5th & Main.
- Jan. 1912 — Clem's Chop House, 5th & Main.
- Feb. 1912 — Elliott Garage definitely operating in Oregon City, location not established by the recovered source.
- Jan.–Mar. 1913 — **Elliott Garage explicitly at Fifth & Main Streets**.
- Aug. 1914 onward — R. L. Holman funeral-directing establishment at the corner of Fifth & Main.

If the photograph's large **GARAGE** sign can be matched to Elliott Garage at the corner, this strongly favors **1913 to early 1914**.

## June 7, 1913 Rose Show parade

Contemporary coverage describes an unusually large Oregon City Rose Show parade on **7 June 1913**:

- parade formed near Fourteenth/Fifteenth and Main and traveled along Main;
- entries included automobiles, business floats, lodges, pedestrians and children's groups;
- prize classes included **“small cowboys on horses, other animals, or on foot”**;
- Oregon City and Redland bands and the G.A.R. fife-and-drum corps participated;
- advance coverage said motion-picture men would record the rose exhibits and parades;
- post-event coverage says the parade traveled up Main Street and returned, and describes numerous decorated vehicles and novelty entries.

## Assessment

**June 7, 1913 is currently the strongest specific candidate date for the undated Fifth/Main parade photograph.**

Why it fits unusually well:

1. Price Bros. was at Sixth/Main.
2. Elliott Garage is explicitly documented at Fifth/Main in early 1913, potentially matching the visible GARAGE sign.
3. The parade specifically included mounted/cowboy entries, matching the visible horse riders.
4. The parade traveled Main Street and was large enough to fill the Fifth/Main view.
5. The post-1905 curb treatment is chronologically compatible.

## Important caution

The date is **not yet proven**. The decisive next step is a direct high-resolution visual comparison of the photograph against:

- Elliott Garage signage/building photographs;
- the 1913 parade's known entry types;
- the Fifth/Main corner footprint and later 501 Main mapping;
- Historical Society catalog records for an exact matching print/negative.

Until that match is made, retain **1913 Rose Show parade** as a strong hypothesis rather than assigning the image an exact date.
