### Address findings

The January business-directory pages continue the neighboring odd-numbered Main Street ladder:

- **W. W. Bradley — 507 Main**
- **H. P. Brightbill — 509 Main**
- **George A. Harding — 511 Main**

The 13 Jan directory page visibly supports these addresses.

The 20 Jan OCR renders Harding as `611 Main`; this is not accepted as an address change because the surrounding repeated directory evidence and other clean page readings establish **511 Main**. This is retained as a concrete warning that OCR address digits must not be accepted without visual confirmation.

No visually confirmed January listing has yet identified an occupant at **503 Main** or **505 Main**.

### OCR-error examples preserved

February pages reinforce the need for visual verification. Search/OCR renderings include examples such as:

- Price Bros. **527 Main** misread as **627 Main**;
- Brightbill **509 Main** misread as **609 Main**;
- similar single-digit substitutions in recurring directory entries.

These are OCR defects, not evidence of business moves.

### Target-address status through February

No reliable visually confirmed February listing has yet been found for **503 Main** or **505 Main**. This is a research-status statement only and must not be treated as evidence of vacancy or absence.
