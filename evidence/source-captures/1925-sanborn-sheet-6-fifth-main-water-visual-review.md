Status: **visually reviewed from City of Oregon City scan**

Purpose: establish the physical relationship among the Oregon City Laundry, Fifth Street, Water Street, Main Street, and the Main Street storefront block that includes the later 501/503/505 sequence.

## What Sheet 6 shows

Sheet 6 covers the riverfront/downtown blocks around **Water Street, Main Street, Fourth Street, Fifth Street, and Sixth Street**.

The block bounded by:

- **Water Street** on the west,
- **Main Street** on the east,
- **Fifth Street** on the south,
- **Sixth Street** on the north

contains a clearly labeled **OREGON CITY LAUNDRY** building/complex on the Fifth Street side of the block.

The Main Street commercial buildings occupy the eastern edge of this same block. This is the block containing the Main Street frontage later associated with the **501 / 503 / 505** address sequence.

## Geometry implication for 501 / 503 / 505 Main

This directly supports the working interpretation that the laundry was a **side/rear-of-block use behind the Main Street storefront frontage**, rather than a Main Street storefront itself.

Therefore, the user's earlier observation that a Fifth Street laundry could appear in a photograph taken at Fifth & Main while sitting behind the 501/503 frontage is geographically consistent with the 1925 Sanborn.

At this stage, the map supports the broad rear-of-block relationship with high confidence. Exact parcel-depth assignment — e.g. whether a particular laundry wall lies specifically behind 501, behind 503, or spans rear portions of multiple Main Street lots — still requires a closer lot-line/footprint comparison and should not be overstated.

## Important 1922-fire implication

The July 1922 Banner-Courier states that the Oregon City Laundry plant and neighboring May & Washburn frame garage were destroyed by fire.

Yet the **May 1925 Sanborn Sheet 6 visibly shows Oregon City Laundry again in the Fifth/Water/Main block**.

That establishes an important post-fire fact:

> **By May 1925, Oregon City Laundry had a physical laundry building/complex again in the Fifth Street block between Water and Main.**

What remains unresolved is whether this was:

1. a rebuild on or near the pre-fire 109 Fifth site;
2. a different structure elsewhere in the same block;
3. a new building that later evolved into the McLoughlin-era property.

The Sanborn visual proves the 1925 physical presence, but not continuity of the exact pre-1922 footprint.

## Relationship to later corridor memo

A later City/ODOT technical memorandum says the 1925 Sanborn lists Oregon City Laundry on the east side of Fifth between Water and Main. The direct Sheet 6 visual is now the preferred evidence for block geometry because it shows the actual building footprint and surrounding streets.
