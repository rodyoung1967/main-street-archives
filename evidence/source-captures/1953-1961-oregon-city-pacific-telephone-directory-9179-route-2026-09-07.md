## Purpose

This pass narrows the correct source class for identifying the visually verified contact telephone **9179** printed in the 20 November 1955 *Eugene Register-Guard* advertisement offering the long-established beer-and-pool business at **505 Main Street, Oregon City** for sale (`S-037` / `E-039`).

The goal is not to infer a subscriber from unrelated four-digit telephone hits. It is to establish whether Oregon City had a distinct local Pacific Telephone directory series in the relevant period and to identify the period directory that should be inspected directly for subscriber **9179**.

## 1953 Oregon City telephone scale control

A contemporary *Medford Mail Tribune* editorial dated **23 November 1953**, citing figures recently released by the **Pacific Telephone and Telegraph Company**, reports **6,094 telephones for Oregon City** among its statewide city totals.

Discovery / OCR route:
`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1953-11-23/ed-1/seq-4/ocr/`

This is useful as a source-series control because it confirms that Oregon City had a substantial Pacific Telephone subscriber base in the same year as the T. W. Salisbury city directory. It does **not** identify 9179, 505 Main, Well's Recreation, Carl R. Harr, or any proprietor.

J. Henry** as Oregon City manager for Pacific Telephone Northwest.

Discovery / scan-PDF route:
`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/2024240281/1961-02-23/ed-1/seq-1.pdf`

It does not establish the publication frequency or prove that the exact 1955 directory survives online.

## Why this matters for the 505 succession question

The 20 November 1955 source gives a highly specific identifier — **9179** — while omitting both the business name and seller's name. A period Oregon City telephone directory could therefore provide an independent person/business bridge capable of testing:

1. whether 9179 was listed directly to the business at 505 Main;
2. whether it was listed to a person surnamed Well / Wells or another proprietor;
3. whether the number corresponds to a residence, business, or both; and
4. whether the subscriber appears independently in the 1953 Salisbury directory or a liquor-license record.

A telephone listing would establish a published subscriber-number association only. It would not by itself prove ownership of the business, ownership of the building, tenancy, or liquor-license ownership.

## Highest-value retrieval target

Recover and visually inspect an **Oregon City Pacific Telephone & Telegraph / Pacific Telephone Northwest telephone directory from approximately 1953–1956**, prioritizing the edition closest to **20 November 1955**. Check:

- subscriber number **9179**;
- 505 Main / 505 Main Street;
- Well / Well's / Wells / Welle / Welsh;
- Harr, Carl R.;
- recreation / pool / billiards / tavern headings if a classified section exists; and
- any cross-reference between the subscriber and 505 Main.

The Wilsonville Northwest Heritage Index independently confirms an earlier **August 1932 Telephone Directory, Oregon City, Clackamas County, Oregon (Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co.)**, showing that Oregon City telephone directories survive as a recognizable local source type, although that earlier volume cannot answer the 1955 subscriber question.
