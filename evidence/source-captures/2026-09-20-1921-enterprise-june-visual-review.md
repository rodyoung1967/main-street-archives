# 1921 Enterprise visual review — June

## Scope and method

Continuation of ANNUAL-1921. **1921 remains IN PROGRESS.**

All four weekly Oregon City Enterprise issues in June 1921 were inventoried and all **32 page images were visually inspected page by page**:
- 3 June — 8/8
- 10 June — 8/8
- 17 June — 8/8
- 24 June — 8/8

The visual review covered the entire **500 block of Main Street**, with special attention to 501/503/505 and direct/indirect Fifth-and-Main evidence. Search attention included named businesses and persons, printed addresses, adjacent-business spatial controls, demolition/teardown, rebuilding/construction, remodeling, fire/damage, property sales/ownership transfers, landlord changes, leases, tenant/business changes, and openings/closings/moves.

## Results

All 32 pages were present and visually inspectable.

No June issue produced a new sufficiently specific fact tying a person, business, ownership/tenancy change, demolition, rebuild, remodeling, fire/damage event, or property transfer to 501, 503, or 505 Main.

No additional June item elsewhere in the 500 block was sufficiently address-specific to propagate into the target-building chronology.

## Evidence limitation

These no-hit findings document coverage only. They do not establish that no physical, ownership, tenancy, or business change occurred. The separate 1921 Morning Enterprise daily run remains an essential source universe and must be visually audited before 1921 can be ONLINE COMPLETE.
