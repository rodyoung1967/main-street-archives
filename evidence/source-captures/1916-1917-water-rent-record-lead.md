https://www.orcity.org/1710/Historic-Documents-and-Research

The city also lists the preceding **1914-1915 Water Rent Record Book 3**.

## Why this matters

The unresolved core question for the current annual pass is the occupant/business sequence at **503 Main** and **505 Main** during 1916. If Water Rent Record Book 4 is organized by customer, premises, street or service address, it may provide direct municipal evidence for:

- a water-service customer at 503 or 505 Main;
- an owner/tenant name associated with either address;
- continuity or change from the 1914-1915 book into 1916-1917;
- shared/multiple service arrangements that could explain storefront subdivisions;
- evidence relevant to the pre-c.1919/c.1920 predecessor structures.
