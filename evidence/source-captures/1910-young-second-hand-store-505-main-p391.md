# 1910 — Young second-hand store at 505 Main; museum catalog says later pool-hall location

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED MUSEUM CATALOG RECORD; underlying historic photograph not yet inspected**

Purpose: preserve a newly verified catalog-level bridge between **505 Main** in 1910 and the later use of that same location as a pool hall, while distinguishing the catalog record from direct inspection of the historic photograph itself.

## Source

**Museum of the Oregon Territory / Clackamas County Historical Society photo-reference spreadsheet**, item **P-391**, visually inspected on PDF page 32 (viewer index 31).

Catalog URL:
https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5755b9f7044262eb95050aa8/t/5b6230c01ae6cfaaeed33907/1533161670139/Photo+reference+spreadsheet+Aug+1+18.pdf

The P-391 catalog entry reads, in substance:

- **Young 2nd hand store**
- **505 Main street, Oregon City**
- **1910**
- **“same location as pool hall is now”**
- photograph back stamped **Ray O. Young, 757 Division Street**
- handwritten/pencil notes identify the four men left-to-right as **Bill Kruger, Dr. Bailey, G. H. Young, and Markon Knot**; George H. Young is described as wearing a white shirt and no coat
- museum/reference number **M/R y # 011-0048**

Verification state for the catalog page: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

The historic photograph represented by P-391 has **not yet been directly inspected**, so the visible storefront, signage, neighboring buildings, alley geometry, and date annotation have not yet been independently checked against the catalog description.

## Important correction — P-372 is a different photograph

The same catalog contains a separate record, **P-372**, on PDF page 30, for:

- `Young's 2nd Hand store 1907`
- notes on back saying the store was **“across alley from 1st National Bank”**
- reference **M/R y # 011-0021**.

Earlier search-engine snippets blended text from P-372 and P-391. The source itself makes clear that they are **different records**. Therefore:

- **P-372 = 1907 / across alley from First National Bank**;
- **P-391 = 1910 / 505 Main / same location as later pool hall**.

Do not merge the two photo descriptions.

## Relationship to George H. Young

The P-391 catalog note explicitly identifies **G. H. Young** in the photograph. Other project evidence independently documents George H. Young in Oregon City's second-hand/curio trade, including a 1912 advertisement placing **George Young, Main Street near Fifth** and later catalog entries identifying **Young's Curio Store at 507 Main in 1916**.

P-391 therefore provides a strong catalog-level identification of George H. Young's second-hand business at **505 Main in 1910**.

## Why this matters for 505 Main and the 1922 fire problem

This source does **not** identify the July 1922 fire-adjacent pool-hall operator or prove that the pool hall mentioned in the catalog note existed in 1922. The word `now` is relative to an unidentified annotation/cataloging moment.

It nevertheless materially strengthens the long-term 505 pool-hall history because the museum's own catalog metadata explicitly remembers **505 Main as the same location later occupied by a pool hall**. That aligns with independent evidence already in the archive:

- 1918 — Wolf & Miller at **505 Main**;
- 1925 Sanborn — **505 Main = billiards/cigars**;
- later — Farr's Pool Hall at **505 Main**;
- 1948 — Harr's Recreation Center at **505 Main**.

The catalog note is therefore **supporting continuity evidence**, not the missing 1922 exact-address bridge.
