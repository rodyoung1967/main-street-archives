## What this conversation completed

### 1. 1920 pool-hall / 505½ Main visual verification

The three preserved 1920 *Oregon City Enterprise* pages were visually inspected and integrated as `S-150` / `E-135`:

- **13 Feb. 1920, p.5** — Clem Dollar is directly identified as proprietor of a pool hall on Main between Fifth and Sixth. No numbered address is stated.
- **4 Jun. 1920, p.3** — the City Council report directly says **A. Leland** was granted permission to operate a pool hall at **505½ Main Street** and erect an electric street sign.
- **3 Dec. 1920, p.1** — a pool hall on Main between Fifth and Sixth is referenced at block level.

These are occupancy/use anchors. They do **not** prove the surviving 505 building was constructed in 1920, and the three pool-hall references are not automatically the same business/premises.

Do not merge A. Leland with O. H. Leland without a source-explicit identity bridge.

### 3. 1919 cleaner/dyer repeat set visually resolved

The *Enterprise* cleaner/dyer classified was visually verified across **eight consecutive weekly issues, 18 Jul.–5 Sep. 1919** (`S-151`, `S-152`, `E-136`). Every inspected scan prints the same key wording:

- `500 Fifth and Main streets`
- `two doors south of new post office`
- `Phone 260`

OCR readings of `600` on 25 Jul. and 5 Sep. are false; the scans print `500`.

Important limit: do **not** silently normalize the unusual printed location to `500 Main` or identify the advertiser as Pantorium. Pantorium is a plausible comparison lead, but its earlier telephone number differs. Phone 260 remains unidentified.
