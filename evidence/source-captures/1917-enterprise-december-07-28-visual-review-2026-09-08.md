# December 1917 Oregon City Enterprise visual review

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**. Source/evidence: `S-302` / `E-276`.

All 32 pages were inspected: 7, 14, 21 and 28 Dec., eight pages each. Originals and checksums are preserved in `newspapers/oregon-city-enterprise/1917-december/manifest.json`. Pages 2 on 21 and 28 Dec. retain an original “Morning Enterprise” running head but are genuine intended-issue pages.

Fashion Stable continues at Main between Fourth and Fifth through 28 Dec. The 7 Dec. p.5 report says juveniles were suspected of robbing printed **“Farrs' market”**; because it gives neither address, “Brothers,” nor a proprietor, it is only a probable Farr Brothers connection and is not evidence for Farr's later 505 pool hall. No exact 501/503/505 occupant or target-building event was found. This is bounded coverage, not vacancy evidence.
