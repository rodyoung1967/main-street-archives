## Identity caution

Separate municipal minutes directly show **C. P. Farr as an Oregon City commissioner** by 1940–1941. This project does **not** currently have a direct source expanding those initials or equating that person with:

- **Edwin F. Farr**, proprietor of Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main;
- **Clarence Farr**, the grocer/butcher associated with Farr Brothers at Seventh/Monroe; or
- any other Farr family member.

The official City historic-resource record for Clarence Farr identifies him by full name but does not provide the middle initial `P` in the material reviewed here. Do not infer `C. P. Farr = Clarence Farr` from surname, geography, or family context.

## What this may eventually help answer

If later records identify C. P. Farr and the rented building, the 1945 payments could help reconstruct City recreation facilities and clarify whether the word **recreation** in municipal finance records has any connection at all to the privately operated **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main**. At present there is no such connection.

The existing 505 chain remains:

- May 1925: 505 mapped as billiards/cigars, operator unnamed;
- Farr's Pool Hall at 505: official historic association, exact operating span unresolved;
- 1945: City historic-resource synthesis reports Harr's Recreation Center at 505, underlying page still needed;
- 11 February 1948: municipal text names Harr and McDougald as pool-hall operators without address;
- late 1948: municipal license text directly prints Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center / 505 Main.
