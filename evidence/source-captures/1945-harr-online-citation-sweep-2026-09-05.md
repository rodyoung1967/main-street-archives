## Current Oregon City historic-inventory guidance — forms are cumulative

Current official City page:

`https://www.orcity.org/1230/Historic-Resources`

Oregon City's current Historic Resources FAQ explains that historic inventory forms were produced under evolving SHPO frameworks and are **cumulative in nature**: a newer form does not replace an older form; together the forms document the evolution of a building.

Research consequence for 505 Main: do **not** request only the 2000 Michelle L. Dennis survey form. Request **all inventory-form versions for 505 Main**, together with photographs, attachments, source annotations, research worksheets, correspondence, and other retained supporting material. An older form may preserve the source note behind the 1941 / 1945 / 1953 business chronology even if a later form omits it.

This is a retrieval-method finding, not an occupant claim.
