## 1916-1917 Clackamas County and Oregon City directory — stronger provenance

Multiple National Register / historic-resource sources identify the directory as:

- **Sohns & Woodbeck, _Clackamas County and Oregon City Directory, 1916-1917_**
- Published in Oregon City.
- A copy is identified as **on file at the Oregon Historical Society, Portland**.

Sources:
- https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/c3bc601a-0c88-48a6-97a6-3edaf7b17d33
- https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/124d5261-922f-4dcb-af15-ede962ef045c/

A separate National Register nomination for the Willamette Falls Historic District cites the same directory for West Linn/Willamette resident listings and gives a page range of **178-191** for that section, demonstrating that the volume contains detailed locality listings rather than merely a business gazetteer.

Source:
- https://westlinnoregon.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/planning/page/54860/willamette_historic_district_national_register_of_historic_places_nomination.pdf

### Why this matters

This increases confidence that the 1916-17 directory is the highest-value unresolved source for 503 and 505 Main. The next acquisition target is the Oregon City street/business section, especially the Main Street sequence around 501-511.

## Contemporary evidence that street numbers were directory-available

The *Oregon City Courier*, 10 Oct 1918, ran a local-business knowledge feature instructing readers to give the street number when available, saying that the number was available when it was **"on the door or in the directory."** The same feature supplies many exact street addresses, including H. P. Brightbill at 509 Main and Wolf & Miller at 505 Main.

Source:
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-10-10/ed-1/seq-4/

This is not proof of the contents of the 1916-17 directory, but it independently confirms that Oregon City businesses were being cross-referenced against a local directory in this period.

## 1918 forward context for the 505 transition

The 10 Oct 1918 business feature confirms:

- **Wolf & Miller — 505 Main**
- **H. P. Brightbill — 509 Main**
- **Terk Bros. — 506 Main**

The odd/even address sequence is useful context when reconstructing the block, but Wolf & Miller remains a later 1918 arrival and should not be back-projected into 1916.
