# Gale City Directories of the United States — Oregon City missing-year / Reel 26 control

Date reviewed: **6 September 2026**

Status: **PUBLISHER AUTHOR-INDEX TEXT / MICROFILM-COVERAGE CONTROL; PDF PAGE IMAGE NOT RENDERED IN THIS INTERFACE. NO 1945 DIRECTORY NONEXISTENCE CLAIM.**

Purpose: refine the source-provenance question behind Oregon City's official-secondary statement placing **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main in 1945**. This capture records the coverage of Oregon City in Gale's *City Directories of the United States* microfilm author index and distinguishes a **missing year in this microfilm series** from proof that no directory or other source existed anywhere in that year.

## Publisher source

Gale / Cengage, **City Directories of the United States — Author Index**:

`https://assets.cengage.com/gale/psm/3065000A.pdf`

Current Gale collection/media-guide landing page:

`https://www.galesupport.com/psm/3065000`

The live publisher PDF is 736 pages. Its extracted text at PDF page marker **P561**, lines corresponding to the Oregon City entry, reads:

- **Oregon.**
- **Oregon City.**
- **1941-1950**
- **West Linn, Willamette, Bolton, Gladstone, Park Place, Clackamas Heights, And Canemah**;
- **Missing years: 1942, 1943, 1944, 1945, 1946, 1947, 1948, 1949, 1950, 1951, 1952, 1954, 1955, 1956, 1957, 1958, 1959.**
- **Reel: 26**

The index's range label is internally broader than the actual surviving years represented by the missing-year list. It therefore must be read as a **collection/reel coverage statement**, not as evidence that a directory was published annually throughout the printed range.

## Relationship to the City's historic-resource bibliography

The project's visually reviewed 2000 Downtown Re-Survey bibliography already identifies two separate Pacific Directory Service sources:

- **Directory of Oregon City, 1941**;
- **Directory of Clackamas County, 1948**.

It does **not** list a specifically titled 1945 directory, although the report labels the bibliography **Selected Bibliography**, so omission is not proof of non-use or nonexistence.

A separate 2011 Oregon City Historic Review Program update bibliography identifies:

- **Clackamas County Directory, 1947–48** — Ticor Title Co.;
- **Oregon City & Vicinity Directory, 1953** — T. W. Salisbury & Co.;
- **Polk's Oregon City Directory, 1960** — R. L. Polk & Co.

Official 2011 City report:

`https://oregon-city.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?clip_id=655&meta_id=31497&view_id=6`

Together, these official City bibliographies and the Gale author index support a more disciplined interpretation of the 1945 Harr chronology: the year **1945** in the City's property chronology should not automatically be converted into the publication year of a directory volume.

## Research consequence

The Gale index materially strengthens a bibliographic distinction already emerging in the project:

1. **1941 Oregon City** is a documented city-directory edition and a microfilm-series control.
2. **1947–48 / 1948 Clackamas County** is a separate county-directory source known to include Oregon City material and remains the highest-value unrecovered volume for Farr/Harr transition work.
3. **1945** is missing from Gale's Oregon City microfilm coverage and is not named as a directory title in the 2000 selected bibliography.
4. Therefore the City's statement **“Harr's Recreation Center — 1945”** must continue to be described as an **official-secondary directory-synthesis chronology point with exact underlying title/effective year/page unresolved**, not as a visually inspected “1945 Oregon City Directory” entry.

This does **not** prove the City's 1945 chronology is wrong. A consultant could have derived the date from a source not represented on Gale Reel 26, from a county directory with an effective/canvass date different from its title date, from an unpublished worksheet, or from another source class retained in the cumulative survey file.

## Reel 26 retrieval value

The author index gives a concrete control, **Reel 26**, for the Oregon City series. If a library or archive holds Gale's *City Directories of the United States* microfilm, Reel 26 can be used to retrieve and visually inspect the available Oregon City directory material without relying on Ancestry indexing.

However, because the publisher's own missing-year list includes **1945** and **1948**, Reel 26 is **not** a substitute for obtaining the separate **1947–48 Clackamas County Directory**. Its main value to the active 505 question is:

- verifying the 1941 Oregon City volume and its contents/page structure;
- checking whether any unexpected supplementary material appears on the reel;
- documenting the limits of this particular reproduced city-directory series.

## Evidence boundary

This finding establishes only what Gale's reproduced/microfilmed Oregon City directory series says it contains and omits. **A missing 1945 year on Gale Reel 26 is not evidence that no 1945 directory, county directory, business listing, license record, or consultant source existed.** Failed or absent catalog coverage remains a source-coverage statement, not negative historical evidence.
