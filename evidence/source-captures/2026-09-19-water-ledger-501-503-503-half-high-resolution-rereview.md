## Ledger structure correction

The repeated dark handwriting `G. A. Harding Agt.` is on the **Owner** lines. It is not the tenant/proprietor name. The much fainter handwriting on the associated **Tenant** lines is the occupant/business information being transcribed.

This distinction matters because the faint tenant writing can visually overlap the following owner line.

## Book 3 — 1914–1915

The visually confirmed sequence remains:

**511 Main → 509 Main → 507 Main → [unnumbered row] → 503 Main → 503½ Main → 501 Main**

### Possible 505 / vacancy

The unnumbered row between 507 and 503 carries the standalone `V` tenant notation. Independent comparison rows elsewhere in Book 3 strongly support `V` as vacant/vacancy notation.

Because 505 is the missing odd number in the numerical sequence, the row is the strongest current candidate for 505 Main. However, the ledger itself does **not** write `505`. Preserve:
- `V` = vacancy notation: **high-confidence visual interpretation**.
- blank row = 505 Main: **PROBABLE / WORKING HYPOTHESIS**, not direct exact-address evidence.
- therefore do not convert this into an unqualified statement that “505 Main was vacant.”

### 503 Main

503 is explicitly numbered and classified **Store**. The tenant handwriting remains unresolved after enlarged rereview.

During the conversation several candidate readings were tested against the handwriting, including L. Hylton and Schiermann. Neither survives as a responsible transcription from the ledger image alone. Preserve both only as independently known historical candidates associated with 503 at other dates; do not use either to force the ledger reading.

### 503½ Main — Fred Steiner

503½ is explicitly numbered and classified **Barber Shop**. On enlarged rereview, the project steward reads the faint tenant name as **Fred Steiner** and states confidence in that transcription.

The Book 4 image shows a very similar faint tenant entry at the same exact 503½ address. Cross-book comparison is compatible with **Fred Steiner** in both books.

Project handling:
- **Fred Steiner at 503½ — steward-verified/probable visual transcription supported by apparent repetition in Book 3 and Book 4.**
- The exact historical identity of this Fred Steiner remains unresolved.
- Do **not** automatically merge this tenant with the separately encountered Fred Steiner of Beaver Creek/Beavercreek without independent identity evidence.
- The transcription establishes what the ledger appears to say; it does not by itself establish that Steiner owned the barber business rather than being its tenant/operator.

### 501 Main

The enlarged Book 3 tenant line appears to contain a business wording ending in **Cafe**. The first word remains too faint to transcribe responsibly. Earlier model reading “Clyde Cole” is explicitly withdrawn.

Preserve Book 3 501 tenant as **[uncertain] Cafe** only as a visual lead. The layered Restaurant/Saloon classification remains as previously recorded.

### 503

A later 19 September steward rereview identifies **C. Schoenheinz** as the closest reading of the faint tenant signature/name and assigns a high probability to that visual transcription. Contemporary newspaper research independently establishes a Charles Schoenheinz in Oregon City in 1916 and a commercial interest in the Theroux music store by May 1917. However, Theroux is repeatedly advertised at **519 Main** during 1916, and no independent source recovered yet prints Schoenheinz at 503. Therefore record **C. Schoenheinz** as the high-confidence steward reading / probable transcription at 503. **Charles Schoenheinz** is a separate identity hypothesis, and a **shoe-store use at 503** is an additional plausible but unproven hypothesis based on the earlier 1911 C. Schoenheinz shoe-shop evidence. Earlier Hylton and Schiermann readings remain withdrawn. See `evidence/source-captures/2026-09-19-charles-schoenheinz-503-main-tenant-hypothesis.md`.

### 501 — Pollock / Fifth Street Restaurant

The 501 tenant surname is now read confidently as **Pollock**. The initials before Pollock remain difficult and are intentionally left **unclear** rather than normalized from later evidence.

A diagonal handwritten annotation is read as **“5th St Restaurant” / “Fifth Street Restaurant”** with high confidence.

This materially improves the prior Book 4 capture, which recorded only a Restaurant reading and an unresolved nearby “5th” notation.

Project handling:
- **501 Main — [initials unclear] Pollock — Fifth Street Restaurant**: strong visual reading from the municipal ledger.
- Later independent evidence associates **W. W. Pollock** with Fifth Street Restaurant in 1919, but the Book 4 initials must not be silently changed to W. W. unless the initials are independently resolved.
- This is an important continuity lead, not yet proof that the Book 4 Pollock and 1919 W. W. Pollock are the same person.
