### Why this matters

If **1950** accurately dates construction of the surviving 145 Main building, then Raye could not have worked in the **present building** during a proposed 1944–1947 Morton stop. That would not, by itself, disprove the family memory. Plausible alternatives remain:

1. the assessor-derived 1950 year is approximate, rounded, or otherwise inaccurate;
2. a previous commercial building stood on the same Lots 4–5 and was replaced about 1950;
3. an earlier Wheel Cafe operated at another Morton address before moving to 145 Main;
4. the business did not yet exist during the LaFarlette family's possible Morton interval, meaning the remembered work/business chronology is shifted;
5. the family Morton chronology itself is inaccurate despite the two-family-member agreement.

Do **not** choose among these alternatives until the original parcel card and early business/newspaper records are inspected.

## Earliest Wheel business evidence remains later than the proposed Morton interval
