Section **3.4 City Directory Listings** states that EDR-supplied city-directory listings for **1953, 1960, 1964, 1969, 1974, 1979, 1984, 1987, 1992, 1995, 2000, 2005, 2010, 2014, 2017, and 2020** were reviewed and that a copy of the **EDR-City Directory Abstract** is included in **Appendix D**.

The same visually inspected page gives examples including **502/506 Main St.** dry-cleaning listings beginning in **1953**, demonstrating that the EDR research did include the 500 block of Main Street and the target year.

Classification for these statements: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT — VISUALLY VERIFIED SECONDARY/DERIVATIVE DIRECTORY RESEARCH**. The report is not the 1953 directory itself; EDR's abstract is a derivative city-directory product and should not supersede visual inspection of the original Salisbury directory.

## What the shortcut can and cannot do

The report proves that a consultant obtained and reviewed an EDR directory abstract that included **1953 Oregon City listings** and that the 500 block was within the study coverage. If the omitted Appendix D can be recovered from another City/ODOT project-file package, it may provide a fast address-level transcription for 503 and 505.

However:

- EDR abstracts can omit listings or normalize names/addresses;
- the report's body only selected environmentally notable listings and therefore silence about 503/505 in section 3.4 is not meaningful;
- even a recovered EDR entry should be treated as a **derivative directory transcription** until checked against the original directory image.
