### New photograph-research lead — Burmeister & Andresen display

Page 8 contains the article **“B. & A. Picture Display Viewed With Interest.”** Visual inspection confirms the article. It reports that George Hankins arranged a Burmeister & Andresen display window containing historical photographs of Oregon City loaned by local residents and the city. The described material includes:
- photographs loaned by Mrs. Anne Jones, including 1890-flood material;
- photographs loaned by Robert Wilson;
- a West Side photograph from Mrs. W. E. Pratt;
- several photographs of sections of Oregon City supplied by Weldon Fuge;
- an old Oregon City photograph from the Miller family, loaned by Miss Rose Miller;
- city-owned photographs of former mayors;
- Booster Day photographs loaned by the publicity department.

This does **not** prove that a photograph of 503/505 was in the display. It is a useful archival provenance lead because the article identifies several 1921 custodians/collections of historic Oregon City photographs that may survive in later institutional collections. It is especially relevant to the project's search for pre-July-1922 views of the 500 block.

## Important annual-source-universe correction

The 1921 audit must not be limited to the weekly Oregon City Enterprise. Historic Oregon Newspapers' title history confirms that the **Morning Enterprise** daily was published 1911–1933 and is a relevant 1921 online newspaper source. The annual completeness inventory therefore must include the 1921 Morning Enterprise run in addition to the weekly Enterprise and any other relevant online titles identified under the research standard.

No ONLINE COMPLETE claim is appropriate until that daily run and the rest of the required source universe are inventoried and reviewed.
