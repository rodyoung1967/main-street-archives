## 2. Two OCR/table-alignment interpretations were visually rejected

A text/table extraction from the City of Oregon City's 2000 downtown historic-resource report appeared to associate **Pantorium Cleaners** with `Willamette River — 1922`.

Visual inspection of the actual table layout showed that interpretation was **wrong**: the `Willamette River — 1922` fields belonged to the **Oregon City–West Linn Bridge** row, not Pantorium.

The Pantorium row instead belongs to a different address/date entry (the table extraction had shifted columns/rows). Therefore:

- do **not** cite the 2000 report as evidence that Pantorium was on the Willamette River in 1922;
- do **not** use that malformed extraction to place Pantorium near the July 1922 ignition room;
- the exact pre-fire Pantorium address remains unresolved in the fire reconstruction unless established by a separate source.

### Latourette-David-Barlow / 503 Main

A second extracted-table alignment initially appeared to label **503 Main** as the **Latourette-David-Barlow Building**.

Visual inspection of the actual report page showed that interpretation was also **wrong**. The report associates the **Latourette-David-Barlow Building with 504–514 Main**, while **503 Main is a separate entry associated with Kwality Cafe / circa 1919 context**.

Therefore:

- do **not** use the Latourette-David-Barlow name to map the Barlow/David ownership thread directly onto 503 Main;
- do **not** infer that Mary E. Barlow / Juliette E. David's 1913 Block 26 ownership proves 503 ownership solely from that building name;
- preserve the Barlow/David 1913 legal-lot evidence separately from later historic-resource building-name assignments.

These rejected interpretations are worth preserving because they are exactly the sort of row/column OCR error that can recur in future searches.
