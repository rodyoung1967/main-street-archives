## OCR observations (discovery only)
| Date | Printed address as OCR transcribed | Other relevant OCR wording |
|---|---|---|
| 24 Aug, p. 2 | 419 Main Street | 'The New Location Of MILLER'S SHOE STORE'; Gilbert Miller signature; purchase of H. S. Goldman secondhand stock; installing shoe-repair machinery |
| 7 Sep, p. 2 | 419 Main Street | 'The New Location Of Miller's Shoe Store'; Gilbert Miller signature; closing out Goldman stock; installing shoe-repair machinery |
| 21 Sep, p. 5 | 209 Fifth Street | Gilbert Miller signature; 'Formerly Goldman's Second-Hand Store'; shoe machinery set up and ready for same shoe repairing as before fire |
| 26 Oct, p. 2 | 419 Main St. | Miller's Shoe Store; expert shoe repairing; ad does not print Gilbert's first name |

The 24 August and 7 September text strongly suggests a continuing 419 Main operation. The 21 September '209 Fifth Street' is anomalous in that context. **It is not safe to conclude that Miller moved 419 Main → 209 Fifth → 419 Main.** Possible explanations include a second premises, a printed-address error, or OCR column/advertisement merging. Do not choose among them until the **21 September original page** is examined at sufficient resolution to establish the advertisement's physical boundaries.
