# 1912 February Main Street construction-candidate screen

Date: 8 September 2026  
Status: **IN PROGRESS — RL-038 / CONSTRUCTION-WATCH CHECKPOINT**

Purpose: test named February 1912 Main Street building projects against the unresolved 10 May 1912 *Courier* statement that buildings were then being torn down on Main Street and new buildings built in their places (`E-090` / `RL-038`). This checkpoint narrows candidate geography; it does not identify the buildings referenced by the 10 May editorial.

## 9 February 1912 Courier construction roundup

The 9 February *Oregon City Courier* front page, under a building-boom headline, lists multiple planned/active projects. The Main Street items include:

1. **Mt. Hood Brewing Co. business block at Main and Eighth** — the paper says work was expected to start about the first of March on a two-story cement business block extending from the Eighth Street corner along Main.
2. **Williams Bros. transfer-office property** — negotiations were underway for the Main Street property occupied by Williams Bros. Transfer Co. and an adjoining barber shop, with a modern business place planned.
3. A reported possible Frank Busch building on the lot north of his store, explicitly presented as report/rumor rather than a confirmed project.
4. V. Harris apartment construction at Fourteenth and Main.

Canonical 9 February page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-02-09/ed-1/seq-1/

## Williams Bros. property geographically resolved outside the target frontage

A 12 January 1912 *Oregon City Enterprise* advertisement directly places **Williams Bros. Transfer Co. at 612 Main Street**. The same exact address repeats in later 1912 Enterprise advertising, including 16 August.

Canonical controls:
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1912-01-12/ed-1/seq-7/
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1912-08-16/ed-1/seq-4/

Therefore the February Williams-property redevelopment proposal is **not a 501/503/505/507/509/511 target-frontage project**. It is useful as a named 1912 Main Street redevelopment candidate but cannot be used as evidence that the Fifth–Sixth odd-numbered frontage was being demolished.

The later 612 advertisement does **not** by itself prove that no construction occurred: Williams Bros. could theoretically have remained through construction, temporarily moved, or returned to a replacement/shared building. The archive therefore preserves only the safe conclusion that the planned property was at 612 Main and outside the target frontage.

## 612 Main multiple-use caution

July 1912 *Oregon Daily Journal* advertising also places **W. F. Schooley & Co. at 612 Main Street**. This shows that 612 cannot be treated as a one-business/one-premises inference without additional building/storefront evidence.

Canonical example:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn85042444/1912-07-03/ed-1/seq-14/

## Mt. Hood Brewing project also outside the target Fifth–Sixth frontage

The 9 February article explicitly locates the Mt. Hood Brewing Co. proposed block at **Main and Eighth**, also outside the 501–511 Fifth–Sixth target frontage. Its construction history may help explain the citywide 10 May editorial but cannot be assigned to the target buildings.

Later evidence of a new business block at Main/Eighth must be handled separately from whether this specific February project began on schedule; no completion claim is promoted from this checkpoint.

## Consequence for E-090 / RL-038

This screen narrows two named February Main Street redevelopment candidates away from the Fifth–Sixth target frontage:

- Williams Bros. property — **612 Main**;
- Mt. Hood Brewing project — **Main & Eighth**.

It does **not** prove that the 10 May editorial referred only to those projects, and it does not exclude simultaneous demolition/rebuilding on the target frontage. `RL-038` remains open.

No new `E-###` or `S-###` is created from this checkpoint because the central historical claim remains the already-registered citywide structural-change statement `E-090`; this file records candidate testing and geographic exclusion only.
