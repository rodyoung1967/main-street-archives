Date reviewed: **7 September 2026**

Purpose: add a county-record route for the unresolved **Harr's Recreation Center → Well's Recreation / 1955 sale** interval at **505 Main Street, Oregon City** without conflating this series with the separate assumed-business-name registers.

## Official inventory

The Oregon State Archives **Clackamas County Inventory** describes the **Clerk and Recorder Miscellaneous Recordings** series (1845–1974) as including, among other document types:

- agreements;
- partnership dissolutions;
- contracts;
- powers of attorney;
- liens;
- leases; and
- affidavits of publication.

The inventory says the records identify the date filed, the person filing, the type of action, and the volume/page where recorded.

For the target period it specifically identifies:

- **Miscellaneous Index, 1948–1974** — 2 volumes at Clackamas County Archives;
- **Miscellaneous Record, vol. 29, 1953–1958** — 1 volume at Oregon State Archives;
- related public-research microfilm/jacket holdings at the Clackamas County Clerk's Recording Office; and
- security microfilm for Miscellaneous Records vol. 1–35, 1850–1967.

Official inventory:
https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/county-records-guide/Pages/clackamas-inventory.aspx

## Why this matters for 505 Main

The dedicated Clackamas assumed-business-name inventory has a listed online/holdings gap between the 1928–1935 certificates and the 1959–1964 record volumes. The Miscellaneous Record must **not** be represented as a substitute assumed-name register: Clackamas already maintained a separate assumed-business-name series beginning in 1928, and no source recovered here says Well's Recreation was filed in Miscellaneous Record vol. 29.

However, the miscellaneous series is independently useful because a 505 business transition could have generated a recorded **partnership dissolution, agreement, lease, contract, power of attorney, lien, or affidavit of publication** even if the missing assumed-name volume cannot currently be located.

## Exact search targets

Inspect the **1948–1974 Miscellaneous Index** first, then pull any referenced 1953–1958 instrument. Search separately for:

- Carl R. Harr / Carl Harr;
- Harr's Recreation Center;
- Well / Well's / Wells / Welle / Welsh;
- Well's Recreation / Wells Recreation and spelling variants;
- **505 Main Street**, Oregon City;
- any named party tied by the underlying record to the 505 pool / beer / recreation business.

Do not merge a surname candidate with the 505 business unless the indexed or underlying instrument provides the bridge.

## Evidence boundary

No indexed name hit or underlying instrument for Harr, Well's Recreation, or 505 Main was recovered online in this pass. This route establishes **surviving record-series coverage and a targeted retrieval method only**. A failed index search or missing instrument remains a retrieval result, not evidence that no transfer, partnership, lease, or other business transaction occurred.
