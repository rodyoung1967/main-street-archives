# 4 May 1910 council minutes — pool/billiard premises detail at Fifth & Main

Status: **PRIMARY MUNICIPAL RECORD VISUALLY/TRANSCRIPTION-VERIFIED; methodological precedent only for 1922 research**

Purpose: document that Oregon City council records in the relevant historical record series can identify pool/billiard operations at a specific intersection and even describe placement within a premises. This supports targeted retrieval of the missing 1922 pool-hall license/minute records. It does **not** establish continuity from this 1910 operation to any 1922 pool hall.

## Source

City of Oregon City, **City Council Minutes, Volume 7**, meeting of **4 May 1910**, Volume 7 p. 135/136 (digital PDF pages 135–136).

## Directly supported fact

At the 4 May 1910 regular council meeting, the minutes record that the petition of **M. Justin** asked permission to operate:

- a soft-drink concern; and
- **pool & billiard tables**;
- **back of his saloon at 5th & Main Streets**;
- on Sunday.

The petition was read and tabled.

The wording is especially useful because it describes not merely an operator but the **specific Fifth/Main premises relationship**, including that the pool/billiard tables were behind the saloon.

## Why this matters to the 1922 fire research

The July 1922 fire began in a Main Street room adjoining an unnamed pool hall. Current 1922 candidates include separate evidence streams for Timms Pool Hall, Leland & Little and the Wheeler pool-room chain.

This 1910 council record demonstrates that Oregon City municipal minutes/petitions can preserve the kind of **premises-level pool/billiard detail** capable of resolving the 1922 address problem if the corresponding 1921–1923 applications, petitions, ordinance files or minutes can be recovered.

A separate visually reviewed Banner-Courier item establishes that the council acted on Sunday poolroom opening on **1 February 1922**. That meeting packet remains a priority target.

## What this source does NOT establish

Do not use this 1910 record to claim that:

- M. Justin's pool/billiard operation survived to 1922;
- M. Justin's premises was 501, 503 or 505 Main;
- the 1910 Fifth/Main pool tables were in the later fire-adjacent pool hall;
- Timms, Leland & Little, Wheeler, or any 1922 operator succeeded M. Justin;
- the same building stood unchanged from 1910 to 1922.

This is a **source-method precedent**, not a business-continuity bridge.
