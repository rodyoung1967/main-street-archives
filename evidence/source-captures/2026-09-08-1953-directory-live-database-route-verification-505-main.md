## Scope

This continuation stayed on the **505 Main Farr → Harr → Well's Recreation** chronology and tested whether the known 1953 T. W. Salisbury directory could be reached through current public database-routing metadata without institutional outreach or paid research.

## Verified live routes

Independent current locality-directory routing confirms that the **1953 Oregon City directory** is represented through two genealogy database routes:

- **Ancestry** — the Oregon City 1953 locality link resolves into **U.S., City Directories, 1822–1995**, collection/database ID **2469**.

These route identifiers are retrieval provenance only. They do **not** substitute for inspection of the original 1953 directory page.

A separate current Ancestry Oregon-specific city-directory collection can resolve under a different collection identifier; that does not supersede the locality link above for the known 1953 Oregon City route. ## What was not recovered

This pass did **not** recover or visually inspect:

- the title/front matter of the 1953 T. W. Salisbury *Oregon City & Vicinity Directory*;
- the street-guide entry for **505 Main**;
- the alphabetical entry for **Well's Recreation** or spelling variants;
- a proprietor/manager notation;
- a telephone number for the business;
- subscriber **9179**; or
- the complete 501–511 odd Main Street address block.

The visually inspected May 2000 Oregon SHPO resource form therefore remains the current source for the official-secondary statement that the **1953 city directory lists “Well's Recreation” at 505 Main**. The underlying directory wording and proprietor remain unresolved.

## Interpretation boundary

Do not infer from these database routes:

- that the original directory spells the business exactly **Well's Recreation**;
- that the proprietor's surname was Well or Wells;
- that any person named Wells found in genealogy records operated 505 Main;
- that telephone **9179** belonged to the business in 1953;
- a Harr → Well transition date; or
- business ownership, tenancy, building ownership, liquor-license ownership, or sale completion.
