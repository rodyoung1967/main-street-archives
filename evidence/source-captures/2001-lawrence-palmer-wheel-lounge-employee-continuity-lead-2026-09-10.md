## Source recovered

A publicly indexed Palmer-family genealogy page reproduces text attributed to **The Oregonian, 18 February 2001**, obituary for **Lawrence Ivan Palmer Jr.**

Public page:
https://www.oregonpalmerfamily.org/p2092.htm

The reproduced obituary says Palmer moved from Yakima to Oregon City in **1972**, worked as a Sears salesman until the **early 1980s**, and then worked as a **janitor for The Wheel Lounge in Oregon City until retiring in the early 1990s**.

The original Oregonian obituary page/scan was **not** recovered or visually inspected in this pass.
