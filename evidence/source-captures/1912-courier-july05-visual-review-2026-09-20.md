## Matt Justin → Frank Uderman transfer — handling

The 5 July Courier directly documents a business/license transition but not its location. A same-event 4 July *Morning Enterprise* report likewise says Justin sold his saloon to Uderman and requested the license transfer, again without an address.

A later 28 March 1913 *Morning Enterprise* liquor-license notice is indexed as placing **Frank Uderman's place of business at 520 Main Street**. That later item was used only as a discovery lead in this pass and was **not visually reviewed here**. Do not backdate 520 Main to July 1912 without a direct contemporaneous address bridge.

Accordingly, no target-address claim or new entity merger is created from the July 5 transfer item.
