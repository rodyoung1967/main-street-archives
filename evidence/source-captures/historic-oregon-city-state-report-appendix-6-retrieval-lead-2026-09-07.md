# `Historic Oregon City` — State of Oregon Appendix 6 photograph retrieval lead

Date reviewed: **7 September 2026**

Status: **OFFICIAL INDEXED-TEXT RETRIEVAL LEAD / VISUAL RENDER GAP — NO FLOR, KWALITY, WHEEL, OWNERSHIP OR 503 CLAIM ESTABLISHED**

## Why this was checked

The City of Oregon City's 503 Main historic-resource narrative says that the restaurant/cocktail-bar operation under **Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor** was still at the site in **1957 when it appeared in the `Historic Oregon City` publication**. The City's 2000 Downtown Final Report separately cites **_Historic Oregon City_ (no publisher), 1957**.

A separate State of Oregon document surfaced another potentially useful surviving-copy route for the same publication family.

## State report indexed-text lead

Current official web indexing for an Oregon Employment Department Government-to-Government report contains the exact inventory wording:

**`Publication (undated) titled Historic Oregon City. See Appendix 6 for photographs of this item.`**

Official routes:

- Oregon Employment Department: https://www.oregon.gov/employ/Agency/Documents/2023/2023-12-Gov2GovReport.pdf
- Oregon Legislature mirror: https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/cis/GovToGovReports/Annual%20Report%20%28Employment%29%202023.pdf

The report PDF is approximately 10.6 MB. In the current research environment, both official PDF routes exceeded the web renderer's fetch limit, so **Appendix 6 itself was not visually inspected**. The indexed/search result exposes the quoted wording but not enough page context to identify the pictured edition, publisher, date, owner/custodian, or whether the photographs show interior business pages.

Classification: **OFFICIAL INDEXED TEXT / VISUAL RENDER RETRIEVAL GAP**. The wording establishes a State-document lead to an undated physical/publication item and claimed Appendix 6 photographs. It does **not** prove that this copy is the City's cited 1957 edition or that it contains Flor/Kwality/503 material.

## Research value

If the Appendix 6 photographs can be recovered, compare them visually against:

- the exact City bibliography target **_Historic Oregon City_ (no publisher), 1957**;
- Oregon Historical Society **Mss 6000, Box/Folder 16/4**, which holds related 1948 / circa-1954 / circa-1955 / undated `Historic Oregon City` material;
- the commercially photographed **_Historic Oregon City: Industry, Commerce, Agriculture, Recreation_** copy described as undated / circa 1955.

Useful comparison points include cover design, subtitle, page count/layout, business advertisements, publisher/Chamber wording, colophon, restaurant/cocktail-bar pages, and any **Flor / Kwality Cafe / 503 Main** text.

Do not infer edition identity from similar artwork or title alone. A visual or bibliographic bridge is required.

## Current evidence boundary

This lead changes **source routing only**. It does not alter the defensible chronology:

- 1957 — official-secondary City synthesis places Mr./Mrs. Albert D. Flor with the 503 restaurant/cocktail-bar operation and cites a `Historic Oregon City` publication;
- by 28 March 1958 — visually verified primary advertising places **Wheel Cafe & Lounge, 503 Main St., phone 9106** at the address;
- the identity/ownership/rename/transfer mechanism between those controls remains unresolved.

Failed visual retrieval of Appendix 6 is a **retrieval gap, not negative evidence**.
