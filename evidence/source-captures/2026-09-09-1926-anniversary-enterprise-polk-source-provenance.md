## Purpose and evidentiary boundary

This continuation narrows the unresolved statement in the City historic-resource material that **1926 newspaper advertisements identify the 503 Main restaurant as the Kwality Restaurant**. The underlying advertisement still has not been recovered or visually inspected, so this record does **not** create a new direct 1926 Kwality-at-503 claim, does not identify a proprietor, and does not bridge the unnamed May 1925 Sanborn restaurant to Kwality.

The useful result is source provenance: the project now has a much tighter candidate newspaper date/title and verified surviving microfilm routes, together with stronger catalog controls for the 1925–1926 Polk source class.

No institutional inquiry, research request, paid lookup, account sign-in, or restricted-access action was sent or authorized in this pass.

## 1. City 503 historic-resource sheet — visually rechecked; no hidden ad locator

Official 2022 Oregon City public-meeting attachment containing the City Planning 503 Main historic-resource text:

https://mccmeetingspublic.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/oregoncity-meet-73673237b9124664a790d047658a9813/ITEM-Attachment-001-49626a7ef29942e0be519c46b2f656fc.pdf

The relevant Exhibit A page was visually inspected from the actual PDF image. It directly reproduces the City Planning text headed **`503 Main Street - Kwality Café`** and says, in substance, that:

- the building appears on the **1925 Sanborn** as a restaurant;
- it appears in **1926 newspaper ads as the Kwality Restaurant**;
- a **1947 business directory** uses Kwality Cafe;
- Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor were still managing the restaurant/cocktail bar there in 1957;
- 1983 survey research reported Wolf-family property ownership approximately 1922–1972.

For this continuation, the important negative bibliographic finding is bounded and explicit: **the reproduced 503 sheet gives no exact 1926 newspaper title, advertisement date, page number, advertiser/proprietor name, or footnote for the 1926-ad statement.** The underlying advertisement therefore remains unrecovered. This is not evidence that the ad did not survive.

## 3. Museum of the Oregon Territory vertical-file citation clarifies the masthead/source family

A scholarly thesis bibliography provides a more exact archival citation for at least one article from the same date:

https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=ef7b625ce7f74d997038aca5e65cb18010195489&repid=rep1&type=pdf

The bibliography cites the **Museum of the Oregon Territory Library, 211 Tumwater Dr., Oregon City**, Vertical Files: Agriculture, for an article titled **`Farming Has Steady Growth`** in the **Morning Enterprise**, Oregon City, **October 27, 1926**, with volume notation **`Volume 32 (LXI)`**.

Evidence handling:

- This is **BIBLIOGRAPHIC / SOURCE-PROVENANCE EVIDENCE**, not visual inspection of the 1926 newspaper page.
- It establishes that at least one article from the exact 27 October 1926 issue was preserved/cited through a Museum of the Oregon Territory vertical file when the thesis research was conducted.
- It does **not** prove that the Museum currently holds the complete anniversary edition rather than an article clipping.
- It does not identify Kwality, 503 Main, 505 Main, Farr, or any target proprietor.
- The difference between the City's shorthand `Oregon City Enterprise` wording and the thesis's more specific `Morning Enterprise` masthead must be preserved rather than silently normalized. Both weekly and daily Enterprise titles existed in this period.

## 7. Consequence for MF-031 / newspaper recovery strategy

The 1923–1926 local-newspaper gap should now be treated as a **known physical-microfilm retrieval target**, not a general question about whether the newspapers survive.

Highest-value first inspection:

1. **27 October 1926 Morning Enterprise**, using the thesis citation `Volume 32 (LXI)` as a source-control clue; inspect the entire anniversary issue page by page.
2. Also check the corresponding **weekly Oregon City Enterprise** issue(s) around the same date because the City's bibliography uses the generic Enterprise title and the two title streams coexisted.
3. Search every visually inspected page for `Kwality`, `Quality`, `503`, `505`, `Farr`, `Jager`, `Leland`, `Little`, `Timms`, restaurant/cafe, billiards/pool, cigars/tobacco, business-sale/move/lease language, and target-block advertising.
4. If Kwality is found, preserve the full page image and record exact masthead, date, page/image number, trade style, street address, proprietor/manager wording, phone, neighboring advertisers, and any opening/new-management language.
5. Do not infer that the anniversary issue contains the City survey's cited advertisement merely because the date is attractive; it is a prioritized test, not a result.

No new `MF-###` item is needed. This refines existing **MF-031** rather than creating duplicate manual work.
