# 1922 March newspaper visual review — 13 September 2026

## Scope and method

This checkpoint records a page-by-page visual review of the complete preserved March 1922 local newspaper corpus currently in `main` for the 503/505 Main Street annual audit. It is a newspaper-review checkpoint, not an annual closeout.

The review used the original page PDFs already preserved under `newspapers/banner-courier/1922-03/` and `newspapers/oregon-city-enterprise/1922-03/`. The PDFs were rendered to page JPEGs from the preserved repository copies and every rendered page was visually inspected. OCR/search text was used only as a discovery aid and did not earn visual-review credit.

No page was credited merely because OCR text existed. No absence from these newspapers is treated as proof of vacancy, closure, demolition, relocation, non-use, ownership, or non-ownership.

## Verified finding relevant to the Fifth/Main fire reconstruction

A recurring Banner-Courier display advertisement visually confirms **S. F. Scripture, general blacksmithing, at 108 Fifth St., between Main and Water Streets**, telephone **276-W**, by **2 March 1922**. The same address/business advertisement was visually confirmed again on **9 March** and **16 March 1922**, and the recurring ad is also visible later in the March run.

This materially sharpens the existing Scripture chronology: Scripture was operating at 108 Fifth by the beginning of March 1922, not merely by the previously retained June 1922 checkpoint. This is evidence of Scripture's business location and operation only. It does **not** prove property ownership, tenancy terms, building identity, continuity through the July fire date, or any relationship to the fire-adjacent pool hall.

Visual source examples:

- The Banner-Courier, 2 March 1922, p. 6 / archive sequence 6 — S. F. Scripture advertisement: `108 Fifth St. Between Main and Water Sts.`
- The Banner-Courier, 9 March 1922, p. 5 / archive sequence 5 — same location and business description.
- The Banner-Courier, 16 March 1922, p. 6 / archive sequence 6 — same location and business description.

The March run also repeatedly carries Miller's Shoe Store advertising locating that business on Main Street between Sixth and Seventh; that does not place it at 503/505 and is not treated as target-address evidence.
