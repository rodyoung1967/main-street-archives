## Steward decision

The prior 1921 ONLINE COMPLETE certification is withdrawn. The year remains **IN PROGRESS** until the 1921 *Banner-Courier* is brought through the annual-audit standard rather than treated only as a manual callout.

## January 1921 issue expectation

The *Banner-Courier* was a weekly Oregon City newspaper. A January 1921 run would ordinarily be expected on Thursdays **6, 13, 20 and 27 January 1921**, subject to confirmation from the actual reel/issue inventory. These dates are an expected inventory, **not a claim that all four issues survive**.

## Retrieval work performed

1. Rechecked Historic Oregon Newspapers / ODNP title coverage. Its public *Banner-Courier* title record contains **52 issues, 5 Jan.–28 Dec. 1922**, not 1921.
2. Rechecked Oregon City's official digitization description. It explicitly lists the digitized *Banner-Courier* as **5 Jan.–28 Dec. 1922**.
3. Rechecked Oregon City Public Library's microfilm holdings. The Library states it holds **Oregon City Banner Courier, 17 Jul. 1919–28 Feb. 1950**. Thus January 1921 lies inside the stated microfilm holding.
4. Checked NewspaperArchive's Oregon City *Banner Courier* collection. Its public collection page reports 124 issues / 799 pages across a broad 1901–1922 label, but the public index/search did not expose a reliable January 1921 issue/page set suitable for page-by-page visual verification in this pass.

## Continued retrieval findings — 20 September 2026

A broader web/index search was run for each expected January date (6, 13, 20, 27 January 1921), the title, and Oregon City. It did **not** surface any January 1921 *Banner-Courier* page image.

NewspaperArchive's title landing page currently describes its Oregon City *Banner Courier* holdings as **124 issues / 799 pages** under a broad **1901–1922** label. Its publicly surfaced popular pages and clippings are overwhelmingly 1905 and 1922 examples; this broad label is therefore **not evidence of a complete 1921 run**. No January 1921 issue was exposed through the public title index in this pass.

An independent Oregon-newspaper aggregation likewise identifies *Banner-Courier* as an Oregon City title, 1919–1950, while identifying the Historic Oregon Newspapers online run specifically as **5 January–28 December 1922**. This corroborates the online-image boundary rather than filling the 1921 gap.

Contemporary independent evidence confirms that the title was actively publishing in January 1921: the Eugene *Morning Register* of **14 January 1921**, page 8, lists **W. E. Hassler, editor, Oregon City Banner-Courier**, among attendees expected at the University editors' conference. This is title-existence/editor evidence only; it does not establish the exact January issue inventory or page counts.
