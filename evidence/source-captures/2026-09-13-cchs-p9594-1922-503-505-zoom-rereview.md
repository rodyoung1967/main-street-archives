## Revised visual reading

The earlier project reading treated the light-toned Fifth/Main corner-zone building as a strong candidate for the Gates fire-replacement and left open whether it represented 501 alone or 501+503.

Rodney's closer spatial identification changes the preferred working interpretation:

1. The **light/white building on the left is identified by the project steward as 501 Main**.
2. Immediately to its right there appears to be a **visible separation / open or low area** before the next substantial building mass.
3. On this mapping, that intervening position corresponds to **503 Main**.
4. The substantial building farther to the right, much of it in shadow, corresponds to **505 Main** and appears compatible with a newer/replacement structure.

The image resolution and oblique viewpoint do not justify converting the apparent separation into direct proof of a vacant 503 parcel. Facade lines, roof geometry, perspective, and shadows remain capable of producing a false gap. Nevertheless, the enlarged view is sufficiently inconsistent with the earlier 501+503 reading that the archive should no longer use `P-9594` as affirmative evidence that a surviving/replacement 503 structure was already present by November 1922.

## Evidence classification

- **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT:** CCHS object identity and November 1922 catalog description; broad physical scene visible in the photograph.
- **PROJECT-STEWARD SPATIAL IDENTIFICATION:** light building at left = 501 Main; shadowed substantial building farther right = 505 Main.
- **PROBABLE / WORKING HYPOTHESIS:** apparent intervening open/separated position is the 503 Main frontage and may indicate that the later 503 building was not yet standing.
- **UNRESOLVED CONFLICT:** this interpretation conflicts with any use of the City's `c.1919` historic-inventory date as proof that the surviving 503 structure was already built by November 1922.

## Consequences for the construction chronology

This re-review **withdraws the earlier project inference that `IMG-0760` proves both 503 and 505 had been rebuilt by November 1922**.

Current working structural chronology should instead keep 503 and 505 separate:

- **505 Main:** `P-9594` is compatible with a substantial newer/replacement building being present by November 1922, but the exact building identity and continuity to the later 505 structure still require corroboration.
- **503 Main:** `P-9594` no longer supplies a November 1922 positive building endpoint. The apparent gap raises a serious possibility that the later structure was erected after this photograph.
- **1925 control:** the visually verified May 1925 Sanborn maps a separate **503 restaurant** and **505 billiards/cigars** occupancy. If the steward's 1922 spatial mapping is correct and if the 1925 mapped 503 structure is the relevant later building, then the change occurred sometime **after November 1922 and by May 1925**. That is a hypothesis-dependent bracket, not an established construction date.
- **City c.1919 date:** retain as a visually verified historic-inventory estimate, but classify the photographic tension explicitly rather than using the estimate as primary construction proof.

## What would disprove or refine this reading

High-value tests are:

1. Obtain a higher-resolution scan or original print of CCHS `P-9594` and inspect the 501/503/505 facade and roof boundaries.
2. Compare the exact roof/facade geometry against **CCHS photograph catalog number 267** (1924–1928), the 1925 Sanborn, the 1929 aerial, and later securely identified views.
3. Retrieve 1922–1925 permits, assessment/improvement records, deeds, and fire-replacement records for Block 4 Lots 3–4.
4. Keep the August 1922 Gates two-story hollow-tile reconstruction report separate until the premises geometry proves whether it covered modern 501 alone, 501+503, or another frontage division.

## Propagation decision

This finding materially changes the **structural-history interpretation**, but it does not establish a new occupant, proprietor, owner, licensee, or trade name. It should therefore propagate as a correction/caveat to `E-213`, 503/505 structural notes, media metadata for `IMG-0760`, and the construction-date/open-question research trail. No business-ownership or occupancy relationship should be manufactured from the photograph.
