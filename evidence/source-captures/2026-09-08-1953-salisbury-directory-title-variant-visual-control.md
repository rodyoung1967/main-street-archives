# 1953 Salisbury Oregon City directory — visually verified title-variant control

Date reviewed: **8 September 2026**

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED SECONDARY BIBLIOGRAPHIC CONTROL / NO 505 OCCUPANT OR PROPRIETOR CLAIM CREATED.**

Purpose: preserve two useful citation/title variants for the unrecovered 1953 T. W. Salisbury directory so catalog and holdings searches can use wording actually printed in a later National Register nomination rather than relying on one normalized project title.

## Source visually inspected

West Linn Historic City Hall National Register nomination / listing packet, 2021, hosted by the City of West Linn:

`https://westlinnoregon.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/planning/page/54301/listing_notice_-_historic_city_hall.pdf`

The original PDF was opened and the relevant pages were visually inspected.

### PDF page 19 / printed nomination page 18

Footnote 58 visibly cites:

**`Pacific Directory Service 1941 Directory: Oregon City-West Linn-Gladstone, and Salisbury’s Oregon City, Gladstone, West Linn City Directory, 1953.`**

This supplies a useful alternate title/coverage form for the same 1953 Salisbury directory already identified elsewhere in official Oregon City preservation documentation as **_Oregon City & Vicinity Directory, 1953_ — T. W. Salisbury & Co.**

### PDF page 20 / printed nomination page 19

Footnote 66 visibly cites:

**`Salisbury’s City Directory for Oregon City, Oregon [and surrounding communities], 1953, 192 [listing for US Government].`**

This independently confirms a normal internal page citation to **p. 192** and provides a second descriptive title form.

## Interpretation

These are **secondary bibliographic citations**, not the underlying directory pages. They do not identify 505 Main, Well's Recreation, Carl R. Harr, or telephone 9179.

The safe catalog/search-key set for the 1953 volume should therefore include all of the following forms without treating any one wording as the uniquely exact title until the original title page is recovered:

- `Oregon City & Vicinity Directory, 1953`;
- `Salisbury's Oregon City, Gladstone, West Linn City Directory, 1953`;
- `Salisbury's City Directory for Oregon City, Oregon [and surrounding communities], 1953`;
- Oregon City / Gladstone / West Linn / Willamette coverage variants;
- T. W. Salisbury / Salisbury.

The actual title/front matter remains a retrieval target.

## Research consequence

Use these visually verified title variants when searching library catalogs, finding aids, digitized preservation attachments, microfilm descriptions, and derivative citations. The broadened title search may expose a copy that a search only for `Oregon City & Vicinity Directory` misses.

If the actual directory is recovered, visually capture the title/front matter first and then the exact 505 Main street entry, Well / Well's / Wells / Welle / Welsh alphabetical entries, Harr, recreation/pool/billiards classifications, proprietor notation, telephone number, 9179, and the complete 501–511 odd Main address block.

## Evidence boundary

This capture improves **bibliographic retrieval precision only**. It creates no new business operator, owner, licensee, tenant, telephone subscriber, transfer date, or 505 chronology event.
