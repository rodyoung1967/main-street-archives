## Best surviving state-record route

The Oregon State Archives OLCC retention schedule identifies **OLCC Commission Meeting Records — minutes, agendas and exhibits — from 1934 onward as permanent records transferred to the State Archives after 20 years**.

Ordinary license files/history/trade-name cards have finite retention.

Therefore the highest-value surviving state source for the 1967 mystery is:

> **OLCC Commission agenda / exhibit / staff material corresponding to the Oregon City Wheel action before the printed 7 June 1967 heading.**

The goal is to recover:

- applicant/licensee;
- trade name and premises;
