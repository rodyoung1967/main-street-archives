Address **unresolved**. Its pre-June existence **disproves using June as the earliest possible formation date**, but does not prove when the partnership formed. The exact 1922 address remains unresolved; later 714 Main associations cannot be backdated automatically. The standalone source capture exists; verify business/person/index/YAML crosslinks in a subsequent full register audit.
