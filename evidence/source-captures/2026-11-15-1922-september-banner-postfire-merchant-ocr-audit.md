## Historical interpretation and controls
- The merchant listings suggest that **Leland & Little was advertising its billiards business in September after the 18 July fire**, but the list alone does not prove **505 Main**, ownership of any building, or continuity of the pre-fire physical pool-hall structure.
- The merchant lists also place **McAnulty & Barry** in a cigars/restaurant category; do not infer from this that they operated the 505 pool hall. Their later 30 November license notice is a separate event requiring exact-premises identification.
- **R. A. Junken** is listed as contractor/builder but that merchant listing does not identify a particular building project.
- A 7 September p.1 reference to an architect preparing rebuilding plans concerns a separate fire event; do not confuse it with the July Fifth/Main fire.
- No exact 503 or 505 reconstruction completion date is established in this pass.
