# 1913 Chicago Store / probable 505 Main fire — source links

This capture preserves the currently recovered online source links for the December 1913 fire at the Chicago clothing store on Main Street immediately south of the Oregon City post office. Based on the established 1911 Chicago Store address at 505 Main and the 1912 description of the store as just below the post office, the fire is treated as a **high-confidence probable 505 Main event**, but not as exact-address proof until a 1913 source explicitly prints `505 Main` for the Chicago Store.

## Direct report

### Oregon City Courier — 25 December 1913, page 1

Headline: **THREE BIG FIRES IN THREE NIGHTS**

Key points in the article:
- The Chicago clothing store was on Main Street, south of the post office.
- The store interior was heavily damaged and the goods ruined.
- The stock was reported fully insured.
- **The building was owned by George Harding.**
- The article groups the Chicago Store fire with the McRoberts bakery and Elliott Bros. fires and notes public suspicion that the three might have been incendiary.

## Same-issue editorial/context reference

### Oregon City Courier — 25 December 1913, page 4

The editorial page comments on the unusual pattern of **three fires in successive nights, at about the same hour, starting in about the same place**. This is contextual corroboration for the fire series rather than an independent exact-address report of the Chicago Store fire.

## Related Morning Enterprise fire-series coverage

### Morning Enterprise — 23 December 1913, page 3

This article covers the third fire in the same three-night sequence and discusses the suspected incendiary pattern. It does not independently identify the Chicago Store by address, so it is retained as contextual fire-series evidence rather than direct 505 evidence.

## Original-binary preservation status

Historic Oregon Newspapers exposes direct PDF and JP2 download options for these public-domain newspaper pages. During the 21 August 2026 pass, direct binary retrieval was attempted through the available container/file-transfer path. The research interface can verify and render the newspaper pages, but the binary-transfer environment could not resolve the Oregon newspaper host, so the original PDF bytes could not be copied into the repository in this session.

The canonical page, OCR, and direct PDF URLs above are therefore preserved now. Original PDF/JP2 binaries should be mirrored into the repository when a binary-capable transfer path is available. This is the same preservation limitation already tracked under the project's physical-source mirroring backlog.

## Search status

Searches were run for combinations of `Chicago Store`, `Chicago clothing store`, `George Harding`, `fire`, `post office`, `south of the postoffice`, and the December 20–25, 1913 date window. The 25 December Courier page 1 report is the strongest recovered direct account. The page 4 Courier editorial and 23 December Morning Enterprise fire-series story provide supporting context. No additional recovered article in this pass independently states `505 Main` for the fire.
