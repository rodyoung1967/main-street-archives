## Target-address interpretation

The continued failure of the recurring newspaper material to name 503 or 505 must remain a **coverage/search-status finding only**. It does not establish vacancy, non-use, demolition, or lack of a business at either address.

This remains especially important when compared with the 1916–1917 water-rent ledger, which separately records 503 and 503½ while omitting an explicit 505 entry in the expected sequence. Newspaper silence and water-account structure are separate facts and should not be merged into a vacancy conclusion.
