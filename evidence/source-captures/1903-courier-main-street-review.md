# 1903 Oregon City Courier — Main Street Review

Reviewed: 20 August 2026  
Scope: 1903 issues, focused on the Main Street frontage between 5th and 6th and relationships that may map to later 501–527 Main addresses.

## Source-preservation status

Archive preference is to retain **both the original source binary and the canonical/original URL**. Direct binary-download attempts against the Oregon Digital PDF endpoints failed in the current environment. Canonical URLs and verified readings are preserved below; physical mirroring remains preferred when another download path is available.

## Handling rules

- Do not turn **next door**, **opposite Postoffice**, or **Willamette Building** into a later numbered address without independent mapping evidence.
- Do not assume Harding's later 511 Main address was already his address in 1903.
- Treat the Fair Store's later 1905 Willamette Building location as a hypothesis for its 1903 move destination until a direct move notice or directory proves it.
