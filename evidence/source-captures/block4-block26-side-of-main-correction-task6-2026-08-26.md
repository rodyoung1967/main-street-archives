# Task 6 — Block 4 / Block 26 side-of-Main correction — 26 August 2026

## Status

**RESOLVED AT THE CADASTRAL-ORIENTATION LEVEL — PRIOR TARGET-BLOCK ASSUMPTION WITHDRAWN.**

The project previously treated the odd-numbered 501/503/505/507 Main frontage between Fifth and Sixth as original Oregon City **Block 26**, and interpreted current `(2) OREGON CITY, Block 4` legal descriptions as evidence of a later block-number conversion. A Task 6 cross-check against current parcel descriptions, the 1913 assessment record, and the Clackamas County subdivision index shows that interpretation was wrong.

The corrected working orientation is:

- **odd-numbered 501/503/505/507 Main frontage = original Oregon City Block 4**;
- **the opposite/even-numbered frontage, including current 504 Main = original Oregon City Block 26**;
- there is therefore **no demonstrated Block 26 → Block 4 conversion for 503/505**. The two original blocks existed simultaneously on opposite sides of Main Street.

This correction supersedes the earlier inference in `E-094` that 501+503+505 occupied original Block 26 Lot 5 and that 507 began in Block 26 Lot 6.

## Primary 1913 control

The visually verified 23 October 1913 *Morning Enterprise* assessment notice already preserved as `S-107` distinguishes original Blocks 4 and 26 in the same boundary description. It runs along the west side of Blocks 7, 6, 5 and **4** to Fifth Street, then east along Fifth to **Block 26**, and north through Block 26 toward Sixth.

The same notice separately lists:

- **Block 4, Lot 3 — G. A. Harding**;
- **Block 4, Lot 4 — G. A. Harding**;
- **Block 26, Lot 5 — Mary E. Barlow / Juliette E. David**;
- **Block 26, Lot 6 — First National Bank**;
- **Block 26, Lot 7 — Frank Jaggar**;
- **Block 26, Lot 8 — Gambrinus Brewing Company**.

Thus original Block 4 and Block 26 were not successive numbering systems in 1913. They were different physical blocks.

## Meaning of `(2) OREGON CITY`

The Clackamas County Surveyor's current plat-name index identifies **OREGON CITY, PLAT OF (McLOUGHLIN'S)** as **Plat No. 0002**, covering the relevant 22E map area. It separately lists specific later Oregon City replats/vacations (for example Blocks 12, 111, 112, 146 and 151). No Block 26 → Block 4 replat is identified in that subdivision index.

County index:
https://www.clackamas.us/surveyor/platnames

The same leading `2 OREGON CITY` format also appears on current properties that retain other high original block numbers, so the leading `2` is best read as the Oregon City plat/subdivision identifier, not as evidence that all blocks were renumbered into a second cadastral system.

## Consequence for `E-094`

The source facts underlying `E-094` remain valuable, but the target-side inference is withdrawn:

- the original Oregon City plat and its lot dimensions remain primary evidence;
- the 1913 Block 26 Lots 5–8 owner list remains visually verified;
- the 1906 William Tell / Gambrinus Lot 8, Block 26 anchor remains valid;
- those Block 26 facts apply to the **opposite/even side of Main**, not to the odd-numbered 501/503/505/507 frontage.

The proportional 1925 Sanborn observation that 501+503+505 span roughly one 66-foot original-lot width may still be geometrically useful, but it must be re-tested against **Block 4 lot geometry**, not assigned to Block 26 Lot 5.

## Harding / OHS title-abstract implication

This correction materially strengthens a different title-chain route. The 1913 assessment lists **G. A. Harding at original Block 4 Lots 3 and 4**. Current 503 is reported as part of Block 4 Lot 4, and current 505 as parts of Block 4 Lots 3 and 4.

The visually verified OHS MSS 1503 Box 19 index (`E-095`) identifies an Oregon City title abstract at Main/Fifth whose title reads **Block 4, Lots 3 & 4**, while its separate Lot field reads **3, 4, 5**. The actual abstract remains unretrieved, so its scope and chain cannot be assumed. But it is now a much more directly relevant **correct-side original-Block-4 title lead**, rather than a supposed bridge from original Block 26 into a later Block 4 system.

That matters because the visually verified July 1922 sale report (`E-096`) says George A. Harding's Main Street property group was sold to **S. Wolf and C. Gates**. A recorded deed or the OHS abstract may therefore connect the 1913 Harding Block 4 ownership directly to the July 1922 Wolf/Gates division.

## What remains unresolved

This Task 6 correction resolves the **block orientation / alleged conversion problem**, but it does not yet prove:

- the exact original Block 4 lot occupied by historic **501 Main**;
- the exact 1922 boundaries between Gates's corner restaurant property and Wolf's remainder;
- whether modern 503/505 parcel lines existed in the same form in 1922;
- whether the OHS Box 19 abstract contains the complete Harding → Wolf/Gates chain;
- whether land and improvements had identical ownership;
- which exact 501/503/505 room contained the July 1922 fire origin.
