## Year-end address and business evidence

- **509 Main.** [November 19 sequence 5](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-11-19/ed-1/seq-5.pdf) directly prints **H. P. Brightbill, 509 Main St., Oregon City** in the grocery ad for Saturday, November 20. It independently corroborates the Courier directories. The ad identifies the advertiser and store number and leaves title and the Henry Brightbill identity question open.
- **Price Bros.** [December 17 sequence 12](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-12-17/ed-1/seq-12.pdf) directly places the clothiers at Sixth and Main. The exact **527 Main** number comes from the separate Courier directories, which continue through December 30 (`E-110`), and is absent from this particular advertisement.
- **Fashion Stable.** The W. S. Eddy ad repeats the Fourth-to-Fifth Main Street wording in all nine issues, ordinarily sequence 7, December 17 sequence 9 and December 31 sequence 8. These repeat the October location and extend the dated 1915 observations through December 31. No exact number is given.
- **Other neighboring controls.** The December 31 sequence 6 greeting prints E. T. Mass and Mass' Hotel without a street number. It corroborates the name after the October opening in former Brunswick quarters. The December 17 sequence 3 ad places Bank of Commerce at **Eighth/Main** and projects December 20 opening. December 24 sequence 5 reports that it opened Monday, consistent with December 20. Keep the 1915 site separate from the later 702–704 Main bank building and from the target block. The December 31 same greeting includes W. B. Eddy, Dry Goods and Shoes, with no street number.
- **No new direct target assignment.** None of the reviewed images established a new 503/503½/505/505½ operator, title transfer or building event. Reports of work at Third/Main, the elevator and named non-target Block 4 additions are geographic controls.

## Public works and licensing context

- [November 5 sequence 5](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-11-05/ed-1/seq-5.pdf) prints superintendent **W. O. Wellman**, Standard Paving, and reports a guilty plea, a $50 fine and remission of the fine after payment of $27.10 costs. The charges concern work beyond eight hours on municipal work. The connection to the September Worswick/Standard labor proceedings is supported by the role and charge, with the different printed initials retained. [November 26 sequence 3](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-11-26/ed-1/seq-3.pdf) independently records Loder's street spending table and the city share of 1915 street work as a separate accounting category. The street entries document public work, with no evidence here of storefront reconstruction at 503 or 505.
- [November 12 sequence 5](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-11-12/ed-1/seq-5.pdf) reports Brunswick-Balke-Collender's suit over Sunday closing and names local officials as defendants. This manufacturer reference is separate from the former Brunswick hotel and identifies no Oregon City poolroom premises.
- [December 31 sequence 5](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-12-31/ed-1/seq-5.pdf) reports projected year-end closing of seven saloons and the Friars' Club. The item locates the seven in Barlow, Sandy, Milwaukie and Oswego. It supplies county prohibition context and gives no 503/505 address or local occupancy timeline.

## Folios and provenance

December 17 has four extra source images relative to the ordinary eight-image week. Sequences 6–10 show handwritten folio markings. Sequences 2, 4, 5 and 11 lack a confidently read original numeral. The five issues in December sum to **44** images. All 12 December 17 images have distinct content, with consistent dated material, the holiday ad package, a p1 story continued on sequence 4 and the week-by-week carryover of notices and serial material. The markings are recorded separately from verified printed numerals.

December 24 sequence 7 and December 31 sequence 8 have same-date *Morning Enterprise* running heads in PDF and alternate IIIF images. December 24 carries the weekly issue's recurring professional and legal page, including notices repeated in the December 17 and December 31 issues. December 31 sequence 8 continues the weekly p1 road-levy article with matching title and subject. The content supports both associations, and the alternate images reproduce the headings. Every original annual archive sequence is accounted for. Courier source exceptions are tracked separately.

## January 8 veterinary-ad correction

The [original January 8 p7 PDF](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1915-01-08/ed-1/seq-7.pdf) was re-rendered and visually inspected at enlarged scale. It clearly prints **W. S. Eddy, V.S., M.D.V.**, at the Fashion Stable on Fifth between Main and Water. The earlier **A. J. Eddy** rendition was a transcription error for this ad. This establishes the same printed name and relative location earlier in 1915 and leaves the Fourth-to-Fifth Main premises' exact number and change date unresolved.
