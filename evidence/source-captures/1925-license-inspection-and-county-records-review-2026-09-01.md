## How these leads could help — research hypotheses, not findings

For the construction question, compare the same legally identified property in 1915, 1920, 1922, 1923, 1924 and 1925. If the records separate land and improvements, transcribe both, plus assessment dates, owner, description, corrections and marginal notes. An improvement-value change may help bound a change but does not itself prove construction: revaluation, parcel splits, damage and changes in assessment practice must be tested. A stable value likewise does not prove an unchanged structure. Do not assume a year's roll reflects work completed during that calendar year without reading its assessment convention.

First establish the historical parcel crosswalk using `E-094`, `E-108` and `RL-026`: the target frontage is **original Block 4**, not opposite-side Block 26. Modern 503/505/507 lot descriptions are research aids, not proof of unchanged 1920 boundaries. Retain neighboring entries and any Fifth Street addresses encountered; distinguish real-property owners from occupants and business-personal-property taxpayers. The later ownership books may help find earlier record references but cannot be backdated without a dated entry.

For occupancy, a 1928–1935 registration might document a later proprietor or provide an explicit earlier-history statement. Only what the actual certificate says can be used. Do not retroject Farr, Kwality/Quality or another later business into the 1925 map from a later filing or similar name.
