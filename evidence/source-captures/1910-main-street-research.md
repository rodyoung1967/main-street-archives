## Research method

This pass follows `evidence/research-protocols/annual-courier-issue-review.md`:

1. targeted exact-address and named-business searches;
2. construction/demolition/fire/repair/rebuilding searches;
3. issue/page-level review of *Oregon City Courier* pages retrievable or indexed in the Historic Oregon Newspapers interface;
4. cross-checking against Oregon City Council records and other newspapers;
5. explicit logging of inaccessible page-image coverage rather than treating it as reviewed.

The page-level Courier review materially helped in 1910: it surfaced the March Price Bros. Sixth/Main advertisement, the September multi-business address page, and the October wooden-awning/projecting-sign action in addition to the major construction reporting.

## S-092 — New Gambrinus business block at Sixth & Main

### Oregon City Council, 29 June 1910

Canonical municipal record:  
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/6375928/File/document

Council record: contractor **J. G. Killgreen** was permitted to use ten feet of Main Street and ten feet of Sixth Street adjoining the **Gambrinus property at Sixth and Main Streets** for placing materials for **construction of a new building at said location**.

### Oregon City Courier, 26 August 1910, page 1

Canonical page:  
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1910-08-26/ed-1/seq-1/

Article heading/context: **Improvement Order of Day / Much Building Being Done in Oregon City**.

Relevant report: basement work was complete for a **two-story business block** to be erected by the **Gambrinus Brewing Company on Main Street at Sixth**; the planned structure was described as modern.

Related evidence: `E-078`.

### Interpretation

This is direct new-construction evidence at the Gambrinus Sixth/Main property. It follows the 1906 William Tell sale to Gambrinus (`E-064`). No direct 1910 demolition notice for the William Tell building was recovered, so the archive does not state that the 1910 block definitely replaced that structure unless/until the demolition/continuity link is documented more explicitly.

## S-093 — Oregon City Council, 1 June 1910: Hodes vacancy and E. R. Case Fifth/Main

Canonical municipal record:  
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/6375928/File/document

### Carl G. Hodes

Council extended C. G. Hodes's saloon-license period to compensate for time missed between **his vacation of his saloon at Sixth and Main Streets** and the license expiration. This proves the 1909 Sixth/Main Hodes saloon had been vacated for some period by 1 June 1910.

Related evidence: `E-079`.

### E. R. Case

Council considered a complaint concerning beer dispensed on Sunday in a **restaurant adjoining E. R. Case's saloon at Fifth and Main Streets**. The matter was dropped after discussion; Case was warned regarding leased property.

Related evidence: `E-080`.

## S-094 — 1910 Courier Main Street business/address anchors

### 4 March 1910, page 12

Canonical page:  
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1910-03-04/ed-1/seq-12/

Price Bros. clothing advertisement gives: **Sixth and Main Sts., Oregon City, Oregon**.

### 23 September 1910, page 3

Canonical page:  
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1910-09-23/ed-1/seq-3/

The local-business contest/result page supplies a broad address snapshot. Relevant entries include:

- H. P. Brightbill — **509 Main St.**
- Price Bros. — **Cor. Main and Sixth Sts.**
- Pioneer Market / H. W. Streibig — **Cor. Fifth and Main Sts.**
- Geo. A. Harding — **611 Main St.**
- Watson B. Eddy & Son — **Cor. Main and Sixth Sts.**
- Kelly's Restaurant — **519 Main St.**
- Electric Hotel — **Main St. Bet. Fourth and Fifth Sts.**

Related evidence: `E-081`.

### Brightbill chronology

This is important because `E-042` places Henry Brightbill at **503 Main on 25 August 1908**. The 23 September 1910 page establishes Brightbill at **509 Main** by that date, narrowing his documented move/readdressing window. The later 5 January 1912 509 Main advertisement (`E-050`) is no longer the earliest known 509 evidence.

### Harding handling

Geo. A. Harding's **611 Main** business listing is not treated as the address of the Harding Building. The Harding Building remains independently unresolved under `E-077` / `RL-029`.

## S-096 — 1910 Main Street sidewalk / awning / sign modernization

### Oregon City Council, 6 July 1910

Canonical municipal record:  
https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/6375928/File/document

Council directed the Street Superintendent to require property owners along **Main Street between Eleventh Street and the Basin** to install concrete walks where necessary. The Fifth–Sixth block is inside the corridor.

### Oregon City Courier, 14 October 1910, page 1

Canonical page:  
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1910-10-14/ed-1/seq-1/

Front-page report: **Wooden Awnings Must Come Down**. The City Council required old wooden awnings projecting over sidewalks to be removed and projecting signs to be placed flat against buildings.

Related evidence: `E-083`.

This is a research-status statement only. It does not imply vacancy, absence of buildings, or non-use of the street-numbering system.

### Construction watch
