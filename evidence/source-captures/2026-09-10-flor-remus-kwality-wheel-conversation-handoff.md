# Purpose of this conversation

The purpose of this conversation is to reconstruct the **pre-Raye business/operator chronology at 503 Main Street, Oregon City**, concentrating on the transition from the **Kwality Cafe / Flor / Remus era** to the first documented **Wheel Cafe & Lounge** period, while separately testing a possible **Morton, Washington Wheel Cafe connection** remembered in family history.

The central historical question is:

> **Who operated, owned, licensed, renamed, sold, or acquired the restaurant/cocktail-bar business at 503 Main between the Kwality/Flor controls and The Wheel, and what are the defensible dates?**

> **Did Albert D. Flor, Margaret/Marge Remus, Fred Remus, Raye/Floyd LaFarlette, or another predecessor have a documented connection to the Wheel Cafe in Morton, Washington?**

These questions must remain separated from:

- ownership of the 503 real estate;

- later Raye → Dick/Sheila business sale;

Do not create a smooth ownership narrative where the sources establish only adjacent operating or licensing controls.
