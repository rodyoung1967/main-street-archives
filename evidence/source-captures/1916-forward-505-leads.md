# 1916 forward leads for 505 Main

Status: active lead file. This file records later evidence that narrows the unresolved 1916 occupancy/business transition at 505 Main. It is not proof of the exact 1916 occupant.

## 1918 anchor — Wolf & Miller at 505 Main

**Oregon City Courier, 10 Oct 1918, p.4**

Source: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-10-10/ed-1/seq-4/

The paper identifies **Wolf & Miller, 505 Main** in a business-profile/contest item. The same item says the firm is dissolving partnership, one partner is leaving for Idaho, and the other will restock and continue the business. It describes the store as selling shoes for the whole family, men's clothing and furnishings, with an up-to-date shoe-repairing department using Landis electric machinery.

### Why this matters for the 1916 pass

- 505 Main is unresolved after the Dec. 1913 Chicago Store fire.
- This 1918 exact-address anchor proves that by Oct. 1918 a shoe/clothing business, Wolf & Miller, occupied 505 Main.
- Therefore the 1916–1918 research should look specifically for the arrival of Wolf & Miller, a predecessor shoe business, a relocation notice, lease/sale notice, or building work at 505.
- This does **not** establish that Wolf & Miller was already at 505 in 1916.

## Search targets

1. `Wolf & Miller` in Oregon City Courier and Oregon City Enterprise during 1916–1918.
2. `505 Main`, `505 Main St`, `five hundred five Main`, and OCR variants.
3. Shoe-store, shoe-repair, clothing, furnishings, partnership, dissolution, relocation, opening, and remodeling notices.
4. Any George Harding / property-owner / lease references tied to the post-1913 fire site.
