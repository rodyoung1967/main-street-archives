# Hoag Brothers — Harding building on Main Street, May 1913

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED AT PAGE LEVEL; exact numbered address unresolved**

Source:
- *Oregon City Courier*, 16 May 1913, page 1
- PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1913-05-16/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

The original newspaper page was opened and visually inspected in the current research session. This is page-level verification only; it does not certify the entire 16 May 1913 issue as reviewed page by page.

## Visually verified report

The front page reports that **Hoag Brothers**, the new firm of **Fred and Otto Hoag**, had rented the **Harding building on Main Street, four doors south of the postoffice**, and expected to open about May 20. Their planned stock included furniture, hardware, building material, and wall paper. The article says both brothers had previously worked for the Frank Busch store.

A 28 March 1913 Courier item independently reports Fred and Otto Hoag resigning from Frank Busch Co., where they had worked for ten and six years respectively.

## Why this matters to the 503/505 reconstruction

The archive already has strong evidence that the **Chicago Store at the documented 505 Main premises was in a building owned by George Harding when it burned in December 1913**. The Hoag Brothers article proves that Harding also controlled another (or possibly the same, not yet established) Main Street commercial premises described by relative position to the post office.

However, **do not assign Hoag Brothers to 505 Main from the phrase `four doors south of the postoffice`.** The exact numbered address of the Hoag store has not been recovered, and the post-office number / door-count reconstruction is not strong enough to convert that relative description into a numbered address. Doing so could also create an apparent conflict with the Chicago Store chronology that must be resolved from direct sources rather than assumptions.

The correct current interpretation is:

- George Harding owned or controlled multiple Main Street commercial premises, or at minimum more than one Harding-associated business location is present in the evidence;
- Hoag Brothers opened in a Harding building on Main Street around 20 May 1913;
- the exact Hoag address is unresolved;
- this source should be used as a property/landlord lead, not as proof of 503 or 505 occupancy.
