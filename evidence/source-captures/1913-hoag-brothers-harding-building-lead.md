# Hoag / Hogg Brothers — Harding building on Main Street, 1913

Status: **May 1913 opening source visually verified at page level; exact opening street number unresolved; strong evidence places the business in the Eighth/Main cluster rather than at 503/505**

## 16 May 1913 — visually verified opening notice

Source:
- *Oregon City Courier*, 16 May 1913, page 1
- PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1913-05-16/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

The original newspaper page was opened and visually inspected in the current research session. This is page-level verification only; it does not certify the entire 16 May 1913 issue as reviewed page by page.

The front page reports that **Hoag Brothers**, the new firm of **Fred and Otto Hoag**, had rented the **Harding building on Main Street, four doors south of the postoffice**, and expected to open about May 20. Their planned stock included furniture, hardware, building material, and wall paper. The article says both brothers had previously worked for the Frank Busch store.

A 28 March 1913 Courier item independently reports Fred and Otto Hoag resigning from Frank Busch Co., where they had worked for ten and six years respectively.

Sources:
- 16 May 1913: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1913-05-16/ed-1/seq-1.pdf
- 28 Mar 1913: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1913-03-28/ed-1/seq-8.pdf

## 13 June 1913 — newly opened store described as just below the post office

The 13 June 1913 Courier reports that **Hogg Bros. had their new Main Street store open to the public** and says **the new store is just below the postoffice**.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1913-06-13/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

This independently reinforces the May relative-location wording but still does not print a numbered address.

## February 1914 — same business described next door to Courier office

The 12 February 1914 industrial edition profiles **Hogg Brothers**, says they **opened their doors for business about nine months ago**, and says **this store is located next door to the Courier office**. It identifies the owners as **F. L. and Otto Hogg** and notes F. L. had worked for Frank Busch & Co. for ten years.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-02-12/ed-1/seq-15/

The Oregon City Courier masthead repeatedly states in 1913 that it was published from the **Courier Building, Eighth and Main streets**. Example:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1913-08-07/ed-1/seq-4/

Taken together, the February profile and Courier masthead strongly place the Hogg Brothers store in the **Eighth/Main commercial cluster** by early 1914. Because the February profile describes the business as having opened roughly nine months earlier and immediately refers to `this store`, continuity with the 1913 opening premises is the leading interpretation, although an unreported move within those nine months remains theoretically possible.

## Later exact-address anchor — 804 Main

By **10 October 1918**, the Courier directly identifies:

**Hogg Bros., 804 Main**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-10-10/ed-1/seq-4/

Later 1928 advertising also gives **Hogg Bros., 804 Main, Oregon City**.

## Name-form caution: Hoag vs. Hogg

The March and May 1913 Courier items print or are transcribed as **Hoag**, while the 1914 profile and later records use **Hogg**. The 1914 profile identifies the proprietors as F. L. and Otto Hogg and closely matches the March 1913 employment history, making these very likely the same brothers/business. Preserve the source spellings rather than silently rewriting the 1913 text; treat **Hogg** as the later well-established surname form pending a definitive personal record.

## Implication for 503/505 research

This materially weakens the earlier possibility that the Harding storefront rented to Hoag/Hogg Brothers could have been the **505 Main** Chicago Store premises.

Current evidence supports:

- Hogg Brothers opened a Harding-owned Main Street store in May/June 1913;
- by February 1914 their store was next to the Courier office at **Eighth & Main**;
- by 1918 the business is explicitly **804 Main**;
- therefore Hogg Brothers should **not be treated as a candidate 503 or 505 tenant** unless a future direct numbered-address source contradicts this Eighth/Main placement.

The George Harding-owned **Chicago Store / 505 Main** fire lead should remain a separate property thread. Harding evidently owned or controlled more than one Main Street commercial premises, or at minimum the evidence concerns distinct Harding-associated storefronts.

## Old post-office caution

The phrases `four doors south of the postoffice` and `just below the postoffice` refer to the **pre-new-federal-building post-office arrangement** in spring 1913. Oregon City was preparing a new post-office building at Eighth/Main later in 1913. Do not use the later post-office building to interpret the May/June relative-location language without first resolving the old post office's exact location.
