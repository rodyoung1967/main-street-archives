# 1922 Schieremann / 501–503–505 frontage geometry addendum — 13 September 2026

Status: **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE / PHOTO-GEOMETRY INFERENCE; exact printed Schieremann and pool-hall street numbers remain unresolved.**

## Purpose

Preserve and propagate the refined spatial reasoning developed with the project steward after the earlier Schieremann exact-address checkpoint and the 13 September Fifth/Main fire/rebuild handoff. This addendum does not replace the underlying contemporary sources. It records how the already-verified fire reporting combines with steward-supplied photographic geometry and the established address framework.

## Direct documentary evidence already established

1. `E-103` / `S-117`, visually verified 20 July 1922 local fire reporting, places the fire origin in a **vacant Main Street storeroom formerly occupied by Alex Schieremann as a tailor shop**, identifies **S. Wolf of Portland** as owner of the ignition building, and says a **pool hall adjoined the former tailoring establishment**. The article does not print Schieremann's street number or the pool hall's street number.
2. The 18 July 1922 Eugene fire account says the fire spread **from the origin shop to the Oregon City Laundry**, which was destroyed. This distinguishes the laundry/fire-spread relationship from the separately described adjoining pool hall, whose billiard tables suffered water damage and whose roof was only partly burned.
3. Existing project evidence directly anchors the Fifth Street Restaurant at the Fifth/Main corner context and records the Harding property sale in which the **Fifth Street Restaurant location went to C. E. Gates**, while the remainder went to **S. Wolf**.
4. Existing project evidence directly documents **Dr. Freeze at 505½ Main through the end of June 1922**, immediately before the July fire.
5. Existing project evidence directly documents **A. Leland at 505½ Main in June 1920** in the pool-hall chain. Do not merge A. Leland with O. H. Leland without a direct identity bridge.
6. Existing 1922 evidence identifies Timms Pool Hall, Leland & Little / Leland and Little, and later billiards activity, but no recovered pre-fire item yet prints an exact 501/503/505/505½ address for the July adjoining pool hall.
7. The 1925 Sanborn independently maps the same Main Street frontage as **501 store / 503 restaurant / 505 billiards-cigar use**. This is a later structural/use control and is not by itself proof of July 1922 occupancy.

## Project-steward photographic / physical observations

The project steward has repeatedly reviewed the available historic photographs of this frontage and supplies the following physical observations for the archive:

- **501 Main is the end-of-block / Fifth Street corner storefront.** Along the Main Street frontage, its only neighboring Main Street property/storefront is the 503 position; the other adjacency turns onto Fifth Street and is not another Main Street storefront.
- Across the relevant photographs, the 501/503/505 frontage shows **three visible storefronts, one at each principal address**. The steward does not observe a fourth Main Street storefront corresponding to a half-number.
- The relevant building/frontage is **one story** in the photographic evidence under discussion. Therefore a half-number such as 505½ should not be presumed to mean an upstairs storefront/office.
- A half-address could instead identify a secondary occupancy, interior room, rear portion, or other sub-address within a principal storefront/property footprint. This is a physical-use hypothesis, not a documentary identification of Dr. Freeze's exact room configuration.

These are steward photo/physical interpretations and must remain attributed as such unless independently established by a map, plan, permit, directory, deed, or other direct source.

## Refined spatial inference

Given the established address geometry, if the vacant Schieremann room occupied the Main Street commercial position adjoining the Fifth Street Restaurant / 501 corner premises, the only Main Street position available is **503**.

The contemporary fire report then places a **pool hall adjoining Schieremann's tailoring establishment**. With 501 at the block end and 503 between 501 and 505 on the Main Street frontage, the simple frontage reconstruction is:

> **501 Main — Fifth Street Restaurant / corner premises → 503 Main — vacant former Schieremann tailor room → 505 Main — adjoining pool/billiard premises.**

This is now the archive's **best-supported physical reconstruction** and should be treated as a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE**, not as a direct exact-address quotation. No recovered contemporary source yet literally prints `Alex Schieremann — 503 Main` or names the July pool hall at `505 Main`.

## Rear-adjacency alternative narrowed

An earlier theoretical alternative was that the pool hall might have adjoined Schieremann at the rear/side rather than as the next Main Street storefront. The combined evidence materially weakens that alternative:

- the separate fire account says flames spread **from the origin shop to the Oregon City Laundry**, and the laundry was destroyed;
- the local report separately describes the **pool hall adjoining the tailoring establishment** as surviving with water-damaged billiard tables and a partly burned roof;
- steward photographic evidence supplies the three-storefront Main Street geometry.

The sources do not literally print `the laundry was behind Schieremann`, so that directional wording must not be promoted as direct fact. But the pool hall's survival/damage description, the separate laundry fire-spread relationship, and the frontage geometry make a rear-pool-hall interpretation substantially less plausible than the 501→503→505 frontage sequence.

## Half-address interpretation

Do **not** create a fourth storefront from `505½` merely because the half-number appears in contemporary records. The photographs, as interpreted by the steward, consistently show only three storefronts at 501, 503, and 505. Because the relevant structure is one story, `505½` may have denoted a secondary occupancy within the 505 footprint — potentially an interior or rear portion — rather than an upstairs or separate fourth facade bay.

This remains a **working spatial interpretation**. The exact physical arrangement of Dr. Freeze's 505½ occupancy and the 1920 A. Leland 505½ pool-hall use still requires a floor plan, directory detail, license application, assessment sketch, or comparable direct evidence.

## What this changes from the 3 September exact-address checkpoint

The earlier checkpoint correctly refused to assign Schieremann to 501/503/505 because it had not yet incorporated the steward's stronger frontage-geometry constraint. This addendum narrows the model:

- **501/503/505 are no longer equally plausible physical positions for Schieremann.**
- The best-supported reconstruction places Schieremann in the **503 physical position** and the adjoining pool hall in the **505 physical position**.
- The remaining documentary question is more precise: can a contemporary source print the exact business/sub-address designation — e.g. 503 versus a possible half/sub-address — and can the July pool-hall operator be identified at 505/505½?

Do not erase the earlier checkpoint; retain it as the audit trail showing how the inference was narrowed.
