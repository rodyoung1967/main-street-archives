Date reviewed: 4 September 2026

Purpose: consolidate a local Oregon City retrieval route for the unresolved historical directory sources needed to bracket Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main. The online CCHS catalog is principally a digitized photograph catalog, so its silence must not be treated as evidence that the document/book holdings lack the requested directories.

## Two distinct organizations at the Museum of the Oregon Territory

The **Clackamas County Historical Society (CCHS)** and the **Clackamas County Family History Society (CCFHS)** maintain separate collections while sharing the Wallace B. Caufield Memorial Building / Museum of the Oregon Territory at **211 Tumwater Drive, Oregon City**.

### CCHS collections route

CCHS states that access to photographs and documents owned by CCHS can be arranged through its collections manager. Its staff page identifies **Johnnie Heintz, Cultural Research Historian**, and gives:

- `collections@clackamashistory.org`
- 503-655-5574

Current official routes:

- `https://clackamashistory.org/family-history-research-library`
- `https://clackamashistory.org/staff`
- `https://clackamashistory.org/collection`

The public CatalogIt hub currently exposes **13,214 digitized photographs**. It is not a comprehensive online catalog of all books, documents, vertical files or archival holdings. Therefore a failed CatalogIt search is not a valid negative holding result for historical directories.

### CCFHS / Wilmer Gardner Research Library route

CCFHS states that the **Wilmer Gardner Research Library** contains **thousands of books, photographs and publications**, plus online resources, and explicitly accepts remote **Research Requests** from people unable to visit in person.

Current contact:

- `ccfhsociety@gmail.com`
- 503-655-5574
- `https://www.clackamascountyfamilyhistory.org/aboutus`
- research-request route linked from that page: `https://www.clackamascountyfamilyhistory.org/researchrequest`

The research-request page itself returned a cache miss in the current environment, but the live About Us page explicitly identifies that service. CCFHS is a separate entity from CCHS; requests should not silently treat their holdings as one collection.

## Exact directory source set to ask about

The holdings inquiry should request a catalog/physical-shelf check for these sources, keeping publisher/source types distinct:

### Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. telephone directories

- **1927** — title/scope cited in a National Register nomination as **“Clackamas County”**.
- **1928, 1929, 1931, 1933** — cited as **“Oregon City and Clackamas County.”**
- **August 1932** — **Telephone Directory, Oregon City, Clackamas County, Oregon**, separately confirmed in Wilsonville Public Library's Heritage Collection as `OR CLAC D-OR.C 1932T`.
- **1930, 1931, 1935, 1939** — a Clackamas County telephone-directory sequence cited by West Linn's 1988 historic-resource survey; exact publisher/title pages still needed.
- **1941** — Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. directory cited as **“Oregon City, West Linn, and Gladstone”** in the final Dr. Edward and Anne McLean House National Register nomination.

The overlapping 1931 references may represent the same PT&T edition or different citation wording. Do not merge them until title pages are inspected.

### Pacific Directory Service city/county directories

- **1941** — *Directory of Oregon City*, Portland: Pacific Directory Service; fuller secondary citation: **Oregon City–West Linn–Gladstone**.
- possible **1942-era Clackamas County Business and Professional Directory / Buyers' Guide**, announced as under compilation in December 1941; completion remains unproved.
- **1947–48 / 1948** — *Directory of Clackamas County*, Pacific Directory Service / H. C. Prudhomme route already tracked separately.

The **1941 Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. telephone directory** and the **1941 Pacific Directory Service city/business directory** are separate publications and must not be conflated merely because both cover Oregon City/West Linn/Gladstone.

Preserve exact printed wording, directory date/effective date, page, subscriber/business name, address, telephone number and classification. Record residences separately from business premises.

## Internet Archive / local-history digitization context

Open Library / Internet Archive exposes a substantial digitized run of **Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. Oregon telephone directories**, especially an Astoria/Clatsop County sequence spanning the 1930s and 1940s. One June 1941 Astoria directory is digitized from a **Clatsop County Historical Society** collection and includes institutional shelf/accession metadata.

This is useful methodological context: the same publisher/source type has been digitized through an Oregon county historical-society partnership. It does **not** imply that Clackamas County Historical Society owns the Oregon City volumes or that an Oregon City scan exists under the same Internet Archive collection.

Targeted public searches of the PT&T Open Library author record and Internet Archive indexing did not expose an Oregon City/Clackamas 1941 volume under obvious title metadata. That remains a **digital retrieval gap**, not absence evidence.

## Evidence boundary

No new directory occupant, Farr/Harr transition date, business address or license claim is created by this holdings-route work. The purpose is to move the unresolved directories from generic catalog searching into the two most relevant local Oregon City research collections while keeping institutional holdings and publisher/source identities separate.
