# H. H. Smith pool hall at Fifth & Main — July 1912 exit to Aurora

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED AT PAGE LEVEL**. The original PDF page was opened and visually inspected in the current research session. This does not certify the full 12 July 1912 issue as page-by-page reviewed.

Source:
- *Oregon City Courier*, 12 July 1912, page 5
- HTML: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-07-12/ed-1/seq-5/
- PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-07-12/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

## Visually verified wording

In the left-side `NEWS OF THE CITY` column, the original scan clearly states that **H. H. Smith**, who had conducted a **pool hall on Fifth and Main streets** in Oregon City, left Wednesday for **Aurora**, where he would open a pool hall.

Because the paper is dated Friday, 12 July 1912, `left Wednesday` indicates approximately **10 July 1912**.

## Relationship to 503 Main

This is important when combined with two earlier 1912 sources:

1. **5 January 1912 visual advertisement** — **H. N. Smith**, Pool Room / Cigars and Tobacco, explicitly **503 Main Street**.
2. **24 March 1912 legal notice** — OCR/text reading gives **H. H. Smith** applying for a pool-hall license at **503 Main Street**.
3. **12 July 1912 visual news item** — **H. H. Smith** had conducted a pool hall at **Fifth and Main** and left for Aurora.

Taken together, the March exact-address notice and July Fifth/Main departure report strongly support **H. H. Smith as the proprietor of the 503 Main pool-hall operation during at least spring to July 1912**.

The January advertisement, however, visibly reads **H. N. Smith**, not H. H. Smith. Given the identical business type and exact 503 address, a typographical error, initial-reading issue, or newspaper inconsistency is increasingly plausible. But the archive should **not silently normalize H. N. to H. H.** until another identity source establishes the proprietor's full name or the January ad's initials are independently corroborated.

## Chronology now supported

- **5 Jan 1912:** visually verified operating pool room at **503 Main**, proprietor printed as **H. N. Smith**.
- **24 Mar 1912:** legal notice identifies **H. H. Smith** seeking a pool-hall license at **503 Main**; original page image still desirable for direct initials verification.
- **12 Jul 1912:** visually verified report says **H. H. Smith had conducted a pool hall at Fifth & Main and left for Aurora** around July 10.

This establishes an end point for the Smith pool-hall tenure in Oregon City by mid-July 1912. It also opens a new occupancy gap for 503 between Smith's departure and **L. Hylton's documented 1914 tenancy**.

## Why this matters to the Cox question

Cox & Ward were independently operating a Main Street cigar/billiard business on 5 January 1912 while Smith was at 503. Eugene Ward sold his interest to `Mr. Cox` in February. The July Smith departure therefore creates a possible later opportunity for another business to occupy 503, but there is **no evidence yet that Cox moved into 503 after Smith left**. Any such move must be proven by an address-specific source.

## Research targets

1. Identify H. H./H. N. Smith by full name from directories, council license/bond files, Aurora newspapers, or later records.
2. Search Aurora newspapers immediately after July 1912 for Smith's new pool hall; a full name or initials there may resolve the discrepancy.
3. Search 503 Main from **July 1912 through 1914** for the immediate successor between Smith and L. Hylton.
4. Continue searching Cox's post-Ward premises separately; do not infer Cox occupied 503 merely because Smith left.
