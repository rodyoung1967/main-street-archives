# 1910 Oregon City Courier page-review note

The annual *Oregon City Courier* page-review protocol materially improved the 1910 pass beyond exact-address OCR searches.

Items surfaced or strengthened through issue/page-level review included:

- the 4 March Price Bros. advertisement explicitly locating the business at **Sixth and Main Streets**;
- the 23 September multi-business page listing **H. P. Brightbill at 509 Main**, Pioneer Market at Fifth/Main, George A. Harding at 611 Main, and Price Bros. / Watson B. Eddy & Son at Sixth/Main;
- the 14 October front-page report requiring removal of old wooden awnings and projecting signs.

The strongest 1910 structural discovery—the new Gambrinus block—was supported by both a City Council record and the 26 August *Courier*. The page-review protocol therefore functions both as a discovery method for small contextual items and as a completeness check around major findings.

As in 1909, this archive does not claim that every physical page image was visually inspected when the Historic Oregon Newspapers interface did not expose a retrievable page. Remaining visual-completeness gaps are tracked under `RL-032`.
