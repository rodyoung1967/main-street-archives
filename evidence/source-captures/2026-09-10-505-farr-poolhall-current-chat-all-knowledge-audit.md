### 505 pool-hall / recreation chronology

The conversation preserved the following chronology and uncertainty controls:

- **1925:** visually verified Sanborn maps 505 Main as billiards/cigars; operator unnamed.
- Oregon City's official historic-resource evidence for 401 Third identifies **Edwin F. Farr and Alice Farr** as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main Street**.
- The same source says the Farrs acquired their 401 Third residence in **1934**; that date is residence context, not proof of the pool hall's opening date.
- CCHS photograph P-1128 is independently cataloged as **1936**; project visual work plus the independent City identification supports a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE** that Farr's Pool Hall was operating at 505 in the photographed 1936 state, but not an opening-date claim.
- City synthesis places **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 in 1945**, with the underlying directory/source still requiring recovery.
- **11 February 1948:** municipal text names Harr and McDougald as pool-hall operators but does not print Harr's first name, business or address on that page.
- a separate late-1948 municipal liquor-license list directly prints **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**; the exact meeting date remains unresolved because the recovered excerpt lacks its opening page.
- **1950 Sanborn:** continues the billiards/cigars/restaurant/beer-related use.

The exact start of Farr's Pool Hall and the exact **Farr → Harr** transition remain unresolved.
