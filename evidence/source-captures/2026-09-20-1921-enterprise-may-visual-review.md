# 1921 Enterprise visual review — May

## Scope and method

Continuation of ANNUAL-1921. **1921 remains IN PROGRESS.**

All four weekly Oregon City Enterprise issues in May 1921 were inventoried and all **32 page images were visually inspected page by page**:
- 6 May — 8/8
- 13 May — 8/8
- 20 May — 8/8
- 27 May — 8/8

The visual pass covered the entire **500 block of Main Street**, with particular attention to 501/503/505. Review criteria included addresses; named people/businesses; Fifth/Main and 500-block descriptions; neighboring spatial controls; demolition/teardown; construction/rebuilding; remodeling; fire/damage; property sale or ownership transfer; landlord/lease/tenant changes; and business openings, closings, or moves.

## Results

All 32 pages were present and visually inspectable.

No May issue produced a new sufficiently specific fact tying a person, business, owner, tenant, sale/transfer, demolition, rebuild, remodeling, fire/damage event, or move to 501, 503, or 505 Main.

No other item observed in the May weekly issues could be anchored securely enough to the 500 block to warrant propagation as a new block-specific fact.

## Evidence limitation

These are coverage/no-hit findings only, not evidence that no physical, ownership, tenancy, or business change occurred. The Morning Enterprise daily run remains necessary and may capture short-lived events absent from the weekly Enterprise.

## Coverage

May: **4 issues / 32 of 32 page images visually verified.**

Cumulative weekly Oregon City Enterprise coverage, 7 January through 27 May 1921:
**21 issues / 168 page images visually verified.**
