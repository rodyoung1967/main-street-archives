## Visually verified notice

The page contains a notice headed:

**Notice of Application for Pool Hall License**

The notice states that the applicant will apply at the next regular meeting of the city council for a license to run and regulate a pool hall at **“my place of business 503 Main Street”** for a period of three months.

The signature line visibly prints:

**H. H. SMTHI**

That unusual spelling/order is preserved exactly as printed in the scan. It may be a typographical transposition of another surname, but the repository must not silently normalize it to Smith or any other identity without an independent bridge.

## Historical significance

This is direct contemporary evidence that, in March 1912, a person signing as printed **H. H. SMTHI** sought a city license to run and regulate a **pool hall at 503 Main Street**.

It establishes a business-use/licensing event at 503 Main. It does **not** by itself establish:

- ownership of the 503 building;
- ownership of the pool-hall business beyond the applicant/license context;
- continuous pool-hall use before or after the three-month license period;
- identity with any later Smith, Champion Smith & Telford, Raasch & Lamb, Leland, Farr, Harr, or other pool/recreation operator.
