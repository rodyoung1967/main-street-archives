## Main historical status at closeout

The Farr → Harr transition date remains **unresolved**.

Controlling chronology:

- **1936:** Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main is supported by CCHS photo P-1128 plus City identification.
- **1945:** SHPO/City historic-resource synthesis states a 1945 city directory lists **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main**. This is official-secondary synthesis; the underlying directory page remains unrecovered.
- **Aug–Sep 1946:** Pacific Directory Service was gathering Clackamas County directory data. Any eventual 1947–48 listing may reflect late-1946 canvass conditions rather than 1948.
- **11 Feb 1948:** Oregon City Commission primary text names Harr and McDougald as pool-hall operators, but that sentence does not print an address.
- **late 1948:** municipal liquor text directly identifies **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street**.
- **1953:** SHPO form states the 1953 city directory lists **"Well's Recreation"** at 505 Main; underlying directory page still unrecovered.
- **20 Nov 1955:** visually verified sale ad offers the beer/pool business at 505 Main; printed contact is **9179**; seller/buyer/trade name remain unknown.
- **11 Nov 1965:** Oregon City Commission liquor renewal directly documents **505 Tavern**.

Nothing in this chat justifies moving the Farr → Harr date earlier or later than those evidence bounds.
