# 2000 downtown re-survey provenance for the 1926 Kwality advertisement lead — 15 September 2026

## Scope

This capture narrows the source trail behind the City of Oregon City statement that **503 Main** appears in **1926 newspaper advertisements as the Kwality Restaurant**. It does **not** recover the underlying advertisement itself.

## 1. The 2000 re-survey created individual historic-resource forms

The official City of Oregon City *Historic Downtown Re-Survey: Final Report* (September 2000), prepared by historic-preservation consultant **Michelle L. Dennis**, states in its introduction that the project generated **59 Oregon Inventory of Historic Properties Historic Resource Survey Forms**: 57 for buildings/structures and two for sites where earlier-survey buildings had been demolished.

The report states that:

- copies of the report and inventory forms were available through the **City of Oregon City**; and
- the **original forms were housed at the Oregon State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) in Salem**.

This makes the individual 503 Main survey form a high-value provenance target because it may retain source annotations, notes, or other details omitted from the City's later web narrative.

Official report: https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/4025/Downtown-Final-Report---2000-PDF

## 2. Archival-research repositories named by the consultant

The report's methodology section says archival research used sources including **Sanborn maps, city directories, biographical encyclopedias, newspapers, tax records, SHPO files, local histories, and historic photographs**.

It further states that information was gathered from:

- **Oregon City Public Library**;
- **Clackamas County Historical Society and Museum**;
- **University of Oregon**; and
- **Oregon State University**.

This establishes the research institutions used by the 2000 survey but does not identify which one supplied the 1926 Kwality advertisement.

## 3. Selected bibliography names the Oregon City Enterprise newspapers

The report's selected bibliography explicitly lists:

> **Oregon City Enterprise (daily and weekly newspapers).**

This is a materially stronger provenance clue for the later 503 Main statement. It demonstrates that the 2000 re-survey consulted the **Oregon City Enterprise newspaper family** in addition to the 1925 Sanborn and directories.

**Evidence limit:** the bibliography is project-wide. It does **not** directly say that the specific 1926 Kwality advertisement was in the weekly *Oregon City Enterprise* rather than the daily *Morning Enterprise*, nor does it prove that no other newspaper title was used for the 503 form. Treat Enterprise as the highest-priority newspaper family, not as a fully proved issue-level citation.

## 4. Oregon City Public Library confirms surviving 1926 microfilm coverage

The current Oregon City Public Library microfilm holdings page lists:

- **Oregon City Enterprise Weekly — 10 Nov. 1871 to 25 Dec. 1942**
- **Oregon City Enterprise — 8 Jan. 1911 to 28 Feb. 1950**

The Library therefore has microfilm spanning **all of 1926** for both the weekly and daily Enterprise-family runs.

Library holdings page:
https://www.orcity.org/490/Microfilm-Scanning-System

The Library says the film/fiche reader is located in the Carnegie Center and that users may print or save images. Questions about specific publication dates can be directed to the Library Reference Department. No contact was sent in this research pass.

## 6. Current evidence state

- **1925 Sanborn:** direct, visually verified restaurant use at 503; no business name.
- **1926 Kwality Restaurant statement:** official secondary/derivative; underlying ad still unrecovered.
- **December 1926 / 5 Jan. 1927 municipal finance text:** searchable primary text lists Kwality Cafe / Meals / $5; original page image remains a visual-render retrieval gap.
- **2000 re-survey provenance:** direct official documentation that the survey used newspaper research, specifically including Oregon City Enterprise daily and weekly titles, and that individual inventory forms were preserved through City/SHPO channels.
- **1926 Enterprise microfilm survival:** directly documented by Oregon City Public Library holdings.

The underlying 1926 ad remains unresolved.
