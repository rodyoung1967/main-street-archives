### Page 5 — Harding / non-target Main Street property control

Page 5 carries the article headed **“$30,500 MAIN STREET REALTY DEAL COMPLETED—NEW BUILDING MAY GO UP.”** The article describes a Main Street real-estate transaction involving property on the **east side of Main Street between Seventh and Eighth Streets**. It says the tract runs back toward the Southern Pacific tracks, except for **“one small corner” belonging to George A. Harding**, and discusses the purchaser’s possible future brick building after an existing lease expires.

This is useful direct evidence that George A. Harding held a small corner interest in a different Main Street tract in June 1916. It is **not** evidence about the target Fifth/Sixth Street frontage and must not be projected onto 501/503/505/505½/507 Main or onto Harding’s target-block ownership.
