# 1916 source-class closure — Morning Enterprise, tax list, and Sanborn maps

Date: 13 September 2026  
Status: **ONLINE ACCESS CLASSIFIED / MANUAL CALLOUTS CREATED WHERE REQUIRED**

This checkpoint resolves three source-class questions for the 1916 annual online audit. It distinguishes sources that do not exist online for 1916 from sources that remain available only through physical/restricted holdings.

## Morning Enterprise (daily)

The University of Oregon title record confirms that *Morning Enterprise* was published in Oregon City from 1911 to 1933 and was also issued on microfilm. The Oregon City digitization project, however, lists the online digitized *Morning Enterprise* run as **8 January 1911 through 23 December 1913**. Current public newspaper aggregators likewise expose Oregon City *Morning Enterprise* only through 1913.

The Oregon Historical Society newspaper-on-microfilm inventory confirms **Morning Enterprise, June 8, 1911–Nov. 2, 1933, Cabinet I, Drawer 5**.

**Disposition:** there is no ordinary public-online 1916 daily run to visually review. Convert the surviving 1916 daily newspaper work to **MF-064 — YEAR-CLOSEOUT / MANUAL CALLOUT**: inspect 1916 *Morning Enterprise* microfilm, with target emphasis on 501/503/503½/505/507 Main, Fifth/Main, occupants, businesses, property transactions, construction/demolition, moves, pool/billiard/cigar/soft-drink activity, and Harding/Hylton references. This manual gap does not block **ONLINE COMPLETE** once properly listed; it does block **ONLINE + MANUAL COMPLETE** until completed or steward-closed.

## Clackamas County 1916 Tax List

The Oregon Secretary of State's Clackamas County historical-records inventory explicitly lists **Clackamas County Tax List, 1916 (1 volume)** at the Museum of the Oregon Territory, Halon Room. The same inventory lists county website assessment/tax summaries only for modern periods and does not expose 1916 page images online.

**Disposition:** reasonable online source discovery establishes that the relevant 1916 tax volume is a physical holding rather than a public online image set. Convert to **MF-065 — YEAR-CLOSEOUT / MANUAL CALLOUT**: inspect the 1916 tax list/related assessment material for Original Oregon City Block 4 Lots 3–4, G. A. Harding and other target owners/agents, improvements/valuation, transfers, and any address/parcel linkage relevant to 501/503/503½/505/507 Main. Do not infer ownership or vacancy from lack of online access.

## Sanborn / fire-insurance map class

The City of Oregon City public Sanborn collection and Library of Congress catalog show the relevant online Oregon City editions around 1916 as **November 1911** and **1925**. No 1916 Sanborn edition is listed in the public series. The repository already preserves/reviews the 1911 and 1925 target-block maps as chronological brackets.

**Disposition:** the 1916 Sanborn source class is **ONLINE CLASS CLOSED**. There is no known 1916 Sanborn edition to retrieve. The 1911 and 1925 maps remain bracketing evidence and must not be treated as direct depictions of conditions in 1916. No manual callout is created solely to obtain a nonexistent 1916 Sanborn edition.
