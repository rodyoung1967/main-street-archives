# 1922 Farr Brothers identity clarification — 3 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL SECONDARY IDENTITY CLARIFICATION; NO 505 OCCUPANCY CLAIM**

Purpose: prevent the 1922 Oregon City merchant-list references to **Farr Brothers** from being used to backdate **Edwin F. Farr / Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main** without a direct bridge.

## Existing 1922 merchant evidence

The already visually verified 8 June 1922 *Banner-Courier* Buyers' Carnival roster lists **Farr Brothers** and **Timms Pool Hall** as separate Oregon City merchant entries. The already reviewed 17 August 1922 *Banner-Courier* labor/business list describes **Farr Brothers** as **Grocers, Butchers**, while separately identifying **Leland and Little** as **Billiards**.

These contemporary business descriptors already caution against reading the Farr Brothers merchant entry as a pool-hall reference.

## Official City identity source

Oregon City's official historic-resource page for **919 7th Street**, historic name **Farr Store**, states that this was the site of the **Farr Brothers Store** and identifies **Louis and Clarence Farr** as the Farr brothers associated with the store. The City says the store remained at that location until the mid-1940s.

Official source:
https://www.orcity.org/2142/919-7th-Street

The City's related Farr-family historic-resource pages independently describe the Farr Brothers business as a grocery/meat or grocer/butcher business on 7th Street. The 919 7th Street page is the controlling source used for this bounded clarification.

## Consequence for the 505 Main research

The archive separately has official historic-resource evidence that **Edwin F. and Alice Farr** were proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main Street**, but the exact start date of that business remains unresolved.

Therefore:

- do **not** treat a 1922 `Farr Brothers` merchant-list hit as evidence that Edwin F. Farr or Farr's Pool Hall was operating at 505 Main in 1922;
- do **not** merge Louis/Clarence Farr's Farr Brothers Store with Edwin F. Farr's later documented pool-hall business merely from the Farr surname;
- this clarification does **not** identify the unnamed July 1922 fire-adjacent pool hall;
- this clarification does **not** identify the May 1925 billiards/cigars occupant at 505 Main;
- Farr's Pool Hall at 505 remains documented at an unresolved date and still requires a contemporary directory, advertisement, license, or other dated exact-address source to bridge it to 1925.

## Research handling

No new source/evidence ID or person/business merge is created by this checkpoint because its function is to preserve a bounded anti-conflation decision using an official secondary identity source, not to create a new 501/503/505 occupancy assignment. Existing Farr's Pool Hall and 1922 billiards records remain authoritative for their own claims.
