## Main Street sequence observed

The relevant ledger page visually shows the Main Street run around the target properties as:

**511 Main → 509 Main → 507 Main → [unnumbered entry] → 503 Main → 503½ Main → 501 Main**

The house-number field for the entry between 507 and 503 is blank in the supplied image.

Because **505** is numerically the address expected between 507 and 503, this unnumbered account is a plausible candidate for 505 Main, but the ledger screenshot does not itself prove that identification.

## Owner/agent pattern

**G. A. Harding, Agt.** appears repeatedly as owner/agent for this group of Main Street entries, including the target block.

## Unnumbered entry between 507 and 503

The tenant field for the unnumbered entry contains a standalone handwritten **"V"**.

Working interpretation: **V probably means Vacant**.

Reasons:

- it appears in the tenant field rather than as a person's/business name;
- the ledger format otherwise places named tenants in this field;
- the mark is a simple standalone V.

This interpretation should be preserved as **probable, not certain**, until confirmed by a ledger key, another page using V in an obviously vacant context, or another municipal record.

The combined working hypothesis is therefore:

> The blank-number entry between 507 and 503 may represent 505 Main, and its tenant field may indicate vacancy.

Confidence in this full statement is **moderate / hypothesis only**, because the house number is not written.

Do not promote it to “505 Main was vacant” without corroboration.

## 503 and 503½

The ledger clearly and separately records:

- **503 Main**
- **503½ Main**

Both occur immediately after the unnumbered/V entry and are associated with G. A. Harding, Agt. as owner/agent. Faint tenant names are present but are not yet transcribed confidently.

The separate 503½ entry is important because it shows the ledger recognized half-addresses directly and argues against treating 503½ as a substitute notation for 505.

## Comparison with 1916–1917 Book 4

Book 4 later shows:

**511 → 509 → 507 → 503 → 503½ → 501 Main**

with no separate 505 entry and no obvious unnumbered line occupying the 505 position in the reviewed sequence.

Taken together:

- 1914–15: there is an **unnumbered account between 507 and 503**, tenant marked probable **V = Vacant**;
- 1916–17: **505 is absent from the expected sequence**, while 503 and 503½ remain separately recorded.

This creates a potentially significant lead on 505 Main's water-service/occupancy status during the 1914–1917 gap, but does not yet establish the reason.

## Confidence / handling rules

- 503 Main recorded: **high confidence**.
- 503½ Main recorded separately: **high confidence**.
- G. A. Harding, Agt. owner/agent pattern: **high confidence visual reading**.
- Blank-number entry exists between 507 and 503: **high confidence**.
- Standalone V appears in tenant field of that entry: **high confidence visual observation**.
- V means Vacant: **probable / moderate-high confidence interpretation**.
- Blank-number entry equals 505 Main: **plausible / moderate confidence hypothesis only**.
- “505 Main was vacant”: **not yet established**.
