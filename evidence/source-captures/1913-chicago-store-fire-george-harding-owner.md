# December 1913 Chicago Store fire — George Harding building-owner lead for 505 Main

Status: **strong primary newspaper OCR/text evidence; page-image visual verification still pending**

The *Oregon City Courier* front-page report for **25 December 1913** describes the Chicago clothing store fire that occurred the previous Saturday night.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1913-12-25/ed-1/seq-1/ocr/

## What the report says

Under the headline **THREE BIG FIRES IN THREE NIGHTS**, the article says the **Chicago clothing store on Main street, south of the postoffice, was gutted by fire**. It says the interior was eaten by flames, the goods were ruined, the stock was reportedly fully insured, and then states:

**The building was owned by George Harding.**

## Relationship to 505 Main

Independent primary advertising places the **Chicago Store at 505 Main Street during 1911–1913**, including exact-address ads in April 1911 and January 1913. Therefore the fire report creates a strong property-history inference that **George Harding owned the building/premises occupied by the Chicago Store at 505 Main at the time of the December 1913 fire**.

The fire article itself does **not** print `505 Main`; it says Main Street south of the post office. The 505 identification comes from cross-referencing the Chicago Store's independently documented exact address.

Because the original 25 December page image has not yet been successfully retrieved for direct visual inspection in the current environment, preserve the distinction:

- **Very strong primary text/OCR:** Chicago Store fire; George Harding owned the building.
- **Strong cross-source inference:** that building was the documented 505 Main Chicago Store premises.
- **Visual certification:** pending original page-image review.
