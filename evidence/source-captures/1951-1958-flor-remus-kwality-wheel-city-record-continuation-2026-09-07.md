## Purpose

Continue the Oregon-side predecessor chain leading into The Wheel at 503 Main without merging business ownership, operation/management, liquor licensing, trade-name use, tenancy, telephone listing, or real-estate ownership.

The working cross-source sequence remains potentially important:

- **5 Dec. 1951** — Albert D. Flor + Margaret Remus jointly appear in an Oregon City liquor-license approval list;
- **Aug. 1953 claims, approved 10 Sep. 1953** — `Al & Marge Kwality Cafe` appears as a City payee/vendor;
- **1953** — Fred Remus is described by the City's 902 Fifth historic inventory as a cook at Kwality Cafe;
- **1957** — the City's later historic-resource synthesis says Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor were managing the restaurant/cocktail bar at 503 Main;

Important limits:

This precision strengthens the business-state evidence because `Al & Marge Kwality Cafe` is not merely an index phrase: it is a dated municipal vendor/payee entry. It still does **not** prove that `Al` = Albert D. Flor or `Marge` = Margaret Remus.
