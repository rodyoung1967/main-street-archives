# 1955–1959 505 Main post-sale regional-newspaper index pass

Date reviewed: **8 September 2026**

Status: **BOUNDED PUBLIC-INDEX SEARCH / NO NEW SUCCESSOR, TRADE NAME, SELLER, BUYER, OR TELEPHONE SUBSCRIBER IDENTIFIED.**

Purpose: test whether publicly indexed Oregon newspapers outside the unavailable local *Enterprise Courier* run can bridge the visually verified **20 November 1955** sale advertisement at 505 Main to a named successor or later trade name.

## Known primary anchor

The repository preserves the original *Eugene Register-Guard*, 20 November 1955, Image 30 at:

- `newspapers/eugene-register-guard/1955-11-20-image-30.jp2`
- source capture: `evidence/source-captures/1955-11-20-505-main-beer-pool-sale-ad-original-ocr.md`

Visual inspection establishes an unnamed, long-established **beer / pool** tavern business at **505 Main Street, Oregon City**, offered for sale with three pool tables, bottled wine/beer sales, amusement machines, and contact **Phone 9179**. The advertisement itself does not name the business, seller, or buyer.

## Repeated-advertisement test

Public search/index passes tested distinctive combinations from the advertisement, including:

- `50 kegs` + `3 pool tables`;
- `BEER POOL` + Oregon City;
- `one of the best taverns` / `long established business`;
- `reasonable down payment will handle` + Oregon City;
- exact address **505 Main Street, Oregon City**;
- contact **9179**; and
- variants of the OCR-corrupted address/telephone wording.

The only defensible indexed match recovered was the already-known **20 November 1955** *Eugene Register-Guard* advertisement. No second indexed copy was recovered that adds a seller name, trade name, asking price, altered contact number, sale-completion language, or buyer identity.

Classification: **BOUNDED REPEATED-AD SEARCH RESULT / NOT NEGATIVE EVIDENCE**. The failure to surface another indexed ad does not prove the advertisement ran only once.

## 1956–1959 successor/address test

Additional public-index searches tested combinations of:

- `505 Main` / `505 Main Street` + Oregon City;
- beer / pool / tavern / recreation;
- 1956, 1957, 1958 and 1959;
- `Well's Recreation` / `Wells Recreation` and spelling variants;
- `Ray Hansen` / Hansen; and
- telephone **9179**.

No defensible contemporary public-index result from 1956–1959 identified:

- a successor operator at 505;
- the seller or buyer of the 1955 business;
- a continuing `Well's Recreation` trade name;
- an early `505 Tavern` occurrence;
- Ray Hansen at 505; or
- the subscriber to 9179.

Unrelated statewide uses of the digits `9179`, modern 505 Main businesses, and later telephone-number matches were rejected.

Classification: **BOUNDED REGIONAL-NEWSPAPER / PUBLIC-INDEX NO-HIT; RETRIEVAL GAP, NOT ABSENCE PROOF.**

## Evidence boundary

This pass does **not** narrow the successor chronology enough to create a new timeline event. In particular, do not infer that:

- Well's Recreation ended in November 1955;
- the advertised business actually sold;
- a buyer took possession immediately;
- 505 Tavern began between 1956 and 1959;
- Ray Hansen was the buyer/operator; or
- 9179 belonged to any Wells/Harr/Hansen candidate.

## Research consequence

The highest-value unresolved post-sale sources remain:

1. the actual **1953 T. W. Salisbury Oregon City directory** entry and cross-references for 505 / Well's Recreation;
2. a **1953–1956 Oregon City Pacific Telephone directory** identifying subscriber 9179 (`MF-056`, prepared but not sent);
3. the **1960 Oregon City Polk directory** 505 Main entry;
4. Clackamas County **1959–1964 assumed-business-name** records for 505 Tavern / Hansen / predecessor names;
5. historical **OLCC licensee-history, trade-name, application and transfer** records; and
6. the local **Enterprise Courier** microfilm for 1955–1959, which remains a separate offline/manual route.

No institutional request was sent and no fee or purchase was authorized during this pass.
