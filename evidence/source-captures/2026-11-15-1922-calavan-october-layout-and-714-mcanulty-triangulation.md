# October 1922 Pirtle–Calavan and 714 Main McAnulty address triangulation

**Date:** 15 November 2026. **Year status:** IN PROGRESS. **New evidence state:** OCR/TEXT ONLY for October 26; previously verified source descriptions retained.

## 26 October 1922 Banner-Courier p. 3 — pool hall sale
Repo OCR `newspapers/banner-courier/1922-10/ocr/1922-10-26-seq-03.txt` reads: **'Ray Pirtle has sold his pool hall to G. C. Calavan of Portland. Mr. Calavan has taken possession.'** Source scan: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-10-26/ed-1/seq-3.pdf

**Important OCR-layout caveat:** The OCR interleaves newspaper columns. The Pirtle item occurs near Farr/Sawtell/Park community items and *before* an OCR-rendered 'MOLALLA' heading later in the text. Therefore the text order **cannot establish which community heading governs the item**. A direct PDF retrieval attempt during this continuation failed, so **the October notice has NOT been newly visually verified**. Do not state it was in Molalla, or that G. C. Calavan = C. G. Calavan, solely from these OCR fragments.

## 28 December 1922 Banner-Courier p. 8 — Molalla control
The separately visually verified December source explicitly places **Molalla Pastime Pool Hall** and **C. G. Calavan → P. C. Calavan** in the **Molalla** section. See `evidence/source-captures/2026-11-15-1922-license-and-molalla-poolhall-visual-verification.md`. ## 714 Main alternative for Barry & McAnulty
The existing **visually verified** 30 November 1922 Banner p.1 license note reports **Barry and McAnulty** were granted a pool-hall license in their former restaurant room; it does **not** give an address. The pre-existing source capture `evidence/source-captures/1922-11-30-mcanulty-barry-pool-hall-license.md` already cites the **Petzold Building, 714 Main** historic-sites entry: McAnulty family restaurant/confectionery 1924–1930, and 1936–1938 Petzold family records noting Barry & McAnulty pool-hall ownership. Primary historic inventory: https://heritagedata.prd.state.or.us/historic/index.cfm?do=v.dsp_siteSummary&resultDisplay=29986

**Critical distinction:** The later 714 Main association is **not proof of the 1922 licensed room's address**. Treat 714 as a serious competing location lead, not a confirmed 1922 placement at either 714 or 505.
