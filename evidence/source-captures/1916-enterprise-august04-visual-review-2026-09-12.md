### Page 8 — Hawley Paper construction chronology south of the target block

The upper-left article is headed **“Second Addition to Hawley Paper Mill Postponed.”** Its subhead states that **erection of buildings along Main Street to the alley between Fourth and Fifth will probably begin early next year**. The article explains that work on the second addition was being delayed until work under way was completed and discusses the Hawley mill program.

This is useful physical-development chronology immediately south of Fifth Street, but the wording places the described Main Street construction **between Fourth and Fifth**, not in the target block between Fifth and Sixth. It therefore must not be assigned to 501/503/505/505½/507 Main.
