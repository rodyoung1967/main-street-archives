### Printed advertisement

A lower-right advertisement headed **KRYPTOK GLASSES — THE INVISIBLE BIFOCALS** names:

**DR. FREEZE**  
**Eye Specialist**  
**505½ Main Street, Oregon City**

The ad discusses optical instruments, trial lenses, eye comfort, consultation and fitting glasses.

A lower-page Dr. Freeze advertisement again prints **505½ Main St., Oregon City** and adds the move notice:

> After July 1 will be at my new location 207 Masonic Bldg.

This directly narrows Dr. Freeze's 505½ occupancy: he was still advertising at 505½ Main on **29 June 1922**, but announced that his new location would be **207 Masonic Building after 1 July**.

## Significance for 505 Main chronology

This supplies precise early post-rebuild tenant/address anchors:

- **10 Oct 1918:** Wolf & Miller directly at **505 Main**, in the predecessor-era premises;
- City historic inventory dates the surviving 505 building approximately **c.1920**;
- **8 Apr 1921:** Dr. Freeze is directly and visually documented at **505½ Main**;
- **29 Jun 1922:** Dr. Freeze is still advertising **505½ Main**, while announcing a move to **207 Masonic Building after 1 July**;
- **18 Jul 1922:** Fifth/Main fire begins in the vacant former Schieremann tailor room; an adjoining pool hall is damaged;
- **1925 Sanborn:** 505 is visually mapped as a separate billiards/cigars occupancy.

The June 1922 move is potentially useful to the fire reconstruction because it shows a **half-numbered 505-associated professional occupancy ending immediately before the July fire**. It does **not** establish what occupied the ground-floor 505 storefront, that 505½ was physically above 505, that Dr. Freeze's former office was the Schieremann room, or that the July fire-adjacent pool hall was at 505. Those remain separate questions requiring premises-specific evidence.

## Improvement over existing archive wording

Existing evidence `E-028` and source `S-035` broadly record Dr. Freeze at 505 / 505½ Main during 1921–1922, with the current `S-035` URL pointing to an 11 November 1921 advertisement. The 8 April 1921 page moves the direct exact-address anchor earlier by at least seven months; the 29 June 1922 ad now supplies a visually verified **end-of-occupancy/move marker** immediately before the fire.

## Research targets

1. Determine whether **505½** represented an upstairs office, rear room, or other subdivision of the 505 premises.
2. Search July 1922 records for the new occupant, vacancy, or reuse of the 505½ space after Freeze moved.
3. Find the exact July 1922 ground-floor occupant of **505 Main** independently of the 505½ office.
4. Search January–7 April 1921 issues for an earlier 505½ Main advertisement or opening/move notice.
5. Search late 1920 for Dr. Freeze at 505/505½ to determine whether his occupancy began during 1920.
6. Search for construction/completion notices for the c.1920 505 building immediately preceding the April 1921 occupancy.
