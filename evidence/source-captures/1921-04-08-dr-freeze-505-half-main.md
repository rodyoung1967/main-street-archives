## Printed advertisement

A lower-right advertisement headed **KRYPTOK GLASSES — THE INVISIBLE BIFOCALS** names:

**DR. FREEZE**  
**Eye Specialist**  
**505½ Main Street, Oregon City**

The ad discusses optical instruments, trial lenses, eye comfort, consultation and fitting glasses.

## Significance for 505 Main chronology

This supplies a precise early post-rebuild tenant/address anchor:

- **10 Oct 1918:** Wolf & Miller directly at **505 Main**, in the predecessor-era premises;
- City historic inventory dates the surviving 505 building approximately **c.1920**;
- **8 Apr 1921:** Dr. Freeze is directly and visually documented at **505½ Main**;
- **1925 Sanborn:** 505 is visually mapped as a separate billiards/cigars occupancy.

Thus by April 1921 the 505 address was supporting at least a half-number/room-style professional occupancy. This is consistent with the possibility that the c.1920 rebuilt premises had already been completed, but the advertisement alone does **not** prove the construction date, floor/room arrangement, or that Dr. Freeze occupied the entire 505 building.

## Improvement over existing archive wording

Existing evidence `E-028` and source `S-035` broadly record Dr. Freeze at 505 / 505½ Main during 1921–1922, with the current `S-035` URL pointing to an 11 November 1921 advertisement. The 8 April 1921 page moves the direct exact-address anchor earlier by at least seven months and is visually verified in this research pass.
