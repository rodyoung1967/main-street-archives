# 1921 annual online source-class closeout — 2026-09-20

## Certification

This certification applies the requirements in `RESEARCH-STANDARD.md`. It certifies completion of the defined online research pass, not resolution of every historical question and not completion of manual/restricted sources.

## Newspaper title universe and coverage

### Oregon City Enterprise — online and visually reviewed

The complete 1921 weekly run used for this audit is **52 issues / 416 pages**, January 7 through December 30. Every online-retrievable page in that run was visually inspected from the original Historic Oregon Newspapers scans or faithful renders during the 19–20 September annual-audit session.

Standalone visual-review records now exist for all months. The previously missing repository documentation for August, October, November and December was reconciled on 20 September from the completed session state and render workflows.

Result: no new securely attributable 501/503/505/500-block chronology fact was promoted from the weekly run beyond already registered findings/leads. The January 28 historical-photo-display article remains a useful provenance lead. Monthly no-hits are coverage controls only.

### Morning Enterprise — 1921 title, not publicly digitized for 1921

The daily **Morning Enterprise** is directly documented as active in 1921, but the public Historic Oregon Newspapers / Oregon City digitization project exposes the re-filmed/digitized Morning Enterprise run only through December 1913. The 1921 daily run is therefore **MF-069**, a manual microfilm/holdings callout.

### The Banner-Courier — 1921 title, complete 1921 online image run not located

A closeout title-universe check identified **The Banner-Courier** as a third relevant Oregon City newspaper in 1921. ODNP documentation specifically identifies the Oregon City preservation project's re-filmed/digitized Banner-Courier run as Jan.–Dec. 1922. Reasonable public/indexed recovery attempts did not expose a complete 1921 page-image corpus. The 1921 run is therefore **MF-071**, a manual microfilm/physical-run callout.

No newspaper source silence is treated as evidence of absence.
