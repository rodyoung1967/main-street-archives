## Current family/oral-history working model preserved

- **Ray Hansen belongs to the 505 tavern business chain**, not automatically to the 505 real-estate chain.
- The project-steward recollection places Hansen's acquisition/operation around the **early 1960s**, with a rough remembered duration of **about ten years**.
- Search both **Hansen** and **Hanson**, but treat `Hanson` only as a spelling-search variant unless a contemporary source proves that spelling belongs to the same person.
- **Wolf belongs to the 505 real-estate hypothesis**: the current family recollection is that a Wolf individual/family may have owned 505 and may have sold the building directly to Dick and Sheila Wiitanen.
- An older competing family account places **Nesmeth / Nemeth / Nesmith** as the pre-Wiitanen seller at 505. Preserve the conflict until deed/assessor evidence resolves it.
- Do not infer that Hansen owned the building merely because he may have owned/operated the tavern business.

These oral-history propositions are preserved as working hypotheses, not documentary fact.
