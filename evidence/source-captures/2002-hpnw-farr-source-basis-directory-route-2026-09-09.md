# 2002 HPNW survey source basis — Farr / 505 directory-retrieval route

Date researched: 9 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL SURVEY METHODOLOGY / BIBLIOGRAPHY VERIFIED; SPECIFIC SOURCE BEHIND THE FARR STATEMENT UNRESOLVED.**

Purpose: preserve a newly useful provenance constraint for the City of Oregon City's 2002-era statement that **Edwin F. and Alice Farr were proprietors of Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main Street**. This checkpoint narrows the source classes and directory years that the Historic Preservation Northwest (HPNW) survey team is documented to have used, but it does not identify which source actually supports the Farr statement.

## Official report

City of Oregon City, **McLoughlin Conservation District 2002 Resurvey — Final Report**, prepared by **Historic Preservation Northwest (HPNW)**, revised 4 December 2003:

https://www.orcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/4359/2002-McLoughlin-Survey-Final-Report-PDF

The report says the City hired HPNW for the survey work completed in fall 2002. Its methodology text states that consultants performed background research on individual resources using **Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps, city building permit records, county deed records, and city directories**, then compiled Statements of Significance for the intensively surveyed resources.

## Connection to the Farr / 505 question

The separate City historic-resource form for **401 Third Street**, recorded **12 April 2002** and credited to **Alex McMurry / HPNW**, identifies Edwin F. and Alice Farr as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main Street** and says they acquired the Third Street residence in 1934.

The project already preserves the critical evidence boundary: the **1934 residence acquisition is not a proved pool-hall opening date**.

The HPNW final report now supplies a useful provenance constraint. Because the same survey program is explicitly documented as using city directories, permits, deeds and Sanborn maps, and because its bibliography specifically names Oregon City directory years **1933, 1941 and 1947** within the Farr/Harr interval, those editions should be treated as high-value source-basis retrieval targets for testing the Farr statement.

This does **not** establish that the 401 Third Farr sentence came from the 1933 directory, the 1941 directory, the 1947 directory, or any particular deed/permit. The final report gives the project-level research corpus, not a footnote mapping an individual sentence to a source.

## Research consequence

The Farr/505 directory sequence should explicitly include **1933** in addition to the already prioritized 1941 and 1947–48 routes:

1. **1933 Oregon City directory** — test whether Edwin F. Farr, Alice Farr, Farr's Pool Hall, 505 Main, 505½ Main, or another pool/billiards/cigars operator appears before the 1934 residence date and before the 1936 photographed Farr state.
2. **1941 Oregon City directory** — test whether Farr remains at 505, whether Harr appears, and verify the City's reported Dr. Roy Briggs / 505½ entry.
3. **1947 / 1947–48 directory material** — test the immediate Farr→Harr bracket before the direct 1948 municipal Harr evidence.

For each volume, inspect actual page images, including alphabetic, classified and street/address sections where available. A catalog citation or later survey transcription does not count as direct directory evidence.

A separate West Linn nomination already supplies a concrete title lead for a **1933 Pacific Telephone & Telegraph directory described as “Oregon City and Clackamas County.”** That title should be used as an access-search term, but it should not be silently equated with the generic “Oregon City directories — 1933” bibliography entry until the actual volume/source identity is checked.

## What this does not prove

This checkpoint does not prove:

- Farr's Pool Hall was operating in 1933;
- Edwin F. Farr was the unnamed 1925 billiards/cigars operator at 505;
- the 1933, 1941 or 1947 directory supplied the City's Farr assertion;
- property ownership at 505;
- the pool hall's opening date;
- the Farr→Harr transfer date; or
- identity of Edwin F. Farr with any similarly named Farr solely from the shared name.
