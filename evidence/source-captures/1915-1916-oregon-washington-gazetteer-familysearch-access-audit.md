### Directory structure

This is an alphabetical business/professional gazetteer, not a resident directory or reverse street directory. Oregon City entries normally give a name and trade but no street address. Consequently:

- the section prints no direct listing for **501, 503, 505, 507, 509, or 511 Main**;
- it prints no target numbered Fifth Street entry, including **102, 104, 106, 107, 108, or 110 Fifth**;
- its silence cannot identify vacancy, non-use, a move date, or a missing occupant.

### Target-name results

The complete town-section scan visually shows:

- **Brightbell H P, grocer** on p. 260. `Brightbell` is the directory's printed spelling; it is treated as a probable reference to the separately documented H. P. Brightbill (`P-042` / `BUS-027`), not silently normalized or merged with Henry Brightbill (`P-034`). No address is printed.
- **Fashion Livery Stable, G A Bergren proprietor** on p. 261. No address is printed, so this listing is not used to equate the business with 110 Fifth or to backdate another Fifth Street location.
- **Harding Geo A, drugs** on p. 261 (`P-001` / `BUS-007`). No address is printed.
- **Holman Roswell L, undertaker** on p. 261 (`P-050`). No address is printed.
- **Randall Thos P, postmaster** on p. 262 (`P-051`). The town section does not list him as Holman's undertaking partner; publication timing and classification cannot be fixed more narrowly from this undated entry.

No Hylton, W. W. Bradley, Frank Cox, Scripture, May, or Scripture & May entry appears in the visually reviewed three-page Oregon City town section. That is a section-coverage statement only, not proof that any person or business was absent from Oregon City or from an address.

## Classified-business review

Targeted full-text discovery across all four DGS parts located relevant classified pages, which were then checked against the scan:

- **Druggists**, printed p. **1129**, Part 3 image **273**, ARK `3QS7-99NM-PKR1`: the Oregon City line lists **Charman & Co; Harding G A; Huntley Bros Co; Jones Drug Co**. This independently corroborates Harding's trade, but no street address is printed.
- **Undertakers and Embalmers**, printed pp. **1641–1642**, Part 4 images **291–292**, ARKs `3QSQ-G9NM-F22Q` and `3QS7-99NM-F2T4`: the continuation on p. 1642 lists **Oregon City — Holman R L**. Randall is not printed in the Oregon City undertaker line. Both page images are preserved in the repository.

Full-text results were used to test the remaining target names and business terms across all four parts. No additional scan-verified exact-address hit was found. This was a targeted classified review, not an exhaustive transcription of every classified page, and search silence is not negative historical evidence.

## Historical disposition

The source independently corroborates business/trade continuity for Harding, the printed Brightbell/Brightbill target, Holman, and Fashion Livery Stable, while identifying Randall as postmaster in the town section. It does **not** resolve any 501–511 Main occupant, any exact numbered Fifth Street business, the unnumbered water-ledger row in the expected 505 position, or the possible tenant reading at 110 Fifth.

Manual callout `1915-D01` is complete because authenticated FamilySearch access recovered and visually reviewed the complete Oregon City town section and the relevant located classified pages. The broader 1915 year remains **IN PROGRESS** for unrelated newspaper and other-source work.
