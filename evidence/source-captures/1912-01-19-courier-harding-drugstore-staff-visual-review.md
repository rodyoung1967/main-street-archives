## Direct scan reading

The upper-left **THE LOCAL NEWS** column directly reports that **Percy Cross** had accepted a position in the **Harding drugstore** during the absence of **A. B. Wilmot**.

The scan was retrieved as a genuine one-page PDF from Historic Oregon Newspapers and rendered to an image for direct visual inspection. The names and business wording above were read from the scan, not accepted from OCR alone.

## What this proves

- Harding Drug Store was actively staffed/operating on 19 January 1912.
- Percy Cross had accepted a position there by that date.
- A. B. Wilmot was associated with the store and was absent at the time of the notice.

The notice does **not** state either man's formal job title, professional license, ownership interest, duration of employment, or reason/duration of Wilmot's absence.

## Address limitation

This page does **not** print Harding Drug Store's street number. Separate visually verified evidence on **5 January 1912** places **Geo. A. Harding at 511 Main** (`E-092`), and a visually verified 7 September item describes a Willamette Building room **over Harding Drug Store** (`E-138`). The 19 January item therefore strengthens same-month operating/staff chronology for the known Harding business, but it is **not an independent exact-address source** and must not be used by itself to assign Cross or Wilmot to 511 Main.
