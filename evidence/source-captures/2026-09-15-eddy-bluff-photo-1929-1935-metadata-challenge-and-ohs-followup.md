## Bridge chronological control

The completed Oregon City–West Linn bridge is plainly visible. The formal opening/dedication was 28 December 1922. This provides an early chronological control for dating the photograph. It is not presented as a correction to CCHS metadata.

Evidence state: **DOCUMENTED chronology + DIRECT VISUAL landmark**.

## Stronger lower bound — Railroad Avenue after the 1929 improvement

The 1934 Municipal Activities Report contains a matched pair captioned:
- `Railroad Avenue from Fifth to Sixth Street, before improvement.`
- `Railroad Avenue from Fifth to Sixth Street, after improvement.`

The project steward supplied faithful images of both pages, permitting direct visual comparison.

The 1929 Municipal Activities Report independently documents the Railroad Avenue improvement from Fifth to Seventh Streets: full 40-foot grading, an 8-foot concrete sidewalk and curb, 32 feet of six-inch concrete roadway, 300 feet of sewer, 1,853 square yards of concrete, total cost $7,593.73.

### Visual match

The bluff photograph matches the **after-improvement** condition.

Strong comparison points:
1. a stop sign appears in the same Railroad Avenue/Fifth Street spatial relationship as in the `after improvement` photograph;
2. the stop-sign post has a conspicuous alternating light/dark or banded treatment in both views;
3. the developed roadway/sidewalk/curb geometry agrees with the after view;
4. the west-side house/building relationship is substantially closer to the later view;
5. the railroad/street edge reads as the developed post-project corridor.

Evidence state: **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE / VISUAL-SPATIAL INFERENCE**.

### Dating consequence

The strongest present lower-bound year is **1929**, pending recovery of the exact project completion month/day.

## Upper-bound control — old Clackamas County courthouse

The old Clackamas County courthouse with clock tower is plainly visible. The Clackamas County Family History Society identifies the older courthouse as finished in 1885 and replaced in 1936–37. The City of Oregon City's historical summary likewise states that a new Clackamas County Courthouse was constructed in 1936–1937.

Until the exact demolition/start-of-construction milestone is documented, retain **1935/early 1936** as the practical upper end rather than claiming an exact cutoff.

Evidence state: **DIRECT VISUAL landmark + DOCUMENTED replacement chronology**.

## Current working date

**Circa 1929–1935/36.**

Confidence:
- moderate-to-strong for 1929-or-later from the Railroad Avenue comparison;
- moderate for the 1935/early-1936 upper endpoint pending exact courthouse demolition/construction chronology.

The range is a project research estimate intended to help CCHS date the image; it is not asserted as institutional metadata.

## Additional supporting control — Elks Lodge

A large bridge-adjacent building is strongly consistent in position and massing with the replacement Oregon City Elks Lodge, whose predecessor burned 2 December 1922 and whose replacement was completed in 1923. This supports the post-1923 setting but does not materially narrow the stronger 1929–1935/36 bracket.
