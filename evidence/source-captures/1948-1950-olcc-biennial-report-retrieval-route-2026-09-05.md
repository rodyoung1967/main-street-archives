# 1948–1950 OLCC Biennial Report — retrieval route for licensing context

Date reviewed: 5 September 2026

Status: **OFFICIAL SECONDARY BIBLIOGRAPHIC LOCATOR + CONTEMPORARY OFFICIAL-REPORT PUBLICITY — ACTUAL BIENNIAL REPORT NOT RECOVERED; NO FARR/HARR/505 CLAIM PROMOTED.**

Purpose: preserve a precise bibliographic route to an Oregon Liquor Control Commission report immediately adjacent to the unresolved Farr's Pool Hall → Harr's Recreation Center transition at 505 Main Street, without treating report existence as evidence that it contains a named Oregon City licensee list.

## Definite report citation

The Oregon Legislative Assembly's Legislative Policy and Research Office, in its 4 January 2021 background brief on alcoholic-beverage legislation, cites:

**Oregon Liquor Control Commission, _1948–1950 Biennial Report_, pages 5–20 (1950).**

Official current source:

`https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/lpro/Publications/Background-Brief-Recent-Alcoholic-Beverage-Industry-Legislation.pdf`

This proves that a report under that title/date existed and was sufficiently accessible to Oregon legislative researchers for page-specific citation. The brief uses pages 5–20 for OLCC historical background, not for a Farr/Harr/505 claim.

## Contemporary evidence that detailed OLCC reports were being used in 1948

The *Medford Mail Tribune*, 19 May 1948, printed a public-information piece headed **READ THE FACTS — From Official Reports of the Oregon Liquor Control Commission**. The article gives 1947-versus-1948 enforcement and licensing-investigation figures, including investigations involving new-license applications and questionable operations.

Historic Oregon Newspapers:

`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1948-05-19/ed-1/seq-3/`

This confirms that detailed Commission reporting was circulating contemporaneously, but the newspaper item does not name Farr, Harr, Harr's Recreation Center, Farr's Pool Hall or 505 Main Street.

## Statutory/public-record context

Later codified Oregon law explicitly treated the Commission's regular monthly, quarterly and annual reports, financial statements, sales reports and minutes as public writings. A 1959 ORS archive exposes that language at ORS 471.785.

This is useful access/provenance context only. It does not prove that every 1940s report, appendix or underlying licensing record survives today.

Targeted current searches for the exact report title and variants across ordinary web indexing, State Library of Oregon results, Internet Archive/HathiTrust/Google Books-style indexing and catalog-style searches did **not** expose a readable scan or catalog record with report contents in this pass.

## Why the report is worth retrieving

The report lies directly after the 1948 municipal Harr evidence and may contain statewide or county licensing statistics, policy changes, license classifications, enforcement summaries, appendices, tables or references useful for interpreting the 1945–1948 licensing record universe.

However, no evidence recovered so far establishes that the report prints individual licensees, Clackamas County applicant names, 505 Main, Farr or Harr. Do not prioritize it ahead of sources already known to be address/name-oriented.

If the report is obtained, visually inspect:

1. title/publication page and complete table of contents;
2. all licensing/license-tax sections;
3. all appendices and tables;
4. any county-, city-, renewal-district- or premises-level lists;
5. any transfer, nonrenewal or license-action summaries;
6. indexes for Oregon City, Clackamas County, Farr, Harr and recreation/pool-hall terminology.

Preserve the full context if a named licensee list is found rather than extracting only Farr/Harr hits.
