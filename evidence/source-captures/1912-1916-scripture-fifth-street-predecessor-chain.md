# Scripture shop predecessor chain — Fifth Street near Main, 1912–1916

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED address/use anchors; business-continuity details partly unresolved**

Purpose: document the earlier blacksmith/wagon/carriage shop sequence on Fifth Street near Main that may precede Scripture & May at 108 Fifth and, potentially, the later May & Washburn garage destroyed in the July 1922 fire.

## 5 Jan 1912 — Scripture & Beauliau on Fifth Street near Main

The actual scanned page of the **Oregon City Courier, 5 Jan 1912, p. 6** was visually inspected.

A business advertisement reads:

- **Scripture & Beauliau**
- general blacksmithing and wagonmaking
- special attention to horseshoeing
- **Fifth Street, near Main, Oregon City**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-01-05/ed-1/seq-6.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This places a Scripture-associated blacksmith/wagon business in the same short Fifth Street near-Main zone at least four years before Oregon City Laundry moved to Fifth Street.

## 17 Feb 1916 — Oregon City Laundry switches to Fifth Street

A separate visually reviewed **17 Feb 1916 Oregon City Courier** item states that E. M. Blakeslee traded away the rear-Courier property on which he had intended to build and acquired a **Fifth Street location** for the Oregon City Laundry instead.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-02-17/ed-1/seq-7.pdf

By 2 Apr 1916, the Sunday Oregonian reports that the new laundry building on **Fifth Street just off Main** was nearing completion.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83045782/1916-04-02/ed-1/seq-14.pdf

## 26 Oct 1916 — Scripture & May precisely at 108 Fifth

The actual scanned page of the **Oregon City Courier, 26 Oct 1916, p. 5** was visually inspected.

A Scripture & May notice states:

- horseshoeing and disc-harrow sharpening
- **SCRIPTURE & MAY**
- **108 Fifth St.**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-10-26/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This is currently the earliest visually verified exact-number anchor recovered for the Scripture & May shop at 108 Fifth.

## Relationship to the laundry

By the 24 Oct 1918 Courier business guide, the same immediate Fifth Street cluster is explicitly numbered:

- **Scripture & May — 108 Fifth St.**
- **Oregon City Laundry — 109 Fifth St.**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-10-24/ed-1/seq-6/

Thus the documented sequence is:

1. **1912** — Scripture & Beauliau, Fifth Street near Main;
2. **Feb–Apr 1916** — Oregon City Laundry acquires a Fifth Street site and builds just off Main;
3. **Oct 1916** — Scripture & May at **108 Fifth**;
4. **1918** — Scripture & May at **108 Fifth**, laundry at **109 Fifth**;
5. **Dec 1920 onward** — May & Washburn partnership is documented;
6. **Jul 1922** — May & Washburn's frame garage and Oregon City Laundry are destroyed in the same Fifth Street fire.

## Interpretation

This materially strengthens the possibility of a **physical/business predecessor chain** from Scripture & Beauliau → Scripture & May → May & Washburn in the near-Main Fifth Street garage/shop premises.

However, the following remain unproven:

- that Scripture & Beauliau occupied the exact later 108 Fifth footprint in 1912;
- that the `May` in Scripture & May is the same person as the `May` in May & Washburn;
- that May & Washburn inherited or continued the 108 Fifth premises;
- that 108 Fifth was the exact May & Washburn garage destroyed in July 1922.

## Why this matters for the 1922 block reconstruction

If May & Washburn can be tied to 108 Fifth, then the pre-fire cluster would gain two consecutive exact-number anchors:

- **108 Fifth — garage/blacksmith lineage**
- **109 Fifth — Oregon City Laundry**

That would substantially improve the ability to position the restaurant, Miller shoe-repair shop, Ketchner confectionery, and Main Street ignition premises relative to the corner.

## Confidence

- Scripture & Beauliau on Fifth Street near Main in Jan 1912: **Very High / visually verified**
- Scripture & May at 108 Fifth by Oct 1916: **Very High / visually verified**
- Laundry moved to Fifth Street in Feb 1916: **Very High / visually verified**
- Scripture/May business chain represents same exact physical shop continuously from 1912: **Plausible / unproven**
- May & Washburn inherited 108 Fifth: **Plausible / unproven**
