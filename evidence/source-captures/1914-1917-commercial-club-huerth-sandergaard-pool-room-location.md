# 1914–1917 — Commercial Club / Huerth & Sandergaard pool-room location

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED primary scans; Huerth & Sandergaard 1917 pool room ruled out as the Fifth/Main fire-adjacent hall at that date**

Purpose: separate another Oregon City pool-room lineage from the unresolved July 1922 Fifth/Main fire-adjacent pool hall.

## 22 January 1914 — Commercial Club moving to Eighth & Main

The actual scanned page of the **Oregon City Courier, 22 Jan 1914, p. 1** has been visually inspected.

Under the heading **“New Quarters February 1”**, the paper reports that the Oregon City Commercial Club was preparing to move into its new home on the **second story of the new business block on the corner of Eighth and Main**.

Canonical page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-01-22/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This fixes the Commercial Club's new 1914 premises at **Eighth & Main**, well north of the Fifth/Main target frontage.

## 7 June 1917 — Huerth & Sandergaard pool room in Commercial Club building

The actual scanned page of the **Oregon City Courier, 7 Jun 1917, p. 4** has been visually inspected.

A report headed **“Auto Backs Through Window”** states that William Trudell backed an automobile through a large plate-glass window in the **pool room owned by Huerth & Sandergaard, in the Commercial Club building**.

Canonical page:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1917-06-07/ed-1/seq-4.pdf

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

Combining the two primary sources places the **Huerth & Sandergaard pool room in 1917 at the Commercial Club building at Eighth & Main**, not at Fifth & Main.

## Relationship to 1922 pool-hall research

A visually verified **25 Feb 1922 Morning Oregonian** Portina advertisement still lists **Huerth & Sandergaard** among Oregon City cigar dealers. The same dealer list also separately includes **Leland & Little** and **Little, W. L.**

That 1922 cigar-dealer appearance does not establish that Huerth & Sandergaard still occupied the same Eighth/Main pool room in 1922; a later move remains possible unless continuity is proved.

However, this result materially improves the pool-hall reconstruction because the firm cannot be back-projected from its 1922 cigar listing into the Fifth/Main fire cluster merely because it had earlier operated a pool room. Its **known 1917 pool-room premises were at Eighth & Main**.

## Fire-reconstruction implication

The July 1922 fire report says a pool hall directly adjoined Alex Schieremann's former Main Street tailor room near Fifth. Current 1921–1922 candidates/evidence streams include Ed Wheeler, Timms Pool Hall, Leland & Little, and other pool/cigar operators.

Huerth & Sandergaard should now be handled as:

- **1917 pool-room operator at Eighth & Main: proven**;
- **1922 Oregon City cigar dealer: proven**;
- **continuing at Eighth/Main in July 1922: unresolved**;
- **Fifth/Main fire-adjacent pool hall: no evidence; 1917 location points elsewhere**.

This is an elimination/narrowing result, not proof identifying Timms or Leland & Little as the fire-adjacent hall.
