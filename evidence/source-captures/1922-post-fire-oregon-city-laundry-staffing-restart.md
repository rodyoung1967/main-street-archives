# 1922 post-fire Oregon City Laundry staffing — production restart evidence

Status: **mixed verification; September ad visually reviewed, November ad OCR/search lead pending visual confirmation**

Purpose: preserve evidence that Oregon City Laundry was recruiting skilled production workers within months of the July 1922 destruction of its Fifth Street plant. This helps distinguish resumed laundry processing from the documented temporary customer office at 519 Main, while leaving the replacement plant address unresolved.

## 16 September 1922 — marker/sorter wanted

**Morning Oregonian, 16 Sep 1922, p. 18** contains a help-wanted advertisement from Oregon City Laundry seeking an **experienced marker and sorter** and directing applicants to Oregon City Laundry Co., Oregon City.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83025138/1922-09-16/ed-1/seq-18/

The scanned newspaper page was visually inspected. This is therefore **VISUALLY VERIFIED** under the project research standard.

### Interpretation

A marker/sorter is a production-side laundry position rather than merely a receiving-desk function. The ad therefore supports a **strong inference that Oregon City Laundry had restored, or was actively restoring, actual processing operations somewhere by mid-September 1922**, roughly two months after the fire.

It does **not** identify where those operations were located. Do not infer that the production plant had already been rebuilt at 109 Fifth or at the later 1925 Sanborn footprint.

## 3 November 1922 — washman wanted

A search/OCR result for **Morning Oregonian, 3 Nov 1922, p. 23** reports an Oregon City Laundry help-wanted notice seeking an experienced **wash man / all-around man**.

The direct page scan was not successfully retrieved during the current review attempt. This item is therefore presently **OCR/TEXT ONLY** and must not be upgraded to visually verified until the actual scanned page is inspected.

If visually confirmed, it would independently reinforce the September evidence for resumed production operations.

## Relationship to other 1922 evidence

- **July 1922:** the Fifth Street Oregon City Laundry plant was destroyed in the Fifth/Main fire.
- **Immediately after the fire:** the business established a temporary office with the Pantorium at **519 Main Street**.
- **16 Sep 1922:** visually verified skilled marker/sorter hiring ad.
- **Sep and Nov 1922:** county expense records show Oregon City Laundry again performing business for the county.
- **May 1925:** Sanborn Sheet 6 visually maps an Oregon City Laundry complex back in the Fifth/Main/Water block.

Together these facts show a rapid business recovery, but they do not yet establish the address or footprint of the late-1922 production plant.

## Confidence

- September 16 marker/sorter ad exists and was visually reviewed: **Very High**
- Skilled-worker recruitment indicates processing activity was restored or being restored: **High inference**
- Production was occurring specifically at 109 Fifth by September 1922: **Unproven**
- Production was occurring at 519 Main: **Unproven; 519 is documented as the temporary office**
