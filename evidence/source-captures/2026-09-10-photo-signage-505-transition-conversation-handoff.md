## Procedural liquor-license controls

The research established useful controls showing Oregon City could process liquor-license matters in **small in-year batches**, not only large annual renewal lists.

Recovered controls included:

- **5 May 1948** — a complete **two-applicant** liquor-license endorsement list;
- an additional **single-applicant** Brandt/Buena Vista Store endorsement in a meeting immediately preceding the visible 4 August 1948 header.

Research consequence:

> Even a complete annual renewal list cannot by itself prove that no Farr→Harr application/transfer/change occurred elsewhere in the year.

This matters when reconstructing the missing 1945–1948 transition.

## 1941 directory route

A better access route was established for the **1941 Oregon City directory**:

- LDSGenealogy identifies an image-online Oregon City 1941 directory in Ancestry;
- an official West Linn nomination independently cites **Pacific Directory Service 1941 Directory: Oregon City–West Linn–Gladstone**.

This is a retrieval/bibliographic control, not itself a 505 occupancy claim.

## 1946–48 Clackamas County directory production route

The conversation documented that Pacific Directory Service was actively compiling a county-wide directory in **August–September 1946**, using house-to-house canvassing in incorporated cities and mailed rural questionnaires.

This materially changes interpretation of the later-labeled **1947–48 / 1948 Clackamas County Directory**:

> A listing in that volume may reflect data gathered in **1946**, so the printed edition year must not automatically be treated as the exact observation/occupancy date.

Additional controls saved:

- Oregon City's March 1948 report of February claims includes **Pacific Directory Service** and a **Directory** item;
- the flattened text does **not** safely support an exact dollar amount, so the earlier `$9.75` reading was withdrawn as overprecise;
- by **8 July 1948**, the Sandy branch of the Clackamas County library had a **new Clackamas County Directory** available;
- later CCHS and official bibliographies support the Pacific Directory Service 1947–48/1948 volume identity.

The dedicated integration was saved as source IDs **S-221 through S-228** and evidence **E-201 / E-202** in the then-current research stream. Existing IDs must never be renumbered.
