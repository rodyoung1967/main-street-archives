# 18 March–8 April 1921 — Dr. Freeze relocates from 507½ to 505½ Main

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED TARGETED SOURCE SET**

Proposed archive IDs: source `S-153`; evidence `E-137`. These pages are a targeted transition review only; they do not certify the complete issues or 1921 as fully reviewed.

## Question

Can the first 1921 Oregon City location of Dr. Harvey Freeze be tied to the numbered Main Street storefront ladder, and when did he move into 505½ Main?

## 18 March 1921 — move from Portland to Oregon City / over Harding's Drug Store

**Oregon City Enterprise, Friday, 18 March 1921, page 5**

- Page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1921-03-18/ed-1/seq-5/
- PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063700/1921-03-18/ed-1/seq-5.pdf
- PDF SHA-256: `f77ab149d62a8655d0f29da025b5be451fd1b26000ae95a43126ce01ed5c2115`

The original full page was fetched and rendered at high resolution on 31 August 2026 and visually inspected. The page masthead/date and the complete short article are readable.

The article headline is **DOCTOR FREEZE TO MOVE OFFICES FROM PORTLAND**. Its subhead reads **Well known eye specialist locating over Hardings Drug Store.**

The body identifies **Dr. Harvey Freeze**, says he had maintained offices at **Sixth and Washington streets, Portland, for the past twelve years**, and says he **has moved to Oregon City to practice his profession**. It adds that he intended to install machinery so he could handle his own lens grinding and describes his training and eighteen years of practical eye-work experience.

The article does **not** print a numbered Oregon City street address for the office or for Harding's Drug Store.

## 8 April 1921 — 505½ Main

The separately preserved and visually verified 8 April page is already recorded in `evidence/source-captures/1921-04-08-dr-freeze-505-half-main.md` (`E-028` / `S-035`). It prints **Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist, 505½ Main Street, Oregon City**.

Therefore the directly verified numbered sequence is:

- **25 March 1921 — 507½ Main**
- **1 April 1921 — 507½ Main**
- **8 April 1921 — 505½ Main**

This establishes that Dr. Freeze changed his advertised exact address from **507½ to 505½ sometime after the 1 April issue and by the 8 April issue**. It does not establish the precise day the physical move occurred.

## Harding Drug Store relationship

The 18 March article says Freeze was locating **over Hardings Drug Store**. The 25 March and 1 April ads then print **507½ Main**.

Taken together, these sources strongly support an address/space relationship between Harding's Drug Store and the 507½ office at that moment. However:

- the 18 March article itself does not print `507` for Harding's Drug Store;
- the archive should label **Harding Drug Store at 507 Main by March 1921 as a strong spatial/address inference**, not a source-explicit numbered address, unless an independent source directly prints Harding at 507;
- this does not prove ownership, building footprint, or that a modern upstairs/downstairs arrangement was identical in 1921.

This is especially significant because visually verified 1911–1915 evidence places George A. Harding's drug business at **511 Main**. The 1921 sequence therefore supplies a tight new lead for when Harding's business shifted south within the block, but it does not yet date that shift directly.

## Relationship to 505 reconstruction question

The result narrows occupancy at the half-numbered address without dating construction:

- **4 June 1920:** A. Leland is permitted to operate a pool hall at **505½ Main** and erect an electric sign (`E-135`).
- **25 March–1 April 1921:** Dr. Freeze advertises at **507½ Main**.
- **8 April 1921:** Dr. Freeze advertises at **505½ Main** (`E-028`).
- **29 June 1922:** Dr. Freeze is still visually documented at **505½ Main** and announces his move after 1 July to the Masonic Building.

The same numbered `505½` appearing for A. Leland in June 1920 and Dr. Freeze by April 1921 is an important address-reuse/occupancy sequence. It does **not** prove that they occupied the same physical room, that Freeze succeeded Leland's pool hall, that 505½ was upstairs, or that the surviving 505 building was built in 1920.

## Research implications / next steps

1. Search **2 April–7 April 1921** for a move/opening notice that names the 505½ destination or explains why Freeze shifted one numbered bay south.
