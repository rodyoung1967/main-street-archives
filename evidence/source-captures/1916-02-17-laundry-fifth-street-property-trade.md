# 17 Feb 1916 — Oregon City Laundry trades planned site for Fifth Street location

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED; resolves the apparent February–April 1916 location conflict**

Purpose: document the primary-source bridge showing that Oregon City Laundry changed its planned replacement-plant site from the rear-Courier / Seventh–Eighth block to a Fifth Street location before construction was completed.

## Source

**Oregon City Courier, 17 Feb 1916, p. 7**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-02-17/ed-1/seq-7.pdf

The actual scanned page was visually inspected.

The local-items column states that **E. M. Blakeslee, of the Oregon City Laundry, had traded the property in the rear of the Courier office, on which he had been going to build, for a location on Fifth Street, and would rebuild the laundry there.**

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

## This resolves the earlier source conflict

Three contemporary reports now form a coherent sequence:

1. **3 Feb 1916 — initial plan.** The Courier reported that the replacement laundry would be built on the block bounded by Seventh, Main, Eighth and Railroad, fronting the alley beside city hall and adjoining the rear of the Courier property.
2. **17 Feb 1916 — site changed.** The Courier explicitly reported that Blakeslee traded the rear-Courier property where he had planned to build **for a Fifth Street location** and would rebuild there.
3. **2 Apr 1916 — Fifth Street plant under construction.** The Sunday Oregonian reported the new Oregon City Laundry building **on Fifth Street just off Main** and nearing completion.

Sources:
- 3 Feb: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-02-03/ed-1/seq-6/
- 17 Feb: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-02-17/ed-1/seq-7.pdf
- 2 Apr: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn83045782/1916-04-02/ed-1/seq-14.pdf

The February and April reports therefore are **not contradictory descriptions of the same fixed plan**. The project should treat them as a documented plan change.

## Link to later exact-address evidence

The 24 Oct 1918 Oregon City Courier business guide later gives the laundry's exact address as **109 Fifth Street**. A 20 Aug 1921 Oregonian classified from the laundry itself says **5th St., near Main**. The July 1922 fire then destroys the Fifth/Main-area laundry plant.

This yields a much stronger continuity chain:

- 3 Feb 1916 — rear-Courier site planned;
- 17 Feb 1916 — that site traded for Fifth Street location;
- 2 Apr 1916 — new laundry on Fifth just off Main nearing completion;
- 24 Oct 1918 — Oregon City Laundry at **109 Fifth**;
- 20 Aug 1921 — Oregon City Laundry at **5th St., near Main**;
- 18 Jul 1922 — plant destroyed in Fifth/Main fire.

The evidence strongly supports the conclusion that the 1916 plan change produced the Fifth Street plant later documented at/around 109 Fifth. The exact proposition `the April 1916 building = the exact same physical footprint numbered 109 Fifth in 1918` is a **strong inference**, not yet a deed/permit-level proof.
