## Direct finding

In the Oregon City business/professional guide on p. 2, the scan directly advertises:

**WHEEL CAFE & LOUNGE**  
**STEAKS**  
**503 Main St.**  
**Ph: 9106**

Classification: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT — VISUALLY VERIFIED PRIMARY ADVERTISEMENT**.

## Research significance

This moves the earliest currently recovered **visually verified exact-number Wheel Cafe & Lounge control at 503 Main** backward from the previously registered **22 August 1958** advertisement (`S-282` / `E-255`) to **11 July 1958**.

The updated documentary boundary is therefore:

**1957 — City official-secondary synthesis places Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor with the restaurant/cocktail-bar operation at 503 Main → by 11 July 1958 — Wheel Cafe & Lounge is directly advertised at 503 Main, telephone 9106.**

This is an **occupancy / trade-name endpoint**, not proof of:

- business ownership;
- building ownership;
- liquor-license ownership;
- a formal Kwality → Wheel rename;
- a sale or transfer;
- Albert Flor operating The Wheel;
- the identity of the person from whom Raye LaFarlette later acquired The Wheel.

No proprietor or licensee is named in the 11 July advertisement.
