## Critical later property result — Harding → Gates / Wolf division

Later repo work materially advanced the property chain and supersedes the earlier simple `503 ignition / 505 pool hall` preference as a ranked conclusion.

The visually verified **13 Jul 1922 Banner-Courier** reports that George A. Harding's Main Street property group was divided between:

- **C. E. Gates**, who received the **Fifth Street Restaurant location**;
- **S. Wolf**, who received the **remainder**.

The 20 Jul fire report then identifies Wolf as owner of the ignition building.

On 17–18 Aug 1922, contemporary reports identify **C. E. Gates** as owner of the Fifth/Main corner restaurant site and describe construction of a **two-story hollow-tile replacement building with two ground-floor storerooms**.

This creates two principal spatial models:

1. Gates principally maps to modern **501**, leaving Wolf beginning around **503**;
2. Gates's two-store replacement maps to **501+503**, leaving Wolf beginning nearer **505**.

The repository's 28 Aug model test selects **neither model**. **501 remains live.** The simple `503 ignition + 505 pool hall` arrangement remains possible but is not comparatively proven.

Important methodological distinction:

- pre-fire property division;
- post-fire replacement footprint;
- modern address mapping;
- and the exact ignition room

are four separate questions and must not be collapsed.
