# 1922 Fifth Street Restaurant — June roster correction / Ed. Blake fire evidence

Status: **CORRECTED after higher-resolution visual review; Ed. Blake fire evidence visually verified; exact restaurant address unresolved**

Purpose: preserve and correct an interpretation error made during the 23 Aug 2026 continuation. A lower-resolution read initially joined `A. D. Wheeler and Co.` with `Fifth Street Restaurant`. Higher-resolution inspection of the actual 8 June 1922 scanned page shows they are **separate comma-delimited entries** in the Buyers' Carnival merchant roster. This correction is retained explicitly for auditability.

## Correction of the 8 June 1922 roster

The actual scanned page of the **Banner-Courier, 8 Jun 1922, p. 10** was re-inspected at higher resolution.

The lower-right merchant list runs, in relevant part:

- `... Morning Enterprise, Oregonian Furniture Co., A. D. Wheeler and Co., Fifth Street Restaurant, Joe Orman, William Weismandel ...`

The list consistently separates participating businesses/people by commas. Therefore:

- **A. D. Wheeler and Co.** is one roster entry;
- **Fifth Street Restaurant** is the next roster entry;
- the page does **not** state that A. D. Wheeler & Co. owned or operated Fifth Street Restaurant.

### Superseded interpretation

The immediately preceding project commit incorrectly transcribed the relevant sequence as `A. D. Wheeler and Co., Fifth Street Restaurant` and treated that wording as a business association. That interpretation is **withdrawn**.

Do not use the superseded reading as evidence of a Wheeler/restaurant relationship.

## 20 July 1922 — local fire report names Ed. Blake

The actual scanned front page of the **Banner-Courier, 20 Jul 1922** has been visually reviewed for the fire reconstruction.

Its fire report says the flames destroyed, among other premises, the **Fifth street restaurant, owned by Ed. Blake**.

## Current conclusion

The June roster proves only that a business called **Fifth Street Restaurant** was active and participating in the Buyers' Carnival about six weeks before the fire.

The July local fire report separately identifies the destroyed Fifth Street Restaurant as **owned by Ed. Blake**.

There is currently **no A. D. Wheeler / Fifth Street Restaurant conflict** supported by the June page. The prior claimed conflict arose from a list-boundary transcription error.

## What remains useful

The corrected June evidence gives a strong pre-fire continuity point:

- **8 Jun 1922** — Fifth Street Restaurant appears as an active Oregon City merchant/business;
- **18 Jul 1922** — Fifth Street Restaurant is destroyed in the Fifth/Main fire;
- **20 Jul 1922 report** — local paper attributes ownership to Ed. Blake.

The June page still provides no numbered Fifth Street address.

## What is NOT established

Do not claim that:

- A. D. Wheeler & Co. owned, operated, occupied, or was otherwise connected with Fifth Street Restaurant;
- A. D. Wheeler was Ed Wheeler;
- Ed. Blake owned the real property rather than the restaurant business solely from the newspaper wording;
- the restaurant had a particular Fifth Street number.
