# 1922 Fifth Street Restaurant — A. D. Wheeler & Co. / Ed. Blake conflict

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED contemporary sources; exact ownership/operator sequence unresolved**

Purpose: preserve a June–July 1922 business-identity conflict for the Fifth Street Restaurant destroyed in the 18 July Fifth/Main fire. Do not silently normalize A. D. Wheeler & Co. and Ed. Blake into one proprietor or assume a transfer without direct evidence.

In the Buyers' Carnival roster of Oregon City merchants/business people, the lower-right text lists:

## Conflict / possible explanations

These contemporary statements are only about six weeks apart:

- **8 Jun 1922:** `A. D. Wheeler and Co., Fifth Street Restaurant`
- **20 Jul 1922:** destroyed `Fifth street restaurant, owned by Ed. Blake`

At present the archive should preserve, rather than resolve, the discrepancy. Possible explanations include:

1. A. D. Wheeler & Co. operated the restaurant while Blake owned the business or premises;
2. a business transfer occurred between 8 June and 18 July;
3. the Buyers' Carnival roster used a firm/business relationship not equivalent to ownership;
4. the fire report's `owned by` wording referred to a different ownership layer than the June roster;
5. one contemporary report was mistaken.

None of these explanations is currently proven.

## Research consequence

The Fifth Street Restaurant remains an important element of the fire-frontage reconstruction, but **operator or ownership identity cannot yet be used as a fixed spatial/property anchor**.

The June roster is nevertheless useful because it proves a named association between **A. D. Wheeler & Co.** and the **Fifth Street Restaurant immediately before the fire**. A directory, restaurant inspection record, city license, deed/lease, or advertisement with an exact numbered address could help place the restaurant relative to Oregon City Laundry at 109 Fifth, May & Washburn, Gilbert Miller, and H. Ketchner.

## What is NOT established

Do not currently claim that:

- A. D. Wheeler & Co. and Ed. Blake were partners;
- Wheeler sold the restaurant to Blake between June and July;
- Ed. Blake was the real-property owner;
- A. D. Wheeler & Co. owned the real property;
- the restaurant had any particular Fifth Street number;
- this `Wheeler` business was connected to the separate **Ed Wheeler pool room at Sixth & Main**.

The surname overlap with Ed Wheeler's pool-hall chain is insufficient for a connection.
