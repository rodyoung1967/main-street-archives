## Direct historical evidence visually confirmed

### 8 June 1922 Banner-Courier, page 10 / sequence 10

The actual rendered page image was inspected at full size.

The page directly shows:

1. **Dr. Freeze, Eye Specialist** advertising at **505½ Main St., opposite Postoffice, Oregon City**. This is direct business/office-location evidence for Dr. Freeze at 505½ Main on 8 June 1922. It does not by itself establish property ownership or building continuity.
2. The Buyers' Carnival merchant list separately names **Timms Pool Hall**.
3. The same list separately names **O. H. Leland**.
4. The same list also names the **Fifth Street Restaurant** and **S. Wolf** among the participating firms/persons.

These names appear as separate entries in the merchant list. The page therefore **does not establish that Timms Pool Hall was O. H. Leland's business, that Timms Pool Hall occupied 505½ Main, or that any listed merchant owned the real estate associated with another listed entry.** Those relationships remain separate questions requiring independent evidence.

This June 8 page is especially useful because it places the 505½ Freeze office, Timms Pool Hall, O. H. Leland, Fifth Street Restaurant, and S. Wolf in the same dated pre-fire commercial context without collapsing their distinct roles or premises.

Source path: `newspapers/banner-courier/1922-06/1922-06-08-seq-10.pdf`

## Evidence cautions

- The merchant-list appearance of a person or business proves participation/listing in the Buyers' Carnival context, not exact street address unless the page separately supplies one.
- Dr. Freeze's ad directly supplies 505½ Main; that address must not be transferred to Timms Pool Hall or O. H. Leland merely because they appear elsewhere on the same page.
- Business operation, tenancy, business ownership, property ownership, licensing, address, and physical-building identity remain separate questions.
- A visually reviewed page with a torn/obscured edge is not evidence that the obscured area contained nothing relevant.
