## Verified business and address findings

### March 23 p5 — gas company office at 718 Main

“Gas Company Here” reports that **Portland Gas & Coke Co. opened a branch office at 718 Main**, with **George J. Hall** in charge. The item identifies the front part of the building occupied by the justice court. This is a dated office-location report, not a deed or proof of the whole building's ownership. The separate p3 justice-court fire/reopening report is not assigned to a numbered fire site solely by combining these items.

### April 6 p5 — Randall sold his undertaking interest to Holman

The local notice directly reports **T. P. Randall sold his interest in the Holman-Randall undertaking establishment to R. L. Holman**. The same page still advertises both men at **Fifth and Main**. Thus later joint-name advertisements establish printed business/address wording, not Randall's continued partnership ownership. The notice gives no exact transaction day, price, deed transfer or numbered 501/503/505 address. This completes the previously pending visual check in the June–August and September–December lead files.

The adjacent **L. J. Hylton** eastern-trip/Twilight residence item is also visually confirmed. It does not establish identity with the 1914 503 proprietor or prove that business closed. A separate item reports **Dr. W. E. Hempstead's office move to the Electric Hotel annex, Sixth/Main**, without a number.

### May 18 p1 — Raasch & Lamb basement permission

The council report explicitly grants a **special three-month permit** to remove the Raasch and Lamb pool hall from a **Main Street room in the Andresen Building to a basement in the same building**. It also describes the street-visibility charter provision and an instruction to prepare an amendment. This is authorization, not proof the move was completed or the charter amended. The story does not print a street number and supplies no 503/505 occupancy. It is earlier than the June relocation leads, which remain separately subject to their original review limits.

May 4 p1 separately reports a half-year pool/billiard license for **Hedrick**, without an address. March 30 p8 names **Cox's pool hall** in a minors complaint, also without a number. Neither is assigned to a target storefront or merged with Raasch & Lamb.
