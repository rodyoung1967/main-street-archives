Reviewed: 20 August 2026  
Scope: 1902 issues, focused on the Main Street frontage between 5th and 6th and evidence that may map to later 501–527 Main addresses.

## 3 January 1902 — Courier-Herald New Year edition, page 24

Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063697/1902-01-03/ed-1/seq-24.pdf

Evidence: a profile of Herman Schrader says that before buying his then-current bakery about a year earlier, he had worked for nearly eighteen years in the **Main-Street Bakery**.

Use: extends the Schrader / Main Street bakery research backward. It does **not** establish the later exact numbered storefront or prove that the older Main-Street Bakery was in the same premises as Schrader's later Main Street bakery near the post office.

Related evidence: `E-057`.

## 24 October 1902 — Courier-Herald, page 5

Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063697/1902-10-24/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

Evidence: Huntley Bros. occupied two adjoining store rooms that were remodeled into essentially one store; a partition was removed and a plate-glass front installed.

Use: potentially useful structural/storefront evidence when reconciling the 1900 Sanborn with later storefronts. The recovered article does **not** establish that Huntley Bros. was on the 5th-to-6th Main frontage, so no numbered Main address should be assigned from this source alone.

Related evidence: `E-058`.

## 7 November 1902 — Oregon City Courier, page 1

Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1902-11-07/ed-1/seq-1/
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1902-11-07/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

Evidence: **The Union Store**, Michael proprietor, advertised a closing/liquidation sale at **"Main St., between 5th and 6th Sts."** The advertisement said the stock and fixtures were to be sold and the stock must be gone by New Year.

Use: direct primary evidence for a named business on the exact Main Street block under study. Exact later 5xx storefront number remains unresolved.

Related evidence: `E-059`.

## 2 January 1903 continuity clue

The first 1903 issue reports that **M. Michael** would shortly leave for Baker City, where he might invest in a furniture house. This is consistent with the Union Store's late-1902 liquidation and is being handled in the 1903 review rather than used to infer a numbered storefront.

## Handling rule

Do not convert a block-level statement such as **"Main St., between 5th and 6th Sts."** into a specific 501–527 address without independent address-mapping evidence.
