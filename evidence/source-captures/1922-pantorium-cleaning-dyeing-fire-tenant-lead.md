# 1922 Pantorium Cleaners — cleaning/dyeing fire-tenant lead

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED pre-fire cleaning/dyeing activity; identity with fire-origin tenant UNPROVEN**

Purpose: document Pantorium Cleaners as a genuine Oregon City cleaning/dyeing concern active before the 18 July 1922 Fifth/Main fire, while preventing the business from being automatically equated with the unnamed cleaning-and-dyeing establishment that a same-day Eugene report says had occupied the fire-origin room until a few days before the fire.

## 25 January 1922 — Pantorium explicitly in dry cleaning and dyeing

The actual scanned page of the **Morning Oregonian, 25 Jan 1922, p. 15** was visually inspected.

A classified advertisement in the help-wanted section reads in substance:

- experienced dry-cleaning and dyeing solicitor wanted;
- only experienced applicant need apply;
- **Pantorium Cleaners, Oregon City**.

This directly establishes that Pantorium Cleaners was an Oregon City concern engaged in the cleaning/dyeing trade by January 1922. The advertisement does **not** give its street address.

## 8 June 1922 — Pantorium remains an active Oregon City merchant

The actual scanned page of the **Banner-Courier, 8 Jun 1922, p. 10** was previously visually inspected.

The Buyers' Carnival roster of approximately seventy Oregon City firms/business people includes **Pantorium** among the participating concerns.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-06-08/ed-1/seq-10/

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This proves Pantorium was still an active local concern about six weeks before the fire. The roster gives no address.

## 20 July 1922 — Pantorium at 519 Main after the fire

The visually reviewed **Banner-Courier, 20 Jul 1922, p. 1** states that Oregon City Laundry arranged to continue business and took a **temporary office with the Pantorium at 519 Main Street** after its plant was destroyed in the 18 July fire.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-07-20/ed-1/seq-1/

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

This establishes Pantorium at **519 Main** immediately after the fire. It does not by itself prove that Pantorium occupied 519 before 18 July, although later evidence shows the business continued using 519 Main in subsequent years.

## Relationship to the same-day Eugene fire account

A separately visually reviewed **18 Jul 1922 Eugene Daily Guard** account says the fire originated in an unoccupied Main Street building and that the room had been occupied until only a few days earlier by a **cleaning-and-dyeing establishment**.

Related capture:
`evidence/source-captures/1922-07-18-eugene-daily-guard-fire-account.md`

Pantorium is therefore a legitimate business-name candidate to test because it is independently proven to have been an Oregon City cleaning/dyeing concern before the fire.

However, the existing evidence does **not** prove that Pantorium was the establishment in the ignition room. Indeed, the local fire report places Pantorium at 519 Main immediately after the fire and uses it as the host location for Oregon City Laundry's temporary office, so the archive must allow for several possibilities:

1. the unnamed ignition-room cleaner was a different cleaning/dyeing business;
2. Pantorium had more than one room/location or had only recently moved from the ignition premises;
3. the Eugene report described a tailoring/pressing/cleaning operation associated with another person or firm;
4. one contemporary account was imprecise about the former occupancy.

No option is currently proven.

## What is NOT established

Do not currently claim that:

- Pantorium occupied 501, 503, or 505 Main;
- Pantorium occupied Alex Schieremann's former tailor room;
- Pantorium moved to 519 Main only days before the fire;
- Pantorium was at 519 Main before 18 July 1922;
- Alex Schieremann operated Pantorium;
- the Eugene report's cleaning-and-dyeing establishment was Pantorium.

## Confidence

- Pantorium Cleaners was engaged in dry cleaning/dyeing in Oregon City by 25 Jan 1922: **Very High / visually verified**
- Pantorium was an active Oregon City merchant on 8 Jun 1922: **Very High / visually verified**
- Pantorium was at 519 Main immediately after the fire: **Very High / visually verified local fire report**
- Pantorium = recent tenant of the fire-origin room: **Unproven hypothesis**
