Status: **RETRIEVAL / SOURCE-COVERAGE CONTROL — NO NEW OWNERSHIP, LICENSEE, MARRIAGE OR BUSINESS-TRANSFER CLAIM ESTABLISHED; 1957 FLOR SOURCE PROVENANCE NARROWED TO A SPECIFIC 1957 PUBLICATION CITATION**

Purpose: continue testing the strong cross-source hypothesis that **Albert D. Flor + Margaret Remus** may be the **“Al & Marge”** associated with Kwality Cafe in September 1953, and narrow the transition from the 1957 Flor-managed restaurant/cocktail-bar control at 503 Main to the visually verified **Wheel Cafe & Lounge** at exact 503 Main by 22 August 1958.

This pass does **not** merge business ownership, building ownership, operation/management, tenancy, liquor-license status or trade-name continuity.

## 1. Margaret Remus → “Mrs. Albert D. Flor” identity test remains open

Targeted public-web searches were run for marriage, household and biographical combinations involving:

- Albert D. Flor / Albert Flor / A. D. Flor;
- Margaret Remus / Marge Remus;
- Albert Flor + Margaret/Marge;
- Oregon City / Clackamas County, especially 1951–1957.

No direct marriage record, household record or contemporary biographical source was recovered that identifies **Margaret Remus as Mrs. Albert D. Flor**.

Classification: **RETRIEVAL GAP / IDENTITY UNPROVED**. Do not promote Margaret Remus to Mrs. Flor from the 1951 + 1953 + 1957 sequence alone.

### Concrete surviving state-record route

The Oregon State Archives official vital-records guide states that its statewide holdings include:

- **Marriage index, Oregon, 1906–1924 and 1946–2008 (microfilm)**; and
- **Marriage returns, 1906–1969**.

Official guide:
https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/records/Pages/aids-vitalrecords.aspx

That makes the statewide index/returns a direct manual-record test for an **Albert D. Flor / Margaret Remus** marriage during the unresolved 1951–1957 interval, if such a marriage occurred in Oregon and is represented in the surviving state series.

Important limit: existence of the series is **not** evidence that a Flor–Remus marriage exists. No institutional request was sent in this pass.

## 5. Telephone-number check remains non-bridging

The undated Larry's Kwality Cafe matchcover directly gives **503 Main / telephone 6106**. The dated 22 August 1958 Wheel advertisement gives **503 Main / telephone 9106**.

Because the Larry's artifact is not yet dated and historical exchange/number changes can occur independently of ownership, the `6106 → 9106` difference is **not** evidence of a sale or operator change.

Classification: **DIRECT** for each artifact's printed number; **UNRESOLVED / NON-BRIDGING** for chronology and ownership meaning.
