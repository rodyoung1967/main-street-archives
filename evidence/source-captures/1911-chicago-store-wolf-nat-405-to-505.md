# Chicago Store — Wolf & Nat proprietors; move from 405 to 505 Main in 1911

Status: **strong primary newspaper evidence; Jan/Feb ads visually/textually recoverable; exact move date still unresolved**

This note records a potentially important early ownership/proprietor lead for the Chicago Store before its documented occupancy of 505 Main.

## 27 January 1911 — Wolf & Nat at 405 Main

The *Oregon City Courier*, 27 January 1911, page 6, advertises:

- **The Chicago Store**
- Gents' Furnishers and Tailors
- **WOLF & NAT, Proprietors**
- **405 Main Street, Oregon City, Oregon**
- Phone Main 3732

## 3 February 1911 — same partnership/location

The 3 February 1911 *Courier*, page 6, repeats the same Chicago Store advertisement with **Wolf & Nat, Proprietors**, **405 Main Street**, phone Main 3732.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-02-03/ed-1/seq-6/

## 1 March 1911 — Chicago Store still at 405 Main

The 1 March 1911 *Morning Enterprise* advertises the Chicago Store at **405 Main St.**, buying and selling second-hand clothes. The recovered ad no longer prints the Wolf & Nat proprietor line.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063701/1911-03-01/ed-1/seq-3.pdf

## 7 April 1911 — Chicago Store at 505 Main

By 7 April 1911, the *Courier* visually shows the Chicago Store at **505 Main St., Oregon City**, offering new and second-hand clothing and cleaning/repairing.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-04-07/ed-1/seq-6.pdf

The 14 April and 28 April issues continue the **505 Main** address.

Sources:
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-04-14/ed-1/seq-7/
- https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1911-04-28/ed-1/seq-7/

## Current move window

The Chicago Store was therefore:

- at **405 Main through at least 1 March 1911**;
- at **505 Main by 7 April 1911**.

The exact move date remains unresolved within March / early April 1911. Search-index silence for intervening issues must not be treated as evidence; the actual March issue pages should be visually reviewed.

## Why the Wolf & Nat proprietor line matters

This introduces a **Wolf-named proprietor into the Chicago Store history before the business moved to 505 Main**, roughly seven years before **Wolf & Miller** is directly documented operating at 505 Main in October 1918.

That surname overlap is potentially significant but is **not proof of continuity**. Current evidence does not identify the full names of `Wolf` or `Nat`, does not establish that either remained with the Chicago Store after its move to 505, and does not connect the 1911 Wolf to the later Wolf & Miller partnership or to the Wolf family later reported as owning 503 Main.

Treat all three Wolf threads separately until a direct identity source connects them:

1. **Wolf & Nat / Chicago Store, 1911**;
2. **Wolf & Miller / 505 Main, 1918**;
3. **Wolf family / 503 property ownership, circa 1922–1972**.

## Research targets
