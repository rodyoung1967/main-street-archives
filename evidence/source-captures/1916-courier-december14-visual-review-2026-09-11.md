## Evidentiary interpretation

The 14 December Christmas issue is valuable because it carries unusually dense retail advertising and repeated address-bearing controls. Even in this enlarged 20-page issue, the visual review did **not** produce an explicit 503/505 address, occupant, trade name, or transaction.

That bounded negative result must remain exactly that: a completed issue review with no target hit. It does **not** prove that either premises was vacant, closed, or unused.

The exact-address controls are useful for preventing address drift:

- Prudden — 612 Main
- Williams Bros. — 612 Main
- Electric Store / PRL&P — 619 Main
- Eddy's Dependable Store — 526 Main
- Brady Mercantile — 1110–1112 Main
- Oregon City Shoe Store — Main at Seventh
- Larsen & Company — Main and Tenth
- Dillman & Howland — Eighth and Main

None should be projected onto 503 or 505.
