# 30 Nov 1922 — McAnulty & Barry pool-hall license

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED from scanned Banner-Courier page; exact 1922 address unresolved**

Purpose: preserve another documented Oregon City pool-hall transition in late 1922 so it is not conflated with Timms Pool Hall, Leland & Little, Ed Wheeler, or the unnamed hall adjoining Alex Schieremann's former tailor shop in the July fire.

## Primary source

**Banner-Courier, 30 Nov 1922, p. 1**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-11-30/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

The actual scanned newspaper page was visually inspected.

A short item headed **“Granted Pool Hall License”** states that **Barry and McAnulty** had been granted a license to operate a pool hall **“in the room formerly occupied by them as a restaurant.”**

Verification state: **VISUALLY VERIFIED**.

## Earlier 1922 business description

The visually reviewed **17 Aug 1922 Banner-Courier** business list identifies:

- **McANULTY AND BARRY, Cigars, Restaurant**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063699/1922-08-17/ed-1/seq-2/

This is consistent with the November statement that their pool hall occupied their former restaurant room.

## Relationship to later 714 Main evidence

The Oregon Historic Sites Database entry for the **Petzold Building, 714 Main Street** says:

- the McAnulty family operated a restaurant/confectionery on the main floor from **1924 through 1930**;
- Petzold family records from **1936–1938** denote a pool hall with **Barry and McAnulty** as owners.

Source:
https://heritagedata.prd.state.or.us/historic/index.cfm?do=v.dsp_siteSummary&resultDisplay=29986

This later historic record strongly associates McAnulty/Barry with **714 Main**, but it does not directly place their November 1922 restaurant/pool-hall room at 714. In fact, the newspaper proves restaurant use by August 1922 and a pool-hall conversion by November 1922, while the retrospective inventory begins its restaurant chronology in 1924. Treat this as a real chronology/address question rather than silently forcing the 1922 business into the later 714 address.

## Relevance to the Fifth/Main fire investigation

This source proves that Oregon City had another pool-hall business transition in late 1922. Therefore the local pool-hall field was more complicated than a single Timms → Leland & Little chain.

At present this McAnulty/Barry chain should be treated as **separate from the July fire-adjacent hall** because:

1. the pool-hall license was granted only in late November 1922, four months after the fire;
2. their August business was explicitly a restaurant/cigar concern, not yet described as a pool hall;
3. later records associate them with the 714 Main/Petzold Building chain, far north of Fifth & Main, although the exact 1922 premises still require proof.

Do **not** use this source as evidence that the July fire-adjacent pool hall was McAnulty & Barry.
