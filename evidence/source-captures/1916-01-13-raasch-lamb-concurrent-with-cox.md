# 13 January 1916 — Raasch & Lamb already operating concurrently with Cox

Status: **original newspaper page visually inspected**

Source: *Oregon City Courier*, 13 January 1916, page 12 (archive sequence 11).

Original page/PDF:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-01-13/ed-1/seq-11.pdf

## Visual finding

The lower-right advertising grid contains a distinct boxed advertisement reading:

**POOL AND BILLIARDS**  
**Everything for Smokers**  
**RAASCH & LAMB**

The original page was visually inspected in the research session. The ad itself does **not** print a numbered street address.

A separate adjacent advertisement above it is for **MILLER & OBST**, a haberdashery, and carries the location **Main and 7th**. Text extraction/OCR can visually concatenate these neighboring boxes and make `Main and 7th` appear associated with Raasch & Lamb. The original page shows that this would be incorrect: **Main and 7th belongs to Miller & Obst, not Raasch & Lamb.**

## Why this matters for the Cox question

Frank D. Cox's pool hall is independently documented as still operating on **30 March 1916**. Therefore:

- Raasch & Lamb were already an active named pool/billiards business by **13 January 1916**;
- Cox's hall was still active more than two months later;
- the businesses therefore overlapped in time and should be treated as **concurrent, separate pool-hall operations**, absent evidence of shared ownership;
- a simple **Cox → Raasch & Lamb succession** in early 1916 is ruled out by the overlap.

This strengthens the archive's caution against using the later June 1916 Raasch & Lamb move into the Andresen Building as evidence that they took over Cox's hall.

## Address handling

Do **not** assign Raasch & Lamb to Main & 7th from this page. Their exact January 1916 address remains unresolved here. Later June 1916 reporting places their then-current hall beside the Andresen Building entrance before a move into the building basement, but that is separate evidence.

## Research implication

The 1916 pool-hall landscape now includes at least two proven contemporaneous named operators — **Frank D. Cox** and **Raasch & Lamb** — plus the December Wade/Oldstein operation at 504 Main. Exact premises should be established independently from directory/license/address evidence rather than inferred from business succession.
