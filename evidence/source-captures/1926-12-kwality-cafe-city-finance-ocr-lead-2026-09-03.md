# December 1926 Kwality Cafe municipal-finance lead — 3 September 2026

Status: **OCR/TEXT ONLY — ORIGINAL SCAN NOT YET VISUALLY VERIFIED.**

Purpose: preserve a newly recovered City Commission record that independently places **Kwality Cafe** in Oregon City municipal business activity by the end of 1926, while keeping the repository's existing evidence boundary intact until the original scan can be visually inspected.

## Source

Oregon City City Commission minutes, **5 January 1927**, current public WebDrawer record/file route:

https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4794937/File/document

The public text layer identifies the meeting as **Oregon City, Oregon, January 5, 1927**. It states that the listed bills were a **continuation of the December Finance Report** and were ordered paid.

Within the vendor list, **Kwality Cafe** appears. The corresponding purpose column is **Meals**. The text layer does not print a street address, proprietor, or business-owner name in this entry.

## What the text layer supports

Subject to visual verification, the record is strong evidence that:

1. **Kwality Cafe was an operating Oregon City business by the December 1926 finance period**; and
2. the City purchased or reimbursed **meals** from the business as part of that December report.

This does **not** by itself establish:

- that the business was at **503 Main** in December 1926;
- the proprietor or operator;
- continuity from the unnamed restaurant on the May 1925 Sanborn;
- continuity from the separate **Quality Restaurant at 427 Main** in June 1922;
- any construction or ownership event.

The repository already preserves the official City historic-resource synthesis stating that **1926 newspaper advertisements identify the 503 Main business as Kwality Restaurant**. This new municipal item is useful as an independent late-1926 operating anchor, but because it prints no address it does not replace the still-missing underlying 1926 advertisement.

## Related research consequence

The municipal records route is now demonstrably capable of surfacing named local businesses in finance and commission records. Continue searching late-1925 through 1927 City Commission/finance/license material for:

- **Kwality / Quality**;
- restaurant inspection, restaurant license, meals, garbage/refuse, or food-service entries;
- **Farr**, pool hall, billiards, cigars/tobacco;
- numbered **503 / 505 Main** entries;
- license applications or transfers naming both business and premises.

The highest-value unresolved target remains an exact-address municipal or newspaper record bridging the **May 1925 503 restaurant** and **505 billiards/cigars** uses to named operators.

No annual status changes. Absence of an address in this one finance entry is not evidence that the business lacked a fixed address or was not at 503.
