# Oregon City Courier — 21 December 1916 — full visual review

## Scope

Canonical issue: *Oregon City Courier*, 21 December 1916, 8 pages.

All **8/8 original page images/PDFs were visually inspected** on 11 September 2026. OCR/text was used only as a discovery aid.

## Page-by-page result

### Page 1
- Front-page civic, industrial, court and local reporting.
- Reports planned Hawley mill additions and other city development.
- No exact 503 or 505 Main premises fact.

### Page 2
- Large advertisement: **“I. Tolpolar’s Auction Sale”**.
- The ad states: doctor says he must quit; entire stock will be sold under the hammer; furniture, hardware, carpets, kitchenware, etc.; auction the following day.
- Signed **I. TOLPOLAR**.
- No numbered street address printed on this page.
- This corroborates the 23 November 1916 liquidation advertisement and supports the identity/business chain for Isaac Tolpolar’s Main Street second-hand/furniture/hardware operation.
- It does **not** establish 503 or 505 Main.

## Conclusion

The full 21 December 1916 issue produced **no exact 503/505 occupant, owner, tenant, licensee, trade-name change, sale, transfer, construction, demolition, closure or vacancy event**.

The significant new item is the visually verified **I. Tolpolar’s Auction Sale** advertisement on page 2. It strengthens the late-1916 Isaac Tolpolar business/liquidation record but supplies no numbered Main Street address. The separate location-check note preserves the 514 Main lead for D. Tolpolar as an unproven identity/business-continuity hypothesis rather than promoting it to Isaac Tolpolar.

Silence elsewhere in the issue is not evidence of vacancy or non-use at 503/505.
