# 1921 Banner-Courier availability reconciliation — 2026-09-20

Status: **TITLE IDENTIFIED; 1921 PUBLIC ONLINE IMAGE RUN NOT LOCATED; MANUAL CALLOUT REQUIRED**

## Why this correction is necessary

The 1921 annual handoff centered on the weekly *Oregon City Enterprise* and the separate *Morning Enterprise*. A fresh title-universe check before annual closeout identified a third relevant Oregon City newspaper: **The Banner-Courier**, LCCN `sn00063699`, published in Oregon City from 1919 to 1950.

Historic Oregon Newspapers' Oregon City/Courier history confirms that the *Oregon City Courier* merged with the *Clackamas County Banner* on 8 July 1919 and continued as *The Banner-Courier*. Contemporary January 1921 material also identifies W. E. Hassler as editor of the Oregon City Banner-Courier.

## Online availability finding

The Oregon Digital Newspaper Program's digitization documentation specifically identifies **The Banner-Courier, Jan.–Dec. 1922** as the run re-filmed and digitized in the Oregon City preservation project. The project's prior 1919 annual audit likewise established the 1919 Banner-Courier run as a microfilm/manual source rather than an online-retrievable page corpus.

Current public/indexed searching recovered 1922 Banner-Courier page images and 1922 pages containing legal notices first published in December 1921, but did **not** expose a complete 1921 Banner-Courier image run suitable for page-by-page visual audit.

Therefore the 1921 Banner-Courier cannot be silently omitted from the annual title universe, and it cannot be counted as online visually reviewed.
