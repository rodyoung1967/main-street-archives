# 1953 Oregon City directory — gated Ancestry / MyHeritage route confirmation

Date reviewed: **8 September 2026**

Status: **CURRENT DATABASE-ROUTE / CATALOG CONFIRMATION; ORIGINAL 1953 DIRECTORY PAGE NOT RECOVERED OR VISUALLY INSPECTED; NO NEW 505 OCCUPANT OR PROPRIETOR CLAIM.**

Purpose: preserve exact current database-routing details for the unrecovered **_Oregon City & Vicinity Directory, 1953_ — T. W. Salisbury & Co.**, the highest-value source for identifying the proprietor behind the City survey's **“Well's Recreation”** entry at **505 Main Street**.

## Locality catalog control

The current LDSGenealogy Oregon City locality page explicitly lists separate image-online entries for:

- **Oregon City Directory 1953 — MyHeritage**; and
- **Oregon City, Oregon, City Directory, 1953 (in U.S. City Directories collection) — Ancestry**.

Locality page:

`https://ldsgenealogy.com/OR/Oregon-City.htm`

The same locality page also links a generic FamilySearch Oregon City directory series. That generic FamilySearch route resolves to catalog **95422**, which the repository has already directly disambiguated as an **R. L. Polk series beginning in 1960**. It is therefore **not** a valid route to the 1953 T. W. Salisbury volume. This pass does not alter that exclusion.

## Ancestry — exact current collection route

Following the LDSGenealogy **1953 Oregon City** Ancestry link resolves through its affiliate redirect to:

`https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2469/`

The live Ancestry destination identifies collection **2469** as:

**U.S., City Directories, 1822-1995**.

This directly confirms that **collection 2469 is the current Ancestry collection route exposed by the 1953 Oregon City locality listing**.

A separate current Ancestry collection titled **Oregon, U.S., City Directories** is also exposed on Ancestry under collection **8855**. No source recovered in this pass establishes that 8855 specifically contains the Salisbury 1953 Oregon City volume. Therefore **do not replace the confirmed 2469 route with 8855** without direct volume-level evidence.

Classification: **CURRENT DATABASE / CATALOG ROUTE CONFIRMATION ONLY**. No 505 directory entry was recovered from Ancestry in this pass.

## MyHeritage — exact current collection route

Following the LDSGenealogy **Oregon City Directory 1953** MyHeritage link resolves through its affiliate redirect to:

`https://www.myheritage.com/research/collection-10705/us-city-directories`

MyHeritage's current collection material identifies **10705** as its **U.S. City Directories** collection containing scanned city directories.

This confirms **collection 10705 as the current MyHeritage collection route exposed by the Oregon City 1953 locality listing**.

Classification: **CURRENT DATABASE / CATALOG ROUTE CONFIRMATION ONLY**. The actual 1953 Oregon City page image was not recovered from the gated collection in this pass.

## What this improves

This pass converts generic “gated Ancestry/MyHeritage images” wording into two exact, reproducible current routes:

- **Ancestry collection 2469** — `U.S., City Directories, 1822-1995`;
- **MyHeritage collection 10705** — `U.S. City Directories`.

It also prevents two source-access errors:

1. **FamilySearch 95422 must remain excluded** from the 1953 Salisbury route because it is the later Polk series beginning in 1960; and
2. Ancestry's separate Oregon-specific collection **8855** must not be substituted for 2469 unless the 1953 Oregon City volume is directly verified there.

## What this does not establish

This route confirmation does **not** establish:

- the exact printed 505 Main entry;
- whether the original directory spells the business `Well's`, `Wells`, `Well`, or another variant;
- the proprietor or manager;
- Carl R. Harr's departure date;
- a Harr → Well transfer;
- a telephone number;
- any connection to the visually verified 1955 sale-ad phone **9179**; or
- business ownership, building ownership, tenancy, or liquor-license status.

## Extraction targets remain unchanged

When either gated image collection or another physical/microfilm copy is successfully accessed, visually capture:

1. title/front matter and publisher/year;
2. exact **505 Main** street entry;
3. alphabetical entries for **Well / Well's / Wells / Welle / Welsh**;
4. **Carl R. Harr / Harr's Recreation Center**;
5. recreation / pool / billiards classifications;
6. proprietor/manager notation;
7. telephone number and **9179** if present;
8. complete **501–511 odd Main Street** block for address control.

The original page image, not database index text alone, remains the target for scan-certified directory evidence.
