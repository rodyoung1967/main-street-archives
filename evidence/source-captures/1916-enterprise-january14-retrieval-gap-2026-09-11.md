## Why this checkpoint exists

The 7 January 1916 issue is already visually certified 8/8. The next unresolved Enterprise date is 14 January 1916. This pass attempted to continue chronologically without substituting OCR for visual inspection.

## What was successfully retrieved

The University of Oregon Historic Oregon Newspapers OCR page for 14 January 1916, page 4, was reachable and confirms the title/date/page identity. The page includes, among other material, a published condition statement for the First National Bank of Oregon City dated from the close of business 31 December 1915. That statement identifies F. J. Meyer as cashier and D. C. Latourette, C. D. Latourette, and M. D. Latourette as directors. It reports a $15,000 value for the banking house. It does **not** print a Main Street address and therefore does not bridge I. Tolpolar's November 1916 description of his store as being on Main Street next door to First National Bank to an exact street number.

Targeted web searches for this issue did not surface a source-explicit `503 Main`, `505 Main`, `Fifth and Main`, `507 Main`, `519 Main`, Raasch, or Tolpolar hit. This is discovery-only information and is **not negative evidence** about occupancy, use, vacancy, demolition, or business presence.
