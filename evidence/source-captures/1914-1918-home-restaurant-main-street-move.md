# Home Restaurant — move from 415 Main to 513 Main, 1914–1918

Status: **primary newspaper chronology; exact move date unresolved**

The Home Restaurant provides a useful address-control lead for the unresolved odd-numbered Main Street storefronts south of Sixth.

## 12 February 1914 — 415 Main

The *Oregon City Courier* industrial edition profiles **John Leary's Home Restaurant** and explicitly states:

**The Home Restaurant is located 415 Main Street.**

The article says Leary had opened the restaurant about nine months earlier.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-02-12/ed-1/seq-15/

## 1915–1917 — Home Restaurant remains active on Main, number not recovered

The business continues to appear in Oregon City newspaper reporting:

- **23 Dec 1915:** West Linn litigation concerns money claimed by the **Home restaurant of Oregon City** for meals furnished to pipeline/reservoir laborers.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1915-12-23/ed-1/seq-8/
- **2 Mar 1916:** burglars entered the **Home restaurant, on Main street**, and stole food. The story does not print the street number.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1916-03-02/ed-1/seq-7/
- **12 Jul 1917:** a benefit dinner at the Home Restaurant raised money for the 3rd Oregon hospital fund; **Miss Ella O'Leary** is identified as manager. The recovered article does not print the street number.
  - https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1917-07-12/ed-1/seq-8/

These references establish business continuity but **not address continuity at 415**.

## 10 October 1918 — 513 Main

The *Courier* `Who's Who in Oregon City` business survey explicitly identifies:

**Home Restaurant, 513 Main.**

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1918-10-10/ed-1/seq-4/

The original page scan has been visually inspected in the current research session and the `Home Restaurant, 513 Main` answer is visible on the page.

## Interpretation

The defensible chronology is:

- **Feb 1914:** Home Restaurant definitely at **415 Main**.
- **Dec 1915–Jul 1917:** business still active in Oregon City / on Main, but recovered references do not give the number.
- **Oct 1918:** Home Restaurant definitely at **513 Main**.

Therefore the restaurant moved from 415 to 513 **sometime after February 1914 and by October 1918**. The exact move date remains unresolved.

This matters to the Frank Cox pool-hall search because **513 Main cannot yet be excluded as Cox's 1915 premises** merely from the later Home Restaurant address. The restaurant may not have reached 513 until after Cox's 1915 raid or after his March 1916 documented activity.

## Research targets

1. Search 1914–1918 ads, health-inspection reports, lawsuits, and directory entries for an exact Home Restaurant address after February 1914.
2. Search the 1916–1917 Sohns & Woodbeck directory for **Home Restaurant, John Leary, Ella O'Leary, 415 Main, and 513 Main**.
3. Determine whether the move to 513 occurred before or after the 1915 Cox pool-hall raid.
4. Do not treat 513 as occupied by Home Restaurant in 1915 without a date-specific address source.
