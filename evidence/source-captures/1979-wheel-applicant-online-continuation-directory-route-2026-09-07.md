## 3. Later City wording control — do not over-read the 1979 minute

A separate, later Oregon City Commission record provides a useful wording/control example but belongs to the **separate 505 Main chain**, not The Wheel:

- WebDrawer record **4779030**;
- **6 April 1988** regular meeting;
- the official text expressly describes an OLCC application as a **`change of ownership`** from **Dick or Sheila Wiitanen** for **The Dug Inn (formerly The Brass Rail), 505 Main Street**, to Stillman H. and M. Diane Cotterell.

Source:

`https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4779030/File/document`

This comparison is used **only as a municipal-record wording control**. It must not be merged into The Wheel chronology.

Research consequence: Oregon City records can explicitly identify a transaction as a **change of ownership** when that information survives in the record. The 6 June 1979 Wheel minute merely says **liquor license application** and supplies no party names or transaction category. Therefore the 1979 wording cannot itself be promoted to ownership-transfer proof.

## 5. Modern `46 years in business` metadata rejected as historical evidence

Current Yellow Pages search results describe **The Wheel Bar and Grill, 503 Main**, phone **503-656-9717**, as having **46 years in business**. In 2026 that superficially points to about 1980.

This was **not accepted as historical evidence** because the metadata may reflect directory/listing/account history rather than the founding of the Wheel, the start of the phone number, or a business-ownership transition. It must not be used to date Raye's sale to the Wiitanens.

Classification: **UNVERIFIED MODERN DIRECTORY METADATA — NOT PROMOTED**.
