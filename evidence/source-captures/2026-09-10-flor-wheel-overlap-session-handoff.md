# Current governing Flor / Remus / Kwality → Wheel sequence

As of current `main`, the defensible sequence remains:

1. **9 Nov. 1949** — official Oregon City searchable text names **Albert D. Flor** in City claims/payee material. Business/address/purpose not stated.
2. **5 Dec. 1951** — official City searchable text names **Albert D. Flor and Margaret Remus** together among approved liquor-license applicants. Premises/trade name/license class not stated.
3. **August 1953 claims presented 10 Sep. 1953** — official City searchable text includes **`Al & Marge Kwality Cafe`**. Surnames/address not printed.
4. **1953** — official-secondary City historic-resource material says **Fred Remus was a cook at Kwality Cafe**. Relationship to Margaret remains unproved.
5. **1957** — official-secondary City synthesis places **Mr. and Mrs. Albert D. Flor** with the restaurant/cocktail-bar operation at **503 Main**. The underlying `Historic Oregon City` page remains unrecovered.
6. **28 Mar. 1958** — visually verified primary advertisement directly prints **Wheel Cafe & Lounge — 503 Main — phone 9106**.

The combined interpretation remains:

**STRONG CROSS-SOURCE CONTINUITY HYPOTHESIS — NOT DIRECT IDENTITY / OWNERSHIP / TRANSFER PROOF.**
