## Interpretation retained

**Documented use of an address does not establish continuity of its physical building.** The earlier c.1919 estimate for 503 and c.1920 estimate for 505 remain survey-derived estimates requiring primary construction or improvement records. Replacement, substantial reconstruction, alteration, and imprecise survey dating remain possibilities; no single explanation is selected. A 505-address structure existed before 1940, but that alone cannot identify which fabric survives or what the 1940 inventory date represents.

The current record does not establish a contemporaneous 1919–1920 construction event at 503 or 505. Failure to find a notice in the bounded searches is not proof that no work occurred.
