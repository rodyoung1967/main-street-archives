## Page-by-page review

### Sequence 1 — VISUALLY VERIFIED

The front-page article **“HOW’S THIS FOR SPRING OPENER? / A Few Starters for the City’s Big Year / BUILDING ON EVERY STREET”** lists multiple Oregon City construction projects.

Relevant screening distinction:

- It says preparations were under way for the **Main Street property where Williams Bros. transfer office was then located, and the barber shop adjoining**, with a modern business place planned.
- The same article separately places the Mt. Hood Brewing Co. block south from Eighth, the library at the Seventh Street stairway, V. Harris at Fourteenth/Main, and other projects outside the target frontage.

The Williams Bros./barber item initially warranted location checking because no number is printed on page 1. It is **not assigned to 501–511 Main** from this wording alone.

### Sequence 2 — VISUALLY VERIFIED

A Williams Bros. Transfer Co. advertisement states:

- office in **Favorite Cigar Store**;
- **opposite Masonic Building**.

This provides same-issue relative-location control for the page-1 Williams Bros. construction item.

The City of Oregon City’s 2000 Downtown Community Plan / Historic Preservation Plan, Appendix B, was visually checked as an independent official-secondary geographic control. The actual scan lists **Masonic Building at 707–709 Main Street** and **Stratton Building at 706–708 Main Street**. This visually checked table corrects a misleading text-extraction/search rendering that swapped those two historic names.

Accordingly, the same-issue “opposite Masonic Building” wording places Williams Bros./Favorite Cigar Store in the **700-block context**, strongly excluding the 501–511 target frontage. It still does not establish an exact Williams Bros. numbered address.
