# 1917 all-online-source-class closeout

Closeout level: **ONLINE COMPLETE**
Source/evidence: `S-303` / `E-277`

## Newspaper certification

Historic Oregon Newspapers' complete ten-page title catalog (451 titles; 11 Oregon City entries) and title JSON establish that the only Oregon City titles with 1917 online issues are the weekly *Oregon City Courier* (`sn00063698`, 52 Thursday issues) and *Oregon City Enterprise* (`sn00063700`, 52 Friday issues). *Morning Enterprise* (`sn00063701`) has no 1917 issues; its online run ends in 1913.

| Title | Issues | Archive slots | Genuine intended-date pages visually reviewed | Gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Oregon City Courier | 52 | 436 | 435 | true 13 Dec. p.21 (`MF-029`) |
| Oregon City Enterprise | 52 | 416 | 414 | true 16 Mar. p.5 (`MF-049`); true 13 Apr. p.8 (`MF-051`) |
| **Total** | **104** | **852** | **849** | **3 true-page gaps** |

All online-retrievable pages were visually reviewed. OCR served only as a discovery index. The Courier 13 Dec. sequence 21 is visibly a 27 Dec. page. Enterprise 16 Mar. sequence 5 is a distinct 23 Mar. p.5, and 13 Apr. sequence 8 is a second p.6 variant. Reasonable online recovery attempts did not recover the three true pages, so they remain precisely identified retrieval gaps/manual callouts.

Verified newspaper findings include: Fashion Stable at Main between Fourth/Fifth through 28 Dec.; J. C. Penney/The Golden Rule at **524–526 Main** on 12 Oct. (`T-101`); the 22 Mar. citywide report that Main Street storerooms were unavailable and two business blocks were contemplated (`T-102`); and qualified, unaddressed Farr Brothers/Farrs' market references. No exact 501/503/505 occupant was established.
