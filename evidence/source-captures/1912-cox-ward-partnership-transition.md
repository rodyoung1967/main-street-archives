# Cox & Ward Main Street billiards/cigar business — 1912 partnership transition

Status: **VISUALLY VERIFIED AT PAGE LEVEL** for the 9 February 1912 transition article. The 5 January 1912 business-grid ad is also visually verified in the separate Jan. 5 source-capture note. This does not certify either full newspaper issue as page-by-page reviewed.

## 5 January 1912 — Cox & Ward active on Main Street

The 5 January 1912 *Oregon City Courier*, page 6, visually shows a separate advertisement for **Cox & Ward** offering:

- choice cigars and candies;
- soft drinks;
- billiard and pool parlors;
- Main Street.

No street number is printed in the recovered ad.

Source:
https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-01-05/ed-1/seq-6/

On the same page, **H. N. Smith is independently and visually documented operating a pool room at 503 Main Street**. Therefore Cox & Ward and Smith were separate businesses on 5 January 1912; Cox & Ward must not be assigned to 503 from business-type similarity.

## 9 February 1912 — Eugene Ward sells out to Mr. Cox

The original page scan of the **9 February 1912 Oregon City Courier, page 8** has been visually inspected. The local-news item states that **Eugene Ward, of the firm of Cox and Ward, cigar store and billiard parlors, Main St., sold his interest to Mr. Cox and left the city**. The article says Ward's health required a move to a higher, drier climate.

Source:

This establishes:

1. the Ward partner was **Eugene Ward**;
2. Cox & Ward operated a **Main Street cigar store and billiard parlors**;
3. by 9 February 1912 Ward had sold his interest to **Mr. Cox**;
4. the Cox partner apparently continued or acquired Ward's interest in the business.

## Relationship to Frank D. Cox

Later evidence establishes **F. D. Cox** as a licensed Oregon City pool/billiard operator in October 1912 and **Frank D. Cox** as a Main Street billiard proprietor by February 1915.

This chronology makes Frank D. Cox a strong candidate for the Cox partner in Cox & Ward, but **the 9 February article does not print Cox's first name or initials**. The identity is therefore not yet proven and should not be merged without a direct source.

If future evidence proves the Cox partner was Frank D. Cox, the sequence would become:

- 5 Jan 1912 — Cox & Ward, Main Street billiards/cigar business, exact number unknown;
- 9 Feb 1912 — Eugene Ward sells his interest to Cox;
- Mar 1912 — H. N./H. H. Smith separately associated with the 503 Main pool room/license;
- 2 Oct 1912 — F. D. Cox receives an Oregon City pool/billiard license;
- 7 Feb 1915 — Frank D. Cox listed as Oregon City's Main Street billiard parlor proprietor.

Even if the identity is proven, this would **not** place Cox at 503, because Smith is visually documented at 503 while Cox & Ward were simultaneously operating on Main Street.
