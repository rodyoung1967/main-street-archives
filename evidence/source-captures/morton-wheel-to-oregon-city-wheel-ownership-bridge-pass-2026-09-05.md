## Morton, Washington — Wheel Cafe chronology

### Business identity

Later public listings and local-history material identify **The Wheel Cafe**, later **Wheel Cafe & Jubilee Room**, at **145 Main Street, Morton, Washington**. These later sources establish the existence/location of the Morton business but do not identify its 1950s–1960s proprietor.

### Early-1950s existence lead

A later obituary for **Faye Edith (Hunt) Kaiser** says she worked as a waitress at **The Wheel Cafe in Morton** before her 1952 marriage. The obituary is secondary retrospective evidence, so it does not establish the exact beginning/end of her employment, but it places the Morton Wheel in the community by the early 1950s.

Classification: **SECONDARY BIOGRAPHICAL EVIDENCE** for existence by the early 1950s; no ownership inference.

### 1957 indexed newspaper lead

Indexed text for the **11 December 1957 Centralia Daily Chronicle** includes **the Wheel Cafe** in a list of Morton businesses. The page image was not recovered in this research environment.

Classification: **OCR/INDEX TEXT ONLY / RETRIEVAL GAP**. It corroborates the business's 1957 presence but does not identify a proprietor.

## Telephone-number boundary

The new direct 1958 evidence establishes **9106** as the Wheel's public business number at that time. Rodney's corrected family memory identifies **656-9717** as the later Wheel business line and **656-5505** as probably Raye's home line.

Do **not** infer a specific conversion/change date between `9106` and `656-9717` without dated telephone-directory or advertisement evidence. Historical telephone-number format changes can reflect exchange conversion as well as business/ownership changes.

## Chronological consequence for the family hypothesis

The oral-history sequence remains plausible but is now more tightly constrained:

1. the Morton Wheel existed by the early 1950s and is independently indexed in 1957;
2. the Oregon City **Wheel Cafe & Lounge is already operating at 503 Main by 22 August 1958**;
3. Raye/Floyd are later associated with the Myrtle Point hotel/coffee-shop/Club Lehua cluster in 1962–1965;
4. Raye later owned/bought the Oregon City Wheel, but her seller and acquisition date remain unresolved.

Therefore, if the same Morton owners later owned the Oregon City Wheel before Raye, the bridge must be demonstrated by **people/proprietor/licensee records**, not by assuming the common Wheel name migrated between towns. Possible chronologies include a Morton proprietor acquiring or already operating the Oregon City Wheel by 1958, or a later pre-Raye ownership interval; the evidence does not yet choose between them.

## Current conclusion

**DOCUMENTED:** Morton had a Wheel Cafe by the early 1950s; Oregon City had **Wheel Cafe & Lounge at exact 503 Main by 22 August 1958, phone 9106**.

**FAMILY / ORAL HISTORY:** Raye worked at the Morton Wheel; the Morton owners later owned the Oregon City Wheel; Raye bought it from them; later Wheel business phone remembered as **656-9717**; **656-5505** likely Raye's home line.

**UNRESOLVED:** the Morton proprietor name(s), the Oregon City Wheel proprietor/licensee in 1958, Raye's exact acquisition date and seller, and whether the same person/couple appears in both towns.

Do **not** merge the Morton Wheel and Oregon City Wheel ownership histories until a contemporary source explicitly bridges the people or transaction.
