Type: Working hypothesis / interpretive synthesis  
Date: 24 August 2026; updated 3 September 2026  
Sources: synthesis of `E-010`, `E-011`, `E-020`, `E-021`, `E-032`, `E-100`, `S-033`, `S-063`, `S-116`, plus the visually reviewed 1928 Municipal Activities Report aerial  
Related records: `evidence/source-captures/505-rear-expansion-1940-date-hypothesis.md`; `evidence/source-captures/1928-municipal-fire-report-leads-503-main-fifth-main-garage.md`

## Claims

A testable but still **unresolved** interpretation holds that the **1940** "primary construction date" reported for **505 Main** on official historic inventories (`E-100` / `S-116`; corroborated in `S-063`) may reflect a **rearward expansion, major rear remodel, or related improvement event** rather than the first erection of the entire premises at that address.

Supporting context already in the archive:

- Mitch Young recalled that the **rear portion of 505 may have been added later** (`E-010`).
- Field/project observation indicates **505 extends farther back than 503** (`E-011`).
- **1925 Sanborn** shows separate **505 billiards/cigars** occupancy (`E-020`).
- **1936 aerial** evidence shows a distinct **505 Main** roof alongside 501 and 503 (`E-021`).
- City **2000 inventory** gives **c.1920** for 505 while later inventories give **1940** (`E-032`).

### 3 September 2026 — 1928-report aerial materially strengthens the rear-expansion model

The original **1928 Municipal Activities Report** is now preserved in the repository and its embedded aerial on **PDF page 4** has been visually inspected. The photograph itself is not separately captioned with an exposure date, so it is treated conservatively as the **1928-report aerial**, not as independently date-stamped 1928 photography.

When the visible Fifth/Main street geometry is aligned with the independently verified 1925 Sanborn address sequence, the photograph shows the later-generation **501 / 503 / 505 Main frontage already in place**. More importantly for this hypothesis, the rear extent associated with **505** is visibly **shallower than the later/current rear form**: the later deeper rear configuration has not yet appeared in the state shown by the photograph.

This is the first report-era visual evidence in this hypothesis that directly supplies a **pre-rear-expansion baseline** for 505. It strengthens the proposition that the modern/later rear portion was added or materially remodeled after the state visible in the aerial.

It still does **not** establish that the work occurred in 1940. The image supplies a relative sequence, while the exact exposure date, construction date, permit, assessor change, owner, builder and scope of the rear work remain unresolved.

See `evidence/source-captures/1928-municipal-fire-report-leads-503-main-fifth-main-garage.md` for the visual-review boundary and source handling.

This hypothesis **does not** supersede the c.1920 synthesis, **does not** prove a 1940 rear addition, and **does not** identify a permit, owner, builder, or exact scope of work.

## Competing interpretations preserved

A whole-building rebuild around 1940, assessor/survey dating convention, phased alterations, or an erroneous inherited 1940 date remain live alternatives for explaining the official inventory conflict. The 1928-report aerial now weighs against interpreting 1940 as the first-ever existence of the visible Main-frontage 505 premises, but it does not by itself determine what work occurred around 1940.

**August 2026 footprint pass (`E-102`):** visually verified **1925 vs. 1950** Sanborn continuity at 505 with **CONC. FL.** by 1950 and major block reconfiguration; preliminary **1936/1940/1944** aerial comparison suggested block-level density change without isolating 505 rear depth; **1939–1941 permits not recovered**. The newly reviewed 1928-report aerial improves the address-level early baseline but does not replace the need for a later dated footprint bridge.

## Confidence

- **High** that 505 existed as a distinct commercial frontage before 1940 and that the later/current rear depth was not yet present in the state visible in the 1928-report aerial.
- **Moderate** that a later rearward expansion/major rear remodel explains at least part of the 1940 inventory-date conflict.
- **Unresolved** for the exact date of the rear work and for whether **1940** specifically marks that event.
- **Very High** that the official **c.1920 vs. 1940** dating conflict exists and requires explanation.

## 5 September 2026 — address-level aerial re-review

A focused re-review of the canonical local `S-023` images now identifies **1940 (`IMG-0008`) as the earliest reviewed frame in which 505 can be distinguished as extending materially farther rearward/westward than 503**. The 1936 frame does not securely show the later asymmetry; that ambiguity is not evidence that the deeper portion did not exist. Later frames and independent 1975 CCHS `P-1293` (`E-203`) confirm the deeper state.

This materially strengthens the rear-expansion/remodel interpretation but does **not** prove a 1940 construction event. The same re-review narrows the separate 501 removal interval to **1936–1940**.
