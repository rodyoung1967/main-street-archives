# E-073 — Pacific Telephone Oregon City Expansion, 1908

Type: Primary newspaper / utility-business evidence  
Date: 31 July 1908  
Source: `S-087`  
Related evidence: `E-065`, `E-070`  
Related research lead: `RL-027`

## Claims Supported

The 31 July 1908 *Oregon City Enterprise* reported that the Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Company's Oregon City office received a **$10,000 appropriation** from the head office to meet growing business and provide additional facilities.

Manager **George J. Hall** had been notified of the appropriation. The article says new cables would serve Parkplace, Gladstone, West Oregon City, Ely, and the Mount Pleasant district and states that **all wires would be moved from Main Street**.

## Significance

This is strong evidence that the company's Oregon City system continued to expand in 1908 after the new central-office plant documented in 1907 (`E-065`). It also independently corroborates implementation of the Main Street overhead-wire removal program (`E-070`).

Later advertisements place Pacific Telephone & Telegraph at **510 Main Street in 1910**. That later address is useful for `RL-027`, but it is not projected backward into 1908 without evidence that the office occupied the same premises and number continuously.

## Limitation

The July 1908 article does not itself print the Oregon City office's numbered address. Although `E-065` places the new 1907 central office on the east side of Main between Fifth and Sixth, this record does not independently prove that the $10,000 work was confined to that building.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the $10,000 appropriation, system expansion, and continued removal of Main Street wires.  
**Unresolved** for the exact 1908 office number and lot.
