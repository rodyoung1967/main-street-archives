# E-084 — Chicago Store Moves from 405 to 505 Main, 1911

Type: Primary newspaper advertising / exact-address business-relocation evidence  
Date: 27 January–28 April 1911  
Source: `S-097`  
Related evidence: `E-026`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-026`

## Claims Supported

The *Oregon City Courier* documents a narrow 1911 relocation window for **The Chicago Store**:

- **27 January 1911:** The Chicago Store advertised at **405 Main Street**. The advertisement identifies **Wolf & Nat, proprietors**, and describes the business as gents' furnishers and tailors.
- **3 February 1911:** the same advertisement again gives **405 Main Street**, Wolf & Nat as proprietors, and the wording **Electric Hotel Building**.
- **31 March 1911:** The Chicago Store advertised at **505 Main St., Oregon City**, selling new and second-hand clothing and offering cleaning and repairing.
- **7, 14, and 28 April 1911:** advertisements repeat **505 Main St.**

The documented move/readdressing therefore occurred **after 3 February 1911 and by 31 March 1911**.

## Significance for 505 Main

This is direct primary evidence that **505 Main was an occupied commercial address by 31 March 1911**. It materially improves the earlier broad `E-026` chronology by showing when The Chicago Store entered the target address.

The 505 advertisements available in this pass do not visibly identify the proprietors. Although Wolf & Nat are explicitly named at 405 Main through 3 February, this evidence record does **not** assume that the same proprietorship continued at 505 without a direct continuity source.

## Building-Date Limitation

Occupancy of **505 Main in 1911** proves the address/storefront existed in use by that date. It does **not** prove that the surviving 505 building later dated approximately 1920/1925 was already standing. The 1911 business could have occupied a predecessor premises at the same address or a footprint later rebuilt or altered.

## Confidence

**Very High** for Chicago Store at 405 Main through 3 February 1911 and at 505 Main by 31 March 1911.  
**Very High** for the relocation window.  
**Unresolved** for proprietorship immediately after the move and for the physical relationship between the 1911 505 premises and the surviving building.
