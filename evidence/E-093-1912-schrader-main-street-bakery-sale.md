# E-093 — Schrader Sells Main Street Bakery to Wemmo & Haas, 1912

Type: Primary newspaper / business-transfer and relative-location evidence  
Date: 5 January and 20 December 1912  
Sources: `S-102`, `S-106`  
Related evidence: `E-051`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-026`

## Claims Supported

Two 1912 *Oregon City Courier* pages establish a business transition for **Schrader's Bakery**:

- **5 January 1912:** Schrader's Bakery advertised on the Oregon City business page as **near the Post Office**. No numbered Main Street address is printed.
- **20 December 1912:** the *Courier* reported that **Herman Schrader**, who had been in the baking business in Oregon City for seven years, sold his two bakeries—one at Seventh and John Adams streets and the other **on Main Street**—to **Wemmo & Haas**, formerly in the baking business in Germany and Portland.

## Significance

This documents a year-end ownership/operator transition for a Main Street bakery whose earlier 1912 advertising placed it in the Post Office vicinity. It is useful to the continuing reconstruction of the Fifth–Sixth commercial cluster and adjacent Post Office businesses.

## Limitation

Neither 1912 item gives a numbered address for Schrader's Main Street bakery. The January relative description **near the Post Office** must not be converted to 505, 507, 509, 511, 513, or another number without independent mapping evidence.

The sale is a business-transfer event and does not by itself establish a building sale, demolition, remodel, or relocation.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the Main Street bakery sale to Wemmo & Haas in December 1912 and for the January near-Post-Office relative location.  
**Unresolved** for the exact numbered Main Street address and physical premises.
