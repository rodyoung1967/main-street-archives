# E-072 — Unidentified Shoe Retailer Between Fifth & Sixth on Main, 1908

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement / block-occupancy evidence  
Date: 22 May 1908; sale advertised for 27 May 1908  
Source: `S-086`

## Claims Supported

A large *Oregon City Courier* advertisement for a one-day shoe sale gives the location twice as:

- **Main Street, between 5th and 6th Streets**; and
- **Main Street, Bet. 5th and 6th, Oregon City, Oregon**.

The advertisement offered a choice from 250 pairs of shoes at a promotional price.

## Significance

This is direct evidence of an active retail storefront in the target Fifth-to-Sixth Main Street block in May 1908, shortly after the 1906 numbering program and the 1907 street/grade reconstruction.

## Limitation

The available OCR/layout does not reliably recover the merchant or store name. The source therefore must **not** be assigned to Brightbill, Tolpolar, 501, 503, 505, 507, or any other business/address without additional evidence.

Identifying the advertiser from the original page image, adjacent issues, or a directory remains a follow-up.

## Confidence

**Very High** for a shoe retailer operating on Main between Fifth and Sixth in May 1908.  
**Unresolved** for merchant identity and exact numbered address.
