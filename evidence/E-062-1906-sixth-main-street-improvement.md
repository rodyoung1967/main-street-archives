# E-062 — Sixth Street Improvement Across Main Street, 1906

Type: Primary municipal notices / newspaper reporting  
Date: May–August 1906  
Source: `S-076`  
Related research lead: `RL-024`

## Claims Supported

Oregon City carried out substantial street work at **Sixth and Main**, the north end of the 5th–6th Main Street block:

- A 25 May 1906 official notice states that Sixth Street would be improved from Water Street to Main Street and from Main Street to the Oregon & California Railroad stone wall on Railroad Avenue.
- Work included crushed rock or gravel across the full width, grading, wooden sidewalks, wooden curbs, drains, gutters, and corner blocks.
- On 13 July 1906 the Council passed an ordinance re-establishing the grade of Sixth Street between Main Street and Railroad Avenue and an ordinance for improvement of Sixth from Water Street to Railroad Avenue.
- The same July meeting considered a petition to abandon/remove the hill steps at Sixth Street and erect steps at Fifth Street instead.

## Significance

This is direct evidence of major **public-realm and grade work immediately adjacent to the target Main Street frontage in 1906**. It should be considered when interpreting entrances, sidewalk elevations, stairs, and later physical changes to Block 26.

## Limitation

No recovered 1906 source ties this street work to demolition, removal, or replacement of the commercial buildings later numbered 501, 503, or 505 Main. The work is therefore block context, not evidence of a storefront replacement.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the Sixth Street improvement scope and grade work.  
**Unresolved** for any effect on individual Main Street buildings.
