# E-083 — Main Street Concrete Walks, Wooden Awning and Projecting-Sign Removal, 1910

Type: Primary municipal record + primary newspaper streetscape evidence  
Date: July–October 1910  
Source: `S-096`  
Related evidence: `E-066`, `E-070`  
Related research lead: `RL-024`

## Claims Supported

Oregon City continued its modernization of Main Street in 1910.

- **6 July 1910:** City Council ordered the Street Superintendent to require property owners along **Main Street between Eleventh Street and the Basin** to install concrete walks where necessary. The Fifth–Sixth block lies within this corridor.
- **14 October 1910:** the *Oregon City Courier* reported a Council order requiring the city's old **wooden awnings** projecting over sidewalks to be removed and requiring projecting signs to be taken down and placed flat against buildings. The paper described this as another step toward improving the city's appearance.

## Significance

Together with the 1907 Main Street track/grade reconstruction (`E-066`) and the 1908 removal of overhead utility poles and wires (`E-070`), these actions document a multi-year transformation of the Main Street public realm immediately around the target block.

Historic photographs before and after 1910 may therefore differ substantially in sidewalks, awnings, projecting signs, utilities, and storefront visibility without indicating that the underlying building was replaced.

## Limitation

Neither action identifies a specific 501/503/505/507 storefront, and neither establishes demolition or replacement of a target building.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the Main Street sidewalk order and citywide awning/projecting-sign removal action.  
**Unresolved** for the timing and physical details of compliance at any individual target storefront.
