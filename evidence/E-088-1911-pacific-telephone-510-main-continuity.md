# E-088 — Pacific Telephone & Telegraph at 510 Main, 1911 Continuity

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement / exact-address continuity evidence  
Date: 6 January 1911  
Source: `S-101`  
Related evidence: `E-065`, `E-073`, `E-082`  
Related research lead: `RL-027`

## Claims Supported

A **6 January 1911** *Oregon City Courier* advertisement identifies **Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. at 510 Main Street, Oregon City**.

This carries the exact-address evidence established in repeated August–December 1910 advertisements (`E-082`) directly into 1911.

## Significance

The sequence is now:

- 1907 — new Pacific States Telephone central-office plant completed on the east side of Main between Fifth and Sixth (`E-065`);
- 1908 — major Oregon City telephone-system expansion (`E-073`);
- August–December 1910 — Pacific Telephone & Telegraph explicitly at **510 Main** (`E-082`);
- **6 January 1911 — 510 Main continues.**

This strengthens the likelihood that 510 Main was the same physical central-office plant completed in 1907, but it still does not directly prove that continuity or justify backdating the exact 510 number to 1907.

## Confidence

**Very High** for Pacific Telephone & Telegraph at 510 Main on 6 January 1911.  
**High** for continuity with the late-1910 510 Main office; exact continuity with the 1907 plant remains unresolved.
