# E-055 — Holman & Pace Business Continuity, 1918–1921

Type: Primary newspaper references / advertisements  
Sources: `S-067`, `S-068`

## Claims Supported

- October–December 1918 Oregon City newspaper references identify a **Holman & Pace chapel / funeral parlors**, establishing the business name and funeral-business continuity in Oregon City.
- A 17 February 1921 *Eastern Clackamas News* advertisement identifies **Holman & Pace** as undertakers, embalmers and funeral directors in Oregon City.

## Limitation

The recovered 1918 and 1921 sources **do not state Fifth & Main or 501 Main**. They therefore cannot be used to claim that Holman & Pace remained at the Fifth-and-Main premises through 1921.

Confidence: **High** for business continuity; **Unresolved** for business location after the January 1917 Fifth-and-Main advertisement.
