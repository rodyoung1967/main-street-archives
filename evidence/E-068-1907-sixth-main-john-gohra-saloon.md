# E-068 — John Gohra Saloon at Main & Sixth, 1907

Type: Primary newspaper / liquor-license notice  
Date: 12 April 1907  
Source: `S-082`

## Claims Supported

A liquor-license notice in the 12 April 1907 *Oregon City Enterprise* states that **John Gohra** sought renewal of a license for his existing place of business at **Main and Sixth streets, Oregon City**.

## Significance

This is a contemporary **north-end business anchor** for the Fifth-to-Sixth Main Street block in 1907, complementing the Mount Hood Cafe evidence at the Fifth Street end.

It is also relevant to the 1906 William Tell / Gambrinus property research at Sixth & Main, but the current evidence does not establish that Gohra's saloon occupied the William Tell building or lot 8, Block 26.

## Limitation

The notice does not print a numbered Main Street address. Do not assign the business to 507, 509, or any other later number without a direct address-mapping source.

## Confidence

**Very High** for John Gohra operating at Main & Sixth in April 1907.  
**Unresolved** for exact numbered address, lot, and relationship to the William Tell / Gambrinus property.
