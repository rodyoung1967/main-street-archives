# E-104 — 501 Main Liquor-Premises Sequence, 1912-1913

## Status

**VISUALLY VERIFIED primary newspaper evidence.**

## What the pages say

- On 19 March 1912, Joe Wilson applied for a three-month liquor license at **the Palace Saloon, 501 Main Street** (`S-118`).
- On 3 October 1913, Hunsaker & Taylor applied for a three-month liquor license at **their place of business, 501 Main Street** (`S-119`).

## What this proves

The two independent notices prove that 501 Main hosted separately documented liquor-license premises in March 1912 and October 1913.

## Limits

The notices do not prove that Hunsaker & Taylor acquired, renamed, or continued Joe Wilson's Palace Saloon; that either applicant owned the building; that the address operated continuously between the notices; or that either business relates to the July 1922 Fifth/Main fire. The exact 1922 origin storefront remains unresolved among 501, 503, and 505.
