# E-075 — Philip Street Building Repair at Sixth & Main, 1909

Type: Primary municipal record / building-repair evidence  
Date: 1 December 1909  
Source: `S-089`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-026`

## Claims Supported

At the 1 December 1909 Oregon City Council meeting, the Recorder was instructed to notify **Philip Street** to remove rubbish from Sixth Street that had been caused by **the repair of his building at Sixth and Main Streets**.

This is direct evidence that a building at the north end of the Fifth–Sixth Main Street block was undergoing repair work in late 1909.

## Significance

This is the strongest 1909 evidence recovered in the current pass for actual work on a building at one of the target block's Main Street intersections. It is distinct from the broader public-works projects on Main and Water Streets.

The record is potentially relevant to the 1906 William Tell / Gambrinus lot-8 anchor at Sixth & Main, but **no evidence currently establishes that Philip Street's building was the William Tell/Gambrinus property, lot 8, or any later numbered address**.

The word **repair** is preserved as the Council record's characterization. It does not establish demolition, replacement, new construction, or the extent of the work.

## Confidence

**Very High** that Philip Street's building at Sixth & Main was under repair and that the work generated debris on Sixth Street in December 1909.  
**Unresolved** for exact street number, lot, building identity, owner/tenant relationship, and scope of repair.
