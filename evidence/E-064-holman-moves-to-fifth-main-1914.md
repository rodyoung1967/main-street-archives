# E-064 — R. L. Holman Moves to Fifth & Main, 1914

Type: Primary newspaper local notice  
Date: 26 February 1914  
Source: *Oregon City Courier*, p. 7  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-02-26/ed-1/seq-7/
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1914-02-26/ed-1/seq-7.pdf

## Claim supported

The *Courier* reports that **R. L. Holman moved his undertaking establishment from the Caufield Building on Eighth and Main Street to his building on Fifth and Main Street.**

## Importance

This resolves the earlier open lead about when Holman's undertaking establishment moved to Fifth & Main. Direct newspaper evidence now establishes the move by **26 February 1914**.

The notice also says **"his building"**, which is evidence that the Fifth-and-Main premises were owned by Holman at the time, although the exact parcel/legal ownership should still be tested against deeds or assessor records.

## Limitation

The notice does **not** give a numbered Main Street address. The later 1925 Sanborn shows 501 Main at/near the Fifth-and-Main corner, but the archive still must not equate Holman's 1914 premises with later-numbered **501 Main** without an independent address-mapping source.

Confidence: **Very High** for the move, old location, new Fifth-and-Main location, and wording that it was Holman's building; **unresolved** for equivalence with 501 Main.
