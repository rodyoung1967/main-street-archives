# E-091 — Gambrinus Saloon at Sixth & Main, William Trudell, 1912

Type: Primary newspaper / business-location and operator evidence  
Date: 24 March 1912  
Source: `S-103`  
Related evidence: `E-064`, `E-078`, `E-086`  
Related research leads: `RL-026`, `RL-031`, `RL-033`

## Claims Supported

A **24 March 1912 Morning Enterprise** report about a short-change attempt identifies **the Gambrinus Saloon at Sixth and Main streets** and names **William Trudell** as proprietor of “The Gambrinus.”

## Significance

This adds a ground-floor commercial-use anchor to the Gambrinus property chronology:

1. **1906:** the historic three-story frame William Tell Hotel at Sixth & Main / lot 8, Block 26 was sold to Gambrinus Brewing Company (`E-064`).
2. **1910:** a new two-story Gambrinus business block was under construction on the Gambrinus property at Sixth & Main (`E-078`).
3. **March 1911:** the new block's second story was occupied as an Electric Hotel annex (`E-086`).
4. **March 1912:** a **Gambrinus Saloon** was operating at Sixth & Main under William Trudell.

The sequence strongly documents continuing Gambrinus-associated use at the north end of Block 26 after the new building was completed.

## Limitation

The 1912 article does not print a numbered Main Street address and does not explicitly state that the saloon occupied the same part of the new block described in 1910–1911. It also does not supply the missing explicit demolition link between the old William Tell structure and the 1910 new building.

Do not assign the Gambrinus Saloon to 507, 509, 511, or another number solely from the Sixth/Main intersection wording.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the Gambrinus Saloon at Sixth & Main and William Trudell as proprietor on 24 March 1912.  
**High** for its relationship to the broader Gambrinus property/new-block chronology; exact storefront number and footprint remain unresolved.
