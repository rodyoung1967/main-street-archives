# 1902–1903 Main Street Mapping Synthesis

## Scope

This note synthesizes the 1902 and 1903 newspaper review for the Main Street frontage between 5th and 6th in Oregon City, with later 501–527 Main numbering used only as a research framework.

## Directly established

### 1902
- **The Union Store**, Michael proprietor, was explicitly at **Main St., between 5th and 6th Sts.** on 7 November 1902 (`E-059`).
- Herman Schrader's 1902 profile says he had worked nearly eighteen years in the **Main-Street Bakery** before buying his then-current bakery (`E-057`). Exact storefront continuity remains unresolved.
- Huntley Bros. occupied two adjoining store rooms that were combined/remodeled in October 1902 (`E-058`), but the recovered notice does not place those rooms on the study block.

### 1903
- **Portland Clothing House** was **next door to Harding's Drug Store** (`E-060`).
- **George A. Harding's drug store** was in the **Willamette Building** in April 1903 (`E-062`).
- **The Fair Store** advertised **opposite the Post Office** in January 1903 and was preparing to move in April-May (`E-061`).

## Working spatial relationships

The evidence permits the following relationship chain without assigning later numbers:

**Portland Clothing House** ⇄ **Harding's Drug Store / Willamette Building**

and separately:

**Fair Store** ⇄ **opposite Post Office**

The Fair Store is independently documented in the **Willamette Building by 1905**, making it plausible that its spring 1903 move was into that building. This is a **research hypothesis**, not a conclusion.

## Numbering-system caution

The 24 August 1906 *Oregon City Courier* reports that the council passed an ordinance **providing for numbering of houses** (`E-063`).

This does not prove that no address numbers existed earlier, but it is strong contextual evidence that Oregon City was formalizing or standardizing house numbering in 1906. Therefore:

- later addresses such as **509 Main** or **511 Main** should not automatically be projected backward into 1902–1904;
- early location language such as **between 5th and 6th**, **opposite Postoffice**, **next door to Harding's**, and named buildings should be treated as primary spatial evidence;
- exact later-number mapping should wait for a directory, map, deed, tax record, or newspaper source that bridges the pre-1906 descriptions to the later numbered system.
