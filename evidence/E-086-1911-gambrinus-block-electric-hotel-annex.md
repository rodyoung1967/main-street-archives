# E-086 — New Gambrinus Block Occupied as Electric Hotel Annex, 1911

Type: Primary newspaper / new-building occupancy evidence  
Date: 3 March 1911  
Source: `S-099`  
Related evidence: `E-078`  
Related research lead: `RL-031`

## Claims Supported

On **3 March 1911**, the *Oregon City Courier* reported that **J. Tobin, proprietor of the Electric Hotel, had rented the second story of the new Gambrinus block** for use as an annex because the hotel could not accommodate demand in its existing rooms.

The report describes the new Gambrinus block as equipped with:

- steam heat;
- electric lights; and
- hot and cold water connections in some rooms.

## Significance

This advances the Gambrinus construction chronology established in `E-078`:

1. June 1910 — construction staging authorized for a new building on the Gambrinus property at Sixth & Main.
2. August 1910 — basement reported complete for the new two-story Gambrinus business block.
3. **March 1911 — the new block was sufficiently complete and usable for the Electric Hotel to rent its entire second story as an annex.**

This is strong evidence that the new Gambrinus block was occupied/in service by early March 1911.

## Limitation

The article does not give a numbered Main Street address for the Gambrinus block. It also does not explicitly say that the new building replaced the three-story frame William Tell Hotel documented on the Gambrinus property in 1906. The direct demolition/replacement link remains open (`RL-031`).

## Confidence

**Very High** for second-story Electric Hotel annex occupancy by 3 March 1911 and for the described building amenities.  
**Unresolved** for exact numbered address and the precise William Tell demolition/replacement sequence.
