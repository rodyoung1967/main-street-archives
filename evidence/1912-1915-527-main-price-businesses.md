# 1912-1915 — 527 Main, Bailey & Price, and Price Bros.

This note resolves an archive-handling problem concerning **Bailey & Price** (billiards/pool) and **Price Bros.** (men's clothing). The two names must not be treated as the same business, and the phrase **"Sixth and Main"** must not be used to exclude Price Bros. from 527 Main without further evidence.

## Bottom line

- **Bailey & Price, Billiard and Pool Parlors** is directly documented at **527 Main Street** on 5 January 1912.
- Oregon City Council granted **Bailey and Price** a pool/billiard license on 2 October 1912, showing that the billiard business continued later in 1912.
- **Price Bros., clothiers**, was separately advertising at **Sixth and Main Streets** during 1912 and continuously through 1913.
- By 1915, business-directory advertising directly gives **Price Bros. — 527 Main St. — Phone 107**.
- The present Swartz Building at 527 is dated **1935**, so all 1912-1915 activity occurred in a predecessor building/storefront arrangement.

The evidence therefore establishes **two distinct contemporary businesses**, not a simple Bailey & Price → Price Bros. rename.

For 1913 specifically, the exact numbered address of Price Bros. is **not yet directly proved** by a recovered source. However, because 527 is associated with the Sixth/Main corner area, the repeated "Sixth and Main" wording is **compatible with 527** and must not be used as evidence that Price Bros. was elsewhere.

## 1. Bailey & Price directly at 527 — 5 January 1912

*Oregon City Courier*, 5 January 1912, page 6:
`https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-01-05/ed-1/seq-6/`

Advertisement:

- **Bailey & Price**
- Billiard and Pool Parlors
- **Opposite Grand Theater**
- **527 Main Street**
- Oregon City

This is direct primary numbered-address evidence.

## 2. Price Bros. is already a separate business in 1912

The same 5 January 1912 page separately advertises **Price Bros., Clothiers**, at **Sixth and Main Streets**.

Thus Bailey & Price's billiard parlor and Price Bros.' clothing store were operating contemporaneously and were plainly different businesses/business descriptions.

Do **not** infer that "Bailey & Price" was merely an earlier name for "Price Bros."

## 3. Bailey & Price still licensed in October 1912

Oregon City Council minutes, 2 October 1912, record that applications for pool and billiard licenses were granted to several operators including **Bailey and Price**.

Municipal source:
`https://ormswd.synergydcs.com/HPRMWebDrawer/Record/4826171/File/document`

This proves the billiard business continued at least into October 1912. The minutes do not provide a numbered address in the recovered text.

## 4. Price Bros. continuity at Sixth and Main through 1913

Price Bros. advertisements repeatedly use **Sixth and Main Streets**, including:

- 1912 advertising
- *Morning Enterprise*, 3 June 1913
- *Oregon City Courier*, 7 August 1913

The August 1913 advertisement identifies Price Bros., "Where Clothes Fit," established 1895, at **Sixth and Main Streets**.

This provides strong business-location continuity through 1913 but not, by itself, an exact numbered address.

## 5. Why "Sixth and Main" does not exclude 527

Earlier working notes incorrectly reasoned that Price Bros. being at "Sixth and Main" meant it should not be placed at 527 Main in 1913.

That inference is withdrawn.

The 527 site is associated with the **Sixth Street end/corner of the block**, and intersection-style business advertising commonly described corner/near-corner locations without printing the numbered address. Therefore "Sixth and Main" may be fully compatible with **527 Main**.

The correct archival position is:

> **Price Bros. — Sixth and Main in 1913; exact numbered address unresolved in recovered 1913 sources; 527 Main is plausible and later directly documented, but should not yet be backdated as direct fact.**

## 6. Price Bros. directly at 527 by 1915

*Oregon City Courier* business directories in 1915 explicitly list:

> **Price Bros. — Where clothes fit — Est. 1895 — 527 Main St. — Phone 107.**

Examples include 26 August, 16 September, 23 September, 30 September, 18 November and 2 December 1915.

This establishes direct 527 occupancy by 1915.

## 7. 1914 Bailey & Price municipal address anomaly

Oregon City Council minutes dated 7 October 1914 contain a recovered transcription stating that an application of **Bailey and Price** to conduct a pool hall at **526 Main Street** for three months was granted.

This creates an important later-location question:

- 1912 advertisement: Bailey & Price = **527 Main**.
- 1914 municipal minutes: Bailey & Price = **526 Main** in the recovered transcription.

Possible explanations include:

1. the billiard business moved across Main Street;
2. the municipal record contains a clerical/numbering error;
3. OCR/transcription has misread the original number;
4. address usage changed or the business occupied another associated space.

Do **not** silently change 527 to 526 or dismiss the 1914 record. The original 1914 minutes image should be visually checked if possible.

## 8. Present building is later

Official City/ODOT historic-resource sources date the **present Swartz Building at 527 Main to 1935** (`E-053`).

Therefore the 1912 Bailey & Price advertisement, the 1912-1913 Price Bros. intersection advertising, and the later direct 527 Price Bros. listing all concern a **pre-1935 predecessor building/storefront arrangement**.

This makes multiple commercial spaces or later reconfiguration plausible and is another reason not to force a one-business-per-modern-building assumption onto the early evidence.

## Recommended 1913 handling

For the 1913 chronological reconstruction:

- **527 Main occupant: unresolved as an exact numbered assignment.**
- **Price Bros.: definitely operating at Sixth and Main throughout 1913; 527 is plausible and becomes directly documented by 1915.**
- **Bailey & Price:** separate billiard/pool business directly at 527 in Jan 1912 and still municipally licensed Oct 1912; later 1914 municipal record reports 526 Main and requires reconciliation.

3. Find 1913 pool/billiard license renewals for Bailey & Price.
