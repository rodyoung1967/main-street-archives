# E-058 — I. Tolpolar on Main Street, 1905

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement  
Date: 3 February 1905  
Source: `S-071`  
Related source capture: `source-captures/1904-1905-main-street-research.md`

## Claim Supported

The 3 February 1905 *Oregon City Enterprise* carries an advertisement for **I. Tolpolar** on **Main Street, Oregon City, Oregon**. The advertisement offers new and second-hand furniture and household goods and is a direct continuation of the Main Street business documented in late 1904 (`E-057`).

## Significance

This establishes business continuity across the 1904–1905 year boundary and supplies a primary 1905 Main Street anchor for the block research.

## Limitation

The advertisement **does not give a numbered address**. It cannot be used to place Tolpolar at modern 503, 505, or 507 Main. No primary 1905 source recovered in the present search directly identifies the occupant of those three modern numbered addresses.

## Confidence

**Very High** for I. Tolpolar operating on Main Street on 3 February 1905.  
**Unresolved** for the exact numbered address and any relationship to 503, 505, or 507 Main.
