# E-063 — Oregon City House-Numbering Ordinance Context, 1906

Type: Primary municipal-news report  
Date: 24 August 1906  
Source: *Oregon City Courier*, p. 3  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1906-08-24/ed-1/seq-3/ocr/

## Claim supported

The Courier's report of the city council meeting says the council passed an ordinance **"Providing for numbering of houses."**

## Interpretation

This is important context for the 1902-1904 storefront reconstruction. It suggests that Oregon City's house/street numbering was being formally established or standardized in 1906.

It does **not** prove that no numbered addresses existed before 1906, nor does it establish the exact date when the later 5xx Main numbering took effect. However, it materially strengthens the handling rule that later addresses such as 509 or 511 Main should not automatically be projected backward into 1902-1904.

For those early years, contemporaneous descriptions such as **between 5th and 6th**, **opposite Postoffice**, **next door to Harding's**, and named buildings may be more reliable than later street numbers until an address-mapping source is found.

Confidence: **Very High** for the ordinance report; **Moderate** for the broader interpretation about numbering standardization.
