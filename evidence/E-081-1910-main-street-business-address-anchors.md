# E-081 — Main Street Business and Address Anchors, 1910

Type: Primary newspaper advertising / address and intersection evidence  
Date: 4 March and 23 September 1910  
Source: `S-094`  
Related evidence: `E-042`, `E-050`, `E-077`  
Related research leads: `RL-025`, `RL-026`, `RL-029`, `RL-033`

## Claims Supported

Two *Oregon City Courier* pages provide unusually useful 1910 Main Street anchors.

### 4 March 1910

A Price Bros. clothing advertisement gives the business location as **Sixth and Main Sts., Oregon City, Oregon**.

### 23 September 1910

A broad local-business contest/result page lists, among many firms:

- **H. P. Brightbill — 509 Main St.**
- **Price Bros. — corner Main and Sixth Sts.**
- **Pioneer Market, H. W. Streibig — corner Fifth and Main Sts.**
- **Geo. A. Harding — 611 Main St.**
- **Watson B. Eddy & Son — corner Main and Sixth Sts.**
- **Kelly's Restaurant — 519 Main St.**
- **Electric Hotel — Main St. between Fourth and Fifth Sts.**

## Significance

### Brightbill movement

`E-042` places Henry Brightbill's store at **503 Main** on 25 August 1908. The September 1910 listing places H. P. Brightbill at **509 Main**, narrowing the documented movement/readdressing window from 503 to 509 to **after 25 August 1908 and by 23 September 1910**. This predates the existing 5 January 1912 509 Main evidence (`E-050`).

### Sixth/Main

Price Bros. is independently placed at Sixth/Main in March and September 1910. Watson B. Eddy & Son is also described at Main/Sixth in September. Because an intersection has multiple corners and premises, these descriptions do not establish that either firm occupied the Gambrinus property or the same building.

### Harding chronology

George A. Harding's own business is listed at **611 Main** in September 1910. This is a personal/business address and must not be treated as the address of the separately named Harding Building around 1909–1911 (`E-077`). It reinforces the need to reconstruct the Harding Building independently rather than deriving its number from George Harding's store.

### Fifth/Main

Pioneer Market provides a direct 1910 commercial anchor at Fifth/Main, independent of E. R. Case's saloon record (`E-080`).

## Limitation

The page does not identify a 1910 occupant explicitly at 501, 503, 505, or 507 Main. Intersection descriptions must not be converted to those numbered addresses without independent mapping evidence.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the printed 1910 addresses and intersection descriptions.  
**Very High** that Brightbill was at 509 Main by 23 September 1910.  
**Unresolved** for how the Sixth/Main businesses map to individual corners/lots and for the exact Harding Building number.
