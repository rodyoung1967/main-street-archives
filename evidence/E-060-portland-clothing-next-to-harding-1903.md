# E-060 — Portland Clothing House Next Door to Harding's Drug Store, 1903

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement  
Date: 2 January 1903  
Source: *Oregon City Courier*, p. 4  
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1903-01-02/ed-1/seq-4.pdf

## Claim supported

The Portland Clothing House advertised its location as **"Next Door to Harding's Drug Store."**

## Interpretation

This is strong storefront-adjacency evidence for the pre-numbered Main Street commercial district. It does **not** itself state a street number for either business.

Later evidence places George A. Harding's drug business at 511 Main, but that later address must **not** be back-projected to 1903 without independent proof that Harding had not moved and that the later numbering scheme already applied.

Confidence: **Very High** for the adjacency; **unresolved** for exact numbered addresses.

Related evidence: `E-062`, `E-063`.
