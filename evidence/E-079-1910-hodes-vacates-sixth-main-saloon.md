# E-079 — Carl G. Hodes Vacates Sixth & Main Saloon, 1910

Type: Primary municipal liquor-license record  
Date: 1 June 1910  
Source: `S-093`  
Related evidence: `E-076`, `E-078`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-026`, `RL-031`

## Claims Supported

At the 1 June 1910 Oregon City Council meeting, the Council acted on **C. G. Hodes's saloon license** and extended its term to account for time missed **between his vacation of his saloon at Sixth and Main Streets and the expiration of the license**.

This establishes that the Hodes saloon documented at Sixth & Main in October 1909 (`E-076`) had been vacated for a period by 1 June 1910.

## Significance

The timing is potentially important because less than a month later, on 29 June 1910, Council authorized construction staging for a **new building on the Gambrinus property at Sixth & Main** (`E-078`). The Hodes vacancy therefore precedes the documented Gambrinus construction activity.

## Limitation

The Council record does not say **why** Hodes vacated, when the vacancy began, whether the saloon building was demolished, or whether Hodes occupied the Gambrinus property. The chronological proximity to the new Gambrinus construction is significant but does not prove causation.

## Confidence

**Very High** that Hodes had vacated his Sixth & Main saloon for a period by 1 June 1910.  
**Unresolved** for the exact premises, vacancy date, reason, and relationship to the new Gambrinus block.
