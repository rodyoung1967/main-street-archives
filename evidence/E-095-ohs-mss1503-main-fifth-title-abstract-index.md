The first page of the Oregon Historical Society's official 24-page **MSS 1503 Title Abstracts Index** was downloaded, rendered at high resolution, and visually inspected on 24 August 2026. The row reads:

- Box: **19**
- Legal Address / Title: **Block 4, Lots 3 & 4, Oregon City**

The title and Lot field conflict. This record intentionally preserves both; it does not decide whether the abstract covers Lots 3–4 or Lots 3–5.

## Official finding-aid context

The ArchivesWest finding aid identifies MSS 1503 as the Oregon Historical Society Research Library's **Land Collection**, says it is open to the public, and places Benton and Clackamas County title abstracts in **Series B, Box 19**. It says the abstracts are county-clerk print copies that convey ownership chains and can include maps, sketches, PLSS descriptions, and later subdivisions.

Those are collection-level facts. They do not prove what appears in this individual abstract.

## Historical significance and limits

The index row strongly overlaps the later/current Block 4 parcel-description problem around 503/505 and possibly part of 507 Main. It is therefore a high-priority route for testing:

- the Lot 5 Barlow/David ownership chain from the 1913 assessment toward 1922;
- whether **S. Wolf** appears in land or building/improvement ownership;
- the reported later Wolf-family ownership of 503;
- the mechanism connecting original Block 26 to later/current Block 4 descriptions.

The actual title abstract has not been found online, retrieved, or visually inspected. Accordingly, `E-095` proves **none** of those property-history propositions. It is a verified archival index finding plus an open **MANUAL CALLOUT**.
