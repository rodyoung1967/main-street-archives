# E-089 — Smith Pool Room / Pool Hall at 503 Main, 1912

Type: Primary newspaper advertising, legal notice, and business-transition evidence  
Date: 5 January–12 July 1912  
Sources: `S-102`, `S-103`, `S-104`  
Related evidence: `E-023`, `E-050`, `E-051`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-037`

## Claims Supported

Three 1912 newspaper items establish a pool-room / pool-hall sequence at or near **503 Main Street**:

- **5 January 1912 — Oregon City Courier:** a visually checked business advertisement reads **H. N. SMITH — Pool Room, Cigars and Tobacco — 503 Main Street**. The page OCR misreads the number as 603, but the page image clearly prints **503**.
- **24 March 1912 — Morning Enterprise:** a legal notice signed **H. H. SMITH** states that he intended to apply at the next City Council meeting for a license to run and regulate a pool hall at **his place of business, 503 Main Street**, for three months.
- **12 July 1912 — Oregon City Courier:** a local item says **H. H. Smith**, who had conducted a pool hall at **Fifth and Main streets**, left Wednesday for Aurora to open a pool hall there.

## Significance

This is strong evidence that **503 Main was an active pool-room / pool-hall address in early 1912** and that the associated Smith operation ended or changed by about 10 July when H. H. Smith left Oregon City for Aurora.

The July Fifth/Main description is especially useful geographic context because the March legal notice gives H. H. Smith's business as 503 Main. It supports a close Fifth/Main relationship for the 503 premises, but it is not treated as independent proof that 503 itself was literally the corner storefront without a direct address-to-lot mapping source.

## Initials Conflict

The January advertisement prints **H. N. Smith**, while the March legal notice and July departure item print **H. H. Smith**. The chronological, business-type, and address/location overlap strongly suggest a relationship and possibly the same operator, but the archive does **not** silently merge H. N. Smith and H. H. Smith as one person until another source resolves the initials discrepancy.

## Building-Date Limitation

The 1912 occupancy proves use of the **503 Main address/storefront**, not that the surviving building later dated about 1919 was already standing. If the c.1919 surviving-building date is correct, Smith occupied a predecessor premises at the same number or a footprint later rebuilt or materially altered.

## Confidence

**Very High** for H. N. Smith's printed 503 Main pool-room advertisement on 5 January, H. H. Smith's 503 Main pool-hall license application on 24 March, and H. H. Smith's departure from a Fifth/Main pool hall in July.  
**High** that the March and July H. H. Smith items describe the same business sequence.  
**Unresolved** whether H. N. Smith and H. H. Smith are the same person and whether the Fifth/Main wording proves a precise corner footprint.
