# 1904 Main Street Mapping Synthesis

## Scope

This note summarizes the 1904 *Oregon City Courier* review for the Main Street frontage between 5th and 6th, using later 501–527 Main numbering only as a research framework.

## Result

No defensible 1904 *Courier* reference was recovered that explicitly gives one of the later target addresses **501, 503, 505, 507, 509, 511, 513, 515, 517, 519, 521, 523, 525, or 527 Main**.

No new 1904 *Courier* item was recovered that explicitly places a named business **on Main between 5th and 6th**.

This is **not evidence of vacancy**. The early papers relied heavily on building names, intersections, landmarks, and neighboring businesses, and the 1906 city-council report that an ordinance was passed **providing for numbering of houses** (`E-063`) reinforces caution about expecting or back-projecting the later 5xx address system in 1904.

## Useful 1904 mapping anchors

### Willamette Building
The 26 August 1904 *Courier* reports Dr. O. A. Stuart with an office in the **Willamette Building**, showing that the named building was active commercial/office premises in 1904.

This matters because:
- George A. Harding's drug store is directly documented in the **Willamette Building** in April 1903 (`E-062`).
- Portland Clothing House is directly documented **next door to Harding's Drug Store** in January 1903 (`E-060`).
- The Fair Store is documented **opposite the Post Office** in January 1903 and preparing to move in spring 1903 (`E-061`).
- By 1905 the Fair Store appears in the **Willamette Building**; another 1905 ad calls its location **Main Street, Harding Block**. Whether **Harding Block** and **Willamette Building** were the same building or overlapping premises is now a high-value mapping question, not an established fact.

### Lamb & Sawyer's Building / Oregon City Bank
The 26 August 1904 *Courier* announces a new book store opening September 1 in **Lamb & Sawyer's Building, next to Oregon City Bank**. This is a useful building-to-landmark adjacency anchor, but it has not yet been mapped to a later 501–527 number.

### Other Main Street businesses
Cross & Shaw and Richard Petzold's Cash Market appear on Main Street in 1904, but the recovered evidence does not place Cross & Shaw in the target block. Later direct evidence places Petzold at **696 Main**, so Petzold should be excluded from the 501–527 candidate set.

Supplemental *Oregon City Enterprise* evidence places:
- **The Farmers' Store** on Main Street opposite Barlow's Grocery.
- **The Electric Grocery** on Main Street next to Albright's Market.

These are valid adjacency clues but are not yet tied to the 5th-to-6th study block.

## Working conclusion after 1902–1904

For these pre-numbering years, the strongest reconstruction method is to build a graph of contemporaneous relationships—**between 5th and 6th**, **opposite Post Office**, **next door to Harding's**, **Willamette Building**, **next to Oregon City Bank**—and only later attach the 5xx numbers when a source bridges the two systems.

The 1902–1904 review currently gives us more confidence in **relative storefront geography** than in exact numbered-address chronology.
