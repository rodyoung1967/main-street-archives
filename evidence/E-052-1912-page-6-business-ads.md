## Claims

Direct inspection of the page establishes or reinforces the following Main Street locations:

- **H. N. Smith** — pool-room / cigars and tobacco — explicitly **503 Main Street**. A separate March 1912 pool-hall-license notice at 503 uses **H. H. Smith** (`S-024`, `E-023`); the differing middle initial is an unresolved source conflict.
- **The Chicago Store** — advertisement says **"Main St. just below Postoffice."** Independent newspaper evidence (`E-026`) places the same business at **505 Main**, so this supplies relative-location evidence for 505 versus the Post Office.
- **G. H. Young, "The Curio Man"** — explicitly **507 Main Street**.
- **H. P. Brightbill** — explicitly **509 Main St.**, phone 74 (`S-059`, `E-050`). Visual inspection supersedes OCR-derived 609 readings.
- **George A. Harding**, druggist — explicitly **511 Main St.**
- **M. E. Dunn**, confectionery — **near Postoffice**; no numbered address printed.
- **Schrader's Bakery** — **near Postoffice**; no numbered address printed.
- **Pioneer Transfer Co.** — office **in Postoffice Building**; no numbered address printed.
- **Bailey & Price, Billiard and Pool Parlors** — explicitly **527 Main Street**, **Opposite Grand Theater**.
- **Clems Chop House** — **5th and Main Street**; no numbered address printed.
- **Wm. Gardner, Watchmaker and Jeweler** — **Main Near 8th**; relative location only.

## Address sequence significance

The page gives a rare same-date run of directly printed odd-side Main Street addresses: **503 H. N. Smith, 507 G. H. Young, 509 H. P. Brightbill, and 511 George A. Harding**. Together with independent evidence placing the Chicago Store at **505**, this materially strengthens the 1912 storefront reconstruction.

The page does **not** establish an exact number for the Post Office, M. E. Dunn, or Schrader's Bakery. Relative-location phrases must not be converted into numbered addresses without independent corroboration.

## 527 Main significance

The direct **Bailey & Price — 527 Main Street** advertisement proves that the numbered address was commercially occupied by January 1912. Later official historic-resource sources (`S-062`, `S-063`) date the **present Swartz Building at 527 Main to 1935**. Therefore Bailey & Price occupied a predecessor building/storefront at 527 unless later evidence establishes a relevant address renumbering.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the directly visible advertisement text and the explicit 503, 507, 509, 511 and 527 Main addresses.

**Unresolved** for whether H. N. Smith and the H. H. Smith named in the March 1912 notice are the same person.
