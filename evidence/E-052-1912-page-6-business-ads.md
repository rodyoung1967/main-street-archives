## Claims

The page-6 business advertisements establish or reinforce the following Main Street locations:

- **The Chicago Store** — advertisement says **"Main St. just below Postoffice."** Independent newspaper evidence (`E-026`) places the same business at **505 Main**, so this provides relative-location evidence for 505 versus the Post Office.
- **Bailey & Price, Billiard and Pool Parlors** — explicitly **527 Main Street**, **Opposite Grand Theater**.
- **Clems Chop House** — **5th and Main Street**; no numbered address printed in the advertisement.
- **Wm. Gardner, Watchmaker and Jeweler** — **Main Near 8th**; relative location only.
- The same issue also contains the H. P. Brightbill advertisement establishing **509 Main Street** (`S-059`, `E-050`).

## 527 Main significance

The direct **Bailey & Price — 527 Main Street** advertisement proves that the numbered address was commercially occupied by January 1912. Later official historic-resource sources (`S-062`, `S-063`) date the **present Swartz Building at 527 Main to 1935**. Therefore Bailey & Price occupied a predecessor building/storefront at 527 unless later evidence establishes a relevant address renumbering.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the text printed in the advertisements and for Bailey & Price at 527 Main on 5 January 1912.

Relative-location phrases such as "just below Postoffice" should not be converted into exact numbered addresses without independent corroboration.
