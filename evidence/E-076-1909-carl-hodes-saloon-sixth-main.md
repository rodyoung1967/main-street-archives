# E-076 — Carl G. Hodes Saloon at Sixth & Main, 1909

Type: Primary municipal record / liquor-license evidence  
Date: 6 October 1909  
Source: `S-090`

## Claims Supported

At the 6 October 1909 Oregon City Council meeting, **Carl G. Hodes** was among the applicants for renewal of saloon licenses. The record separately identifies a Hodes location at **Fourth and Main Streets** and another at **Sixth and Main Streets**. The Council ordered the renewal licenses granted subject to the required fees and bonds.

This directly establishes a saloon associated with Carl G. Hodes at **Sixth & Main** in 1909.

## Significance

The Hodes license provides a contemporary business anchor at the north end of the Fifth–Sixth target block, replacing the need to rely on later or ambiguous Harding Building descriptions for a 1909 intersection anchor.

## Limitation

The Council record does not print a numbered Main Street address and does not establish that the Hodes saloon occupied:

- Philip Street's building under repair later in 1909 (`E-075`);
- the former William Tell / Gambrinus lot-8 property (`E-064`); or
- any particular later-numbered storefront such as 507, 509, or 511 Main.

Those relationships remain unresolved pending the 1906 numbering plat, lot mapping, deeds, directories, or other direct address evidence.

## Confidence

**Very High** for Carl G. Hodes operating a licensed saloon at Sixth & Main in October 1909.  
**Unresolved** for exact number, lot, and building identity.
