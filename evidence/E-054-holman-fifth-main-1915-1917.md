# E-054 — Holman / Randall Undertaking Business at Fifth & Main, 1915–1917

Type: Primary newspaper advertisements  
Sources: `S-064`, `S-065`, `S-066`; supplemental batches `S-120` / `E-107` and `S-124` / `E-112`

## Claims Supported

- On 17 June 1915, the *Oregon City Courier* advertised **R. L. Holman and T. P. Randall** as leading undertakers at **Fifth and Main St.**
- On 2 March 1916, the *Courier* again placed **Holman and Randall** at **Fifth and Main St.**
- On 11 January 1917, the *Courier* placed **R. L. Holman** at **Fifth and Main St.**
- The saved 29 July–21 October 1915 batch repeatedly prints Holman/Randall at **Fifth and Main**, with dates/pages in [the visual log](source-captures/1915-courier-sheets-073-100.md). This strengthens the dated location record but gives no numbered storefront; the September 23 p3 near-accident item likewise adds location context only.

## Interpretation

These are direct primary sources for an undertaking business at the **Fifth-and-Main corner/location from at least June 1915 through January 1917**.

They **do not print the numbered address 501 Main**. Because the 1925 Sanborn later shows 501 as the corner store space, Holman's premises are an important candidate for the predecessor/earlier use of that space, but that address mapping remains unproven.

Confidence: **Very High** for Fifth-and-Main location and dates; **Moderate hypothesis only** for equivalence with later-numbered 501 Main.

Related building: `B-005` 501 Main (candidate relationship only).  
Related research lead: `RL-019`.

## October 28–November 25, 1915 corroboration

E-109/S-121 visually verifies Holman/Randall Fifth/Main advertisements on October 28 pp 2/7, November 4 p 7, November 11 p 7/seq 8, November 18 pp 2/7 and November 25 p 7. No numbered Fifth address or501 Main equation is supplied. November 11seq8's printed 6 pagination anomaly is tracked separately as1915-M04.

## 7 May 1915 partnership formation

The visually verified *Morning Enterprise* report on 7 May p5 says **T. P. Randall and R. L. Holman would become partners** in the undertaking establishment then conducted by Holman **on Main Street near Fifth**, and that the firm would be known as **Holman & Randall** (`S-124` / `E-112`). This supplies a formation milestone before the 17 June Fifth-and-Main advertisement. It does not print 501 Main or a numbered Fifth address.
