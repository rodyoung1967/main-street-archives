# E-054 — Holman / Randall Undertaking Business at Fifth & Main, 1915–1917

Type: Primary newspaper advertisements  
Sources: `S-064`, `S-065`, `S-066`

## Claims Supported

- On 17 June 1915, the *Oregon City Courier* advertised **R. L. Holman and T. P. Randall** as leading undertakers at **Fifth and Main St.**
- On 2 March 1916, the *Courier* again placed **Holman and Randall** at **Fifth and Main St.**
- On 11 January 1917, the *Courier* placed **R. L. Holman** at **Fifth and Main St.**

## Interpretation

These are direct primary sources for an undertaking business at the **Fifth-and-Main corner/location from at least June 1915 through January 1917**.

They **do not print the numbered address 501 Main**. Because the 1925 Sanborn later shows 501 as the corner store space, Holman's premises are an important candidate for the predecessor/earlier use of that space, but that address mapping remains unproven.

Confidence: **Very High** for Fifth-and-Main location and dates; **Moderate hypothesis only** for equivalence with later-numbered 501 Main.

Related building: `B-005` 501 Main (candidate relationship only).  
Related research lead: `RL-019`.
