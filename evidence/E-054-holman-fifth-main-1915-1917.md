# E-054 — Holman / Randall Undertaking Business at Fifth & Main, 1914–1917

Type: Primary newspaper local notice and advertisements  
Sources: `E-064`, `S-064`, `S-065`, `S-066`

## Claims Supported

- On **26 February 1914**, the *Oregon City Courier* reported that **R. L. Holman moved his undertaking establishment from the Caufield Building on Eighth and Main Street to his building on Fifth and Main Street** (`E-064`).
- On 17 June 1915, the *Oregon City Courier* advertised **R. L. Holman and T. P. Randall** as leading undertakers at **Fifth and Main St.**
- On 2 March 1916, the *Courier* again placed **Holman and Randall** at **Fifth and Main St.**
- On 11 January 1917, the *Courier* placed **R. L. Holman** at **Fifth and Main St.**

## Interpretation

The evidence now directly establishes the undertaking establishment at **Fifth and Main from February 1914 through January 1917**. The 1914 notice is especially useful because it explicitly describes a move from Eighth & Main to **Holman's own building at Fifth & Main**.

These sources **do not print the numbered address 501 Main**. Because the 1925 Sanborn later shows 501 as the corner store space, Holman's premises are an important candidate for the predecessor/earlier use of that space, but that address mapping remains unproven.

Confidence: **Very High** for Fifth-and-Main location and dates; **Moderate hypothesis only** for equivalence with later-numbered 501 Main.
