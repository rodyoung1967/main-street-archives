# E-053 — 527 Main / Swartz Building, 1935

Type: Official historic-resource survey evidence / secondary source  
Sources: `S-062`, `S-063`  
Related building: `B-004`  
Related source note: `../records/historic-surveys/527-main-swartz-building-1935-source-note.md`

## Claims

1. The **present building at 527 Main Street, Oregon City** is identified as the **Swartz Building** and dated **1935** by the City of Oregon City's 2000 downtown historic-resource report (`S-062`).
2. ODOT's 12 May 2021 cultural-resources report independently repeats the inventory entry **527 Main St — Swartz Building — 1935** (`S-063`, p. 16 Table 2).
3. `S-061` documents **Bailey & Price, Billiard and Pool Parlors, at 527 Main Street on 5 January 1912**. Therefore the 1912 business occupied an earlier structure/storefront at the same numbered address unless later evidence establishes a relevant address renumbering.
4. Because the present building is dated 1935, it predates the **1 September 1967 Main Street fire** (`E-045`) and was not a wholesale post-fire replacement. This does not establish the extent of any lesser fire, smoke, water, or repair damage.
5. The City report also notes a later alteration of the entrance of the Swartz Building in its discussion of changes since the 1983 survey; the modern facade should therefore not be assumed to be the original 1935 appearance.

## Confidence

**High** for the accepted historic-inventory identification and 1935 construction date. Both official sources agree, although the 2021 ODOT table may derive partly from earlier inventory work and should not automatically be treated as a wholly independent original dating study.

**Very High** that the 1912 Bailey & Price address predates the current 1935 building, assuming the documented street number was not subsequently renumbered.

## Follow-up

Primary corroboration remains desirable: 1935 building permit, assessor card, deed, newspaper construction notice, architect/builder records, and any 1967 fire/insurance/repair documentation for 527 Main.
