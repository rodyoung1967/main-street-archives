# E-087 — Sixth Street Grade and Improvement Across Main, 1911

Type: Primary newspaper reporting of municipal public works  
Date: March–October 1911  
Source: `S-100`  
Related evidence: `E-062`, `E-078`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-026`

## Claims Supported

Oregon City undertook another significant **Sixth Street grade/improvement process in 1911**, affecting the street that crosses Main at the north end of the target Fifth–Sixth block.

- **3 March 1911:** the *Courier* reported disagreement over the proposed Sixth Street improvement. J. F. Albright favored changing the grade so there would not be a break in the middle of the block; E. F. Story and F. D. Sturgis opposed the change. The city engineer and street committee favored the change, the engineer's favorable report was accepted, and the Council ordered the necessary steps toward the work.
- **24 March 1911:** additional Council reporting shows active consideration of Sixth Street grade changes, including a petition concerning the block between Madison and Jefferson.
- **21 July 1911:** Council considered property-owner assessments for the **improvement of Sixth Street** and appointed a committee to hear/equalize objections.
- **6 October 1911:** an ordinance was read concerning assessments for the **improvement of Sixth Street from High to Jackson**.

Because the High-to-Jackson corridor crosses Main Street, this establishes direct 1911 public-works/grade activity at the north end of the target block.

## Significance

This should be considered with the 1906 Sixth Street improvement (`E-062`), the 1907 Main Street grade increase (`E-066`), and the 1910 Gambrinus construction (`E-078`) when interpreting photographs, sidewalk levels, entrances, and apparent building elevations near Sixth & Main.

## Limitation

The records do not establish demolition, movement, or replacement of a specific 501/503/505/507 predecessor building. Grade/street work must not be converted into a building-replacement claim without direct structural evidence.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the 1911 Sixth Street grade/improvement process and its crossing of Main Street.  
**Unresolved** for effects on individual storefront structures.
