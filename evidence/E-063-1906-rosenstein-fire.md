# E-063 — Rosenstein’s Clothing House Fire, 1906

Type: Primary newspaper advertisements / fire aftermath evidence  
Date: October–December 1906; fire occurred in November 1906  
Source: `S-077`  
Related research lead: `RL-024`

## Claims Supported

- An October 1906 advertisement places **Rosenstein’s Clothing House** on **Main Street, two doors south of the Post Office**.
- A 14 December 1906 advertisement for the store formerly known as Rosenstein’s Clothing House repeats the same location and states that the Rosenstein stock had been **slightly damaged by fire in November**.
- The December advertisement says the store was being cleared before the New Year and describes the sale as the end of Rosenstein’s Store.

## Significance

This establishes a real **Main Street fire event in November 1906** and a business closure/clear-out immediately afterward. Because the location is expressed relative to the Post Office rather than by number, it may become useful for exact block reconstruction if the 1906 numbering plat or a securely dated Post Office address is recovered.

## Limitation

The recovered sources do **not** give Rosenstein’s numbered address. Do not assign the store or fire to 501, 503, 505, 507, or another exact address based only on later Post Office hypotheses.

The advertisements establish fire damage to stock but do not establish that the building itself was destroyed, demolished, or replaced.

## Confidence

**Very High** for Rosenstein’s relative Main Street location and November 1906 fire damage.  
**Unresolved** for exact address, building damage, or rebuilding.
