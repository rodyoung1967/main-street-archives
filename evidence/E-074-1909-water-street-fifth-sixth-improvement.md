# E-074 — Water Street Improvement, Fifth–Sixth Block, 1909

Type: Primary municipal record and newspaper notice / public-works evidence  
Date: March–October 1909  
Source: `S-088`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-026`

## Claims Supported

Oregon City carried out a substantial improvement of **Water Street from Fifth Street to Sixth Street** in 1909, on the river-side edge of the same Fifth–Sixth block whose Main Street frontage contains the later 501/503/505/507 sequence.

The documented work included:

- grading;
- crushed rock / gravel surfacing;
- wooden sidewalks and crosswalks;
- concrete curbs;
- corner blocks and drains;
- assessment and contracting through the city improvement process.

A 12 March 1909 *Oregon City Courier* municipal notice describes the proposed Fifth-to-Sixth Water Street improvement. Later City Council records show the work was completed: on 27 September 1909 the City Engineer reported contractor **Harry Jones's** work on Water Street from Fifth to Sixth satisfactory and in accordance with the contract, and the Council accepted the work and authorized payment.

The same block received additional infrastructure attention. On 8 September the Council ordered a fire hydrant placed on Water Street either at Sixth Street or at the Water Street end of the alley running through the block between Fifth and Sixth. On 6 October the Council recorded publication of the Water Street assessment notice in the *Courier* on 27 August and 3 September.

## Significance for Block 26

This establishes a major 1909 physical change on the **Water Street / river side of the Fifth–Sixth block**. It should be considered together with the 1907 Main Street track/grade reconstruction and 1908 overhead-wire removal when interpreting the block's physical evolution.

It does **not** by itself prove work on, demolition of, or replacement of the predecessor buildings later numbered 501, 503, 505, or 507 Main. The exact rear-lot relationships remain dependent on the Block 26 lot-to-address reconstruction (`RL-026`).

## Confidence

**Very High** for the Water Street Fifth–Sixth improvement, completion, and same-block public-works context.  
**Unresolved** for direct structural effects on individual Main Street buildings.
