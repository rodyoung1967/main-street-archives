# E-071 — I. Tolpolar Creditor Sale Opposite the Post Office, 1908

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement / business-transition evidence  
Date: 3 July 1908  
Source: `S-085`  
Related evidence: `E-069`

## Claims Supported

A 3 July 1908 *Oregon City Enterprise* advertisement announces a major sale of **I. Tolpolar's** stock **to satisfy creditors** and places the business at **Main St. Opp. P.O.**

The advertised stock includes furniture and household goods, ranges, hardware, crockery, wallpaper, paint, and related merchandise, consistent with Tolpolar's earlier Main Street trade.

## Significance

This extends the strong 1907 relative-location anchor (`E-069`) into 1908 and documents a meaningful business/financial transition. Once the Post Office's exact 1908 number or the 1906 numbering plat is recovered, Tolpolar's opposite-P.O. wording may help map this storefront and nearby businesses.

## Limitation

The advertisement does not print Tolpolar's numbered address. The creditor sale does **not** by itself establish that the business permanently closed, changed owners, or that the building was altered or replaced.

Do not project Tolpolar's later numbered address backward into 1908.

## Confidence

**Very High** for Tolpolar's Main-Street-opposite-Post-Office location and the July 1908 creditor-sale event.  
**Unresolved** for exact number and ultimate disposition of the business after the sale.
