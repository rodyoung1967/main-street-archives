# E-059 — Fifth Street Improvement Across Main Street, 1905

Type: Primary municipal notice published in newspaper  
Date: August–October 1905  
Source: `S-072`  
Related source capture: `source-captures/1904-1905-main-street-research.md`

## Claims Supported

A published Oregon City street-improvement notice in the 25 August 1905 *Oregon City Enterprise* states that **Fifth Street** would be improved on both sides of **Main Street**:

- from the west line of Railroad Avenue to the east line of Main Street; and
- from the west line of Main Street to the east line of Water Street.

The notice describes grading and improvements using crushed rock, sidewalks, concrete curbs, corner blocks, and drains. A later 13 October 1905 notice solicited proposals for the same Fifth Street work.

## Significance

This is primary 1905 evidence for public work at the **Fifth-and-Main intersection**, the southern end of the Main Street frontage relevant to the 501/503/505/507 research. It helps document the physical and municipal context of the block during 1905.

## Limitation

The notices identify streets and improvement limits, **not business occupants or numbered storefronts**. They do not identify the occupant of 503, 505, or 507 Main and should not be used as address evidence for any business.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the 1905 Fifth Street improvement limits across Main Street.  
**None** for business occupancy at 503, 505, or 507 from this source.
