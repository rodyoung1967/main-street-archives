# E-159 — Joseph Wilson Palace Saloon license application at Fifth/Main, 20 June 1911

Source: `S-179`.

The visually verified *Morning Enterprise* notice names Joseph Wilson applying for a three-month liquor license at The Palace Saloon, corner 5th and Main streets. This moves verified Joseph Wilson/Palace evidence back to June 1911. The page does not print 501 or prove grant/title/continuous operation. Read with `E-104` and `E-153`, Joe Wilson = Joseph Wilson remains a strong inference rather than an explicit legal-name statement.
