# E-058 — Huntley Bros. Two-Store Remodel, 1902

Type: Primary newspaper report  
Date: 24 October 1902  
Source: *Oregon City Courier-Herald*, p. 5  
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063697/1902-10-24/ed-1/seq-5.pdf

## Claim supported

The paper reports that Huntley Bros. occupied two adjoining store rooms that were remodeled into essentially one store, with the partition between them removed and a plate-glass front installed.

## Interpretation

This is potentially useful structural evidence when comparing the 1900 Sanborn storefront pattern with later maps. The recovered item does **not** establish that the Huntley premises were on the Main Street frontage between 5th and 6th, so it must not be assigned to a later 5xx Main address without additional evidence.

Confidence: **Very High** for the described remodel; **unresolved** for relevance to the study block.
