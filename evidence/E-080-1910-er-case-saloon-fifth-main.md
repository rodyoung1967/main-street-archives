# E-080 — E. R. Case Saloon / Adjoining Restaurant at Fifth & Main, 1910

Type: Primary municipal record / business and property-use anchor  
Date: 1 June 1910  
Source: `S-093`  
Related evidence: `E-067`  
Related research leads: `RL-024`, `RL-026`

## Claims Supported

At the 1 June 1910 Oregon City Council meeting, the Council considered a complaint involving beer reportedly dispensed on Sunday in a **restaurant adjoining E. R. Case's saloon at Fifth and Main Streets**. The matter was dropped after discussion, with Case warned concerning property leased by him.

This directly establishes **E. R. Case's saloon at Fifth & Main** and an adjoining restaurant in June 1910.

## Significance

The record provides a contemporary south-end anchor for the Fifth–Sixth Main Street block. It also shows more than one commercial use at or immediately adjoining the Fifth/Main corner context.

## Limitation

The Council record does not print a numbered Main Street address. The saloon or adjoining restaurant must **not** be assigned to 501, 503, or another later number without independent address/lot evidence.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the E. R. Case saloon at Fifth & Main and the adjoining-restaurant relationship in June 1910.  
**Unresolved** for exact number, lot, and footprint.
