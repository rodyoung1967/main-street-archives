# E-065 — Pacific States Telephone Central Office Between Fifth & Sixth, 1907

Type: Primary newspaper / construction evidence  
Date: 31 March–1 April 1907  
Source: `S-079`  
Related research lead: `RL-024`

## Claims Supported

A contemporaneous *Morning Oregonian* report states that the Pacific States Telephone & Telegraph Company had nearly completed a new Oregon City central-office plant:

- the new quarters were on the **east side of Main Street between Fifth and Sixth Streets**;
- work on the improvement had begun more than a year earlier;
- the company reported spending about **$30,000** improving the local plant;
- carpenters were finishing the quarters and the office was expected to move into the new plant during the first week of April 1907;
- the plant included modern local and long-distance switchboards, steam heat, manager/cashier space, and telephone booths.

## Significance for the Building-Change Watch

This is direct evidence of a substantial **new or newly completed commercial/utility facility within the Fifth-to-Sixth Main Street block** during the 1906–1907 transition period.

The source explicitly places it on the **east side of Main**. It therefore must not be treated as evidence that a predecessor building at later odd-numbered 501, 503, 505, or 507 Main was replaced. Recovering the 1906 numbering plat and exact telephone-office address may nevertheless help establish the address sequence and frontage mapping on both sides of Main.

## Limitation

The recovered source does not print a street number and does not identify the underlying lot. No claim is made that this building corresponds to any modern numbered address.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the east-side Main / Fifth-to-Sixth location and 1907 completion status.  
**Unresolved** for exact numbered address, lot, and later building identity.
