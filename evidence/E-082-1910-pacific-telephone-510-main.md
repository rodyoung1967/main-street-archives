# E-082 — Pacific Telephone & Telegraph at 510 Main, 1910

Type: Primary newspaper advertisements / exact-address evidence  
Date: August–December 1910  
Source: `S-095`  
Related evidence: `E-065`, `E-073`  
Related research lead: `RL-027`

## Claims Supported

Repeated *Oregon City Courier* advertisements in the second half of 1910 identify:

**Pacific Telephone & Telegraph Co. — 510 Main St., Oregon City, Ore.**

The exact 510 Main address appears repeatedly from August through December 1910.

## Significance

`E-065` establishes that Pacific States Telephone & Telegraph completed a new central office in 1907 on the **east side of Main between Fifth and Sixth**, and `E-073` documents major Oregon City system expansion in 1908. The 1910 advertisements now establish an exact numbered office address within that same east-side block context: **510 Main**.

This substantially advances `RL-027` and independently confirms that even-numbered Main addresses were in active commercial use in the Fifth–Sixth area by 1910.

## Limitation

Although the geography strongly supports continuity, the recovered 1910 advertisements do not explicitly state that the 510 Main office was the identical physical central-office plant completed in 1907. Until a directory, deed/lease, company record, or explicit continuity statement is recovered, 510 Main should not be mechanically backdated to 1907.

## Confidence

**Very High** for Pacific Telephone & Telegraph at 510 Main in August–December 1910.  
**High** that this relates to the 1907 east-side central-office plant; exact 1907–1910 premises continuity remains to be directly documented.
