# E-059 — Union Store on Main Between 5th and 6th, 1902

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement  
Date: 7 November 1902  
Source: *Oregon City Courier*, p. 1  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1902-11-07/ed-1/seq-1/  
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1902-11-07/ed-1/seq-1.pdf

## Claim supported

An advertisement for **The Union Store**, with **Michael** named as proprietor, gives its location as **"Main St., between 5th and 6th Sts."** The advertisement announces a liquidation/closing sale of stock and fixtures and says the stock must be gone by New Year.

## Interpretation

This is direct evidence for a named business on the exact Main Street block now being reconstructed. It does **not** identify which later 501–527 numbered storefront the Union Store occupied.

The 2 January 1903 Courier reports that **M. Michael** would shortly leave for Baker City, which is consistent with the late-1902 liquidation; this continuity clue does not independently prove a numbered address.

Confidence: **Very High** for business name, proprietor, block location and closing-sale context; **unresolved** for exact 5xx Main number.
