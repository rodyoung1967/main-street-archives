# E-070 — Main Street Overhead Wire and Pole Removal, 1908

Type: Primary newspaper / utility and streetscape evidence  
Date: January–July 1908  
Source: `S-084`  
Related evidence: `E-065`, `E-066`  
Related research lead: `RL-024`

## Claims Supported

Oregon City continued the physical modernization of Main Street in 1908 by requiring removal of overhead utility infrastructure:

- **10 January 1908:** the *Morning Oregonian* reported that Mayor Carll and the City Council ordered all telegraph, telephone, and electric-light poles on **Main Street between Eleventh Street and the Basin** removed before March. Companies were to place wires underground or bring them into Main from side streets.
- **14 February 1908:** the *Oregon City Enterprise* reported that Pacific Telephone & Telegraph and Portland General Electric were arranging to remove their cables and wires from Main Street.
- **31 July 1908:** an *Enterprise* report on Pacific Telephone improvements states that **all wires would be moved from Main Street**.

## Significance for Fifth–Sixth Main

The stated Eleventh-to-Basin corridor necessarily includes the target block between Fifth and Sixth. Combined with the 1907 track/paving reconstruction and approximately six-inch grade increase (`E-066`), this documents a substantial two-year transformation of the public realm immediately in front of the target storefronts.

This may affect interpretation of historic photographs: poles, overhead wire networks, facade attachments, sidewalk conditions, and sight lines can differ sharply between pre-1908 and post-1908 views without implying that the commercial buildings themselves were replaced.

## Limitation

No recovered source states that the utility-removal work required demolition, moving, or replacement of the predecessor buildings later numbered 501, 503, 505, or 507 Main.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the 1908 order and implementation to remove Main Street overhead utility infrastructure.  
**Unresolved** for any structural effect on individual target buildings.
