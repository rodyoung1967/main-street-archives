# E-069 — I. Tolpolar Opposite the Post Office, 1907

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement / relative-location evidence  
Date: 30 August 1907  
Source: `S-083`

## Claims Supported

An *Oregon City Enterprise* advertisement identifies:

**I. TOLPOLAR — Main St. Opp. Postoffice — Oregon City, Oregon.**

This materially improves the location evidence for Tolpolar beyond the 1904–1905 advertisements that gave only “Main Street.”

## Significance

The advertisement creates a strong 1907 **relative-location anchor** between Tolpolar's store and the Oregon City Post Office. Once the 1906 numbering plat or a securely dated Post Office street number is recovered, this relationship may help map Tolpolar and neighboring businesses within the Main Street address sequence.

## Limitation

The advertisement does not print Tolpolar's numbered address. Do not project a later Tolpolar address backward into 1907 and do not assign this store to 501, 503, 505, or 507 based only on the “opposite Postoffice” wording.

## Confidence

**Very High** for Tolpolar operating on Main Street opposite the Post Office in August 1907.  
**Unresolved** for exact numbered address.
