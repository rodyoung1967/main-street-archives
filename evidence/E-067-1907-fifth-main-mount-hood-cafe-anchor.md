# E-067 — Mount Hood Cafe / Fifth & Main Anchor, 1907

Type: Primary newspaper / geographic anchor  
Date: 4 January 1907  
Source: `S-081`

## Claims Supported

A 4 January 1907 *Oregon City Enterprise* report describing a runaway horse places the **Mount Hood Cafe at or immediately associated with the Fifth-and-Main intersection**. The report says that at the corner of Fifth Street, opposite the Mount Hood Cafe, the horse attempted to turn up Fifth toward the railroad.

## Significance

This provides a useful contemporary **south-end geographic anchor** for reconstructing the Fifth-to-Sixth Main Street block shortly after Oregon City's 1906 numbering program.

## Limitation

The report does not print the cafe's numbered street address. Later sources may provide a numbered address, but that number must not be projected backward into January 1907 without independent evidence that the premises and numbering were unchanged.

The source therefore does not establish an occupant for 501, 503, 505, or 507 Main.

## Confidence

**Very High** for the Mount Hood Cafe's relationship to Fifth & Main in January 1907.  
**Unresolved** for exact numbered address and exact storefront footprint.
