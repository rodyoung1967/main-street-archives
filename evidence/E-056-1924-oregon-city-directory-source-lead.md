# E-056 — 1924 Oregon City Directory Existence / Repository Lead

Type: Official National Register documentation citing a historical directory  
Source: `S-069`

A 1992 National Register of Historic Places nomination for the DeWitt Clinton Latourette House includes **R. L. Polk and Company, Oregon City Directory, 1924** in its bibliography. The same form identifies the **Oregon City Planning Department** as the primary local repository for additional data used in the nomination.

## What This Proves

- The nomination preparers cited a source as **Oregon City Directory, 1924**. The actual edition/title-page identity has not been independently confirmed.
- Oregon City Planning Department is a strong repository lead for locating that directory or related research files.

## What This Does Not Prove

The nomination does **not reproduce the directory's 501/503/505 Main entries**. Until the directory itself is obtained, it is not evidence for a particular occupant at any of those addresses.

Confidence: **Very High** for the bibliography citation and supporting-file repository lead; **Unresolved** for exact edition identity/current holdings; **None yet** for 501/503/505 occupants from that directory.
