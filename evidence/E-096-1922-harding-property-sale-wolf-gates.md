# E-096 — Harding Main Street Property Sale to S. Wolf and C. Gates, July 1922

## Evidence type

Primary newspaper property-transfer and storefront-allocation evidence; direct visual inspection.

## Source

- `S-111` — *Banner-Courier*, 13 July 1922, page 1, “Property Changes Hands.”
- Repository PDF: `newspapers/banner-courier/1922-07-13-page-1-harding-wolf-gates-property-sale.pdf`
- Fire cross-reference: `S-117` / `E-103`.

Detailed audit note: `evidence/source-captures/1922-harding-wolf-gates-sale-and-rebuild.md`

## Visually verified facts

1. The article describes a Main Street property group as belonging to George A. Harding.
2. It identifies stores occupied by the Fifth Street Restaurant, Miller's shoe store, an adjoining barber shop, and the Red Front Clothing Store within that group.
3. It reports the property sold to S. Wolf and C. Gates, both of Portland.
4. Its `latter` / `former` wording assigns the Fifth Street Restaurant location to C. Gates and the remainder to S. Wolf.
5. Publication was five days before the 18 July Fifth/Main fire.

## Strong cross-source inference

The visually verified 20 July fire report names S. Wolf of Portland as owner of the building where the fire began. The tightly spaced dates, same Main/Fifth business cluster, same S. Wolf residence wording, and the named Miller/restaurant stores make the freshly reported Harding-to-Wolf remainder the strongest current property chain for the ignition building.

## Limits

- The source is a contemporary newspaper sale report, not the recorded deed.
- It gives no legal description, block/lot, dimensions, consideration, instrument number, or exact numbered storefront.
- It does not prove that the sale had been recorded or define the effective date under title law.
- It does not map the Gates/Wolf allocation to modern 501, 503, or 505.
- It does not identify S. Wolf by full name or connect him to other Wolf people/businesses.
