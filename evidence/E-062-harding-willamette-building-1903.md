# E-062 — George A. Harding in the Willamette Building, 1903

Type: Primary newspaper advertisement  
Date: 6 April 1903  
Source: *Clackamas County Record*, p. 8  
Canonical page: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063710/1903-04-06/ed-1/seq-8/
Canonical PDF: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063710/1903-04-06/ed-1/seq-8.pdf

## Claim supported

The advertisement reads **"Geo. A. Harding — Willamette Building — The Leading Drug Store of Oregon City."**

## Interpretation

This establishes Harding's drug business in the **Willamette Building** in April 1903. Combined with `E-060`, it also places the Portland Clothing House next door to a Willamette-Building storefront.

The page provides several other useful Oregon City location anchors, including Shank & Bissell on Main Street opposite Huntley's, but those are contextual and should not be assigned to the 5th-to-6th study block without further mapping evidence.

Later evidence places Harding at 511 Main. Do **not** back-project 511 to 1903 unless a separate source establishes the numbering/location continuity.

Confidence: **Very High** for Harding in the Willamette Building.
