## Claims Supported

Primary advertisements in the *Oregon City Enterprise* place **I. Tolpolar** on **Main Street, Oregon City** in late 1904. The advertisements offer new and second-hand furniture and household goods, including furniture, wall paper, carpets, crockery, hardware, glassware, and graniteware.

Verified examples include:

- 21 October 1904, page 1 — I. Tolpolar, Main Street.
- 4 November 1904, page 1 — I. Tolpolar, Main Street, Oregon City.
- 30 December 1904, page 1 — I. Tolpolar, Main Street, Oregon City, Oregon.

## Limitation

These advertisements **do not print a numbered Main Street address**. They therefore do **not** prove that Tolpolar occupied modern 503, 505, or 507 Main in 1904. The 1900 Sanborn confirms a developed commercial frontage in this block, but the archive has not yet mapped the older 1900 storefront sequence securely onto the later 501/503/505/507 numbering.

Later Tolpolar evidence must not be projected backward to 1904 without a direct source. In particular, later documentation at another numbered address does not establish his 1904 location.

## Confidence

**Very High** that I. Tolpolar operated a Main Street furniture/household-goods business in Oregon City during late 1904.  
**Unresolved** for the exact numbered address and for any connection to modern 503, 505, or 507 Main.
