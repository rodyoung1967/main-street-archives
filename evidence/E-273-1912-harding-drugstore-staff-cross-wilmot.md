# E-273 — Harding Drug Store staff item: Percy Cross and A. B. Wilmot, 1912

Type: Primary newspaper scan / business-staff evidence  
Date: **19 January 1912**  
Source: `S-299`  
Related business: `BUS-007`  
Related people: `P-399`, `P-400`

## Claims supported

A visually inspected page 8 of the 19 January 1912 *Oregon City Courier* directly reports that **Percy Cross** had accepted a position in the **Harding drugstore** during the absence of **A. B. Wilmot**.

This establishes active Harding Drug Store staffing/operation on that date and directly associates Cross and Wilmot with the business.

## Address / role limitation

The item does not print a street number and does not define either man's formal job title, professional license, ownership status, employment duration, or Wilmot's reason for absence.

Separate 5 January 1912 evidence places **Geo. A. Harding at 511 Main** (`E-092`). The same-month relationship is useful chronology, but the 19 January notice is not itself an exact-address source and does not independently prove that Cross or Wilmot worked at 511.

## Confidence

**Very High / DOCUMENTED-DIRECT / VISUALLY VERIFIED** for the published staffing statement and business association.  
**Unresolved** for exact job titles, duration, ownership/tenancy, and the address on this particular page.
