## Post Office cluster

Contemporary advertisements use the Oregon City Post Office as a landmark:

- **The Chicago Store**, independently documented at **505 Main**, says **"Main St. just below Postoffice"** on 5 January 1912. This is now one of the strongest same-date anchors for reconstructing the Post Office location, but "just below" must not be converted into an exact numbered address without corroboration.
- **I. Tolpolar** operated a furniture/hardware/household-goods business on Main Street and advertised as **opposite the Post Office**. Later evidence places Tolpolar at **514 Main**, making the post office's location on the opposite, odd-numbered side of Main a strong geographic anchor.
- **M. E. Dunn** operated a confectionery selling cigars, candies, Wilhoit water, and soft drinks. Contemporary wording places Dunn **near** and, in *Morning Enterprise* advertising, **next door to the P.O.** Exact street number remains unproven.
- **Schrader's Bakery** was on Main Street and was described in the 5 Jan 1912 *Courier* business material as **near the Post Office**. The exact Main Street number remains unproven. Schrader also had a separate bakery at Seventh and John Adams by 1912; the two locations must not be conflated.
- **Pioneer Transfer Co.** advertised an office **in the Postoffice Building**, further establishing the Post Office Building as a commercial landmark.

## Current address hypothesis — NOT established fact

Earlier reconstruction made **513 Main** a strong candidate for the Post Office because Tolpolar was opposite it at 514. The newly documented **505 Main Chicago Store = "just below Postoffice"** statement adds an important constraint and means the hypothesis should remain open rather than harden into a conclusion. The meaning and distance implied by "just below" in 1912 Oregon City usage need corroboration.

If 513 ultimately proves to be the Post Office, Dunn's "next door" location would likely be 511 or 515; because Harding is independently associated with 511, **515 becomes a plausible Dunn candidate**. This remains inference and must not be promoted to fact until direct address evidence is found.

Do **not** assign Schrader's Bakery to 503 or 505. Current evidence only supports Main Street / near-Post-Office location.
