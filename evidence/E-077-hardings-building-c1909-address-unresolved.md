# E-077 — Harding Building Existed c.1909; Exact Address Unresolved

Type: Primary newspaper retrospective and later location evidence / chronology correction  
Date: c.1909 event recalled in 1918; 1911 direct location context  
Source: `S-091`  
Related research lead: `RL-029`

## Claims Supported

Two later primary newspaper references support a narrower Harding Building chronology than the archive previously carried:

- A 26 September 1918 *Oregon City Courier* article states that **nine years earlier** the local Christian Science church had been holding services in an office room in the **Harding building**. This retrospectively places a Harding Building in use around **1909**.
- A 10 March 1911 *Courier* item says that George Young had formerly occupied **the Harding building on Main street between Fifth and Sixth streets** before moving his second-hand store one door north into the Willamette building.

Together, these sources support the existence of the Harding Building by approximately 1909 and place it within the Fifth–Sixth Main Street context by 1911.

## Archive Correction

The former master-timeline statement **“By 1909, 507 Main was known as the Harding Building” is not supported by the recovered evidence and must not be retained as an exact-address claim**.

The current evidence does **not** establish that the Harding Building equaled 507 Main in 1909. Later address listings for George A. Harding and later building references may help reconstruct the footprint, but they must not be projected backward without direct mapping evidence.

## Significance

This correction is important because the Harding name may refer to a larger corner/block building or premises whose relationship to later numbered storefronts changed. Treating it prematurely as 507 would distort the Block 26 lot-to-address reconstruction.

## Confidence

**High** that a Harding Building existed around 1909 based on the 1918 retrospective; **Very High** that a Harding Building was on Main between Fifth and Sixth by March 1911.  
**Unresolved** for its exact 1909 number, lot, footprint, and relationship to later 507/509/511 addresses.
