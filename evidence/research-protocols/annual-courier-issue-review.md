# Annual Oregon City Courier Issue-by-Issue Review Protocol

Adopted: 21 August 2026  
Scope: year-by-year reconstruction of 501, 503, 505, and 507 Main Street, Oregon City, with the Fifth-to-Sixth Main Street block used as the core search area.

## Purpose

Keyword/OCR search alone is not sufficient for the Main Street reconstruction. Historical newspaper OCR can miss small advertisements, decorative type, broken columns, street numbers, business names, and short local notices. Beginning with the 1909 research pass, each year should therefore include an **issue-by-issue review of the Oregon City Courier in addition to targeted OCR/keyword searches**.

## Required annual review

For every available issue of the *Oregon City Courier* in the research year:

1. Record the issue date and number of available pages/images.
2. Review the issue page-by-page, with special attention to:
   - local/business pages and display advertising;
   - classifieds and legal/municipal notices;
   - local-personal columns that mention business moves or property work;
   - fires, demolitions, removals, moving buildings, excavations, permits, new construction, additions, remodeling, and rebuilding;
   - street grading, paving, sidewalks, curbs, utility work, railroad/streetcar work, and changes to street elevation;
   - Fifth & Main, Sixth & Main, and wording such as "between Fifth and Sixth," "opposite Post Office," "near Post Office," and named neighboring businesses;
   - exact or damaged OCR forms of 501, 503, 505, 507 and adjacent Main Street numbers.
3. Use OCR text as a finding aid, but do not treat a failed OCR search as a completed page review.
4. When a page image/layout is available and text appears incomplete, visually inspect the page or relevant advertisement/column rather than relying on OCR alone.
5. Cross-check findings against the *Oregon City Enterprise*, *Oregonian*, directories, Sanborn maps, municipal records, and existing archive evidence where useful.

## Annual completion standard

A year may be marked research-complete with unresolved occupants or construction questions only when:

- targeted address/name/construction searches have been performed;
- the available *Courier* issues have been reviewed issue-by-issue/page-by-page to the extent the archive exposes them;
- relevant findings are registered as `E-###` / `S-###` records;
- unresolved items and inaccessible/missing pages are explicitly logged;
- construction/demolition/fire/street-change findings and negative results are preserved separately from occupant/address claims.

## Negative-search handling

"Not found" means only that no relevant item was recovered in the reviewed material. It must **not** be converted into a claim that a storefront was vacant, a building did not exist, or no construction/demolition occurred.

For each year, the source-capture note should state the degree of Courier coverage actually completed (for example, all available issues/pages reviewed, or specific missing/inaccessible issues/pages).

## Standing construction watch

During every issue review, keep a specific watch for physical changes on Main Street between Fifth and Sixth that could help identify when the commercial footprints shown on the 1900 Sanborn were replaced or altered before the later 501/503/505 pattern. This supplements `RL-024` and should be treated as part of the normal annual workflow, not as an optional follow-up.
