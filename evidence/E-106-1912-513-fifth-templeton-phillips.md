# E-106 — 513 Fifth Street Residence, 1912

## Status

**VISUALLY VERIFIED primary newspaper address evidence.**

## Claim

A 19 March 1912 local item says Mr. and Mrs. **H. M. Templeton** planned to return to their home at **513 Fifth Street**, then occupied by **M. D. Phillips and family**.

## Limit

This is an exact residential address, but it does not establish its modern parcel, its place in Fifth Street's historic numbering geometry, or any relationship to the Main/Water commercial/fire cluster.
