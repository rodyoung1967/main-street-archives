WORK OF TOURIST BODY
. Automobile clubs all over the United
States and Canada are showing their
interst in the Pacific Northwest by
their willingness to display the new
wall map of this region which is being
distributed by the Pacific Northwest
Tourist Association.
'In fifteen years experience in tour
ist matters," state Herbert Cuthbert,
executive secretary of the Associa
tion, "I have not known of any piece
of advertising that has been so univer
sally commended and sought after as
this. Upon return to the office after
five weeks in California, I found hun
dreds of letters acknowledging the re
ceipt of the map and asking for addi
tional copies.
"A typical one from Lloyd C. Harris,
secretary of the automobile club of
Holyoke, Mass., says:
" 'It will certainly be a valuable ad
dition to our display of wall maps
and by far one of the most interesting
and attractive. This is just the map
we have been looking for for some
time as it takes in all the territory
in which our members are intersted
who are planning summer tours. Per
sonally I believe that the views on the
border are as much a feature as is
the map itself and there is no doubt
but they add interest and beauty to
it
" I am particularly interested in the
map as I was born and brought up in
(Spokane, so that region naturally
means much to me. If it were possible
we could very conveniently use two
more of these maps.

ilar vein to that of Paul L. Powers of
