LIST PREDICTED AT 80
NEW YORK, June 13. The death
list resulting from the cyclonic storm
that smashed and swirled its way
through New York and its 'suburbs
Sunday evening continues to .grow.
The known dead now total forty-five,
with sixteen other persons reported
misting.

find at least a score more victims,
enough to push the death total past
eighty. Witnesses of the swift trag
edy placed the number even higher.
