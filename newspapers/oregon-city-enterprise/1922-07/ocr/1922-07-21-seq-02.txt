De Forest Pierce -Dies
In California
JENNINGS ' LODGE, July 19
Mrs. Mable Pierce has returned from
Redlands, Calif., where she was call
ed to the funeral of her son, De For
rest Pierce, a young attorney of St.
Bernardino, who passed away very
suddenly with an attack of his"fceart
on June 29.
The deceased had made his home
with his grandmother, Mrs. Mary
Pierce and had lived in Redlands 8
years, graduating from the high
school there in 1919. He had been
the winner of the Citrus Belt debates
for several years. He took up the
study of law and was associated with
Homer Nelson. In January, 1922, he
was admiitted to the bar and since
that time his rise in legal circles has
been rapid.
He was married to Miss Georgia
Williams June 4, 1921 and as death
came without warning was a great
shock to her as well as to his parents,
Mr. and Mrs. Albert Pierce and his
twin sister. Mrs. Hugh B. Fleming and
Teddy Pierce of this place.
The funeral was held on July 1, and
the burial was at Hillsdale cemetery
with six of his high school class
mates acting as pallbearers.
