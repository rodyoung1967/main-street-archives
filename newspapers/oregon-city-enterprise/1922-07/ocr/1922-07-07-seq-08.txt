MOONSHINE IS FATAL TO
INDIAN; 3 ARE ARRESTED
McMINNVILLE, Ore., June 30
James Langley, aged about 40; Leo
nard Armstrong, 22, and Sammy
Countryman, 17, living near Grand
Ronde, this county, were arrested last
evening by Sheriff F. B. Ferguson on
the charge bf selling moonshine whis
key to Andrew Hoffer, an indian, liv
ing near Grand Ronde and whose
death occurred Wednesday evening
following an overdose of the liquor.
The sheriff has a sample of the whis
ky and will have it analyzed.
Countryman confessed and gave a
full account of the circumstances sur
rounding the sale of the liquor to the
Indian. He said the whiskey was
made by Langley and sold to the
youthful bootleggers, who sold it to
Hoffer.
SAN FRANCISCO, Cal., June 23.
The state railroad commission an
nounced today that as a result of the
United States supreme court decision
separating the Southern Pacific and
Central Pacific systems, it will peti
tion the interstate commerce commis
sion for "such grouping and such con
solidation of railroads as best will
serve the ever growing transportation
needs of the entire state of -California."
The interstate body wil be ask
ed to take up the situation "as soon
as possible," the statement said.
According to the statement, it will
be legally impossible for the state
commission to take any action before
the supreme court because the law
does not provide for such appearance.
The statement was issued, the com
mission said, because of many in
quiries as to what its course would be
in the unmerger emergency.
