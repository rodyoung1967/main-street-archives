EX-CHAMPION IS SHOT
SEATTLE, Wash., March, 10.
Johnny G'Leary, former Canadian
champion lightweight boxer, was shot
and instantly killed here this morning
by E. Frank Leslie, a ship worker,
who later surrendered to the police.
