## Visible advertisements and address evidence

### The Chicago Store

Visible text:

> The CHICAGO STORE  
> Gents furnishings and clothing  
> All kinds of tailoring  
> Cleaning and pressing  
> Main St. just below Postoffice

The advertisement does **not** print a street number in the visible crop. Independent primary advertisements already catalogued as `E-026` place The Chicago Store at **505 Main Street** in 1911–1913. Therefore this page provides an important geographic statement: **505 Main was described on 5 January 1912 as "just below Postoffice."**

## Significance for the Post Office reconstruction

The Chicago Store evidence is especially important because its numbered address is independently established as **505 Main** (`E-026`), while this same-date advertisement says it was **"just below Postoffice."** This is stronger evidence than treating 513 Main as the default Post Office candidate solely from businesses described as opposite/near/next to the Post Office.

The phrase "just below" must be interpreted in the context of Oregon City's Main Street numbering and local directional usage before assigning the Post Office a specific number. It does **not**, by itself, prove 507, 509, 511, 513, or another address.
