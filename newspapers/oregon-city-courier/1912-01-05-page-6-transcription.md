# Oregon City Courier — 5 January 1912 — Page 6

## Provenance

Primary newspaper page: *Oregon City Courier*, Oregon City, Oregon, 5 January 1912, page 6. LCCN `sn00063698`.

Canonical online page target: `https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1912-01-05/ed-1/seq-6/`

Repository PDF: `1912-01-05-page-6-main-street-ads.pdf`

The numbered-address readings below are based on direct visual inspection of the page/captured primary image. This is important because machine OCR misreads several digits, including **509** as 609 and parts of **511**.

## Main Street address evidence

### 503 Main — H. N. Smith

The advertisement identifies **H. N. Smith** at **503 Main Street** in a pool-room / cigars-and-tobacco context.

A separate March 1912 pool-hall-license notice at 503 is recorded as **H. H. Smith** (`S-024`, `E-023`). Preserve the H. N. / H. H. discrepancy as unresolved rather than silently standardizing the initials.

### 505 Main — The Chicago Store

The page advertisement describes The Chicago Store as **"Main St. just below Postoffice."** It does not print a number in this ad, but independent primary advertisements (`E-026`) place the business at **505 Main Street** in 1911-1913.

### 507 Main — G. H. Young

The advertisement identifies **G. H. Young, "The Curio Man,"** at **507 Main Street**, dealing in second-hand goods.

### 509 Main — H. P. Brightbill

The advertisement identifies **H. P. Brightbill** at **509 Main St.**, phone 74, Oregon City. This direct visual reading supersedes OCR-derived **609** readings (`S-059`, `E-050`).

### 511 Main — George A. Harding

The advertisement identifies **George A. Harding**, druggist, at **511 Main St.**

### 527 Main — Bailey & Price

The advertisement identifies **Bailey & Price, Billiard and Pool Parlors**, at **527 Main Street**, described as opposite the Grand Theater.

## Post Office cluster evidence on the same page

### M. E. Dunn

M. E. Dunn's confectionery advertisement places the business **near Postoffice**. No numbered address is printed here. Other 1912 *Morning Enterprise* advertisements describe Dunn as **next door to the P.O.**

### Schrader's Bakery

Schrader's Bakery is described as **near Postoffice**. No numbered address is printed. A 20 December 1912 *Courier* report later states that Herman Schrader sold his Main Street bakery and his Seventh/John Adams bakery to **Wemmo & Haas**.

### Pioneer Transfer Co.

Pioneer Transfer Co. identifies its office as being **in Postoffice Building**. No numbered address is printed.

## Other visible Main Street businesses

### Clems Chop House

The advertisement places **Clems Chop House** at **5th and Main Street** but supplies no numbered address.

### Wm. Gardner

The advertisement places **Wm. Gardner, Watchmaker and Jeweler**, on **Main near 8th**. This is relative-location evidence only.

## Interpretation rule

The page directly supports **503, 507, 509, 511 and 527 Main**. Independent evidence supplies **505 Main** for The Chicago Store.

It does **not** establish a numbered address for the Post Office, M. E. Dunn, Schrader's Bakery, Pioneer Transfer, Clems Chop House, or Wm. Gardner. Relative descriptions such as "near," "next door," "opposite," and "just below" must remain relative-location evidence unless corroborated by a numbered-address source.
