SALMON SEASON
ON IN EARNEST
Many Big Ones Registered! for Salmon
Club Prize
Salmon are running again and the
lure of the rod and line has called
many fishermen to the haunts ofthe
big gamey fish. Those who know the
game are not having much difficulty
in bringing in the limit, three in one
day, and even the neophite is having
fair luck.
Dewey Krueger of Farr Bros, made
the record catch of the season so far
last Sunday in the Clackamas river
when he landed a salmon weighing ex
actly 48 pounds, on light tackle such
as has been specified by the Oregon
City Salmon Club. This fish will be
entered in the official contest staged
this year by the club. Several large
fish have been caught, L. A. King of
the Oregon City High School faculty
has registered three button fish, Ir
ving Rau one and Mark Sturgis one.
Twenty one prizes, totaling over
one hundred and fifty dollars worth
of merchandise have been offered by
local and Portland merchants for tie
twenty-one best fish caught on light
tackle by members of the club this
year. In addition to these prizes
Dollar Day Sale!

of the Salmon Club of Oregon has
offered a silver cup to be awarded the
fisherman catching the largest num
ber of button fish, two years in suc
cession on light tackle. The cup re
mains in the possession of the ungler
during the year of the catch. The
prizes are on exhibition at Burmeister
& Andresens.
