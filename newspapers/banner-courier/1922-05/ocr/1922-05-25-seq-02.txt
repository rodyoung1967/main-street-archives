appearance and took the ladies com

splendid orchestra composed of six
pieces with them, which furnished
some excellent music for dancing.
Several old fashioned quadrilles were
danced. Ice cream and cake were
served to more than 100 guests. Fred
Johnson was master of ceremonies.
