# H. N. / H. H. Smith Pool Room and Pool Hall

Business ID: `BUS-019`. Address: 503 Main Street (`B-001`). Related person record: `P-023`.

- A visually reviewed 5 January 1912 advertisement identifies **H. N. Smith**, Pool Room, Cigars and Tobacco, at **503 Main** (`E-089`, `E-092`).
- A 24 March 1912 legal notice is signed **H. H. Smith** and applies for a pool-hall license at **503 Main** (`E-023` / `S-024`).
- A July report says H. H. Smith was leaving after conducting a pool hall at Fifth/Main (`E-089`).

One operator or a corrected-initial reading is plausible, but the H. N./H. H. conflict remains unresolved. The application does not prove grant, and exact-address use does not prove continuity of the surviving later building.
