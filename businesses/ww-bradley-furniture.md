# W. W. Bradley Furniture

Business ID: `BUS-026`. Related person/address: `P-041`, `B-003`.

Dated Oregon City *Courier* directory advertisements place **W. W. Bradley, furniture, at 507 Main Street** from August through November 1915 and again in selected 1916 issues (`E-107`, `E-109`, `E-116`, `E-119`). These are repeated exact-address observations.

The records do not establish Bradley's given names, property ownership, uninterrupted occupancy between sampled dates, or a transfer from G. H. Young's earlier business at 507. The later consolidated Harding Building footprint must not be backdated to this storefront without separate evidence.

Sources: `S-120`, `S-121`, `S-130`, `S-133`.

## CCHS MOT:P-502 conflict with the 1916 507 Main advertisements

CCHS MOT:P-502 (`S-209` / `E-189` / `IMG-0738`) catalogs a G. H. Young Store photograph as **507 Main Street, 1916**. This conflicts with the selected 1916 newspaper advertisements that list **W. W. Bradley at 507 Main** (`E-116`, `E-119`). Preserve both; the record does not yet establish whether the catalog date is approximate, whether a mid-year transition occurred, or whether another explanation applies.
