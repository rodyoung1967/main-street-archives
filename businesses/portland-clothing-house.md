# Portland Clothing House

Business ID: `BUS-082`.

A 2 January 1903 *Oregon City Courier* advertisement, preserved as `S-164` / `E-147`, places **Portland Clothing House next door to Harding's Drug Store**. The source does not print a number.

This is a storefront-adjacency anchor only. It must not be assigned to 505, 507, 509, 511, or another later number without an independent pre-/post-numbering bridge.
