# Mass's Hotel and Restaurant

Business **BUS-030**; operator **E. T. Mass (P-045)**. Evidence **E-109**, source **S-121**, timeline **T-019**.

The [October 28, 1915 Courier p5](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1915-10-28/ed-1/seq-5/) announces a Saturday opening at eleven, names Mass's Hotel and Restaurant and explicitly identifies renovated/rebuilt former Brunswick quarters. The [November 4 p5 report](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1915-11-04/ed-1/seq-5/) confirms that more than200 people attended the opening lastSaturday and that permanent lodgers had moved into the refurnished/decorated hotel. **October30** is calendar-derived, not an independently printed date.

Both pages were visually inspected. The September 23 Main-near-Seventh location is a separate S-120 contextual anchor; no exact number is printed in these October/November reports. Renovation wording does not establish demolition/replacement, a final repair cost or real-estate title. The business succession in the same quarters is explicit; the legal interest and parcel remain open under RL-043.

November 18 pp 1/5 provide subsequent dining/meeting references. [Batch page log and originals](../evidence/source-captures/1915-courier-sheets-101-110.md).
