# Farr's Pool Hall

Business ID: `BUS-013`. Address: 505 Main Street (`B-002`). Proprietors: Edwin F. Farr (`P-028`) and Alice Farr (`P-029`).

Oregon City's official inventory for the Farr residence identifies Edwin and Alice as proprietors of **Farr's Pool Hall at 505 Main** (`E-033` / `S-036`). Their 1934 residence-acquisition date is not the pool hall opening date. Harr's Recreation Center is independently documented later; the businesses remain separate and their transition date is unresolved.
