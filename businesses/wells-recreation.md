# “Well's Recreation”

Business ID: `BUS-015`. Reported address: 505 Main Street (`B-002`).

Oregon City's official synthesis reports a **1953 directory** entry for **“Well's Recreation”** at 505 (`E-032` / `S-033`). The original page is unrecovered, so the City's spelling is preserved rather than normalized. Operator, dates, and relationship to Harr's or 505 Tavern remain open.
