# The Union Store

Business ID: `BUS-081`.

A 7 November 1902 *Oregon City Courier* advertisement, preserved from the legacy branch as `S-163` / `E-146`, places **The Union Store** on **Main Street between Fifth and Sixth Streets** and identifies the proprietor only as **Michael**. The advertisement announces liquidation/closing and says stock and fixtures are to be sold by New Year.

The exact later 5xx storefront, legal ownership, opening date, proprietor's full identity, and whether the liquidation completed exactly as advertised remain unresolved. Do not infer vacancy after the advertisement without a later source.
