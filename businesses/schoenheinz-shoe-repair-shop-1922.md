# Schoenheinz Shoe Repair Shop

**Business ID:** BUS-111

The original 29 September 1922 Enterprise p.8 advertisement directly prints this name and **Pacific Highway Garage Bldg., Oregon City**. It advertises half soles, heels and made-to-order shoes (`E-359` / `S-393`, visually verified 15 November 2026). Earlier research documents April 14, July 14 and September 22 advertisements at this descriptive location; the new September 29 control extends the dated examples.

The proprietor is unnamed and no street number appears. Do not merge the business with the 1911 Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop at 716 Main, the 1920 K. Schoenheinz contact, or Book 4 C. Schoenheinz at 503 merely by surname. The older Karl/Carl (`P-404`), younger Charles (`P-405`) and Edward (`P-406`) are identity research context, not established owners/operators of this shop. No 503/505 occupancy, physical-building continuity or uninterrupted operation is established.
