# Jones Drug Company

Business ID: `BUS-040`. Related address: `B-013`.

A June 1916 report described a prospective Jones Drug Company move into a former Portland Railway Light & Power / Schilling room during Andresen Building rearrangements (`E-119`). Visually verified advertisements now directly place **Jones Drug Company at 617 Main Street by 15 March 1917**, repeat the exact address on **22 March 1917** (`E-227` / `S-253`), and repeat 617 again on **1 November 1917** (`E-120`).

The 1916 future-tense report and 1917 exact address do not establish the move day, lease terms, legal succession, uninterrupted occupancy, or that every named business used the same room.

Sources: `S-133`, `S-253`, `S-134`.
