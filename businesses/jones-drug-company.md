# Jones Drug Company

Business ID: `BUS-040`. Related address: `B-013`.

A June 1916 report described a prospective Jones Drug Company move into a former Portland Railway Light & Power / Schilling room during Andresen Building rearrangements (`E-119`). A visually verified 1 November 1917 advertisement directly places **Jones Drug Company at 617 Main Street** (`E-120`).

The 1916 future-tense report and 1917 exact address do not establish the move day, lease terms, legal succession, uninterrupted occupancy, or that every named business used the same room.

Sources: `S-133`, `S-134`.
