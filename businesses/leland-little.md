# Leland & Little Billiards

Business ID: `BUS-077`. Related people/leads: `P-089`, `P-090`.

Leland & Little appears in an Oregon City cigar-dealer list on 25 February 1922. The 8 June merchant roster separately names Walter Little, O. H. Leland, and Timms Pool Hall. On 17 August, a visually verified list directly identifies **Leland and Little, Billiards** (`E-126`).

The combination of cigars and billiards is functionally compatible with the 1925 Sanborn's **505 Main billiards/cigars** label, but it is not an address bridge. The partnership is not proved at 505, as Timms's successor, or as the pool hall adjoining the July ignition room. The equation W. L. Little = Walter L. Little is a strong identity inference, not yet a same-context explicit statement.
