# Palace Saloon

Business ID: `BUS-024`. Address: 501 Main Street (`B-005`). Operator: Joe Wilson (`P-037`).

The visually reviewed 19 March 1912 *Morning Enterprise* prints Joe Wilson's liquor-license application for his place of business, **the Palace Saloon, 501 Main Street** (`E-104` / `S-118`). This is direct evidence for the name, operator, address, and application date, but not proof of grant, opening/closing dates, building ownership, succession from A. J. Surber, or continuity into Hunsaker & Taylor's separate 1913 premises.
