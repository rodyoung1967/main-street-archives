# Businesses Directory

The canonical complete business list is [business-index.md](business-index.md), synchronized with `database/businesses.yml`.

Standalone `.md` pages are used for businesses with a multi-date chronology, a central relationship to 503/505 or the Fifth/Main cluster, or an important identity/address conflict. A bounded one-date business can remain fully registered in the index/YAML without requiring a separate page.

Accuracy rules:

- The Wheel was only at 503 Main.
- 505 Tavern, Brass Rail, Dug Inn, Sports Keg, and Thirsty Duck are distinct business-name/operator records at 505.
- Property ownership, business ownership, operation, and liquor-license ownership are separate questions.
- Timms Pool Hall, Leland & Little, and the unnamed July 1922 fire-adjacent pool hall are not merged.
- A merchant roster without addresses does not assign a business to 501, 503, 505, 108 Fifth, or 109 Fifth.
