# Timms Pool Hall

Business ID: `BUS-076`. Person/style: `P-091`.

The visually verified 8 June 1922 Buyers' Carnival roster separately names **Timms Pool Hall**, Walter Little, and O. H. Leland (`E-126`). This proves the named business existed by that date.

No recovered source gives its address or identifies it as the pool hall adjoining the July fire-origin room. Timms must remain distinct from Leland & Little and the separate Ed Wheeler pool room until a license, directory, advertisement, or transfer record supplies a bridge.
