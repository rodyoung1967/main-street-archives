# J. C. Penney Co. / The Golden Rule — 1917 Oregon City

Business ID: `BUS-105`.

## Direct 1917 observation

A visually verified **14 September 1917 Oregon City Enterprise p3** advertisement (`E-262` / `S-288`) visibly prints **J. C. Penney Co.**, **THE GOLDEN RULE**, **175 BUSY STORES**, **Main Street Between Fifth and Sixth**, and **Phone 270**.

A visually verified four-page advertisement on **12 October 1917, pp.3–6** (`E-274` / `S-300`, `T-101`) directly prints the more precise address **524–526 Main Street**.

## Limits

The October range does not establish which physical bay/building occupied each number, property ownership, lease terms, construction, an opening date, or continuous occupancy. It excludes assignment to 501/503/505 based on the September block wording alone.

No matching ad was located in the already completed August Enterprise text backstop or the other September issue text layers. That silence is not an opening/closure boundary.
