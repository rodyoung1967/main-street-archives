# Scripture & May

Business ID: `BUS-039`. Related people/address: `P-082`, `P-083`, `B-008`.

Visually verified advertisements place **Scripture & May at 108 Fifth Street** in selected late-1916 issues and again on 4 January and 1 February 1917. The 24 October 1918 business guide repeats the exact address (`E-119`, `E-120`, `E-124`). The firm advertised horseshoeing and disc-harrow sharpening; a 1917 item also describes its Oregon City blacksmith shop.

This exact 108 Fifth chain does not prove that the probable Scripture & May handwriting in the 1914–1915 water ledger at 110 Fifth is the same premises, a renumbering, or a move. L. E. May is not automatically identified with the May in later May & Washburn.

Sources: `S-133`, `S-134`, `S-138`.
