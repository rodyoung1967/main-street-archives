# McAnulty & Barry / Barry and McAnulty

Business ID: `BUS-113`.

Previously visually reviewed 17 August 1922 Banner-Courier p2 merchant list describes **McAnulty and Barry, Cigars, Restaurant**, without address (S-141). On **30 November 1922 p1**, the original notice headed **Granted Pool Hall License** reports that **Barry and McAnulty** have been granted a license to operate a pool hall **in the room formerly occupied by them as a restaurant** (E-362/S-397; inspected again 17 November 2026). This establishes what the newspaper reports, not independent inspection of the official license.

The exact 1922 room/address remains unresolved. Later Petzold Building/714 Main historic-inventory associations are a serious competing lead, not a direct 1922 address. Do not assign this business to 503/505 or merge it with Timms, Leland & Little or the July fire-adjacent hall. Given names and underlying municipal license remain documentary targets.
