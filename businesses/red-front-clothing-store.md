# Red Front Clothing Store

Business ID: `BUS-074`.

## 22 September 1921 — exact 507 Main address

The project steward located an advertisement in the **Aurora Observer, 22 September 1921, p.3** that prints the **Red Front Clothing Store at 507 Main Street, Oregon City**. The steward visually read the original newspaper advertisement. Evidence state for the printed 1921 address: **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT — STEWARD VISUALLY VERIFIED**. The archive still needs its own preserved page/crop or independent scan review before assigning ordinary archive visual-verification credit to the source file itself.

This exact-address record materially changes the earlier location problem. Red Front is no longer an unnumbered business in 1921: it is directly anchored at **507 Main**.

## July 1922 property sale and fire

The visually verified **13 July 1922 Banner-Courier p.1** report says George A. Harding's Main Street property group included the Fifth Street Restaurant, Miller's shoe store, the adjoining barber shop, and the **Red Front Clothing Store**. The group was sold to **S. Wolf and C. Gates, both of Portland**; Gates took the Fifth Street Restaurant location and Wolf took **the remainder** (`E-096` / `S-111`). The complete original page is already preserved as `newspapers/banner-courier/1922-07/1922-07-13-seq-01.pdf`.

The steward-supplied image of that article confirms the wording and sequence. It does not print Red Front's number, but the 22 September 1921 advertisement supplies a direct earlier 507 Main anchor.

The visually verified **21 July 1922 Oregon City Enterprise p.1** identifies **L. Wolf** as owner of the Red Front clothing store damaged in the fire and says Wolf had recently purchased the shoe-store building in which the fire started. Preserve the **S. Wolf / L. Wolf** initial conflict rather than silently merging them.

## Current location conclusion

- **Red Front at 507 Main on 22 September 1921:** **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT** from the address-bearing advertisement.
- **Red Front at 507 Main at the time of the 18 July 1922 fire:** **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE**, not yet a direct July-1922 numbered-address statement. The business is explicitly still in Harding's property group on 13 July and in the fire loss reporting days later, with no recovered move notice between the 1921 exact-address ad and the fire.
- Do not treat the 1921 exact address alone as proof of uninterrupted occupancy through every intervening date; a nearer 1922 exact-address ad remains desirable corroboration.

This makes 507 Main the leading and strongly supported fire-time Red Front location and helps constrain the frontage reconstruction around 503/505/507.

## Wolf identity / Wolf & Miller connection

A probable working identity remains that **L. Wolf of Red Front may be the Wolf of Wolf & Miller**, which operated at 505 Main in 1918 selling men's clothing, shoes, and shoe-repair services. The October 1918 Wolf & Miller sale notice says one partner would go to Idaho and the other would restock and continue the business. By 1922 Gilbert Miller has a separate shoe business and Wolf is associated with a separate clothing business, making a post-partnership division plausible.

Evidence state: **PROBABLE / WORKING IDENTITY HYPOTHESIS** only. The Wolf & Miller partner's full name and the exact division of business assets are not yet directly documented, and the S. Wolf/L. Wolf newspaper-initial conflict remains unresolved.
