# Red Front Clothing Store

Business ID: `BUS-074`.

The visually verified 13 July 1922 report names the Red Front Clothing Store as one occupant of George A. Harding's Main Street property group sold to S. Wolf and C. Gates (`E-096`). Fire reports describe the Red Front as damaged rather than consistently placing it among the completely destroyed core businesses.

No numbered address, proprietor, deed interest, or exact Gates/Wolf allocation has been established. The name also appears in earlier Oregon City contexts; do not merge those periods or backdate a location without a direct bridge.
