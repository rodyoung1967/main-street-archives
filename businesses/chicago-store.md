# The Chicago Store

Business ID: `BUS-011`. Address: 505 Main Street (`B-002`).

The store advertised at 405 Main on 27 January and 3 February 1911; the latter names Wolf & Nat as proprietors. By 31 March and through April 1911 it advertised at **505 Main**, and an 8 January 1913 advertisement again gives 505 while offering new/second-hand clothing and cleaning/repairing (`E-026` / `S-032`; `E-084`). The 505 ads do not themselves repeat Wolf & Nat. A December 1913 fire report concerns its building and strongly associates the premises with George A. Harding, without proving deed ownership or surviving-building continuity.

See the [505 Main unified timeline](../timelines/505-main.md).

Each visibly prints The Chicago Store at **505 Main**. These ads do not themselves repeat the earlier Wolf & Nat proprietor line.
