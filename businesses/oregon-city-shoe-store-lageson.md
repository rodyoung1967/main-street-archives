# Oregon City Shoe Store

**Business ID:** `BUS-109`  
**Documented location:** Main Street opposite Post Office in 1910; **508 Main Street** by 26 April 1912  
**Documented proprietor:** Justin S. Lageson in 1910 (`P-403`)

## Direct evidence

Primary newspaper advertising visually/directly establishes:

- **28 January 1910** — Oregon City Shoe Store advertisement prints **JUSTIN S. LAGESON** and **Opposite Postoffice**.
- **1 April 1910** — advertisement directly prints **Oregon City Shoe Store — JUSTIN S. LAGESON, Prop. — MAIN STREET**.
- **26 April 1912** — advertisement directly prints **Oregon City Shoe Store — 508 Main St. — Opposite Postoffice**.

The 29 March and 5 April 1912 *Courier* issues also advertise Oregon City Shoe Store but do not print an address or proprietor.

## Identity / continuity limits

Do not automatically extend Lageson's 1910 proprietorship through 1912 without a source explicitly bridging the interval.

Do not merge Oregon City Shoe Store with **C. Schoenheinz's Old Reliable Oregon City Shoe Shop**, directly at 716 Main in 1911. They are separately named and independently located contemporary shoe-business strands.

No relationship to 503 Main is established.
