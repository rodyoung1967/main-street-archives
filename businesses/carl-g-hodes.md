# Carl G. Hodes Liquor Premises

Business ID: `BUS-056`. Related person: `P-066`.

Three separate dated records form the current chronology:

- On 18 September 1908, Carl G. Hodes applied for a three-month liquor license at **327 Main** (`E-123`, `S-137`).
- In 1909, Council minutes list a Hodes saloon at **Sixth and Main** and approve renewal subject to fees and bonds (`E-076`, `S-090`).
- In 1910, Council minutes refer to the period after Hodes vacated his saloon at **Sixth and Main** (`E-079`, `S-093`).

The records do not establish that Hodes moved from 327 to Sixth/Main, bought J. Stewart & Son's business, occupied the Gambrinus property, or vacated because of construction. Exact Sixth/Main premises remain unresolved.
