# Raasch & Lamb Pool and Billiard Hall

Business ID: `BUS-036`.

On 18 May 1916, the *Courier* reported that Raasch & Lamb had three months' permission to move their pool and billiard hall into the basement of the same Andresen Building. A 1 June report repeated the planned basement rearrangement (`E-116`, `E-119`).

Both notices describe a proposed internal move. They do not prove when or whether it was completed, the exact Main Street number, property ownership, or any connection to 503/505. The later documented Andresen Building address of 619 Main is not automatically backdated to this room.

Sources: `S-130`, `S-133`.
