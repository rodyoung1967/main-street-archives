# May & Washburn Garage

Business ID: `BUS-069`. People: `P-083`, `P-084`.

May & Washburn is documented by December 1920, appears in county-road payment records in 1921 and June 1922, and was an operating garage in the July 1922 Fifth/Main fire cluster. The fire destroyed its frame garage and two automobiles (`E-103`). The partnership continued after the fire.

A visually verified 16 February 1922 advertisement jointly heads a horseshoeing price reduction **“May & Washburn and S. F. Scripture”** (`E-347` / `S-371`). It proves coordinated advertising only. Scripture's separate same-page ad gives 108 Fifth; the joint notice gives May & Washburn no address and proves no merger, transfer, shared shop, or identity of May with L. E. May.

The shared **May** name and nearby trade history make L. E. May of Scripture & May a legitimate identity/succession question, but no source yet proves that identity, a transfer of the 108 Fifth premises, or the exact pre-fire garage address. Do not assign May & Washburn to `B-008` without an independent numbered source.
