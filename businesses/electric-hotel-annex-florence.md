# Electric Hotel Annex / Florence Hotel

Business ID: BUS-114.

Banner December 21 p1 reports Gambrinus Brewing Company sold lot 8 Block 26 at southeast Sixth/Main to Ben Levin of Portland; revenue stamps indicate $39,000. Two-story building houses J.C. Penney and Meilke/Koellmeier meat market below Electric Hotel Annex. Enterprise December 22 p6 corroborates sale, describes brick building and tenant leases, one extending to 1925; upstairs 16 sleeping apartments called Florence Hotel. Its p5 separately reports Florence Kerr purchased hotel business from J.J. Tobin and Central Apartments across the street; Tobin retained Electric Hotel on Fourth Street. Kerr’s hotel-business purchase is not a deed to Levin’s property. Exact numbered address and 1911-to-1922 structural continuity require corroboration; no target Block 4/503/505 assignment.

Separate from Electric Hotel’s Fourth Street operation, the underlying real estate, and modern 503/505 businesses. E-086 supplies the 1911 annex association, not an uninterrupted proprietor chain.
