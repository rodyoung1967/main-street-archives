# Star Theatre — Main Street near Eighth, 1912

**Business ID:** `BUS-110`  
**Location in 1912:** Main Street at/near Eighth Street, Oregon City; exact numbered bay unresolved  
**Opening:** 1 June 1912

## Direct evidence

The 2 February 1912 *Oregon City Courier* says Mt. Hood Brewing Co. leased the Hood property at the corner of Main and Eighth for a new two-story business block containing a moving-picture theatre, and that **George Simmons had already leased the picture theatre for a long term**.

The 24 May *Courier* says an unnamed new motion-picture theatre on Main Street was nearly ready to open.

A visually reviewed primary clipping from the 31 May *Morning Enterprise*, preserved by the University of Oregon Oregon Theater Project, announces **STAR THEATRE WILL OPEN TOMORROW** and says:
- George Simmons was manager;
- the building was on Main Street near Eighth;
- it was constructed especially for a moving-picture theatre;
- seating capacity was 300.

Together these support a strong continuity chain from the February planned theatre through the May construction notice to the Star opening.

Evidence: `S-391` / `E-357`.

## Project relevance / exclusion control

This is an important **non-target 1912 Main Street construction control**. It demonstrates one specific Main Street replacement/redevelopment project during the same season as the 10 May Courier's general “tear down / build up” editorial.

It does **not** establish that the 10 May editorial referred specifically to this block, and it supplies no construction evidence for 501/503/505/507 at Fifth/Main.

## Numbered-address conflict

Do not assign a numbered 1912 Star Theatre address from later metadata without reconciliation.

Later sources conflict:
- City historic-resource table associates **716 Main** with c.1912 Star Theatre.
- Oregon Theater Project / 1925 Sanborn-derived metadata associates the Star with **720 Main**.

The direct 1912 sources control for the opening state: **Main Street at/near Eighth**. Numbering and later building continuity remain unresolved.
