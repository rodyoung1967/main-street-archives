# The Dug Inn

Business ID: `BUS-016`. Address: 505 Main Street (`B-002`).

## Documented Record

Oregon City Commission Manager's Report No. 88-49, dated 6 April 1988, identifies **The Dug Inn (formerly The Brass Rail), 505 Main Street** and a change-of-ownership application from Dick or Sheila Wiitanen to the Cotterells (`E-030`).

This proves the trade-name relationship and the Wiitanen transfer role at that date. It does not prove uninterrupted operation from Dick and Sheila's earlier Brass Rail period. Family history places the 1988 transaction within a later sale/default/repossession/transfer cycle after the Wiitanens had begun as Brass Rail operators and later became owners of the 505 real estate.
