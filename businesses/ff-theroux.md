# F. F. Theroux — Pianos and Sewing Machines

Business ID: `BUS-029`. Related person/address: `P-044`, `B-011`.

Dated 1915 and 1916 advertisements place F. F. Theroux's piano and sewing-machine business at **519 Main Street** (`E-107`, `E-109`, `E-116`, `E-119`). A visually verified 4 October 1917 advertisement again identifies **Theroux Music House, 519 Main Street** (`E-120`).

These observations support a multi-year address chronology, but not uninterrupted daily occupancy, property ownership, or a surviving-building identity. Earlier Fifth/Main wording is not treated as proof of a move into 519.

Sources: `S-120`, `S-121`, `S-130`, `S-133`, `S-134`.

## February 1919 — 210 Seventh Street
<!-- 1919 February Enterprise Courier visual review — 2026-09-07 -->

`E-264` / `S-290` visually verifies **Theroux Music House at 210 Seventh Street** in Enterprise advertisements on **7, 14 and 21 February 1919**; the 7 Feb. ad adds **near Elevator**. This is direct 1919 address evidence. It does not by itself date Theroux's departure from 519 Main; separate evidence already places a different occupant at 519 by October 1918.
