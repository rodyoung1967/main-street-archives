# H. P. Brightbill Grocery

Business ID: `BUS-027`. Related person/address: `P-042`, `B-003`.

Dated directory advertisements place **H. P. Brightbill, grocer, at 509 Main Street** in 1915 and selected 1916 issues (`E-107`, `E-109`, `E-116`, `E-119`). The repeated number is an exact storefront anchor, not proof of ownership or the later 507–511 Harding Building footprint.

H. P. Brightbill is not merged with **Henry Brightbill** (`P-034`), whose store is directly documented at 503 Main in 1908. A relationship or succession between them remains unresolved.

Sources: `S-120`, `S-121`, `S-130`, `S-133`.
