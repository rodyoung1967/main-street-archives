# Holman & Randall Undertaking Establishment

Business ID: `BUS-035`. Related people: `P-050`, `P-051`.

A 7 May 1915 report documents the formation of **Holman & Randall** at Holman's undertaking establishment on Main Street near Fifth (`E-112`). On 6 April 1916, the *Courier* reported that R. L. Holman bought T. P. Randall's interest (`E-116`). Joint-name advertising continued afterward, while Holman-only copy appears by August 1916 (`E-119`).

Later joint advertising does not override the reported sale or prove that Randall retained an ownership interest. The recovered sources give Fifth/Main or relative-location wording but do not prove the numbered premises was 501, 503, or 505, and they do not establish real-estate ownership.

Sources: `S-064`–`S-066`, `S-130`, `S-133`.
