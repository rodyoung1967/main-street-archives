# Cox & Ward tobacco / billiard rooms

**Business ID:** `BUS-108`  
**Location:** Main Street, Oregon City; exact numbered premises unresolved  
**Known date:** 9 February 1912

## Direct evidence

The visually verified *Oregon City Courier*, 9 February 1912, p.5 (`S-387` / `E-353`) directly describes **Cox & Ward** as **tobacco dealers and billiard-room proprietors of Main Street**.

The same notice reports that the partnership had **dissolved** and that **Mr. Ward sold to his partner**. It says Ward had been in poor health and that the indoor occupation had not agreed with him.

## Evidence limits

The source does **not** print a street number, either partner's given name, a successor trade name, whether the remaining partner continued at the same premises, property ownership, or a license transfer.

No separate person IDs are assigned yet because the source supplies only partnership surnames and does not independently establish full individual identities.

## Do-not-merge controls

Do not merge this business with:
- **H. H. Smith pool hall** (`BUS-019`) at 503 Main in March 1912;
- **F. D. Cox's 1919 billiard parlors**; or
- Farr/Harr/Well's/other 505 Main billiard/pool-hall businesses.

The timing and similar trade make those comparisons useful research leads, not evidence of identity or premises continuity.
