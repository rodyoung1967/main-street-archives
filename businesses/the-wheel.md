## Known / Working History

**The Wheel was only at 503 Main Street. It was never a 505 Main business.**

Family confirmation: **Raye Grisham** (`P-006`, grandmother; formerly Raye LaFarlette) **bought The Wheel at 503 Main**. Floyd LaFarlette (`P-022`) is identified with The Wheel in family history as Raye's first husband.

The LaFarlette / Grisham name difference refers to **the same woman**, not two successive owners. A record changing from Raye LaFarlette to Raye Grisham should therefore not be treated by itself as a sale or transfer of The Wheel.

Oregon City Commission minutes of **7 June 1967** authorize the mayor to sign a liquor application for **"the Wheel located at 503 Main Street"** (`E-025` / `S-027`). That is primary municipal evidence fixing the address by that date.

Earlier and later newspapers use **Wheel Cafe** and **Wheel Restaurant** at this location: 5th and Main in 1960 (`E-044`); spared in the 1 September 1967 fire (`E-045`); downtown lunch landmark in 1983 (`E-047`); **The Wheel Cafe, 503 Main St.**, with **Richard M. Wiitanen** as owner in August 1985 (`E-048`, `E-049`).

A 2017 article uses the phrase **"The Wheel Tavern"** (`E-001`). Family correction says this was **never an official business name**. Preserve the phrase only as the article's wording; do not treat it as a historical trade name or legal-name variant.

## Important 503 / 505 Separation Rule

The Wheel at 503 and the tavern business at 505 were **separate businesses with separate ownership chains**.

The important overlap is real-estate ownership: while Dick and Sheila Wiitanen were associated with operating The Wheel at 503, they also owned the **505 building**. That does **not** mean they normally operated the 505 tavern business.

Family clarification says any Dick/Sheila control of the 505 business itself may have occurred only briefly during a **repossession, non-payment/default, or transfer interval**. The April 1988 municipal change-of-ownership record (`E-030`) confirms a Wiitanen-to-Cotterell business transfer at 505, but it does not by itself establish that Dick and Sheila routinely operated the 505 tavern while running The Wheel.

Mitch Young (`P-011` / `P-018`) later owned and operated **505 Tavern at 505 Main** from March 1991 to March 2004. That period is **not** The Wheel.

Do not merge The Wheel with 505 Tavern, Brass Rail, The Dug Inn, Sports Keg, or Thirsty Duck.

## Documented / Reported Ownership and Operation

- Raye LaFarlette / Raye Grisham is the **same person** and bought The Wheel at 503 (`E-022`).
- Victor independently managed the restaurant portion during Raye's years and early Dick/Sheila years (`E-007`).
- Working family interpretation: the later **Raye Grisham → Wiitanen** transition at The Wheel was a **sale of the 503 business**. Exact date, terms, and liquor-license transfer remain to be documented.
- Dick and Sheila Wiitanen purchased the **503 building** about 1975 from Mrs. Nemeth (spelling uncertain) (`E-038`); real-estate acquisition is separate from the business-sale chronology.
- Richard M. Wiitanen is independently documented as owner of **The Wheel Cafe at 503 Main** in August 1985 (`E-049`).
- A 2010 Portland Mercury feature identifies the Wheel Lounge at 503 Main (`S-029`); this is secondary naming evidence and not proof of a formal legal name.
