# Portland House

Business ID `BUS-118`. [Southeast Fifth/Main site](../buildings/426-main-portland-house-site.md) (`B-021`). The November 1911 Sanborn map directly identifies the Portland House boarding premises at historic 426 Main. August 1915 reporting independently names the Portland House on Mrs. M. L. Foster's southeast-corner property. Same-site identification is strong across these sources. Evidence `E-371`, `E-318`, `E-279`.

The July 21, 1916 Enterprise names [Minnie L. Foster](../people/profiles/minnie-l-foster.md) as property owner and reports that Frank and Mark Beck had a three-year lease dating June 1, 1916. The December 1919 fire report calls Mr. and Mrs. Beck owners, without specifying which interest they held or documenting a transfer. The 1896 photographed sign belongs to the trade-name research, with building continuity unresolved.

The January 11, 1917 Courier reports [Frank Beck](../people/profiles/frank-beck.md) bought the 34-by-105-foot lot beneath the Portland House for approximately $8,500, names Foster as prior owner and cites Dillman & Howland as intermediary (`E-378`). The reported purchase is distinct from the 1916 lease. The actual instrument and the exact rights held in 1919 remain unverified.
