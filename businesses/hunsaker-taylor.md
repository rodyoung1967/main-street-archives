# Hunsaker & Taylor Liquor Premises

Business ID: `BUS-025`. Address: 501 Main Street (`B-005`). Related entity record: `P-038`.

The visually reviewed 3 October 1913 *Morning Enterprise* prints a liquor-license application by **Hunsaker & Taylor** for their place of business at **501 Main Street** (`E-104` / `S-119`). It establishes the partnership/business style, address, and application—not the partners' given names, a license grant, or a sale/rename/continuation from Joe Wilson's Palace Saloon.
