# Alex Schieremann Tailor Shop

Business ID: `BUS-075`. Person: `P-085`.

The visually verified local **20 July 1922 Banner-Courier** report says the fire began in a **vacant Main Street storeroom formerly occupied by Alex Schieremann as a tailor shop**, in a building owned by S. Wolf; a pool hall adjoined the former tailor premises (`E-103`, `S-117`).

The visually verified **21 July 1922 Oregon City Enterprise p.1** sharpens the geometry by describing the origin area as a **vacant half** formerly occupied by Schiermann and also calling the structure **the shoe-store building in which the fire started**. The same Enterprise account identifies the building immediately north of the fire as saved by fireproof construction.

Combined with the reconstructed frontage and the adjoining pool/billiard hall at 505 Main, the project now treats **Schiermann's former tailor portion as part of the 503 Main fire-origin building** as a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE**. The same structure also contained Gilbert Miller's shoe-store premises. This resolves the earlier apparent conflict between a vacant tailor room and an active shoe business by allowing the building to have been internally divided into portions/halves.

No recovered Schiermann advertisement yet directly prints `503 Main`, so the exact address remains inferred rather than DOCUMENTED / DIRECT. The date Schiermann vacated and whether the same-day cleaning/dyeing description refers to his operation or another tenant remain separate questions.
