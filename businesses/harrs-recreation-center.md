# Harr's Recreation Center

Business ID: `BUS-014`. Address: 505 Main Street (`B-002`). Operator/license applicant: Carl R. Harr (`P-094`).

The City synthesis transcribes a 1945 directory entry at 505 (`E-032` / `S-033`). Commission text of 11 February 1948 names Harr as a pool-hall operator without first name/business/address; a later-1948 liquor-license list directly identifies **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street** (`E-127` / `S-142`). The retrieved municipal text was not page-image certified, and the later excerpt lacks its opening meeting page.

This proves Harr's is not a Farr transcription error. Farr's is a separate business; transition date, opening/closing, and property ownership remain unresolved.
