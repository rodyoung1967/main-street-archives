# Harr's Recreation Center

Business ID: `BUS-014`. Address: 505 Main Street (`B-002`). Operator/license applicant: Carl R. Harr (`P-094`).

The City historic-resource synthesis reports **Harr's Recreation Center at 505 Main in 1945** (`E-032` / `S-033`), but the exact underlying contemporary source is still unresolved. Visual review of the City's 2000 Downtown Historic Resource Survey selected bibliography found Pacific Directory Service volumes for **1941 Oregon City** and **1948 Clackamas County**, but no listed 1945 directory; therefore the 1945 City statement should not be described specifically as a "1945 directory entry" unless the 505 survey research notes or another source later proves that provenance. See `../evidence/source-captures/2000-downtown-survey-1945-harr-source-provenance-gap-2026-09-04.md`.

Commission text of 11 February 1948 names Harr as a pool-hall operator without first name/business/address; a later-1948 liquor-license list directly identifies **Carl R. Harr — Harr's Recreation Center — 505 Main Street** (`E-127` / `S-142`). The retrieved municipal text was not page-image certified, and the later excerpt lacks its opening meeting page.

This proves Harr's is not a Farr transcription error. Farr's is a separate business; transition date, opening/closing, 1945 source provenance, and property ownership remain unresolved.
