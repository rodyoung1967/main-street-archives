# Schrader's Bakery and the Wemmo & Haas Sale

Business IDs: `BUS-063` (Schrader's Bakery), `BUS-080` (Wemmo & Haas bakeries). Related people: `P-096`, `P-097`.

A visually verified 18 September 1908 hiring advertisement names **Schrader's Bakery on Main Street** but gives no street number or proprietor (`E-123`, `S-137`). January 1912 advertising places Schrader's Bakery near the Post Office. On 20 December 1912, the *Courier* reported that **Herman Schrader sold two Oregon City bakeries, including one on Main Street, to Wemmo & Haas** (`E-093`, `S-102`, `S-106`).

The sale is a business-transfer record, not a deed. The sources do not establish the Main Street number, whether the 1908 and 1912 shops occupied the same room continuously, the purchasers' individual names, or the length of Wemmo & Haas operation. “Main 33” in the 1908 advertisement is a telephone number, not a street address.

## 1902 Main-Street Bakery background

Legacy-branch source `S-161` / evidence `E-145` preserves a 3 January 1902 profile saying Herman Schrader had worked nearly eighteen years in the **Main-Street Bakery** before purchasing his then-current bakery about a year earlier. This extends the trade chronology but does not prove the exact later Main Street storefront or continuity to the 1912 premises.
