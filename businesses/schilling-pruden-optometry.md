# Schilling and Pruden optometry practices

**BUS-037 / P-052 — Wm. A. Schilling.** Earlier verified 1916 ads give 617 Main (E-116). June 1 p8 announces a new location opposite at **612 Main**; future tense does not prove completion on that date.

**BUS-038 / P-053 — Pruden.** October 5 p5 explicitly typesets **Pruden, Optometrist, 612 Main, Successor to Wm. A. Schilling**. November 2 p7 and December 7 p5 repeat the signature-style name and successor line. This supersedes the older **Cruden** OCR interpretation as an identity basis. December 14 itself remains unreviewed; September 28 is a separate OCR-only successor lead.

Given-name expansion, exact move/succession dates, sale terms and internal sharing of 612 remain unresolved. No real-estate ownership or target-building occupancy is inferred. Source **S-133**, evidence **E-119**; [originals and page ledger](../evidence/source-captures/1916-courier-first-monthly-june-december.md).
