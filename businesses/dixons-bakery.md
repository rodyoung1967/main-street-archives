# Dixon's Bakery / Dixon's Pastry Shop

Business ID: `BUS-023`. Related address group: `B-003`.

A visually reviewed *Oregonian* classified dated **10 June 1964** directly advertises for a waitress at **Dixon's Pastry Shop, 509 Main St., Oregon City** (`E-129` / `S-144` / `IMG-0716`). This is the archive's first exact-number anchor for the Dixon name.

The *Oregonian* fire report of **2 September 1967** separately names **Dixon's Bakery** among businesses gutted in the Main Street block fire (`E-045` / `S-052` / `IMG-0047`). The two sources strongly associate the Dixon business with the 507–511 address group, but they do not by themselves prove an unchanged legal name, ownership, uninterrupted operation, or that the 1967 bakery occupied precisely the same 509 room.

An unnamed **baking and coffee shop** was offered for sale through **509 Main** on 5 December 1965 (`E-130` / `S-145` / `IMG-0715`). The archive does not assume that this was Dixon's or that a sale occurred.

## Mid-1960s P-4565 photograph

CCHS `P-4565` (`S-220` / `E-200` / `IMG-0749`) directly shows **DIXON’S Pastry Shop** in the photographed row. This visually corroborates the independent 10 June 1964 advertisement at **509 Main** (`E-129`) and precedes the 1 September 1967 fire report naming Dixon’s Bakery (`E-045`).
