# Henry Brightbill's Store

Business ID: `BUS-078`. Address: 503 Main Street (`B-001`). Merchant: Henry Brightbill (`P-034`).

The *Oregonian* of 25 August 1908 identifies Henry Brightbill as an Oregon City merchant whose store was at **503 Main Street** (`E-042` / `S-049` / `IMG-0044`). The article gives no trade name, merchandise, ownership, or transfer information. H. P. Brightbill at 509 is not merged with Henry solely from surname and chronology. The address observation does not prove the surviving c.1919-estimated building existed in 1908.
