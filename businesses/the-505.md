## Known / Working History

**505 Tavern** is the documented and family-confirmed trade name for the tavern business at **505 Main Street**.

This is a **separate business from The Wheel at 503 Main**.

Working sequence (cyclical, not a one-way rename):

1. **505 Tavern** is documented in Oregon City Commission liquor-license renewals in **November 1965** and **March 1972** (`E-029`).
2. **Ray Hansen** (`P-005`) owned the 505 Tavern **business** and **sold it to Dick and Sheila Wiitanen** (`E-005`, `E-022`, `E-031`).
3. Dick and Sheila **renamed it Brass Rail Tavern** (`BUS-010`).
4. They later sold and repossessed the business; one documented 1988 transfer is **The Dug Inn (formerly The Brass Rail)** to the Cotterells (`BUS-016`, `E-030`).
5. After a later repossession, **Mitch / Mitchell (C.) Young** (`P-011` / `P-018`, same person) purchased the tavern business from Dick and Sheila in **March 1991** and **ran** it as 505 Tavern through **March 2004** (`E-004`, `E-022`). Do not claim he restored the name, fixtures, or building; that is unknown.
