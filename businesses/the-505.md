## Known / Working History

**505 Tavern** is the documented and family-confirmed trade name for the tavern business at **505 Main Street**.

This was **always a separate business from The Wheel at 503 Main**. **The Wheel was never at 505**, and **"The Wheel Tavern" was never an official name** for either business.

Working sequence (cyclical, not a one-way rename):

1. **505 Tavern** is documented in Oregon City Commission liquor-license renewals in **November 1965** and **March 1972** (`E-029`) and again by a 1976 classified (`E-046`).
2. **Ray Hansen** (`P-005`) belongs to the earlier 505 Tavern ownership/operator history. Older family history described a Hansen-to-Wiitanen sale and Brass Rail rename (`E-005`, `E-031`), but the 19 August 2026 clarification narrows that account.
3. While **Dick and Sheila Wiitanen ran The Wheel at 503**, they also owned the **505 building**. That was a **landlord/real-estate role**, not ordinary operation of the 505 tavern business.
4. Any period in which Dick and/or Sheila actually controlled the **505 business** may have been brief during **repossession, non-payment/default, or a transfer interval**. Primary municipal evidence confirms one such transfer role: on **6 April 1988**, The Dug Inn (formerly Brass Rail) had a change-of-ownership application from Dick or Sheila Wiitanen to the Cotterells (`E-030`).
5. Family history places additional sales/repossessions and names including **Sports Keg** in the late 1980s / early 1990s. The exact owner/operator chain still needs reconstruction.
6. After a later repossession, **Mitch / Mitchell (C.) Young** (`P-011` / `P-018`, same person) purchased the tavern business in **March 1991** and **ran** 505 Tavern through **March 2004** (`E-004`, `E-022`). Do not claim he restored the name, fixtures, or building; that is unknown.

## Location / Ownership Handling

- Correct location: **505 Main Street, Oregon City, Oregon**.
- Do **not** file any 505 period as The Wheel.
- Do **not** use Dick and Sheila's ownership of the **505 real estate** as evidence that they routinely operated the 505 tavern.
- A Wiitanen appearance in a business-transfer or liquor record should be evaluated as a possible repossession/default/transfer interval unless stronger evidence establishes ordinary operation.
- `BUS-009` **The 505** remains in the archive as the earlier handling label so old cross-references stay valid. The current working trade name for Mitch's 1991-2004 period is **505 Tavern** (`BUS-005`), pending license/directory pages.
