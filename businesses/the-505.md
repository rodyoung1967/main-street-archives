## Known / Working History

**505 Tavern** is the documented and family-confirmed trade name for the tavern business at **505 Main Street**.

This was **always a separate business from The Wheel at 503 Main**. **The Wheel was never at 505**, and **"The Wheel Tavern" was never an official name** for either business.

Working sequence (business ownership and real-estate ownership are separate):

1. **505 Tavern** is documented in Oregon City Commission liquor-license renewals in **November 1965** and **March 1972** (`E-029`) and again by a 1976 classified (`E-046`).
2. **Ray Hansen** (`P-005`) belongs to the earlier 505 Tavern ownership/operator history. Family history places a later transfer to Dick and Sheila Wiitanen, after which the business became **Brass Rail** (`E-005`, `E-031`). Exact transaction and rename dates remain open.
3. **Dick and Sheila ran Brass Rail at 505 Main before they owned the building.** During this first Brass Rail phase they were business operators/owners but **not yet the 505 real-estate owners** (`E-022`, `E-038`).
4. Dick and Sheila later purchased the **505 building** from a woman remembered as **Mrs. Nesmeth / Nemeth** (spelling uncertain). This corrects the earlier archive claim that the Mrs. Nemeth purchase was the 503 building (`E-038`).
5. After acquiring the real estate, the Wiitanens retained the **505 building** while the operating tavern business later passed through other owners/operators. Subsequent Wiitanen appearances in 505 business records may represent voluntary sale, repossession, non-payment/default, or transfer intervals rather than continuous operation.
6. Primary municipal evidence confirms one later transfer role: on **6 April 1988**, The Dug Inn (formerly Brass Rail) had a change-of-ownership application from Dick or Sheila Wiitanen to the Cotterells (`E-030`).
7. Family history places additional sales/repossessions and names including **Sports Keg** in the late 1980s / early 1990s. The exact owner/operator chain still needs reconstruction.
8. After a later repossession returned the business to Dick and Sheila Wiitanen, they renamed it **505 Tavern** before selling it to their son **Mitch / Mitchell (C.) Young** (`P-011` / `P-018`, same person). Mitch acquired the business under that existing public/trade name in **March 1991** and ran it through **March 2004** (`E-004`, `E-022`). He did not introduce or restore the name; the exact rename date and formal license/entity wording remain open.
9. The Wiitanen family retained the **505 real estate** through Richard / Dick Wiitanen's death in **November 2024**. The building then passed to **Rodney Young and Mitchell Young as part of Dick's estate**; current ownership is reflected through 505 LLC.

## Location / Ownership Handling

- Correct location: **505 Main Street, Oregon City, Oregon**.
- Do **not** file any 505 period as The Wheel.
- **Dick and Sheila's original Brass Rail operation predates their ownership of the 505 building.**
- After they bought the building, distinguish **property ownership/landlord status** from ownership or operation of whichever tavern business occupied 505 at a given time.
- A later Wiitanen appearance in a business-transfer or liquor record should be evaluated in context; it may represent repossession/default/transfer rather than continuous day-to-day operation.
- `BUS-009` **The 505** remains in the archive as the earlier handling label so old cross-references stay valid. Direct participant testimony establishes **505 Tavern** (`BUS-005`) as the public/trade name already in use when Mitch acquired the business in March 1991; license/entity documents are still desirable.
