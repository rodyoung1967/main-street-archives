# Eddy's Dependable Store

Business **BUS-032**. Dated address: **526 Main**, November 25, 1915. Evidence **E-109**; source **S-121**; timeline **T-020**.

The [November25 Courier p6 advertisement](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn00063698/1915-11-25/ed-1/seq-6/) visibly prints “Eddy's Dependable Store” and526Main, advertising sweaters, coats, caps, children's apparel and household/clothing merchandise.

No proprietor is named. Do not merge this with the1910 **Watson B. Eddy & Son / Main & Sixth** record solely on surname and vicinity. An identity/succession bridge, entry/exit dates and historic parcel remain open (RL-043). **526 is not 527**; no B-004 assignment is made.
