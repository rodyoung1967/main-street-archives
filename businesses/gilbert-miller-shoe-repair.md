# Gilbert Miller Shoe Repair / Miller's Shoe Store

Business ID: `BUS-071`. Person: `P-046`.

Gilbert Miller's Springfield association with Wolf & Miller is directly documented in February 1916 (`E-113`). The Oregon City business later appears in the 1922 Fifth/Main cluster as Miller's shoe store / Gilbert Miller's shoe-repair shop: it was part of Harding's reported store group and was destroyed in the July fire (`E-096`, `E-103`).

By September 1922, Miller advertised restored shoe machinery at **209 Fifth Street**, formerly Goldman's second-hand store. By October he advertised at **419 Main Street**. These are post-fire relocations and do not identify the pre-fire shop's exact address.

The project preserves the 1918 **C. E. Miller / Gilbert Miller** source discrepancy and does not assume the Wolf partner's identity or legal ownership share.
