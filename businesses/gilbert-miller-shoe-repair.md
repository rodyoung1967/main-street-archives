# Gilbert Miller Shoe Repair / Miller's Shoe Store

Business ID: `BUS-071`. Person: `P-046`.

Gilbert Miller's Springfield association with Wolf & Miller is directly documented in February 1916 (`E-113`). Wolf & Miller is then directly documented at 505 Main in 1918 selling men's clothing, shoes and shoe-repair services. The October 1918 sale notice says one partner would leave for Idaho while the other would restock and continue the business; the exact post-partnership division remains unresolved.

## Pre-fire 1922 location

The Oregon City business later appears in the 1922 Fifth/Main cluster as Miller's shoe store / Gilbert Miller's shoe-repair shop. The visually verified **13 July 1922 Banner-Courier p.1** property-sale report places Miller's shoe store within George A. Harding's Main Street property group, together with the Fifth Street Restaurant, an adjoining barber shop and Red Front Clothing Store. C. Gates received the Fifth Street Restaurant location and S. Wolf the remainder (`E-096`, `S-111`).

The visually verified **20 July 1922 Banner-Courier** fire report says Miller's shoe-repair shop and stock were destroyed and that the fire began in a vacant storeroom formerly occupied by Alex Schieremann as a tailor; the adjoining pool hall suffered water/roof damage rather than being razed (`E-103`, `S-117`). The visually verified **21 July 1922 Oregon City Enterprise p.1** goes further: it calls the origin structure **the shoe-store building in which the fire started** while also describing the origin as a **vacant half** formerly occupied by Schiermann.

Taken with the established frontage sequence and the adjoining 505 pool-hall reconstruction, the project now treats **Miller's Shoe Store at 503 Main immediately before the 18 July 1922 fire as a STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE**. The evidence supports the same 503 structure containing Miller's shoe-store premises and a vacant former-Schiermann portion/half. No recovered pre-fire advertisement yet literally prints `503 Main`, so this is not labeled DOCUMENTED / DIRECT.

## Post-fire relocation

A steward-supplied and visually verified **24 August 1922 Banner-Courier p.2** advertisement explicitly gives **419 MAIN STREET** as **“The New Location Of MILLER'S SHOE STORE”** and says Miller was installing his shoe-repair machinery after the fire. This is **DOCUMENTED / DIRECT** for Miller at 419 Main by 24 August 1922.

Earlier archive wording that placed Miller at **209 Fifth Street** after the fire is superseded unless a separately visually verified primary source establishes a temporary/intermediate location there. Do not use 209 Fifth as the post-fire address by default.

The project preserves the 1918 **C. E. Miller / Gilbert Miller** source discrepancy and does not assume the Wolf partner's identity or legal ownership share. A probable working hypothesis now connects the unidentified Wolf & Miller partner with **L. Wolf**, later owner of Red Front, but that identity and the exact 1918 business division remain unproved.
