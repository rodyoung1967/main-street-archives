# Fifth Street Restaurant

Business ID: `BUS-070`.

## Documented Record

The restaurant appears in the 8 June 1922 Buyers' Carnival roster. On 13 July, the *Banner-Courier* reported that the Fifth Street Restaurant location formed the part of George A. Harding's Main Street property group allocated to C. Gates (`E-096`). The restaurant building was destroyed in the 18 July fire (`E-103`). On 17–18 August, reports identified C. E. Gates's two-story hollow-tile replacement at Fifth/Main, with two ground-floor storerooms and upper rooms (`E-097`).

## Owner / Operator Conflict

Contemporary fire accounts variously name **Ed Gage**, **J. Blake**, and **Ed. Blake** in owner/operator roles. Preserve the conflict; do not merge Gage and Blake or convert a wire-service transmission into a settled ownership chain.

## Address Limit

No recovered source gives the restaurant's numbered address or frontage width. It may map principally to 501 Main or include 503, but neither model is established.
