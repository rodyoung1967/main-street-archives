# L. Hylton — Cigars, Tobacco, and Soft Drinks

Business ID: `BUS-020`. Address: 503 Main Street (`B-001`). Person: `P-024`.

The 12 February 1914 *Oregon City Courier* industrial edition profiles **L. Hylton at 503 Main**, dealing in cigars, tobacco, smokers' supplies, and soft drinks. A 23 April Home Industry advertisement repeats the exact address and trade (`E-024` / `S-025`). These do not establish opening/closing dates, a Smith-to-Hylton transfer, property ownership, later Kwality continuity, or identity of the surviving building.
