# Oregon City Laundry

Business ID: `BUS-068`. Principal reconstructed Fifth Street address: 109 Fifth (`B-009`). Temporary post-fire office: 519 Main (`B-011`).

## Location Chronology

- 1910–1912: Main near Third / Third and Main, under Blakeslee names.
- February 1916: the intended rear-Courier construction site was traded for a Fifth Street location (`E-125`).
- 2 April 1916: the new plant on Fifth just off Main was nearing completion.
- 24 October 1918: directly listed at **109 Fifth Street**, M. L. Blakeslee proprietor (`E-124`).
- August 1921: the laundry advertised on Fifth near Main.
- 18 July 1922: the Fifth/Main-area plant was destroyed; Knoepel & Schofield were reported operators (`E-103`).
- 20 July 1922: a temporary receiving office was arranged with Pantorium at **519 Main**. This was not proof of a production plant there.
- By May 1925: the Sanborn map again shows an Oregon City Laundry physical complex in the Fifth/Main/Water block, behind multiple Main-frontage positions (`E-102`).

## Limits

The exact pre-fire and post-fire footprints, legal ownership, rebuild date, and all operator transitions remain open. The 1916 plant and 1918 109 Fifth premises are a strong continuity chain, not yet exact parcel/permit proof. The 1925 building cannot be treated as surviving pre-fire fabric because the 1922 plant burned.
