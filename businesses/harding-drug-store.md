## Known / Working History

Harding Drug Store is associated with George A. Harding and the original Harding Building at 507 Main Street.

The archive currently treats Harding Drug Store as a key business connected to 507 Main, but the exact operating dates, legal ownership, tenancy arrangement, and documentary source trail still need to be established.

## George A. Harding Connection

### Dated 1915 address correction

The visually reviewed August–October 1915 directories list **Geo. A. Harding's drug business at 511 Main**, while W. W. Bradley is at **507** and H. P. Brightbill at **509** (`E-107` / `S-120`). See [dated batch pages](../evidence/source-captures/1915-courier-sheets-073-100.md). The older 507 association above is retained as later/general building context, **not a valid exact address for Harding in 1915**. This batch does not establish a move date, a legal business-name change, uninterrupted operation into 1967, or the historic Harding Building's exact footprint.

George A. Harding is associated with both Harding Drug Store and the Harding Building.

- Person record: `P-001`
- Building record: `B-003`
- Business record: `BUS-007`

The archive should not assume whether Harding owned the building, developed it, leased space in it, or operated the drug store there until primary documents support the relationship.

## Building Context

Harding Drug Store belongs to the original historic Harding Building story, not the 1968 replacement building unless later evidence proves continuity.

The original Harding Building was destroyed in the Main Street fire the night of **1 September 1967** (`E-045`). Harding Drug Store was among the businesses gutted. That fire date remains documented by the Oregonian; replacement-building date is still open (`RL-004`).
